Free SEO Audit: See What Is Holding Your Website Back

Enter your website and get an instant SEO health report. We run over 110 checks across technical, on-page, content, image, link, schema, local, mobile, accessibility and AI search SEO, and show you exactly what to fix and where.

What the audit checks

  • Crawlability: status code, robots.txt, XML sitemap, noindex tags, canonical tags and redirects.
  • HTTPS and security: SSL, HTTP to HTTPS redirects, mixed content and HSTS.
  • On-page SEO: title tag, meta description, H1 and subheadings, and language.
  • Content: word count and image alt text.
  • Internal linking: internal links and broken links.
  • Markup and social: schema structured data, Open Graph, Twitter cards and favicon.
  • Mobile and speed: viewport, server response, compression and Google Core Web Vitals.

The audit checks your home page or any page you enter. For a full site crawl, keyword research and a plan to fix everything, book a free call.

FAQs

Free SEO audit questions

Is the SEO audit really free?

Yes. The audit is free and you see your report straight away. We ask for your name and email so we can follow up with advice if you want it.

How is the SEO score worked out?

Each check is graded as an error, warning or notice, in the same way as professional site audit tools. Errors count most. Your score is the share of weighted checks your page passes, from 0 to 100.

Which page does the audit check?

It checks the page you enter in depth, then crawls up to 25 more pages from your internal links and XML sitemap, plus your robots.txt, llms.txt, security headers and external links. Enter your home page for the best overview.

Where does the speed score come from?

Speed and Core Web Vitals come from Google PageSpeed Insights, tested on a mobile device. Google can take up to a minute, so that section loads after the main report.
