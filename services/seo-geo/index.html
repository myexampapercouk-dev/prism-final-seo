How it works

Our SEO process

SEO compounds over time. A clear process makes sure the effort goes into the pages and fixes that matter most.

1. Audit and research

We crawl the site, review Search Console data and research the searches your buyers actually make, including the questions they ask AI assistants.

2. Prioritised plan

You get a short, prioritised list: technical fixes, pages to improve, new pages to create and links worth earning. Each item has a clear reason.

3. Fix and publish

We fix technical issues, improve titles, content and internal links, and publish new pages that answer buyer questions properly.

4. Measure and refine

Monthly reporting focuses on the queries and pages that generate enquiries and sales, not vanity metrics, and the plan is adjusted as data comes in.

SEO vs GEO

Why SEO and GEO belong together

Search is no longer only ten blue links. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity now answer many questions directly, and they draw on the same signals good SEO already builds: clear, well-structured pages, consistent facts about your business, expert content and mentions on trusted websites.

That is why we treat generative engine optimisation as part of SEO rather than a separate product. We make sure your key pages answer questions directly, that your organisation, services and prices are described consistently, and that structured data helps machines understand who you are. Read our guide to what GEO is and how it works for more detail.

Authority still matters for both. Our digital PR and content work earns the coverage and links that help pages rank and get cited.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to work?

SEO and paid media campaigns typically begin showing meaningful results within 3–6 months. Technical fixes can show sooner; competitive terms take longer. We never promise rankings we cannot control.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation is the work of making your brand visible and cited in AI-generated answers from tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity.

Do you do SEO for Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. Ecommerce SEO covers category and product pages, site architecture, faceted navigation, structured data and speed for Shopify and WooCommerce stores.

Will I get reports?

Yes. Reporting is included in our retainers and focuses on the searches, pages and enquiries that matter commercially.

Can you help after a website redesign lost rankings?

Yes. We check redirects, indexation, internal links and content changes to find what was lost and fix it. Planning the migration properly in advance is even better.
