How it works

Our SEO process

SEO compounds over time. A clear process makes sure the effort goes into the pages and fixes that matter most.

1. Audit and research

We crawl the site, review Search Console data and research the searches your buyers actually make, including the questions they ask AI assistants.

2. Prioritised plan

You get a short, prioritised list: technical fixes, pages to improve, new pages to create and links worth earning. Each item has a clear reason.

3. Fix and publish

We fix technical issues, improve titles, content and internal links, and publish new pages that answer buyer questions properly.

4. Measure and refine

Monthly reporting focuses on the queries and pages that generate enquiries and sales, not vanity metrics, and the plan is adjusted as data comes in.

SEO vs GEO

Why SEO and GEO belong together

Search is no longer only ten blue links. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity now answer many questions directly, and they draw on the same signals good SEO already builds: clear, well-structured pages, consistent facts about your business, expert content and mentions on trusted websites.

That is why we treat generative engine optimisation as part of SEO rather than a separate product. We make sure your key pages answer questions directly, that your organisation, services and prices are described consistently, and that structured data helps machines understand who you are. Read our guide to what GEO is and how it works for more detail.

Authority still matters for both. Our digital PR and content work earns the coverage and links that help pages rank and get cited.

Choosing an SEO agency in the UK: what good SEO services include

A good SEO agency in the UK should fix the technical issues stopping your pages being crawled and understood, build the pages your buyers actually search for, and earn the authority that decides competitive rankings. It should also make sure the same pages are clear enough for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity to quote, because that is now part of how people find suppliers.

This guide explains what SEO services from Prism Works Studio involve in practice, how to tell whether an agency is the right fit, and what to expect in the first few months. If you are weighing up budgets first, our guide to how much SEO costs in the UK sets out typical ranges across the market.

How SEO services work month to month

SEO is not one task. It is a rolling list of improvements, ordered by how much each one is likely to matter for enquiries or sales. Most engagements move through three overlapping layers.

1. Foundations: can search engines read the site?

Before content or links can work, the site has to be crawlable, indexable and fast enough on mobile. That means checking which pages Google has indexed, finding duplicate or thin pages, fixing redirect chains and broken internal links, adding structured data and making sure templates do not hide important content. Our technical SEO audit checklist lists the checks we run.

2. Relevance: does each page match a real search?

Next comes matching pages to intent. A service business usually needs one strong page per service and location it genuinely serves. An online shop needs category pages that reflect how shoppers search, product pages with unique copy, and buying guides that answer pre-purchase questions. Each page gets a clear title, a direct answer near the top and internal links that show search engines how the site fits together.

3. Authority: why should Google trust you over a competitor?

When several pages are equally relevant, authority usually decides the order. That comes from links and mentions on relevant, trusted websites, plus consistent facts about your business across the web. We earn these through useful content and digital PR, never through bought links or private blog networks.

Common SEO problems and how they are fixed

Most sites that stall have one or two of the issues below. Diagnosing the right one first saves months of effort on work that will not move the needle.

Pages are published but never appear in GoogleIndexation blocks, weak internal linking or thin, near-duplicate contentFix robots and canonical rules, link to the page from relevant pages, make the content distinct
Traffic fell after a redesignChanged URLs without redirects, removed content or new templatesMap old URLs to new ones, restore lost content and recheck templates
Ranking on page two for valuable termsCompetitors have more authority or a better-matched pageImprove the page for intent, strengthen internal links and earn relevant links
Lots of traffic but few enquiriesRanking for informational terms with no route to a service pageTarget commercial searches and add clear next steps to guides
Not mentioned in AI answersInconsistent business facts, vague pages and few third-party mentionsAnswer-first content, consistent entity details, schema and earned coverage

Where GEO fits into SEO services

Generative engine optimisation is not a separate trick. AI assistants tend to cite pages that state facts clearly, answer a question in the first sentences, and come from brands that are described consistently across the web. So the same work that helps rankings also helps AI visibility, with a few additions: tighter answer-first copy, clear comparison tables, consistent organisation details and structured data. Our explainer on what generative engine optimisation is covers this in more depth, and choosing a GEO agency explains what to look for if AI visibility is your main concern.

How to choose an SEO agency: questions to ask

Whoever you speak to, these questions quickly separate a considered plan from a generic package:

  • What will you do in the first 30 days? Expect an audit, keyword and competitor research, and a prioritised list, not a vague promise of “optimisation”.
  • How do you decide what to fix first? Priorities should be tied to pages that can produce enquiries or sales.
  • How do you earn links? Listen for content and PR. Be wary of anyone selling links by the dozen.
  • What will the monthly report show? Queries, pages, enquiries and actions taken, not just a visibility score.
  • Who owns the work? Content, accounts and data should stay yours if you leave.
  • Do you guarantee rankings? The honest answer is no. Nobody controls Google.
  • Agency or freelancer? Both can work. Our comparison of an SEO agency vs a freelancer sets out the trade-offs.

How remote SEO projects run across the UK

SEO does not need anyone on site. We work with businesses across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland using video calls, shared documents and access to your Search Console, analytics and CMS. A typical rhythm looks like this:

  1. Kick-off call to agree goals, priority services or products and the locations you serve.
  2. Access set-up for Search Console, GA4 and your website, with your accounts staying in your name.
  3. Audit and plan shared as a short, prioritised document you can comment on.
  4. Monthly delivery of fixes and content, with drafts sent for approval before anything goes live.
  5. Monthly report and review covering what changed, what it did and what comes next.

Results from SEO typically build over 3–6 months, depending on competition, the state of the site and how quickly changes can be published. Technical fixes can show effects sooner; authority takes longer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to work?

SEO and paid media campaigns typically begin showing meaningful results within 3–6 months. Technical fixes can show sooner; competitive terms take longer. We never promise rankings we cannot control.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation is the work of making your brand visible and cited in AI-generated answers from tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity.

Do you do SEO for Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. Ecommerce SEO covers category and product pages, site architecture, faceted navigation, structured data and speed for Shopify and WooCommerce stores.

Will I get reports?

Yes. Reporting is included in our retainers and focuses on the searches, pages and enquiries that matter commercially.

Can you help after a website redesign lost rankings?

Yes. We check redirects, indexation, internal links and content changes to find what was lost and fix it. Planning the migration properly in advance is even better.

How do I know if an SEO agency in the UK is any good?

Ask what they will do in the first month, how they decide priorities, how they earn links and what their reports show. A clear, prioritised plan and honest answers about timelines are better signs than guarantees or big traffic promises.

What do SEO services UK businesses need most often?

Usually a technical tidy-up, stronger service or category pages matched to real searches, better internal linking and a steady flow of useful content. Local businesses also need Google Business Profile work.

Do I need to change my website platform for SEO?

Rarely. Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress can all rank well. Most problems come from templates, content and structure rather than the platform itself.

Will you write content as part of SEO?

Yes. Our retainers include 4, 8 or 12 posts a month depending on the plan, and drafts are sent for your approval before they are published.

Do I keep access to my Search Console and analytics?

Yes. Accounts stay in your name and we work with granted access, so your data and history remain yours if you ever move on.

Can SEO and paid ads run at the same time?

Yes, and they often help each other. Paid search data shows which terms convert, which helps SEO priorities, while SEO builds traffic that does not stop when ad spend does. Ads management is included in our Scale retainer.
