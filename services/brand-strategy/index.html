Brand strategy

What brand strategy covers

01

Positioning

Who you serve, the problem you solve and why customers should choose you over the alternatives.

02

Messaging

Value proposition, key messages and proof points written for your buyers, ready to use on your website and in ads.

03

Tone of voice

How your brand sounds, with simple guidelines and examples so everyone writes consistently.

04

Visual identity

Logo, colour, typography and brand assets that work on screen, in social feeds and in print.

05

Brand guidelines

A practical guide your team and suppliers can follow, not a document that sits in a drawer.

06

Launch and rebrand

Rolling a new or refreshed brand out across your website, profiles and marketing without losing search visibility.

How it works

Our brand process

1. Discovery

Conversations with you and a look at customers, competitors and how people currently describe your business.

2. Strategy

Positioning and messaging options, discussed and refined with you until they feel right and true.

3. Identity

Visual concepts developed from the strategy, then applied to real examples such as web pages, ads and social posts.

4. Roll-out

Guidelines, files and support to apply the brand across your website and marketing.

Why it matters

Brand and performance are not opposites

Strong positioning improves performance marketing: ads get clearer, landing pages convert better and content has a consistent point of view. It also helps with search and AI visibility, because a brand described the same way everywhere is easier for people and machines to understand and remember.

Brand work pairs naturally with website design and content. If you are choosing a partner for this, our guide on how to choose a digital marketing agency in the UK may help.

Pricing

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need brand strategy before a new website?

It helps. Even a short positioning and messaging exercise makes the website clearer and faster to build, because the words and priorities are agreed first.

Do you design logos?

Yes. Visual identity, including logo, colours and typography, is part of brand strategy, developed from the positioning rather than in isolation.

Will a rebrand hurt my SEO?

It can if domains, URLs or page content change without a plan. We map redirects and keep key content so search visibility carries over.

How is brand strategy priced?

As a scoped project. We agree deliverables and revisions up front so there are no surprises.

Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. Many businesses need a sharper message rather than a new logo, and we will tell you honestly which you need.
