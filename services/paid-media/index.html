How it works

Tracking first, then scale

Paid campaigns only improve when the data is right. That is why we add paid campaigns once conversion tracking works.

1. Tracking and audit

We check GA4, conversion tags, Merchant Center and offline conversions, and fix gaps before any budget is increased.

2. Strategy and structure

Campaigns are structured around your margins, products or services and buyer intent, with clear budgets and targets.

3. Creative and landing pages

Ads and landing pages are built around a single goal. We A/B test messages and designs to improve conversion rates.

4. Optimise and report

Search terms, audiences, bids and creative are reviewed continuously, and reporting shows cost per lead or return on ad spend in plain English.

Good fit

Who paid media suits

Works well when

  • You have a clear offer and a working website or store
  • Conversion tracking is in place (or you will let us set it up)
  • You can fund a test budget long enough to learn
  • You want results sooner than SEO alone can deliver

Fix these first

  • A slow or confusing website that does not convert
  • No way to measure leads or sales
  • Unclear margins or pricing
  • Expecting guaranteed results in the first week

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Advertising spend is paid separately by the client, directly to the ad platform. Our fee covers strategy, set-up and management.

Which platform should I start with?

For most businesses with existing demand, Google Search or Google Shopping comes first because people are already looking. Meta and TikTok suit products that sell visually; LinkedIn suits B2B.

How quickly will paid ads work?

Ads can bring traffic from day one, but campaigns need a learning period. Expect the first weeks to be about testing, then steady improvement as data builds.

Can you take over my existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We start with an audit of tracking, structure and search terms, then fix wasted spend before making bigger changes. You keep ownership of the account.

Do you build landing pages for ads?

Yes. Landing page design is part of our website service, and pages are built around a single goal and tested to improve conversion rates.
