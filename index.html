FAQ

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

What services does your agency provide?

Prism Works Studio offers a full suite of digital marketing and ecommerce services including ecommerce website design and development, SEO, paid media management (PPC and Google Shopping), digital PR, content marketing, and brand strategy. Whether you need a single service or a fully integrated ecommerce growth strategy, we tailor our approach to your goals.

Can I update the website content myself?

Yes. We build ecommerce stores and websites on user-friendly platforms like Shopify and WooCommerce, giving you full control to manage products, update content, and run promotions without any coding knowledge. We also provide training so you’re confident managing your store from day one.

How does the web design process work?

Our process begins with a discovery call to understand your brand, ecommerce goals, and audience. From there, we move through research, wireframing, design, development, and testing — keeping you involved at every stage. Most ecommerce projects are completed within 5–8 weeks depending on scope.

How long will my project take to complete?

A standard website typically takes 3–5 weeks, while ecommerce builds range from 5–12 weeks depending on catalogue size and custom functionality. SEO and paid media campaigns typically begin showing meaningful results within 3–6 months of launch.

Do you offer website maintenance and support?

Absolutely. We offer ongoing maintenance packages for both websites and ecommerce stores, covering platform updates, security monitoring, performance optimisation, and priority support. We keep your store fast, secure, and converting.

Can you redesign my existing website or ecommerce store?

Yes — ecommerce redesigns are one of our specialities. We audit your existing store for performance, UX, conversion rate, and SEO issues, then build a new version that significantly improves speed, design, and sales performance.
