Restaurant marketing in Dubai means getting your restaurant, cafe or food concept found by the right diners, convincing them to choose you and turning that interest into bookings, walk-ins and orders. In one of the world's most competitive dining cities, that depends on a fast website with a clear menu, strong visibility on Google Maps, social video that shows the food and atmosphere, and paid advertising aimed at the right neighbourhoods and moments.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We support restaurants in Dubai remotely, through website builds, local SEO and our paid media service. We have no office in the UAE and work through video calls, shared documents and your own accounts. We write in English, and Arabic content is prepared with your team or a professional translator. This guide explains how we approach restaurant marketing in Dubai, what it costs and where to focus first.

The Dubai dining market in brief

Dubai's restaurant scene ranges from neighbourhood cafes and casual chains to destination fine dining, beach clubs, brunch venues and delivery-led kitchens. Diners include residents from many nationalities, tourists, business visitors and families, and they discover restaurants in different ways. Residents often search on Google Maps or ask friends, tourists browse social media and review sites before they arrive, and many people order through delivery apps such as Talabat.

That variety means there is no single channel that works for everyone. A brunch venue in a hotel, a shawarma spot in Deira and a specialty coffee shop in a creative district need very different marketing mixes. The common thread is that diners make decisions quickly, usually on a phone, and they judge you by what they can see in a few seconds.

A website that works on a phone in seconds

Most diners look at a restaurant website on a phone, often while deciding where to eat that evening. They want the menu, prices, location, opening hours and a way to book, and they want them immediately. Slow pages, PDF menus that are hard to read on mobile and hidden booking buttons lose customers to the next result.

We build restaurant websites around those needs:

  • Menus as web pages, not only PDFs, so they load fast, read clearly and can be found by search engines.
  • Booking and order buttons visible on every page, linking to your reservation system or ordering platform.
  • Branch pages for each location with maps, parking notes, hours and contact details.
  • Event and occasion pages for brunches, private dining, celebrations and seasonal menus.
  • Arabic and English versions where your audience needs both, with proper right-to-left layouts.

Our guide to restaurant website design explains these principles in detail, and our article on web design in Dubai covers our approach to bilingual sites for the city. A small restaurant website typically costs £1,000 to £2,000 and takes three to five weeks.

Google Maps and local SEO

For many restaurants, Google Maps is the most valuable free marketing channel. When someone searches for a cuisine near them, the businesses shown on the map get most of the attention. We help Dubai restaurants improve their visibility through:

  • Complete, accurate Google Business Profile details for each branch, including categories, hours, menu links and attributes.
  • Regular, high-quality photos of dishes, interiors and the outside of the venue.
  • A simple process for asking happy guests for reviews and replying to every review politely.
  • Website pages that match what people search for, such as cuisine, area and occasion.

Search visibility builds over time. SEO work usually needs three to six months before results become clear. Our guide to the SEO agency service for Dubai explains our wider search approach in the city.

Social video and paid ads

Food is visual, and short video does much of the selling. A sizzling grill, a dessert being plated or a busy terrace at sunset tells diners more than any description. We help you plan content that can be used both organically and in paid campaigns.

For paid social, we usually start with tight location targeting around your venue, then test different angles such as signature dishes, offers, events and atmosphere. Our guides to the Meta ads agency service for the UAE and the TikTok ads agency service for the UAE explain how we run those platforms in the Emirates. We do not film content ourselves, so videos come from your team, a content producer or creators you work with.

Search advertising captures people who already know what they want. Campaigns for your brand name, your cuisine in your area and occasions like birthday dinners or business lunches can drive bookings at a sensible cost if tracking is in place. Our guide to Google Ads in Dubai covers the details.

Arabic and English content

Dubai's diners are highly international, and English is widely used, but Arabic content still matters for many audiences and shows respect for local customers. Menus, key pages, Google Business Profile descriptions and selected adverts often benefit from Arabic versions. Because we write in English, Arabic content is written with your team or a translator, and we make sure both versions stay consistent across your website, profiles and ads.

Planning around Dubai's calendar

Restaurant demand in Dubai changes through the year. The cooler months bring outdoor dining, tourists and events, while summer changes habits and many residents travel. Ramadan brings iftar and suhoor menus and very different opening patterns, and Eid, New Year, Valentine's Day and major city events all create spikes in demand.

We plan content, offers and ad budgets ahead of these moments, so pages and campaigns are ready before people start searching. A simple marketing calendar agreed at the start of each quarter makes this much easier to manage.

Measuring bookings and orders

Restaurant marketing is only useful if you can see what it brings in. We set up tracking for the actions that matter: booking clicks, completed reservations where your system allows, calls from your website and Google profile, direction requests, WhatsApp chats and online orders. That lets you compare channels fairly and put budget where it works.

Some results will always be harder to measure, such as walk-ins prompted by a social video. We are honest about those limits and look at trends over time rather than claiming credit for every busy night.

Advertising rules and licensing

Restaurant advertising in Dubai must follow local law, platform policies and cultural expectations. Some topics, including alcohol and certain promotions, have specific rules about where and how they can be advertised. We keep our guidance general and recommend checking official guidance or speaking with your own adviser before running campaigns. Responsibility for licensing and compliance of your adverts rests with you.

FAQs

What does restaurant marketing in Dubai involve?

Restaurant marketing in Dubai usually covers a fast menu website, Google Maps and local SEO, social video, paid ads and tracking of bookings and orders. We provide this remotely from the UK, with no office in Dubai.

Do restaurants in Dubai need a website if they are on delivery apps?

Yes. Delivery apps help with orders, but your own website gives you control over your menu, brand, bookings and search visibility, and it supports your Google profile and adverts.

Which social platform is best for Dubai restaurants?

Instagram remains central for most venues, while TikTok suits trend-led concepts and younger diners. Many restaurants use both, with content planned to work across platforms.

Can you create Arabic content for our restaurant?

We write in English. Arabic menus, pages and adverts are written with your team or a translator, and we handle the layout, structure and consistency across channels.

How much should a Dubai restaurant spend on ads?

It depends on your location, concept and goals. We agree a test budget before launch and adjust it as results come in. Dirham figures are quoted on request.

How long does restaurant marketing take to work?

Some paid campaigns bring bookings within weeks, but SEO and steady paid performance typically take three to six months to build and refine.
