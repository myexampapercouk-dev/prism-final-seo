SEO Agency for Berlin Businesses: English and German Search Explained

Looking for an SEO agency in Berlin? How SEO works for German and English searches, what a remote UK studio can handle, what to ask and how pricing works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • SEO for a Berlin business often means planning for two audiences: German-speaking customers at home and English-speaking buyers across Europe and beyond.
  • A remote, UK-based studio can lead technical SEO, English-language content and reporting for German companies, working with your team or a translator on German pages.
  • SEO results typically take three to six months to build, and retainers are priced in pounds, with other currencies such as euro quoted on request.

An SEO agency for a Berlin business should help you rank for the searches your customers actually make, whether they search in German, in English or in both, and turn that visibility into qualified leads or sales. Good SEO support covers technical health, language and country setup, keyword research, strong pages, earned links and clear reporting tied to business results.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely with companies in Germany and across Europe. We have no office in Berlin and will not suggest otherwise. Our SEO and generative engine optimisation work is delivered online, one hour behind Berlin time, with regular calls and shared documents. We are strongest on English-language SEO and the technical foundations that support every language version of a site. This guide explains what to expect, what to ask and how the work is usually priced.

Why Berlin businesses often need SEO in two languages

Berlin is home to many start-ups, software companies, agencies and international teams. Plenty of them sell well beyond Germany, and English is often their working language. At the same time, most German consumers and many German business buyers search in German. That creates a choice every company has to make deliberately.

  • German-first businesses. Local services, retailers and firms selling mainly to German customers need strong German-language pages, written or reviewed by native speakers.
  • English-first businesses. Software, technology and B2B companies selling internationally often lead with English, sometimes adding German pages for key products or for buyers who prefer it.
  • Both at once. Many companies need both, with a clear structure so search engines show the German page to German searchers and the English page to everyone else.

Getting this wrong leads to familiar problems: the English page ranking in Germany when the German one should, automatic translations that read poorly, or two versions competing for the same searches. Our international SEO guide covers the main structural options.

Keyword research in German and English

Translating an English keyword list into German rarely works. German searchers often use compound nouns, different word order or English terms that have become standard in their industry. In technology and marketing, for example, many German buyers search using English product terms alongside German words.

Good research looks at each language separately, using data set to the right country, and then maps terms to pages. The method in our keyword research guide applies directly; the difference is running it twice, once per language and market. For German-language research and copy, we work alongside your in-house team or a native-speaking writer or translator, so the German pages read naturally.

SEO for Berlin start-ups and software companies

Many Berlin companies sell software or digital services internationally, and their SEO priorities look different from a local business. Product and feature pages, comparison pages, integration pages and helpful guides tend to do the heavy lifting, alongside a fast, well-structured site that search engines and AI assistants can understand.

Our SaaS sector page explains how we approach marketing for software companies, and our guide to choosing a SaaS SEO agency covers the questions to ask. Structured data can also help search engines interpret products, organisations and FAQs; our schema markup guide sets out the basics.

Privacy, legal pages and compliance

Germany is known for taking data protection seriously, and SEO work touches analytics, tracking and site content. Germany applies the EU General Data Protection Regulation alongside its own national rules on cookies and telecoms privacy, and non-essential tracking generally needs consent. German websites are also commonly expected to publish a legal notice page with company details.

We set up analytics and consent tools in line with common practice and make sure legal pages are easy to find, but we are not lawyers and do not write legal text. Rules and guidance change, so check current official guidance or take legal advice for your business.

How a remote SEO agency works with Berlin companies

SEO happens on your website, in search and analytics tools and in shared documents, so distance makes little practical difference. A clear process does.

  1. Kick-off call. We agree goals, markets, languages, priority products or services and how success is measured.
  2. Access. You grant access to Search Console, analytics and the website through your own accounts, so you keep ownership.
  3. Audit and roadmap. We audit technical health and content across language versions, then build a prioritised three-month plan.
  4. Monthly delivery. Technical fixes, page improvements and new content, with drafts shared for approval and German copy coordinated with your writer or translator.
  5. Reporting. A monthly report and call. Our guide to SEO reporting shows what a useful report includes.

Berlin is one hour ahead of the UK, so calls fit comfortably within both working days.

Questions to ask any SEO agency in Berlin

  • Which languages will you work in directly, and who writes or reviews German content?
  • How will you set up language and country versions so they do not compete?
  • What will you do in the first three months, and in what order?
  • How will reporting connect rankings and traffic to leads or revenue?
  • Who owns the accounts, content and any links built?
  • How do you approach AI search features and answer engines?

Treat guaranteed rankings and link packages sold by volume with caution, wherever the agency is based.

FAQs

How do I choose an SEO agency in Berlin?

Look for a clear plan for each language you need, sound technical knowledge of multi-language sites, transparent reporting and fair contract terms. Ask who handles German copy. Whether the agency is in Berlin or works remotely matters less than how clearly it explains the work.

Do you have an office in Berlin?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK and works remotely with companies in Germany and across Europe, using video calls, shared documents and direct access to your website and analytics.

Do you write SEO content in German?

Our own content work is in English. For German pages, we work alongside your in-house team or a native-speaking writer or translator, providing the keyword research, page briefs and technical setup so the German content is built to rank.

Should my German website have an English version?

If you sell to international customers or recruit internationally, an English version is often worthwhile. It should be set up with proper language signals so search engines show each version to the right audience.

Do you quote in euro?

Our published prices are in pounds, and other currencies, including euro, can be quoted on request. Please check the VAT treatment of cross-border services with your tax adviser.

How long does SEO take to work in Germany?

Results typically take three to six months to build. Sites with several language versions or very competitive German search results can take longer, as each version needs time to earn visibility.
