Law Firm Marketing in the USA: Websites, SEO and Google Ads for Attorneys

Law firm marketing in the USA: attorney websites, local SEO, Google Ads and intake tracking, from a UK studio that works remotely and keeps bar rules in mind.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • Law firm marketing in the USA works when a clear website, practice-area SEO, a strong Google Business Profile and carefully tracked search ads all feed a fast intake process.
  • We are a UK-based studio that provides websites, SEO and paid media remotely for US law firms, with no office in the United States.
  • Retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, ad spend is paid to the platforms, US dollar pricing is quoted on request, and approval under your state bar's advertising rules rests with your firm.

Law firm marketing in the USA means getting your firm in front of people at the moment they need an attorney, then turning that first search into a consultation. The firms that do this well pair a clear, credible website with practice-area SEO, a strong local presence on Google, well-controlled search ads and an intake process that answers every call and form quickly.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with US businesses and have no office in the United States. This guide explains how we approach marketing for personal injury, family, criminal defense, immigration, estate planning, employment and business law firms across American cities and states.

Who this guide is for

We have written this for managing partners, marketing leads and solo attorneys who want more qualified enquiries, including:

  • Personal injury firms competing in some of the most expensive search markets online.
  • Family and divorce attorneys whose clients need reassurance as much as information.
  • Criminal defense lawyers who need to be reachable at short notice.
  • Immigration, estate planning and employment firms with steady, research-led demand.
  • Business and commercial firms that win work through expertise and referrals.

A solo practice in Columbus and a multi-office firm across California face different levels of competition, but the building blocks are the same.

A website that earns trust and enquiries

People looking for a lawyer are often stressed, short on time and unsure what to do next. Your website should tell them quickly whether you handle their type of matter, where you practise, how to reach you and what happens after they get in touch. Strong attorney sites lead with practice areas, explain the process in plain English, show real attorney profiles and offer several contact routes, such as a click-to-call button, a short form and clear office hours.

We build firm websites on WordPress so your team can publish updates and new practice pages easily. Each practice area deserves its own page, and larger areas, such as car accident claims within personal injury, often need several. Our guide to landing page design covers how we build focused pages for paid campaigns, and our article on conversion rate optimisation for law firms explains how small changes to forms and calls to action can lift enquiries.

SEO for law firms

Legal searches are highly competitive, especially for personal injury and criminal defense in large metros such as Los Angeles, Houston and New York. Winning organic visibility takes depth rather than volume. In practice that means:

  • Thorough practice-area pages that explain the process, timelines, common questions and what clients should do next.
  • Location pages for each office, with genuinely local details rather than a copied template.
  • Attorney profile pages that show experience and areas of focus, supporting the trust signals search engines look for.
  • Helpful articles that answer real client questions, reviewed by an attorney before publication.
  • Technical foundations such as fast loading, clean indexing and structured data.

We plan this around a content strategy so each page has a clear job. Our guides to content strategy and local SEO versus national SEO explain how we decide whether a firm should target one city, several metros or a whole state. Most firms see clear movement within three to six months, with competitive practice areas taking longer. Our SEO work also covers making your content easy for AI search tools to cite.

Google Business Profile and reviews

For many firms, the local map results bring a large share of calls. Your profile needs the right categories, an accurate address for each office, current photos, business hours and a description that matches your practice areas. Our guide to Google Business Profile setup covers the basics we check first.

Reviews from former clients carry weight, but state bar rules on testimonials and client communications vary. Ask in a consistent way without incentives, and keep replies short and general, never confirming details of a matter. Check your own state's rules before building a review process.

Google Ads for attorneys

Search ads give firms immediate visibility, but legal keywords are among the most expensive on the platform, so waste is costly. The settings we focus on include:

  • Tight keyword lists built around the matters you actually take.
  • Extensive negative keywords to filter out job seekers, free advice searches and practice areas you do not handle.
  • Location targeting that matches where you are licensed and willing to take cases.
  • Ad schedules that match when your intake team can answer the phone.
  • Call and form tracking so you can see which campaigns bring real consultations.

Our guides to negative keywords and Quality Score explain two of the biggest levers on cost per enquiry. Our paid media service page shows how we manage Google, Meta, YouTube and LinkedIn campaigns together.

Intake: where marketing succeeds or fails

A well-run campaign can still disappoint if calls go to voicemail or forms wait until the next day. Before scaling ad spend, we look at how enquiries are answered, how quickly forms get a reply, and whether your team records where each consultation came from. Simple tracking, such as unique call numbers for ads and source fields on forms, lets you judge marketing on signed matters rather than clicks.

Social ads, video and remarketing

Meta Ads and YouTube Ads can build awareness for consumer practice areas and remind past site visitors to get in touch. They work best with clear, calm messages and images or video your firm supplies. We do not film or edit video, so we plan campaigns around existing footage or footage you commission, and we handle the targeting, copy, budgets and reporting. For business and employment law, LinkedIn Ads can reach decision-makers by job title and industry.

Bar rules, privacy and compliance

Attorney advertising in the US is governed by each state's rules of professional conduct and bar guidance, which can cover disclaimers, testimonials, claims about results, specialist titles and contact with prospective clients. Privacy laws on tracking and data collection also vary by state. We keep this deliberately general: we are a marketing studio, not a law firm advising you. Please check your state bar's official guidance and your firm's own ethics counsel. Approval of all website copy, ads and review processes rests with your firm, and we are happy to build any required disclaimers into pages and ads. FAQs

What is included in law firm marketing in the USA?

It usually covers a firm website, practice-area SEO, Google Business Profile management, Google Ads and sometimes Meta, YouTube or LinkedIn Ads, plus tracking so you can see which channels bring consultations.

Do you have an office in the US?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely with US firms. We schedule calls to suit your time zone and share reports online.

How much does marketing for a law firm cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, and a small firm website is typically £1,000 to £2,000. Ad spend is paid separately. US dollar pricing is quoted on request.

Why are legal Google Ads clicks so expensive?

Many firms compete for a limited number of high-value searches. Tight keywords, strong negatives, good landing pages and accurate tracking help you avoid paying for clicks that will never become clients.

Can you make sure our marketing meets state bar rules?

We follow platform policies and include any disclaimers you need, but we do not give legal or ethics advice. Please check your state bar's guidance and your own counsel. Final approval rests with your firm.

How long does SEO take for a law firm?

Most firms see clear movement within three to six months. Highly competitive practice areas in large cities can take longer, which is why many firms run Google Ads while SEO builds.
