Why Google Ads suits Crawley businesses

Crawley has a business mix shaped by Gatwick Airport and the Manor Royal business district, one of the largest in the South East. Aviation, logistics, engineering, technology and professional services firms sit alongside retailers, trades and hospitality businesses serving the town and nearby areas such as Horsham, Horley, Redhill, East Grinstead and Three Bridges.

Many of these businesses compete with firms based in London and Brighton for the same searches. Google Ads lets a Crawley business appear at the top of results for chosen searches straight away, without waiting months for organic rankings. It suits:

  • Urgent services such as locksmiths, emergency plumbers and vehicle repairs, where buyers act within minutes.
  • Airport-related services such as parking, transfers, hotels and travel services, where searches peak around holidays.
  • B2B suppliers in Manor Royal who want to reach buyers searching for specific products or services.
  • New businesses that need enquiries before SEO has time to work.

Choosing the right campaign types

Google Ads offers several campaign types. For most Crawley businesses, Search campaigns are the place to start, because they show ads to people actively looking for a service. Other types can help depending on your goals.

  • Search for service businesses and B2B firms targeting specific queries.
  • Performance Max for businesses with strong conversion data that want to reach people across Google's networks. Our Performance Max guide explains when it works.
  • Shopping for online retailers selling physical products. See our Google Shopping ads guide.
  • YouTube for brand awareness and remarketing using video you already have.

Landing pages make or break PPC

The most common reason Google Ads fails for local businesses is not the campaign itself but where the clicks go. Sending every visitor to a generic homepage usually means fewer enquiries. A good landing page matches the search, explains the offer quickly, shows trust signals and makes it easy to call or fill in a short form.

We can build focused landing pages as part of a campaign setup, or rebuild a full site through our website development service. Our landing page design guide covers what to include.

Tracking that shows real results

Without tracking, you cannot tell which keywords bring enquiries and which just spend money. We set up conversion tracking for calls, forms and bookings, connect Google Ads with GA4 and make sure cookie consent is handled properly under UK GDPR and PECR. Our GA4 setup guide explains the basics. For specific compliance questions, check official ICO guidance or speak with your own adviser.

How to judge a Google Ads agency

  • Account ownership. You should own your Google Ads account and keep access if you change agency.
  • Clear fees. Management fees should be separate from ad spend and easy to understand.
  • Reporting on leads. Reports should show calls, forms and cost per lead, not just clicks.
  • Realistic expectations. Paid search improves over weeks of testing, and wider results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

Setting a sensible starting budget

One of the first questions Crawley owners ask is how much they should spend on ads. The honest answer depends on how much each click costs in your market, how many enquiries you need and how well your website converts. Competitive services, such as legal advice, airport parking or emergency trades, often have higher click costs than niche B2B products. We usually suggest starting with a budget large enough to gather useful data within a few weeks, then adjusting once we can see real cost per lead figures.

We also recommend starting narrow. A small number of well-chosen keywords, a tight location radius and a single focused landing page often outperform a broad campaign spread thinly across dozens of services. Once the core campaign is profitable, we expand into further services, nearby towns and additional campaign types.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

  • Broad match keywords with no negatives, which show ads for searches that have nothing to do with your business.
  • Location set to interest rather than presence, so ads reach people who are only curious about Crawley rather than people actually there.
  • No call tracking, which hides the enquiries many local businesses rely on most.
  • Automated suggestions accepted without review, which can raise spend without improving results.
  • Every ad pointing at the homepage, rather than a page that matches the search.

Fixing these basics often lowers the cost per lead before any new campaigns are launched. If you would like to compare our national approach, see our page on choosing a Google Ads agency in the UK.
