Google Ads Agency for Stockholm Businesses: Remote PPC Management

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Stockholm? Swedish and English campaigns, tracking, consent, what a remote UK studio manages and our fees in pounds.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Stockholm Google Ads accounts usually need separate Swedish and English campaigns, accurate conversion tracking with consent and landing pages in the searcher's language.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Sweden; we manage Google Ads remotely, write English ads and work with your team or a translator on Swedish copy.
  • Management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month with ad spend paid separately to Google, quoted in pounds with other currencies on request.

A Google Ads agency for a Stockholm business should turn your ad spend into enquiries and sales you can measure, with campaigns built around how Swedish and international buyers actually search. That means a sensible account structure, accurate conversion tracking, ads and landing pages in the right language and regular, honest reporting.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for businesses across Europe. We have no office in Stockholm or anywhere in Sweden. Our paid media service runs by video call and shared reports, one hour behind Stockholm time. We write English ads in-house and work with your team or a translator on Swedish copy.

What makes Google Ads in Stockholm different

Stockholm has a large technology and software sector, a strong start-up community, design and consumer brands, financial and professional services, and many businesses that sell beyond Sweden from an early stage. Several features shape how a Google Ads account should be run:

  • Swedish and English searches. English is widely used, but many buyers still search in Swedish, and the two groups behave differently.
  • International ambitions. Many Stockholm companies advertise across the Nordic countries, the rest of Europe or further afield.
  • Software and B2B buyers. Longer sales cycles mean tracking needs to capture trials, demos and qualified leads, not just clicks.
  • High expectations of privacy. Consent and data handling need to be set up carefully, which affects how much conversion data you see.

If you advertise in several countries, our guide to international PPC explains how to structure accounts by market and language.

Swedish and English campaigns

Mixing languages in one campaign makes it hard to control spend and judge results. We usually separate campaigns by language and by country, so you can see what each audience costs and returns. Google Ads targets by the languages people use, not by the language of their search alone, so language settings, keywords and ads all need to line up.

Prism writes English ads in-house. For Swedish ads and landing pages, we brief your team or a native-speaking writer or translator with the keywords, messages and character limits, then build and check the ads in the account. Our guide to responsive search ads explains how we write and test headlines and descriptions.

Conversion tracking and consent

Google Ads only improves when it knows which clicks lead to real enquiries or sales. We set up conversion tracking for the actions that matter, such as form submissions, calls, demo bookings, trials and purchases. Where it suits the business, we also connect offline results, such as qualified leads or closed deals, so bidding learns from quality rather than volume.

In Sweden and the rest of the EU, analytics and advertising cookies normally need consent. That affects how much data reaches Google Ads, so consent mode and the consent banner need to be set up properly. Our conversion tracking setup guide and our guide to enhanced conversions cover the details. We keep legal advice general: check current official guidance and confirm your setup with your legal adviser.

Campaign types we use

  • Search campaigns for people actively looking for your product or service.
  • Performance Max for online shops and businesses with good conversion data, set up with clear asset groups and exclusions.
  • Shopping campaigns for ecommerce stores with a well-maintained product feed.
  • Remarketing to bring back visitors who did not convert first time.
  • YouTube campaigns where video suits the product and the budget allows.

We choose campaign types based on your goals and data, not on what is newest. Our bidding strategies guide explains how we decide between manual and automated bidding.

Google Ads for software and B2B companies

Stockholm has many software and B2B companies whose buyers research for weeks before signing up. For these accounts, we focus on:

  • Keywords that show buying intent, such as comparisons, alternatives and pricing searches.
  • Landing pages built for one offer, such as a demo or a free trial, rather than the home page.
  • Tracking that separates sign-ups from qualified leads, so spend follows quality.
  • Negative keywords to filter out job seekers, students and free-tool searches.

Our guide to PPC for SaaS companies goes into more detail on trials, demos and longer sales cycles.

Common problems in inherited accounts

When we review an existing account, we often find the same issues: broad keywords spending on irrelevant searches, conversion actions that count page views as leads, Swedish and English keywords in the same ad group, and budgets spread too thinly across too many campaigns. Fixing tracking and structure first usually makes every later change more effective.

Other frequent issues include search partner and display placements switched on without anyone noticing, location settings that show ads to people merely interested in Sweden rather than located there, and ads still pointing to old landing pages. None of these need a bigger budget to fix. They need a careful review, clear priorities and a record of every change so you can see what made a difference. Our PPC audit checklist sets out what we look at first.
