SEO Agency for Stockholm Businesses: Swedish and English Search

Looking for an SEO agency in Stockholm? How SEO works across Swedish and English search, what a remote UK studio can handle, what to ask and how pricing works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Stockholm companies often need SEO for two audiences: Swedish-speaking customers at home and English-speaking buyers across Europe and further afield.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Sweden; we lead technical SEO, English content and reporting remotely, working with your team or a translator on Swedish pages.
  • SEO results typically build over three to six months, and retainers are priced in pounds, with Swedish krona, euro and other currencies quoted on request.

An SEO agency for a Stockholm business should help you rank for the searches your buyers really make, in Swedish, in English or in both, and turn that visibility into enquiries, trials or sales. Good SEO covers technical health, language setup, keyword research, strong pages, earned links and reporting tied to business outcomes.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio working remotely with companies in Sweden and across Europe. We have no office in Stockholm. Our SEO and generative engine optimisation service is delivered online, one hour behind Stockholm time, with regular calls, shared documents and clear monthly reporting. We are strongest on English-language SEO and the technical foundations that support every language version of a site.

Why Stockholm businesses often need SEO in two languages

Stockholm is known for its technology, design, gaming and consumer brands, and many of them sell internationally from an early stage. English is common in business and widely understood, yet Swedish customers frequently search in Swedish, especially for local services, retail and anything that feels personal or high-trust.

  • Swedish-first businesses. Local services and retailers selling mainly within Sweden need strong Swedish pages written or reviewed by native speakers.
  • English-first businesses. Software, B2B and export-led companies often lead with English to reach buyers in the UK, Europe and North America.
  • Both. Many companies need both, with a structure that helps search engines show the Swedish page to Swedish searchers and the English page to everyone else.

Mistakes here are common: English pages ranking in Sweden when the Swedish ones should, machine translations that read awkwardly, or two versions competing for the same terms. Our international SEO guide explains the main structural choices.

Keyword research in Swedish and English

Translating an English keyword list into Swedish rarely works. Swedish searchers often combine words into compounds, use different phrasing, or search with English product terms that have become standard in their sector. The only reliable approach is to research each language separately, with data set to the right country, and then map terms to the right pages.

The method in our keyword research guide applies directly; you simply run it once per language and market. Prism writes in English. For Swedish research checks and copy, we work alongside your in-house team or a native-speaking writer or translator, so Swedish pages read naturally and match how people actually search.

SEO for Stockholm start-ups and software companies

Many Stockholm companies sell software or digital services to international buyers, and their SEO priorities differ from a local business. Feature pages, use-case pages, comparison pages, integration pages and practical guides tend to do the heavy lifting, alongside a clean technical setup for any product documentation or help centre.

For software firms, we focus on searches that show buying intent, such as comparisons and problem-led queries, rather than chasing broad terms with little commercial value. Our SaaS sector page explains how we approach software marketing more widely.

Search beyond Google: AI answers and overviews

Buyers increasingly use AI assistants and AI-generated search summaries to shortlist suppliers. Content that answers questions clearly, cites facts plainly and is well structured tends to be easier for these systems to understand and reference. That does not replace classic SEO; it builds on it.

Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO explains what changes and what stays the same. In practice, the same work that helps you rank, such as clear pages, strong structure and genuine expertise, also helps you appear in AI-generated answers.

How remote SEO works with a Swedish team

Remote SEO works best with clear ownership. We agree at the start who approves content, who publishes changes, who handles Swedish copy and how often we meet. A typical rhythm looks like this:

  1. Onboarding. Access to Search Console, analytics and the website, plus a call about goals and markets.
  2. Audit and plan. A technical audit and keyword research, turned into a prioritised plan.
  3. Monthly delivery. Technical fixes, page improvements, new content and link-earning activity.
  4. Reporting. A monthly report and call covering what was done, what moved and what comes next.

Questions to ask before hiring an SEO agency for Stockholm

Whoever you speak to, local or remote, these questions help separate a careful partner from one making easy promises:

  • How will you structure Swedish and English pages, and how will search engines know which to show?
  • Who writes or reviews the Swedish content, and how is quality checked before publishing?
  • Which searches will you prioritise first, and why do they matter commercially?
  • What will you deliver each month, and what do you need from our team?
  • How will you report progress, and which numbers will you focus on?
  • What happens to our accounts, content and data if we stop working together?

A good answer is specific and written down. Be cautious of anyone who promises first-page rankings by a set date or who cannot explain their plan in plain language. Clear scope, honest timelines and steady delivery matter far more than an office address.

Are you an SEO agency in Stockholm?

We are a UK-based studio that works remotely with Stockholm businesses. We have no office in Sweden. Work is delivered through video calls, shared documents and monthly reporting, one hour behind Stockholm time.

Can you do SEO in Swedish?

We handle the technical setup and structure for Swedish pages and lead English-language content ourselves. Swedish copy is written or reviewed by your own team or a native-speaking translator or writer, so it reads naturally.

How much does SEO cost for a Stockholm business?

Our retainers run from £350 to £1,500 per month depending on scope, priced in pounds with other currencies quoted on request. Translation, tools and any paid placements are usually separate costs.

How long does SEO take to work in Sweden?

Results typically build over three to six months. Timing depends on your starting point, competition, technical health and how quickly content in each language can be published.

If you sell to Swedish customers and international buyers, usually yes. Each version should be written for its audience and correctly signalled to search engines. If you mainly sell abroad, English may be enough at first.

No. No honest agency can guarantee rankings, because search engines control them. We commit to clear plans, steady delivery and transparent reporting, and we explain what we expect and why.
