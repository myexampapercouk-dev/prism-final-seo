Beauty Salon Marketing in Doha: How Salons Win More Bookings

What makes the Doha salon market different

Doha has a large and varied population of Qatari families and residents from across the world, and beauty and grooming matter to many of them. Salons range from small neighbourhood spaces to luxury salons in hotels and malls, with many offering ladies-only services, home visits, bridal packages and treatments for special occasions and Eid.

Clients expect convenience. Many book by WhatsApp or through a booking app, compare salons on Instagram, and read Google reviews before choosing. Areas such as The Pearl, West Bay, Lusail, Al Waab and Al Sadd each have their own mix of salons and clients. A salon that is easy to find, shows its work well and replies quickly tends to win the booking.

Start with Google Business Profile and reviews

For a salon, the Google Business Profile is often the first thing a new client sees. Choose the right primary category, list every service, add opening hours and holiday hours, upload real photos of the salon and your work, and post updates about offers and seasonal treatments. If you serve women only, say so clearly.

Reviews are a big part of how people choose. Ask happy clients to leave one, reply to every review politely, and handle complaints calmly. A steady flow of recent reviews tends to help both trust and visibility. Our Google Business Profile guide covers setup in detail, and the tool works the same way in Qatar.

A website that turns visits into bookings

A salon website does not need to be large, but it must be clear. Each main treatment should have its own page with a description, duration, price or starting price, aftercare notes and a book button. A gallery of your work, a short page about your team and the salon, and clear location details with parking notes help new clients feel confident.

Online booking reduces missed enquiries, especially outside opening hours. We can connect a booking system you already use or recommend one that suits your salon, and add a WhatsApp button for people who prefer to message. If your clients use both Arabic and English, our bilingual Arabic and English website guide explains how to set it up. For more ideas, see our beauty salon marketing UK guide, and our web design Qatar post for country-specific points.

Local SEO for Doha salons

People search for treatments with a place attached: "lash extensions Doha", "hair salon The Pearl", "bridal makeup Doha" or "nail salon near me". Pages built around those searches give Google a clear reason to show your salon.

Helpful pages for most salons include:

  • One page per main treatment, such as hair colour, keratin, lashes, brows, nails, facials and makeup.
  • Bridal and occasion pages, including packages for weddings and Eid.
  • Home service pages if you offer visits, with the areas you cover.
  • Price list pages that are kept up to date.
  • Short guides that answer common questions about treatments and aftercare.

Clear answers on these pages also help you appear in AI search summaries. Our SEO agency Qatar post covers search in the country more broadly, and our SEO and GEO service explains how we work.

Paid ads that fill quiet days

Google Ads helps when you want bookings quickly, for example to fill quiet weekdays or launch a new treatment. Campaigns target treatment searches near your salon and land on the matching treatment page. Call and booking tracking show which searches lead to appointments. Our Google Ads agency Qatar post covers account setup.

Instagram and Facebook Ads suit salons because the work is visual. Before and after images, short clips and offers can reach people living or working near the salon. Retargeting reminds people who visited your website but did not book. Our Meta Ads agency Qatar post explains targeting in more detail.

TikTok Ads work well for trend-led treatments and younger clients. We plan the campaign and write the brief, and your team or a local creator films the clips. Our paid media service page explains how we manage ads across platforms. Treatment claims should be accurate and fair, and some treatments may be regulated, so check current Qatari guidance and your own adviser. Approval of any health or treatment claims rests with you.

Keep clients coming back

Winning a new client is only half the job. Simple follow-ups help them return: a thank-you message, a reminder when their next appointment is due, and offers for birthdays or special occasions. We set up the tracking and messaging plan, and your team or booking system sends the messages.

Plan around the salon calendar

Demand for beauty services in Doha rises and falls through the year, and the best marketing plans follow that pattern rather than running the same message every week. Planning ahead lets you prepare offers, pages and ads before the busy weeks arrive.

  • Ramadan and the run-up to Eid often bring a rush for hair, nails, henna, makeup and grooming appointments.
  • Wedding season brings demand for bridal packages, trials and group bookings for family members.
  • National Day and other public holidays can bring busy weeks for occasion styling.
  • The cooler months often bring more events, parties and evenings out.
  • The hottest summer weeks can be quieter as many residents travel, which is a good time for offers aimed at those who stay.

We map these dates with you, then plan the website updates, Google Business Profile posts and ad budgets for each one. Seasonal landing pages can be updated each year rather than rebuilt, so they keep the value they have earned in search. Quiet periods are a good time to test new treatments and offers with a small budget before the next busy season.
