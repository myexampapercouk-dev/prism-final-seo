YouTube Ads Agency in Vancouver: Video Ads That Drive Leads and Sales

A YouTube Ads agency in Vancouver plans formats, audiences, tracking and budgets so video campaigns win BC leads and sales, managed remotely by a UK studio.

A YouTube Ads agency in Vancouver plans and manages video campaigns on YouTube that reach the right people across Metro Vancouver and BC, then measures them against leads and sales rather than views alone. The work covers format choice, audience strategy, Google Ads conversion tracking, creative briefs, budget pacing and reporting.

We have no office in Vancouver or anywhere in Canada, and we work through video calls, shared documents and written reports. We do not film or edit video. Your team or a local production provider supplies the footage, and we handle strategy, campaign setup, targeting, tracking, optimisation and reporting. We write in English, so any French or other language copy comes from your team or a translator.

Why YouTube Ads matter for Vancouver businesses

Vancouver audiences watch a lot of YouTube, on phones during commutes, on laptops at work and on connected TVs at home. For many buying decisions, from a home renovation to a new software tool, people use YouTube to research before they act. That makes it useful at several stages: building awareness, keeping your brand in mind and bringing back people who have already visited your site.

Sectors that often benefit include real estate and property developers, home services and renovation firms, outdoor and lifestyle brands, tech and SaaS companies, education providers, health and wellness clinics, and ecommerce brands shipping across Canada. Choosing the right YouTube ad formats

YouTube offers several formats inside Google Ads, and each suits a different job. Picking the wrong one is one of the most common reasons campaigns underperform.

FormatHow it worksBest use in Vancouver
Skippable in-streamPlays before or during videos, skippable after a few secondsLead generation and site visits with a strong opening
Non-skippable in-streamShort ads viewers must watchBrand awareness for launches and events
Bumper adsVery short non-skippable adsReminders and frequency alongside longer ads
In-feed video adsAppear in search results and feeds as a thumbnailTutorials, demos and consideration content
Shorts adsVertical ads between ShortsYounger audiences and mobile-first products
Demand GenRuns across YouTube and other Google surfacesConversions and retargeting with mixed image and video

Most Vancouver accounts start with one conversion-focused campaign and one remarketing campaign, then add awareness formats when the budget allows.

Audiences: reaching the right people in Metro Vancouver

YouTube targeting is powerful when it is built on good signals. We usually combine several layers.

  • Location: Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, North Vancouver and other municipalities you serve, or the whole province for ecommerce.
  • Your data: website visitors, YouTube channel viewers and customer lists, used where consent allows.
  • Search intent: people who have searched on Google for terms related to your service.
  • In-market and life events: people Google sees as actively shopping in your category or going through a relevant change, such as moving home.
  • Placements and topics: specific channels or subject areas where your buyers spend time.

Vancouver is a diverse, multilingual city. YouTube can target by language, but the ad must match. If you want ads in Cantonese, Mandarin, Punjabi or another language, that copy and voiceover come from your team or a translator, and we set up the campaign structure to keep each language separate.

Creative briefs, not video production

Strong YouTube ads grab attention fast, show the brand early, make one clear point and end with a specific action. They do not need a big production budget. A well-lit phone recording, a screen recording of your product, or existing brand footage can perform well when it is cut for the format.

Because we do not film or edit video, our role is the brief. We write the hook, the structure, the key messages and the calls to action for each length and format, and suggest how to adapt one shoot into several versions. Your team or a local production provider films and edits. We then upload, label, test and track every version so you can see which one earns its place.

Tracking YouTube results properly

YouTube campaigns are managed in Google Ads, so they can use the same conversion tracking as search. We set up or audit conversion actions, link Google Analytics, and check that form fills, calls and purchases are recorded. We also look at view-through and engaged-view conversions, which show when someone watched your ad and later converted, while being clear about the limits of that data.

Budgets and pricing

YouTube has no fixed minimum spend, but very small budgets rarely give the algorithm enough data. We agree a test budget based on your goals, then shift money toward the formats, audiences and videos that perform. We do not promise a fixed cost per view or lead, because results depend on your offer and creative.

Our management fees are separate from ad spend, which you pay directly to Google. Retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Fees are listed in pounds on our pricing page, and Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request. Paid results typically build over three to six months.

Landing pages that match the ad

A viewer who clicks a YouTube ad expects the page to continue the story. If the ad promises a free consultation and the page buries the form, they leave. We review every landing page and recommend changes to speed, headline, proof and form length. Common YouTube Ads mistakes to avoid

When we audit Vancouver accounts, the same issues come up again and again.

  • Using a TV ad as a YouTube ad: slow openings lose viewers before the skip button appears.
  • Optimising for views when you want leads: the campaign goal shapes who Google shows your ad to.
  • No conversion tracking: without it, smart bidding has nothing to learn from.
  • Leaving placements unchecked: ads can appear on channels that do not suit your brand unless you review and exclude them.
  • One video for every format: vertical Shorts, short bumpers and longer in-stream ads each need their own cut.

Fixing these early often makes a bigger difference than raising the budget.

Privacy and advertising rules in BC

Canada has federal privacy and anti-spam laws, and British Columbia has its own private-sector privacy law. Remarketing lists, customer match uploads and follow-up emails all need proper consent. We set up tracking and audiences in line with common good practice, but we are not lawyers. Check official Canadian and BC guidance or ask your own adviser, as compliance rests with your business.

Regulated sectors such as health, finance and real estate have extra rules on claims and disclosures. In those cases we keep messaging general and your compliance team or broker approves every ad.

FAQs

What does a YouTube Ads agency in Vancouver do?

It plans, sets up and manages YouTube video campaigns for Vancouver businesses. That includes choosing formats, building audiences, writing creative briefs, setting up conversion tracking, managing budgets and reporting on leads and sales.

Do you make the YouTube videos?

No, we do not film or edit video. We write the briefs and scripts, and your team or a local production provider creates the footage, which we then upload, test and track.

Is Prism Works Studio based in Vancouver?

No, we are UK-based and work remotely with no office in Vancouver or Canada. We work through video calls, shared documents and written reports, with calls planned around the time difference.

How much should I spend on YouTube Ads in Vancouver?

There is no single right amount, because it depends on your goals and margins. We agree a test budget big enough for the algorithm to learn, and ad spend is paid directly to Google, separate from our retainer.

Can YouTube Ads generate leads, not just views?

Yes, YouTube Ads can drive leads when campaigns use conversion goals, good audiences and a strong landing page. Formats such as skippable in-stream and Demand Gen are built for actions, not just views.

How long do YouTube Ads take to work?

Early data appears within weeks, but paid results typically build over three to six months. That time allows testing of formats, audiences and creative before scaling.
