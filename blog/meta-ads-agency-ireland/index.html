Meta Ads Agency for Irish Businesses: Remote Facebook and Instagram Advertising

Looking for a Meta Ads agency in Ireland? How Facebook and Instagram campaigns are planned, tracked with consent and managed remotely, plus our fees in pounds.

Key takeaways

  • A Meta Ads agency for an Irish business should plan Facebook and Instagram campaigns around real audiences, strong creative and consent-based tracking, then manage budgets for profit rather than clicks.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Ireland; we manage Meta campaigns remotely on the same time zone and write ads in English, with Irish-language copy from your team or a translator.
  • Management runs on retainers of £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, quoted in pounds with euro quotes on request; ad spend is paid separately to Meta.

A Meta Ads agency for an Irish business should put your brand in front of the right people on Facebook and Instagram, at a cost that leaves you a profit. That means defining audiences, producing creative that stops the scroll, tracking results with consent in place and adjusting budgets every week based on what actually sells.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Meta advertising remotely for businesses in Ireland and across Europe. We have no office in Dublin, Cork, Galway or anywhere else in Ireland. Our paid media service runs by video call, shared documents and monthly reports, and because Ireland shares a time zone with the UK, our working day matches yours.

What Irish businesses need from Meta advertising

Facebook and Instagram are part of daily life for a large share of people in Ireland, which makes Meta one of the most useful paid channels for consumer brands, local services and online shops. It works differently from search advertising. People are not looking for you when they see your ad, so the creative and the offer have to do the work of grabbing attention and creating demand.

Irish businesses we speak to usually want Meta Ads for one of these jobs:

  • Online sales. Irish e-commerce brands selling to customers across the Republic, Northern Ireland, the UK and the wider EU.
  • Lead generation. Service firms, clinics, trades, education providers and property businesses collecting enquiries through forms or landing pages.
  • Local footfall. Restaurants, gyms, salons, shops and attractions reaching people within a set distance.
  • Brand awareness. New products, launches and events that need reach before search demand exists.

Audiences in a smaller market

Ireland has a far smaller population than the UK, Germany or France, and that changes how Meta campaigns should be built. Splitting a modest budget across many narrow audiences leaves each ad set with too little data to learn from, and the same people see your ads again and again. We usually start with broader targeting, let strong creative find the right buyers, and use remarketing for people who have already visited your site or engaged with your pages.

Location settings matter too. A Dublin restaurant may target a few kilometres around the venue, while an online shop might target the whole island of Ireland, then add the UK or other EU countries with their own campaigns. If you sell into several countries, our guide to running Meta Ads in multiple countries explains how we split budgets, currencies and creative.

Creative that works in Ireland

On Meta, creative is the main lever. Audiences can tell when an ad has been made for somewhere else, so we write in natural, local English and plan visuals that reflect your customers and setting. A typical testing plan includes:

  1. Short video. Product in use, behind the scenes or a quick explanation, cut for Reels and Stories in vertical format.
  2. Static images. Clear product shots or a simple offer, useful for fast tests.
  3. Carousels. Several products, steps or benefits in one ad.
  4. Customer-style content. Content filmed by your team or creators you work with, used with clear permission.

Prism writes ad copy in English in-house. If you want Irish-language versions, for example for a campaign aimed at Irish-speaking audiences, the wording should come from your team or a translator, and we will build the campaign around it. We also check every advert against Meta's advertising standards before it goes live.

Tracking, cookie consent and privacy

Meta campaigns only optimise well when the platform knows which ads lead to sales or enquiries. We set up the Meta pixel and, where it suits your site, the Conversions API, which sends events from your server as well as the browser. On Shopify and WordPress this is usually done through official integrations.

Irish websites fall under the GDPR for personal data, and the EU ePrivacy rules, as applied in Irish law, mean advertising cookies and pixels normally need the visitor's consent before they load. Ireland's Data Protection Commission publishes guidance on cookies and tracking that is worth reading. We connect tracking to your consent banner so tags fire only when they should, and our guide to cookie banner design covers the practical side. Decisions on legal wording and compliance should come from your own legal adviser, and official guidance should be checked before relying on any summary, including this one.

Landing pages and the European Accessibility Act

Ads can only be as good as the page they send people to. A slow page, an unclear offer or a hard-to-use form can waste a large share of your budget. Our guide to landing page design sets out what we look for.

The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer-facing products and services, including a wide range of online shops and booking services. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt, but building to WCAG AA, the level referenced by the harmonised standard EN 301 549, is good practice for any page that receives paid traffic. Clear contrast, readable text, labelled form fields and keyboard-friendly checkouts help every visitor, not only those using assistive technology. Whether the Act applies to your business is a question for your adviser.

Retargeting and the full funnel

Most people do not buy the first time they see an ad. Retargeting shows a follow-up message to people who visited your site, added to basket or watched a video, where their consent allows. Our remarketing guide explains how we set frequency and exclusions so returning visitors are reminded without being followed around the internet.

Meta often works best alongside search. People who see your Instagram ad may search for your brand name later, so Google Ads and Meta Ads support each other. Our post on Google Ads for Irish businesses covers the search side, and if you are weighing up other social channels, our comparison of TikTok Ads and Meta Ads sets out where each fits.

Fees, ad budgets and timelines

Our management fees are listed on our pricing page: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. The right level depends on the number of campaigns, markets and the volume of creative you need. Ad spend is paid separately, directly to Meta. FAQs

Do you have a Meta Ads agency office in Ireland?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Ireland. We work remotely by video call, shared documents and monthly reports, on the same time zone as Irish businesses.

How much does a Meta Ads agency in Ireland cost with your studio?

Our management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, quoted in pounds with euro quotes on request. Ad spend is paid separately to Meta, and creative production is quoted separately if needed.

Can you run Facebook and Instagram ads in Irish as well as English?

Yes. We write English ads in-house, and Irish-language copy comes from your team or a translator. We then build and manage the campaigns around that copy.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads need cookie consent in Ireland?

Advertising pixels and cookies normally need consent before they load under the EU ePrivacy rules as applied in Ireland. We connect tracking to your consent banner, and your legal adviser should approve the wording.

Can you target Northern Ireland and the Republic separately?

Yes. Meta lets us target by country, region, city or radius, so we can run separate campaigns where prices, currency, delivery or offers differ.

How long do Meta Ads take to work?

Results typically build over three to six months. Early weeks focus on tracking, creative tests and audiences, and performance improves as the account learns which ads lead to sales.
