Who we work with in Winnipeg

Winnipeg is a market of established neighbourhoods, newer suburban developments, character homes, condos near downtown and a steady flow of newcomers and first-time buyers. We work with:

  • Individual realtors and teams that want their own brand and lead flow alongside their brokerage.
  • Brokerages that need a site with agent profiles, office information and recruitment pages.
  • New-home builders and developers launching communities on the edges of the city and in nearby towns.
  • Property managers who want more landlord enquiries and better tenant applications.
  • Related businesses such as mortgage brokers, home inspectors, stagers and renovators.

A real estate website built for enquiries

Many realtor websites are templates that look the same as every other agent's. Sellers and buyers notice, and they have little reason to get in touch. We design real estate websites that:

  • Lead with a clear offer for sellers, such as a simple home valuation request.
  • Connect to a listings feed where your brokerage and board permit it.
  • Include neighbourhood pages with genuinely useful information about each area you serve.
  • Show your brokerage details and contact information clearly on every page.
  • Load quickly on phones, where most property browsing happens.
  • Make it easy to book a call or request a showing without hunting for a form.

Our estate agent website design guide covers the design principles in more detail, and our Winnipeg web design guide explains how we build for businesses in the city.

Local SEO for Winnipeg realtors

Property search is local. People search by neighbourhood, by home type and by life stage, such as bungalows in River Heights, condos in the Exchange District, family homes in Bridgwater or starter homes in Transcona. We help agents rank for those searches by:

  • Writing helpful neighbourhood pages with real detail, not copied boilerplate.
  • Optimising your Google Business Profile with accurate categories, service areas and photos.
  • Building a steady, honest review routine with past clients.
  • Fixing technical problems such as slow pages and duplicate listing URLs.
  • Publishing guides that answer the questions buyers and sellers actually ask.

Our SEO and GEO service explains how we approach search and AI answers. The Winnipeg SEO agency guide covers local search in the city, and our Google Business Profile guide explains profile basics that apply in Canada too.

Google Ads for sellers, buyers and builders

Search ads work when people are actively looking. We separate campaigns by goal so budgets are easy to control and results are easy to read:

  • Seller campaigns for searches about selling a home or getting a valuation in a specific area.
  • Buyer campaigns for searches about a property type or neighbourhood.
  • New-home campaigns for builders promoting a community, show home or release.
  • Brand campaigns so people searching your name find you first.

Every campaign sends people to a matching landing page and we track calls and form enquiries. Our guide to Google Ads in Winnipeg explains our setup in more depth.

Meta and Instagram ads for listings and launches

Facebook and Instagram are useful for putting homes, open houses and new communities in front of local audiences. Housing ads on Meta may need to run under its special ad category rules, which restrict some targeting options, so we plan campaigns around the rules that currently apply. We use Meta ads to:

  • Promote new listings and open houses using your photography.
  • Build interest in new communities ahead of a release.
  • Retarget people who viewed your valuation or listing pages.
  • Share market guides that bring future sellers into your pipeline.

Prism does not film or edit video. If your team or photographer supplies walkthrough clips, we can use them in campaigns. Our guide to Meta ads in Winnipeg has more detail.

Marketing for builders and new communities

Builders and developers have different needs from individual agents. A community website needs to explain location, lot types, floor plans, show home hours and timelines, and collect registrations well before releases. For builders we typically create a focused project site, run search and social campaigns to grow a registration list, and set up clear reporting on enquiries and show home visits. Our property developer marketing guide covers the approach in more detail.

Seasonality in the Winnipeg market

Winnipeg winters are long, and activity often picks up in spring and early summer. That does not mean marketing should stop in winter. Quieter months are a good time to build your website, publish neighbourhood guides and collect seller leads who plan to list in spring. We plan budgets around your own calendar, so ad spend rises when buyers and sellers are most active and content work carries on all year.

Advertising rules and privacy

Real estate is a regulated profession. At a general level, real estate advertising in Manitoba is subject to provincial rules, and your brokerage may have its own policies on how listings, names and claims appear. Commercial emails are covered by CASL, Canada's anti-spam law, and personal information is generally covered by PIPEDA.

We build clear disclaimers, consent forms and privacy page templates, but compliance approval rests with you and your brokerage. Please check guidance from the provincial regulator, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your own adviser before campaigns go live.

Common real estate marketing mistakes

  • Relying only on brokerage pages, with no website or brand of your own.
  • Neighbourhood pages that repeat the same generic text for every area.
  • Ads that send sellers to a home page instead of a valuation page.
  • Slow replies to enquiries, especially in the evenings and at weekends.
  • Collecting email addresses without clear consent.

Fixing these basics usually does more for enquiries than adding new channels. We can also set up automatic acknowledgement emails and notifications so every enquiry reaches you quickly.
