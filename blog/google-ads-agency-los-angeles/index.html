Google Ads Agency Los Angeles: How Remote PPC Management Works

Need a Google Ads agency in Los Angeles? Remote PPC can run search, Shopping and Performance Max for LA firms. Here is how it works and what it costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Google Ads agency for a Los Angeles business needs clean conversion tracking, tight location targeting and reporting tied to leads and sales.
  • LA's size and cost of clicks make account structure, negative keywords and landing pages more important than simply raising the budget.
  • We are a UK-based studio managing campaigns remotely for US clients, with management fees in pounds and ad spend paid separately to Google.

If you are looking for a Google Ads agency in Los Angeles, what you need is an account that turns clicks into calls, leads and sales at a cost you can live with. A remote agency can manage search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns for LA businesses just as well as a local one, because everything happens inside your Google Ads account, not in a meeting room in Santa Monica.

We are a UK-based studio that manages paid media remotely for US businesses. We have no Los Angeles office and no US office. This guide explains what Google Ads management for an LA business should cover, how to target a region as large as Los Angeles County, how ecommerce and lead generation accounts differ, how a remote arrangement runs and what it costs.

What a Google Ads agency in Los Angeles should cover

Los Angeles is a huge and varied market. Entertainment and media, fashion and apparel, beauty, food and drink, real estate, healthcare, legal services, home services and a large ecommerce scene all compete for attention, often on the same search results pages. A competent Google Ads agency should handle:

  • Conversion tracking: calls, forms, bookings and purchases tracked properly, ideally with enhanced conversions, before any money is spent on scaling.
  • Account structure: campaigns and ad groups organised around services, products and locations, so budgets go where they earn the most.
  • Keyword strategy: high-intent searches first, with a growing negative keyword list to cut waste.
  • Ads and assets: responsive search ads, sitelinks, callouts, images and call assets that match each search.
  • Bidding: the right bid strategy for your data volume and goals, reviewed as the account matures.
  • Landing pages: pages that match the ad and make it easy to act.
  • Reporting: plain-language reports on cost per lead, cost per sale and return on ad spend.

Tracking is the foundation. Our Google Ads conversion tracking guide explains the set-up we check first, and our guide to negative keywords shows how we stop budget leaking into irrelevant searches.

Location targeting across Los Angeles

Los Angeles County alone covers dozens of cities and neighbourhoods, and travel times shape who will actually become a customer. A plumber in the San Fernando Valley, a clinic in Pasadena and a restaurant group in Downtown LA each need very different targeting. Getting this right is one of the quickest ways to cut wasted spend.

  • Presence, not interest: for most local businesses, target people located in your area rather than people who have merely shown interest in it.
  • Radius or zip code targeting: match your real service area, and exclude places you cannot reach in reasonable time.
  • Location bid adjustments: spend more where leads convert best, once you have the data to show it.
  • Separate campaigns where needed: if Orange County, the Inland Empire or Ventura County behave differently, give them their own budgets.
  • Spanish-language searches: many LA customers search in Spanish. If you serve them, properly written Spanish ads and landing pages usually work better than relying on automatic translation.

Our Google Ads location targeting guide walks through these settings in detail.

Google Ads for LA ecommerce brands

Los Angeles has a large number of direct-to-consumer brands in fashion, beauty, wellness and lifestyle. For these businesses, Google Ads is usually a mix of Shopping, Performance Max and branded search, all driven by the product feed in Google Merchant Center.

  • Feed quality: accurate titles, images, prices, availability and product categories have a big effect on Shopping performance.
  • Campaign split: separating best sellers, new lines and low-margin products so each gets an appropriate target.
  • Brand protection: branded search campaigns so competitors and resellers do not take your own name searches.
  • Return on ad spend targets: set using your real margins, not a generic figure.

Our guides to Google Shopping for US stores and Performance Max explain how we structure ecommerce accounts.

Google Ads for LA lead generation businesses

Service businesses such as law firms, clinics, contractors, real estate teams and B2B firms usually need calls and form fills rather than online sales. Clicks in competitive LA categories can be expensive, so the focus is on quality rather than volume:

  • Track calls from ads and from the website, and count only calls long enough to be real enquiries.
  • Import qualified leads or closed deals back into Google Ads where your CRM allows, so bidding learns what a good lead looks like.
  • Use ad schedules that match when your team can answer the phone.
  • Send traffic to focused landing pages rather than a general homepage.

Regulated sectors such as legal, financial and health services have extra advertising rules from Google and from their own professional bodies. We give general guidance only, and approval of ad copy rests with your own compliance team or lawyer, who should check current official guidance.

How remote Google Ads management works

Los Angeles is usually eight hours behind the UK. Calls happen in your morning, which is our afternoon, and day-to-day optimisation continues while you are offline. A typical arrangement:

  • Access: you own the Google Ads account and give us manager access; billing stays on your card with Google.
  • Audit: a review of tracking, structure, search terms and landing pages, with a prioritised fix list.
  • Rebuild or refine: changes made in stages so performance can be compared fairly.
  • Ongoing management: regular search term reviews, bid and budget changes, ad testing and a monthly report.

Common Google Ads problems we see in competitive cities

When we audit accounts for businesses in large, competitive markets like Los Angeles, the same issues come up again and again. None of them are unusual, and most can be fixed within the first few weeks:

  • Broad match without guardrails: broad keywords running on small budgets with few negatives, so spend drifts into loosely related searches.
  • Tracking that counts the wrong things: page views or button clicks recorded as conversions, which teaches smart bidding to chase the wrong people.
  • One campaign for everything: every service and location sharing a single budget, so the most expensive searches take the lion's share.
  • Display and search partners left on by default: settings that suit some accounts but often add low-quality traffic to lead generation campaigns.
  • Homepage as the landing page: visitors arriving from a specific search then having to hunt for the relevant service.
  • No review of search terms: the search terms report left unchecked for months, so waste repeats every day.

Fixing these basics usually comes before any talk of raising budgets. Once the account spends efficiently, it becomes much clearer where extra budget would earn a return.

How much a Google Ads agency costs for an LA business

There are two costs to plan for: the management fee and the ad spend itself. Ad spend is paid separately, directly to Google, and is entirely under your control. Our management retainers are priced in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request:

  • Launch, £350 a month: a single, focused campaign set for a smaller business.
  • Growth, £750 a month: multiple campaigns, Shopping or Performance Max and regular testing.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: larger accounts, several locations or combined search and social work.

Paid campaigns can produce traffic quickly, but stable, profitable results typically take three to six months of data and refinement. Our pricing page sets out what each retainer includes, and our paid media service explains how we manage Google, Microsoft, Meta, TikTok and YouTube campaigns.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Los Angeles?

Check that they will audit your conversion tracking first, that you keep ownership of the account, and that reports focus on cost per lead or return on ad spend. Ask how they handle search terms, negatives and landing pages.

Do you have an office in Los Angeles?

No. We are a UK-based studio and work remotely with US businesses. We have no LA office or other US office, and we manage accounts through manager access, video calls and written reports.

Can you target specific parts of Los Angeles?

Yes. Google Ads lets you target by radius, zip code, city or county, and exclude areas you do not serve. We set targeting around where your real customers are and adjust it as the data builds.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Google. Prices are in pounds, and other currencies are quoted on request.

Do you manage Shopping and Performance Max campaigns?

Yes. For ecommerce brands we manage Merchant Center feeds, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, with targets based on your real margins rather than a generic figure.

How long before Google Ads works?

Traffic can start within days, but stable, profitable results typically take three to six months as the account gathers data and we refine keywords, bids and landing pages.
