Why Google Ads suits London, Ontario businesses

London is a mid-sized city with a wide mix of businesses: healthcare and research linked to the hospitals and Western University, education around Fanshawe College, manufacturing and logistics along the 401 and 402 corridors, and a large base of local trades, clinics, professional services and independent retailers. Neighbourhoods such as Richmond Row, Old East Village, Wortley Village, Masonville and the growing suburbs in the south and west each have their own customer base.

That mix makes search advertising useful. When someone types "furnace repair London Ontario" or "physiotherapy near Masonville", they are telling you exactly what they need and where. Google Ads lets you appear at that moment, pay only when someone clicks, and measure which searches turn into calls, bookings and sales.

London is also close enough to the Greater Toronto Area, Kitchener-Waterloo and the US border that some businesses serve a wider region. Good location targeting lets you decide precisely how far your ads should reach, so you are not paying for clicks from people you cannot serve.

Tracking comes before spending

The single most common problem we see in Google Ads accounts is poor tracking. If the account cannot tell a real enquiry from a page view, the bidding system optimises for the wrong thing and reports look better than reality. Before we scale any budget, we make sure the key actions on your website are tracked properly.

For most London service businesses, that means phone calls from ads, calls from the website, contact form submissions and online bookings. For online shops, it means purchases with values. We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics where it makes sense, and enhanced conversions where they are appropriate and allowed. Our conversion tracking setup guide explains the process in more detail.

Privacy matters too. Canada has its own privacy laws, and any tracking should be described accurately in your privacy notice and cookie banner. We keep this general and suggest you check official guidance or speak to your own adviser about what applies to your business.

Building campaigns around how London customers search

We start with your services, your margins and your capacity. A plumbing firm that can only take five new jobs a week needs a very different setup from a regional supplier with a warehouse full of stock. From there, we research the terms people use, group them into tightly themed ad groups and write ads that speak directly to each group.

Location targeting is especially important in and around London. Some businesses want to cover the city only; others want St. Thomas, Strathroy, Woodstock or the wider Middlesex County area. We set targeting to people in or regularly in your chosen locations, rather than people who have merely shown interest in them, unless there is a good reason to do otherwise. Our location targeting guide covers the options.

Negative keywords keep budgets clean. Searches for jobs, free courses, DIY guides or other cities called London can waste money quickly. That last point matters more than you might think: London, England, gets far more searches than London, Ontario, so careful targeting and negative keywords are essential. Our negative keywords guide shows how we approach it.

Google Ads agency London Ontario: how we work remotely

Working with a remote Google Ads agency in London, Ontario is straightforward when the process is clear. Here is how a typical engagement runs.

  1. Discovery call. We talk through your business, services, service area, goals and any past advertising.
  2. Access and audit. You give us access to your Google Ads, Analytics and Tag Manager accounts. If you do not have an account, we help you create one in your own name.
  3. Tracking setup. We fix or set up conversion tracking so we can measure real enquiries.
  4. Campaign plan. We share the proposed structure, keywords, budgets and ad copy for your approval.
  5. Launch. Campaigns go live with careful daily checks in the first weeks.
  6. Monthly cycle. We review search terms, adjust bids and budgets, test new ads and send a monthly report with a call.

Because we are in the UK, we have a few hours of overlap with Eastern time each working day. That is plenty for calls and quick questions, and we often make changes while you are still asleep, so they are live by the time your phones open.

Landing pages that convert local searchers

Even the best ads struggle if they send people to a slow, generic homepage. Each campaign should point to a page that matches the search: a furnace repair ad goes to a furnace repair page with your service area, clear pricing guidance if you share it, reviews and a prominent phone number.

If your current website is holding you back, we can advise on changes or build focused landing pages. Our landing page design guide explains what we look for, and our web design guide for London, Ontario covers full website projects.

How much does Google Ads cost in London, Ontario?

There are two separate costs: your ad spend, which you pay directly to Google, and our management fee. Ad spend depends on your sector, competition and goals. Competitive trades and professional services cost more per click than niche products. We recommend starting with a budget that can generate enough clicks to learn from, then adjusting based on results.

Search, Performance Max or something else?

For most local London businesses, standard search campaigns are the best starting point. They give the most control and the clearest link between a search and a result. Once tracking is solid and search campaigns are performing, we may test Performance Max for shops with a product feed, or YouTube and Display for remarketing.

SEO and Google Ads together

Google Ads delivers visibility quickly, but you pay for every click. SEO builds visibility that does not cost per click but takes longer, typically three to six months to show meaningful movement. Many London businesses use ads to generate enquiries now while their organic rankings grow.

Search term data from your ads is also valuable for SEO, because it shows exactly which phrases convert. Our SEO guide for London, Ontario explains how we approach organic search, and our wider paid media service covers Google Ads alongside Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube Ads.

Auditing an existing account

If you already run Google Ads, we start with an audit. We look at structure, match types, search terms, negatives, location settings, bidding strategy, conversion tracking, ad strength and landing pages. You get a clear list of issues ranked by impact. Our PPC audit checklist shows the areas we review.

What results to expect, and when

Google Ads can start sending clicks on the first day, but the first few weeks are a learning period. The account needs data to work out which searches, ads and times convert best. Most accounts settle into a steady rhythm after one to three months, with further improvement over three to six months as we refine targeting and creative.

We do not promise specific numbers. Results depend on your market, budget, offer, website and how quickly you follow up enquiries. What we do promise is transparency: you will always see what is being spent, on which searches, and what came back.
