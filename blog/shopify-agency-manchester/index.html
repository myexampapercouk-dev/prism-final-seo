Choosing a Shopify Agency in Manchester: Builds, Migrations, Apps and Ongoing Support

Need a Shopify agency in Manchester? What to check on store builds, migrations, apps, UK VAT and delivery setup, SEO and support, plus costs and timelines.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good Shopify agency for a Manchester brand handles the whole store, from theme and product structure to UK VAT, delivery rates, payments, apps and SEO, not just the design.
  • Ask to see how the agency plans migrations, limits app bloat, protects search rankings and hands over a store your team can run day to day.
  • Prism Works Studio is a UK studio that works remotely, with no Manchester office; e-commerce builds typically cost £1,500 to £3,500 and take 5 to 12 weeks.

The right Shopify agency for a Manchester business builds a store that is fast, easy to manage and set up correctly for UK selling, with VAT, delivery, payments and SEO handled from the start, then supports you as you grow. Where the agency is based matters much less than how it plans, builds and hands over the store, so this guide sets out what to check.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio that builds and supports Shopify stores remotely for brands across the UK, Manchester included. We do not have a Manchester office. This guide is designed to help you choose well, whoever you hire.

Why Manchester brands choose Shopify

Manchester has a busy independent retail and direct-to-consumer scene, from fashion, beauty and lifestyle labels to food and drink producers, homeware makers and gift brands. Many start with a market stall, a pop-up or social media and then need a proper online store that can grow with them.

Shopify suits that path well. It handles hosting, security and checkout, has a large app ecosystem, supports point of sale for pop-ups and shops, and can grow into Shopify Plus for larger brands. It also lets your own team add products, run discounts and manage orders without a developer for every change.

But a Shopify store is only as good as its setup. Poor product structure, too many apps, slow themes and missing UK settings are common problems, and an agency's job is to avoid them.

What to check when hiring a Shopify agency in Manchester

A discovery stage before a fixed quote. A good agency asks about your products, order volumes, fulfilment, team and plans before quoting. Without that, the price is a guess and the scope will likely change later.

A clear migration plan. If you are moving from WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix or another platform, ask how products, customers, orders and URLs will move, and how redirects will protect existing rankings. Our Shopify SEO guide covers the redirects and settings that matter.

A light approach to apps. Apps are useful, but each one can add scripts, costs and possible conflicts. Ask which apps the agency would recommend and why, and whether a theme feature could do the job instead.

Speed and mobile performance. Most shoppers browse on their phones. Ask how the agency tests speed and what it does to keep pages fast once products, images and apps are added.

Conversion basics. Clear product pages, honest delivery information, trust signals and a simple checkout matter more than clever design. Our ecommerce CRO guide sets out what to prioritise.

A proper handover. You should own the store, theme and apps, and your team should know how to add products, edit pages and run promotions. Ask what training and documentation are included.

Planning a Shopify store that can grow

Many Manchester brands sell in more than one way. A label might sell online, at weekend markets and through a handful of stockists. A food producer might run an online shop alongside a stall and a subscription box. A good agency plans for these channels from the start rather than bolting them on later.

Shopify can support several of them in one place. Shopify POS lets you sell in person at pop-ups, markets or a shop while keeping stock and orders in the same system. Wholesale and trade customers can be handled with separate pricing or a dedicated area, depending on your plan and apps. Subscriptions, gift cards and bundles are also possible, though each adds complexity and sometimes extra monthly costs.

Payments deserve particular thought. Card payments, digital wallets and buy now pay later options can all affect conversion, fees and customer expectations. Our guide to payment gateways for UK online shops compares the main choices.

Ask any agency how it would set the store up for the channels you use now and the ones you plan to add in the next year or two. A store that is structured well from day one is far cheaper to extend than one that has to be rebuilt.

It is also worth asking how the agency approaches international selling if you expect orders from outside the UK. Shopify Markets can handle currencies, languages and local pricing, but customs, duties and delivery options need careful planning. Content in other languages would come from your own team or a translator rather than from us.

Questions to ask in a first call

  1. Which theme would you recommend for our products, and why?
  2. How would you structure our collections, variants and filters?
  3. How will you protect our search rankings if we are migrating?
  4. Which apps would you install, and what will they cost each month?
  5. What training and support do we get after launch?
  6. Who owns the store, theme and accounts?

Be cautious about agencies that start with mood boards before asking about stock, fulfilment or margins, or that propose large amounts of custom code for features Shopify already handles.

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Manchester?

Look for an agency that runs a discovery stage, explains its theme and app choices, has a clear migration and SEO plan, and hands over a store your team can manage. Compare proposals on scope, not just price.

Does Prism Works Studio have a Manchester office?

No. We are a UK-based studio that works remotely with brands across the UK, including Manchester and Greater Manchester.

How long does a Shopify store take to build?

Our e-commerce builds typically take 5 to 12 weeks, depending on the number of products, migration work and customisation.

How much does a Shopify agency in Manchester cost?

At Prism Works Studio, e-commerce sites typically cost £1,500 to £3,500. Shopify's subscription and paid apps are separate.

Can you move my store to Shopify without losing rankings?

A careful migration with URL mapping and redirects reduces the risk significantly, although some short-term movement in rankings is normal after any platform change.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Most small and growing brands do not. Plus tends to suit higher-volume stores that need more automation, checkout control or multiple storefronts.
