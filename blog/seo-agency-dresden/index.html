The Dresden search landscape

Dresden is the capital of Saxony and has a strong mix of industries: semiconductors and microelectronics, research institutes, engineering firms, a large university, tourism built around its historic old town and a wide base of local trades and services. That mix creates very different search behaviour depending on who you sell to.

  • Local service businesses compete for map results and searches that include the city or a district.
  • B2B and industrial firms compete for specific technical terms with low volume but high value.
  • Tourism and hospitality businesses compete with large booking and review platforms.
  • Exporters need visibility in several countries and languages at once.

A useful SEO plan starts by deciding which of these applies to you, because the work is quite different for each.

Technical SEO first

Many sites we review have problems that no amount of new content will fix. Common examples include pages blocked from indexing, duplicate versions of the same page, broken redirects after a redesign, slow mobile load times and missing language tags. We start with a technical audit, rank the issues by impact and fix the most important ones first.

Speed deserves special attention. Our guide to Core Web Vitals explains the measures Google uses and the fixes that usually help most. If your site runs on WordPress, outdated plugins and heavy page builders are frequent causes of slow pages.

Content that matches real searches

Good SEO content answers the question behind a search clearly and completely. For a Dresden engineering supplier, that might mean a detailed page for each product family, with specifications, applications and an easy way to request a quote. For a local clinic or law firm, it might mean a page for each service with clear information about process, location and how to book.

We map keywords to pages so each page has one clear purpose. That avoids two pages competing for the same term and makes it obvious where new content should go. Because Prism writes in English, German pages are written or translated by your team or a translator. We provide outlines, keyword targets and on-page recommendations, then review the finished pages for structure and technical accuracy.

Local SEO in Dresden

If customers come to you, or you serve them in person, local SEO is often the fastest route to more enquiries. The main levers are:

  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with the right primary category.
  • Consistent name, address and phone details across directories.
  • A steady flow of genuine reviews and replies to them.
  • A location page on your website that matches your profile.
  • Regular updates and photos on your profile.

Our local SEO guide and our article on Google Business Profile categories explain these steps in detail. The principles apply in Dresden just as they do in the UK.

International SEO for Saxon exporters

Many Dresden companies sell well beyond Germany. If you target buyers in several countries, the site structure matters as much as the content. Most businesses do best with a folder per language or market, correct hreflang tags and content written for each audience rather than a direct copy. Our international SEO guide and multilingual website design article cover the options.

English-language pages are where we can contribute most directly, writing content for buyers in the UK, Ireland, North America and other English-speaking markets, while your team or translators handle German and other languages.

What reporting should look like

Rankings alone do not pay the bills. A good monthly report should show organic traffic to the pages that matter, enquiries or sales from search, progress on agreed tasks and what is planned next. We keep reports short and in plain English, with a clear list of actions rather than pages of charts.

Working with a remote UK studio

Dresden is normally one hour ahead of the UK, so working hours overlap almost completely. We agree priorities at the start of each month, share a task list you can see at any time and meet by video call when it helps. Germany follows the EU GDPR and has its own rules on website notices, so the legal wording on your site should be checked by your own adviser. Official guidance is available from the German data protection authorities.

Common SEO mistakes we see

Many Dresden businesses have already tried some SEO, often with mixed results. The same mistakes come up again and again, and avoiding them saves both time and budget.

  • Targeting only broad terms such as a single industry word, instead of the specific searches buyers actually make.
  • Thin service pages with a few lines of text that do not answer real questions about price, process or timing.
  • Redesigns without redirects, which can wipe out years of search visibility overnight.
  • Buying links from low-quality sites, which can do more harm than good.
  • Ignoring AI search, where clear, well-structured answers are more likely to be quoted in AI-generated summaries.

Fixing these basics often delivers more than chasing new tactics. We start every engagement with a review of what has been done before, so we build on what works and stop what does not.
