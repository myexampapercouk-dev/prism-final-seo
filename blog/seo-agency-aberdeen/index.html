What an SEO agency in Aberdeen actually does

Search engine optimisation is the work of making your website easier to find, easier to understand and more useful than the competing pages. For an Aberdeen business that usually breaks down into four areas of work that run side by side.

  • Local SEO: your Google Business Profile, your name, address and phone details across directories, reviews and the location signals on your own site.
  • Technical SEO: page speed, mobile layout, crawling, indexing, redirects, structured data and the fixes that stop good pages being ignored.
  • Content: service pages, location pages and guides that answer the questions your customers type into Google.
  • Authority: earning mentions and links from relevant local and industry websites so search engines trust your pages.

If you want a broader picture of how these pieces fit together, our local SEO guide for UK businesses covers the basics in more depth, and our SEO and GEO service page sets out how we run the work.

Why search in Aberdeen behaves differently

Aberdeen is a compact city with a large surrounding county, and that shapes how people search. Someone in the city centre might type "plumber near me", while someone in Westhill, Ellon, Stonehaven or Inverurie may add the town name or simply "Aberdeenshire". A good SEO plan accounts for both patterns rather than assuming every customer searches the same way.

The local economy also matters. Energy, engineering, marine and offshore supply chains sit alongside hospitality, retail, trades, professional services and a large student population. Business-to-business firms in the energy sector often target buyers well beyond the North East, while a café or salon only needs to win searches within a few miles. The right strategy depends on where your customers are, not just where you are based.

Finally, the region's search volumes are smaller than those in Glasgow or Edinburgh. That is good news in one sense: there are fewer competing pages for many terms. It also means every page has to work harder, because there are fewer searches to go around and each enquiry is worth more.

How long SEO takes to work in Aberdeen

SEO results typically take three to six months to build. Quick wins such as fixing a broken Google Business Profile, correcting titles or resolving indexing problems can show movement sooner, but lasting rankings come from steady work over several months.

The timeline depends on a few things:

  1. How competitive your sector is in the North East and beyond.
  2. The current state of your website, including speed, structure and content.
  3. How many reviews and local mentions your business already has.
  4. Whether you target only Aberdeen or the wider UK market.
  5. How quickly changes can be made and approved on your side.

Local SEO in Aberdeen: the practical checklist

For most local businesses, the map pack is where the enquiries come from. These are the steps we work through first.

  • Claim and fully complete your Google Business Profile, with accurate categories, opening hours, services and photos.
  • Make sure your business name, address and phone number match exactly on your site and across major directories.
  • Build a simple process for asking happy customers for reviews, and reply to every review politely.
  • Create a clear page for each main service, and separate area pages only where you genuinely serve those towns.
  • Add local business structured data so search engines understand your details.
  • Earn mentions from local organisations, trade bodies and suppliers you already work with.

Be careful with area pages. Creating dozens of near-identical pages for every Aberdeenshire village rarely helps and can hurt. A handful of genuinely useful pages, each with real detail about the work you do in that area, is far better.

How to choose an SEO agency for your Aberdeen business

Whoever you hire, ask these questions before you sign anything.

  • What exactly will you do each month, and how will you report it?
  • Who owns the work, the content and the accounts if we stop?
  • How long is the minimum contract?
  • Do you guarantee rankings? (A good agency will say no, because nobody controls Google.)
  • How will you handle my Google Business Profile and reviews?
