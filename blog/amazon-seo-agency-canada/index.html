Amazon SEO Agency for Canadian Sellers: Amazon.ca Explained

Looking for an Amazon SEO agency in Canada? How Amazon.ca listing optimisation works, what affects ranking, bilingual listings, fees and what to ask first.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Amazon SEO for Canadian sellers means making Amazon.ca listings easy to find and convincing to buy from, using titles, bullets, images, back-end terms and reviews.
  • Amazon.ca is its own marketplace, so keyword research, pricing, stock and listing content should be checked for Canadian shoppers rather than copied from another country.
  • A remote agency can optimise listings well when you keep ownership of Seller Central and approve every change before it goes live.

An Amazon SEO agency for a Canadian seller should make your products easier to find on Amazon.ca and more convincing once shoppers land on them, by improving titles, bullet points, images, descriptions, back-end search terms and the signals Amazon uses to rank listings. That work happens inside Seller Central, so it can be done remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that provides marketplace listing SEO for Canadian businesses online, with no office in Canada.

This guide explains how Amazon search usually works for Canadian sellers, what affects ranking, how to handle English and French listings, how a remote arrangement runs and what our support costs.

What an Amazon SEO agency in Canada should do

Amazon is a shopping search engine. People arrive ready to buy, and the listings that appear first tend to be those that match the search closely and convert visitors into buyers. A good Amazon SEO agency should focus on both halves of that equation:

  • Keyword research for Amazon.ca, using the phrases Canadian shoppers actually type rather than those borrowed from another marketplace.
  • Listing content that places the most important terms naturally in titles, bullet points and descriptions without stuffing.
  • Back-end search terms that cover useful variations not already in the visible listing.
  • Images and enhanced content that answer questions visually and help shoppers decide.
  • Ongoing review of search terms, conversion and competitor changes.

Marketplace listing SEO sits within our SEO and GEO service, so it can be planned alongside search work for your own website.

How Amazon.ca ranks products

Amazon does not publish its ranking formula, and it changes over time. Across marketplaces, though, the factors that usually matter fall into two groups: relevance, meaning how well a listing matches the search, and performance, meaning how well it sells once people see it. Amazon.ca is its own marketplace

Many Canadian sellers also sell on Amazon.com, and some sellers from other countries expand into Canada. Either way, Amazon.ca should be treated as a market in its own right. Canadian shoppers may use different spellings, product names and sizes, and competitor sets on Amazon.ca are often different from those on other marketplaces.

Practical differences worth checking include metric and imperial measurements, Canadian spellings in titles and descriptions, prices and shipping expectations, and the availability of stock through your chosen fulfilment method. Tax and import questions for selling across borders are for you and your accountant, so check current official guidance before expanding. Amazon's own programmes and rules for linking marketplaces change from time to time, so confirm current options inside Seller Central.

English and French Amazon listings

Amazon.ca lets shoppers browse in English or French, and French-speaking shoppers, particularly in Quebec, may search in French. Whether French listing content is worth adding depends on your products, your audience and any obligations that apply to your packaging and labelling.

Prism researches keywords and writes listing content in English. Where French listings make sense, we plan which listings to prioritise and what each should cover, and the French text is written with your team or a professional translator. Language and labelling rules can apply to products sold in Canada, so check current official guidance for your category.

Images, A+ content and brand stores

Shoppers cannot touch the product, so images do much of the selling. A strong image set usually includes a clean main image that meets Amazon's rules, close-ups of important details, images showing scale, lifestyle shots in real use and simple graphics explaining key features. Our ecommerce product photography guide covers how to brief a photographer for this.

Brand-registered sellers can usually add enhanced content, often called A+ content, and a brand store. These do not replace a strong title and bullets, but they can answer questions, compare products in your range and build confidence. We plan the structure and copy, and work with your designer or photographer on the visuals.

How remote Amazon SEO works

The UK is usually five hours ahead of eastern Canada and eight hours ahead of the Pacific coast, so calls are usually arranged for UK afternoons. Most of the work is asynchronous: we share keyword research, draft listings and recommendations in documents your team can review at a convenient time.

  1. Access. You keep ownership of Seller Central and grant user access with suitable permissions.
  2. Audit. We review priority listings, search terms, images and competitors.
  3. Research. We build keyword lists for Amazon.ca and agree target terms for each product.
  4. Rewrite. We draft titles, bullets, descriptions and back-end terms for your approval.
  5. Publish and monitor. Approved changes go live, and we track visibility and conversion over the following weeks.

We focus on the listings that matter most to your sales first rather than rewriting an entire catalogue at once.

A few common mistakes are worth avoiding along the way. Stuffing titles with every possible keyword makes listings harder to read and can break category rules. Changing many listing elements at once makes it impossible to tell what helped. Ignoring stock levels can undo good optimisation work, because listings that run out tend to lose visibility. And any offer to buy reviews or manipulate ratings should be refused outright, since it breaks Amazon's policies and can put your whole account at risk.

FAQs

How do I choose an Amazon SEO agency in Canada?

Look for an agency that researches keywords specifically for Amazon.ca, explains its changes clearly, follows Amazon's rules, never offers to manipulate reviews and leaves Seller Central fully in your control. Ask how they handle French listings.

Can a UK agency optimise my Amazon.ca listings?

Yes. Listing work happens inside Seller Central, so it can be done remotely. Prism has no Canadian office; we work with Canadian sellers online and schedule calls in the time-zone overlap.

How much does Amazon SEO cost with Prism?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Amazon fees and ad spend are paid separately, and we can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Can I copy my Amazon.com listings to Amazon.ca?

You can start from them, but it is worth checking keywords, spellings, measurements, pricing and competitors for Canadian shoppers. Small changes for the Canadian market often make listings more relevant.

Do I need French listings on Amazon.ca?

It depends on your products and customers. If many of your buyers browse in French, French listing content can help. We plan it, and the French text is written with your team or a professional translator.

How long does Amazon SEO take to work?

Some changes can show effects within weeks, but steady improvement in visibility and sales typically builds over three to six months, depending on competition, stock, pricing and reviews.
