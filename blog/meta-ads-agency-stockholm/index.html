Who we work with in Stockholm

Stockholm has a busy mix of direct-to-consumer brands, design-led retailers, tech startups, restaurants, fitness studios and professional services. Meta ads suit businesses that sell something people can want before they search for it. We typically work with:

  • Ecommerce brands selling fashion, homeware, beauty, outdoor gear or food online, often to customers across Sweden and the Nordics.
  • Consumer apps and subscriptions that need steady sign-ups at a cost they can sustain.
  • Local service businesses such as clinics, gyms, salons and studios that want booked appointments from people nearby.
  • Hospitality and events that need to fill tables, rooms or tickets in a specific window.
  • B2B companies that use Meta for retargeting and awareness alongside search and LinkedIn.

If most of your demand already comes from people searching for a product by name, search ads may be the better first step. Our guide to Google Ads for Stockholm businesses covers that side.

What a Meta ads agency in Stockholm should do for you

Good Meta ads management is less about clever targeting tricks and more about doing the basics properly and testing with discipline. Here is what we handle.

Tracking before spend

We start by checking that the Meta pixel and the Conversions API are sending the right events, such as purchases, leads, add-to-carts and bookings, with the right values. On Shopify and WordPress this usually means checking the official integrations, removing duplicate events and confirming that consent choices are respected. Without clean tracking the algorithm optimises towards the wrong signals and your reports mislead you.

Sweden follows the EU GDPR and the ePrivacy rules on cookies and tracking. We set up consent-aware tracking in a general, practical way, but the legal decisions about your cookie banner and privacy policy rest with you and your own adviser. The Swedish data protection authority, IMY, publishes official guidance worth reading.

Account structure and audiences

We keep campaign structures simple so that the algorithm has enough data to learn. That usually means a small number of campaigns split by goal, such as prospecting, retargeting and customer retention, with broad or lookalike audiences rather than dozens of narrow interest groups. For Stockholm local businesses we use location targeting around the areas you actually serve, whether that is Södermalm, Östermalm, Kungsholmen, the inner city or the wider Stockholm region.

Creative testing

Creative is the biggest lever in Meta ads. We plan tests around hooks, offers, formats and angles, write English copy variations, and brief your team on the static images, carousels and short clips you supply. Prism does not film or edit video, so motion assets come from your team, your photographer or a production partner you choose. We then read the results and tell you which ideas to make more of.

Landing pages and offers

An ad can only be as good as the page it sends people to. We review your product pages, landing pages and checkout for speed, clarity and trust signals, and suggest changes. If your site needs more than tweaks, we can rebuild it as part of a website project.

Selling across Sweden and the Nordics

Many Stockholm brands sell nationally and then expand into Norway, Denmark and Finland. Meta makes it easy to target several countries from one account, but it is rarely wise to lump them together in one campaign. Languages, prices, delivery promises and returns differ, and each market needs ads and landing pages that match. Our guide to running Meta ads in multiple countries explains how we structure this.

Swedish shoppers are used to smooth checkouts. Showing the payment methods people expect, such as Klarna or Swish where your platform supports them, along with clear delivery times and return terms, often matters as much as the ad itself. If your store runs on Shopify, our guide to Shopify for Swedish brands covers the store side.

Meta ads compared with other channels

Meta is rarely the only channel a Stockholm business needs. The right mix depends on how your customers find you.

  • Meta ads create demand by showing your product to people who were not looking for it yet. They suit visual products, offers and local services.
  • Google Ads capture demand from people already searching. They suit urgent needs and known product categories.
  • TikTok ads reach a younger audience with native short-form content. See our guide to TikTok ads in Sweden.
  • SEO builds long-term organic visibility. Our guide to SEO for Stockholm businesses explains how that works.

We often run Meta alongside one of these, with a shared report so you can see how each channel contributes.

How AI fits into our Meta ads work

Meta's own tools, such as Advantage+ campaigns and automated placements, use machine learning heavily. We use them where they help and keep manual control where they do not. We also use AI-powered tools in our own workflow to draft copy variations, group search and comment themes, and spot patterns in reporting. A person on our team checks every output before it goes live, and nothing is published in your name without your approval.

Our process

  1. Discovery call. We learn about your products, margins, customers and goals, and look at any past campaign data.
  2. Audit and tracking. We review your ad account, pixel, Conversions API and landing pages, and fix the tracking gaps first.
  3. Plan. We agree goals, budgets, audiences, creative tests and reporting.
  4. Launch. We build campaigns in your ad account and send previews for sign-off.
  5. Test and improve. We review results weekly, refresh creative, shift budget to what works and pause what does not.
  6. Report. You get a plain-English monthly report covering spend, results, what we learned and what we will try next.

Paid social and search results typically take three to six months to settle into a reliable pattern, because the first weeks are spent learning what works. We will not promise a particular return on ad spend, because it depends on your product, prices, site and market.

Choosing a Meta ads agency in Stockholm

Whether you pick us or someone else, a few questions help you judge any agency:

  • Will the ad account, pixel and data stay in my name?
  • How will you check that tracking is accurate before spending money?
  • How often will you test new creative, and who supplies it?
  • What will the monthly report show, and how will you explain it?
  • How much notice do I need to give to stop working together?

If an agency cannot answer these clearly, keep looking. For more on what to expect from a Meta partner, see our guide to choosing a Meta ads agency.
