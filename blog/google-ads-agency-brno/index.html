The Brno market in brief

Brno is the Czech Republic's second largest city and the centre of South Moravia. It has large universities, a growing technology and software sector, engineering and manufacturing firms, a busy trade fair calendar and many service businesses that serve the city and surrounding region. A lot of Brno companies also sell to customers in Prague, Slovakia, Austria and further afield.

That mix shapes how you should advertise. A local dentist or car service needs campaigns tightly focused on Brno and nearby towns. A software company or industrial supplier may need campaigns in Czech for domestic buyers and in English or other languages for international ones. If you also target the capital, our guide to Google Ads in Prague covers the more competitive market there.

Google and Seznam: where Czech searchers look

The Czech Republic is one of the few European markets where Google has a well known local rival. Seznam is a Czech search engine and portal that many people still use, alongside Google. It runs its own advertising platform, separate from Google Ads.

We manage Google Ads, not Seznam advertising. For many Brno businesses, especially those selling to younger audiences, business buyers or international customers, Google is where most search activity happens. If your customers lean towards Seznam, it is worth discussing that with a specialist in that platform as well. We will always be clear about which channels we run and which we do not.

Google Ads agency Brno: what good management includes

Google Ads is easy to switch on and easy to waste money on. Good management is about structure, control and steady improvement rather than simply raising the budget. A competent agency should handle the following.

  • Keyword research in the right language. Czech search terms, including the many grammatical forms of the same word, plus any English terms your buyers use.
  • Tight campaign structure. Separate campaigns for each main service or product group, so budget goes where it earns most.
  • Location targeting. Brno city, the South Moravian region, the whole country or international markets, depending on who you serve.
  • Negative keywords. Blocking searches for jobs, free versions, unrelated products and anything else that wastes clicks.
  • Conversion tracking. Forms, calls, bookings and sales tracked properly, so bidding decisions use real outcomes.
  • Landing pages that match the ad. The page a visitor lands on should answer exactly what they searched for.
  • Regular reporting. Clear monthly reports showing cost, conversions and cost per conversion, not just clicks.

Writing ads in Czech

Czech is a highly inflected language, which means the same word can appear in many forms depending on how it is used. This affects keyword matching and ad copy. Google handles close variants reasonably well, but ad text still needs to read naturally to a native speaker.

Prism writes in English. Czech ad copy and landing page text should come from your own team or a professional translator. We build the campaign structure, keyword lists and testing plan, and we work with your Czech copy to make sure it fits Google's character limits and matches each keyword group. For English-language campaigns aimed at international buyers, we can write the copy ourselves.

Click prices in the Czech market are often lower than in the UK, but competitive sectors can still be expensive.

What results to expect, and when

Paid search can bring traffic from the first day, but profitable campaigns take time to refine. The first weeks are about gathering data: which keywords convert, which waste money and which ad messages work. Most accounts improve steadily over the first three to six months as negative keywords build up and bidding learns from real conversions.

Be cautious of any agency that promises a fixed number of leads or a guaranteed return. Results depend on your offer, your prices, your landing pages and your market, as well as the campaigns themselves. Working with a remote agency from Brno

The Czech Republic is one hour ahead of the UK for most of the year, so working hours overlap almost completely. Everything in Google Ads is managed online, which means location matters far less than process and communication.

You will own your Google Ads account, and we work inside it with access you grant and can remove at any time. Ads, SEO and your website together

Google Ads works best alongside organic search and a strong website. Search term reports from your ads show which phrases bring enquiries, which helps plan SEO content. Over time, ranking organically for your best terms can reduce how much you rely on paid clicks. FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Brno?

Choose an agency that lets you own your account, sets up conversion tracking before raising spend, explains its campaign structure and reports on cost per enquiry or sale rather than clicks alone. Avoid guaranteed results and long lock-in contracts.

Does Prism Works Studio have an office in Brno?

No. We are a UK-based studio that works remotely. We manage Google Ads for Czech businesses through shared dashboards, video calls and email, and we have no office in the Czech Republic.

Do you manage Seznam ads as well as Google Ads?

No. We manage Google Ads, along with Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn Ads. Seznam runs its own advertising platform, which needs a specialist in that system.

Can you write my ads in Czech?

We write in English. Czech ad copy should come from your team or a translator, and we fit it into the campaign structure, keyword groups and character limits.

How much should a Brno business spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your sector, keywords and goals. Many businesses start with a modest test budget, measure cost per enquiry and increase spend once campaigns are profitable. Ad spend is paid separately from our management fee.

Can you quote in Czech koruna?

Yes. Our management fees are set in pounds, starting at £350 a month, and we can provide koruna or euro quotes on request.
