Google Ads Agency for Winnipeg Businesses: How Remote PPC Works

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Winnipeg? How remote PPC works for Manitoba firms, from targeting and tracking to budgets, fees in pounds and what to ask.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Google Ads agency for a Winnipeg business should set up accurate conversion tracking first, then build tightly themed campaigns around the searches that bring real enquiries.
  • Location targeting matters in Manitoba, because many firms serve Winnipeg and nearby towns while others sell across the Prairies or the whole of Canada.
  • Management fees and ad spend are separate, and campaigns usually need three to six months of testing and refinement before performance settles.

A Google Ads agency for a Winnipeg business should set up reliable conversion tracking, build campaigns around the searches your customers actually make and manage bids, budgets and ads so that your spend goes towards enquiries and sales rather than wasted clicks. All of that happens inside your Google Ads account, website and analytics, so it can be done remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads for Canadian businesses online, and we have no office in Winnipeg or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide explains what Google Ads management involves for Winnipeg and Manitoba businesses, how to think about targeting across the city and the wider Prairies, what our fees look like and the questions worth asking any agency before you hand over your account.

What a Google Ads agency in Winnipeg should do

Winnipeg has a broad economy, with manufacturing, agriculture and food processing, transport and logistics, insurance and finance, healthcare, education, trades and a large number of independent retailers and service firms. The details change by sector, but good Google Ads management usually covers the same core jobs:

  • Conversion tracking. Calls, form submissions, bookings and purchases recorded accurately, so decisions are based on outcomes rather than clicks. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking explains the set-up.
  • Keyword and campaign structure. Campaigns grouped by service or product, with ads and landing pages that match each group closely.
  • Negative keywords. Regularly excluding searches that will never turn into customers, such as job hunters or people looking for free advice. See our negative keywords guide.
  • Ads and assets. Clear headlines, descriptions, sitelinks and call assets that set honest expectations.
  • Bidding and budgets. Choosing a bid strategy that suits your data and adjusting spend as results become clearer.
  • Reporting. Plain-language updates on cost, enquiries, sales and next steps.

Our paid media service covers each of these, along with Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube campaigns where they fit.

Targeting Winnipeg, Manitoba and the Prairies

Location settings are one of the easiest places to waste money in Google Ads. A heating contractor in St. Vital or a dental clinic in Transcona may only want customers within a reasonable drive, while a manufacturer, software firm or online shop may want enquiries from across Manitoba, Saskatchewan and beyond. Getting the radius, the excluded areas and the presence settings right keeps your ads in front of people who can actually buy from you.

Seasonality matters too. Manitoba winters change what people search for, from furnace repairs and snow removal to indoor activities and winter tyres, and spring and summer bring different demand for landscaping, construction and tourism. We plan budgets and ad copy around those shifts rather than leaving campaigns on the same settings all year. Our location targeting guide covers the options in detail.

Campaign types worth considering

Search campaigns remain the foundation for most service businesses, because they reach people at the moment they are looking for help. Beyond Search, the right mix depends on what you sell:

  • Shopping and Performance Max suit online stores with a clean product feed. Our Performance Max guide explains how these campaigns work and where they need careful supervision.
  • Remarketing reminds past visitors about your business, which helps for higher-value purchases that take time. See our remarketing guide.
  • YouTube can build awareness before people search, especially for consumer brands and attractions.

We usually start with a focused Search set-up, prove what works and then expand, rather than launching every campaign type at once and splitting a modest budget too thinly.

Landing pages and conversion rate

Many Google Ads problems are really landing page problems. If an ad promises fast furnace repair in Winnipeg and the click lands on a generic home page, a good share of that budget is lost before the visitor has had a chance to enquire. Each campaign should point to a page that repeats the promise in the ad, shows evidence that you can deliver it and makes the next step obvious.

We review landing pages as part of the set-up and suggest changes, from headline wording and form length to page speed on mobile. Where a page needs rebuilding, we can design and build it. Our landing page design guide covers what usually makes the difference.

English, French and Canadian ad rules

Winnipeg has a long-standing francophone community centred on St. Boniface, and some Manitoba organisations serve customers in French as well as English. If French searches matter to you, separate French campaigns with their own keywords and ads usually work better than mixing languages. We write in English; French ad copy and landing pages are written with your team or a professional translator.

A few rules are worth keeping in mind in general terms. Google applies its own advertising policies, with extra requirements for areas such as financial services and healthcare. If your campaigns collect contact details for follow-up email, Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) sets out consent rules, and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) covers how personal information is handled. This is general information rather than legal advice, so check the official guidance from the relevant Canadian regulators and take advice where needed. In regulated sectors, approval of ads and landing pages rests with your compliance team.

How remote Google Ads management works

Winnipeg runs on Central Time, so the UK is usually six hours ahead. UK afternoons overlap with Winnipeg mornings, which works well for calls, and most of the work happens in shared accounts and written updates that you can read when it suits you.

  1. Audit. We review your account, tracking, landing pages and competitors, or plan a new account from scratch. Our PPC audit checklist shows what we look at.
  2. Plan. We agree goals, target areas, campaign structure and a sensible starting budget.
  3. Build. We set up tracking, campaigns, ads, assets and audiences.
  4. Optimise. We review search terms, bids, ads and budgets, and test improvements.
  5. Report. Monthly updates on spend, enquiries, sales and what we plan next.

You keep ownership of the Google Ads account, analytics and all data throughout. We work as users on your account rather than running campaigns from one we control.

Questions to ask any Google Ads agency in Winnipeg

  • How will you track calls, forms and sales, and how will I see the results?
  • Will I own the Google Ads account and all its data?
  • How will you set location targeting across Winnipeg and Manitoba?
  • How often will you review search terms and add negative keywords?
  • Are your fees separate from ad spend, and what is the notice period?
  • What would you change on our landing pages before we spend more?

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Winnipeg?

Look for an agency that starts with tracking, explains its campaign structure clearly, keeps fees separate from ad spend and leaves you owning the account. Ask how it would target your service area across Winnipeg and the rest of Manitoba.

Can a UK agency manage Google Ads for a Winnipeg business?

Yes. Google Ads is managed online, so location is not a barrier. Prism has no Winnipeg office; we work remotely and schedule calls in the overlap between UK afternoons and Winnipeg mornings.

How much does Google Ads management cost with Prism?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Google. We can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

How long does it take for Google Ads to work?

Ads can bring enquiries soon after launch, but most accounts need three to six months of testing and refinement before performance settles and costs become more predictable.

Should I run separate campaigns for French searches?

If French-speaking customers matter to you, separate French campaigns and landing pages usually perform better than mixed-language ads. French copy is written with your team or a professional translator.

Do I need a new website before running Google Ads?

Not always. A focused landing page that matches the ad, loads quickly on mobile and makes enquiring easy is often enough to start. We review your current pages and suggest changes before increasing spend.
