Shopify Agency for Vancouver Brands: How Remote Store Projects Work

Looking for a Shopify agency in Vancouver? How a remote studio builds, migrates and grows Shopify stores for BC brands, with timelines, tax notes and costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify agency for a Vancouver brand should plan the store around products, shipping and search before choosing themes and apps.
  • Many Vancouver brands sell across Canada and into the United States, so shipping profiles, currencies, duties and tax settings need careful planning from the start.
  • An ecommerce build typically takes five to twelve weeks and costs £1,500 to £3,500 with Prism, with other currencies quoted on request.

A Shopify agency for a Vancouver brand should set up a store that loads quickly, makes products easy to find, handles Canadian and cross-border shipping properly and gives you a clean base for search and advertising. Shopify projects run through the platform admin, shared documents and a preview theme, so the work can be delivered remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that builds and improves Shopify stores for Canadian brands online, and we have no office in Vancouver or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide covers what a Shopify agency should do for a Vancouver or British Columbia business, how cross-border selling affects store set-up, how a remote project runs, what it costs with us and what to ask before choosing any agency.

What a Shopify agency in Vancouver should do

Vancouver has a strong direct-to-consumer scene, from outdoor and activewear brands to coffee roasters, skincare, homeware, specialty food and design-led gifts. Whatever you sell, a good Shopify partner should help with:

  • Store planning. Mapping products, collections, navigation and the customer journey before any theme work. Our guide to Shopify collections structure shows how we organise catalogues.
  • Theme set-up and customisation. Using a well-supported theme and adapting it to your brand rather than piling on apps.
  • Product pages with clear photography, useful descriptions, size or variant guidance and delivery information.
  • Shipping, tax and payments configured for the places you actually sell.
  • Speed and search foundations, including clean URLs, structured data and sensible page titles.
  • Handover, so your team can add products, run discounts and manage orders confidently.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers new builds, redesigns and migrations to Shopify.

New builds, redesigns and migrations

Vancouver brands come to Shopify from different starting points. Some are launching their first store, some have outgrown a builder such as Wix or Squarespace and some are moving from WooCommerce to reduce maintenance. Each route needs a different plan.

For migrations, the main risks are losing search rankings and losing data. We map old URLs to new ones, move products, customers and order history where possible and set up redirects so existing links and rankings carry over. Our WooCommerce to Shopify migration guide walks through the process. Larger brands with high order volumes or complex wholesale needs may also consider Shopify Plus, which we cover in our Shopify Plus migration article.

Search, speed and conversion

A store that looks good but cannot be found will struggle. We build search foundations into every Shopify project: descriptive collection and product titles, unique descriptions, image alt text, structured data and a logical internal link structure. Our Shopify SEO guide covers the main settings, and our article on ecommerce SEO in Canada looks at search across the Canadian market more widely.

Speed matters too. Each app adds code, and stores often slow down as apps accumulate. We keep the app list short, compress images and review theme code so pages load quickly on mobile connections. Our Shopify speed optimisation guide explains what we check.

Bilingual stores and Canadian rules

If you sell into Quebec or want to serve French-speaking customers elsewhere, a French version of the store is worth considering. Shopify supports multiple languages, and we set up the structure and language switching. French product descriptions and pages are written with your team or a professional translator, not by automatic translation alone.

Online stores collect personal information, so the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) generally applies to how it is handled, and British Columbia also has its own private sector privacy law. Marketing emails fall under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), which affects how you collect consent at checkout and sign-up. If you sell to customers in Quebec, that province's privacy law, often called Law 25, may add requirements. These notes are general information, not legal advice, so check official guidance from the relevant regulators.

Product pages that answer buyer questions

Product pages do most of the selling in a Shopify store, so we give them real attention. Shoppers comparing brands want to know what a product is made of, how it fits or works, how it compares with similar items in your range, when it will arrive and what happens if it is not right. Pages that answer those questions clearly tend to reduce support emails as well as abandoned carts.

For outdoor, apparel and gear brands, that often means size guides, fabric or material notes and care instructions. For food, coffee and skincare, it means ingredients, usage guidance and storage notes. For homeware and gifts, it means dimensions, finishes and gift options. We build reusable sections and metafields so this information stays consistent across the catalogue and is easy for your team to update without editing theme code.

Good photography matters as much as copy. We advise on image sizes, angles and file formats so photos look sharp without slowing pages down, and we make sure every image has descriptive alt text for accessibility and search.

How a remote Shopify project runs

Vancouver is on Pacific Time, so the UK is usually eight hours ahead. That leaves a smaller live overlap than with eastern Canada, so we schedule calls for early Vancouver mornings, which fall in UK late afternoons, and keep most communication in writing. Progress continues while you sleep, and you review the preview store at the start of your day.

  1. Discovery. Products, customers, competitors, markets and must-have features.
  2. Plan. Store structure, apps, shipping and tax decisions, content responsibilities.
  3. Design and build. Theme customisation and page building on a preview theme.
  4. Products and data. Product import, collections, filters and any migration work.
  5. Testing. Test orders, payments, emails, mobile layouts and tracking.
  6. Launch and handover. Domain connection, redirects, analytics and a walkthrough for your team.

Questions to ask any Shopify agency

  • Which theme do you recommend, and how much will you customise it?
  • Which apps do you plan to install, and what will they cost each month?
  • How will you handle shipping and currencies for US customers?
  • How do you protect search rankings during a migration?
  • Will we own the store, theme files and all content?
  • What support is available after launch?

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Vancouver?

Look for an agency that plans the store around your products and markets, keeps apps to a minimum, protects rankings during migrations and gives you a clear written scope. Ask how it handles cross-border shipping and currencies.

Can a UK agency build a Shopify store for a Vancouver brand?

Yes. Shopify projects run through the admin, a preview theme and shared documents. Prism has no Vancouver office; we work remotely and schedule calls for early Pacific Time mornings.

How much does a Shopify store cost with Prism?

An ecommerce site typically costs £1,500 to £3,500. Shopify subscription, app and transaction fees are separate, and we can quote in Canadian dollars or other currencies on request.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Most ecommerce builds take five to twelve weeks, depending on catalogue size, migration work and integrations.

Can you set up a French version of our store?

Yes. We set up the language structure and switching, while French content is written with your team or a professional translator.

Will moving to Shopify hurt our search rankings?

It does not have to. Mapping old URLs to new ones, setting up redirects and keeping useful content in place protects most of the visibility a store has already earned.
