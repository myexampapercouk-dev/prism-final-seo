The Lausanne hotel market

Lausanne sits on the northern shore of Lake Geneva, with the Alps across the water and the Lavaux vineyard terraces close by. It is home to the International Olympic Committee, many international sports federations, major universities and a busy calendar of congresses and events. Leisure guests come for the lake, the old town, museums, wine tourism and easy trips into the mountains.

That creates a distinctive pattern of demand:

  • Business and institutional travel on weekdays, often booked at short notice.
  • Leisure stays at weekends and in summer, with longer booking windows.
  • Event-driven peaks around conferences, sporting events and university dates.
  • Visiting family and friends of the large student and international population.
  • Several source languages, mainly French, German, English and Italian.

Start with the website and booking path

Every channel sends guests to your website, so it needs to load fast on mobile, show rooms clearly and make booking simple. The most common problems we see are slow image galleries, booking engines that open in confusing pop-ups, rates that are hard to compare and missing information on parking, transport links and check-in times.

A strong hotel website for Lausanne should include:

  • Clear room pages with real photos, sizes, bed types and views.
  • A visible book-direct button on every page.
  • Honest reasons to book direct, such as flexible terms or added extras you actually offer.
  • Location information covering the lake, the metro, the station and nearby venues.
  • Language versions planned with proper URLs and hreflang tags.

Our guide to hotel direct bookings goes into more detail on reducing reliance on booking platforms. For multilingual set-up, see our multilingual website design guide.

Hotel SEO for Lausanne searches

Hotel SEO focuses on searches where you can realistically compete with large booking sites. Broad terms are hard to win, but more specific searches are often within reach, such as hotels near a particular venue, lakeside hotels with parking, family rooms near the old town or long-stay apartments for visiting academics.

We map these searches to dedicated pages, improve titles and content, fix technical issues and strengthen internal links. Our hotel SEO guide explains the approach. As we write in English, French and German pages are written or translated by your team or a translator, and we provide the outlines and keyword targets for each.

Google Business Profile and reviews

For many guests, the first impression of your hotel is your Google Business Profile. Keep it complete and current with accurate categories, good photos of rooms and public areas, correct check-in details and prompt replies to reviews. Our Google Business Profile guide covers the set-up and upkeep.

Paid campaigns for business and leisure guests

Paid media lets you respond quickly to demand. For a Lausanne hotel, a typical set-up includes:

  • Brand search campaigns so guests searching your hotel name land on your site.
  • Event and date campaigns around congresses and sporting events.
  • Meta Ads promoting weekend breaks, wine-region stays and summer offers to chosen source markets.
  • Retargeting to bring back visitors who looked at rooms but did not book.

Our paid media service covers Google, Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn Ads. Ad spend is paid separately from our management fee, directly to the ad platforms.

Planning around the Lausanne calendar

A simple marketing calendar keeps campaigns ahead of demand rather than chasing it. Plan around the summer lake season, the busiest congress periods, university terms, Christmas markets and winter trips to the mountains. Launch offers well before each peak and use quieter weeks for longer-stay deals aimed at business visitors and academics.

Privacy and compliance

Switzerland has its own Federal Act on Data Protection, and many of your guests will be covered by the EU GDPR. Booking forms, cookies and tracking should be set up with consent and privacy in mind. We set sensible defaults, but your privacy notice and legal wording should be checked by your own adviser, and official guidance is published by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Email and repeat guests

Past guests are often the cheapest source of future bookings. A simple email programme can bring business travellers back for their next trip and remind leisure guests of seasonal offers. Useful emails include a pre-arrival message with practical information, a thank-you after the stay with a link to leave a review, and occasional offers timed around the lake season, events and festive breaks.

Keep emails short, useful and clearly consented. Collect permission at booking or check-in, make unsubscribing easy and segment by guest type where you can, so business travellers and families receive different messages. Email also works well alongside retargeting ads, reaching the same guests through different channels without paying commission on the booking.

Content that helps guests plan

Helpful content attracts guests early in their planning and supports your SEO. For a Lausanne hotel, that could mean guides to getting around by metro, day trips to the Lavaux terraces, visiting the Olympic Museum, lakeside walks or attending a congress nearby. Each guide should be genuinely useful, accurate and linked to the rooms and offers that fit that type of stay.

We plan topics, write English versions and provide outlines for French and German versions written by your team or a translator. Good content also gives your social channels and emails something worth sharing, which helps more guests discover the hotel before they reach a booking site.

Measuring what matters

Hotel marketing should be judged on direct bookings and revenue, not clicks. We set up tracking from the first visit through to the booking engine wherever your system allows, so you can see which channels bring bookings and at what cost. Each month we report direct bookings, revenue from each channel, cost per booking from ads and the actions planned for the next month. Our SEO and GEO service page explains how we measure organic visibility, including mentions in AI-generated answers. Our guide to Google Ads costs explains how ad budgets work. We also suggest comparing direct and platform bookings over time, because shifting even a small share of reservations to your own website can make a real difference to margins.
