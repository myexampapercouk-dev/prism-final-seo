Meta Ads Agency for Amman Businesses: Facebook and Instagram Campaigns

Meta Ads agency for Amman businesses: Facebook and Instagram campaigns with proper tracking from a UK-based studio working remotely, with no office in Jordan.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Meta Ads agency in Amman should pair strong creative in Arabic and English with accurate tracking, clear audiences and weekly testing on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that runs Meta Ads remotely for Amman businesses and has no office in Amman or anywhere in Jordan.
  • Management retainers start at £350 a month, ad spend is paid separately to Meta, and Jordanian dinar pricing is quoted on request.

A Meta Ads agency in Amman should help you reach the right people on Facebook and Instagram with creative that stops the scroll, then turn that attention into messages, leads and sales you can measure. Prism Works Studio plans, builds and manages Meta Ads campaigns for Amman businesses, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in Amman or anywhere in Jordan. We work through video calls, shared dashboards and clear written reports. We write in English; Arabic ad copy and landing pages are written with your team or a translator, and we build the campaigns around that copy.

Who we work with in Amman

Facebook and Instagram are widely used in Jordan, and many people discover local brands, services and offers through their feeds, stories and reels. We run Meta Ads for:

  • Online stores and retailers selling fashion, beauty, home goods and gifts across Jordan.
  • Restaurants, cafes and food brands promoting menus, offers and delivery.
  • Clinics and wellness providers where careful wording is required (approval rests with the client).
  • Education and training providers including language schools, courses and academies.
  • Tech start-ups and app businesses in Amman's growing start-up scene.
  • Real estate and service businesses generating leads through forms and messaging.

How we set up a Meta Ads account

  1. Discovery call. We learn your products, customers, margins, sales process and what a good lead or sale is worth.
  2. Tracking. We set up the Meta Pixel and Conversions API where your platform supports it, plus GA4, so purchases, leads and messages are measured.
  3. Audience plan. We plan broad audiences, interest groups, lookalikes and remarketing based on site visitors, video viewers and engagers.
  4. Creative plan. We brief images, short videos and reels, and suggest hooks, formats and offers to test.
  5. Campaign build. We structure campaigns by objective and stage, write English copy and place Arabic copy from your team or translator.
  6. Launch and testing. We review results weekly, keep what works, cut what does not and refresh creative before it tires.

Creative for Amman audiences

On Meta, creative does much of the targeting work. The ad itself decides who stops, watches and clicks. We focus on short vertical video, clear product shots, real use and simple offers. Ads should feel native to Instagram and Facebook rather than like a polished television spot.

Language matters as well. Many people in Amman use Arabic on social media, often in a local, conversational tone, while others prefer English. We usually test both. Arabic copy is written with your team or a translator so it sounds natural, and we check that it displays correctly right to left in every placement.

Messaging, WhatsApp and lead forms

Many Jordanian businesses close sales in chat rather than through a checkout. Meta's messaging objectives can start conversations in Messenger, Instagram Direct or WhatsApp. We set these up with welcome messages and quick replies, and agree with you how fast your team can respond, because slow replies waste ad spend.

For lead generation, instant forms are quick to fill in but can bring lower-quality leads. We often add a qualifying question or send traffic to a landing page instead. Our guide to website contact forms explains how to keep forms short and useful.

Landing pages and your website

Ads that send people to a slow or confusing page lose money. We review your landing pages for speed, mobile layout, Arabic display and the clarity of the offer, and can design new pages where needed. Our page on web design in Jordan covers full site projects, and our guide to hero section design explains what the first screen should do.

Meta Ads alongside Google Ads and SEO

Meta Ads create demand by reaching people who were not searching yet. Google Ads and SEO capture people who are already searching. Most Amman businesses benefit from both. Our pages on Google Ads in Jordan and SEO in Jordan explain how we run those channels, and our paid media service covers the full scope across platforms.

If you also sell into the Gulf, our page on Meta Ads in the UAE covers that market.

Seasonal planning

Ramadan and Eid change browsing and buying patterns, with more late-evening activity and gift and food purchases. Back-to-school, summer and end-of-year sales also bring peaks. We plan budgets, creative and offers ahead of these periods so ads are approved and live on time.

Pricing and timelines

We manage Meta Ads on a monthly retainer: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, depending on campaigns, languages and creative volume. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and is separate from our fee. Jordanian dinar pricing is quoted on request. See our pricing page for details.

Campaigns usually need a few weeks to exit the learning phase, and results typically build over 3 to 6 months as we test creative and audiences. We do not promise a fixed cost per lead or return on ad spend.

Rules and compliance

Meta has strict policies on health, finance, employment, housing and other special categories, and Jordan has its own consumer protection, e-commerce and advertising rules. We work within Meta's policies and general good practice, but we are not legal advisers. Please check official guidance or your own adviser. In regulated sectors, approval of ad wording rests with you and your compliance team.

Reporting you can understand

Meta Ads Manager shows a lot of numbers, and not all of them matter. We report on the figures that link to your goals: purchases and revenue for stores, qualified leads for service businesses, and conversations started for chat-led sales. We also show cost per result, frequency and creative performance so you can see which ads are working and which are tiring.

Each report includes short notes on what we changed, what we learned and what we plan to test next. You always keep ownership of your Business Manager, ad account, Pixel and data.

Common mistakes we fix

  • Boosting posts without a clear objective or tracking.
  • Too many small ad sets that split the budget and stop Meta from learning.
  • Running the same creative for months until results fade.
  • No remarketing to people who visited the site or watched a video.
  • Missing or broken Pixel events, so sales and leads are not counted.

Working with a remote studio

Because we are UK-based with no Amman office, everything runs online. Working hours overlap for most of the day, so calls and quick questions are easy to arrange. You get a shared dashboard, regular calls and written updates, and we work with your in-house team or content creators on creative.

Before we start, we agree goals, budgets, reporting dates and who on your side approves ads and replies to messages. Clear roles from the first week make testing faster and keep campaigns running smoothly through busy seasons such as Ramadan and Eid.

FAQs

How do I choose a Meta Ads agency in Amman?

Look for proper tracking, a clear creative testing plan, campaigns in both Arabic and English, honest reporting and an ad account you own. Ask how they handle messaging and lead quality.

Do you have an office in Amman?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely. We have no office in Amman or Jordan and work through video calls, shared dashboards and written reports.

Can you run Facebook and Instagram ads in Arabic?

Yes. Arabic ad copy is written with your team or a translator, and we build, check and test it across Facebook and Instagram placements.

Do you make the ad creative?

We brief and plan creative, suggest hooks and formats, and can adapt images and short videos you supply. Prism does not film or edit video, so footage comes from your team or a creator you hire.

How much does Meta Ads management cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month. Ad spend is paid to Meta separately, and Jordanian dinar pricing is quoted on request.

Can Meta Ads send people to WhatsApp?

Yes. We can run campaigns that start conversations in WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram Direct, as long as your team can reply quickly.
