YouTube Ads Agency for US Brands: How Remote YouTube Advertising Works

Need a YouTube Ads agency for a US brand? Remote management can plan, launch and optimise video campaigns that drive sales and leads. Here is how it works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A YouTube Ads agency for a US brand should start with the goal, the audience and the creative, then set up tracking before spending at scale.
  • YouTube works best when video ads are matched to intent: short, direct ads for action and longer stories for awareness and consideration.
  • We are a UK-based studio managing YouTube campaigns remotely for US clients, with fees in pounds and ad spend paid separately to Google.

If you are looking for a YouTube Ads agency for a US brand, you need a partner that can turn video views into sales, leads or measurable demand, not just cheap impressions. A remote agency can plan, launch and optimise YouTube campaigns for US businesses, because everything runs inside your own Google Ads account and the creative is shared and approved online.

We are a UK-based studio that manages paid media, including YouTube Ads, remotely for US businesses. We have no US office. This guide explains what a YouTube Ads agency should do, which campaign types suit which goals, what makes video creative work, how tracking and reporting should be set up, how a remote arrangement runs and what it costs.

What a YouTube Ads agency for US brands should do

YouTube is part of Google Ads, so a YouTube agency needs both video advertising knowledge and solid Google Ads skills. A complete service usually covers:

  • Strategy: agreeing whether the campaign is about sales, leads, sign-ups or awareness, and what a good result looks like.
  • Audience planning: choosing who should see the ads, from people actively searching for related topics to your own customer lists and site visitors.
  • Creative guidance: briefing or adapting video so the first few seconds work, the message is clear and the call to action is obvious.
  • Campaign set-up: the right campaign types, formats, bidding, budgets and placements.
  • Tracking: conversion tracking, YouTube channel linking and UTM tags so results show up in analytics.
  • Optimisation: testing creative, refining audiences and excluding poor placements.
  • Reporting: plain-language reports on cost per result, view rates and the effect on search and sales.

Targeting US audiences on YouTube

The US is a large and varied market, and YouTube targeting lets you focus on the people most likely to buy rather than the whole country:

  • Location: nationwide, by state, by metro area or by zip code, depending on where you sell and ship.
  • Custom segments: people who have searched for terms related to your products or visited similar sites.
  • In-market and life-event audiences: people Google identifies as actively researching a purchase or going through a relevant change.
  • Your data: website visitors, YouTube channel viewers and customer lists, used in line with your privacy policy and consent settings.
  • Exclusions: existing customers where appropriate, poor-quality placements and content categories that do not suit your brand.
  • Language: if you serve Spanish-speaking customers, separate Spanish creative and targeting usually performs better than one ad for everyone.

US privacy rules vary by state and change over time. We follow platform requirements and your own consent set-up, but decisions on data use and privacy notices rest with you and your lawyer, who should check current official guidance.

What makes YouTube creative work

On YouTube, creative does most of the heavy lifting. Targeting and bidding matter, but a weak video rarely performs however well the campaign is built. The principles we brief to are:

  • Hook early: show the product, the problem or the brand in the opening seconds, before the skip button appears.
  • One message per ad: a single clear benefit lands better than a list of features.
  • Show, do not just tell: product in use, before and after, or a quick demonstration.
  • Design for sound off and on: captions and on-screen text help, especially on mobile and Shorts.
  • Clear call to action: say what to do next and make sure the landing page matches.
  • Several versions: different hooks, lengths and formats, including vertical for Shorts, so testing has something to work with.

Many brands already have usable footage from social media, product shoots or founders talking to camera. We help adapt existing material into ad-ready cuts where possible, and brief new video when it is needed.

Landing pages and the journey after the click

A YouTube viewer is often earlier in the buying journey than someone clicking a search ad. The landing page needs to pick up where the video left off: the same offer, the same visual style and a simple next step. Sending video traffic to a generic homepage usually wastes much of the attention the ad earned. Our landing page design guide covers what we look for.

It also helps to measure the wider effect of video. YouTube campaigns often lift branded searches and direct visits over the following weeks, so we look at those trends alongside the conversions reported in Google Ads.

YouTube versus other video platforms

YouTube is not the only place to run video ads. TikTok and Meta both offer strong video formats, and the right mix depends on your audience, product and creative. YouTube tends to suit searchable categories and longer-consideration purchases, as well as brands that want to reach people on TV screens as well as phones. Our comparison of YouTube Ads and TikTok Ads sets out the differences, and our guide to a TikTok Ads agency for US brands covers that platform in detail.

How remote YouTube Ads management works

US time zones are five to eight hours behind the UK, so calls take place in your morning and campaign work continues while you are offline. A typical arrangement:

  • Access: you own the Google Ads account and YouTube channel and give us manager access; ad spend is billed to you by Google.
  • Kick-off: goals, audiences, products, budgets and existing creative reviewed on a call.
  • Set-up: tracking checked, audiences built, campaigns launched in stages.
  • Testing and optimisation: creative and audience tests run, with weak placements and audiences removed.
  • Reporting: a monthly report with results, what we learned and next steps.

How much a YouTube Ads agency costs

There are two costs: the management fee and your ad spend. Ad spend is paid separately, directly to Google, and stays under your control. Our management retainers are priced in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request:

  • Launch, £350 a month: a focused YouTube campaign, often remarketing plus one prospecting audience.
  • Growth, £750 a month: several campaigns, regular creative testing and combined search and video work.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: larger budgets, multiple products or markets, and video alongside search, Shopping and social.

Video production is not included in management and is quoted separately where needed. Paid campaigns can start producing data within days, but stable, profitable results typically take three to six months of testing and refinement. FAQs

Do you work as a YouTube Ads agency for US brands?

Yes. We are a UK-based studio that manages YouTube Ads remotely for US businesses. We have no US office, and we work through manager access to your Google Ads account, video calls and written reports.

How much should a US brand spend on YouTube Ads?

It depends on your goal, audience size and margins. We usually recommend a test budget large enough to gather meaningful data across a few creative versions, then scaling what works. Ad spend is paid directly to Google.

Do I need professional video for YouTube Ads?

Not always. Clear, authentic footage often works well if the hook and message are strong. We can help adapt existing videos into ad cuts, and brief new production when your current material is not suitable.

Can YouTube Ads drive direct sales?

Yes, especially for ecommerce brands with good tracking, a strong product feed and clear landing pages. YouTube also tends to support sales indirectly by increasing branded searches and return visits.

How do you measure YouTube Ads results?

We track conversions in Google Ads and analytics, tag traffic with UTM parameters and look at view rates, cost per result and changes in branded search and direct traffic over time.

How much does YouTube Ads management cost?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Google. Prices are in pounds, and other currencies are quoted on request.
