A WordPress agency for Germany designs, builds and looks after WordPress websites for German businesses, so they load quickly, stay secure and are easy to update. Prism Works Studio builds and supports WordPress sites for companies across Germany, working remotely from the UK.

Who we build WordPress sites for in Germany

WordPress powers a large share of business websites in Germany, from small local firms to larger companies with many pages. We work with businesses in cities such as Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Frankfurt and Cologne, and with firms in smaller towns that sell nationally. Typical clients include:

  • Service businesses such as consultancies, clinics, studios and trades.
  • B2B companies and manufacturers with detailed product and service pages.
  • Software and tech firms that want a flexible marketing site alongside their product.
  • Content-led businesses with blogs, guides and resource libraries.
  • Small online shops using WooCommerce for a simple catalogue.

Lean builds that stay fast

Many slow WordPress sites suffer from heavy themes, too many plugins and page builders that load more code than needed. We keep builds lean, use the block editor where it suits, and add only the plugins a site truly needs. Our guides on WordPress page builders and the block editor explain the trade-offs.

If you already have a site, a WordPress plugin audit is often the quickest way to find what is slowing it down or creating risk. We also look at hosting, image sizes and caching, which our WordPress hosting guide covers. For hosting, we usually recommend a provider with data centres in the EU, though the final choice is yours.

Security and maintenance

An unmaintained WordPress site can become a target. We set up sensible security from the start: strong admin access, limited user roles, regular updates and reliable backups. Our WordPress security guide explains the basics, and we set user roles so each person has the right level of access.

After launch, we can look after updates and monitoring as part of a retainer, or hand the site over to your team with clear notes so they can manage it themselves.

German language and multilingual sites

Many German businesses need their site in German plus English, and sometimes other languages for neighbouring markets. WordPress handles this well with the right setup. Each language version gets its own address and correct language tags, and visitors can switch easily.

We write English content and build the structure. German text comes from your team or a professional translator, and we check the finished pages for layout, headings, menus and links. German words are often long, so we test designs with real German text early to avoid broken buttons and menus.

Legal pages, consent and accessibility

German websites usually need a legal notice page, a privacy policy and a cookie consent setup. We build these pages into the site structure and make sure analytics and marketing tags respect consent choices. The legal wording and final sign-off rest with you and your own adviser, and official German and EU guidance is the best starting point.

Accessibility is increasingly important for sites selling to consumers in the EU. Our overview of the European Accessibility Act explains what it may mean in general terms. We build with readable contrast, clear forms and keyboard-friendly navigation as standard.

WordPress and SEO

A good WordPress build gives SEO a strong base. We set clean permalinks, logical categories, fast templates and structured data where it fits. If you want ongoing growth, our SEO and GEO services cover technical fixes, content planning and AI search visibility for German and international markets.

How a remote WordPress project works

Germany is one hour ahead of the UK for most of the year, so it is easy to schedule calls. Our process is clear and documented.

  1. Discovery: we agree goals, pages, languages and features.
  2. Sitemap and wireframes: we plan the structure before design starts.
  3. Design: we share designs for feedback and sign-off.
  4. Build: we develop on a private staging site you can review.
  5. Content: we add English copy and your German copy.
  6. Launch and handover: we test, go live and show your team how to edit.

Common WordPress problems we see in Germany

When German businesses ask us to look at an existing WordPress site, the same issues come up again and again. Most are fixable without a full rebuild.

  • Too many plugins, some unused, some outdated and some doing the same job.
  • Heavy themes and page builders that slow every page down.
  • Old PHP versions that hurt speed and security, which is why we check the server setup early.
  • Consent banners that break layouts or block content on mobile.
  • Forms that stop sending emails, so enquiries are lost without anyone noticing.
  • Broken language switching, where German and English pages are not linked properly.

We start with a clear list of problems and priorities, then fix them in order of impact. If the base is too weak, we will say so and explain why a rebuild may cost less in the long run.

WooCommerce for German online shops

WooCommerce turns WordPress into an online shop. It suits smaller catalogues and businesses that want their shop and content in one place. German shops need clear pricing, shipping information, legal pages and a checkout that works smoothly on phones. We build the shop structure and checkout, while the legal text for terms, returns and pricing displays rests with you and your adviser.

For larger catalogues or fast-growing shops, Shopify can be a better fit. We will give you an honest recommendation based on your products, team and plans.

Training your team

A WordPress site should be easy for your team to update. At handover, we show your editors how to add pages, posts and images without breaking the design. We keep editing screens simple and give each person only the access they need.

Moving to WordPress from another platform

Some German businesses come to us from website builders or older systems that have become hard to manage. Moving to WordPress gives more control over pages, speed and search settings. We plan the move carefully: we map every old page to its new address, set up redirects, move content and images, and check that forms and tracking still work. This protects the search visibility you already have and avoids broken links for returning visitors. We test everything on a staging site before the switch, and we check search console data in the weeks after launch to catch any problems early.
