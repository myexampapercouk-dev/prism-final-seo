Why Lyon businesses need a specific SEO plan

Lyon is France's second economic hub after Paris, with a business base that mixes life sciences and pharmaceuticals, chemicals, software, logistics, professional services, gastronomy and tourism. The city also has a strong network of independent shops in areas such as the Presqu'île, Croix-Rousse and Vieux Lyon, and a large student population. That mix creates very different search patterns.

  • Local service businesses such as clinics, trades, salons and restaurants compete mainly in the Google map pack and in searches that include an arrondissement or neighbourhood.
  • B2B and technology firms usually sell across France and Europe, so they need specialist content that ranks for high-intent professional searches rather than city terms.
  • Ecommerce brands based in Lyon need category and product SEO aimed at national searches, and often international ones too.
  • Hospitality and food businesses need accurate listings, menus, opening hours and pages that answer visitor questions in French and English.

A single generic SEO package rarely fits all of these. The first job is to work out which searches actually bring you customers and build the plan around them.

Working in French as a remote UK studio

We write in English. For a Lyon business, that means French content needs a clear workflow. In practice, most clients choose one of three routes:

  1. Your team writes, we optimise. We supply keyword research, page briefs and structure in English, and your team or translator writes the French copy. We then review headings, metadata and internal links.
  2. We draft in English, a translator localises. Useful for technical or B2B pages where the structure matters more than tone. A professional translator adapts the text for French readers rather than translating word for word.
  3. Bilingual sites. If you serve both French and international customers, we plan the site structure, hreflang tags and URL patterns so each language version ranks in the right market.

Whichever route you choose, final French wording comes from your team or a translator, not from us. Our guide to multilingual website design explains how to structure language versions so they do not compete with each other.

Local SEO for Lyon: maps, reviews and neighbourhood searches

Local search matters most for businesses that serve people in person. Lyon searchers often include the arrondissement, a neighbourhood or a nearby commune such as Villeurbanne, Vénissieux, Caluire-et-Cuire or Écully in their searches. A good local plan covers:

  • A complete Google Business Profile with the right primary category, services, photos, opening hours and regular posts.
  • Consistent business name, address and phone details across directories, including established French listings such as PagesJaunes.
  • A simple, compliant process for asking customers for reviews and replying to them.
  • Location or service-area pages that describe real coverage rather than thin copies with the town name swapped.

Our Google Business Profile guide is written for UK firms, but the same principles apply to profiles in France.

International SEO for Lyon exporters

Lyon sits close to Switzerland and Italy and has strong rail links to Paris, Geneva and Turin, so many local firms sell beyond France. If you want to rank in several countries, international SEO needs deliberate planning: choosing between country folders and separate domains, setting hreflang correctly, deciding which pages to localise and building authority in each market. We cover the options in our article on international SEO.

For ecommerce exporters, the same thinking applies to product feeds, shipping pages and returns information for each country. A fast, technically clean store makes international SEO far easier, and our notes on Core Web Vitals explain why speed and stability matter for rankings.

AI search and generative results

Searchers in France increasingly see AI-generated summaries above traditional results, and many also ask questions in AI assistants. We treat this as part of SEO rather than a separate service. Our AI-powered approach focuses on clear answer-first content, well-structured FAQs, accurate structured data and consistent facts about your business across the web. These are the signals that help AI tools understand and cite a business, and they also improve standard rankings.

Combining SEO with paid search

Because organic rankings take time, many Lyon firms run paid search alongside SEO. Google Ads can capture demand immediately while content and links build. The keyword data from the ads account also shows which terms convert, which helps prioritise SEO pages.   • Month two: technical fixes, on-page updates to key service or category pages, and Google Business Profile improvements.

Regulation and compliance

French and EU rules on cookies, consent, data protection and advertising apply to your website and marketing. We follow good practice, such as using a proper consent banner and keeping tracking aligned with consent choices, but we are not lawyers. For legal questions, check official guidance from the CNIL or speak to your own adviser. In regulated sectors such as health or finance, compliance approval for content rests with your team.
