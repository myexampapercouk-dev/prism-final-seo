LinkedIn Ads Agency for Calgary Businesses: Reaching Decision-Makers

LinkedIn Ads for Calgary businesses: targeting decision-makers in energy, tech and services, lead forms and tracking, run remotely by a UK studio on retainer.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 28 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • LinkedIn Ads suit Calgary firms that sell to other businesses, because you can target by job title, seniority, industry and company size rather than by interests.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Calgary or Canada; retainers run from £350 to £1,500 a month, ad spend is separate and Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request.
  • Clicks on LinkedIn are usually expensive, so success depends on a tight audience, a useful offer and conversion tracking that follows leads into your sales process.

A LinkedIn Ads agency in Calgary helps businesses that sell to other businesses put their message in front of the right decision-makers, using targeting by job title, seniority, industry and company size. For firms in energy services, engineering, technology, logistics, construction and professional services, LinkedIn is often the most direct paid route to the people who sign contracts.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Calgary or anywhere in Canada. We plan, build and manage LinkedIn campaigns through video calls, shared documents and written reports. We write in English, and any French or other language versions of ads come from your team or a translator.

Why LinkedIn suits the Calgary market

Calgary has long been home to head offices and service firms in the energy sector, and its economy now also includes a growing technology scene, agriculture and food businesses, logistics and transport, financial services and a wide range of consultancies. Many of these firms sell high-value products or services to a small number of buyers, often over long sales cycles.

That is exactly where LinkedIn tends to work. Instead of reaching everyone in the city, you can show ads only to operations managers at mid-sized oil and gas service companies, IT directors at firms with more than two hundred staff, or founders of software start-ups across Alberta. The audience is smaller, but it is the right one.

LinkedIn Ads agency Calgary: what we set up

A LinkedIn programme usually has a few core parts:

  • Audience design. We define who buys, who influences the decision and who signs off, then build audiences around job function, seniority, industry, company size and location.
  • Offer and message. We work with you on something genuinely useful to offer, such as a guide, a benchmark checklist, a consultation or a webinar run by your team.
  • Ad formats. We choose between single image ads, document ads, conversation ads, text ads and lead gen forms based on the goal.
  • Tracking. We install the Insight Tag, set up conversion events and make sure leads flow into your CRM or inbox so you can follow them up.
  • Testing and reporting. We test audiences and messages, cut what does not work, and send plain written reports.

Choosing the right ad format

Each LinkedIn format fits a different stage of the buying journey. The table below gives a simple guide.

Budgets and costs

LinkedIn clicks are usually more expensive than clicks on Meta or Google, because the targeting is so specific. That is fine when one new client is worth a lot, but it means wasted spend adds up quickly. We keep audiences focused, cap spend while testing, and look closely at cost per qualified lead rather than cost per click.

Our fees are in pounds sterling, and Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request. Ad spend is paid separately, directly to LinkedIn.

  • Launch, £350 a month: a single focused campaign for one audience.
  • Growth, £750 a month: LinkedIn plus one other channel, or several LinkedIn audiences.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: multi-channel paid media with ongoing testing and reporting.

How LinkedIn fits with other channels

LinkedIn rarely works best alone. Decision-makers who see your ads will often search for your firm by name, visit your website and check your case pages before getting in touch. That makes the rest of your marketing important:

  1. Search. Google Ads captures people who already know they need a service. Our guide to Google Ads in Calgary covers that side.
  2. Organic visibility. SEO helps when buyers research providers. See our guide to SEO in Calgary.
  3. Website. A clear, fast site with service pages and simple contact routes turns ad clicks into enquiries. Our post on web design in Calgary explains what that looks like.
  4. Retargeting on other platforms. Some firms add Meta retargeting to stay visible at lower cost, covered in our Meta Ads guide for Calgary.

Using AI tools sensibly

We use AI-powered tools to speed up research, draft ad variations for testing and spot patterns in campaign data. A person still reviews every audience, message and report before anything goes live. Our guide to AI-powered marketing in Canada explains how we approach this.

Privacy, consent and regulated sectors

Lead gen forms and website tracking collect personal information, so your privacy policy, consent wording and follow-up emails need to respect Canadian privacy and anti-spam laws. We build consent into forms and keep tracking transparent, but we are not legal advisers. Firms in regulated areas such as financial services should check official guidance and their own adviser, and final compliance approval rests with you.

What to expect in the first three months

In the first month, we agree goals, build audiences, set up tracking and launch a small test. In the second month, we review which audiences and messages produce qualified leads, cut what is not working and expand what is. By the third month, you should have a clear view of cost per lead and lead quality, and a plan for scaling. Paid campaigns typically take three to six months to settle into steady results, especially with long sales cycles.

Building audiences that are not too narrow

One of the most common problems with LinkedIn campaigns is an audience that looks perfect on paper but is too small to deliver. If you stack job title, seniority, industry, company size and a single city together, you can end up with a few hundred people and very high costs. We usually start a little broader, for example by job function and seniority across Alberta, and then narrow down once we can see which segments respond.

We also use matched audiences where they help. If you have a list of target accounts, we can upload company names to focus spend on the firms you most want to win. Website retargeting reaches people who have already visited key service pages. Both approaches need care with privacy and consent, and both work best when combined with a clear offer.

Turning LinkedIn leads into clients

A lead from LinkedIn is the start of a conversation, not a finished sale. Many business buyers download a guide or request a call well before they are ready to commit. The firms that get the most from the platform follow up quickly, qualify leads politely and keep in touch with useful content over the following months.

We help by making sure leads arrive in the right place, by tagging them by campaign and audience, and by reporting on which sources lead to real sales conversations. That feedback loop is what lets us shift budget towards the audiences that matter, rather than the ones that simply produce the cheapest form fills.

FAQs

What does a LinkedIn Ads agency in Calgary do?

A LinkedIn Ads agency plans, builds and manages campaigns that reach business decision-makers by job title, industry and company size. That includes audience design, ad copy, lead forms, tracking and regular reporting.

Do you have an office in Calgary?

No, we have no office in Calgary or anywhere in Canada. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely through video calls, shared documents and written reports.

Are LinkedIn Ads worth it for small businesses?

LinkedIn Ads are worth it when you sell to businesses and one new client is worth a lot. If you sell low-cost products to the general public, Meta or Google usually give better value.

How much should we spend on LinkedIn Ads?

Spend depends on audience size, goals and the value of a new client, and it is paid separately to LinkedIn. We usually start with a modest test budget and scale only once lead quality is clear.

Can you make video ads for LinkedIn?

No, we do not film or edit video. Your team or a separate provider supplies any footage, and we plan, target and manage the campaigns that use it.

How long until LinkedIn campaigns bring leads?

Many campaigns bring first leads within weeks, but steady, qualified results typically take three to six months. Long sales cycles mean it can take longer to see which leads become clients.
