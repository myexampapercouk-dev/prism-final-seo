Shopify Gift Wrapping and Order Notes: How to Offer Gift Options

How to offer gift wrapping, gift messages and order notes on a Shopify store, with practical setup options, fulfilment tips and mistakes to avoid at checkout.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 1 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Shopify does not include a dedicated gift wrapping feature, but you can offer it as a product, a variant option or through cart attributes.
  • Cart notes and custom fields let customers leave gift messages and instructions, as long as your team actually reads them.
  • Whatever approach you choose, make the option clear, price it transparently and build it into your fulfilment process.

Gift options can make a real difference to how customers feel about buying from you, especially for stores selling products people buy for others. Being able to add gift wrapping, include a handwritten message or leave delivery instructions can turn a simple purchase into something more personal.

Shopify does not have a single built-in gift wrapping feature, but there are several ways to offer gift options using the tools already in the platform and your theme. This guide covers the main approaches, how to handle order notes and how to make sure gift requests are fulfilled correctly.

What gift options can you offer?

Before choosing how to set things up, decide what you actually want to offer. Common options include:

  • Gift wrapping. Wrapping items in paper, tissue or a branded box.
  • Gift messages. A short note printed or handwritten and included in the parcel.
  • Hiding prices. Leaving out a priced receipt so the recipient does not see what was paid.
  • Delivery instructions. Notes about where to leave a parcel or when it is needed.
  • Gift receipts. A document allowing the recipient to return or exchange items.

Each option needs a way for customers to request it and a way for your team to see and act on it. Keep the list short and only offer what you can deliver reliably.

Option one: gift wrapping as a product

The simplest approach is to create a separate product called something like "Gift wrapping" and let customers add it to their cart. You can give it an image showing what the wrapping looks like, a clear description and a price, or set it to free if you prefer.

This method has some advantages:

  • It works with any theme and requires no custom code.
  • The charge appears clearly on the order and in your reports.
  • You can track it as a product without inventory, or track stock of wrapping materials if you want.

The main drawback is that it is not always clear which items should be wrapped if the order contains several products. Pair it with a note field so customers can explain, or set a clear policy such as wrapping all items together.

You may want to hide this product from collections and search so it only appears where it is relevant, such as in the cart or on product pages. Check your theme settings for options to do this.

Option two: gift wrapping as a variant or product option

For stores where each item might be wrapped individually, you can add a variant option such as "Gift wrapped: yes or no" to products. Each variant can have its own price, so the wrapped version costs a little more if you charge for it.

This keeps the wrapping choice tied to the specific item. However, it can complicate your product data, particularly if products already have several variants for size or colour, because each combination multiplies the number of variants. It can also affect inventory tracking, since the wrapped and unwrapped versions are treated as separate variants.

This approach suits stores with a small number of simple products more than those with large catalogues.

Option three: cart attributes and custom fields

Shopify themes can collect extra information in the cart using cart attributes. These are custom fields such as a checkbox for "This is a gift" or a text box for a gift message. The information is saved with the order and appears in the order details in the admin.

Many themes include a cart note field by default, which you can enable in the theme editor. Adding more specific fields, such as a gift message box with a character limit, usually requires editing the theme code. Our guide to Shopify theme customisation explains how theme changes work and when to involve a developer.

Cart attributes do not add a charge by themselves. If you want to charge for gift wrapping, combine a cart attribute for the message with a gift wrapping product for the fee.

Line item properties are a related feature. They let customers add details to a specific product in their basket, such as a personalised message for one item. Like cart attributes, they appear in the order details and usually need theme changes to add.

Using the cart note for order notes

The cart note is a simple free-text box that customers can fill in before checkout. It is useful for general instructions, gift messages or questions. The note appears on the order in the admin and can be included on packing slips.

To make cart notes work well:

  • Give the field a clear label, such as "Add a gift message or delivery note".
  • Explain any limits, such as a maximum length for printed messages.
  • Make sure the field is visible on both the cart page and any slide-out cart drawer your theme uses.
  • Avoid asking customers to put essential information only in the note, because it is easy to miss.

Customers should not be asked to enter sensitive personal information, such as health details or payment information, in a free-text note field.

Making sure gift requests reach fulfilment

Gift options only work if the person packing the order sees them. This is where many stores run into trouble.

Packing slips

Shopify lets you customise packing slip templates in your shipping settings. You can edit the template so it shows cart notes, cart attributes and line item properties clearly, and so it leaves out prices for gift orders. Test the template with a sample order to check everything appears as expected.

Order tags and filters

Tagging gift orders makes them easy to spot. Your team can tag them manually, or a developer can help set up automation using Shopify's built-in workflow tools if available on your plan. Saved filters in the Orders area can then show all orders marked as gifts.

Notifications

Order confirmation and shipping emails can be edited to show the gift message, so the customer can check it was recorded correctly. Be careful, though: if a gift is being sent directly to the recipient, make sure shipping notifications go to the buyer rather than revealing details to the recipient.

Gift messages and printed notes

If you print or handwrite gift messages, decide how you will handle long or unusual messages. A character limit helps keep notes to a size that fits your card. Clearly explain what customers can expect, such as whether the message will be handwritten, printed or included as a separate card.

It is also wise to have a simple process for checking messages before printing, in case of typos or anything inappropriate. If a message cannot be included as written, contact the customer rather than changing it yourself.

Gift receipts and returns

Recipients may want to return or exchange a gift. Make your returns policy clear about whether gift recipients can return items and whether refunds go back to the original buyer or as store credit. Consumer rights rules in the UK may affect what you have to offer, especially for online purchases, so check the current guidance or take advice when writing your policy.

Gift cards are another way to help customers buy for others. Our guide to ecommerce gift cards and vouchers covers how they work and what to think about.

Presenting gift options on your store

Gift options should be easy to find but not distracting. Consider:

  • Mentioning gift wrapping on product pages for items that are popular as gifts.
  • Adding a clear option in the cart, close to the checkout button.
  • Showing an image of the wrapping or packaging so customers know what to expect.
  • Explaining the cost upfront rather than surprising customers later.
  • Including gift information on your delivery or FAQ page.

FAQs

Does Shopify have built-in gift wrapping?

Shopify does not have a dedicated gift wrapping feature. Most stores offer it as a product, a variant option or through custom cart fields set up in the theme.

Where do customers' order notes appear?

Cart notes and cart attributes appear in the order details in the Shopify admin. They can also be added to packing slips and notification emails by editing the relevant templates.

Can I hide prices on a gift order?

You can edit your packing slip template to leave out prices, either for all orders or for orders marked as gifts. Test it carefully before relying on it.

Can customers add a different message to each item?

Yes, using line item properties, which let customers enter details for individual products. These usually require theme changes to set up.

Should I charge for gift wrapping?

That is a business decision. Some stores include it free as a service, while others charge to cover materials and time. Whatever you choose, make the price clear before checkout.
