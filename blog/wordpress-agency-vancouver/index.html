When WordPress is the right choice

WordPress is a strong choice when content is central to how you win customers. Its editor makes it easy to publish blog posts, case pages, service pages and resources, and its huge plugin ecosystem covers most common needs. It also gives you full ownership of your site and data, and you can host it with almost any provider.

WordPress is not always the best fit. If you mainly sell products online and want a hosted platform that handles payments, security and updates for you, Shopify may be simpler. Our guide to Shopify in Vancouver compares the options. If you only need a small brochure site and rarely change it, a lighter build can also work. We help you choose rather than defaulting to one platform.

What a WordPress project looks like

A typical small WordPress website takes 3 to 5 weeks from kick-off to launch. Larger sites and WooCommerce stores take longer, usually 5 to 12 weeks, depending on the number of products, integrations and content.

  1. Discovery: we agree goals, audiences, pages and the actions you want visitors to take.
  2. Structure: we plan the sitemap, page layouts and the keywords each page should target.
  3. Design: we design key pages for mobile first, then larger screens.
  4. Build: we develop the site on a staging copy using a lightweight theme and a short list of trusted plugins.
  5. Content: we enter your content and write or edit English copy where agreed.
  6. Testing and launch: we test forms, speed, accessibility basics and redirects, then go live.
  7. Training: we show your team how to edit pages and publish posts safely.

Speed, hosting and plugins

Many slow WordPress sites suffer from the same problems: heavy page builders, too many plugins, oversized images and cheap shared hosting. We keep builds lean, choose plugins carefully, compress and lazy-load images, and recommend hosting that suits your traffic. Where your visitors are mainly in Western Canada, hosting or a content delivery network with nearby servers can help pages load faster.

Speed matters for both visitors and search engines. A faster site usually means more people stay, read and enquire, and it supports your SEO in Vancouver efforts.

Bilingual and multilingual WordPress sites

Most Vancouver businesses publish in English, but some also serve French-speaking customers in other provinces or customers who speak other languages. WordPress can handle multilingual sites through well-known plugins that manage translated pages and language tags.

We write in English. French or other language content should be written by your own team or a professional translator. We set up the structure, language switcher, hreflang tags and URLs so each version is indexed correctly. Our WordPress agency Canada guide covers national considerations in more depth.

Privacy, accessibility and Canadian rules

A business website collects data through contact forms, analytics, cookies and newsletter sign-ups. At a general level, PIPEDA covers how many private organisations in Canada handle personal information, and British Columbia has its own private sector privacy law. CASL applies to commercial electronic messages such as marketing emails, so consent for newsletters matters. Quebec's Law 25 adds requirements for businesses that handle personal information of people in Quebec.

These notes are general. Please check official guidance or ask your own legal adviser what applies to you. We build sensible foundations, such as clear consent checkboxes, a privacy policy page you supply, and cookie consent tools, and we follow accessibility good practice such as readable contrast, headings in order, alt text and keyboard-friendly navigation.

Ongoing care after launch

WordPress sites need regular attention. Core, theme and plugin updates fix security issues and bugs, backups protect against mistakes, and uptime monitoring alerts you if something breaks. We can handle this as part of a retainer, or train your team to manage it safely, with a clear checklist for each month.

How to choose a WordPress agency in Vancouver

  • Ask about plugins: fewer, well-maintained plugins usually mean fewer problems.
  • Ask about page builders: heavy builders can slow sites and make them hard to change later.
  • Ask who owns what: you should own the domain, hosting and admin access.
  • Ask about SEO during rebuilds: redirects and metadata protect existing rankings.
  • Ask about aftercare: updates and backups should be planned, not left to chance.

FAQs

What does a WordPress agency in Vancouver do?

A WordPress agency in Vancouver plans, designs, builds and maintains WordPress websites, including WooCommerce stores, speed fixes and SEO setup. We provide this remotely from the UK, with no local office.

WordPress suits content-led sites and businesses that want full control. Shopify suits product-led stores that prefer a hosted platform. We help you choose based on your needs.
