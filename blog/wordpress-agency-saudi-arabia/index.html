Why WordPress suits many Saudi businesses

WordPress powers a large share of the world's websites, which means a big ecosystem of themes, plugins, hosting options and developers. For Saudi businesses that brings several practical benefits.

  • Ownership. You own the site and its content and can move hosting or developers if you need to.
  • Editing. Your team can update pages, news and services through a familiar editor.
  • Arabic support. WordPress has mature right-to-left support, and the admin area itself can be used in Arabic.
  • SEO. Clean URLs, flexible page structures and good plugins make it a solid base for search.
  • Growth. WooCommerce, booking tools, membership features and integrations can be added later.

WordPress is not the right answer for everyone. Pure online shops with large catalogues sometimes do better on Shopify, and our Shopify vs WooCommerce comparison explains when each platform makes sense. If you are selling online in the Kingdom, our guide to Shopify in Saudi Arabia covers the alternative.

WordPress agency Saudi Arabia: our process

A typical WordPress project with us follows these stages.

  1. Discovery. We agree goals, audiences, pages, languages and any integrations such as booking, CRM or payment tools.
  2. Structure. We plan the sitemap, page templates and URL structure for both Arabic and English.
  3. Design. We design key pages for desktop and mobile, with right-to-left layouts checked carefully for Arabic.
  4. Build. We build on a staging site using a lightweight theme and a small, well-chosen set of plugins.
  5. Content. We write English content and load Arabic content supplied by your team or a translator.
  6. Testing. We test speed, forms, devices, browsers, accessibility basics and both language versions.
  7. Launch and handover. We set up redirects, analytics and Search Console, then train your team to edit the site.

A small business website usually takes 3 to 5 weeks. An online shop usually takes 5 to 12 weeks, depending on the number of products, payment and delivery setup, and how quickly content arrives.

Arabic and English on one WordPress site

Bilingual sites are where many WordPress projects in Saudi Arabia go wrong. Common problems include Arabic pages with broken right-to-left layouts, mixed-direction text that displays in the wrong order, missing translations of menus and forms, and both language versions competing in search.

We set up a proper multilingual structure, usually with separate language folders, translated menus and forms, and hreflang tags so Google shows each visitor the right version. We choose Arabic web fonts that are readable on mobile and check every template in both directions. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites explains the structure in more detail.

Prism writes in English. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend a fluent speaker on your side checks every page before launch.

WooCommerce for Saudi online shops

WooCommerce turns WordPress into an online shop. It suits businesses that want full control, need custom product options or already have a content-heavy WordPress site. For the Saudi market, the main questions are payments, delivery and tax.

Speed and hosting

Most Saudi visitors browse on phones, and a slow site loses customers and rankings. WordPress sites become slow when they rely on heavy page builders, dozens of plugins, oversized images or cheap shared hosting. We build lean, use a caching layer and optimised images, and recommend hosting with good performance for visitors in the Gulf. Our guides to WordPress hosting and Core Web Vitals explain what we look for.

Security and maintenance

WordPress is secure when it is maintained and vulnerable when it is neglected. Outdated plugins, weak passwords and abandoned themes are the most common causes of hacked sites. We keep core, themes and plugins updated, use security hardening, run regular backups stored off the server, and monitor uptime. Our WordPress security guide lists the measures we use.

Personal data collected through forms and shops should be handled carefully. Saudi Arabia has its own personal data protection law. We are not legal advisers, so check official guidance and ask your compliance team or adviser to confirm your privacy notices and data handling.

SEO built into the site

A WordPress site should be ready for search on launch day: clear page titles, logical headings, fast pages, structured data, a clean sitemap and properly linked Arabic and English versions. We set these foundations as standard. Ongoing SEO, such as keyword research, content and local signals, is available on a monthly retainer. Our guide to SEO in Riyadh explains that work for Saudi businesses, and our WordPress guide for Dubai covers the neighbouring UAE market.

Working with a remote WordPress team

Because we are UK-based and have no office in Saudi Arabia, we run projects through scheduled video calls, a shared project plan and a staging site you can review at any stage. Feedback is collected in one place so nothing is lost between Arabic and English versions. We plan calls around the Saudi working week and the time difference, and we agree launch dates that avoid busy periods such as the last days of Ramadan or major sales events.

Your domain, hosting and WordPress admin accounts stay in your name. We use our own user account with the access we need, and at handover we document the plugins, settings and processes so your team or any future developer can continue confidently. That way you are never locked in to us.

Redesigning an existing WordPress site

Many Saudi businesses already have a WordPress site that has become slow, outdated or hard to edit. A redesign can often keep the content and rankings that work while fixing structure, speed and design. We map existing URLs, set up redirects and compare search performance before and after launch so nothing valuable is lost. In some cases a careful clean-up of plugins and the theme is enough, and we will say so if a full rebuild is not needed.

FAQs

What does a WordPress agency in Saudi Arabia do?

It designs, builds, secures and maintains WordPress websites, often bilingual, and may add WooCommerce for online selling. We provide this remotely from the UK.

Do you have an office in Saudi Arabia?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK, has no office in Saudi Arabia and works remotely through calls, shared documents and staging sites.

Can WordPress handle Arabic properly?

Yes, when built carefully. WordPress supports right-to-left layouts, and we test every template in Arabic. Arabic content is written with your team or a translator.

Should we choose WordPress or Shopify?

WordPress suits content-led sites and shops needing flexibility. Shopify suits businesses that mainly sell products and want less technical upkeep. We help you choose.

How long does a WordPress website take?

A small website usually takes 3 to 5 weeks. An online shop with WooCommerce usually takes 5 to 12 weeks, depending on products and content.

Can you look after our site after launch?

Yes. Our retainers start at £350 per month and cover updates, backups, security and improvements, with SEO available on larger plans.
