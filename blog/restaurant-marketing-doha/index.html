Restaurant Marketing in Doha: Websites, Local SEO and Social Ads for Restaurants and Cafes

Restaurant marketing in Doha means making sure hungry diners find you, like what they see and book a table or order quickly. Prism Works Studio builds restaurant websites and runs local SEO and social ad campaigns for restaurants, cafes and dessert shops in Doha, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in Doha or anywhere in Qatar, and we work through video calls, shared previews and written reports. We write in English; Arabic menus, pages and ad copy are written with your team or a translator, and we build everything around that copy.

Who we work with in Doha

Doha's dining scene runs from family restaurants and shisha lounges to specialty coffee, fine dining and food halls in malls and waterfront districts. Diners include Qatari families, a large expatriate population, office workers and visitors. We support:

  • Independent restaurants that want more bookings and fewer quiet nights.
  • Cafes and specialty coffee shops building a regular local crowd.
  • Dessert, bakery and sweet shops selling for events and gifting.
  • Small restaurant groups with several branches that need one clear website.
  • Catering and private dining businesses chasing corporate and event orders.

A restaurant website that people actually use

Many diners check a restaurant's website for three things: the menu, the location and how to book or order. If those take more than a few seconds to find, they move on. We build restaurant sites that put those three things first:

  • Menus written as real web text, not only PDFs or images, so they load fast and can rank in search.
  • One page for each branch with address, map, hours, parking notes and phone or WhatsApp button.
  • Clear links to your booking system or delivery apps, so the next step is one tap away.
  • Pages for private dining, events, catering and gift orders, which often bring larger bookings.
  • Photo galleries that load quickly on mobile.

Our guide to restaurant website design goes into more detail on layouts and features. Read about our build process on the website and ecommerce development page.

Local SEO and Google Maps

Most restaurant searches are local: "breakfast near me", "best burger in Doha", "seafood restaurant West Bay" or "cafe in The Pearl". Google Maps results often show above everything else, so your Google Business Profile matters as much as your website. Our local SEO work covers:

  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile for each branch, with the right categories, hours, menu link and photos.
  • Updates for Ramadan hours, public holidays and special events, so diners do not arrive to closed doors.
  • A simple routine for asking happy guests for reviews and replying to every review politely.
  • Website pages that match each branch and area, so Google connects your profile and site.
  • Menu and dish content that answers what diners search for.

Our Google Business Profile guide explains the setup step by step, and the SEO and GEO service page covers how we run SEO each month. For broader search work in Qatar, see our page on SEO in Qatar.

Social ads for Doha diners

Food is visual, and Doha diners discover new places on Instagram and TikTok. Paid social ads let you reach people near your branches with new menus, seasonal offers, Ramadan iftar and suhoor menus, and events. We plan campaigns, write copy, set targeting and test different versions. We do not film or edit video, so your team or a content creator supplies the photos and clips, and we turn them into well-run campaigns.

Our page on Meta ads in Qatar explains how we set up Facebook and Instagram campaigns for local businesses. Ad spend is paid separately, straight to the platforms.

Arabic and English for Doha restaurants

Many Doha diners prefer Arabic, while many others read English first. A bilingual menu and website makes every guest feel welcome. We write in English, and your team or a professional translator writes the Arabic. We then build right-to-left pages, a simple language switcher and separate page addresses for each language. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website explains how it works.

Planning around the year

Restaurant demand in Doha shifts through the year. The cooler months bring outdoor dining and busy weekends. Ramadan changes opening hours and eating patterns, with strong demand for iftar and suhoor. Eid, school holidays, national celebrations and major sports or cultural events also change footfall. We build a simple calendar with your team so that menus, offers, profile updates and ads go live before each busy period, and so that tone during Ramadan is respectful and right for your brand. Your team knows your guests best, and we plan around that knowledge.

Common mistakes we fix

Restaurants often lose diners for simple reasons. The menu is a heavy PDF that will not open on a phone. Hours on Google are wrong during Ramadan. Each branch shares one vague listing. The website has no booking link, or the link is broken. Reviews go unanswered for months. Ads promote a general brand message with no clear offer. We fix these basics first because they cost little and protect every other channel.

What to measure each month

Restaurant marketing should be judged by what happens at the door, not only by likes. Each month we report on the numbers that matter to you and explain them in plain language:

  • Calls, direction requests and website clicks from each Google Business Profile.
  • Clicks on booking, delivery and WhatsApp buttons on your website.
  • Catering and private dining enquiries from forms and ads.
  • Reach, clicks and cost for each social campaign.
  • New reviews and the replies sent.

Your team adds what only it can see, such as covers, order volumes and feedback from staff, so we can decide together where to spend next month.

Working with delivery apps

Delivery apps bring many orders in Doha, but they also take commission and control the customer relationship. Your own website and Google profile give diners a direct route to you. We link clearly to the delivery apps you use, while also promoting direct bookings, pickup orders, catering and private dining, which you keep fully in your own hands. Over time, that balance can help protect your margins and build a loyal local following.

FAQs

What does restaurant marketing in Doha include?

It usually includes a fast restaurant website, local SEO and Google Business Profile work, review management, and social ads on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Google Ads can also help with catering and private dining enquiries.

Do you have an office in Doha?

No. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Doha or Qatar and work through video calls, shared previews and written reports.

Can you translate our menu into Arabic?

We write in English. Arabic menus and pages are written by your team or a professional translator, and we build the Arabic pages and right-to-left layout around that copy.

Do you make food videos for TikTok?

No. We do not film or edit video. Your team or a content creator supplies clips, and we plan, write, target and test the TikTok and Meta campaigns.

How much does a restaurant website cost?

Smaller restaurant websites cost £1,000 to £2,000, and sites with online shops or gift orders cost £1,500 to £3,500. Riyal pricing is quoted on request.

How can my restaurant show up on Google Maps in Doha?

Keep a complete Google Business Profile for each branch with correct categories, hours, photos and a menu link, collect and reply to reviews, and link each profile to a matching branch page on your website.
