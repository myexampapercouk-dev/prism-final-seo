Why Krakow businesses use Google Ads

Krakow has one of the most varied economies in Central Europe. It is home to a large shared services and IT sector, many software houses, universities and research centres, a huge tourism and hospitality industry around the Old Town and Kazimierz, and a growing number of ecommerce and local service businesses. Google Ads suits many of them.

  • Tourism and hospitality businesses can reach visitors who are planning trips and searching for tours, accommodation and restaurants.
  • Local services such as clinics, language schools, trades and fitness studios can target searches in specific districts and nearby towns.
  • Software houses and B2B firms can reach buyers in Western Europe, the UK and North America searching for development or outsourcing partners.
  • Online stores can use Shopping and Performance Max campaigns to put products in front of buyers across Poland and the EU.

Polish-language ads and landing pages

Most Krakow customers search in Polish, and Polish grammar changes word endings depending on context, so ad copy and keywords need a native touch. We write in English. Polish ad text and landing page copy come from your team or a professional translator, and we handle the structure, keyword grouping, extensions and testing around it.

For tourism businesses, English, German and other language campaigns often matter as much as Polish, because many visitors search before they arrive. We set up separate campaigns by language so budgets and messaging stay clear.

Ads only work as well as the pages they send people to. If your landing pages are slow or do not match the search, costs rise. Our article on landing page design covers what a strong paid search landing page needs.

Google Ads for Krakow ecommerce and Allegro sellers

Many Polish online sellers list on Allegro as well as running their own store. Google Ads and Shopping campaigns can drive traffic to your own site, where margins are usually better and you own the customer relationship. We often see the best results when both channels are planned together. We also offer marketplace SEO for Allegro listings, covering titles, attributes, images and descriptions.

For Shopping campaigns, a clean product feed is essential. We review feed titles, product categories, images and pricing so products appear for the right searches. Our ecommerce sector page explains how we combine store builds, SEO and paid campaigns.

Reaching international buyers from Krakow

Krakow's software houses and outsourcing firms often sell to companies in the UK, Germany, the Nordics and the USA. Google Ads can put your services in front of decision-makers searching for development partners. These searches are competitive and expensive, so tight targeting matters: exact keyword themes, strong negatives, careful country and language targeting and landing pages that show real capabilities. LinkedIn Ads can work alongside Google Ads for account-based targeting, and we manage both.

Tracking, consent and data

Conversion tracking in Poland and the wider EU must respect user consent. We set up a consent banner and Google consent mode so tags respond to visitor choices, and we configure GA4 and Google Ads conversions for calls, forms, bookings and purchases. For legal questions on data protection or advertising rules, check guidance from the Polish data protection authority or speak to your own adviser. In regulated sectors such as health or finance, compliance approval for ads rests with your team.

Google Ads and SEO together

Paid search can bring traffic from the first week, while SEO usually takes 3 to 6 months to show steady movement. Many Krakow firms run both: ads capture demand now, and search term data shows which keywords convert, which helps plan SEO content. Our paid media service covers Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn Ads, and our SEO and GEO service covers organic search and AI-powered visibility.

Structuring a Google Ads account for Krakow

A clear account structure makes campaigns easier to manage and easier to understand in reports. We usually separate campaigns by goal, language and location. A Krakow clinic, for example, might have one campaign for core treatments in Polish targeting the city and surrounding towns such as Wieliczka and Skawina, a separate brand campaign protecting its name, and a remarketing campaign for people who visited but did not book.

A tour operator might split campaigns by language and tour type, with separate budgets for English, German and Polish searches. A software house might run campaigns by service, such as web development or mobile apps, targeted only at the countries where it wants clients. Keeping these separate means budgets go where they are intended and results are easy to read.

Within each campaign, we group keywords into tight themes so ads and landing pages match the search closely. This usually improves relevance, which can help both click-through rates and costs over time.

Avoiding wasted spend

The most common problem we see in inherited accounts is spend on irrelevant searches. Broad keywords without negatives can trigger ads for job seekers, students, free information or unrelated products. We review search term reports regularly and build negative keyword lists in every language the account targets.

Other common sources of waste include ads running at times when no one can answer the phone, location settings that include people merely interested in Krakow rather than in or near it, and automated campaigns running without good conversion data. We check each of these during the audit and fix them before increasing budgets.

Bidding and budgets

Google Ads offers automated bid strategies that aim for conversions or a target cost per acquisition. These work well once an account has reliable conversion data, but they can struggle in the early weeks. We usually start with simpler strategies, gather data, then move to automated bidding when there is enough to learn from. Budgets are paced through the month so spend does not run out early or sit unused.

We discuss budget levels honestly. If a budget is too small to compete for the searches you want, we will say so and suggest a narrower focus rather than spreading spend thinly.
