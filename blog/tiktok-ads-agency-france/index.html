Creative: who makes the videos?

Prism does not film or edit video, and we do not offer video production. TikTok creative comes from your own team, from creators you work with, or from a production partner you choose. What we do is help that creative succeed:

  • We write clear creative briefs with hooks, angles and calls to action.
  • We review existing footage and suggest which clips to test.
  • We test variations to learn which hooks and messages work for French audiences.
  • We share what the data shows, so the next round of videos is stronger.

Prism writes in English. French scripts, captions and on-screen text come from your team, your creators or a translator. Ads that sound natural in French matter a lot on a platform built around authentic content.

Tracking, pixels and consent in France

TikTok campaigns rely on the pixel and conversion events to find buyers. We set up the TikTok pixel and key events such as add to cart, checkout and purchase, and connect your product catalogue if you run a shop. Our TikTok pixel setup guide explains the steps.

France takes privacy seriously, and the French data protection authority, the CNIL, publishes guidance on cookies and tracking. These notes are general information, not legal advice. Please check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

  • GDPR. Personal data collected through ads, pixels and forms is covered by the GDPR.
  • Cookie consent. EU ePrivacy rules, applied in France through national law, generally require consent before non-essential cookies and trackers such as advertising pixels are set. We set up tracking so it respects the choices your banner records. See our guide to cookie banner design.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied to many consumer-facing digital products and services in the EU since June 2025, including many online shops. Microenterprises that provide services are generally exempt. The usual technical reference is EN 301 549, which points to WCAG at level AA. Our guide to the European Accessibility Act for websites covers the basics.

Landing pages and your online shop

TikTok users move fast. If your landing page is slow, confusing or not in French, many will leave before buying. We review your product and landing pages and recommend changes, such as faster loading, clearer prices in euros, simple delivery information and an easy checkout on mobile.

If you sell on Shopify, we can help improve your store too. See our guide to Shopify conversion rate optimisation. If you are expanding into France from another country, our guide to selling internationally from an online store covers pricing, delivery and language.

Working with French creators

Many brands in France get their best TikTok results from content made by creators rather than polished studio adverts. Creator videos tend to feel more natural in the feed, and they show your product being used by real people. If you work with creators, we can help in a few practical ways:

  • Briefs. We write clear English briefs covering the product, key messages, hooks to try and what to avoid. Your team or a translator can adapt them into French.
  • Usage rights. We remind you to agree in writing how and for how long creator content can be used in paid ads. The agreement itself is between you and the creator.
  • Spark Ads. Where it fits, we can run ads from a creator's own post with their permission, which often keeps the native look and the social proof.
  • Disclosure. Paid partnerships should be clearly labelled. France has specific rules for influencer marketing, so check official guidance or your adviser before a campaign goes live.

We then test creator videos side by side, so you learn which people, styles and messages bring the best results for your budget.

Common mistakes we see

A few avoidable problems hold back many TikTok accounts. The most common are running one or two ads for months until they wear out, sending traffic to slow pages not built for mobile, using English-only ads for French audiences, and judging campaigns after only a few days.   2. Setup. We set up or audit your TikTok Ads account, pixel, events and catalogue.
  3. Launch. We launch campaigns with a test budget and several creative angles.
