Why Cork search results are different

Cork is the second largest city in Ireland, and it has its own business character. The city and county mix pharmaceutical and technology employers, a busy port, food and drink producers, tourism along the coast and a large base of trades and professional services. Many searches in Cork are local: people looking for a solicitor in the city centre, a dentist in Douglas or Ballincollig, a builder in Carrigaline or a restaurant near the English Market.

At the same time, Cork businesses often compete against Dublin firms and national brands that rank for broad Irish terms. A Cork accountant may find that national practices take the top organic positions for "accountant Ireland" while the map pack for "accountant Cork" is far more winnable. Good SEO for Cork starts by separating these two kinds of search and putting effort where a Cork business can realistically win.

What an SEO agency in Cork should actually do

SEO is not one task. It is a set of jobs that support each other, and an agency should be clear about which of them it does each month. For most Cork businesses, the work falls into five areas.

  • Technical SEO. Making sure Google can crawl and index the site, that pages load quickly on mobile, that there are no broken redirects or duplicate pages, and that structured data is in place. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows what a thorough audit covers.
  • Keyword research. Finding the searches with real buying intent, including the town and suburb names your customers use. The approach in our keyword research guide works just as well for Irish searches.
  • On-page optimisation. Writing titles, headings and page copy that match each search, and giving every service its own focused page rather than one long list.
  • Local SEO. Building and maintaining your Google Business Profile, keeping your name, address and phone number consistent, and earning reviews.
  • Content and links. Publishing helpful guides and earning links from relevant Irish websites, trade bodies and local organisations.

An agency that only reports rankings, or only writes blog posts, is covering part of the job. Ask for a plan that names which of these areas will get attention in the first three months and why.

Local SEO for Cork businesses

For many Cork firms, local search is where most of the value sits. When someone searches "plumber Cork" or "physio near me", Google usually shows a map with three businesses above the organic results. Getting into that map pack depends on three broad factors: how relevant your profile is to the search, how close you are to the searcher and how prominent your business appears across the web.

You cannot change where you are, but you can improve relevance and prominence. That means choosing the right primary category on your Google Business Profile, writing a clear description, adding real photos, listing your services and keeping opening hours accurate, including bank holidays. It also means asking happy customers for reviews and replying to them politely.

If you serve several parts of the county, such as Cobh, Midleton, Mallow or Kinsale, it can make sense to create location pages. These only work if each page says something genuinely useful about that area, such as the services you offer there or how you travel to customers. Sectors we see in Cork search

Cork has a broad economy, and each sector searches a little differently. A few patterns are worth knowing before you plan your SEO.

  • Hospitality and tourism. Hotels, restaurants and attractions in the city and along the West Cork coast compete with booking sites and review platforms. Strong direct booking pages and well written local guides can help you win searches the big platforms do not cover well. See our hospitality marketing page for how we approach this sector.
  • Professional services. Solicitors, accountants and consultants benefit from detailed service pages and clear answers to the questions clients ask before they call.
  • Trades and home services. Builders, electricians and installers rely heavily on the map pack and on reviews.

Working with a remote studio means you are choosing on skill, process and fit rather than postcode. How to choose an SEO agency for your Cork business

Whether you hire us or someone else, a few questions will help you compare agencies fairly.

  1. What will you do in the first ninety days, and in what order?
  2. Will I own my Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics accounts?
  3. How do you build links, and can you show the types of sites you approach?
  4. What will the monthly report contain, and will it show enquiries as well as rankings?
  5. What is the notice period if I want to stop?

Clear answers to these questions are a good sign. Vague answers, guaranteed rankings or long lock-in contracts are worth treating with caution. You can read more about our approach on our SEO and GEO services page, which also covers how we prepare content for AI search tools.
