What a Shopify agency in Edmonton should do for you

Edmonton is home to a wide mix of businesses that sell online, from food and drink producers and outdoor and workwear brands to home goods, health and beauty products, specialist trade suppliers and independent shops that want to reach customers beyond the city. Some sell mainly to Albertans, while others ship across Canada or into the United States.

Whatever you sell, a good Shopify partner should:

  • Understand your products and customers before choosing a theme or apps.
  • Plan the store structure, including collections, navigation, filters and product page layout, so customers find what they need quickly.
  • Build on a solid theme, customised to your brand, rather than piling on apps that slow the store down.
  • Set up shipping, taxes and payments correctly for Canada and any other markets you sell to.
  • Migrate products, customers and orders carefully if you are moving from another platform.
  • Train your team to add products, run promotions and manage orders without help.
  • Support growth after launch with SEO, paid ads and conversion improvements.

Shipping, taxes and payments for Alberta stores

Getting the commercial setup right is one of the most important parts of a Canadian Shopify build. Shopify supports Canadian dollars, Canadian carriers and Canadian sales tax settings, but each store needs to be configured for how the business actually operates.

  • Shipping: Edmonton stores often ship locally, across the Prairies and nationally. We set up shipping profiles and rates that reflect real costs, including local delivery or in-store pickup if you offer them, and can connect Canadian carriers such as Canada Post and Purolator where they fit your operation.
  • Sales tax: Alberta does not have a provincial sales tax, but GST applies, and sales to customers in other provinces may involve HST or provincial sales taxes depending on your situation. We configure Shopify's tax settings to match the approach your accountant confirms, and you should check the Canada Revenue Agency guidance or your own adviser for your obligations.
  • Payments: We set up the payment options that suit your customers and business, and test the full checkout before launch.
  • Selling into the United States: If you ship south of the border, Shopify Markets can help manage currency, pricing and shipping for each market.

Designing a store that sells

A good-looking store is only the start. The stores that convert well tend to share a few traits:

  • Clear navigation and collections that match how customers think about your products.
  • Product pages with good photos, honest descriptions, sizing or specification details and clear delivery information.
  • Fast load times on mobile, where many people browse and buy.
  • A short, trustworthy checkout with the payment options your customers expect.
  • Clear policies on returns, delivery times and contact details.

English is the main language for most Edmonton shoppers. If you also want to reach French-speaking customers in Quebec or elsewhere, Shopify can run more than one language on the same store. We build the structure and the English content, and the French content is written with your team or a professional translator so it reads naturally.

Growing your Edmonton Shopify store after launch

Launch is the beginning, not the end. Most stores need steady work on visibility and conversion to grow. That usually combines:

  • Search engine optimisation, so collection and product pages rank for the terms customers search. Our guide to Shopify SEO covers the platform-specific details, and our article on SEO in Edmonton looks at the local picture.
  • Paid search and shopping ads, which put your products in front of people ready to buy. Our guide to Google Ads in Edmonton explains how that works.
  • Paid social on Meta, TikTok or YouTube to build demand for visual products.
  • Conversion improvements, testing product pages, offers and checkout steps.

We use AI-powered tools to speed up research, product description drafts and reporting, but a person reviews everything before it goes live.

Privacy and email marketing in general terms

An online store collects personal information at every order, so privacy and consent matter. In general terms:

  • Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act applies to many private sector organisations in the province, and PIPEDA, the federal law, can apply to commercial activity that crosses provincial borders.
  • Quebec's Law 25 sets its own expectations if you sell to customers in Quebec.
  • CASL, Canada's anti-spam legislation, governs commercial emails and texts, so newsletter sign-ups and abandoned cart messages need a proper consent approach.

We set up consent tools, sign-up forms and tracking in line with the approach you choose, but we are not lawyers. Check the official guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Alberta's privacy commissioner and the CRTC, and speak to your own adviser where needed.
