How patients choose a dentist in Vancouver

Most people start with a search on their phone. They type something like a dentist near me, an emergency dentist, or a treatment plus a neighbourhood: Kitsilano, Mount Pleasant, the West End, Yaletown, Commercial Drive or across the river in Burnaby and Richmond. They scan the map results, check reviews, look at the clinic website and decide within minutes whether to call or book online.

That journey tells you where to focus. A clinic needs to appear in the map pack, show accurate hours and services, have a mobile-friendly website that answers basic questions, and make booking simple. Paid ads can add visibility, but they rarely fix a weak website or an incomplete profile. Our guide to dental marketing covers these principles in a UK context, and the same logic applies in British Columbia.

A website that earns trust

A dental website should feel calm, clear and professional. Patients want to know what you treat, where you are, whether you are accepting new patients, how direct billing or insurance works, what languages your team speaks and how to book. Anxious patients want reassurance about comfort and what happens at a first visit.

We build clinic websites with:

  • Dedicated pages for each main service, such as general check-ups, hygiene, children's dentistry, emergency care and the treatments you offer.
  • Clear calls to call or book online on every page, especially on mobile.
  • Accessible design with good contrast, readable text and labelled forms.
  • Fast loading on phones and clean integration with your booking software where it allows.
  • Location details and directions that match your Google Business Profile exactly.

A small clinic website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and takes three to five weeks. Our guide to web design in Vancouver explains how we build for businesses in the city.

Many Vancouver clinics serve patients in several languages. Prism writes in English. If you want pages in Cantonese, Mandarin, Punjabi, Korean, French or other languages, the translated content should come from your team or a professional translator, and we set up the site to support it.

Local SEO and your Google Business Profile

For most clinics, map results bring more calls than any other channel. We help you complete and maintain your Google Business Profile with the right primary category, services, hours, holiday hours, photos and booking link. We also set up a simple process for asking patients for reviews in a way that follows Google's policies and any professional rules that apply to you. Our guide to Google Business Profile walks through the setup.

On the website, local SEO means well-structured treatment pages, consistent clinic details, LocalBusiness or Dentist schema, internal links and helpful content that answers real patient questions. We never create fake addresses or location pages for areas you do not serve. Our articles on SEO in Vancouver and local SEO across Canada go further, and our SEO and GEO service explains how we also prepare content for AI search tools.

Paid ads for dental clinics

Google Ads can put your clinic at the top of results for searches like emergency dentist or a treatment near a neighbourhood. That is valuable when you have open appointment slots or are launching a new location. Campaigns need tight keyword lists, negative keywords to filter out irrelevant searches, clear ad text and landing pages built for booking. See our guide to Google Ads in Vancouver.

Meta ads suit awareness: introducing a new clinic, a new associate, extended hours or family dentistry to people living nearby. Healthcare is sensitive on Meta, which limits some targeting options, so campaigns rely on location, broad audiences and strong creative. Our guide to Meta Ads in Vancouver explains our approach. Ad spend is always paid separately to the platforms.

Regulation and advertising rules

Dentistry in British Columbia is a regulated profession, and there are rules about how dental services can be advertised, including how you describe treatments, credentials, comparisons and patient reviews. Platform policies from Google and Meta add further restrictions on healthcare advertising.

We provide general marketing information only. We will not write claims about outcomes, guarantees or superiority, and every page and advert should be reviewed by the clinic before publishing. Approval rests with the clinic and its own adviser, and the relevant regulator's official guidance is the place to check what is permitted. Privacy matters too: patient information should never be used in ads or tracking in ways your privacy obligations do not allow.

What a first ninety days can look like

  1. Weeks one and two: audit of the website, Google Business Profile, tracking and any existing ads.
  2. Weeks three to six: fixes to the profile, service pages, booking calls to action and tracking.
  3. Weeks six to ten: new or improved treatment content, review process and, if agreed, a focused Google Ads campaign.
  4. Weeks ten to thirteen: reporting on calls, bookings and visibility, and a plan for the next quarter.

SEO and paid results typically take three to six months to build, so we focus first on the foundations that support everything else.

Measuring what matters for a clinic

Clinic owners rarely need a long dashboard. They need to know how many new patients called or booked, where those patients came from and what each channel cost. We set up tracking for phone calls from the website and Google Business Profile, online booking completions and form enquiries, and we report them monthly in plain English alongside search visibility and ad spend.

We also look at the gaps between channels. A clinic with plenty of profile views but few calls may have an issue with hours, photos or reviews. A clinic with strong website traffic but few bookings may have a slow booking flow or unclear calls to action. A clinic paying for ads that lead to the homepage may simply need a focused landing page. Fixing these small problems often does more than raising budgets.

Where you have several chairs or more than one location, we help you see which treatments and which neighbourhoods are growing, so you can plan associates, hours and marketing together. Patient data stays in your practice software; our reports use aggregated figures only.

FAQs

What does dental marketing in Vancouver cost?

With Prism Works Studio, a clinic website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and monthly retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500. Ad spend is paid separately, and Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request.

Do you have an office in Vancouver?

What should a dental clinic fix first?

Usually the Google Business Profile and the website: accurate details, clear services, fast mobile pages and an easy way to book. Ads work better once these are in place.

Can you write content about specific treatments?

Yes, we write clear, general patient information in English. Every page should be reviewed and approved by your clinic, because responsibility for clinical accuracy and advertising compliance rests with you.

Can you advertise dental services on Meta?
