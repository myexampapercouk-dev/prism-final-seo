What does an SEO consulting company do?

SEO consulting is the thinking part of search engine optimisation. Before anyone writes a page or fixes a redirect, a consultant needs to understand your business, your customers, your competitors and the current state of your website. The output is a set of decisions: which keywords matter, which pages should target them, what is technically broken and what order to fix things in.

A typical engagement covers the following areas:

  • Technical review. Crawling the site to find broken links, redirect chains, indexing problems, slow templates and duplicate pages. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows the kind of checks involved.
  • Keyword and intent research. Finding the phrases your buyers type, grouping them by intent and matching each group to a page.
  • Content review. Deciding which pages to keep, merge, improve or remove. A website content audit is often the fastest win for older sites.
  • On-page guidance. Titles, headings, internal links and structured data for priority pages.
  • Measurement. Setting up or checking Google Search Console and analytics so progress can be tracked honestly.
  • Roadmap. A prioritised list of actions with effort and expected impact, so your team knows what to do first.

SEO consulting versus a full-service SEO agency

The word "consulting" is used loosely. Some firms deliver a report and leave. Others stay on to implement. Knowing which model you need saves money and frustration.

Most US small and mid-sized businesses we speak to have nobody in-house who can implement technical SEO advice. For them, a consulting company that also does the work is usually better value than a beautiful report.

Questions to ask an SEO consulting company

A good consultant should welcome direct questions. Before you sign, ask:

  • What will you look at in the first month, and what will I receive at the end of it?
  • Who will make the changes on my website: you, my developer or me?
  • How do you choose which keywords to target, and how will you show me the reasoning?
  • Which reports will I get, and which data sources are they built on?
  • What happens to the work and content if we stop working together?
  • How do you handle AI search, such as Google AI Overviews and chat assistants?

Be wary of any company that promises a specific ranking position or a guaranteed traffic number. Nobody controls Google's results, and honest consultants talk about probabilities, priorities and effort instead.

Signs the advice is good

You do not need to be an SEO expert to judge consulting quality. Good advice tends to share a few traits:

  • It is specific. "Improve site speed" is vague. "Compress the hero images on these twelve templates" is actionable.
  • It is ranked. Every recommendation has a priority so your team knows where to start.
  • It is tied to revenue. The plan focuses on pages that lead to enquiries or sales, not vanity terms.
  • It explains trade-offs. Merging pages, changing URLs or rewriting content all carry risk, and a good consultant says so.
  • It is measurable. You can see whether each change was made and what happened afterwards.

The focus of an SEO consulting engagement changes with the business model.

Combining SEO consulting with paid search

SEO and paid search answer the same customer questions in different ways. A consultant who understands both can use paid data to test which keywords convert before committing months of SEO effort to them. If you already run Google Ads, share that account data with your SEO consultant. How long before SEO consulting shows results?

The audit and roadmap usually arrive within the first few weeks. Technical fixes can be made quickly once access is in place. Ranking and traffic changes typically take three to six months to show, depending on competition, the age and strength of your site and how quickly recommendations are implemented. The slowest projects are usually those where advice waits in a queue for developer time, which is one reason we prefer to do the work ourselves where clients allow it.

FAQs

What does an SEO consulting company do?

An SEO consulting company audits your website, researches the keywords your customers use, finds technical and content problems and gives you a ranked plan to fix them. Many, including Prism Works Studio, also carry out the work rather than leaving it to your team.

Is an SEO consultant different from an SEO agency?

The terms overlap. A pure consultant mainly advises, while an agency mainly implements. Many firms do both. The important thing is to agree in writing who makes the changes and what you receive each month.

Can a UK-based SEO consulting company work with US businesses?

Yes. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely with US clients through calls, shared documents and dashboards. We have no US office, and dollar quotes are available on request.

How much does SEO consulting cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month depending on scope, and each includes consulting and delivery. Prices in other currencies are quoted on request.

How quickly will I see results from SEO consulting?

You receive the audit and roadmap within weeks, but ranking and traffic improvements typically take three to six months, depending on competition and how quickly changes are made.

Do I need SEO consulting before a website redesign?

It is strongly recommended. A redesign can change URLs, templates and content, and without planning you can lose rankings you already have. A consultant maps old pages to new ones and checks redirects before launch.
