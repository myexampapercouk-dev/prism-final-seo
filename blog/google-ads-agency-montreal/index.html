Google Ads Agency for Montreal Businesses: Running PPC in French and English

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Montreal? How remote PPC works for Quebec firms, with French and English campaigns, Law 25 notes, fees in pounds and costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Google Ads agency for a Montreal business should plan French and English campaigns separately, each with its own keywords, ads and landing pages.
  • Accurate conversion tracking and consent handling come first, and Quebec's Law 25 makes it worth reviewing how your site collects personal information.
  • Management fees and ad spend are separate, and campaigns usually need three to six months of testing before performance settles.

A Google Ads agency for a Montreal business should build separate French and English campaigns around the searches your customers actually make, track calls, forms and sales accurately and manage bids and budgets so that spend goes towards real enquiries. All of that happens inside your Google Ads account, website and analytics, so it can be managed remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that runs Google Ads for Canadian businesses online, and we have no office in Montreal or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide explains what Google Ads management involves for Montreal and Quebec businesses, how bilingual campaigns work in practice, what to keep in mind about privacy and language rules, what our fees look like and the questions to ask any agency.

What a Google Ads agency in Montreal should do

Montreal has a varied economy, with aerospace, life sciences, video games and technology, fashion and retail, hospitality and tourism, education, professional services and a large base of independent shops, restaurants and trades. The sector changes the detail, but sound Google Ads management covers the same essentials:

  • Conversion tracking. Recording calls, form submissions, bookings and purchases so that decisions rest on outcomes. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking explains the set-up.
  • Campaign structure. Campaigns split by language, service and location, so ads and landing pages match each search closely.
  • Negative keywords. Excluding irrelevant searches on a regular schedule, in both languages. See our negative keywords guide.
  • Ad copy and assets. Clear, accurate headlines, descriptions and sitelinks. Our guide to responsive search ads covers how Google combines them.
  • Bidding and budgets. Choosing a strategy that suits the data available. Our bidding strategies guide explains the options.
  • Reporting on spend, enquiries, sales and next steps in plain language.

Our paid media service covers each of these, along with Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube campaigns where they suit your goals.

Running French and English campaigns

Most Montreal businesses need to reach both French-speaking and English-speaking customers, and many searches happen in French. Google Ads targets languages based on signals about the user rather than the language of each individual search, so the cleanest approach is usually separate campaigns for each language, each with its own keywords, ads and landing pages.

French keywords are not simple translations of English ones. People search with different words, different spellings and Quebec-specific terms, and search volumes can vary widely between the two languages. Keyword research should be carried out in each language rather than translated across. We write in English; French ad copy and landing pages are written with your team or a professional translator, and we build and manage the campaign structure around them.

Targeting Montreal, Laval and the rest of Quebec

Location settings decide who sees your ads. A renovation contractor in Rosemont or a dental clinic in the Plateau may want customers within a short distance, while a manufacturer on the South Shore or an online store may want buyers across Quebec, Ontario or all of Canada. The radius, excluded areas and presence settings should reflect where you genuinely serve customers.

Seasonality matters too. Winter, construction season, festival periods and holiday shopping all shift demand, so budgets and ad copy should be planned around them. Our location targeting guide explains the main settings.

Landing pages and quality

A well-built campaign can still underperform if the click lands on a page that does not match the ad. A French ad should lead to a French page that repeats the promise in the ad, shows evidence that you can deliver it and makes enquiring simple on a phone. The same applies to English campaigns.

Landing page relevance also feeds into Quality Score, which influences how much you pay for each click. Our guides to the Google Ads Quality Score and landing page design explain what usually makes the difference. Where a page needs rebuilding, we can design and build it as part of the project.

Privacy, consent and language rules in Quebec

Quebec's Law 25 updated the province's private sector privacy rules, with requirements around consent, transparency and how organisations handle personal information. That can affect the tracking tags, cookie consent and enquiry forms used alongside Google Ads. Federally, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) covers personal information in many commercial settings, and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) applies if you follow up leads by email.

Quebec's Charter of the French Language also sets rules on the use of French in commerce, which can apply to advertising and websites. These notes are general information rather than legal advice. Check the official guidance from the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, the Office québécois de la langue française and federal regulators, and take legal advice where needed. In regulated sectors, approval of ads and landing pages rests with your compliance team.

How remote Google Ads management works

Montreal runs on Eastern Time, so the UK is usually five hours ahead. UK afternoons overlap with Montreal mornings for calls, and most of the work happens in shared accounts and written updates.

  1. Audit. We review your account, tracking, landing pages and competitors, or plan a new account.
  2. Plan. We agree goals, languages, target areas, structure and a starting budget.
  3. Build. We set up tracking, campaigns and assets, with French copy supplied by your team or translator.
  4. Optimise. We review search terms, bids, ads and budgets in each language and test improvements.
  5. Report. Monthly updates on spend, enquiries, sales and next steps.

You keep ownership of the Google Ads account, analytics and data throughout. We work as users on your account rather than one we control.

Questions to ask any Google Ads agency in Montreal

  • How will you structure French and English campaigns?
  • Who writes the French ad copy, and how is it reviewed?
  • How will you track calls, forms and sales, and handle consent?
  • Will I own the Google Ads account and all its data?
  • Are your fees separate from ad spend, and what is the notice period?
  • What would you change on our landing pages before increasing spend?

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Montreal?

Look for an agency that plans French and English campaigns separately, starts with tracking, keeps fees apart from ad spend and leaves you owning the account. Ask how it handles French copy and Quebec privacy requirements.

Can a UK agency manage Google Ads for a Montreal business?

Yes. Google Ads is managed online, so location is not a barrier. Prism has no Montreal office; we work remotely and schedule calls in the overlap between UK afternoons and Montreal mornings.

Do you write French ads?

We write in English. French ad copy and landing pages are written with your team or a professional translator, and we handle keyword structure, campaign set-up and ongoing management.

How much does Google Ads management cost with Prism?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Google. We can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Does Law 25 affect my Google Ads tracking?

It may affect how your site collects consent and personal information through tracking tags and forms. Check the official Quebec guidance and take legal advice on your obligations; we can set up tracking to work with the consent approach you decide on.

How long does it take for Google Ads to work?

Ads can bring enquiries soon after launch, but most accounts need three to six months of testing and refinement before costs and results become more predictable.
