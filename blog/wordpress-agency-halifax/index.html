Why WordPress suits Halifax organisations

Halifax has a broad economy: ocean and marine industries, defence, healthcare, government and public sector bodies, universities such as Dalhousie and Saint Mary's, tourism around the waterfront, and a large community of professional services firms, trades, charities and independent shops across the peninsula, Dartmouth and Bedford.

Many of these organisations publish a lot of content: news, events, research, services, job openings and resources. WordPress is well suited to that. It is flexible, widely supported and lets non-technical staff add pages and posts without a developer. With the right setup, it can be fast, accessible and secure. With the wrong setup, it becomes slow, fragile and a target for attacks. The difference is in how it is built and maintained.

WordPress agency Halifax: how we build

We start with your goals and your audience, then plan the structure: which pages you need, how they link together and what each one should help visitors do. We write a short brief, agree a design direction and build on a lightweight, well-supported theme. We avoid heavy page builders where a simpler approach will do, because they often slow sites down and make future changes harder.

We keep plugins to a minimum. Each one is chosen for a clear reason, comes from a reputable developer and is kept up to date. Fewer plugins mean fewer conflicts, fewer security risks and a faster site. Our WordPress plugin audit guide explains how we decide what stays and what goes.

When the site is ready, we test it on phones, tablets and desktops, check speed and accessibility basics, and give your team a short training session plus written notes on how to edit pages, add posts and manage menus.

Accessibility and good practice

Accessibility matters for every website, and Nova Scotia has its own accessibility legislation that affects some organisations. We build with accessibility in mind: clear headings, good colour contrast, descriptive link text, alt text for images, keyboard-friendly navigation and readable forms. We keep regulatory guidance general, so we recommend checking official guidance or your own adviser about what applies to your organisation.

Privacy is similar. Canada has federal privacy law, and your website's forms, analytics and cookies should be described accurately in your privacy notice. We set up tracking carefully and leave the legal judgement to you and your advisers.

Hosting, security and care

A WordPress site is only as good as its hosting and maintenance. Cheap shared hosting can be slow and unreliable, and an unmaintained site with outdated plugins is one of the most common ways websites get hacked. We help you choose sensible hosting in your own name, then keep the site updated, backed up and monitored.

Our care work covers core, theme and plugin updates, off-site backups, uptime checks, security scanning and small content changes. If something goes wrong, we fix it. Our guides to WordPress hosting and WordPress security explain the principles, which apply just as much in Halifax as in the UK.

Fixing and rescuing existing WordPress sites

Many Halifax organisations already have a WordPress site that has become slow, broken or hard to edit, often after a previous developer moved on. We can take it over. We start with an audit covering speed, plugins, theme, hosting, security, backups, SEO basics and accessibility, then give you a clear list of fixes ranked by importance.

Sometimes a few targeted fixes are enough. Sometimes a rebuild is better value than patching an old site. We will tell you honestly which we think is right and why.

Moving to WordPress from another platform

If you started on Wix, Squarespace or another website builder and have outgrown it, WordPress gives you more control over content, SEO and integrations. A careful migration moves your pages and posts, rebuilds the design and sets up redirects from old URLs so you keep your search visibility. Our Wix to WordPress migration guide walks through the process.

A well-built WordPress site gives you a strong base for search. We set up clean URLs, page titles, meta descriptions, structured data, XML sitemaps and internal links, and make sure pages load quickly. For ongoing growth, we can help with content planning, local SEO and technical improvements. What to prepare before a WordPress project

Projects run faster and cost less when the groundwork is ready. Before we start, it helps to gather a few things:

  • Access details: your domain registrar, current hosting, WordPress admin and any analytics or Search Console accounts, shared securely.
  • Content: the text for key pages, or notes we can shape into copy, plus photos and logos in good quality.
  • Examples: two or three websites you like, and a few you do not, with a sentence on why.
  • Goals: what the site should help people do, such as call, book, donate, apply or buy.
  • Decision makers: who approves designs and content, so feedback arrives in one clear round rather than many.

Content is the most common cause of delay. If your team is busy, we can plan the page structure first and give you simple templates to fill in, which keeps the project moving and avoids last-minute rewrites before launch.
