Why WordPress suits many Indianapolis businesses

The trade-off is that WordPress needs care: updates, backups, security and sensible plugin choices.

Building a new WordPress site

A good build starts with structure, not colours. We agree the pages you need, the services or products each should explain, the questions customers ask and the actions you want visitors to take. That plan shapes navigation, internal links and the content your team or ours will write.

We build with the block editor and a lightweight theme where possible, rather than stacking heavy page builders. That keeps pages fast and editing simple. Each page gets a clear title, a sensible heading structure and the technical basics search engines need, such as clean URLs, an XML sitemap and correct structured data.

If you are still deciding whether to rebuild or start fresh, our guide to web design in Indianapolis covers the wider design side.

Rescuing a slow or broken WordPress site

Many Indianapolis businesses already have a WordPress site that has grown messy over the years. Common problems include dozens of plugins, an outdated theme, slow shared hosting, broken forms and old pages that no longer reflect the business.

We start with an audit. We list every plugin and check whether it is needed, maintained and safe. We test speed on mobile, review security settings and check how search engines see the site. Our guide to a WordPress plugin audit shows the kind of checks involved. Then we fix the highest-impact problems first, keeping your content and rankings intact.

Security, hosting and updates

WordPress powers a large part of the web, which makes it a regular target for automated attacks. Most problems come from outdated plugins, weak passwords, abandoned themes and poor hosting. Good care plans deal with all of these.

  • Updates: core, theme and plugin updates applied and tested regularly.
  • Backups: automatic, stored off the server, with restores tested.
  • Security: sensible user roles, two-factor sign-in for admins and a firewall where appropriate.
  • Hosting: managed hosting with good support, in a US data centre if most visitors are local.

Our guides to WordPress security and WordPress hosting were written for UK businesses, but the practical advice applies just as well in Indiana. Hosting is paid directly by you, so you always keep ownership of the account.

Choosing between a theme, a page builder and custom blocks

One of the first decisions in any WordPress project is how pages will be built and edited. There are three common routes, and each suits different businesses.

  • A lightweight theme with the block editor: fast, simple and well supported. Best for most small business sites in Indianapolis.
  • A page builder: flexible drag-and-drop editing, but often slower and harder to move away from later.
  • Custom blocks: purpose-built sections that match your brand and keep editing safe, suited to larger sites with many similar pages.

We usually recommend the block editor with a small number of reusable patterns. Your team can add pages and posts without breaking the layout, and the site stays quick. If you already rely on a page builder, we can keep it where that makes sense or plan a gradual move away from it.

Editing should feel safe. We set user roles so staff only see what they need, add simple guidance notes inside the editor where helpful and give a short recorded walkthrough of common tasks. Most Indianapolis teams we speak to want to update services, add news and change opening hours themselves, and a well-built WordPress site makes that easy.

Forms deserve attention too. Contact, quote and booking forms should be short, send to the right inbox, store submissions safely and be tracked as conversions. A broken form can quietly cost months of enquiries, so we test every form at launch and check them as part of ongoing care. Spam protection that does not frustrate real visitors is part of that work too.
