Google Ads Agency Seattle: How Remote PPC Works for Seattle Businesses

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Seattle? A remote team can set up tracking, build campaigns and manage spend across Puget Sound. See how it works and costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good Google Ads agency for a Seattle business starts with conversion tracking, builds tightly themed campaigns and reviews search terms, bids and landing pages every week.
  • Puget Sound is spread out and divided by water, so location targeting, ad scheduling and budget control matter as much as keyword choice.
  • We are a UK-based studio managing Google Ads remotely, with retainers from £350 a month, ad spend paid separately and other currencies quoted on request.

If you need a Google Ads agency in Seattle, the right choice is the team that can turn your ad spend into calls, leads and sales you can measure, wherever that team sits. A remote team can set up tracking, build campaigns and manage spend for Seattle software companies, contractors, clinics, professional firms and online stores, as long as the work is grounded in your margins and your buyers.

We are a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for US businesses. We have no Seattle office and no US office. This guide explains what Google Ads management for a Seattle business should include, how to target a region as spread out as Puget Sound, how a remote engagement works across an eight-hour time difference and what it costs.

What a Google Ads agency in Seattle should do

Seattle's economy leans heavily on technology and cloud software, but it also includes aerospace, maritime and logistics, healthcare and life sciences, retail, outdoor and lifestyle brands, construction and a large base of home services. Many of these sectors bid on the same high-intent searches, and click costs in a high-income metro area can be steep, so wasted spend adds up quickly. Good Google Ads management usually covers:

  • Conversion tracking: calls, form submissions, demo requests, bookings and purchases recorded accurately before any budget is scaled.
  • Account structure: campaigns and ad groups grouped by service and intent, so each ad matches the search behind it.
  • Keyword strategy: a focus on searches with buying intent, with clear match types and budgets that follow value.
  • Negative keywords: regular reviews of search terms to block irrelevant clicks, such as job seekers and students.
  • Ads and assets: responsive search ads, sitelinks, callouts and call assets that answer the searcher directly.
  • Landing pages: pages that match the ad, load quickly and make the next step obvious.
  • Reporting: a monthly summary of spend, conversions, cost per lead and what changes next.

Tracking comes first, because without it every other decision is guesswork. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking explains how to set it up, and our negative keywords guide shows how to stop paying for clicks that will never convert. A tech-heavy city produces plenty of searches from people looking for jobs rather than services, so negatives matter more here than in many markets.

Targeting Puget Sound without wasting budget

The Seattle area stretches from Everett in the north to Tacoma in the south, with Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland and Issaquah across Lake Washington and communities such as Bainbridge Island and Bremerton across the Sound. Bridges, ferries and traffic mean a service business in Tacoma may not want a job in Everett, even though both sit in the same broad metro area. Your targeting should follow where you can actually deliver:

  • Radius or city targeting: set around the areas you genuinely serve, rather than the whole of Washington State.
  • Presence, not interest: target people located in your area, unless you deliberately want people searching about it from elsewhere, such as relocations or visitors.
  • Location bid adjustments: raise or lower bids by area once there is enough data to show where leads convert best.
  • Separate campaigns: for areas with very different costs or margins, so one does not eat the other's budget.

Our location targeting guide explains these settings in detail. National software and ecommerce companies based in Seattle often do the opposite and target the whole US, splitting campaigns by region or time zone instead.

Google Ads for Seattle software and B2B companies

Many Seattle companies sell software or services to other businesses across the country. For them, Google Ads is less about the city and more about catching buyers who are actively searching for a solution, comparing vendors or looking for an alternative to a tool they already use. That usually means:

  • Campaigns built around problems, categories and competitor alternatives rather than generic industry terms.
  • Landing pages for each main use case, with a clear demo, trial or contact step.
  • Offline conversion imports from your CRM, so bidding learns from qualified leads rather than every form fill.
  • Careful bidding, because B2B search volumes are often small and smart bidding needs enough data to work well.

Our guide to Google Ads bidding strategies explains when to use manual control, target CPA or target ROAS, and how much conversion data each one needs.

Landing pages that convert Seattle searchers

A good campaign can still fail if the page behind it is slow, vague or hard to use on a phone. Each main service or product should have a landing page that repeats the promise of the ad, explains what happens next, shows prices or a clear starting point where possible and gives a single obvious action. Our landing page design guide covers the essentials, and the same principles apply to US audiences.

If your ads mention healthcare, legal or financial services, keep claims factual and check the copy with your own compliance team or advisers before it goes live. Google also has its own policies for these categories, and approval of regulated wording rests with you. Privacy and accessibility expectations vary by state and sector too, so check your tracking, consent and form setup against official guidance or with your own adviser.

How remote Google Ads management works for a Seattle business

Seattle runs on Pacific Time, usually eight hours behind the UK. The shared window is short: your early morning overlaps with our late afternoon. In practice that is enough, because most PPC work happens in the account rather than on calls. We review campaigns during our day, so changes are ready when you start yours. A typical arrangement looks like this:

  1. Audit: a review of your account, tracking and landing pages. Our PPC audit checklist shows what we look at.
  2. Setup or rebuild: tracking fixed first, then campaigns restructured around intent and margin.
  3. Weekly optimisation: search terms, bids, budgets, ads and assets reviewed and adjusted.
  4. Monthly reporting: a written summary plus a call booked in your morning.

Our guide to working with a remote PPC agency for US businesses covers billing, access and communication in more detail. If organic search matters to you as well, our guide to SEO in Seattle shows how paid and organic work can support each other.

How much a Google Ads agency costs for a Seattle business

  • Launch, £350 a month: one focused campaign set for a smaller business or a single location.
  • Growth, £750 a month: several campaigns, landing page input and more frequent optimisation.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: larger accounts, several campaign types or multiple regions.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Seattle?

Look for an agency that fixes tracking first, explains its account structure, shows you search terms and reports on leads or sales rather than clicks. Ask who owns the account and what happens to it if you part ways.

Do you have an office in Seattle?

No. We are a UK-based studio and manage Google Ads remotely for US businesses. We have no Seattle office or any other US office, and we work through video calls, shared dashboards and written reports.

How does the time difference with Seattle work?

Seattle is usually eight hours behind the UK. Calls fit into your early morning, and we make account changes during our day so they are ready when you start work.

Can you manage Google Ads for a Seattle software company?

Yes. We build campaigns around problems, categories and alternatives, and where possible import qualified leads from your CRM so bidding learns from real pipeline rather than every form fill.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from our management retainer, which starts at £350 a month. Other currencies are quoted on request.

How long before Google Ads works?

Ads can bring traffic straight away, but campaigns typically need three to six months of data and optimisation to reach steady, profitable performance.
