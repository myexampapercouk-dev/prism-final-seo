Why Exeter businesses advertise on Facebook and Instagram

Exeter has a busy mix of independent shops, cafes and restaurants, a large university population, professional services, health and wellbeing businesses, and a growing number of online brands. Many of these businesses sell something people did not know they wanted until they saw it. That is where Meta Ads shine.

Unlike search ads, which catch people who are already looking, Facebook and Instagram ads put your offer in front of people based on their interests, behaviour and location. A new restaurant near the Quay, a fitness studio in Heavitree, a gift shop on Gandy Street or an online clothing brand selling across the UK can all build demand this way.

Meta Ads also work well for reaching people across the wider area, from Exmouth and Topsham to Crediton and Tiverton, without paying for each search term separately. What a Meta Ads agency in Exeter should do

Good Meta Ads management is a cycle of planning, testing and learning. Here is what we handle each month.

  • Tracking setup. We install and check the Meta Pixel and Conversions API so purchases, leads and bookings are measured accurately.
  • Audience planning. We build audiences from location, interests, website visitors, customer lists and lookalikes, then let Meta's broad targeting work where it performs better.
  • Creative strategy. We plan ad angles, write copy and brief image and short-form creative that suits Facebook and Instagram placements. Your team or your creator supplies the photos or clips; we shape them into ads.
  • Campaign structure. We keep accounts simple, with clear prospecting and retargeting campaigns and enough budget in each to learn.
  • Testing. We test new hooks, formats and offers each month and cut what is not working.
  • Reporting. You get a plain monthly report on spend, results, cost per result and next steps.

Creative is the biggest lever

On Facebook and Instagram, creative does much of the targeting work. The image, opening line and offer decide who stops scrolling. A strong ad shows the product or result clearly, speaks to a specific person and makes the next step obvious.

We plan creative around a few core angles, such as the problem you solve, the quality of what you make, social proof your business has already earned, and a clear offer. Then we test variations. We do not film or edit video, but we can brief your team or a local photographer on the shots and clips that tend to work, and turn your existing photos and short clips into ads in the right formats for feed, Stories and Reels.

Fresh creative matters because ads tire quickly. When the same people see the same ad again and again, costs rise. We watch frequency and results and plan new creative before performance drops.

Local targeting around Exeter and Devon

Location targeting on Meta works differently from Google. You choose a city, postcode or radius, and Meta shows ads to people who live in or have recently been in that area. For a cafe or salon, a tight radius around the city centre makes sense. For a trades business, it might cover Exeter plus the surrounding towns. For an online brand, it might be the whole UK.

We also think about timing. Exeter has a large student population that changes with the university year, plus visitors in summer and busy periods around Christmas. Budgets and messages can shift with those patterns, so spend goes where demand is.

Tracking, privacy and honest numbers

Since browser and phone privacy changes, tracking on Meta has become less complete. The Pixel alone can miss conversions. We set up the Conversions API alongside the Pixel where your platform supports it, which sends events from your website server and gives a fuller picture.

We also compare Meta's numbers with your own sales or lead data, because platform reports tend to claim credit generously. Our aim is a realistic view of what your ads are actually doing, not an inflated one. If you run a Shopify or WordPress site, we can handle the setup as part of the work.

Landing pages and your website

People who click a Facebook or Instagram ad are often on a phone and have only a few seconds to decide. If the page is slow, cluttered or does not match the ad, they leave. We check that landing pages load fast, carry the same message as the ad and make it easy to buy, book or enquire.

Meta Ads alongside other channels

Meta Ads rarely work alone. Many Exeter businesses pair them with Google Ads to catch people searching, or with TikTok Ads to reach younger audiences. Our full paid media service covers Google, Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn, so campaigns can share data and creative ideas.

FAQs

How do I choose a Meta Ads agency in Exeter?

Look for an agency that sets up tracking properly, tests creative regularly, reports honestly against your own sales data and lets you keep ownership of your ad account. Ask how they plan creative and how often they refresh it.

Do you have an office in Exeter?

No. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We run all projects through video calls, shared reports and email, so Exeter and Devon clients get the same service as anyone else.

Do you make video ads?

We do not film or edit video. We plan ad angles, write copy and brief your team or a photographer on what to capture, then build ads from the photos and clips you supply.

How much should I spend on Facebook and Instagram ads?

It depends on your goals, margins and audience size. Many small businesses start with a modest monthly budget and increase it once testing shows what works. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and is separate from our fee.

How long before Meta Ads bring results?

Campaigns need time to learn and test. You may see early results within weeks, but performance typically builds over 3 to 6 months as we find the best audiences, offers and creative.

Can you run Meta Ads for an online store?

Yes. We run sales campaigns, catalogue ads and retargeting for Shopify and WordPress stores, and we can set up product feeds and tracking as part of the work.
