What a Google Ads agency in Leicester does

Google Ads puts your business at the top of search results for the terms you choose, and you pay when someone clicks. Done well, it brings steady, measurable enquiries. Done badly, it burns money on irrelevant searches. A good agency handles:

  • Keyword and audience research: finding the searches that lead to sales, not just traffic.
  • Campaign structure: grouping services and locations so budgets and messages stay focused.
  • Ad copy and assets: clear headlines, descriptions, sitelinks and calls.
  • Conversion tracking: recording calls, forms and sales accurately.
  • Ongoing optimisation: negative keywords, bids, budgets and landing page improvements.

Our paid media service sets out how we manage each part. If you are new to paid search, our guide to Google Ads for small UK businesses is a good starting point.

How Leicester customers search

Leicester is a diverse, busy city surrounded by a large county. Searches come from the city centre and suburbs such as Oadby, Wigston, Braunstone and Birstall, and from towns including Loughborough, Hinckley, Market Harborough, Coalville and Melton Mowbray. Location targeting needs to reflect where your customers actually are and how far you are willing to travel or deliver.

The local economy includes textiles and manufacturing, food businesses, logistics, retail, professional services, two large universities and a strong independent business community. A wholesaler may target buyers nationally, while a takeaway, salon or emergency trade only wants clicks from within a few miles. Getting this right is often the single biggest lever on cost per enquiry.

Leicester is also a multilingual city. We write ads and landing pages in English; if you want campaigns in other languages, that content needs to come from your own team or a translator, and we can then build and manage those campaigns.

How long Google Ads takes to work

Ads can start appearing within days of launch, but campaigns typically take three to six months to reach a stable, efficient level. The first weeks are about learning: which searches convert, which waste money and which ads people respond to. Each month should bring a lower cost per enquiry as the account improves.

What helps most:

  1. Accurate conversion tracking from day one.
  2. Landing pages that match the ad and make it easy to get in touch.
  3. A budget large enough to gather useful data.
  4. Fast feedback from you on lead quality.

Our guide to conversion tracking setup explains why measurement comes first.

Cutting wasted spend

Many Leicester accounts we see lose budget in the same ways: broad keywords matching irrelevant searches, location settings that show ads far outside the service area, ads running when nobody can answer the phone and landing pages that do not explain the offer. We check search terms every week, add negative keywords, review locations and schedules and suggest page fixes. Our negative keywords guide and location targeting guide cover these in more depth.

Landing pages matter as much as ads

A good ad sends people to a page. If that page is slow, vague or hard to use on a phone, you pay for the click and lose the enquiry. We review landing pages as part of every account and recommend changes such as clearer headlines, visible phone numbers, short forms and trust information. If your site needs a rebuild, a small website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and takes three to five weeks; read our guide to web design in Leicester for more.

AI-powered tools in Google Ads

Google now relies heavily on automated bidding and AI-generated suggestions. These tools can work well, but only when fed accurate conversion data and sensible limits. We use AI-powered analysis to spot patterns in search terms and performance, while keeping human control over budgets, keywords, messaging and brand safety.

Common Google Ads mistakes in Leicester accounts

When we audit Leicester accounts, a few problems come up again and again. Campaigns use the default setting that shows ads to people merely interested in Leicester, rather than people located there, so budget leaks to searchers in other parts of the country. Broad match keywords run without negative lists, so a plumbing firm pays for searches about plumbing courses or jobs. Conversion tracking counts page views as leads, which makes the account look healthier than it is and teaches automated bidding the wrong lessons.

Other common issues include a single ad group for every service, ads that send everyone to the homepage, no call tracking for businesses that mainly win work by phone, and budgets spread across too many campaigns to learn anything. A typical first month with us

In the first week we audit the account or plan a new one, agree goals and set up or fix conversion tracking. In the second week we build or restructure campaigns, write ads and add assets such as sitelinks and callouts. Once campaigns are live, we review search terms several times in the early weeks, add negative keywords, adjust bids and budgets and flag any landing page problems. At the end of the month you receive a report covering spend, enquiries, cost per enquiry and our plan for the next month.

From there, each month builds on the last. We test new ad angles, refine location and schedule settings and expand into new keywords or campaign types only when the core campaigns are working well. The aim is simple: more qualified enquiries from Leicester and Leicestershire for the same or lower spend.
