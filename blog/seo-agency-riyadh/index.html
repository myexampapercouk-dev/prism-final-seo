An SEO agency for a Riyadh business should help you rank for the Arabic and English searches your customers actually use, fix the technical issues holding your site back and build content that earns trust from both people and search engines. Why SEO in Riyadh needs its own approach

Search behaviour in Saudi Arabia differs from the UK or US in ways that change how a campaign should be run. Many people search in Arabic, some switch between Arabic and English, and spelling varies with transliteration. Local competitors may have strong Arabic content while international competitors dominate English results. A plan that simply translates English keywords will miss much of the demand.

  • Arabic keyword research. Customers may use dialect terms, formal Arabic or transliterated English. Each needs researching separately rather than assumed.
  • Bilingual site structure. If you serve both languages, each page needs a clear language version and correct technical signals so search engines show the right one. Our international SEO guide covers how this works.
  • Mobile performance. Most searches happen on phones, so page speed and mobile usability matter. See our notes on Core Web Vitals.
  • Local presence. For businesses with a physical location in Riyadh, a complete and accurate Google Business Profile supports visibility in map results.
  • AI search. More searchers now see AI-generated answers. Clear, well-structured content is more likely to be understood and cited. Our explainer on generative engine optimisation covers the basics.

SEO agency Riyadh: questions to ask before you sign

Agency proposals can look similar on the surface. These questions help you see the difference.

  1. Who does the Arabic keyword research and content? Ask whether a fluent Arabic speaker is involved and how dialect and spelling variations are handled.
  2. What will you do in the first three months? A clear plan is a good sign. Vague promises of "optimisation" are not.
  3. How do you report? Ask to see a sample report. It should connect search visibility to enquiries or sales, not just list rankings.
  4. Do you guarantee rankings? The honest answer is no. Nobody controls Google, and guarantees often signal risky tactics.
  5. How do you build links? Paid link schemes and low-quality directories can harm your site. Ask for the approach in writing.
  6. What is the contract length and notice period? Our guide to marketing agency contracts explains what reasonable terms look like.
  7. Who owns the work? Content, reports and accounts such as Search Console and analytics should belong to you.

Local agency or remote SEO partner?

A Riyadh-based agency may offer in-person meetings and deep local market knowledge. A remote studio can offer a different perspective, transparent processes and access to international SEO experience. Neither is automatically better. What matters is the quality of the work, the clarity of reporting and whether the team can handle the languages you need.

If you work with us, we combine our technical and content process with your team's local knowledge. You review Arabic content and approve anything that makes claims about your services. We will tell you plainly where a specialist translator or local partner would add value. Our guide on outsourcing SEO to an overseas agency covers the pros and cons in more detail.

How long SEO takes and what it costs

SEO results typically take three to six months to build, sometimes longer in competitive sectors or for new websites. Early months usually focus on fixing technical issues and improving key pages; content and authority work then compounds over time.

Our monthly retainers, shown on our pricing page, are:

  • Launch, £350 a month: suited to smaller sites focusing on a handful of priority services or one language first.
  • Growth, £750 a month: for businesses building content in both Arabic and English and competing for broader terms.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: for larger sites, multiple service lines or ecommerce catalogues.

We price in pounds sterling and can quote in other currencies on request. Translation by a specialist, if needed, is usually quoted separately. We will always explain what each month includes before you commit.

Sector considerations in Saudi Arabia

Some sectors, such as healthcare, financial services and real estate, may be subject to specific advertising and content rules in the Kingdom. We keep our guidance general here: check current official guidance from the relevant authority, and have your legal or compliance team approve any claims before they go live. The same applies to privacy notices and how your website handles personal data under Saudi data protection rules.

For ecommerce brands, product and category pages need careful Arabic and English optimisation, alongside clear delivery and returns information. Service businesses usually benefit most from strong, specific service pages and genuinely helpful guides that answer customer questions.

What to prepare before speaking to an agency

You will get more useful proposals, and a faster start, if you gather a few things first. None of this needs to be perfect, but it helps any agency understand where you are today.

  • Access to your data. Google Search Console and Google Analytics access, or permission to set them up if they are missing. Our guide to setting up Google Search Console walks through the steps.
  • Your priority services or products. Which offers bring the most valuable customers, and which would you like to grow?
  • Known competitors. The businesses you lose work to, and the ones you see appearing in search results for your main services.
  • Language priorities. Whether Arabic, English or both matter most for your customers, and who on your team can review Arabic content.
  • Past SEO work. Any previous agencies, link building or site migrations, so nothing risky is repeated and any old problems can be checked.

With this in hand, a first conversation can focus on priorities rather than basic fact-finding, and the audit can begin as soon as you are ready.

FAQs

How do I choose an SEO agency in Riyadh?

Look for a clear process, honest timelines, sample reporting and real capability in Arabic keyword research and content. Avoid anyone who guarantees rankings or will not explain how they build links.

How much does an SEO agency in Riyadh cost with Prism Works Studio?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. We price in pounds sterling and can quote in other currencies on request.

Do you have an office in Saudi Arabia?

No. We are a UK-based studio working remotely with clients in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. We run campaigns through video calls, shared documents and monthly reports.

Can you do SEO in Arabic?

Yes, we plan Arabic and English SEO together. Arabic content should be written or reviewed by a fluent speaker, and your team approves all content before publication.

How long does SEO take to work in Saudi Arabia?

Results typically take three to six months to build, and longer in very competitive sectors or for brand new websites. Technical fixes often come first, followed by content and authority work.

Is SEO or Google Ads better for a Riyadh business?

Ads can bring traffic quickly while you pay, and SEO builds visibility that lasts. Many businesses use both, starting with ads for immediate enquiries while SEO grows in the background.
