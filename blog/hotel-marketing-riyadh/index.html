Who books hotels in Riyadh

Riyadh draws a wide mix of guests. Business travellers come for meetings in districts such as Olaya, King Abdullah Financial District and the diplomatic quarter. Event visitors arrive for conferences, exhibitions, sports and entertainment seasons. Families visit relatives or come for shopping and leisure, and a growing number of international tourists include the capital in a wider Saudi trip.

Each group searches differently. A business traveller wants a hotel close to the office with reliable Wi-Fi and easy airport transfers. An event visitor searches by venue and dates. A family looks for connecting rooms, apartments and nearby malls. Your marketing should speak to each group with pages, offers and ads built around what they actually need.

Hotel marketing Riyadh: the channels that matter

Most hotels rely heavily on online travel agencies, and those agencies charge commission on every booking. The aim of good hotel marketing is not to abandon them but to shift a healthy share of bookings to your own website, where margin and guest data stay with you.

A website built to take bookings

A hotel website has one main job: turning a visitor into a confirmed booking. That means fast loading on mobile, clear room pages with real photos and honest descriptions, a visible best-rate message and a booking button on every page. Guests should see location, parking, check-in times and airport transfer options without hunting.

We build small hotel websites for £1,000 to £2,000 and larger sites with shops for vouchers, spa packages or dining experiences for £1,500 to £3,500. Builds take three to five weeks for smaller sites and five to twelve weeks for e-commerce. We connect your existing booking engine rather than replacing it. Our web design guide for Riyadh explains hosting, speed and bilingual layout in more detail.

We write in English. Arabic content comes from your team or a translator, and we build pages so English and Arabic versions sit together cleanly, with right-to-left layout where needed.

SEO for districts, venues and guest types

Guests rarely search for a hotel by name until they already know you. They search for things like a hotel near a specific business district, serviced apartments for a month-long stay or a family hotel close to a major mall. We build landing pages for these real searches, add hotel structured data so search engines understand your rooms and amenities, and fix technical problems that slow pages down.

We also build event pages ahead of major conferences and seasons so that, when visitors start searching, your hotel already appears. Our SEO guide for Riyadh shows how we plan this kind of work. Most hotels see SEO results build over three to six months.

Google Ads to protect your brand and win searches

One of the simplest wins for hotels is a brand campaign. When someone searches your hotel name, online travel agencies often bid on it and push their own listing above yours. A brand campaign keeps your official site at the top, with a direct-booking message.

Beyond brand, we run campaigns around districts, event dates and guest types, with conversion tracking connected to your booking engine where it supports it. Our Google Ads guide for Riyadh covers account structure and budget planning. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and separate from our fee.

Meta Ads for leisure and family stays

Meta Ads help you reach people before they start searching. We run campaigns for weekend packages, family stays, Ramadan and Eid offers and entertainment season breaks, with retargeting for people who visited your site but did not book. Your team supplies photos and short phone clips; we do not film or edit video, but we can suggest what to capture.

Our Meta Ads guide for Saudi Arabia explains targeting and creative choices for the Saudi market more broadly.

Reviews, profiles and reputation

Guests read reviews before they book. We help you keep your Google profile complete and accurate, reply to reviews in a consistent tone and turn common feedback into website and marketing improvements. If a review highlights slow check-in, for example, we would rather help you fix the message and the expectation than hide it.

We also make sure your details, photos and amenities match across your website, Google and the main booking platforms, so guests see one clear picture of your hotel.

Restaurants, spas and meeting rooms

Many Riyadh hotels earn a large share of revenue from outlets: restaurants, cafés, spas and meeting rooms. Each needs its own page, its own Google profile where appropriate and its own small campaign. Our restaurant marketing guide for Riyadh covers outlet marketing in more depth, and the hospitality sector page shows how we approach hotels and venues together.

AI-powered tools, used carefully

We use AI-powered tools to group search terms, analyse review themes and draft first versions of ad copy and page outlines. Everything is edited and checked by a person before it goes live. AI helps us work faster and spot patterns; your brand voice and guest experience remain in human hands.

Measuring what matters

Hotel marketing only improves when you can see what is working. We set up tracking so you can tell which channels bring booking engine visits, completed bookings where your engine allows it, calls and enquiry forms. Each monthly report compares direct bookings with the previous period and shows which campaigns, pages and searches contributed, in plain language rather than a wall of charts.

We also look at the full picture with your revenue team: occupancy patterns, busy and quiet weeks and the share of bookings coming from travel agencies. That helps us shift budget towards the dates and guest types you most need to fill, rather than spending evenly across the year.
