What SEO looks like for Cambridge businesses

Cambridge has an unusual business mix: technology and life sciences companies, professional services, independent retailers, hospitality serving students and visitors, and trades serving a growing population in the city and surrounding towns such as Ely, Newmarket, St Ives and Huntingdon. Each needs a different SEO approach.

  • Local service businesses need strong Google Business Profiles, location pages and reviews to show in the map pack.
  • Technology and B2B firms often sell nationally or internationally, so they need content that ranks for specialist, high-intent searches rather than local terms.
  • Hospitality and retail need accurate listings, opening hours and pages that answer visitor questions.
  • Ecommerce brands based in Cambridge need product and category SEO aimed at UK-wide searches.

Local SEO in Cambridge

For businesses serving customers in person, the Google Business Profile is often the biggest single lever. We check categories, services, photos, opening hours and the description, and set up a simple process for asking customers for reviews. Our Google Business Profile guide covers the setup in detail.

If you serve several towns across Cambridgeshire, we create genuinely useful location pages rather than thin copies with the town name swapped. Each page should explain the services you offer there, how you work with customers in that area and answers to common local questions.

Technical SEO and your website

Many Cambridge businesses have websites that look fine but hold back their search performance: slow pages, duplicate content, broken redirects from an old redesign, or important pages that are hard for Google to reach. We start every engagement with a technical review, following the steps in our technical SEO audit checklist, and fix the issues that matter most first.

If your site needs a rebuild rather than fixes, we also design and build websites on WordPress and Shopify, with SEO planned in from the start.

Content for expert and specialist businesses

Cambridge has many businesses whose customers search for specialist terms. Ranking for those searches needs content that explains complex services clearly and shows genuine expertise. We research keywords, plan pages around customer questions, and write or edit content with your team, who know the subject best. Our keyword research guide shows how we find the right topics.

For regulated sectors such as healthcare or financial services, we keep content general and factual, and approval for compliance always rests with your own team or adviser.

How long SEO takes

SEO usually takes 3 to 6 months to show steady movement, sometimes longer in competitive markets or for newer websites. Early months typically focus on technical fixes, profile improvements and the most important pages. Content and authority work then builds over time. If you need enquiries sooner, Google Ads can bring traffic while organic rankings grow, and we manage both. Our SEO and GEO service page explains how we combine them.

How we report on SEO

Each month we share a short report showing rankings for target searches, organic traffic, enquiries or sales from organic search, and the work completed. We explain what changed and what we plan next. We do not promise specific rankings, because nobody controls Google's results, but we are clear about what we are doing and why.

Choosing an SEO agency in Cambridge

Whether you work with us or another agency, ask a few questions before signing:

  1. What will you do in the first three months, specifically?
  2. How will you measure success beyond rankings?
  3. Who owns the content, accounts and data?
  4. How do you earn links, and do you avoid buying them?
  5. What is the notice period on the contract?

Our guide to choosing a local SEO agency in the UK covers more of the warning signs to look out for.

A typical first three months of SEO

Every site is different, but most Cambridge SEO engagements follow a similar shape in the early months, so you know what to expect before results start to show.

  • Month one. Technical review, Search Console and Analytics checks, keyword research and a prioritised plan. We fix urgent issues such as broken redirects, indexing problems or missing titles straight away.
  • Month two. Improvements to the most important service or product pages, Google Business Profile updates, and internal linking so key pages are easier to reach.
  • Month three. New content aimed at gaps in your current coverage, a review process for customer feedback, and early authority work such as identifying genuine link opportunities.

From there, the work moves into a steady rhythm of content, technical upkeep and authority building, adjusted each month based on what the data shows. If your plans change, such as adding a new service or opening to customers in a new town, we update the plan rather than sticking rigidly to the original list. We also share a simple log of every change made to the site, so your team can see exactly what has been updated and when, and so nothing conflicts with work your own developers or content writers are doing at the same time.

SEO for Cambridge businesses selling beyond the city

Many Cambridge companies sell across the UK or overseas. For them, local SEO is only a small part of the picture. The focus shifts to national keywords, comparison and problem-led content, and making sure the site is technically ready for larger volumes of pages. For businesses selling into several countries, we also plan language and country targeting carefully. Prism writes in English, so any content in other languages comes from your team or a translator. Online shops can also add marketplace SEO, improving how listings rank on Amazon and similar marketplaces alongside their own site.
