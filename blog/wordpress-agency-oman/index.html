Bilingual WordPress done properly

Most Omani businesses need Arabic and English. On WordPress, that usually means a translation plugin that gives each page a version in each language, with separate URLs and the right language tags. Arabic pages need right-to-left layouts, mirrored menus and clear Arabic fonts. The language switcher should take visitors to the same page in the other language, not back to the home page.

We set this up at the start, so your team can add new pages in both languages without help. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website explains the setup in more depth.

Building a site your team can manage

Many Omani businesses tell us their current site is hard to edit. Small text changes need a developer, or a page builder breaks the layout every time someone touches it. We build with the WordPress block editor and reusable patterns, so your team can update text, add images and create new pages that match the design. Our guides to the WordPress block editor and WordPress user roles show how we keep editing safe and simple.

At handover, we give your team a short training call and a written guide covering the tasks they do most often.

Speed and hosting

Slow WordPress sites usually suffer from heavy themes, too many plugins, large images and cheap hosting. Visitors in Oman mostly browse on phones, so speed matters. We trim plugins, compress images, set up caching and choose hosting with good performance for Gulf visitors. Our WordPress plugin audit guide shows how we decide what stays and what goes, and our WordPress hosting guide covers how to choose a host.

Security and ongoing care

WordPress is safe when it is kept up to date and set up with care. Problems usually come from old plugins, weak passwords and missing backups. Our care work includes:

  • Regular updates to WordPress, themes and plugins, tested first where possible.
  • Daily or weekly backups stored away from the main server.
  • Uptime and security monitoring.
  • Removing unused plugins and admin accounts.
  • A short monthly report on what was done.

Read our WordPress security guide for the checks we use.

WooCommerce for Omani shops

If you sell online, WooCommerce can turn your WordPress site into a shop. We set up products, categories, delivery zones across governorates and the payment gateway you choose. You should check which gateways are available to your business through your bank or payment provider, and we connect the one you select. Cash on delivery can also be offered if you support it. For larger shops, Shopify may be a better fit, and we can help you compare.

SEO on WordPress

A WordPress site is a good base for SEO, but only if the technical setup is right. We set clean URLs, page titles, structured data, an XML sitemap and language tags for both versions. After launch, our SEO retainers add content, fix technical issues and improve local search for each branch. Read more on our SEO and GEO service page, or see our guide to SEO in Oman for the local search side.

Common WordPress problems we fix

Typical issues we see on Omani WordPress sites include broken Arabic layouts after a theme update, contact forms that stop sending emails, mixed content warnings after moving to HTTPS, and critical errors after a plugin update. We fix these quickly and then look at why they happened, so they do not keep coming back. If you need a full rebuild instead, our web design in Oman guide covers that route.

How we work with Omani businesses

Oman is usually three or four hours ahead of the UK, depending on the season, so morning calls in the UK fall in the Omani working afternoon. Every project has a clear plan, a preview link and a shared folder. You keep ownership of your domain, hosting, website and analytics at all times.

Moving an existing site to a better build

Some Omani businesses have a WordPress site that has grown untidy over the years: a theme that is no longer supported, dozens of plugins, pages built with three different page builders and an Arabic version that only covers half the site. Rather than patching it forever, it is often better to rebuild on a clean base while keeping the content and rankings you already have.

A careful move follows a few steps. We audit the current site and list every page that gets traffic or links. We plan the new structure and decide which pages to keep, merge or remove. We build the new site on a private preview, move the content across and set up redirects from every old URL to its new home. After launch, we watch Search Console for errors and fix them quickly. Done this way, a rebuild should protect your search visibility rather than reset it.

Forms, enquiries and integrations

For most Omani service businesses, the website exists to generate enquiries. We make sure every form works, sends to the right inbox and arrives reliably, which often means setting up proper email sending rather than relying on the server's default mail. We add WhatsApp and click-to-call buttons, and connect forms to your CRM or a simple spreadsheet if you use one. Analytics events then show which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries.

We can also connect booking tools, newsletter sign-ups, careers pages and document downloads. Where AI-powered tools help, such as sorting enquiries or drafting first replies for your team to check, we can set them up, with a person always reviewing before anything is sent.

Choosing between WordPress and Shopify

WordPress is our first choice for service businesses, publishers and companies that want full control over content. For shops with a large catalogue or a strong focus on online sales, Shopify can be simpler to run day to day. We are happy to compare both for your business and recommend the one that fits, rather than pushing a single platform.
