Web Design for Zurich Businesses: Fast, Multilingual Websites Built Remotely

Web design for Zurich businesses: fast, multilingual sites built remotely by a UK-based studio. Small sites £1,000 to £2,000; Swiss franc prices on request.

Key takeaways

  • Web design for a Zurich business should deliver a fast, clear and trustworthy site that works in the languages your customers use.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Zurich or Switzerland; German, French or Italian copy comes from your team or a translator.
  • Small websites cost £1,000 to £2,000, online shops £1,500 to £3,500, and Swiss franc pricing is quoted on request.

Web design for a Zurich business means building a fast, polished website that earns trust quickly and turns visitors into enquiries or sales. Prism Works Studio designs and builds websites for Zurich companies, working remotely from the UK, with small sites typically live in 3 to 5 weeks.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in Zurich or anywhere in Switzerland. We work through video calls, shared design previews and written updates. We write in English. German, French or Italian copy comes from your own team or a professional translator, and we build the site so each language version works properly for visitors and search engines.

Who we build websites for in Zurich

Zurich is a business and finance centre with high expectations for quality. Buyers are used to well-made, precise brands, and a dated or slow website can cost you a shortlist place before anyone calls. We work with:

  • Professional services firms such as consultancies, law-adjacent services and advisers that need a credible, clear site.
  • Tech and software companies that need product pages, sign-up flows and international reach.
  • B2B suppliers and manufacturers that want enquiries from buyers in Switzerland and abroad.
  • Online shops selling to Swiss customers and, in some cases, into the EU.
  • Hospitality, wellness and lifestyle brands that rely on strong visuals and simple booking.

If you sell to other companies, our B2B marketing page explains how we connect websites, search and ads for longer sales cycles.

Web design in Zurich: what a good site needs

A good business website does a few jobs well. It explains what you do in seconds. It loads quickly on phones. It gives visitors proof and detail. And it makes the next step obvious, whether that is a call, a form or a purchase. Everything else supports those jobs.

Multilingual websites for Zurich

Zurich is in the German-speaking part of Switzerland, but many companies also serve French- and Italian-speaking regions or work in English with international clients. Language planning is one of the most important decisions in a Swiss web project.

We help you decide which languages you need at launch and which can follow later. Each version gets its own address and correct language tags, and visitors can switch easily. We build the structure and write English content. Swiss German, French or Italian copy comes from your team or a translator, and we check the finished pages for layout, headings and links. Getting this right early avoids the common problem of one language ranking well while another is invisible.

WordPress or Shopify?

Most of the Zurich sites we build run on WordPress or Shopify. WordPress suits service businesses, content-rich sites and companies that want full control over pages. Shopify suits shops that want a reliable checkout and simple product management.

If you choose WordPress, we keep the build lean and easy to manage. Our guides on WordPress for small business websites and WordPress security explain how we keep sites stable and safe. For shops, our Shopify website cost guide shows what affects the price and timeline.

Design that earns trust

Trust is especially important in Zurich, where many buyers are cautious and detail-focused. We design with plenty of space, clear typography, honest copy and a calm layout. We avoid gimmicks that slow the page or distract from your message.

Landing pages deserve special care if you run ads or campaigns. Our landing page design guide explains how we structure pages so visitors understand the offer and act. We also make sure each page has a single clear goal rather than several competing ones.

Speed, accessibility and search

We build with performance in mind from the start. Our Core Web Vitals guide explains the speed measures Google uses and how we meet them. We test on real phones as well as desktops, because many first visits happen on mobile.

Accessibility helps every visitor and is increasingly expected for sites selling to consumers in Europe. Switzerland has its own rules, and companies selling into the EU may also need to consider EU requirements. Our overview of the European Accessibility Act covers the EU side in general terms. For your exact obligations, check official guidance or ask your own adviser.

Data protection and legal pages

Swiss websites usually need a clear privacy policy and a legal notice, and Switzerland has its own data protection law alongside EU rules for companies that serve EU customers. We set up forms, analytics and cookie consent so they respect visitor choices. The legal wording and final sign-off rest with you and your adviser. If you work in a regulated area such as finance, compliance approval of all content rests with your own compliance team.

How a remote web project works

Zurich is one hour ahead of the UK for most of the year, so it is easy to find shared time for calls. Our process keeps decisions clear and documented.

  1. Discovery: we agree goals, pages, languages and integrations.
  2. Structure and wireframes: we plan the sitemap and page layouts.
  3. Design: we share visual designs for your feedback.
  4. Build: we develop the site on a private preview link.
  5. Content and translation: we add English copy and your translated copy.
  6. Testing and launch: we check speed, forms, tracking and languages, then go live.

A small business website typically takes 3 to 5 weeks. An online shop takes 5 to 12 weeks depending on products, payments and integrations. Multilingual projects can take longer if translation arrives late, so we plan content deadlines early.

After launch

A website works best when it keeps improving. Many Zurich clients add an SEO or paid media retainer after launch so the new site starts bringing in visitors. We also offer AI-powered marketing support, such as faster content planning and smarter reporting, always reviewed by people before anything goes live.

Common website problems we fix for Zurich firms

Many Zurich companies come to us with a site that looks fine but does not perform. Common problems include slow pages caused by heavy images or themes, language versions that are hard to find, contact forms that fail quietly, and service pages that say too little. We review your current site first, keep what works and fix what does not, with redirects in place to protect any search traffic you already have.

Keeping your site up to date

Launch is only the start. Zurich businesses change services, add team updates and publish news, so the site needs to be easy to edit. We set up simple editing screens, train your team at handover and leave clear notes. If you prefer, we can handle updates, plugin maintenance and small improvements as part of an ongoing retainer, with changes agreed in writing each month.
