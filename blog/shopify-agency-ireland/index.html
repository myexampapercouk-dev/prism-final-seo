Shopify Agency for Irish Brands: Builds, Migrations and Growth

Looking for a Shopify agency in Ireland? What a remote UK studio can build, how Irish and UK selling fits together, timelines, pricing and what to ask first.

Irish ecommerce brands range from food and drink producers to fashion, gifts, homeware, beauty and outdoor gear. Most sell domestically first, many ship to the UK, and some sell across the EU. A good agency plans the store around that reality rather than a generic template.

Selling to Ireland, the UK and the EU from one Shopify store

Many Irish brands find the UK is their second-largest market. Since the UK left the EU, selling across the Irish Sea involves customs and tax considerations that did not apply before, and selling into other EU countries has its own VAT arrangements. Shopify can handle multiple currencies, prices and delivery options from one store, but the setup needs care.

  • Currencies. Euro for Irish and EU customers, sterling for UK customers, with rounding rules that keep prices tidy.
  • Delivery zones. Separate rates and timeframes for Ireland, Northern Ireland, Great Britain and the rest of Europe, explained plainly at checkout.
  • Tax settings. Configured with your accountant, because the right setup depends on your business, your sales volumes and current rules.
  • Duties and returns. Clear information so customers are not surprised by charges or confused about where to send returns.

We set up the store structure and display; your accountant confirms the tax position. For a deeper look at multi-country setup, see our guide to Shopify Markets for Europe.

Shopify migrations for Irish businesses

Plenty of Irish stores start on WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or Etsy and move to Shopify as they grow. A careful migration moves products, variants, images, customers and order history, keeps URLs or redirects them properly, and protects the search rankings you have already earned.

The main risks are lost redirects, broken images, missing product data and changed page structures that confuse search engines. We plan a full URL map, test on a staging copy and check everything again after launch. If you are moving from WordPress, our WooCommerce to Shopify migration guide explains the process step by step, and larger brands on Shopify Plus can ask about our Plus migration work.

Growing an Irish Shopify store after launch

A new store needs steady traffic and a site that turns visits into orders. Most Irish brands get the best results from a mix of search, paid ads and email rather than any single channel.

  1. Search. Clean collection and product pages, useful copy and a tidy technical setup. Our Shopify SEO guide covers the basics.
  2. Paid ads. Google Shopping and Meta ads targeted by country, with budgets split between Ireland and the UK where both matter.
  3. Email. Welcome flows, abandoned basket reminders and post-purchase messages, sent with proper consent.
  4. Conversion. Faster pages, clearer delivery information and simpler checkout steps, tested and improved over time.

Our Shopify conversion rate optimisation guide sets out the changes that most often make a difference to a growing store.

Designing a Shopify store that Irish shoppers trust

Irish shoppers buy from Irish, UK and international stores every day, so a new store has to earn trust quickly. The details that build confidence are usually practical rather than decorative, and they matter most on mobile, where most browsing happens.

  • Clear delivery promises. Show delivery costs and typical timeframes for Ireland early, on product pages and in the basket, not only at checkout.
  • Prices in euro. Irish customers expect to see euro prices without converting from sterling or dollars in their heads.
  • Easy returns. A plain-English returns page that explains the process and where items go back to.
  • Visible contact details. A real email address and clear business details, so customers know who they are buying from.
  • Honest product information. Accurate descriptions, sizes, materials and good photography that match what arrives.

Our guide to website trust signals goes into more detail on the elements that help visitors feel safe placing an order.

Brands that make or grow their products in Ireland often have a strong story to tell, whether that is a family farm, a small production kitchen or a workshop. We build that story into collection pages, product copy and the about page so it supports sales rather than sitting in a forgotten corner of the site. Good product photography and clear, specific copy usually do more for conversion than extra apps or animations.

Speed matters too. Every app, script and oversized image adds weight to a page, and Irish shoppers on mobile data will leave a slow store. We keep the theme lean, review apps before installing them and test on real devices before launch.

Working remotely with a UK Shopify agency

Ireland and the UK share a time zone and a language, so working with a UK studio feels much like working with a local one. We run projects through video calls, a shared task board and written updates, and you keep full ownership of the store, domain, apps and data from day one.

Before hiring any agency, Irish or otherwise, ask these questions:

  • Have you set up stores that sell in both euro and sterling?
  • How will you handle redirects and SEO during a migration?
  • Who owns the store, theme, apps and accounts?
  • What happens after launch, and what support is included?
  • How do you work with our accountant on tax settings?

FAQs

Do you have an office in Ireland?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK and works remotely with Irish businesses. We share a time zone with Ireland, so calls and working hours line up easily, and all project work happens in shared online tools.

How much does a Shopify agency in Ireland cost?

Our Shopify builds typically cost £1,500 to £3,500, priced in pounds, with euro quotes available on request. Ongoing marketing retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, with ad spend paid separately.

Can one Shopify store sell in euro and sterling?

Yes. Shopify Markets lets one store show local currencies, prices and delivery options for different countries. The tax side should be confirmed with your accountant, as it depends on your business and current rules.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Most ecommerce builds take five to twelve weeks. Simple stores are quicker, while large catalogues, custom features and migrations from other platforms take longer. Having product data ready early helps most.

Can you migrate our store to Shopify without losing rankings?

We plan migrations with a full URL map, redirects and post-launch checks to protect existing rankings. No agency can guarantee rankings, but careful planning greatly reduces the risk of losing traffic.

Can you build Irish-language pages?

Prism writes in English. If you want pages in Irish, we set up the structure and work with your team or a translator on the copy, so each language version reads naturally.
