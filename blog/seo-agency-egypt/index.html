SEO Agency for Egypt: Remote SEO for Egyptian Businesses

Prism Works Studio provides technical SEO, content planning, local search and AI search visibility for businesses in Cairo, Alexandria, Giza and across Egypt, working remotely from the UK.

Who we help in Egypt

Egypt has a large, young and mobile-first online audience, and competition in search is growing in almost every sector. We work with:

  • Online shops selling fashion, home goods, electronics accessories or beauty products.
  • Service businesses such as clinics, training centres, agencies and consultancies.
  • Exporters and manufacturers that want to be found by buyers in the Gulf, Europe and the UK.
  • Real estate and property firms marketing new compounds and developments.
  • Software and tech companies building products for regional or global markets.

Technical SEO first

Many Egyptian websites carry the same technical problems: slow themes, oversized images, duplicate pages for each language, broken redirects after a redesign and product filters that create thousands of near-identical URLs. Search engines waste time on these pages and may never reach the ones that matter.

We start every project with a technical review. We check how Google crawls and indexes the site, how quickly key pages load on a mid-range phone, whether Arabic and English pages are linked correctly, and whether structured data is in place. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows the kind of checks we run. Fixes are then ranked by impact, so the most important ones are done first.

Arabic, English and mixed searches

Search behaviour in Egypt is varied. One customer types a query in Arabic, another uses English, and a third writes Arabic words in Latin letters. A shop selling air conditioners may see searches in all three forms. Brand names and product models are often typed in English even inside an Arabic query.

We map these variations during keyword research and decide which page should target each group of searches. Usually that means one Arabic page and one English page for each main topic, linked with the right language tags so Google shows the correct version. We write the English content, and your team or a translator writes the Arabic, ideally in a natural Egyptian-friendly tone rather than a word-for-word translation. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website covers the build side in more detail.

Content that earns rankings

Ranking in Cairo for competitive terms takes more than a short service description. Pages need to answer the questions customers ask before they buy. For a service business, that could be what the service includes, who it suits, how long it takes and how to book. For an online shop, it means useful category pages, detailed product descriptions and buying guides.

We plan content in a simple calendar, starting with pages closest to a sale. Each month we add or improve a set number of pages, depending on your retainer. If you run an online store, our ecommerce SEO checklist lists the basics we check on every shop.

Local SEO for Cairo, Alexandria and Giza

If customers visit you in person, local search matters. People search for "near me" results, district names such as Maadi, Heliopolis, Zamalek or New Cairo, and city names. We help you:

  • Set up or clean up each branch's Google Business Profile with accurate categories, hours and photos you supply.
  • Build a page for each branch or service area, with address, map and directions.
  • Keep your business name, address and phone number consistent across the web.
  • Ask customers for reviews in a way that follows Google's rules.
  • Track calls, direction requests and WhatsApp clicks from your profiles.

Marketplaces alongside your own site

Many Egyptian shoppers start their search on marketplaces such as Amazon, Noon and Jumia. If you sell there, marketplace listing SEO can work alongside your own website. We improve product titles, bullet points, descriptions and backend search terms within each platform's rules, while your website builds brand searches and direct orders. For a wider view of how we handle marketplace and Gulf search, see our guides to Noon SEO in the UAE and SEO in Dubai, which share many of the same methods.

Reaching buyers outside Egypt

Egyptian exporters, software firms and service providers often want leads from the Gulf, Europe or the UK. That is a different SEO job: separate pages or sections for each target market, content that speaks to foreign buyers, and correct language and region tags. We plan international structure carefully so your Egyptian pages and export pages do not compete with each other. If you also target Jordan or the wider Levant, our guide to SEO in Jordan covers that market.

AI search and SEO in Egypt

AI summaries and chat-based search tools are changing how people find answers. These tools tend to quote pages that give short, direct answers and use clear headings and structured data. We write and structure content so it is easy to quote, without chasing tricks. We also use AI-powered tools to speed up research and drafting, and every piece is checked by a person and approved by you. Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO explains the approach.

How we report

Each month you get a written report covering:

  • Search visibility for your main Arabic and English keywords.
  • Organic visits, enquiries, calls and orders from search.
  • Technical issues fixed and any new ones found.
  • Pages published or improved that month.
  • Our plan for the next month.

We keep reports short and plain. If something is not working, we say so and suggest a change.

How long SEO takes

SEO typically takes 3 to 6 months to show steady results. Technical fixes can help sooner, especially on sites with serious indexing problems, while content and local work build over time. Very competitive terms in Cairo can take longer. If you need enquiries quickly, Google Ads can run alongside SEO while rankings grow.

Working with a remote studio

We are based in the UK and work with Egyptian businesses remotely. Egypt is usually one or two hours ahead of the UK, depending on the time of year, so there is a good overlap in working hours for calls. You will have a shared folder for documents, a clear task list and a fixed monthly report. Website access, analytics and Search Console stay in your name, so you always own your data.
