Why Antwerp brands choose Shopify

Antwerp has a strong retail and design identity. The city is known for fashion, diamonds and jewellery, interiors and design, food and drink, and it sits next to one of Europe's largest ports, which makes it a natural base for brands that ship across the continent. Many Antwerp businesses sell to Flemish customers first, then expand into Wallonia, the Netherlands, France and Germany.

Shopify suits this kind of growth. It handles payments, stock, shipping and taxes reliably, needs little technical upkeep and scales from a first collection to a large catalogue. With Shopify Markets, one store can serve several countries with local languages, currencies and prices, which matters in a country where shoppers expect to be addressed in their own language.

Selling to Belgian shoppers

Belgian shoppers have clear expectations. They want to pay with familiar methods, see delivery costs and times before checkout and return items easily. Bancontact is widely used in Belgium, so it should be available at checkout alongside cards and other common wallets. Delivery options often include home delivery and pick-up points, and many shoppers prefer collecting parcels near home or work.

We build product pages and checkout flows around these expectations. That means visible delivery and returns information, prices that include VAT where required, trust signals such as clear contact details and policies, and a checkout with as few steps as possible. For VAT, consumer law and returns rules, check official guidance or ask your own adviser, as approval of legal wording rests with you.

Dutch, French and English stores

Antwerp is in Flanders, so most local customers read Dutch, but Belgium also has a large French-speaking population, and many brands sell to English speakers and international buyers. A store aimed at the whole country should usually offer at least Dutch and French.

We use Shopify Markets and a clear language structure so each version has its own URLs, titles and descriptions, with hreflang tags telling search engines which page is which. Flemish Dutch has its own preferences in vocabulary compared with Dutch used in the Netherlands, so your translator should write with your main audience in mind. Our guide to Shopify Markets for Europe explains the setup in more detail.

Expanding into the Netherlands and beyond

Many Antwerp brands treat the Netherlands as their first export market, since the language, delivery networks and shopping habits are close. Dutch shoppers commonly expect iDEAL at checkout, and delivery times and costs need to be shown just as clearly. Our guide to Shopify for Dutch brands covers this market in more depth.

Marketplaces can also play a part. bol is a major marketplace in both Belgium and the Netherlands, and listing selected products there can reach shoppers who start their search on the marketplace rather than on Google. We offer marketplace listing SEO alongside Shopify work, and our guide to bol marketplace SEO explains how to improve product visibility there.

Store design that sells

Antwerp brands often have a strong visual identity, and the store should reflect it without slowing down. We design around your brand, then make sure the basics are right: fast-loading product images, clear sizing or specification information, simple navigation, helpful filters on larger catalogues and product pages that answer the questions shoppers ask before they buy.

For fashion and jewellery brands, that means detailed product photography you supply, size guides, care information and clear returns terms. For interiors and design brands, it means dimensions, materials, lead times and delivery details. For food and drink, it means ingredients, allergens and shipping rules. Every store is tested on phones before launch, because most shoppers arrive on mobile.

Migrating to Shopify without losing traffic

If you already sell online on another platform, moving to Shopify needs careful planning. We move products, collections, customers and order history where the platform allows it, rebuild key pages and map every old URL to its new home with redirects. Without redirects, a migration can lose rankings that took years to earn.

We also check structured data, image alt text, internal links and page titles after launch, then monitor search performance closely in the first weeks.

Our guides to SEO in Brussels and Google Ads in Brussels explain how we approach search in Belgium, and our e-commerce marketing page shows how we combine channels.

Apps, integrations and running costs

Shopify's app store makes it easy to add features, but every app adds cost and can slow the store down. We keep the app list short and choose tools that do a clear job, such as reviews, subscriptions, product bundles or shipping labels. Where the theme can handle a feature natively, we use that instead.

Many Antwerp brands also need the store to connect with other systems: accounting software, a warehouse or fulfilment partner, an ERP for wholesale customers or a point-of-sale system in a physical shop. We plan these connections early, because they shape how products, stock and orders flow. We also explain the running costs you will pay directly, such as your Shopify plan, any paid apps, payment fees and your theme licence, so there are no surprises after launch.

Finally, we set up analytics and conversion tracking properly, with cookie consent that follows European privacy rules, so you can see which channels and products drive sales from day one.
