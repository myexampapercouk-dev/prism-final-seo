Real Estate Marketing in Doha: Websites, SEO and Ads for Developers and Brokers

Real estate marketing in Doha: project websites, Arabic and English SEO, Google and Meta ads and lead tracking for builders and brokers from a UK-based studio.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • Real estate marketing in Doha combines clear project and listing pages, Arabic and English SEO, targeted ads and fast follow-up of every enquiry, often through WhatsApp.
  • We are a UK-based studio that provides websites, SEO and paid media remotely for Doha developers and brokers, with no office in Qatar, and Arabic content is written with your team or a translator.
  • Retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, ad spend is separate, compliance approval for property ads rests with you, and Qatari riyal pricing is quoted on request.

Real estate marketing in Doha means helping developers, brokers and property companies reach buyers, tenants and investors through project websites, search engine optimisation, paid advertising and organised lead follow-up, in Arabic and English. Doha's property market serves Qatari nationals, a large expatriate population and overseas investors, so clear information and fast, professional responses make a real difference.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with real estate businesses in Doha through our paid media service, alongside SEO and website development. We have no office in Qatar and work through video calls, shared documents and your own advertising and analytics accounts. This guide explains how we approach property marketing in Doha, what it costs and where the main risks lie.

Who this guide is for

Doha's real estate sector includes several types of business, each with different marketing needs:

  • Developers launching residential, commercial or mixed-use projects, including off-plan units.
  • Brokers and agencies listing apartments, villas, townhouses and offices for sale or rent.
  • Property managers looking for landlords and tenants across the city.
  • Commercial specialists leasing offices, retail units, warehouses and land.

Each needs a different mix of channels, but the foundations are the same: a credible website, accurate information, good search visibility and reliable tracking of every enquiry.

Websites for developers and brokers

A property website has to answer the questions buyers, tenants and investors ask before they enquire: where the property is, what unit types are available, prices or price ranges where you choose to publish them, construction stage, payment plans, amenities, nearby schools and transport, and how to book a viewing. Vague brochure pages with a single form rarely convert well.

For developers we usually build a dedicated page or microsite for each major project, with floor plans, galleries, location details, a short video and clear enquiry options. For brokers we focus on well-organised listings, area guides and simple search. Every page is built for phones first. Our guide to web design in Qatar explains how we build for the Qatari market, and our bilingual Arabic and English website guide covers right-to-left layouts. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator.

Landing pages for campaigns follow the same principles: one project, one clear offer and one obvious next step. Our landing page design guide explains what makes them work.

SEO for Doha property businesses

People search for property in many ways: by area, such as The Pearl, Lusail or West Bay, by property type, by budget, by number of bedrooms and by whether they want to buy or rent. A strong SEO plan builds pages around those searches in both languages, supported by helpful guides on topics such as renting in Doha, buying as an expatriate where permitted, or choosing an area.

We also optimise your Google Business Profile, improve site speed and fix technical issues that stop listings being indexed. Our SEO guide for Qatar explains our wider approach. SEO usually takes three to six months to show meaningful progress, so it works best alongside paid campaigns that bring enquiries sooner.

Paid advertising for Doha real estate

Google Ads captures people already searching for property, while Meta, TikTok and YouTube help you reach people before they start searching. For launches, a mix of both usually works best. Our Google Ads guide for Qatar and Meta Ads guide for Qatar explain how we run those channels locally.

Property advertising needs careful setup. We separate campaigns by language, project and audience, exclude irrelevant searches and track which campaigns produce qualified leads, not just form fills. Where possible, we feed lead quality back into the ad platforms so they can optimise towards serious buyers and tenants. Meta and Google apply special rules to housing ads in some countries, and policies change, so we check current requirements for every campaign.

Video, photography and content

Property is a visual purchase, and the quality of your photos and video shapes how buyers judge a project before they visit. Wide, well-lit photography, accurate floor plans, short walkthrough videos and drone footage of the surrounding area all help people picture themselves living or working there. For off-plan projects, renders and show-unit footage need to be clearly labelled so expectations stay realistic.

We plan the content each project needs, write briefs for your photographer or production company and make sure the finished assets are used consistently across your website, portals and ads. Vertical cuts for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts are planned from the start, so one shoot produces material for every channel.

Written content matters too. Area guides, explanations of the buying or renting process, service charge information and answers to common questions help your website rank and save your sales team time. We write in English, and Arabic versions are prepared with your team or a professional translator.

Reaching overseas investors

Some Doha projects attract buyers from other Gulf countries and further afield. Campaigns for overseas audiences need their own targeting, messaging and time zones for follow-up, and the website should explain clearly what foreign buyers can and cannot purchase, with a reminder to take independent advice.

Lead handling and follow-up

Many property enquiries in Doha come through WhatsApp, calls or instant forms, and speed matters. A lead contacted within minutes is far more likely to book a viewing than one contacted the next day. We help you set up a simple process:

  1. Capture every enquiry with its source, campaign and project.
  2. Route it to the right agent or sales team immediately.
  3. Respond quickly in the enquirer's preferred language.
  4. Record the outcome, such as viewing booked, offer made or unqualified.
  5. Review results monthly so budget moves to the campaigns that produce real deals.

We work with whichever CRM you already use, or suggest simple options if you do not have one yet.

Compliance and accuracy

Property advertising in Qatar is subject to local rules, including requirements around licensing, ownership rights for non-Qatari buyers in designated areas and accurate representation of projects. We keep ads and pages clear and factual and flag anything that may need checking, but we do not give legal advice. Compliance approval of all property ads and pages rests with you and your own adviser, and you should refer to official guidance from the relevant Qatari authorities. Privacy also matters: consent for marketing messages and clear privacy notices should be confirmed with your adviser.
