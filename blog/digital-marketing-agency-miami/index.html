Digital Marketing Agency Miami: How Remote Marketing Works for Miami Businesses

Looking for a digital marketing agency in Miami? A remote team can run SEO, paid ads and websites for Miami firms by video call. Here is how it works and costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A digital marketing agency for a Miami business needs a plan built on your customers and US data, not a Brickell office.
  • Many Miami businesses serve English and Spanish speakers, so bilingual pages, ads and profiles are worth planning from the start.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US clients, with retainers priced in pounds and other currencies quoted on request.

If you are searching for a digital marketing agency in Miami, the key question is whether the agency can bring you more of the right customers through search, ads and your website, and prove it with clear reporting. A remote team can run SEO, paid ads and website work for Miami businesses by video call and shared tools, provided the plan is built around your audience and US data.

We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US businesses. We do not have a Miami office or any US office. This guide explains what digital marketing for a Miami business should include, how bilingual audiences and seasonal demand affect the plan, how remote work runs and what it costs.

What a digital marketing agency in Miami should cover

Miami's economy leans heavily on hospitality, tourism, real estate, health and wellness, professional services, retail and a growing technology scene. Most businesses there compete hard for attention, both from locals and from visitors. A good digital marketing plan usually draws on several channels:

  • SEO: helping your site and Google Business Profile appear for the searches your customers make.
  • Paid search: Google Ads for people actively looking for what you offer.
  • Paid social: Meta, TikTok and YouTube ads to reach people before they start searching.
  • Website improvements: faster pages, clearer offers and better booking or enquiry flows.
  • Tracking and reporting: knowing which channel produces calls, bookings and sales.

Not every business needs every channel. A dental practice may need local SEO and Google Ads above everything else, while a fashion brand may lean on Meta and TikTok. Our guide on how to choose a digital marketing agency explains how to match channels to your goals before you hire anyone.

Bilingual marketing for Miami audiences

Miami is one of the most bilingual cities in the United States, and many businesses serve customers who prefer English, Spanish or both. That affects almost every part of a marketing plan:

  • Website: separate, properly structured English and Spanish pages tend to work better than automatic translation widgets.
  • SEO: people search in both languages, often with different phrasing, so keyword research should cover each.
  • Ads: campaigns can be split by language, with ad copy and landing pages that match.
  • Google Business Profile: your profile, posts and replies to reviews can reflect the languages your customers use.
  • Reviews and trust: customers are reassured when they can read about your service in their preferred language.

Translation should be done or checked by a fluent speaker, ideally someone who knows how your customers talk. Our guide to multilingual website design covers page structure, language switching and SEO for sites in more than one language.

Seasonal demand and visitor traffic

Many Miami businesses see demand rise and fall with the tourist season, events and the weather. Hotels, restaurants, tours, rentals and beauty businesses often have busy winter months and quieter periods later in the year. A sensible plan reflects that:

  • Build SEO content and pages ahead of busy periods, because organic results take months to grow.
  • Increase paid budgets when demand is high and pull back when it is not.
  • Target visitors before they travel, for example people planning trips, as well as people already in the city.
  • Use quieter months for website improvements, content and testing.

Our guide to Google Ads location targeting explains how to reach people in your area and people searching about your area from elsewhere, which matters a great deal for visitor-led businesses. If you run a hotel or rental, our guide to hotel direct bookings covers how to win more bookings on your own site.

Local search across Miami-Dade

People rarely search for just the city name. They search for a service near them, or in a specific area such as Brickell, Wynwood, Coral Gables, Doral or Miami Beach. Local rankings depend on your own business address or service area, the completeness of your Google Business Profile, your reviews and how clearly your pages describe where you work. They do not depend on where your agency sits.

For a business with several locations, each one needs its own accurate profile and a genuinely useful page. For a mobile or service-area business, the aim is to describe the areas you cover honestly, without creating dozens of near-identical pages that add nothing for the reader. A remote agency can carry out all of this research and optimisation, while your real presence in the city supplies the local signals.

How remote digital marketing works

Digital marketing is carried out in ad platforms, analytics, content tools and your website, so it suits remote delivery well. Working with us usually looks like this:

  1. Discovery call: a video call about your customers, services, seasons, competitors and goals.
  2. Access: you add us as users on your ad accounts, analytics, Search Console and website. You keep ownership of everything.
  3. Audit and plan: a review of what is working now and a prioritised plan for the first three months.
  4. Delivery: campaign setup or restructuring, SEO work and website changes, carried out in monthly cycles.
  5. Reporting: a monthly written report and a short call to agree next steps.

Miami is usually five hours behind the UK. Our afternoon overlaps comfortably with your morning, which makes regular calls easy, and work requested at the end of your day is often under way before you return. Tracking matters more than ever when several channels run at once, so we check conversion tracking early. Our guide to UTM tracking links shows one simple way to keep channel reporting tidy.

What a digital marketing agency costs

Our retainers are priced in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platforms and is separate from our fees.

  • Launch: £350 a month, typically one main channel for a smaller business.
  • Growth: £750 a month, for two channels or more competitive markets.
  • Scale: £1,500 a month, for several channels, larger budgets or multiple locations.

Website projects are priced separately: small business websites are £1,000 to £2,000 and ecommerce websites £1,500 to £3,500. SEO and paid results typically take three to six months to show clearly. Our pricing page sets out what each level includes, and our paid media service and SEO and GEO service explain how each channel is run.

Questions to ask any Miami marketing agency

  • Which channels do you recommend for us, and why those first?
  • Who will manage our accounts each month?
  • How will you handle Spanish-language pages, ads or reviews?
  • How will you plan around our busy and quiet seasons?
  • Will we own every ad account, analytics property and piece of content?
  • What are the notice period and minimum term?

Regulated sectors, such as health, finance or real estate, have their own advertising rules. An agency can follow platform policies, but approval of claims and wording rests with your own compliance team or lawyer, so check current official guidance before campaigns go live.

FAQs

Do you have an office as a digital marketing agency in Miami?

No. We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US businesses, including companies in Miami. Meetings take place by video call and you add us as users on your own accounts.

How much does a digital marketing agency in Miami cost?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month. Prices are in pounds, other currencies are quoted on request, and ad spend is paid separately.

Can you market to Spanish-speaking customers?

We can plan bilingual websites, keyword research and campaigns split by language. Spanish copy should be written or checked by a fluent speaker who understands your customers, and we build that step into the plan.

Which channel should a Miami business start with?

It depends on how customers find you. Local services often start with Google Business Profile, SEO and Google Ads, while consumer brands may start with Meta or TikTok ads. A short audit usually makes the first step clear.

How long until we see results?

Paid campaigns can produce enquiries within weeks, but SEO and paid results typically take three to six months to show clearly as data builds and campaigns are refined.

Can you also build or redesign our website?

Yes. Small business websites are £1,000 to £2,000 and ecommerce websites £1,500 to £3,500, typically delivered in three to five weeks and five to twelve weeks respectively.
