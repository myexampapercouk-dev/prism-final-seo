Google Ads Agency for Madrid Businesses: Remote PPC Management Explained

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Madrid? How PPC works for Spanish and English searches, what a remote UK studio manages, what to ask and how fees work.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Google Ads for a Madrid business works best with campaigns split by language and location, accurate conversion tracking and landing pages that match each search.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Spain; we manage campaigns remotely, one hour behind Madrid time, and work with your team or a translator on Spanish ad copy.
  • Management retainers are priced in pounds, with euro quoted on request, and ad spend is always paid separately, directly to Google.

A Google Ads agency for a Madrid business should turn your ad budget into qualified enquiries or sales, with campaigns built around the searches your customers make in Spanish, English or both. That means sound account structure, accurate conversion tracking, careful keyword and location targeting, and landing pages that match what each searcher wants.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for businesses in Spain and across Europe. We have no office in Madrid or anywhere in Spain. Our paid media service is delivered online, one hour behind Madrid time, with shared reports, regular calls and full access to your own ad account at all times.

Google Ads agency Madrid: what good management looks like

Madrid is a large, competitive market for professional services, property, hospitality, education, ecommerce and B2B companies. Many businesses there serve both Spanish-speaking customers and international clients, visitors or expatriates who search in English. Good management reflects that reality rather than running one generic campaign.

  • Language-specific campaigns. Spanish and English searches handled in separate campaigns or ad groups, with ads and landing pages in the matching language.
  • Accurate location targeting. Ads shown to people in or interested in the right areas, whether that is central Madrid, the wider region, all of Spain or several countries.
  • Reliable tracking. Calls, forms, bookings and purchases measured properly, so bidding decisions rest on real results.
  • Tight keyword control. Relevant search terms, a maintained negative keyword list and regular search term reviews.
  • Clear reporting. Spend, leads, sales and cost per result explained in plain language.

Running Spanish and English campaigns side by side

Google Ads does not translate ads for you, and machine-translated copy often reads badly to native speakers. Spanish searchers may also use different terms from the ones an English keyword list suggests, including regional phrasing and English loanwords common in some sectors.

Prism writes in English. For Spanish ad copy, keyword checks and landing pages, we work alongside your own team or a native-speaking translator or copywriter you choose. We structure the account so each language is measured separately, which shows where your budget works hardest. Our international PPC agency guide explains how multi-language and multi-country accounts are usually organised.

Location targeting for Madrid and beyond

Location settings are one of the most common sources of wasted spend. By default, Google can show ads to people who are interested in a location as well as those physically there. That suits a hotel or language school attracting visitors, but may waste money for a local service business that only works within the city.

We set location options deliberately for each campaign and review location reports regularly. Our Google Ads location targeting guide covers the settings in detail.

Conversion tracking and privacy

Without accurate tracking, automated bidding simply guesses. We set up conversion actions for the results that matter to you, such as enquiries, calls and purchases, and test them before spending more. Our Google Ads conversion tracking guide explains the setup.

For visitors in Spain and the wider EU, consent rules affect how tracking and advertising cookies can be used. We build tracking that works with a consent setup, but the details of your consent banner and privacy information should be checked against current official guidance and, where needed, reviewed by a legal adviser.

Keywords, negatives and search terms

Broad keywords without negatives can quickly spend money on irrelevant searches, such as job seekers, students or people looking for free alternatives. We build negative keyword lists in both languages and review search terms regularly. Our negative keywords guide shows how this works in practice.

Performance Max and Shopping for Spanish online shops

Ecommerce brands selling in Spain often use Shopping campaigns and Performance Max alongside search. These rely heavily on product feed quality, conversion data and clear asset groups. Feeds should use the right language and currency for each country you sell into. Our Performance Max guide explains when it helps and when tighter control works better.

Landing pages that convert Madrid searchers

Ads can only do so much if the landing page is slow, generic or in the wrong language. A Spanish searcher clicking a Spanish ad should land on a Spanish page that matches the offer. We review landing pages as part of every account and can design or improve them where needed. Our landing page design guide covers what makes a page convert.

How a remote Google Ads engagement runs

Remote management works well when responsibilities are clear from day one. A typical engagement with Prism follows these steps:

  1. Introductory call. We discuss your services, markets, languages, margins and what a good lead or sale is worth to you.
  2. Access and audit. You grant access to your Google Ads, analytics and tag accounts, and we audit settings, tracking and wasted spend.
  3. Plan and copy. We propose an account structure and write English ads; Spanish copy is prepared with your team or translator and reviewed before launch.
  4. Launch and refine. Campaigns go live with agreed budgets, then we review search terms, bids and ads each week.
  5. Monthly review. A written report and call covering spend, results, lessons and next steps.

We agree in writing who approves ad copy, who changes landing pages and how quickly questions are answered. That keeps campaigns moving even when nobody is in the same room, and it means you always know what is happening with your budget and why.

FAQs

Are you a Google Ads agency in Madrid?

We are a UK-based studio managing Google Ads remotely for Madrid businesses. We have no office in Spain. Work runs through video calls, shared reports and direct access to your own Google Ads account.

Can you run Google Ads in Spanish?

Yes. We build and manage Spanish campaigns, with ad copy and keyword checks written or reviewed by your team or a native-speaking translator. English campaigns are written by us.

How much does a Google Ads agency cost for a Madrid business?

Our management retainers run from £350 to £1,500 per month depending on scope, priced in pounds with euro quoted on request. Ad spend is paid separately and directly to Google.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. We work in an account in your business's name, or link to your existing one, so you keep full access to the data, history and billing if you ever change agency.

Can you advertise in Spain and other countries at once?

Yes. We can run campaigns across several countries and languages from one account, with budgets, ads and reporting split by market so you can see where your spend performs best.
