Why Galway brands sell on Shopify

Galway is a creative, independent city on the west coast with a strong mix of craft, food and drink, fashion, gifts, outdoor gear, wellness products and tourism-linked retail. Many Galway makers start selling at markets or in local shops and then move online to reach customers across Ireland, the UK, the EU and North America. Shopify suits them because it offers:

  • A hosted platform with no servers to maintain.
  • A reliable checkout with cards, wallets and buy-now-pay-later options.
  • Shopify Markets for selling in several currencies and countries from one store.
  • Apps for subscriptions, reviews, gift cards, wholesale and shipping.
  • Simple product and order management for small teams.

Growing brands can move to Shopify Plus later for more checkout control and automation, and we handle Shopify Plus migrations as well.

Selling to Ireland, the UK and the EU

Since Brexit, selling from Ireland to the UK involves customs and VAT considerations, while selling within the EU follows EU VAT rules for distance sales. Shopify can show the right currency, prices and duties to each market, but the setup needs care.

  • Markets and currencies. Euro for Ireland and the EU, sterling for the UK, and other currencies if you ship further.
  • Shipping profiles. Separate rates and delivery times for Ireland, Northern Ireland, Great Britain, the EU and the rest of the world.
  • Duties and taxes. Clear messaging at checkout so customers are not surprised by charges on delivery.
  • Policies. Returns, delivery and privacy pages that reflect each market.

We set up the store; tax and customs decisions should be confirmed with your accountant using official Irish Revenue and EU guidance. Our guides to Shopify Markets in Europe and Shopify shipping profiles explain the settings.

Design that sells Galway products

Galway brands often have a strong story: handmade goods, local ingredients, Irish wool, coastal inspiration or a family business. A good store design lets that story support sales rather than hiding the products.

  • Clean collection pages with useful filters, so shoppers find products quickly.
  • Product pages with clear photos, sizes, materials, care details and delivery times.
  • An about page and brand story that builds trust without slowing the path to buy.
  • Gift options, such as gift cards, wrapping and notes, for a gift-heavy audience.
  • Fast load times on mobile, with lean apps and compressed images.

Our posts on Shopify collections structure and gift wrapping and order notes go into detail.

Migrating to Shopify

Many Galway businesses start on Etsy, WooCommerce, Wix or an older platform and outgrow it. A careful migration protects the search rankings and customer data you have built.

  1. Audit the current store, products, customers and top-ranking pages.
  2. Map old URLs to new Shopify URLs.
  3. Import products, customers and order history where supported.
  4. Set up redirects and test them before launch.
  5. Monitor search visibility and fix issues in the weeks after launch.

See our guides to Etsy to Shopify migration and WooCommerce to Shopify migration.

Growing the store after launch

Launching a store is the start. Most growth comes from steady SEO, product feeds and paid advertising over the following months. SEO and paid results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

  • Shopify SEO. Collection and product page optimisation, technical fixes and helpful content.
  • Google Shopping. A clean product feed in Merchant Center and well-structured Shopping or Performance Max campaigns.
  • Meta Ads. Prospecting and remarketing on Facebook and Instagram using your existing photos and videos.
  • Marketplace SEO. Listing optimisation if you also sell on Amazon or similar marketplaces.
  • AI-powered insights. Using AI tools to speed up product description drafts and spot search gaps, with human review.

Choosing the right Shopify theme

The theme shapes how your store looks, how fast it loads and how easily your team can change it. For most Galway brands we start from a well-supported theme from the Shopify Theme Store rather than building everything from scratch. That keeps costs within budget, makes future updates simpler and gives you a solid base for speed and accessibility.

We then customise the theme to fit your brand and products. Typical changes include custom sections for brand stories or product care, tailored collection layouts, trust messages about delivery and returns near the add-to-cart button, and cleaner navigation for larger catalogues. We avoid stacking apps for features the theme can already handle, because each extra app can slow the store and add a monthly fee.

Before launch we test the theme on common phones and browsers, check that product variants, filters and search work correctly, and make sure your team knows how to update banners, collections and homepage sections without needing a developer for every change.

Preparing your Galway business for launch

A store launches faster and performs better when the groundwork is done early. Before the build starts, it helps to have:

  • Product data in a spreadsheet, with titles, prices, variants, weights and stock levels.
  • Good product photos on plain or consistent backgrounds.
  • Delivery rates and carriers agreed for Ireland, the UK and any EU countries you serve.
  • Returns, privacy and terms wording reviewed by your own adviser.
  • A business bank account and payment provider details ready to connect.
  • A domain name, or a plan to move your existing one to Shopify.

Our guide to briefing a Shopify developer includes a fuller checklist. Once the store is live, we review sales data, search terms and customer behaviour monthly and suggest the next improvements, so the store keeps getting better rather than standing still after launch day. Many Galway brands also see strong seasonal patterns around Christmas, summer tourism and gifting occasions, and we plan collections, offers and ad budgets around those peaks well in advance.
