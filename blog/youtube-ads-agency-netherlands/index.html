Why YouTube Ads suit Dutch businesses

The Netherlands has a highly connected population, a strong ecommerce sector and many businesses that sell across borders. Video lets you show a product, a service or a team in a way that text ads cannot. YouTube Ads work particularly well for:

  • Online shops and direct-to-consumer brands. Showing products in use, then retargeting viewers with shopping or search ads.
  • Software and technology firms. Explaining a product in under a minute to Dutch and international buyers.
  • Exporters. Reaching buyers in other countries with English video while running Dutch versions at home.
  • Recruitment. Showing what it is like to work at a company when hiring is competitive.
  • Local services and hospitality. Short videos targeted to people in or near a city.

Many Dutch viewers are comfortable in English, but Dutch-language video usually feels more natural for consumer audiences. We help you decide where each language earns its place.

Choosing the right YouTube ad formats

YouTube offers several ad formats, each suited to a different job:

  • Skippable in-stream ads. Play before or during videos and can be skipped after a few seconds. Good for explaining a product and driving visits, as long as the first seconds grab attention.
  • Non-skippable in-stream ads. Short ads viewers must watch in full. Useful for a simple message when reach matters.
  • Bumper ads. Six-second ads for reminders and repetition, often alongside a longer video.
  • In-feed ads. Appear in search results and recommendations, inviting viewers to click and watch.
  • Shorts ads. Vertical ads between YouTube Shorts, suited to mobile-first products and younger audiences.

Google also offers campaign types that use video across YouTube and its partner network automatically. They can work well, but we check placements and results closely so budget is not spent where it does little good. Video for Dutch and English audiences

A good YouTube ad makes its point in the first few seconds, works with the sound off and ends with a clear next step. For Dutch campaigns, the main decisions are:

  • Voiceover or subtitles. A Dutch voiceover feels most natural for consumer products, while subtitles can adapt an English video quickly.
  • On-screen text. Dutch phrases are often longer than English ones, so text layouts need room to grow.
  • Calls to action. The landing page should match the language of the ad, so a Dutch ad leads to a Dutch page.
  • Cut-downs. One longer video can be edited into several shorter versions for bumpers, Shorts and tests.

Targeting, remarketing and tracking

YouTube targeting combines who people are, what they are interested in and what they have recently searched for. For most Dutch advertisers we start with a small number of clear audiences, such as people searching for your type of product on Google, people interested in your category and visitors to your website, then expand once we see which ones convert.

Remarketing is often where YouTube pays off. Someone who watched your video can later see a search or shopping ad, and someone who visited your shop can see a reminder video. Our remarketing guide explains the approach. To judge any of this fairly, conversions must be tracked properly, which our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking covers.

If you also sell through bol, YouTube can drive awareness that shows up as marketplace searches as well as site visits. Our guide to bol marketplace SEO explains how to make those listings ready to convert.

Consent, privacy and accessibility

Dutch websites fall under the GDPR for personal data, and the EU ePrivacy rules mean advertising and analytics cookies normally need consent before they load. That affects remarketing audiences and conversion tracking, because visitors who decline are not tracked in the usual way. We set up consent mode alongside a clear banner so measurement respects each visitor's choice. Our guide to cookie banner design covers the approach.

Accessibility matters for video too. Subtitles help viewers watching without sound and people with hearing loss, and landing pages built to WCAG AA, the level referenced by the harmonised standard EN 301 549, are easier for everyone to use. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer-facing services, with an exemption for microenterprises providing services. Legal wording and compliance decisions should come from your legal adviser, and official guidance should be checked before launch.

FAQs

Do you have a YouTube Ads agency office in the Netherlands?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in the Netherlands. We manage campaigns remotely by video call and shared dashboards, one hour behind Dutch time.

Can you run YouTube Ads in Dutch and English?

Yes. We plan and manage campaigns in both languages. Scripts and copy are written in English in-house, and Dutch versions come from your team or a native-speaking translator.

How much does a YouTube Ads agency in the Netherlands cost with your studio?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Google. We quote in pounds, with other currencies on request. Video production is separate.

Do I need professionally shot video?

Not always. Clear, well-lit videos filmed on a phone can work, especially for Shorts. What matters most is a strong opening, a clear message and a simple call to action.

How does cookie consent affect YouTube Ads?

Advertising cookies normally need consent, so some conversions and remarketing audiences are smaller than they would otherwise be. We set up consent mode, and your legal adviser approves the privacy wording.

How long before YouTube Ads show results?

Views can start within days, but results typically build over three to six months as audiences, creative and remarketing are refined.
