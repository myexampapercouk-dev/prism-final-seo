Tracking first: Pixel, Conversions API and analytics

Meta ads depend on good data. If the platform cannot see which ads lead to purchases or leads, it cannot optimise properly. Before scaling any campaign, we check and set up:

  • The Meta Pixel on every relevant page of your site.
  • The Conversions API, so key events are sent from your server or platform as well as the browser.
  • Correct standard events, such as lead, add to cart and purchase.
  • Domain verification and event priority settings.
  • Google Analytics 4 with consistent campaign tagging.

Shopify, WordPress and most booking tools can connect to Meta. We set these up so your reporting is reliable from day one.

Audiences that fit Mississauga customers

Meta's targeting has become broader over time, and its algorithm now does much of the audience finding. Our job is to give it the right signals. We typically use:

  • Location targeting around Mississauga, Brampton, Oakville and the wider GTA where you serve customers.
  • Broad prospecting guided by strong creative and good conversion data.
  • Custom audiences from website visitors, video viewers and engaged Instagram followers.
  • Customer list audiences where you have consent to use that data.
  • Lookalike audiences based on your best customers.

Mississauga's population speaks many languages. Prism writes ads in English. If you want ads in other languages, that copy comes from your team or a translator, and we set up separate ad sets to test it.

Creative testing that keeps ads working

On Meta, creative is usually the biggest driver of results. Ads that worked well last month can tire quickly as the same people see them again. We run a steady testing routine:

  • Testing different hooks, headlines and offers.
  • Comparing static images, carousels and short clips you supply.
  • Rotating fresh creative before performance drops.
  • Using Instagram Stories and Reels placements with correctly sized assets.

Prism does not film or edit video. Your team supplies photos and clips, and we write copy, design simple static ads and organise testing. Our article on TikTok ads versus Meta ads explains how creative needs differ between the two platforms.

Special ad categories and regulated sectors

Meta has special ad categories for housing, employment, credit and political or social issue ads. These limit some targeting options, such as age and narrow location targeting. If your business falls into one of these categories, we plan campaigns within those rules. In health, finance and other regulated sectors, ad wording must also meet professional and provincial rules, and final approval of ads rests with your compliance team or adviser.

Privacy, consent and follow-up emails

Meta ads and lead forms collect personal information, and many businesses follow up by email or text. At a general level:

  • PIPEDA is the federal privacy law that applies to many private-sector businesses in commercial activity.
  • CASL sets rules on consent and unsubscribe options for commercial emails and texts.
  • If you sell to customers in Quebec, Law 25 may add further requirements around consent and transparency.

We build consent wording into forms and set up cookie banners, but how your business meets these rules is your decision. We recommend guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the CRTC, or your own adviser.

Common Meta ads mistakes we fix

When we audit Meta ad accounts for Mississauga businesses, a few problems appear often. Each one makes ads more expensive than they need to be:

  • Boosting posts instead of running proper campaigns. Boosts are quick but offer limited control over objectives, placements and tracking.
  • Optimising for the wrong event. Campaigns set to traffic or engagement when the real goal is leads or sales.
  • Too many small ad sets. Budget is spread so thinly that none of them gather enough data to learn.
  • Running the same creative for months. Results fade as audiences tire of seeing the same ad.
  • Sending traffic to slow or generic pages. Social visitors on phones leave quickly if the page does not match the ad.
  • Lead forms with no follow-up process. Leads sit in a spreadsheet for days and go cold.

Fixing these basics often improves results before any increase in budget. Our guide to Facebook ads for small businesses covers several of these points in more depth.

Lead quality and follow-up

Meta instant forms make it very easy for people to enquire, which can also mean a higher share of low-quality leads. We manage this by adding a short qualifying question or two, using the higher-intent form type where it suits, and sending leads straight to your inbox or CRM so your team can respond quickly. We also feed information about which leads became customers back into reporting where your systems allow it. Fast, consistent follow-up usually matters as much as the ads themselves.
