An SEO agency for a Barcelona business should help the right people find you in Google when they search for what you offer, whether they search in Spanish, Catalan or English. That means keyword research in each language, a technically sound website, pages that genuinely answer those searches and clear reporting on what is changing.

What makes SEO in Barcelona different

Barcelona combines tourism and hospitality, a strong start-up and technology community, design and fashion brands, food and drink, education, property and professional services. Several features of the market shape an SEO plan:

  • Three search languages. Local customers may search in Spanish or Catalan, while visitors, international residents and overseas buyers often search in English.
  • Heavy tourist demand. Hotels, restaurants, tours and attractions compete for searches made by people planning a trip from abroad, often months in advance.
  • National and international reach. Many Barcelona firms sell across Spain, and some sell into the rest of Europe and beyond.
  • Strong competition in English. English-language searches for Barcelona services are contested by booking platforms, directories and large travel sites.

If you sell into several countries, our guide to international SEO explains how to structure sites across languages and markets.

Spanish, Catalan and English pages done properly

Many Barcelona websites have one strong language version and others that were added quickly, with missing pages or machine-translated text. Search engines treat each language as its own set of pages, so each needs its own keyword research, titles, descriptions and content. Hreflang tags tell Google which version to show to which searcher, and they must be set up consistently, or the wrong version can appear in results.

Whether you need all three languages depends on your customers. A restaurant aimed at visitors may put English first, while a business serving local households may lead with Spanish and Catalan. We help you decide based on search demand and your actual customers, rather than building versions that nobody uses.

Prism writes English content in-house. For Spanish and Catalan content, we brief your team or a native-speaking writer or translator with the keyword research, page structure and SEO requirements, then check the finished pages technically. Our multilingual website design guide covers language switchers, URLs and how to keep each version manageable.

Technical SEO for Barcelona websites

A technical audit usually finds improvements that help every language version at once:

  • Language versions that point to the wrong pages, or hreflang tags that are missing or inconsistent.
  • Booking engines or third-party widgets that slow pages down or hide content from search engines.
  • Broken redirects after a past redesign, losing value from older links.
  • Slow mobile pages caused by large images, sliders or too many scripts.
  • Thin location or service pages created only to target extra keywords.

Our technical SEO audit checklist lists what we check, and our guide to Core Web Vitals explains the speed measures Google uses.

Local SEO for Barcelona

If customers visit your premises, your Google Business Profile is often the first thing they see. We help with accurate categories, services, photos, opening hours and descriptions, and with building a steady flow of genuine reviews. Reviews in several languages are normal for a Barcelona business and help both visitors and locals judge whether you suit them.

Location pages should exist only for neighbourhoods or areas you genuinely serve, with useful details rather than copies of the same text with a different district name. Our Google Business Profile guide explains the essentials.

SEO for hospitality and tourism in Barcelona

Hotels, apartments, restaurants, tours and attractions in Barcelona compete with booking platforms and review sites for the same searches. Winning more direct traffic usually depends on:

  • Specific pages for the experiences, rooms, menus or tours people search for, rather than one general page.
  • Helpful planning content that answers questions visitors ask before they book.
  • A fast, simple booking path so visitors do not leave to book elsewhere.
  • Consistent details across your website, Google profile and listings.

Our hotel SEO guide covers this in more depth, and much of it applies to restaurants and attractions too.

Search engines and AI answers

Travellers and buyers increasingly use AI tools and AI summaries in search results to plan and compare. Clear, well-structured pages that answer real questions are more likely to be used as sources. Our SEO work includes generative engine optimisation, which means making your content easy for these systems to understand and cite. We do not promise placements in AI answers, but we can make your pages clearer and more useful.

Data protection and legal pages in Spain

Spanish websites usually need a legal notice, a privacy policy and a cookie policy, and analytics and marketing cookies need consent under EU and Spanish rules. These requirements affect how analytics data is collected and reported. We keep our advice general: check current official guidance and confirm your setup with your legal adviser, who has final approval on legal pages and consent wording.
