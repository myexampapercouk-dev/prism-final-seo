Tracking first, then campaigns

The most common problem we see in Google Ads accounts is poor tracking. If Google cannot see which clicks led to real enquiries or sales, its automated bidding will optimise for the wrong things. Before we spend more of your budget, we check that the Google tag, GA4 and conversion actions are set up correctly, that each conversion counts once and that the values make sense.

For B2B and SaaS companies with longer sales cycles, we can import offline conversions from your CRM, such as qualified leads or closed deals, so Google learns which searches lead to revenue rather than just form fills. This is especially useful in a market like Waterloo Region, where many businesses sell high-value products to a limited pool of buyers.

Search campaigns for local and B2B buyers

Search campaigns remain the core of most Google Ads accounts. We group keywords by intent, write ads that match each group, and add useful assets such as sitelinks, callouts and call buttons. We also build negative keyword lists to filter out searches that are unlikely to convert, such as job seekers, students looking for definitions or people outside your area.

For local services, we target Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge and surrounding communities according to where you work. For businesses selling across Canada or internationally, we plan location targeting and budgets by market. If you are also targeting Toronto or the wider Greater Toronto Area, our guides to Google Ads in Toronto and Google Ads in Mississauga may help, and Google Ads in Hamilton covers another nearby market.

Shopping and Performance Max for retailers

If you sell products online, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns can show your products with images and prices across Google Search, Shopping, YouTube and other placements. They rely on a clean product feed in Google Merchant Center, with accurate titles, descriptions, prices, availability and shipping settings.

We review and improve your feed, structure campaigns by margin or product priority, and keep a close eye on search terms to make sure budgets are not wasted. If your store needs work first, our Shopify agency Canada guide explains how we build and improve stores.

SaaS and technology companies

Waterloo Region has many software companies, and Google Ads for SaaS needs a slightly different approach. Search volumes for specialist software can be low, cost per click can be high and the buyer journey often involves several people. We usually focus on high-intent terms such as category plus "software" or "platform", competitor alternatives where appropriate and problem-led searches, then send traffic to focused landing pages with a clear trial or demo offer.

LinkedIn Ads can complement Google Ads by reaching decision makers by job title and company. Our LinkedIn Ads in Canada guide explains how the two work together, and our SaaS marketing page covers our wider approach.

Landing pages and SEO

Every ad should lead to a page that matches what the searcher was looking for, loads quickly and makes the next step obvious. We review your landing pages and can build new ones where needed. Our guide to web design in Kitchener-Waterloo covers website builds in the region.

Paid search and organic search work well together. Google Ads data shows which keywords convert, which helps shape your SEO plan, while strong organic rankings can reduce how much you need to spend on ads over time. Our SEO in Kitchener-Waterloo guide explains the organic side.

Privacy and Canadian rules

Google Ads tracking and remarketing involve cookies and personal data. At a general level, PIPEDA covers how many private organisations in Canada collect and use personal information, some provinces have their own privacy laws, and Quebec's Law 25 adds requirements for businesses that handle personal information of people in Quebec. CASL applies to follow-up marketing emails and texts. These notes are general, so please check official guidance or ask your own adviser. In regulated sectors, approval of ad claims rests with your compliance team.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

When we review existing accounts, the same issues come up again and again. Broad match keywords run without enough negative keywords, so budgets leak to irrelevant searches. Location settings target people "interested in" an area rather than people physically there. Conversion actions count page views or duplicate form submissions as leads. Ads send every visitor to the home page instead of a relevant landing page. Fixing these basics often makes a noticeable difference before any new campaigns are built, so an audit is usually our first step with an existing account.

How to choose a Google Ads agency in Kitchener-Waterloo

  1. Account ownership: the account should be in your name.
  2. Tracking checks: ask how conversions are verified before scaling spend.
  3. Search term reviews: ask how often they review search terms and add negatives.
  4. Reporting: look for clear explanations, not only charts.
  5. Fee transparency: management fees and ad spend should be separate and clear.
  6. Contract terms: check notice periods and what happens if you leave.
