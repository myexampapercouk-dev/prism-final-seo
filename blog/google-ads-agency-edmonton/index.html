Google Ads Agency for Edmonton Businesses: How Remote PPC Works

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Edmonton? How remote PPC management works, Alberta targeting, tracking, fees in pounds and what to ask before you sign.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Google Ads agency for an Edmonton business should set up reliable conversion tracking first, then refine keywords, bids and landing pages month by month.
  • Edmonton campaigns often need careful location settings across the city, surrounding communities and wider Alberta service areas.
  • Ad spend is paid to Google from your own account and is separate from management fees, so both should be shown clearly before you start.

A Google Ads agency for an Edmonton business should put your ads in front of people searching for what you sell, measure which clicks become calls, forms or sales, and stop spending money on searches that never pay back. All of that happens inside your Google Ads account, so it can be managed remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that runs paid search for Canadian businesses online, and we have no office in Edmonton or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide explains what good Google Ads management looks like for Edmonton companies, how to handle targeting across the city and the wider region, how a remote arrangement works across time zones, what management costs with us and which questions to ask any agency before you hand over a budget.

What a Google Ads agency in Edmonton should do

Edmonton's economy mixes energy services, construction, trades, logistics, healthcare, education, retail and a growing technology scene. Those businesses buy and sell in very different ways, but the core job of a paid search agency is the same for all of them:

  • Conversion tracking that you can trust, so spend is judged on enquiries and sales rather than clicks. Our conversion tracking guide explains how this is set up.
  • Keyword and search term control, including a maintained list of negative keywords so budget is not wasted on job seekers, DIY researchers or unrelated searches.
  • Bidding that matches your goals and the amount of conversion data your account actually has.
  • Ads and landing pages that work together, so the promise in the ad is repeated on the page people land on.
  • Plain-English reporting on spend, results and the changes made each month.

Our paid media service covers Google Ads alongside Microsoft Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube, so search can be planned alongside other channels rather than in isolation.

Location targeting across Edmonton and the capital region

Many Edmonton businesses serve more than the city itself. A roofer or HVAC company may cover St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Fort Saskatchewan. An industrial supplier may sell across northern Alberta. An online retailer may ship across Canada. Each of those needs a different location set-up.

Google Ads location settings deserve care, because the defaults can show ads to people who are merely interested in a place rather than physically in it. For most local service businesses, we target people in or regularly in your chosen areas, then check location reports to see where clicks and conversions really come from. Seasonality and budgets in an Alberta market

Edmonton's long winters shape demand for many businesses. Furnace repair, snow removal and winter tyre searches rise in the cold months, while landscaping, decks, roofing and exterior painting tend to peak in spring and summer. A good agency plans budgets around those patterns rather than spreading spend evenly through the year.

That might mean building campaigns ahead of the busy season so they have data when demand arrives, using ad schedules to focus on the hours when calls are most likely to be answered, and reducing spend during quiet months without switching campaigns off entirely. The right approach depends on your own search data, which is why we look at past performance before suggesting changes.

Google Ads for B2B and industrial firms in Edmonton

Energy services, engineering, manufacturing and logistics firms often have small search volumes, specialist terminology and high-value leads. Broad keywords can attract students, job hunters and researchers, so search term reviews and negative keywords matter even more than usual. Conversion tracking should focus on qualified actions, such as quote requests and calls of a meaningful length, rather than any page view.

For some B2B offers, LinkedIn reaches decision-makers by job title and company in a way search cannot. Our comparison of LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads for B2B explains when each channel makes sense, and our B2B sector page outlines how we support longer sales cycles.

Which campaign types suit Edmonton businesses

Not every business needs every Google Ads format. Search campaigns are usually the starting point because they reach people already looking for a service or product. Shopping and Performance Max campaigns suit retailers with a product feed. Remarketing reminds previous visitors about you, and YouTube campaigns can build familiarity before people search.

We usually begin with the campaign type closest to buying intent, prove that tracking and landing pages work, and only then expand into other formats. That keeps early budgets focused and makes it easier to see what each campaign contributes. If you are weighing up paid search against organic work, our guide to SEO vs PPC sets out the trade-offs, and many Edmonton firms combine both over time.

Landing pages and what happens after the click

Paid search sends people to a specific page, and that page does much of the persuading. A strong landing page repeats the offer from the ad, explains clearly what you do and where you work, shows genuine evidence such as real reviews or project photos, and makes the next step obvious. It should load quickly on a phone, because many local searches happen on mobile.

Our guide to landing page design covers the elements that usually matter most. We review landing pages during campaign set-up and can design or improve them where needed through our website work.

How remote Google Ads management works

Edmonton runs on Mountain Time, so the UK is usually seven hours ahead. UK late afternoons overlap with Edmonton mornings, which is enough for regular calls. Most optimisation happens inside your account, and we share changes, reports and recommendations in writing so your team can review them at a convenient time.

  1. Audit or set-up. We review your existing account or build a new one in your name.
  2. Tracking. We confirm calls, forms and sales are recorded accurately before scaling spend.
  3. Launch. Campaigns go live with agreed budgets, locations and schedules.
  4. Optimisation. Regular search term reviews, bid and budget changes, and ad testing.
  5. Reporting. A monthly summary of spend, results and changes, with clear next steps.

If you already run campaigns, our PPC audit checklist shows the areas we look at first, from account structure to wasted spend.

What Google Ads management costs with Prism

Management is billed on monthly retainers priced in pounds sterling: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Prices in Canadian dollars or other currencies can be quoted on request. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own account and is always separate from our fees. Full details are on our pricing page.

Paid search can bring traffic from the first day, but campaigns need time to gather data and improve. Results typically settle over three to six months as targeting, bidding and landing pages are refined. Tax, invoicing and contract questions for cross-border services are worth checking with your own accountant, as rules can change.

Questions to ask any Google Ads agency

  • Will the Google Ads account be in my name, with full admin access for me?
  • How will conversions be tracked, and can I see that tracking working?
  • How are management fees separated from ad spend on invoices?
  • How often will search terms be reviewed and negative keywords added?
  • How will budgets change through the year for my seasonal demand?
  • What will the monthly report include, and who will explain it?

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Edmonton?

Choose an agency that keeps the account in your name, sets up conversion tracking before increasing spend, separates fees from ad spend and reports in plain language. Ask how they would handle your service area and seasonal demand across the year.

Can a UK agency manage Google Ads for an Edmonton business?

Yes. Google Ads is managed online, so location is not a barrier. Prism has no Edmonton office; we work remotely with Canadian businesses and schedule calls in the overlap between UK afternoons and Edmonton mornings.

How much does Google Ads management cost with Prism?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Ad spend is paid to Google separately. We can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Should my ads cover all of Alberta or just Edmonton?

It depends on where you can deliver profitably. Local services usually target the city and nearby communities they serve, while suppliers and online stores may target the province or the whole country. Location reports help refine this over time.

How quickly will Google Ads bring enquiries?

Ads can bring clicks as soon as they go live, but campaigns usually improve over three to six months as conversion data builds up and keywords, bids and landing pages are refined.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. We set up or work within an account in your name, paid with your own billing details, and you can remove our access at any time without losing data or campaign history.
