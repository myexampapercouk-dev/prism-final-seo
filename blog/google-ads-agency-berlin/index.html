  • B2B firms that want qualified leads rather than cheap clicks.
  • English-speaking businesses serving Berlin's international community.

Accurate tracking comes first

Good bidding depends on good data. Before we scale any account, we check that conversions are tracked correctly, that duplicate conversions are removed and that the values passed back to Google reflect real business outcomes. Our conversion tracking setup guide and GA4 setup guide explain the process in more detail.

In Germany, consent rules shape how tracking works. Visitors need to agree before most marketing tags run, and that affects the data you see. We set up tracking that respects consent choices and explain the gaps it creates. The legal wording of your cookie banner and privacy policy rests with you and your adviser, and official EU and German guidance is the best place to check your obligations.

Keywords, languages and targeting

Berlin searchers use German, English and a mix of both. A service aimed at international residents may get most of its searches in English, while a local trade will see mostly German queries. We research both, separate them into campaigns where it helps, and match each ad to the right landing page.

Location targeting matters too. You may want only Berlin, Berlin plus Brandenburg, all of Germany or several EU countries. Our location targeting guide explains the settings that stop ads showing to people outside your area. We also build negative keyword lists from day one, as described in our negative keywords guide.

Ad copy and landing pages

We write ad copy in English and prepare clear briefs for German versions. Your team or translator supplies the German text, and we check it fits the character limits and matches the keywords. Our responsive search ads guide explains how we structure headlines and descriptions.

The landing page often matters more than the ad. A fast, focused page with one clear action usually lifts conversion rates and improves quality signals. If your current pages are slow or unclear, we can build new ones as part of a website project.

How quickly will Google Ads work?

Google Ads can bring traffic from the first day, but steady, profitable performance usually takes time. The first weeks are about learning which keywords, ads and pages convert. Results typically settle over 3 to 6 months as data builds and waste is removed. We are honest about what your budget can realistically achieve in a competitive Berlin market.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix in Berlin accounts

When we review an existing account, we often find the same issues. None of them are unusual, and most can be fixed within the first few weeks. Fixing them usually frees budget that can be moved to the campaigns that actually bring enquiries.

  • Broad keywords with no negatives, so ads show for searches that have nothing to do with the offer.
  • Mixed languages in one ad group, so German searchers see English ads and the other way round.
  • Location settings that are too wide, showing ads to people interested in Berlin rather than people in it.
  • Conversions counted twice, which makes campaigns look better than they are and confuses automated bidding.
  • Every click sent to the home page, rather than a page that matches the ad.
  • Performance Max running without checks, so spend drifts into brand terms or weak placements.

We explain each fix in plain English and show the before and after in our reports, so you can see where the budget is going.

Budgets and bidding

There is no single right budget for Google Ads in Berlin. It depends on how competitive your keywords are, how much a customer is worth to you and how quickly you want to test. We help you set a starting budget that is large enough to learn from, but not so large that early mistakes become expensive.

Automated bidding can work well once tracking is accurate and there is enough conversion data. Before that point, simpler strategies are often safer. Our bidding strategies guide explains the options. We review bidding regularly and only move to more automated strategies when the data supports it.

Using AI tools sensibly

Google Ads now uses a lot of automation, from responsive ads to Performance Max. We also use AI-powered tools to speed up keyword grouping, search term reviews and reporting. Every recommendation is checked by a person before it changes your account, because automation without oversight can waste money quickly.
