The Madrid property market and why marketing matters

Madrid draws a wide mix of property buyers and renters: local families moving between neighbourhoods, professionals relocating from other Spanish cities, international buyers looking for a home or investment, and students and young workers searching for rentals. Each group searches differently and responds to different messages.

Most agencies list on the major Spanish portals such as Idealista and Fotocasa, and those portals remain an important source of leads. The problem is that every competitor is there too, and the portal owns the relationship with the searcher. A strong own-brand presence helps you win valuation requests, attract sellers, build a list of buyers and reduce dependence on paid portal packages.

What real estate marketing Madrid agencies need

  • A fast, credible website: clear area pages, listings or a feed from your CRM, and easy ways to book a valuation or viewing.
  • Local SEO: visibility for searches such as an estate agent in a specific neighbourhood or district.
  • Google Ads: campaigns for high-intent searches like property valuations or new developments.
  • Meta Ads: visual campaigns for new developments, open days and seller leads.
  • Tracking: clear measurement of which channels bring real enquiries.

Websites for Madrid estate agencies and developers

Your website is the one channel you fully control. For agencies, the most valuable pages are often not listings but neighbourhood guides, seller pages and valuation forms. Pages about Salamanca, Chamberí, Retiro, Chamartín or other districts you genuinely cover can rank for local searches and show sellers that you know the area.

For developers, a dedicated site or landing page for each development works well, with floor plans, specifications, location details, an enquiry form and clear next steps. Our guide to landing page design covers what makes these pages convert, and our web design Madrid page explains how we build sites for the city.

Small websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and take 3 to 5 weeks. Larger builds with listing integrations are quoted after a discovery call, within our published pricing where they fit.

SEO for Madrid property businesses

Portals dominate broad searches for flats and houses in Madrid. Independent agencies rarely beat them for those terms, so we focus on searches where you can win:

  • Agency and valuation searches for specific districts and neighbourhoods.
  • Seller questions, such as how to prepare a home for sale or how long selling takes.
  • Searches for named developments and new-build areas.
  • English-language searches from international buyers and relocating professionals.

Our SEO and GEO service covers technical fixes, local SEO, content and AI search visibility. If you want our wider organic approach for the city, see our SEO agency Madrid page.

Marketing to international buyers

Madrid attracts buyers and renters from Latin America, the rest of Europe, the UK, the Middle East and beyond. English-language pages and campaigns can reach many of them, and Spanish pages reach Latin American buyers too. If you run campaigns across several countries, our guide to Meta Ads in multiple countries explains how to structure them.

A multilingual site should have separate language versions with their own URLs and hreflang tags. Each version should be written for its audience rather than machine translated. We write English content directly; other languages come from your team or a translator.

Compliance and advertising rules

Property advertising in Spain is subject to consumer protection and regional rules, including requirements about the information shown for properties and developments. Ad platforms also have their own policies for housing ads. We provide general marketing support only. Your own team and advisers are responsible for checking that listings, ads and claims meet the rules that apply to you, and compliance approval rests with you. For official guidance, check the relevant Spanish and Madrid regional authorities.

Winning seller instructions online

For most agencies, sellers are the most valuable leads, because each instruction can bring several buyer enquiries. Yet many agency websites focus almost entirely on listings. A seller-focused section can make a real difference. Useful pages include a clear valuation request form, an explanation of your selling process, honest information about fees or how they are calculated, and neighbourhood pages that show you understand local prices and demand.

Content can also attract sellers earlier in their journey. Articles about preparing a home for sale, the documents usually needed, how long sales tend to take and what affects a property's value all answer common questions. We write these in English for international owners and provide briefs for Spanish versions that your team or translator can write.

Paid campaigns can support this. Google Ads for valuation searches in specific districts, and Meta Ads offering a free valuation to homeowners in your area, both work well when the landing page is clear and the follow-up is quick.

Lead handling and follow-up

Marketing only pays off if enquiries are handled well. Property leads go cold quickly, so we help you set up forms that send enquiries straight to the right person, track calls and WhatsApp clicks where possible, and connect lead forms to your CRM. We also report which channels and campaigns bring enquiries that turn into viewings, valuations and instructions, not just clicks, so budgets go where they work best.

Simple steps help: replying within the hour, asking a couple of qualifying questions on forms, and sending a short follow-up message with useful information. These habits often matter as much as the ads themselves.
