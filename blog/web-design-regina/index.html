Web Design for Regina Businesses: How a Remote Website Build Works

Looking for web design in Regina? How a remote website build works for Saskatchewan businesses, from planning and platforms to SEO, accessibility and costs.

Web design for a Regina business should give you a fast, clear and mobile-friendly website that explains what you do, builds trust quickly and makes it easy for customers to call, book, enquire or buy. Because planning, design, development, content reviews and launch can all happen online, a website can be built remotely. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that designs and builds websites for Canadian businesses online; we have no office in Regina or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide explains what a good website needs, how to choose a platform, how the remote process works, what our websites cost and what to ask before choosing a web designer.

What good web design in Regina looks like

Regina is Saskatchewan's capital, with an economy that includes government and public services, agriculture and agri-business, insurance and financial services, energy, manufacturing, construction and trades, health care, education and a wide range of independent retailers, restaurants and professional firms. Customers in all of these sectors expect the same basics from a website.

  • Clarity: visitors should understand what you do, where you work and how to contact you within seconds.
  • Speed: pages should load quickly on a phone, including on slower rural connections across the province.
  • Trust: real photos, clear service descriptions, transparent contact details and a professional design.
  • Easy next steps: visible phone numbers, simple forms, booking tools or a straightforward checkout.
  • Search visibility: a structure that helps your pages appear when people search for your services in Regina.
  • Easy updates: a system your team can use to change text, images and offers without a developer.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers design, build, launch and support, with search basics built in from the start.

Choosing the right platform

For most small and medium-sized businesses, the choice comes down to WordPress for service and content-led sites and Shopify for online stores. Both are well supported and widely used, and both can be managed by your own team after launch.

Planning pages around how Regina customers search

A website works best when its structure matches what customers look for. Before design begins, we map out the pages you need, usually a home page, a page for each main service or product range, an about page, a contact page and, where relevant, pages for the areas you serve. Each page targets a clear topic, so search engines and visitors understand it.

A Regina roofing firm, for example, might have separate pages for repairs, replacements and inspections, each explaining the service, the areas covered and how to get a quote. A retailer might organise collections the way shoppers browse. This planning also makes later SEO work faster, and our guide to local SEO in Canada explains how a well-structured site supports map pack and local search visibility.

Single campaigns or promotions often benefit from a focused page of their own, and our guide to landing page design covers what makes one convert.

Language, accessibility and privacy basics

English is the main language for most Regina customers. If you also want French or other language versions of key pages, we plan the structure in English, and the translated content is written with your team or a professional translator.

Accessibility matters for customers and, increasingly, for the law. Several provinces have accessibility legislation, and the international WCAG guidelines are a widely used reference for web accessibility. We build with clear headings, good colour contrast, descriptive link text, image alternatives and keyboard-friendly navigation as standard. What rules apply to your organisation depends on its circumstances, so check the official guidance and take advice where needed.

Privacy is the other foundation. In general terms, PIPEDA, the federal private sector privacy law, applies to many businesses' commercial activity in Saskatchewan, including information collected through website forms, analytics and cookies. CASL, Canada's anti-spam legislation, governs commercial emails and texts, which matters if your site collects newsletter sign-ups. These are general notes only, so check the official guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the CRTC, and take advice from your own adviser. In regulated sectors such as health or finance, compliance approval rests with your business.

Canadian dollar quotes are available on request. How long a website takes

A typical small business website takes three to five weeks from agreed brief to launch. An ecommerce site usually takes five to twelve weeks, depending on the number of products, payment and shipping setup and any integrations with stock or accounting systems. The biggest factor in timing is often content: having text, photos and product details ready early keeps the project moving.

Keeping your website working after launch

A website is not finished on launch day. WordPress sites need regular updates to the core software, theme and plugins, along with backups and security monitoring. Shopify handles much of this for you, but apps, themes and product content still need care. Either way, someone should check that forms still deliver enquiries, that pages load quickly and that contact details, prices and opening hours stay accurate.

It also pays to review the site every few months with fresh eyes. Look at which pages people visit, where they leave and which enquiries turn into work, then improve the pages that matter most. Seasonal businesses in Regina, from landscaping and snow clearing to tourism and events, often benefit from updating offers and photos ahead of each busy period. Small, regular improvements usually do more than an expensive redesign every few years.

How a remote website build works

  1. Discovery call on video to understand your business, customers and goals.
  2. Proposal and site plan setting out pages, features, platform, timeline and price.
  3. Design of key pages, shared online for your feedback.
  4. Build on a private staging site you can review at any time.
  5. Content and testing across phones, tablets and desktop browsers.
  6. Launch, with redirects, analytics and search basics in place.
  7. Handover, including a walkthrough so your team can make updates.

Saskatchewan does not change its clocks, so Regina is six or seven hours behind the UK depending on the season. UK afternoons overlap with Regina mornings, and we agree regular check-ins that suit you. You own your domain, hosting account and website content throughout.

Choosing a web designer in Regina

Whether you choose a local designer or a remote studio, ask:

  • What exactly is included in the price, and what costs extra?
  • Which platform do they recommend, and why?
  • Who owns the domain, hosting and website once it is finished?
  • How will search basics, accessibility and speed be handled?
  • What support is available after launch?

FAQs

How do I choose web design in Regina?

Look for a designer who asks about your customers and goals, recommends a platform with clear reasons, explains all costs upfront and makes sure you own your domain and site. Ask how they handle search, speed and accessibility.

Can a UK studio build a website for a Regina business?

Yes. Website projects are planned, designed and reviewed online. Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Regina, and we schedule calls at times that suit Saskatchewan.

How much does a website cost for a Regina business?

Our small business websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and ecommerce sites £1,500 to £3,500. Canadian dollar quotes are available on request, and ongoing costs such as hosting are explained upfront.

How long does it take to build a website?

A small business website typically takes three to five weeks, and an ecommerce site five to twelve weeks, depending on scope and how quickly content is ready.

Should my Regina business use WordPress or Shopify?

WordPress usually suits service and content-led businesses, while Shopify suits online stores. We recommend a platform based on what you sell and how your team wants to manage the site.

Will my new website be ready for SEO?

Yes. We build in search basics such as clean structure, page titles, fast loading and mobile-friendly design. Ongoing SEO is a separate service if you want to grow visibility further.
