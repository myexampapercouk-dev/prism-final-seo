A real estate website that earns enquiries

Many agent websites are thin templates that look the same as every other agent's site. Buyers and sellers notice. We design real estate websites that:

  • Lead with a clear value for sellers, such as a simple home valuation request.
  • Connect to your listings feed where your brokerage and board allow it.
  • Include neighbourhood pages with real, useful information about each area you serve.
  • Show your contact details, brokerage information and licence status clearly.
  • Load fast on phones, where most property browsing happens.

Our estate agent website design guide covers the design principles in detail, and our Halifax web design guide explains how we build for businesses in the city.

Local SEO for Halifax agents

Property searches are highly local. People search by neighbourhood, by property type and by need, such as condos near downtown Halifax, family homes in Bedford or waterfront homes on the South Shore. We help agents rank for those searches by:

  • Writing helpful neighbourhood and community pages, not copied boilerplate.
  • Optimising your Google Business Profile with accurate categories, service areas and photos.
  • Building a steady, honest review process with past clients.
  • Fixing technical issues such as duplicate listing pages and slow loading.
  • Publishing guides that answer common buyer and seller questions.

Our full SEO and GEO service explains how we approach search and AI answers, and the Halifax SEO agency guide covers local search in the city. The Google Business Profile guide explains profile basics that apply in Canada too.

Google Ads for sellers, buyers and developers

Search ads work well when people are actively looking. We usually separate campaigns by goal:

  • Seller campaigns for searches about selling a home or getting a valuation in a specific area.
  • Buyer campaigns for searches about a property type or neighbourhood.
  • New-build campaigns for developers promoting a specific project and its sales centre.

Every campaign sends people to a matching landing page, not a generic home page, and we track calls and form enquiries. Our guide to Google Ads in Halifax explains our setup in more depth.

Meta and Instagram ads for listings and launches

Meta and Instagram are useful for showing homes, open houses and new developments to local audiences. Housing ads on Meta may need to run under its special ad category rules, which limit some targeting options, so we plan campaigns around the rules that currently apply. We use Meta ads to:

  • Promote open houses and new listings with your photography.
  • Build interest in pre-construction projects before launch.
  • Retarget people who viewed your valuation or listings pages.
  • Share helpful market guides that bring sellers into your pipeline.

Prism does not film or edit video. If your team or photographer provides walkthrough clips, we can use them in campaigns. Our guide to Meta ads in Halifax has more on our approach.

For developers we typically build a focused project website, run search and social campaigns to grow a registration list, and set up clear reporting on enquiries. Advertising rules and privacy

Real estate is a regulated profession. At a general level, real estate advertising in Nova Scotia is subject to rules set by the provincial regulator, and your brokerage may have its own policies on how listings, names and claims appear. Commercial emails are covered by CASL, Canada's anti-spam law, and personal information is generally covered by PIPEDA.

We build clear disclaimers, consent forms and privacy page templates, but compliance approval rests with you and your brokerage. Please check the Nova Scotia Real Estate Commission's guidance, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or your own adviser before campaigns go live.

Common real estate marketing mistakes

  • Relying only on brokerage listings pages, with no personal site or brand of your own.
  • Neighbourhood pages that repeat the same generic text for every area.
  • Ads that send sellers to a general home page instead of a valuation page.
  • No follow-up process, so enquiries from evenings and weekends go cold.
  • Collecting email addresses without clear consent.

Fixing these basics usually does more for enquiry numbers than adding new channels.

Speed of response matters too. Buyers and sellers often contact several agents at once, and the first helpful reply tends to win the conversation. We can set up simple automatic acknowledgement emails and notifications so every enquiry reaches you quickly, with any follow-up messages handled in line with CASL consent rules.
