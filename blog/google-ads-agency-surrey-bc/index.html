Why Google Ads work in Surrey

Surrey is one of the fastest-growing cities in British Columbia, with new housing in areas such as Clayton, Grandview Heights and City Centre, plus established communities in Newton, Fleetwood, Guildford, Cloverdale and South Surrey. That growth creates steady demand for home services, renovations, clinics, childcare, driving schools and local shops. Many of those customers start with a Google search.

The city also has a large industrial and logistics base, with businesses that sell to other firms rather than households. Their buyers search too, often with specific, technical phrases. A well-built search campaign can capture those enquiries without paying for broad, low-intent traffic.

Calls, forms and local intent

Many Surrey customers prefer to phone. A homeowner with a burst pipe or a parent booking a dental check-up often wants to speak to someone straight away. We add call assets, schedule ads around your opening hours and track calls so you can see which keywords lead to real conversations, not just clicks.

Location assets linked to your Google Business Profile help too, showing your address and distance in ads. For clinics in particular, our guide to dental marketing in Vancouver covers points that apply across Metro Vancouver. In health and other regulated areas, ad wording should follow your professional body's rules and official guidance, and final approval rests with your business.

Multilingual audiences

Surrey has a large South Asian community, and many residents use Punjabi, Hindi or other languages alongside English. Google Ads can target users by language setting, and separate campaigns per language keep reporting clean. We build the structure, keywords and tracking, while your team or a translator writes and checks non-English ads and landing pages.

Landing pages and tracking

A strong ad sent to a weak page wastes money. We review speed, mobile layout, headline match, trust signals and the form or booking flow before increasing budgets. Each ad group should point to the most relevant page, not the home page.

Conversion tracking lets Google's bidding learn and shows you which searches earn money. We set it up through Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, test each event and compare results with your own records.

What we need from you to start

A campaign can only be as good as the information behind it. Before we build anything, we ask for access to your Google Ads account, or we help you open one in your own name so the account and its data always belong to you. We also need access to Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and your Google Business Profile, or permission to set them up properly.

Next, we ask which services or products earn you the most, which neighbourhoods and nearby cities you cover, and what a new customer is typically worth. A roofing job, a long-term physio patient and a single takeaway order are worth very different amounts, and bidding targets should reflect that. We also agree who answers the phone during ad hours and how quickly web forms are followed up, because slow replies waste good clicks.

With these details in place, most search campaigns can be researched, built, tested and launched within a few weeks. From there, the first months focus on cleaning search terms, improving ads and moving budget towards the keywords and areas that bring real enquiries.

Bidding, budgets and search terms

Automated bidding works best once an account has recorded enough conversions. For new accounts or small budgets, we usually start with tighter keyword lists and simpler bidding, then switch to strategies such as maximise conversions once the data supports it. This prevents the algorithm from spending freely before it has learned what a good lead looks like.

In the early months we review the search terms report every week. It shows the actual searches that triggered your ads and is where most wasted spend is found. Adding negative keywords, such as jobs, free or do it yourself terms, keeps budget focused on real buyers. Over time the account becomes leaner and enquiries usually become cheaper.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Targeting all of Metro Vancouver. Paying for clicks from areas you do not serve wastes budget.
  • Running ads when nobody answers the phone. Schedule call ads around staffed hours.
  • Ignoring the search terms report. Irrelevant searches quietly drain spend.
  • No conversion tracking. Without it, you are guessing what works.
  • Changing everything at once. Frequent big edits reset learning and blur results.

Fixing these basics rarely costs extra. It takes a clear structure, honest tracking and steady attention, which together give Surrey businesses a fair chance to compete with larger firms across the Lower Mainland, without needing a huge monthly budget.
