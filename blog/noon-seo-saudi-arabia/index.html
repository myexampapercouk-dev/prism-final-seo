Noon SEO in Saudi Arabia: How to Rank Your Product Listings on Noon

Noon SEO in Saudi Arabia means listings Noon search can match: clear titles, full attributes, strong images. How we optimise them from the UK, and the cost.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 8 min read

  • Noon SEO in Saudi Arabia is about making every listing easy for Noon search to match and easy for Saudi shoppers to choose, through accurate titles, complete attributes, clear images and a competitive offer.
  • We optimise Noon listings remotely from the UK with no office in Saudi Arabia, and Arabic listing content is written with your team or a professional translator.
  • Listing optimisation sits within our retainers (Launch £350, Growth £750, Scale £1,500 per month); other currencies are quoted on request and results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

Noon SEO in Saudi Arabia is the work of helping your product listings appear higher in Noon search results and convert more of the Saudi shoppers who see them. It covers titles, attributes, descriptions, images, categories, pricing and stock, because Noon decides what to show based on how relevant and appealing each listing looks to its customers.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We offer marketplace listing SEO as part of our SEO service and work remotely with sellers in Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf. We have no office in the Kingdom. This guide explains how Noon search works in practical terms for the Saudi market, what we change on your listings, and what the work costs.

What Noon SEO means for Saudi sellers

Noon is one of the largest online marketplaces serving Saudi Arabia, alongside Amazon. Shoppers in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and smaller cities use its search bar and category filters, then compare price, delivery promise, ratings and images before they buy. Like every marketplace, Noon wants to show products that match the search and are likely to sell, so it rewards listings that are relevant, complete and trusted.

That means Noon SEO is not just a keyword exercise. A clever title on a listing with weak images, empty attributes or an uncompetitive price will still struggle. When we review a Saudi catalogue we look at five groups of factors:

  • Relevance. Do the title, attributes and description use the words Saudi shoppers actually type, in Arabic and English?
  • Completeness. Is every category attribute filled in so the product shows up when shoppers filter by size, colour, brand or specification?
  • Appeal. Do the main image, title and key points make the product stand out in a crowded results page?
  • Offer strength. Is the price competitive, is stock reliable and does the delivery promise suit the category?
  • Customer signals. Do ratings, reviews and return levels suggest buyers are happy with what arrives?

We cannot control pricing or fulfilment for you, but we can show where each listing falls short on these points and fix the content side ourselves.

Noon SEO Saudi Arabia: our process

Our Noon SEO work for Saudi sellers follows a clear, repeatable process. We start with the listings that matter most to revenue and work through the rest of the catalogue in priority order, so effort goes where it can make the biggest difference first.

  1. Catalogue audit. We review priority products for title quality, attribute gaps, category placement, image problems and differences between the Arabic and English versions.
  2. Keyword research. We collect the phrases shoppers use for your products, drawing on Noon search suggestions and wider search data. Arabic phrases are researched and checked with your team or a translator.
  3. Title and attribute rewrites. We set a consistent title pattern for each category and complete every relevant attribute, such as model, size, capacity, material and compatibility.
  4. Key points and descriptions. We write scannable copy that answers the questions shoppers ask before buying, without padding or repeated keywords.
  5. Image briefs. We set out the images each listing needs: a clean main image, scale and detail shots, and context images where they help.
  6. Monitoring and refinement. We track visibility, traffic and conversion on priority listings and keep improving them month by month.

Arabic-first listings for the Saudi market

Many shoppers in Saudi Arabia search in Arabic first, and a large share switch between Arabic and English depending on the product. Arabic search terms are rarely direct translations of English ones, and Saudi shoppers may use regional words that differ from those used elsewhere in the Gulf.

We write English listing content. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend that a fluent speaker familiar with Saudi usage reviews it before it goes live. We then check that both versions carry the same accurate details, so a product does not show one specification in English and another in Arabic. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites covers the same issues for your own site.

Product content that converts on Noon

Getting a listing seen is only half the work. Once a shopper opens it, the content must answer their questions quickly and give them confidence to buy. The principles are close to those for product pages on your own website, which we cover in our guide to product page SEO and our article on writing ecommerce product descriptions.

For Saudi Noon listings we focus on:

  • A title that leads with brand, product type and the most important detail, written for people first rather than stuffed with keywords.
  • Key points that cover size, compatibility, materials, what is in the box and how to use or care for the product.
  • A main image that meets category rules and reads clearly at thumbnail size on a phone, followed by detail and context images.
  • Variation setup that keeps sizes and colours on one listing, so they share reviews instead of competing with each other.
  • Accurate details that reduce returns, complaints and negative reviews.

Strong images matter as much as words. Our ecommerce photography brief shows how to plan shots your photographer or supplier can produce in Saudi Arabia.

Common Noon listing mistakes we see

When we audit Noon catalogues aimed at Saudi shoppers, the same problems appear again and again. Fixing them is often the quickest way to improve visibility before deeper keyword work starts:

  • Arabic content that is missing, machine translated without review, or different from the English version.
  • Titles that are too short to match real searches, or so long they read like a keyword list.
  • Empty attribute fields that keep products out of filtered results.
  • Products placed in a broad or wrong category, so they compete in the wrong place.
  • Duplicate listings for the same product that split sales and reviews.
  • Main images that are dark, cluttered or hard to read on a small screen.

Marketplace rules and compliance

Noon has its own seller policies, category rules, image standards and restricted product lists, and these change over time. Some categories need extra approval or documents before you can sell in Saudi Arabia. The Kingdom also has rules covering e-commerce, consumer protection, product labelling and conformity. We follow Noon's content guidelines when we write listings, but we are not legal advisers. Check the current Noon seller guidance and official Saudi guidance, and confirm product claims with your own compliance team.

We cannot photograph stock in Saudi Arabia, but we can brief your photographer or supplier on exactly what each listing needs.
