Why Bath businesses use Google Ads

Bath is a small city with a big profile. It is a World Heritage city, a magnet for visitors, home to two universities and a base for professional services, independent retail, wellness and hospitality. Competition for attention is high, and many local searches happen on phones, often minutes before someone makes a decision.

Google Ads lets you appear at the top of those searches straight away. A boutique hotel can reach people searching for weekend breaks. A dental practice can reach people searching for an emergency appointment. A wedding venue near Bath can reach couples comparing options across Somerset and Wiltshire. An online shop based in the city can sell to customers anywhere in the UK through Shopping ads.

Unlike organic search, which typically takes months, ads can start bringing traffic within days of launch. The challenge is making that traffic profitable, and that is where careful management matters.

What a Google Ads agency in Bath should do

Good Google Ads management is mostly unglamorous, careful work. When we take on a Bath account, our work usually includes:

  • Keyword research: finding the searches that signal real intent, including local phrases such as "near Bath" or nearby towns like Keynsham, Frome and Bradford on Avon.
  • Campaign structure: separating services, locations and products so budgets go where they perform best.
  • Negative keywords: blocking irrelevant searches such as jobs, free or DIY terms. Our negative keywords guide explains why this saves so much wasted spend.
  • Ad copy: clear headlines that match the search, with prices, offers or availability where they help.
  • Conversion tracking: calls, forms, bookings and purchases tracked in GA4 and Google Ads so we optimise for real results, not clicks.
  • Landing pages: advice or builds for pages that match each ad group and make it easy to enquire or buy.
  • Ongoing optimisation: weekly checks on search terms, bids, budgets and ads, plus a monthly report in plain English.

Campaign types that suit Bath businesses

Google Ads has several campaign types, and the right mix depends on what you sell and who you sell to.

Search campaigns are the foundation for most service businesses. They show text ads when someone types a relevant search, and they give the most control over keywords and budgets. Solicitors, accountants, clinics, trades and estate agents in Bath usually start here.

Shopping and Performance Max suit retailers. Bath has many independent shops selling fashion, homeware, gifts and food, and Shopping ads put your product, price and image directly in the search results. Performance Max extends reach across Google's networks using your product feed and creative assets.

Local and call-focused ads help businesses that want phone calls or visits. Linking your Google Business Profile allows location details to show with your ads, which is useful for restaurants, salons and clinics. Our guide to your Google Business Profile covers how to get it ready.

YouTube ads can build awareness for venues, attractions and lifestyle brands, provided you already have suitable video. We manage YouTube campaigns, but we do not film or edit video, so the footage needs to come from you or a production partner.

Google Ads for Bath's hospitality and tourism sector

Hotels, guest houses, spas, restaurants and attractions make up a big part of Bath's economy. Their Google Ads needs are different from a typical service business. Demand is seasonal, peaking around summer, the Christmas market and major events, and many searches come from people planning a visit from London, Bristol or further afield.

For these businesses, we focus on targeting people outside the city who are planning a trip, as well as those already in Bath looking for somewhere to eat or stay tonight. Budgets can shift with the seasons, and ads can promote direct booking to reduce reliance on third-party booking sites. Our hospitality marketing page explains how we approach the sector more broadly.

Landing pages: where most Bath campaigns are won or lost

Many Google Ads accounts struggle not because of the ads but because of the page people land on. If someone searches for "emergency dentist Bath" and lands on a general homepage, many will leave. A focused page with the service, the location, opening hours, prices where possible and a clear call button performs far better.

We can review your existing pages and suggest changes, or build dedicated landing pages as part of a website project. Small websites start from £1,000 to £2,000 and typically take three to five weeks. Our guide to landing page design in the UK sets out what makes a page convert.

How to tell if your Google Ads are being managed well

If you already run Google Ads, through another agency or yourself, a few checks will tell you a lot:

  1. Do you own the Google Ads account, and can you log in?
  2. Is conversion tracking set up, and does it count real enquiries or sales rather than page views?
  3. When did someone last review the search terms report and add negatives?
  4. Do your ads point to relevant pages, or does everything go to the homepage?
  5. Does your monthly report show cost per enquiry or return on ad spend, not just clicks and impressions?

If the answer to several of these is no, there is probably wasted spend in the account. Our PPC audit checklist goes into more detail, and our paid media service page explains how we take over and improve existing accounts.

Combining Google Ads with SEO

Google Ads and SEO work well together. Ads bring immediate traffic and data about which searches convert. SEO builds lasting visibility that does not stop when you pause spending. Many Bath businesses start with ads to get enquiries flowing, then use what they learn to guide their SEO. Both channels typically take three to six months to show their full value, so it helps to plan with that in mind.

Our Growth retainer is often used to combine the two. If you are a small business weighing up where to start, our guide to Google Ads for small businesses is a useful next read.

What to prepare before starting Google Ads in Bath

A little preparation helps any new campaign start well. Before we launch, it is useful to know your average order or job value, how many enquiries you can handle each week, which services or products matter most and any seasonal patterns in your business. Access to your website, Google Analytics and any existing Google Ads account lets us check tracking and history. With that in place, we can set sensible budgets and judge success against goals that make sense for your business.
