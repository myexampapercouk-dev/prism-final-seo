It rests on three pieces of work: a fast website with a clear booking path, search visibility for the trips and neighbourhoods guests are planning, and paid campaigns that reach travellers close to booking. Why hotel marketing in Amman needs a direct booking plan

Amman welcomes many kinds of guests: tourists using the capital as a base for Petra, Wadi Rum, Jerash and the Dead Sea, business travellers, conference visitors, people visiting family and regional guests on short breaks. Most of them plan online, and many first land on the large booking platforms. Those platforms bring useful demand, but each booking carries a commission, and they control much of the guest relationship.

A direct booking plan does not mean leaving the platforms. It means giving guests good reasons and an easy way to book with you instead, so a larger share of bookings arrive without commission and you can build a relationship with returning guests. For our wider approach, see our hospitality marketing page and our guide to winning more direct hotel bookings.

A hotel website built for direct bookings

Your website is where direct bookings happen, so it needs to be fast, attractive and simple to use on a phone. We build hotel sites with strong room pages, clear photos, honest descriptions of the location, a prominent booking button on every page and a booking engine that connects smoothly. Guests should see availability and prices quickly, without hunting through menus.

We also add reasons to book direct, such as flexible cancellation, a welcome extra or the best available rate, using only offers you actually provide. A smaller hotel or guesthouse website usually falls in our £1,000 to £2,000 range and takes three to five weeks. Our website design and development service covers the build, and our guide to hotel and B&B website design covers what good hotel sites include.

Hotel SEO for Amman

Hotel SEO helps you appear when travellers search for stays in particular parts of Amman or near the places they want to visit. We start with your Google Business Profile, which shows in map results and is often the first thing a guest sees, then build pages for your rooms, location and the trips guests take from Amman. Helpful guides, such as getting from the airport or planning a day trip, attract guests early in their planning and show them why your location works.

Structured data, fast pages and consistent details across your site and listings help search engines understand your hotel. Clear, well-organised content also helps your hotel appear in AI-generated travel suggestions. Our SEO and GEO service covers this work, and our article on hotel SEO explains the principles in more depth.

Paid campaigns that bring bookings

Paid search catches travellers when they are close to booking. Google Ads campaigns can target searches for hotels in Amman from the countries your guests come from, and send them straight to the right room or offer page. Brand campaigns protect searches for your hotel name, so guests looking for you land on your own site rather than a booking platform.

Meta Ads can promote seasonal offers, longer stays or weekend breaks to regional audiences, and YouTube Ads can share existing footage of your property with travellers planning a trip to Jordan, using material you already have. We do not film or edit video. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms and is separate from our fees. Our paid media service explains how we plan and manage campaigns.

Reviews and guest communication

Reviews influence hotel choice heavily. A simple process for inviting guests to leave a review after their stay, plus calm and helpful replies to every review, builds trust over time. Never offer incentives for reviews or write them on behalf of guests.

Direct guests also give you the chance to stay in touch. With clear consent, a short pre-arrival email with helpful information and an occasional offer for past guests can bring repeat bookings without paying commission. We can set up these emails, while your team handles personal guest contact.

Planning around Amman's travel seasons

Demand in Amman shifts through the year. Spring and autumn bring many touring visitors heading to Petra and the desert, summer brings regional and family travellers, and conferences and events create their own busy weeks. We plan content and campaigns around those patterns, publishing seasonal guides and offers well ahead of each peak so they have time to gain visibility. During quieter periods we focus budget on the guest types still travelling, such as business visitors and longer stays, so your marketing spend follows real demand rather than staying flat all year. Monthly reports show bookings by source, so you can see how direct bookings change across the seasons.

English and Arabic content

Many Amman hotels welcome both Arabic-speaking and international guests. We write all our content in English. Arabic versions should come from your own team or a translator, and we can build the site so both languages sit side by side with clean navigation and correct page setup for search engines.

How AI-powered tools fit into hotel marketing

We use AI-powered tools for research and reporting, such as grouping traveller searches, spotting content gaps and summarising campaign and booking trends. Guest-facing content is written and checked by people, and you approve everything before it goes live.
