Who we work with in Jeddah

Jeddah is a busy commercial and port city with a large retail, hospitality and services economy, and a strong flow of visitors heading to Makkah and Madinah. Many of its buyers search on mobile, often in Arabic, and expect a quick answer. We run Google Ads for:

  • Online stores selling fashion, abayas, fragrance, beauty, electronics and home goods across the Kingdom.
  • Restaurants, cafes and food brands that want bookings, orders and footfall.
  • Hotels and serviced apartments chasing direct bookings rather than relying only on travel sites.
  • Clinics and health providers that need careful, compliant ads (approval rests with the client).
  • Property developers and brokers generating qualified buyer and tenant leads.
  • Business services and trade suppliers serving the port, logistics and construction sectors.

How we set up a Jeddah account

A good account starts with structure, not with spend. Our setup usually follows these steps:

  1. Discovery call. We learn your products, margins, service areas, busy seasons and what a good lead or sale is worth to you.
  2. Keyword research in both languages. We map Arabic and English searches, including the way people mix the two, and note local terms such as district names across Jeddah.
  3. Conversion tracking. We set up Google Ads and GA4 tracking for forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks and purchases so we can see what each campaign produces.
  4. Campaign build. We group keywords tightly, write ad copy in English, place Arabic copy supplied by your team or translator, and add sitelinks, calls and location details.
  5. Landing page review. We check speed, mobile layout, right-to-left display and the clarity of the offer, and suggest fixes or build new pages.
  6. Launch and weekly tuning. We add negative keywords, adjust bids and budgets, test new ads and pause what does not work.

Arabic and English campaigns

In Jeddah, many people search in Arabic, while others use English or a mix, especially for brands, technology and travel. We usually run separate campaigns or ad groups per language so that ad text, keywords and landing pages match. A search in Arabic should lead to an Arabic page, laid out right to left, with prices, delivery details and contact options that make sense to a Saudi customer.

We write and plan in English. Arabic headlines, descriptions and page copy are written with your team or a professional translator, then we load them into the account and check that they fit the character limits. This keeps the meaning accurate and the tone right for your brand.

Shopping ads for Jeddah online stores

If you sell online, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns can put your products, images and prices directly into search results. These campaigns depend on a clean product feed in Google Merchant Center. We check titles, images, prices, availability and shipping settings, fix feed errors and split products into groups by margin or best sellers so budget goes where it earns most.

Our Google Shopping ads guide explains how feeds and campaigns work in more detail. If your store runs on Shopify, our page on Shopify for Saudi brands covers store setup, and Noon SEO in Saudi Arabia covers selling on the marketplace alongside your own site.

Seasonal demand in Jeddah

Search behaviour in Jeddah shifts through the year. Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha change when and how people shop, often moving activity late into the evening. National Day, Founding Day, the summer season and major sales events such as White Friday also bring spikes. Pilgrimage seasons affect hotels, transport and retail near the city.

We plan budgets and ad copy around these periods with you. That might mean raising budgets ahead of a known peak, changing ad schedules to match late-night browsing, or preparing seasonal offers and pages in advance so they are approved and live in time.

Landing pages that convert

Even a strong campaign struggles if the landing page is slow or unclear. Many visitors in Jeddah arrive on a phone, sometimes on a busy mobile connection. We look at load speed, the first screen, the call to action and trust details such as delivery terms, returns and contact options, including WhatsApp where you use it.

Where pages need more than small changes, we can design and build new ones. Our page on web design in Jeddah covers full site work, and our guide to website speed optimisation explains the checks we run.

Google Ads and SEO together

Paid search brings traffic quickly, while SEO builds visibility that does not stop when the budget does. Search term reports from Google Ads show which phrases bring real enquiries, which helps decide which pages to improve for organic search. Many Jeddah businesses run both. Our page on SEO in Jeddah explains how we approach organic search for the city.

Pricing and timelines

We manage Google Ads on a monthly retainer. Our Launch plan is £350 a month, Growth is £750 a month and Scale is £1,500 a month, depending on the number of campaigns, languages and channels. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from our fee. Saudi riyal pricing is quoted on request. Most accounts need a few weeks of data before patterns are clear, and paid results typically build over 3 to 6 months as we refine keywords, ads, bids and pages. We do not promise a fixed cost per lead or return, because those depend on your market, offer and budget.

Rules and compliance

Google Ads has its own policies on restricted content, including health, finance and some regulated products, and Saudi Arabia has its own laws on advertising, e-commerce and consumer protection. We keep campaigns within Google policies and general good practice, but we are not legal advisers. Please check official guidance or your own adviser for local requirements. In regulated sectors, final approval of ad wording and claims rests with you and your compliance team.

Working with a remote studio

Because we are UK-based with no Jeddah office, everything runs online. You get a shared report, a regular call and clear notes on what changed and why. The time difference is small, so working hours overlap for most of the day. You keep ownership of your Google Ads account, your Merchant Center and your data at all times.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Jeddah?

Look for clear tracking of calls, forms and sales, campaigns built for both Arabic and English searches, honest reporting and an account you own. Ask how they handle landing pages and seasonal peaks such as Ramadan and White Friday.

Do you have an office in Jeddah?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely. We have no office in Jeddah or elsewhere in Saudi Arabia, and we work through video calls, shared dashboards and written updates.

Can you run Google Ads in Arabic?

Yes. We build Arabic campaigns and ad groups, and the Arabic ad copy and landing page text are written with your team or a translator. We then load, check and test that copy in the account.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month depending on scope. Ad spend is paid to Google separately, and Saudi riyal pricing is quoted on request.

How long until Google Ads shows results?

Ads can start bringing clicks within days of approval, but results typically build over 3 to 6 months as we refine keywords, bids, ads and pages using real data.

Do you also run Shopping ads for Jeddah stores?

Yes. We set up and manage Shopping and Performance Max campaigns, including fixing Merchant Center feed issues, for online stores selling across Saudi Arabia.
