Google Ads Agency for Jordan: Search, Shopping and YouTube Campaigns for Amman and Beyond

Google Ads for businesses in Jordan: Arabic and English search, tracking, budgets and costs, managed remotely by a UK-based studio with no office in Amman.

A Google Ads agency for Jordan plans, builds and manages paid search, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube campaigns that put a business in front of people searching in Amman, Irbid, Zarqa, Aqaba and across the country. The best results come from campaigns that respect how Jordanians actually search, in Arabic, English and a mix of both, and from tracking that measures real enquiries rather than clicks.

We have no office in Jordan and work through video calls, shared documents and your own advertising accounts. This guide explains how we approach Google Ads for the Jordanian market, what it costs and how to judge whether it is working.

Why Google Ads suits Jordanian businesses

Google is the main search engine in Jordan, and people use it at the moment they need something: a dentist in Abdoun, a supplier for a construction project, a language course, a hotel in Aqaba or a software tool for their company. Paid search lets you appear at that moment, with a message written for the exact search, and you pay mainly when someone clicks.

That intent is what separates search ads from most social advertising. Social platforms are excellent for building awareness and demand, but Google Ads captures demand that already exists. For many Jordanian firms, especially in services, education, healthcare, tourism and B2B, that makes it one of the most direct routes to enquiries. It also complements organic search, which our guide to SEO in Jordan covers in detail, and our comparison of SEO and PPC explains how the two work together.

Google Ads agency Jordan: how we set up and run campaigns

Every account is different, but our work for Jordanian clients usually follows the same sequence.

  1. Audit or discovery. If you already advertise, we review your account structure, search terms, budgets and tracking. If you are new to Google Ads, we agree goals, audiences, locations and the value of a lead or sale.
  2. Keyword research in both languages. We research Arabic and English searches, including transliterated and mixed-language queries, and group them by intent.
  3. Tracking first. We set up conversion tracking for forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks and purchases before spending meaningful budget.
  4. Campaign build. We create tightly themed campaigns with relevant ads, assets and landing pages, plus negative keyword lists from day one.
  5. Launch and learning. We start with controlled budgets, watch search terms closely and remove waste early.
  6. Optimisation and reporting. We adjust bids, ads, keywords and landing pages each month and send a plain-language report on what changed and why.

We cover the technical side of tracking in our conversion tracking guide, which applies equally to accounts targeting Jordan.

Arabic, English and mixed-language search

Language is the single biggest factor in Jordanian search campaigns. Many people search in Arabic, many search in English, especially for professional, technical and international products, and some type Arabic words in Latin characters. Each group needs its own keywords and its own ad copy.

We normally recommend separate Arabic and English campaigns, or at least separate ad groups, so that each audience sees ads and landing pages in the language they used. Mixing languages in one ad group makes it hard to control relevance, Quality Score and budget. Better relevance tends to improve Quality Score, which in turn affects how much you pay for each click.

Prism writes in English. Arabic ad copy, keywords and landing page text are written with your team or a professional translator, and we ask a fluent speaker on your side to approve them before launch. Jordanian Arabic has its own expressions, and a phrase that reads well in one Arab country can feel unnatural in another.

Location targeting across Jordan

Jordan's population is concentrated in Amman, Zarqa and Irbid, with Aqaba important for tourism and logistics. A clinic in west Amman and a tour operator selling Petra and Wadi Rum trips need very different location settings. The clinic should target a tight radius around its location, while the tour operator may target travellers in other countries as well as people already in Jordan.

We also check whether Google should target people located in an area or people showing interest in it. For tourism and real estate, interest-based targeting can reach buyers planning ahead from abroad. For local services it usually wastes money. Our location targeting guide explains the settings in more depth, and our international PPC guide covers advertising to several countries at once.

Tracking enquiries properly

Many Jordanian customers prefer to call or send a WhatsApp message rather than fill in a form. If your tracking only records form submissions, Google's automated bidding will learn from an incomplete picture and may cut back the very keywords that produce your best leads.

We set up conversion actions for calls from ads, calls from your website, WhatsApp click-throughs, form submissions and, for online shops, purchases with their values. Where possible we use enhanced conversions and import offline outcomes, such as qualified leads or closed sales, so the account optimises towards revenue rather than raw volume. Analytics and advertising accounts stay in your name so you keep full ownership of the data.

Controlling waste

Broad match and automated bidding can work well, but they also find searches that have nothing to do with your business. In a market where budgets are often modest, a few hundred irrelevant clicks can wipe out a month's results.

  • We review search terms frequently in the first weeks and add negatives as we go.
  • We exclude job seekers, students looking for free resources and other irrelevant intents where appropriate.
  • We set ad schedules around your opening hours and the Jordanian working week.
  • We adjust activity during Ramadan and public holidays, when search patterns and response times change.

Our negative keywords guide shows the method we use.

Landing pages that convert

An ad can only be as good as the page it sends people to. A Jordanian visitor who searches in Arabic should land on a clear Arabic page that loads quickly on a phone, shows the service they searched for, explains the next step and makes calling or messaging easy. Sending all traffic to a generic home page usually lowers conversion rates and raises costs.

If your website needs work, we can build or improve landing pages as part of the project. Our guide to web design in Jordan explains how we build bilingual, mobile-first sites for Jordanian businesses, and our landing page design guide covers the principles that apply in any market.

Regulated sectors

Healthcare, finance, education and some other sectors face extra rules in Jordan and under Google's own advertising policies. We keep ads factual and avoid exaggerated claims, but we are not legal advisers. Check official Jordanian guidance and Google's policies, and ask your own adviser to confirm what applies. In regulated sectors, compliance approval for ads and landing pages rests with your business.

Is Google Ads right for your business?

Google Ads suits businesses that sell something people actively search for and that can handle enquiries promptly. If people do not yet know your product exists, social advertising or content may need to build demand first. If your margins are very thin or your website cannot convert visitors, fixing those foundations comes before scaling spend. We are happy to tell you if we think another channel would serve you better.

FAQs

What does a Google Ads agency in Jordan do?

It researches keywords, builds and manages campaigns, sets up tracking, controls wasted spend and reports on results. We provide this service remotely from the UK.

Do you have an office in Amman?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK, has no office in Jordan and works remotely with Jordanian businesses through calls, shared documents and your own accounts.

Can you run Google Ads in Arabic?

Yes. We build Arabic campaigns, but the Arabic ad copy and landing page text are written with your team or a professional translator and approved by a fluent speaker.

How much should we spend on Google Ads in Jordan?

It depends on competition, your services and the value of a lead. We recommend a budget after research, start in a controlled way and scale once tracking shows what works.

How long before Google Ads works?

Ads can bring enquiries within days of launch, but accounts usually need 3 to 6 months of data and optimisation before they become consistently efficient.

Can you track WhatsApp and phone enquiries?

Yes. We set up conversion actions for calls, WhatsApp clicks and forms so that Google's bidding learns from the enquiries that matter to your business.
