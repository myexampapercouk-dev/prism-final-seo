Why Google Ads suits Lisbon businesses

Lisbon has a mixed audience. Local residents search in European Portuguese. A large international community, including remote workers, students and people who have relocated, often searches in English. Tourists search in many languages, frequently on their phones while already in the city. Google Ads lets you reach each of these groups separately, with different keywords, budgets and messages.

The businesses that tend to benefit most include:

  • Hospitality and tourism. Hotels, guest houses, restaurants, tours and experiences across the city and nearby coast.
  • Professional services. Accountants, lawyers, relocation advisers and consultancies serving residents and newcomers.
  • Health and wellness. Clinics, physiotherapists and studios, within the limits of advertising rules for their field.
  • Property. Agencies and property services working with local and international buyers and tenants.
  • Technology companies. Start-ups and software firms based in Lisbon that sell across Europe.

Portuguese and English campaigns

Mixing languages in one campaign is a common mistake. Google matches ads to the user's language settings and search terms, so a campaign with Portuguese and English keywords and ads together tends to show the wrong ad to the wrong person. Separate campaigns let you set budgets, bids and messages for each audience and see clearly which one performs.

European Portuguese matters too. Many keyword tools and ad templates lean towards Brazilian Portuguese, and the differences in vocabulary and spelling are noticeable to people in Lisbon. Your team or translator should check every Portuguese ad and landing page before it goes live.

Landing pages should match the ad's language and promise. An English ad that leads to a Portuguese page, or a page that does not mention the service in the ad, wastes the click. Our guide to landing page design covers what a strong page includes, and our post on web design for Lisbon businesses covers bilingual site setup.

Location targeting and tourism

Location settings decide who sees your ads. A dental clinic in Lisbon usually wants people physically in or near the city. A hotel may want people in its main source markets abroad who are researching Lisbon, and may want to exclude people already nearby. A software company may want all of Portugal or several countries. Google Ads offers options for presence and interest, and the right choice depends on the goal. Our Google Ads location targeting guide explains the settings.

For tourism businesses, timing matters as well. Searches for tours, restaurants and activities often happen on the day, on mobile, so call assets, location assets and fast mobile pages are especially useful.

Tracking that you can trust

Google Ads can only be optimised well if it knows which clicks turn into enquiries. We set up conversion tracking for forms, calls and bookings, and test it before campaigns spend meaningfully. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking explains the steps.

In Portugal, as across the EU, analytics and advertising tags generally need consent before they run. That means reported conversions will usually be lower than real ones. Consent-aware tracking features can help fill some of the gap, but campaign decisions should allow for it. We explain this clearly in reporting rather than presenting partial numbers as the whole picture.

Google Ads alongside SEO

Paid search brings enquiries quickly once campaigns and tracking are ready. SEO builds visibility more slowly but keeps working without paying for each click. Many Lisbon businesses use both: ads for immediate demand and competitive terms, SEO for longer-term growth. Our post on SEO for Lisbon businesses covers the organic side and explains how the two channels share keyword insights.

We also use AI-powered tools to help generate ad variations and spot patterns in search terms. Every ad is still reviewed by a person, and Portuguese copy is checked by a fluent speaker.

  1. Discovery call. We discuss your services, audiences, languages, budget and any past campaigns.

Compliance basics for Portuguese advertisers

These notes are general information only. Please follow official guidance from the relevant authorities and take advice from your own legal adviser.

  • GDPR. Lead forms, call tracking and remarketing lists involve personal data. You need a clear privacy notice, a lawful basis and sensible retention. Portugal's data protection authority publishes guidance.
  • Cookie consent. Under the ePrivacy rules as applied in Portuguese law, analytics and advertising cookies generally need prior consent, and rejecting should be as easy as accepting. Our post on cookie banner design covers the practical side.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer services, including e-commerce. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt, but accessible landing pages help every visitor. The expected standard is EN 301 549, which points to WCAG at level AA. See our overview of the European Accessibility Act for websites.

In regulated fields such as health, finance and legal services, advertising rules can be strict, and compliance approval for ad copy rests with you and your advisers.
