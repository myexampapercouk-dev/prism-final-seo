How we build Google Ads campaigns for Rotterdam

We start with research, not guesswork. We look at what your buyers search for in Dutch and English, how competitive those terms are and what competitors say in their ads. Then we build campaigns around clear groups of keywords, each pointing to a relevant landing page.

  • Keyword research in Dutch and English, with negative keywords to block irrelevant searches.
  • Location targeting set to Rotterdam, the wider South Holland region, the Netherlands or further, depending on who you serve.
  • Ad copy written in English and checked or adapted into Dutch by your team or translator.
  • Conversion tracking for form fills, calls and sales, set up to respect cookie consent.
  • Landing page advice so the page matches the ad and makes it easy to act.

For business-to-business campaigns, we often bid on narrow, high-intent terms rather than broad phrases. A short list of buyers searching for a specific service is usually worth more than lots of curious clicks.

Privacy, consent and accessibility

Ads and tracking depend on personal data, so European rules apply. These notes are general, so please check official guidance or ask your adviser.

  • GDPR covers the personal data collected through forms, calls and tracking tags.
  • ePrivacy and cookie consent rules generally require consent before advertising and analytics cookies are set. We set up Google's consent mode and tags to work with your consent banner, which affects how conversions are measured.
  • The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer-facing digital services, including e-commerce. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt. The harmonised standard EN 301 549 points to WCAG level AA, a sensible target for landing pages.

Our website accessibility guide covers practical fixes for landing pages and forms.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

When Rotterdam businesses ask us to review an existing account, we often find the same issues. Fixing them can make a real difference to how far the budget goes, without needing to spend more.

  • Broad keywords with no negatives. Ads show for searches that have nothing to do with your service, such as job seekers or students. We add negative keyword lists and tighten match types.
  • Missing or broken conversion tracking. Without accurate tracking, automated bidding has nothing reliable to learn from. We check every form, call and purchase is recorded once and only once.
  • One landing page for everything. Sending every ad to the home page makes it harder for visitors to act. We match each ad group to the most relevant page.
  • Wrong location settings. Campaigns set to show to people interested in a location, rather than in it, can waste money. We check the setting suits your business.
  • Languages mixed up. Dutch ads shown to English speakers, or the other way round. We separate campaigns or ad groups by language where it helps.
  • Automated recommendations accepted blindly. Some suggestions raise spend without improving results. We review each one before applying it.

We also look at timing. Business-to-business searches around Rotterdam often peak during working hours on weekdays, while consumer searches can be busier in the evening and at weekends. Ad schedules and bid adjustments can follow that pattern so the budget is spent when your buyers are most likely to act.

Finally, we keep the account in your name. You own your Google Ads account, your data and your conversion history, and we work inside it with the access you grant. If you ever move on, everything stays with you.
