Noon SEO in the UAE: How to Get Your Product Listings Found on Noon

Noon SEO in the UAE means writing titles, attributes and images that Noon search can match. How we optimise Noon listings from the UK and what it costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Noon SEO is about making each listing easy for Noon search to match and easy for shoppers to choose: accurate titles, full attributes, clear images and competitive offers.
  • We optimise Noon listings remotely from the UK with no office in the UAE, and Arabic listing content is written with your team or a professional translator.
  • Listing optimisation sits within our retainers (Launch £350, Growth £750, Scale £1,500 per month); other currencies are quoted on request and results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

Noon SEO in the UAE is the work of getting your product listings to appear higher in Noon search results and convert more of the shoppers who see them. It covers product titles, attributes, descriptions, images, categories, pricing and stock, because Noon decides what to show based on how relevant and appealing each listing looks.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We offer marketplace listing SEO as part of our SEO service, working remotely with sellers in the UAE and the wider Gulf. We have no office in the UAE. This guide explains how Noon search works in practical terms, what we change on listings, and what the work costs.

What Noon SEO actually involves

Noon is one of the main online marketplaces in the UAE, alongside Amazon. Shoppers use its search bar and category filters to find products, then compare price, delivery speed, ratings and images before buying. Like any marketplace, Noon wants to show listings that match what people type and that are likely to sell.

That means Noon SEO is not only about keywords. A well-written title on a listing with poor images, missing attributes or a weak offer will still struggle. We look at the whole listing and the factors around it:

  • Relevance. Does the title, attribute data and description match the words shoppers actually use?
  • Completeness. Are all the category attributes filled in so the product appears in filtered searches?
  • Appeal. Do the images, title and bullet points make someone want to click and buy?
  • Offer strength. Is the price competitive, is stock reliable and is delivery fast enough for the category?
  • Customer signals. Do ratings, reviews and return rates suggest buyers are happy?

We cannot control every one of these factors, especially pricing and fulfilment, but we can show you where each listing is falling short and fix the content side ourselves.

Noon SEO UAE: how we optimise your listings

Our Noon SEO work in the UAE follows a clear process. We start with the listings that matter most to revenue, then work through the rest of the catalogue in priority order.

  1. Catalogue audit. We review your top products for title quality, attribute gaps, image issues, category placement and content in both languages.
  2. Keyword research. We gather the phrases shoppers use for your products in English and, with your team or a translator, in Arabic, using Noon search suggestions and wider search data.
  3. Title and attribute rewrites. We rewrite titles in a consistent pattern and fill every relevant attribute such as size, colour, material, model and compatibility.
  4. Descriptions and bullet points. We write clear, scannable copy that answers the questions shoppers ask before buying.
  5. Image guidance. We brief the images each listing needs, such as a clean main image, scale shots, detail shots and lifestyle images.
  6. Monitoring and iteration. We track search visibility, traffic and conversion for priority listings and keep refining them.

Writing listings in Arabic and English

Noon shows content in both Arabic and English, and many shoppers in the UAE switch between the two. A listing that only reads well in one language misses part of its audience. Arabic search terms are not simple translations of English ones either: shoppers may use different words, regional spellings or brand names written in Latin script.

We write English listing content. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend a fluent speaker reviews it before it goes live. We then make sure both versions carry the same accurate details, so the product does not appear one way in English and another in Arabic. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites covers similar issues for your own site.

Noon versus Amazon and your own store

Many UAE sellers list on Noon and Amazon at the same time and also run their own online store. Each channel has its own search system, fees and customer expectations, so copying the same listing everywhere rarely gets the best result. Product content that converts

Getting a listing seen is only half the job. Once a shopper lands on it, the content has to answer their questions quickly. The principles are much the same as for product pages on your own site, which we cover in our guide to product page SEO.

For Noon listings we focus on:

  • A title that leads with brand, product type and the most important detail, without keyword stuffing.
  • Bullet points that cover size, compatibility, materials, what is in the box and care or usage.
  • A main image on a clean background that matches category rules, followed by detail and context shots.
  • Consistent variation setup so sizes and colours sit on one listing rather than competing against each other.
  • Accurate information that reduces returns and negative reviews.

Our article on marketplace SEO for a UK retailer marketplace shows how the same listing principles apply across different platforms.

Common Noon listing mistakes

When we audit a Noon catalogue, the same problems come up again and again. Fixing them is often the quickest way to improve visibility before any deeper keyword work begins:

  • Titles that are too short to match real searches, or so long they read like a list of keywords.
  • Empty attribute fields, which keep products out of filtered results such as size or colour.
  • Products listed in a broad or wrong category, so they compete in the wrong place.
  • Arabic content that is missing, machine translated or different from the English version.
  • Duplicate listings for the same product that split sales and reviews.
  • Main images that are dark, cluttered or do not show the product clearly at thumbnail size.

None of these needs a big budget to fix, but together they can hold a whole catalogue back.

Marketplace rules and compliance

Noon has its own seller policies, category rules, image standards and restricted product lists, and these change over time. Some categories need extra approval or documentation. The UAE also has rules on consumer protection, product labelling and e-commerce. We follow Noon's content guidelines when writing listings, but we are not legal advisers. Check the current Noon seller guidance and official UAE guidance, and confirm product claims with your own compliance team.

What you need to provide

Because we work remotely, we rely on you for some inputs. These usually include access to your Noon seller account or exported listing data, product specifications, existing images or the ability to arrange new ones, and someone who can approve changes and answer product questions. We cannot photograph products in the UAE, but we can brief your photographer or supplier on exactly what each listing needs.
