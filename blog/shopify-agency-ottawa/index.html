Shopify for Ottawa Businesses: How a Remote Shopify Agency Works

Looking for a Shopify agency in Ottawa? How remote Shopify design, setup and migration works for Ontario retailers, from bilingual stores to shipping and costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify agency for an Ottawa business should plan, design and set up a fast store that is easy to manage, with payments, shipping, taxes and apps configured for selling across Canada.
  • Ottawa sits on the Ontario and Quebec border, so many stores need English and French content, written with your team or a translator, plus a general awareness of privacy and language rules.
  • Our ecommerce builds cost £1,500 to £3,500 and typically take five to twelve weeks, with ongoing retainers from £350 a month and Canadian dollar quotes available on request.

A Shopify agency working with an Ottawa business should plan, design and set up an online store that looks professional, loads quickly, is easy for your team to manage and turns visitors into paying customers across Canada and beyond. Because store planning, theme design, product setup, app configuration and launch can all happen online, Shopify work can be delivered remotely. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that builds and improves Shopify stores for Canadian businesses online; we have no office in Ottawa or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide explains what a Shopify agency should do for an Ottawa retailer, how to plan a bilingual store, what to set up for payments, shipping and taxes, what our Shopify work costs and what to ask before choosing a provider.

What a Shopify agency in Ottawa should do for you

Ottawa's economy is best known for the federal government, but it also includes a large technology sector, education, health care, tourism, food and drink, arts and culture, and a growing number of independent brands selling clothing, homeware, gifts, beauty products, specialty foods and more. Shopify suits many of these businesses, from a single-shop retailer adding online sales to a brand that sells only online.

A good Shopify agency should:

  • Plan the store around your products, customers and how they like to browse.
  • Design or customise a theme that reflects your brand and works well on phones.
  • Set up products and collections with clear titles, descriptions, images and variants.
  • Configure payments, shipping and taxes for the places you sell.
  • Choose apps carefully, using only what you need to avoid slowing the store down.
  • Build in search basics so products and collections can be found on Google.
  • Train your team to manage orders, products and content after launch.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers Shopify builds, redesigns and migrations, and our ecommerce marketing page explains how we support stores after launch. For points that apply nationwide, see our guide to Shopify in Canada.

Planning a bilingual Shopify store

Ottawa is a bilingual city on the border with Quebec, and many customers in the region, including across the river in Gatineau, prefer to shop in French. If you sell to Quebec or want to serve French-speaking customers properly, a bilingual store is often worth planning from the start rather than adding later.

Shopify supports multiple languages within one store, so you can offer English and French versions of products, collections, pages and checkout. We plan the structure, navigation and templates in English; French product descriptions, pages and marketing content are written with your team or a professional translator, and we make sure both versions are set up consistently.

If you sell to customers in Quebec, the Charter of the French Language sets rules for commercial content, including websites and product information. What applies depends on your business, so check the official guidance from the Office québécois de la langue française and take advice where needed. Responsibility for compliance rests with your business.

Moving to Shopify from another platform

Many Ottawa businesses come to Shopify from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or an older custom system. A migration needs careful planning so you keep your products, customers, order history where possible and, importantly, your search visibility. That means mapping old URLs to new ones with redirects, keeping page titles and descriptions that already perform and checking that images and product data transfer cleanly.

Our guide to WooCommerce to Shopify migration explains the process in detail, and the same principles apply to other platforms.

Privacy and accessibility in general terms

Online stores collect personal information through checkout, accounts, analytics and email sign-ups. In general terms:

  • PIPEDA, the federal private sector privacy law, generally applies to commercial activity in Ontario, including online sales.
  • Quebec's Law 25 updated the province's private sector privacy rules, which may matter if you collect information from customers in Quebec.
  • CASL, Canada's anti-spam legislation, governs commercial emails and texts, so newsletter and abandoned cart messages need the right consent.
  • Ontario's accessibility law sets requirements for some organisations, and the international WCAG guidelines are a widely used reference for accessible websites.

We set up consent tools, forms and email sign-ups in line with your approach, and build with accessibility in mind, but we are not lawyers. Check the official guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Quebec's privacy regulator and the CRTC, and take advice from your own adviser. In regulated sectors, compliance approval rests with your business.

Look for an agency that plans the store around your products and customers, explains costs clearly, uses apps sparingly, builds in search basics and makes sure you own your store. Ask how they handle French content and migrations.

Yes. Shopify projects are planned and built online. Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Ottawa, and we schedule calls at times that suit Eastern Time.

Our Shopify builds cost £1,500 to £3,500 depending on scope. Shopify's subscription, apps and payment fees are separate, and Canadian dollar quotes are available on request.

Can my Shopify store be in English and French?

Yes. Shopify supports multiple languages. We set up the structure, and French content is written with your team or a professional translator.
