Hiring a Remote PPC Agency for Your US Business: Google, Meta and TikTok Ads

Hiring a remote PPC agency for your US business? See how overseas ad management works for Google, Meta and TikTok, and what to check on tracking and access.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A remote PPC agency can run Google, Meta and TikTok campaigns for a US business well, as long as the ad accounts, billing and tracking stay in your name.
  • Campaigns should be scheduled, targeted and reported in your own time zone and currency, with ad copy written for US audiences.
  • Ad spend is always paid separately to the platforms; our management retainers are published in pounds and other currencies are quoted on request.

A remote PPC agency can manage Google, Meta and TikTok ads for a US business just as effectively as a local one, because every platform is run online and every change is logged in the ad account. What matters is that the accounts and billing stay in your name, tracking is set up properly, and campaigns are targeted, scheduled and reported for your market and time zone.

We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US businesses on paid media, SEO and websites. This guide explains how overseas ad management works, what to check before you hand over access, and how to tell a careful agency from a careless one.

Remote PPC agency for US businesses: why location matters less than you think

Paid media is one of the most remote-friendly marketing services. Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager and TikTok Ads Manager are web tools; an account manager in another country sees exactly what a manager down the street would see. Bids, budgets, audiences and ads are all adjusted online, and every change is recorded in the account history for you to review.

The things that do depend on location are knowledge of your market and availability when you need answers. Both can be checked before you sign:

  • Market knowledge: does the agency research US search terms, US competitors and US audience behaviour, or reuse what works elsewhere?
  • Availability: is there a regular call slot in your working hours, and a route for urgent issues such as a disapproved campaign or broken tracking?

Keep accounts, billing and data in your name

This is non-negotiable, wherever your agency is based. Before any campaign goes live, confirm:

  1. You own the ad accounts. Google Ads, Meta Business Manager and TikTok Business Center should be created under your business, with the agency added as a partner or user.
  2. Billing goes on your card or invoice. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms, in your currency, never routed through the agency's account without a clear written agreement.
  3. Tracking tools belong to you. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and pixels should sit in your accounts so the data stays with you if you change agency.
  4. Access can be removed. You should be able to remove the agency's access at any time without losing campaign history.

Tracking comes before spending

Ads without reliable conversion tracking are guesswork. A careful agency will check and fix tracking before scaling budgets. That means conversion actions for leads, calls or purchases, consent settings that reflect your privacy approach, and a clear view of which campaigns produce results.

Our step-by-step guide to Google Ads conversion tracking shows what should be in place. If your accounts have been running for a while, our PPC audit checklist is a good way to judge their current state before any agency takes over.

US privacy rules vary by state and continue to change, so ask your lawyer or check current official guidance on consent and data sharing for your business. Your agency can configure the tools, but the policy decisions are yours.

Targeting, scheduling and copy for US audiences

A remote agency must set campaigns up around your market, not its own office hours. Check that:

  • Location targeting matches where you actually sell or serve, whether that is the whole country, chosen states or a radius around your locations. Our location targeting guide explains the options.
  • Ad schedules use your account's time zone, so ads run when your phones are answered or your team can respond.
  • Ad copy uses US spelling, US terms and dollar prices. We write in UK English for our own site, but your ads should read as though they were written next door.
  • Claims in ads are accurate and supportable. Advertising rules, including the FTC's guidance on endorsements and claims, apply to your ads, so check current official guidance and take legal advice for regulated products.
  • Landing pages match the ad and work well on mobile, because even a well-run campaign struggles with a weak page.

Communication and reporting across time zones

The UK is usually five hours ahead of the US East Coast and eight ahead of the West Coast. That gives a dependable overlap in the US morning. A sensible rhythm looks like this:

  • A short weekly or fortnightly call in the overlap window during the first months.
  • A monthly written report covering spend, conversions, cost per result and what changed.
  • Clear rules on budget changes, such as which changes the agency can make alone and which need your approval.
  • An agreed response time for urgent issues, such as a disapproved ad or a tracking fault.

Because changes are logged in each platform, you can always see what was done and when, whatever the time difference.

What remote ad management costs

There are two separate costs: the ad spend you pay the platforms, and the management fee you pay the agency. Our management retainers are published in pounds: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 and Scale at £1,500, with ad spend paid separately. Other currencies, including US dollars, are quoted on request. See what each level covers on our pricing page, and read our guide to PPC management costs for the different fee models agencies use.

Paid results typically take 3 to 6 months to settle, as campaigns gather data and the agency tests audiences, keywords and creative. Be wary of anyone promising a fixed return on ad spend before seeing your accounts.

What to prepare before the first call

You will get a faster, more useful start if you gather a few things before speaking to any agency:

  • Admin access details for your existing ad accounts, analytics and tag manager, ready to share as user invitations rather than passwords.
  • Your average order value or the value of a typical lead, so targets can be set against real economics.
  • The products, services or locations you most want to grow, and any you do not want to advertise.
  • Your monthly ad budget range and any seasonal peaks.
  • Past campaign history, including what has worked and what has not.

With this in hand, a good agency can give you a candid view of your accounts within the first few weeks.

Red flags when choosing a remote PPC agency

  • They want to run ads from their own account rather than yours.
  • They plan to increase budgets before checking tracking.
  • Reports show clicks and impressions but not leads or sales.
  • Ad copy is generic and could apply to any country.
  • No clear rule on who approves budget changes.
  • Long notice periods before any results have been reviewed.

FAQs

Should I hire a remote PPC agency for my US business?

It can work well, because ad platforms are run online and every change is logged. Make sure accounts, billing and tracking stay in your name and that the agency targets and writes for your market.

Can a UK agency run Google Ads for a US company?

Yes. The agency is added to your Google Ads account as a user or manager, and campaigns are targeted, scheduled and billed in your account's own settings and currency.

Who pays the ad spend?

You pay the platforms directly for ad spend. The agency's management fee is separate and invoiced by the agency.

Will my ads run in my time zone?

They should. Ad schedules follow the time zone set on your ad account, so ads can run during your opening hours wherever the agency is based.

How long before paid ads show results?

Some data arrives quickly, but paid results typically take 3 to 6 months to settle as campaigns are tested and refined.

Can I pay the management fee in US dollars?

Our published prices are in pounds sterling. Other currencies, including US dollars, are quoted on request.
