Meta Ads Agency for UAE Businesses: Facebook and Instagram Ads Explained

Looking for a Meta ads agency in the UAE? How Facebook and Instagram campaigns are planned, tracked and managed for Dubai and Abu Dhabi brands, plus fees.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Meta ads for a UAE business work best with accurate tracking, creative made for Instagram and Facebook placements, and audiences split by emirate, language and intent.
  • A good agency runs campaigns in your own ad account, explains its structure and reports on leads or sales rather than likes and reach alone.
  • Our paid media retainers are priced in pounds from £350 per month, ad spend is paid separately to Meta, and other currencies are quoted on request.

A Meta ads agency for a UAE business plans, runs and improves Facebook and Instagram campaigns that reach the right people in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the other emirates, then turns their attention into leads, bookings or sales. Good management depends on reliable tracking, creative built for each placement, sensible audience choices and regular, honest reporting.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Meta ads remotely for businesses in the UAE and across the Gulf. We have no office in the UAE, and every campaign runs in your own Meta Business account, with video calls and shared reports keeping you informed. This guide explains how our paid media management works for UAE brands, what it costs and what to ask any agency.

Why Meta ads suit UAE businesses

Instagram and Facebook are part of daily life for many people in the UAE, and Meta's tools let you reach audiences by location, language, interests and behaviour. That makes them a strong fit for consumer brands, restaurants, beauty and wellness businesses, property firms, education providers, events and e-commerce stores.

Unlike search ads, which reach people already looking for something, Meta ads create demand. They work well for introducing a product, promoting an offer, building an audience you can retarget later and supporting launches. Our remarketing guide explains how retargeting fits in. Many UAE businesses run Meta alongside Google Ads, with each covering a different stage of the customer journey.

Targeting across the emirates

The UAE is a compact country, but audiences differ by emirate, language and lifestyle. A restaurant in Dubai Marina, a clinic in Abu Dhabi and an online shop delivering across the country need very different settings.

  • Location. Target the emirates or areas you can genuinely serve, and decide whether to include visitors or only residents.
  • Language. Run separate ad sets or ads for Arabic and English where your customers use both, rather than mixing languages in one ad.
  • Audiences. Combine broad targeting, which often performs well once tracking is solid, with custom audiences built from your site visitors and customer lists.
  • Lookalikes. Where you have enough data, lookalike audiences can help find people similar to your best customers. Our guide to Meta lookalike audiences explains how they work.

If you sell into several Gulf countries, it usually makes sense to separate campaigns by country so budgets and results stay clear. Our notes on running Meta ads in multiple countries cover the trade-offs.

Creative that works on Instagram and Facebook

Creative is often the biggest single factor in Meta performance. Ads need to stop the scroll, make the offer clear within seconds and look natural in each placement.

  • Vertical video for Reels and Stories, with captions because many people watch without sound.
  • Square or portrait images for feeds, with one clear message and minimal text.
  • Carousels for showing several products, services or steps.
  • Arabic and English versions written for each audience, not translated word for word.
  • Regular refreshes, because the same ad shown too often tends to lose impact.

We can work with photos and footage you supply, brief your in-house team or photographer, or prepare simple designs ourselves. We always share ads for your approval before launch.

How much does a Meta ads agency cost in the UAE?

There are two parts to the cost: the management fee you pay us and the ad spend you pay Meta directly. We price management in pounds through our retainers and quote other currencies on request.

  • Launch, £350 per month, suits a single channel with a focused campaign.
  • Growth, £750 per month, suits several campaigns or channels with regular creative testing.
  • Scale, £1,500 per month, suits larger budgets, multiple markets and more intensive testing.

Ad spend is separate and paid to Meta from your own account. We will suggest a starting budget that gives campaigns enough data to learn, and paid results typically build over 3 to 6 months as tracking, creative and audiences improve. See our pricing page for what each retainer includes.

Lead forms, WhatsApp and landing pages

How people respond to an ad matters as much as the ad itself. Meta offers several ways to capture interest, and each suits different businesses.

  • Instant lead forms open inside Facebook or Instagram and are quick to complete, which can increase volume. Lead quality varies, so we add qualifying questions and make sure leads reach your team fast.
  • Click-to-message ads open a chat in WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram. They suit businesses that already handle enquiries by message and can reply promptly.
  • Website landing pages give you full control over the message and let you track sales or bookings in detail. They work well for e-commerce and higher-value services.

Whichever route you choose, someone needs to follow up quickly. Many leads go cold if they wait a day for a reply, so we agree a simple response process with you before campaigns go live.

Rules, approvals and sensitive sectors

Meta has its own advertising policies, and the UAE has rules on advertising, consumer protection, data and certain sectors such as healthcare, finance, property and education. These rules change, so we always ask clients to check current official guidance, and approval of regulated claims rests with your own compliance team or adviser. We write accurate, careful ads and avoid claims that cannot be supported.

Questions to ask any Meta ads agency

  • Will the ad account, pixel and pages stay in my name?
  • How will you set up and test tracking before launch?
  • Who creates the ads, and how many versions will you test each month?
  • How will you handle Arabic and English audiences?
  • What will you report on, and how often?
  • What happens to the account and data if we stop working together?

If you are also building or improving a landing page for your campaigns, our website and e-commerce development service can make sure the page matches the ad and loads quickly on mobile.

FAQs

Do you work as a Meta ads agency for UAE businesses?

Yes. We manage Facebook and Instagram ads remotely from the UK for businesses across the UAE. We have no office there, and all campaigns run in your own Meta Business account.

How much does Meta ads management cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month depending on scope, priced in pounds. Ad spend is paid separately to Meta, and other currencies are quoted on request.

Can you run Meta ads in Arabic and English?

Yes. We usually run separate ads or ad sets for each language. We recommend that Arabic copy is written or checked by a fluent speaker so it sounds natural.

Do you create the ad creative?

We can prepare simple designs, work with your photos and video, or brief your team. Every ad is shared for your approval before it goes live.

How quickly will I see results from Meta ads?

Early data arrives within days, but results typically build over 3 to 6 months as tracking, creative and audiences are refined. We report openly on progress each month.

Should I use Meta ads or Google Ads in the UAE?

It depends on your product and customers. Google Ads reaches people already searching, while Meta builds demand and retargets visitors. Many businesses use both, and we can advise on the right split.
