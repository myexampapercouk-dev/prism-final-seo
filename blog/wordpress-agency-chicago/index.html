WordPress Agency Chicago: How Remote WordPress Design and Support Works for Chicago Businesses

Need a WordPress agency in Chicago? A remote studio can build, fix, secure and look after your WordPress site for your Chicago business. See costs and process.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good WordPress agency for a Chicago business builds on a clean theme, keeps plugins lean, sets up security and backups and leaves you able to edit your own content.
  • Many Chicago firms already run WordPress, so audits, speed fixes, redesigns and ongoing care are often as valuable as a brand-new build.
  • We are a UK-based studio working on WordPress remotely, with small sites from £1,000 to £2,000, builds typically in three to five weeks and other currencies quoted on request.

If you are looking for a WordPress agency in Chicago, the right partner is the team that can build or improve a WordPress site that is fast, secure and easy for you to update, wherever that team is based. A remote studio can design new sites, rescue slow or broken ones and provide ongoing care for Chicago law firms, manufacturers, logistics companies, clinics, restaurants and professional services, as long as the process is clear and the work is documented.

We are a UK-based studio that designs, builds and supports WordPress sites remotely for US businesses. We have no Chicago office and no US office. This guide explains what a WordPress agency should do for a Chicago business, the most common WordPress problems we see, how a remote project works and what it costs.

What a WordPress agency in Chicago should do

Chicago has a deep and varied economy: finance, law, manufacturing, logistics, food production, healthcare, education, hospitality and a large base of trades and home services. WordPress suits many of these businesses because it is flexible, widely supported and easy to edit. A good WordPress agency usually offers:

  • New builds: custom designs on a lightweight theme or the block editor, without unnecessary bloat.
  • Redesigns: a fresh design and structure that keeps existing rankings intact through careful redirects.
  • Migrations: moving from another platform to WordPress, or from poor hosting to better hosting.
  • Speed improvements: image optimisation, caching, script clean-up and better hosting where needed.
  • Security: updates, strong logins, limited user roles, firewall rules and reliable backups.
  • SEO foundations: clean URLs, titles, headings, structured data and sensible site structure.
  • Ongoing care: updates, monitoring, small content changes and fixes when something breaks.

Security and hosting are the two areas most often neglected. Our guide to WordPress security covers the essentials, and our guide to WordPress hosting explains what to look for in a host.

Common WordPress problems for Chicago businesses

Many Chicago firms built their WordPress site years ago and have added to it ever since. Over time, that tends to create the same set of problems:

  • Too many plugins: overlapping features, abandoned plugins and conflicts that slow the site or break it after updates.
  • Heavy page builders: layouts that look fine but load slowly and are hard to change.
  • Out-of-date software: old PHP versions, themes and plugins that carry security risks.
  • Unclear ownership: hosting, domain or admin accounts held by a former supplier.
  • Thin or messy content: duplicate pages, old blog posts and service pages that no longer match what you offer.
  • No backups you can trust: backups stored on the same server, or never tested.

A short audit usually reveals which of these apply and in what order to fix them. Our WordPress plugin audit guide shows how to decide what to keep, replace or remove. If you are moving to WordPress from a website builder, our guide to Wix to WordPress migration walks through the steps.

Speed, accessibility and SEO on WordPress

WordPress can be very fast, but only if it is built with care. We keep themes light, limit plugins to what is genuinely needed, compress images and use caching suited to your host. Accessibility is part of the build too: readable text, good contrast, logical headings, keyboard-friendly menus, alt text and properly labelled forms. US businesses often ask about the Americans with Disabilities Act, and how it applies to websites can vary, so check official guidance or speak to your own adviser about your obligations.

Search visibility depends heavily on the site's foundations. Clean permalinks, sensible categories, internal links and well-written titles all matter, and a redesign should never be launched without redirects from old URLs to new ones. If search is a priority, our guide to choosing an SEO agency in Chicago explains how ongoing SEO builds on a well-made site.

We use AI-powered tools to help with tasks such as content outlines, image optimisation and testing checklists, but every design, code change and page of copy is reviewed by a person before it reaches your live site.

How remote WordPress work runs for a Chicago business

Chicago is on Central Time, usually six hours behind the UK. That gives a workable overlap from your morning into our afternoon for calls and approvals, and updates or fixes can often be made outside your busiest hours. New builds and redesigns happen on a private staging site you can review at any time. A typical project looks like this:

  1. Discovery call: goals, pages, features, current problems and who will edit the site.
  2. Audit or plan: a review of any existing site, then scope, timeline and price agreed in writing.
  3. Design: homepage and key page designs, refined with your feedback.
  4. Build: development on staging, with content added and checked across devices.
  5. Launch: go-live, redirects, tracking checks and a final round of testing.
  6. Handover and care: training, documentation and optional ongoing support.

Your domain, hosting and every admin account stay in your name. After launch, regular care keeps the site healthy. Our guide to website maintenance explains what ongoing support should include.

How much a WordPress agency costs for a Chicago business

Costs depend on the number of pages, custom features, integrations and the state of any existing site. Our fees are in pounds, and we can quote US dollars or other currencies on request:

  • Small WordPress website: £1,000 to £2,000, suited to service businesses with a focused set of pages.
  • WooCommerce or ecommerce site: £1,500 to £3,500, depending on products, payments and integrations.
  • Timeline: websites typically take three to five weeks, and ecommerce builds five to twelve weeks.
  • Ongoing care, SEO and marketing: monthly retainers from £350 a month.

Our pricing page sets out what is included, and our website and ecommerce development service explains how we design and build. FAQs

How do I choose a WordPress agency in Chicago?

Look for a team that audits before quoting, keeps plugins lean, builds on staging, puts every account in your name and explains ongoing care clearly. Ask how they handle updates, backups and redirects.

Do you have an office in Chicago?

No. We are a UK-based studio and work remotely with US businesses. We have no Chicago office or any other US office, and we work through video calls, staging sites and written feedback.

Can you fix our existing WordPress site rather than rebuild it?

Often, yes. We start with an audit of speed, plugins, security and content. Sometimes targeted fixes are enough, and sometimes a rebuild is better value. We explain the trade-offs before you decide.

How long does a WordPress website take?

Most WordPress sites take three to five weeks, and ecommerce builds take five to twelve weeks. Late content or complex integrations can extend the timeline.

How much does a WordPress agency in Chicago cost?

Our small websites are £1,000 to £2,000, and ecommerce sites are £1,500 to £3,500, with care retainers from £350 a month. Prices are in pounds, and other currencies are quoted on request.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. We build so that your team can edit pages, posts and images in the WordPress editor, and we provide training and simple written guides at handover.
