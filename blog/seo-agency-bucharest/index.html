Why SEO matters for Bucharest businesses

Bucharest is Romania's capital and its largest economy, with strong technology, outsourcing, finance, retail, real estate, healthcare and professional services sectors. Many companies serve local customers while also selling software, services or products to clients across the EU, the UK and the USA. Organic search supports both goals because it:

  • Brings steady traffic that does not stop when ad budgets pause.
  • Builds credibility with buyers who research before contacting a supplier.
  • Supports paid campaigns by improving landing pages and brand searches.
  • Feeds the sources AI search tools use when they summarise a market.

Technical foundations first

Many Bucharest websites have grown over years, with several languages, plugins and redesigns layered on top of each other. Before writing new content, we check that search engines can crawl and understand what is already there.

  • Indexing problems, duplicate pages and thin tag or archive pages.
  • Incorrect or missing hreflang tags between Romanian and English versions.
  • Slow pages on mobile, heavy images and unnecessary scripts.
  • Broken internal links and redirect chains from past migrations.
  • Missing or invalid structured data for organisations, products or articles.

Our technical SEO audit checklist lists the checks we run.

Romanian and English content strategy

Most consumer searches in Bucharest happen in Romanian. Many B2B, technology and expat-focused searches happen in English. A good strategy usually covers both, with separate pages for each language rather than mixed content.

  • Romanian pages for local services, products and consumer topics.
  • English pages for international buyers, investors, expats and B2B clients.
  • Clear URL structure with a folder for each language and hreflang linking them.
  • Translation workflow. Prism writes in English. Romanian content comes from your team or a translator, and we brief it with keywords and structure.

Our guide to multilingual website design explains the structural choices, and our keyword cannibalisation guide shows how to stop language versions or similar pages competing with each other.

Local SEO in Bucharest

For clinics, law and accounting firms, restaurants, property agencies, gyms and trades, appearing in the local map results for Bucharest searches is often more valuable than ranking nationally. Local SEO includes:

  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with the right categories and services.
  • Consistent name, address and phone details across directories.
  • Pages for each location or service area if you have more than one branch or serve different sectors of the city.
  • A steady flow of genuine customer reviews and timely responses.

See our guides to Google Business Profile categories and local versus national SEO for more detail.

Bucharest has a large software and IT services sector, and many companies sell mostly to clients abroad. Their SEO looks different from local businesses:

  • English-first content aimed at buyers in the UK, EU and USA.
  • Feature, integration, comparison and use-case pages.
  • Thought-leadership content that earns links and mentions.
  • Strong technical SEO on documentation and product pages.

Ecommerce SEO for Romanian online stores

Online stores in Bucharest compete with large marketplaces for product searches. Ecommerce SEO focuses on category pages, product page quality, filters that do not create endless duplicate URLs, and structured data that helps products appear with prices and availability. Our first 90 days of SEO

Every SEO engagement starts with a clear plan, so you know what will happen and when. A typical first three months for a Bucharest client looks like this.

  1. Weeks one to two. Access to Search Console, Analytics and the website. Technical crawl, keyword research and competitor review.
  2. Weeks three to four. A prioritised action list covering technical fixes, page updates and content opportunities, agreed with you in writing.
  3. Month two. Technical fixes implemented or handed to your developers, key service and category pages improved, Google Business Profile updated.
  4. Month three. New content published, internal links strengthened, first authority-building outreach, and a review of early ranking movement.

From then on we work in monthly cycles. Each report explains what we changed, how visibility and traffic moved, and the priorities for the next month.

Choosing an SEO agency in Bucharest

Whether you work with us or another provider, a few questions help you judge any SEO agency:

  • Do they explain what they will do each month in plain language?
  • Do you keep ownership of your website, Search Console and Analytics accounts?
  • Do they avoid promises of guaranteed rankings or instant results?
  • Do they cover technical, content and authority work, not just one of the three?
  • Can they handle the languages and markets you need, or explain how translation fits in?
  • Do they avoid low-quality link schemes that could harm your site?

Good SEO is steady, transparent work. Any agency that cannot explain its approach clearly, or that relies on shortcuts, is a risk to a site you have spent years building. We prefer to agree realistic goals at the start, such as more enquiries from key service pages or better visibility for priority product categories, and measure progress against them every month.
