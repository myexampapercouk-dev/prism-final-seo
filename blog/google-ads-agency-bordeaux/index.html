Why Bordeaux businesses use Google Ads

Bordeaux is one of the most visited cities in France and the capital of a major wine region. Its economy spans wine and spirits, tourism and hospitality, aerospace and defence, logistics, digital services, health and a large student population. Many businesses sell well beyond the city, to customers across Nouvelle-Aquitaine, the rest of France and abroad.

Google Ads suits this mix because it puts you in front of buyers at the moment they search. A wine estate can reach visitors planning tastings, a hotel can capture last-minute bookings, a B2B supplier can target procurement searches across France and an online shop can show products to shoppers comparing prices. Unlike SEO, results can start within days, which makes it useful for launches, seasonal peaks and new markets.

Campaign types that suit Bordeaux businesses

Different businesses need different campaign types. These are the most common choices for companies in and around Bordeaux.

  • Search campaigns: text ads for people searching specific services or products, ideal for professional services, trades, clinics and B2B suppliers.
  • Performance Max: a campaign type that runs across Google's networks, useful for ecommerce and lead generation once tracking is reliable.
  • Shopping campaigns: product ads with prices and images, essential for online shops selling wine accessories, food, fashion or homeware.
  • Remarketing: ads shown to past visitors, helpful for hotels, courses and higher-value purchases with longer decision times.
  • Local campaigns and location assets: drive calls and visits for businesses with a physical premises in the city.

Some businesses use video and social ads alongside search. Our guide to working with a Meta Ads agency in France covers how social campaigns can complement Google Ads.

Sectors in Bordeaux that use Google Ads well

The city's economy creates several strong use cases for paid search.

  • Wine tourism and estates: tastings, tours and direct wine sales, often to international visitors searching in English. Alcohol advertising in France is tightly regulated, so check current rules and Google's own policies with your adviser before launching, and compliance approval rests with you.
  • Hotels and hospitality: direct bookings for hotels, guesthouses and restaurants competing with large booking platforms.
  • Professional and B2B services: consultancies, engineering suppliers and logistics firms targeting buyers across France.
  • Ecommerce: online shops selling nationally and across Europe, often built on Shopify or WordPress.
  • Education and training: schools, language courses and training providers reaching the city's large student population.

Language and targeting

Most Bordeaux campaigns need French ads, and many also benefit from English campaigns aimed at tourists, expats and international buyers. Prism writes in English. French ad copy and landing page text should come from your team or a professional translator, and we build the campaign structure, keyword lists and testing plan around it.

Location targeting needs care too. A restaurant may want people within a short distance of the city centre, a hotel may target travellers in the UK, Belgium and Germany, and a B2B supplier may target the whole of France. Separate campaigns by language and location usually make results clearer and budgets easier to control. Our guide to international PPC explains how to run campaigns across several countries.

Timelines and what to expect

Google Ads can generate traffic within days of launch, but the first weeks are a learning period. The account gathers data, we remove irrelevant search terms, adjust bids and test new ads. Most accounts reach steady, efficient performance within 3 to 6 months.

  1. Weeks one and two: audit, tracking setup, keyword research and campaign build.
  2. Weeks three to six: launch, daily checks, negative keywords and early ad tests.
  3. Months two and three: budget shifts towards the best campaigns, landing page improvements and wider tests.
  4. Ongoing: monthly reporting, new opportunities and steady efficiency gains.

If you already run campaigns, an audit is a good place to start. Our PPC audit checklist shows the checks we run.

Google Ads or SEO first?

Many Bordeaux businesses ask whether to start with paid search or SEO. Google Ads gives fast visibility and clear data, but you pay for every click. SEO takes longer but builds traffic that does not stop when the budget does. Most businesses benefit from both, starting with ads for immediate enquiries while SEO grows. Working with a remote Google Ads team

Google Ads is well suited to remote management, because everything happens inside the ad account and your website. You keep ownership of the account and billing, and we work through manager access. We agree goals and budgets upfront, share a simple dashboard and hold regular video calls. France is one hour ahead of the UK for most of the year, so meetings fit easily into the French working day.

Privacy and consent rules in the EU affect tracking, so your cookie banner and consent setup should follow current guidance from the French data protection authority and your own adviser. We configure tracking to work within the consent choices your visitors make.

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Bordeaux?

Can you write ads in French?

Prism writes in English. French ad copy should come from your team or a professional translator. We plan the campaigns, keywords, structure and tests, and we can run English campaigns for international audiences directly.
