This guide explains how we approach Shopify projects for Winnipeg businesses, what they cost and how to judge whether an agency is the right fit.

Setting up Shopify for Canada

Getting the basics right for Canada avoids headaches later. When we build a store for a Winnipeg business, we work through:

  • Taxes. Canadian sales tax depends on where your customers are, combining federal and provincial taxes in different ways across provinces. In Manitoba, the federal GST applies alongside the provincial retail sales tax. Shopify can calculate taxes, but your accountant should confirm your registrations and settings.
  • Shipping. We set up shipping zones and rates for Manitoba, the rest of Canada and any international markets, with options such as Canada Post and local delivery.
  • Local pickup. If you have a shop or warehouse in Winnipeg, in-store or curbside pickup can be a strong selling point.
  • Payments. We configure Shopify Payments or another provider, plus wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay where available.
  • Currency and markets. If you sell to the United States or elsewhere, we set up markets, currencies and pricing rules carefully.

Our guide to Shopify store setup walks through the general process, much of which applies to Canadian stores too.

Design that sells, without the bloat

A good Shopify store is not just attractive. It helps shoppers find the right product quickly and buy with confidence. Our design priorities are:

  • Clear navigation and collection structure that match how customers think about your range.
  • Product pages with strong photos, clear variant options, honest descriptions, sizing and shipping information.
  • Trust signals such as clear returns, contact details and secure checkout messaging.
  • A fast theme with as few apps as possible.

Every app adds code, and too many apps are one of the most common causes of slow Shopify stores. Our guide to Shopify speed optimisation explains how we keep stores quick.

Migrating to Shopify

Many Winnipeg retailers come to Shopify from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or an older custom system. A careful migration protects your sales and search rankings. We map products, variants, customers and order history, recreate important pages, and set up redirects from every old URL to its new home. Our guide to WooCommerce to Shopify migration covers the process in detail. For larger brands, we also handle Shopify Plus migrations.

Getting found: ecommerce SEO and ads

A new store needs traffic. Search engines remain one of the most valuable long-term sources, especially for collection pages that match how people search for products. We optimise collection and product titles, descriptions, structured data, internal links and site speed, and we plan content that answers buyer questions. Our guide to ecommerce SEO in Canada explains our approach for Canadian stores, and our guide to choosing an SEO agency in Winnipeg covers local search.

Paid campaigns can bring faster sales. Google Shopping and Performance Max use your product feed, while Meta Ads work well for visual products and retargeting. We explain how we run search campaigns in our guide to choosing a Google Ads agency in Winnipeg. If you also sell on Amazon or other marketplaces, we can help optimise those listings too.

Running the store after launch

Launch day is the start, not the finish. A store that keeps growing needs regular attention, and we make sure your team can handle everyday tasks confidently. After launch we typically help with:

  • Training on adding products, editing collections, processing orders and issuing refunds.
  • Setting up staff accounts with sensible permissions.
  • Seasonal updates for busy periods such as the holidays and back-to-school.
  • Reviewing reports each month to spot best sellers, slow lines and abandoned checkouts.
  • Automating repetitive jobs, such as tagging orders or alerting staff about low stock, with Shopify Flow where your plan includes it.

Ongoing support can sit within a monthly retainer or be booked as separate projects, depending on how much help you need.

Privacy, consent and French content

Online stores collect personal information at checkout and through email sign-ups. PIPEDA, Canada's federal private sector privacy law, governs how that information is handled, and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, known as CASL, requires consent for commercial emails and texts. If you sell to customers in Quebec, the province's Law 25 and French language rules may also be relevant. We keep our notes general and are not legal advisers, so please check official guidance or speak to your own adviser. Responsibility for compliance rests with you.

We write in English. If you want your store in French as well, product and page translations should come from your team or a professional translator, and we set up Shopify's language features to display them properly.

How to choose a Shopify agency

Whether you work with us or someone else, ask these questions before you sign:

  • Will you own the store, theme, domain and all content?
  • How will the agency handle taxes, shipping and payments for Canada?
  • Which apps will they install, and what will they cost each month?
  • How will they protect your search rankings during a migration?
  • What training and support do you get after launch?

FAQs

What does a Shopify agency in Winnipeg do?

A Shopify agency builds, migrates and improves online stores. That includes design, product set-up, Canadian taxes and shipping, payments, speed, SEO and the apps that run your store day to day.

Do you have an office in Winnipeg?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely, with calls scheduled around Central Time. We have no office in Canada.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

Our e-commerce builds cost £1,500 to £3,500, with Canadian dollar pricing quoted on request. Shopify fees, paid apps and ad spend are separate.

Can Shopify handle Manitoba sales tax?

Shopify can calculate Canadian sales taxes, including federal and provincial taxes. Your accountant should confirm your registrations and the correct settings for your business.

Will I lose my search rankings if I move to Shopify?

A careful migration with full redirects, matched page content and checked structured data protects most rankings. Some short-term movement is normal while search engines process the changes.

We set up Shopify's language features, while the French text itself should come from your team or a professional translator.
