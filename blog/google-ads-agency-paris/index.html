Google Ads Agency for Paris Businesses: Remote PPC Management from a UK Studio

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Paris? How French and English campaigns are planned, tracked and managed remotely, plus consent rules and our fees in pounds.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • A Google Ads agency for a Paris business should build campaigns around real French and English search terms, track conversions with consent in place and manage budgets for profit, not clicks.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in France; we manage campaigns remotely, write ads in English and work with your team or a translator on French ad copy.

A Google Ads agency for a Paris business should put your ads in front of people searching for what you sell, at a cost that leaves you a profit. That means researching the French and English terms buyers really use, structuring campaigns tightly, writing ads that match each search, tracking conversions properly and adjusting bids and budgets every week.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for businesses across Europe. We have no office in Paris or anywhere in France. Our paid media service runs by video call, shared documents and monthly reports, with Paris one hour ahead of UK time. We write ads in English and work with your team or a translator on French versions.

What Paris businesses need from Google Ads

Paris is the French capital and one of Europe's largest business centres. It is home to corporate headquarters, luxury and fashion brands, technology start-ups, professional services, hospitality and tourism, education providers and a large number of online shops. Search competition is intense, and costs per click in many Paris sectors are high, so wasted spend adds up quickly.

Campaigns for Paris businesses often need to reach several audiences:

  • French-speaking buyers in Paris and the Île-de-France region. Local customers who search in French and expect French landing pages.
  • Buyers across France. National customers for online shops and service firms that work remotely.
  • International clients. Companies elsewhere in Europe and beyond who search in English for Paris-based suppliers.
  • Visitors. Tourists and business travellers searching in English for hotels, restaurants, tours and services.

French and English campaigns

Translating English keywords into French rarely works. French buyers often use different terms, and the same idea may be searched with or without accents, in singular or plural forms, or with an English loanword. We research each language separately and give each language its own campaigns, so French searches see French ads that lead to French landing pages.

Prism writes English ads in-house. For French, we supply the structure, keyword themes and English drafts, then a native speaker on your team or a translator writes the final ad copy, which we check against character limits and the campaign plan. If your website does not yet have strong French landing pages, we will tell you, because sending French ads to English pages wastes budget.

Location targeting around Paris

Google Ads lets you target by city, region, postcode area or radius. For a Paris business serving local customers, targeting usually covers the city and the surrounding Île-de-France region, sometimes with different bids for central and outer areas. National online shops target the whole of France, and businesses selling internationally may add other countries with their own campaigns.

The default settings can show ads to people merely interested in a location rather than physically there, which can waste money. Our guide to Google Ads location targeting explains the settings we use.

Campaign types we use

  1. Search campaigns. Text ads on searches with clear buying intent. The core of most accounts.
  2. Shopping and Performance Max. For online shops with a product feed in Google Merchant Center. Our Performance Max guide explains when it works and when to hold back.
  3. Remarketing. Ads shown to people who visited your site, where consent allows.
  4. YouTube and Demand Gen. For brands that need awareness alongside search demand.

Tracking and consent

Google Ads only optimises well when it knows which clicks turn into enquiries or sales. We set up conversion tracking for forms, calls, bookings and purchases. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking setup covers the process.

French websites fall under the GDPR for personal data, and the EU ePrivacy rules mean analytics and advertising cookies normally need consent before they load. We connect tracking to your consent banner and set up Google's consent mode so the account still receives modelled data when visitors decline. Legal wording and decisions on compliance should come from your legal adviser, and official guidance should be checked before relying on any summary.

Landing pages matter too. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer-facing products and services, including a wide range of online shops. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt, but building to WCAG AA, the level referenced by the harmonised standard EN 301 549, is good practice for any page you send paid traffic to.

Cutting wasted spend

High click costs in Paris make waste expensive. The main sources we look for are broad keywords matching irrelevant searches, ads showing to people outside your service area, poor landing pages and budgets spread too thinly across too many campaigns. Each week we review the search terms report and add negative keywords. Our negative keywords guide explains how this works.

We also look at when and where your ads run. Ad schedules can reduce spend at hours when enquiries rarely come in, and device and audience adjustments can shift budget towards the people most likely to buy. Smaller changes made steadily each week usually work better than large, sudden rebuilds, because the account keeps the data it has already learned from.

FAQs

Do you have a Google Ads agency office in Paris?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in France. We work remotely by video call, shared documents and monthly reports, one hour behind Paris time.

Can you run Google Ads in French and English?

Yes. We build separate campaigns for each language. English ads are written in-house, and French ads come from your team or a native-speaking translator, which we check against the plan.

How much does a Google Ads agency in Paris cost with your studio?

Our management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, quoted in pounds with other currencies on request. Ad spend and translation are paid separately.

Does cookie consent affect Google Ads tracking?

Yes. Advertising cookies normally need consent before they load, so some conversions are not observed directly. We set up consent mode so the account still receives modelled data, and your legal adviser approves the wording.

Can you take over an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We start with an audit, keep what works, fix tracking and structure, and cut wasted spend before expanding.

How long does Google Ads take to work?

Results typically build over three to six months. Early weeks focus on data, tracking and cutting waste, and performance improves as the account learns.
