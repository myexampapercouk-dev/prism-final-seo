What SEO looks like for Reading businesses

Reading has one of the strongest business economies outside London. It is home to many technology, software and telecoms firms, regional headquarters, professional services, a large university, a busy retail centre at The Oracle and Broad Street, and thousands of service businesses covering towns such as Wokingham, Bracknell, Newbury, Henley-on-Thames and Maidenhead. Those businesses need very different SEO strategies.

  • Technology and SaaS firms usually sell nationally or internationally, so they need content that ranks for problem-led and comparison searches rather than local terms.
  • Professional services such as solicitors, accountants and consultants need strong service pages and local visibility across Berkshire.
  • Trades and home services rely on Google Business Profile, reviews and location pages to win map pack positions.
  • Retail and hospitality need accurate listings, opening hours and pages that answer visitor questions.

Local SEO across Reading and Berkshire

For businesses that serve local customers, the Google map pack is often where the most valuable clicks happen. Local SEO focuses on the signals Google uses to decide which businesses appear there.

  • A complete and accurate Google Business Profile with the right categories, services and photos.
  • Consistent business name, address and phone details across directories.
  • A steady flow of genuine customer reviews, with replies.
  • Service and location pages for the areas you actually cover, written for people rather than stuffed with town names.
  • Local links from organisations, events and suppliers you already work with.

Our local SEO guide covers each of these in detail, and our article on choosing a local SEO agency explains what to expect from a provider.

Technical SEO for Reading websites

Many Reading firms, especially in technology, have websites built on modern JavaScript frameworks or complex content systems. These can look great but create problems for search engines if pages are slow, rendered only in the browser or hidden behind filters. Common technical issues we fix include:

IssueWhy it mattersTypical fix
Slow page loadsHurts user experience and can affect rankingsImage optimisation, caching, lighter scripts
Pages not indexedPages that are not indexed cannot rankFix noindex tags, sitemaps and internal links
Duplicate contentSplits ranking signals across similar pagesCanonical tags, merging or rewriting pages
Client-side renderingContent may not be seen reliably by crawlersServer-side rendering or pre-rendered pages
Broken redirects after a redesignLoses rankings and links built over yearsRedirect mapping from old URLs to new ones

Content and AI search visibility

Search is changing. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and other AI tools now answer many questions directly, often citing a small number of sources. To be one of those sources, content needs to answer questions clearly, show real expertise and be well structured. We build content that serves both traditional rankings and AI-powered search, with clear headings, direct answers and useful tables.

For Reading technology and B2B firms, this often means comparison pages, use-case guides and plain-English explainers of complex products. For local firms, it means detailed service pages and honest answers to common customer questions about price, timing and process.

How to choose an SEO agency

Whether you work with us or another provider, ask a few direct questions. What will be done each month? Who writes the content? How are results reported? Are there long lock-in contracts? Will you own all the content and accounts? A good agency will answer clearly, avoid guaranteed ranking promises and explain the trade-offs honestly. Common SEO mistakes we see in Reading

When we review websites for Reading and Berkshire businesses, a few problems appear often. Service pages are thin or missing, so one page tries to rank for everything. Location pages repeat the same text with only the town name changed. Redesigns launch without redirects, losing years of rankings overnight. Blogs are full of news posts that nobody searches for, while the questions customers really ask go unanswered. Google Business Profiles are left half-complete, with old photos and no replies to reviews.

None of these need a large budget to fix. They need a clear plan, steady work and someone checking that each change actually helps. That is the focus of our retainers, whatever their size. If you are unsure where to start, a short call is usually enough for us to point out the two or three changes most likely to help your site first.
