Web Design Agency for Cluj-Napoca Businesses: A Practical Guide

Picking a web design agency for a Cluj-Napoca business: site types, multilingual set-up, speed, SEO foundations, timelines and £ prices from a remote UK studio.

Key takeaways

  • A good website for a Cluj-Napoca business is fast, clear about what you sell, built for the languages your buyers use and ready for SEO from day one.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Cluj-Napoca or Romania; small websites start at £1,000 to £2,000 and take around 3 to 5 weeks.

A web design agency for a Cluj-Napoca business should give you a fast, clear website that explains what you do, works in the languages your customers use and is built so it can rank in search from the first day. For most small and mid-sized companies that means a focused site of a handful of well-written pages, a clean content management system and a simple path to contact or buy.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Cluj-Napoca or anywhere in Romania. We work with companies that are comfortable with video calls, shared documents and clear written briefs. We write in English, so Romanian, Hungarian or other language content comes from your own team or a professional translator, and we plan the page structure around it.

Why Cluj-Napoca businesses need a stronger website

Cluj-Napoca is one of the largest cities in Romania and a well-known centre for software, outsourcing, universities and start-ups. Alongside the technology sector sit manufacturers, professional services firms, clinics, hospitality businesses and a growing number of online shops. Many of these companies sell to buyers outside Romania, which raises the bar for their websites.

Common issues we see when reviewing sites for companies in similar markets include:

  • Template sites that look like every competitor and do not explain the offer quickly.
  • Slow pages weighed down by large images, sliders and unused plugins.
  • Language versions bolted on later, with mixed URLs and missing translations.
  • No clear next step, so visitors leave without getting in touch.
  • Weak search foundations, such as duplicate titles and pages with no real content.

A well-planned rebuild fixes these at the root rather than patching them one by one.

Choosing the right platform

The platform should fit how your team works, not the other way round. For most Cluj-Napoca businesses the choice comes down to three options.

  • WordPress suits service businesses, B2B firms and content-heavy sites. It is flexible, widely supported and easy for staff to edit once set up properly.
  • Shopify suits online shops that want reliable checkout, payments and stock management without running their own servers.
  • A lightweight static site suits companies that want maximum speed and rarely change their pages.

If you are weighing up ecommerce options, our comparison of Shopify and WooCommerce explains the trade-offs in plain terms. For WordPress budgets, see our guide to WordPress website costs.

Designing for more than one language

Many Cluj-Napoca companies need at least two languages, often Romanian and English, and some also serve Hungarian-speaking or German-speaking customers. Getting this right early saves a lot of trouble later.

The key decisions are:

  • URL structure, usually a folder per language such as /en/ and /ro/.
  • Hreflang tags so search engines show the right version to the right visitor.
  • Translated, not just copied, page titles and descriptions for every language.
  • A language switcher that keeps visitors on the equivalent page.
  • A workflow for adding new pages in every language at the same time.

We write and edit in English. Your team or a translator provides other languages, and we make sure the structure, templates and technical tags support them. Our guide to multilingual website design covers this in more depth, and our international SEO guide explains how to rank in several countries.

Speed, accessibility and search foundations

A beautiful site that loads slowly loses visitors before they read a word. We build with speed in mind from the start: compressed images, minimal scripts, sensible fonts and clean templates. Our guide to Core Web Vitals explains the measures Google uses.

Search foundations are built in rather than added later:

  • One clear topic and heading structure per page.
  • Unique titles and meta descriptions.
  • Clean URLs and a logical internal linking structure.
  • Structured data where it genuinely applies.
  • An XML sitemap and search console set up at launch.

Accessibility matters too. Good colour contrast, readable text sizes, descriptive links and keyboard-friendly navigation help every visitor, not only those using assistive technology.

Local visibility in Cluj-Napoca

If customers visit you in person, such as a clinic, restaurant, shop or office, your website should work hand in hand with your Google Business Profile. Consistent name, address and phone details, a clear location page and genuine reviews all help you appear in local results. Our Google Business Profile guide walks through the set-up.

For companies that sell mainly to clients abroad, local visibility matters less than strong service pages, case-style explanations of how you work and content that answers the questions overseas buyers ask.

  • Software and SaaS companies that need clear product pages, pricing pages and demo requests. See our SaaS marketing page.
  • Outsourcing and IT services firms selling to clients in the UK, Europe and North America.

Questions to ask before you hire

Whether you choose a local Cluj-Napoca agency or a remote studio, a few direct questions will tell you a lot about how the project will run.

  • Who writes the content? Agree early whether the agency writes copy, edits yours or only builds pages.
  • Who owns the site? You should own the domain, hosting account and content, with admin access from day one.
  • How are changes handled after launch? Ask what small edits cost and how quickly they are made.
  • How will you measure success? Enquiries, sales or demo requests matter more than page views.
  • What happens to existing rankings? A rebuild needs redirects from old URLs so you keep the search visibility you already have.

Clear answers to these questions protect your budget and make the project easier for everyone involved.

Working with a remote UK studio

Working remotely is straightforward when the process is clear. We agree a written brief, share designs and a staging site for review, and keep feedback in one place. Cluj-Napoca is normally two hours ahead of the UK, which leaves a comfortable overlap for calls during the working day.

Data protection is part of the build. Romania follows the EU GDPR, so forms, cookies and analytics should be set up with consent and privacy in mind. We set up sensible defaults, but the final responsibility for your privacy notice and legal wording rests with you and your own adviser, and the national data protection authority publishes official guidance.
