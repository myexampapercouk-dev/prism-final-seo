Why Gothenburg businesses use a Google Ads agency

Gothenburg is Sweden's second city and home to its largest port. The region has deep roots in automotive engineering, logistics, life sciences and manufacturing, alongside a lively retail, hospitality and events scene. Many of the companies based here sell to other businesses across Scandinavia and Europe, while others serve consumers in West Sweden and online.

That variety means Google Ads can do very different jobs. For a B2B supplier, it might capture a small number of high-value searches from engineers or buyers. For an online shop, it might drive daily sales through Shopping campaigns. For a local service, it might fill the diary with calls from people nearby. The common thread is discipline: clear goals, accurate tracking and steady improvement.

Tracking first, then spend

The most common problem we find in new accounts is weak conversion tracking. If Google does not know which clicks became sales or enquiries, its automated bidding has nothing reliable to learn from, and budget drifts towards cheap clicks that never convert.

Before we scale any campaign, we check and fix tracking for the actions that matter: purchases with correct values, form submissions, calls and key steps such as quote requests. We also set up consent mode properly, because Sweden follows European privacy rules and visitors must have a real choice about cookies. For how the rules apply to your business, check official guidance or your own adviser.

Campaign structure for Swedish searches

Swedish searchers often combine words into single compound terms, and they mix Swedish and English terms, especially in technical and B2B fields. We research both, look at real search terms in your account and build campaigns around the phrases that actually lead to conversions.

We usually separate campaigns by language and by intent, so budgets and messages stay clear. Brand searches, high-intent product or service searches and broader research searches each get their own treatment. A long list of negative keywords stops spend leaking to job seekers, students and unrelated searches.

For businesses selling across Scandinavia, we may set up separate campaigns for Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland, each with its own language, currency and budget. Our guides to Google Ads in Stockholm and Google Ads in Copenhagen cover how we handle nearby markets.

Google Ads for Gothenburg online shops

For e-commerce brands, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns usually do the heavy lifting. Their success depends on the product feed. We improve product titles, descriptions, categories and attributes so Google can match your products to the right searches, and we split products by margin and performance so the best sellers get the budget they deserve.

We also look at the store itself. Slow product pages, unclear delivery costs and complicated checkouts waste paid traffic. If your store runs on Shopify, our guide to Shopify Markets for Europe explains how to handle currencies and languages for Nordic buyers, and our e-commerce marketing page shows how we combine ads with store improvements.

Google Ads for B2B and industrial firms

Many Gothenburg companies sell specialist products to other businesses. Search volumes are lower, clicks can be expensive and sales cycles are long, so the setup needs care. We focus on precise keywords, strong negative lists, landing pages that answer technical questions and tracking that captures every enquiry, even if the sale closes months later.

Where it fits, we pair Google Ads with LinkedIn Ads to reach buyers by job title and industry. Both channels can run from the same retainer, with ad spend paid separately to each platform.

Landing pages that convert

Sending ads to your homepage rarely works well. Each campaign should lead to a page that matches the search, explains the offer clearly and makes the next step obvious. We design and build landing pages as part of our web design work, and we can adjust existing pages to improve conversion rates. Small changes, such as a clearer headline, a shorter form or visible delivery information, often make a big difference to cost per lead.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix in Gothenburg accounts

When we audit an existing account, the same problems appear again and again. Fixing them often improves results before we add a single new campaign:

  • Broad keywords with no negatives. Budget leaks to searches for jobs, free downloads, competitors' support pages and unrelated products.
  • Every conversion counted equally. A newsletter sign-up and a large quote request should not carry the same value, or bidding will chase the cheap action.
  • One campaign for everything. Mixing brand, product and research searches in one campaign hides what is working and what is not.
  • Auto-applied recommendations left switched on. Some suggestions raise spend without improving results, so we review each one before accepting it.
  • Ads that ignore the landing page. If the ad promises one thing and the page says another, quality scores and conversion rates both fall.

Smaller businesses face the same issues on tighter budgets. Our guide to Google Ads for small businesses explains how to keep a modest account focused.
