SEO Agency Seattle: How Remote SEO Works for Seattle Companies

Looking for an SEO agency in Seattle? Remote SEO can cover tech, B2B, local and ecommerce search for Seattle firms. Here is what it involves and what it costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • An SEO agency for a Seattle business needs US search data, a clear plan and reporting tied to leads and sales, not a local office.
  • Seattle's mix of tech, B2B, ecommerce and neighbourhood service businesses means the SEO plan should match how your own buyers search.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US clients, with retainers priced in pounds and other currencies quoted on request.

If you are searching for an SEO agency in Seattle, the question that matters is whether the agency can win the searches that bring you customers, not whether it has a desk in South Lake Union. Remote SEO can serve Seattle tech companies, B2B firms, online shops and local service businesses well, provided the work is built on US search data and judged against enquiries, demos and sales.

We are a UK-based studio that works remotely with US businesses. We have no Seattle office and no US office. This guide covers what SEO for a Seattle company should include, how the plan changes for tech, B2B, ecommerce and local businesses, how a remote arrangement runs across time zones and what it costs.

What an SEO agency in Seattle should cover

Seattle's economy leans heavily on software, cloud services, ecommerce and research, but it also includes healthcare, maritime and logistics, construction, hospitality, retail and many trade and home service businesses across King County. Whatever your sector, a sound SEO programme usually includes:

  • Technical health: crawling, indexing, site speed, rendering and structured data, so search engines can read every page that matters.
  • Keyword and intent research: the US searches your buyers actually type, from comparison and pricing queries to urgent local ones.
  • On-page work: titles, headings, service and product pages and internal links built around those searches.
  • Content: guides, comparisons and answers that help buyers decide and that AI search tools can quote.
  • Authority: genuine links and mentions earned from relevant publications and organisations.
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile, reviews and location pages where you serve customers in person.
  • Reporting: Search Console and analytics linked to leads, trials and revenue rather than rankings alone.

We usually start with a technical review. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows the checks we run first, and our keyword research guide explains how we turn search data into a page plan.

SEO for Seattle tech and SaaS companies

Many Seattle businesses sell software or technical services, often to buyers across the US and beyond. Their SEO is rarely about the word Seattle at all. It is about ranking for the problems their product solves, the categories buyers compare and the integrations they rely on.

For SaaS and tech firms, the work usually focuses on:

  • Problem and use-case pages: one strong page per job your product does, written for the people who search for that job.
  • Comparison and alternative pages: fair, accurate pages for buyers weighing options, which tend to convert well.
  • Integration pages: pages for the tools your product connects with, where there is real search demand.
  • Documentation and help content: making sure useful support content is indexable and linked properly.
  • Technical foundations: JavaScript rendering, faceted pages and app subdomains often need careful handling.

Our SaaS SEO guide goes further into this, and our SaaS sector page explains how we work with software companies.

Local SEO across Seattle and the wider area

For businesses that serve customers in person, such as clinics, contractors, restaurants and home services, local search is where most enquiries start. Someone in Capitol Hill, Ballard, Fremont, Bellevue or Redmond can see different local results for the same search, because local rankings depend on where the searcher is, your Google Business Profile, your reviews and how relevant your pages are. Your agency's location makes no difference to any of that.

  • Google Business Profile: correct categories, services, hours and photos for each real location.
  • Service-area pages: useful, distinct pages for the areas you genuinely serve, not copies with the place name swapped.
  • Reviews: a steady, rule-compliant way of asking happy customers for feedback, and replying to it.
  • Citations: the same business name, address and phone number everywhere you are listed.

Our guides to Google Business Profile and local SEO versus national SEO explain the principles, which work the same way for US businesses.

SEO for Seattle ecommerce brands

Seattle has a strong online retail culture, from outdoor and coffee brands to homeware and specialist goods. Ecommerce SEO depends on clean category structures, product pages that answer buyer questions, sensible handling of filters and variants, and fast pages on mobile. If you sell on Shopify, theme and app choices can affect speed and duplicate pages, so the technical side needs regular checks.

Online shops also benefit from linking SEO with paid search and shopping campaigns, because the same product data feeds both. Our ecommerce SEO guide for US DTC brands covers this in more detail.

How remote SEO works for a Seattle business

Seattle is usually eight hours behind the UK. In practice this means a shared window in your morning, which is our afternoon, for calls and quick decisions, with most of the work done asynchronously. A typical remote arrangement looks like this:

  • Onboarding: access to Search Console, analytics and your CMS, plus a call to agree goals, priority services or products and how leads are measured.
  • Audit and plan: a technical and content review followed by a prioritised plan for the first three months.
  • Monthly cycle: agreed tasks, written updates and a short monthly report with clear next steps.
  • Calls: scheduled in your morning, recorded or summarised in writing so nothing is lost.

Good remote SEO relies on clear written communication and shared access to data. If you are comparing remote options, our guide to outsourcing SEO to an overseas agency sets out the questions worth asking.

How much an SEO agency costs for a Seattle business

SEO pricing varies widely, but most ongoing work is billed as a monthly retainer. Our retainers are priced in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request:

  • Launch, £350 a month: a focused plan for a smaller site or a single location.
  • Growth, £750 a month: broader content, technical and local work for growing businesses.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: larger sites, multiple locations or competitive national searches.

SEO results typically take three to six months to show clearly, depending on your starting point and competition. Our pricing page sets out what each retainer includes, and our SEO and GEO service explains how the work is structured, including optimisation for AI search.

Questions to ask any SEO agency

  • What will you do in the first 90 days, and why in that order?
  • How will you measure success beyond rankings?
  • Who owns the content, accounts and data if we part ways?
  • How do you handle technical changes that need our developers?
  • What will the monthly report show, and when will we get it?
  • How do you approach AI search and AI Overviews?

Clear answers to these questions tell you more than any sales deck. Be cautious of guaranteed rankings or vague promises without a plan behind them.

FAQs

How do I choose an SEO agency in Seattle?

Look for a clear plan, honest timelines, reporting tied to leads or sales and a track record of explaining their work in plain language. Ask what they would do in the first 90 days and who owns your accounts and content.

Do you have an office in Seattle?

No. We are a UK-based studio and work remotely with US businesses. We have no Seattle office or other US office, and we run projects through video calls, shared documents and written reports.

Can a remote agency improve local rankings in Seattle?

Yes. Local rankings depend on your Google Business Profile, reviews, citations and page relevance, not on where your agency sits. The work is the same wherever the agency is based.

How much does SEO cost for a Seattle business?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month. Prices are in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request.

How long does SEO take to work?

Results typically take three to six months to show clearly. Sites with technical problems or very competitive targets can take longer, while quick technical fixes sometimes help sooner.

Do you work with Seattle SaaS and tech companies?

Yes. For software and tech firms we focus on use-case, comparison and integration pages, technical foundations and content that supports demos and trials. National and international searches usually matter more than local ones for these businesses.
