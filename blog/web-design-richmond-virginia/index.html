Why your website matters in Richmond

Richmond has a varied economy: finance and insurance, law and professional services, healthcare, government and education, construction and trades, a busy food and drink scene, and creative and independent businesses from Scott's Addition to Carytown and Manchester. Customers come from across the metro, including Henrico, Chesterfield, Midlothian, Short Pump and Mechanicsville.

Whatever you sell, people check your website before they call. They want to know what you offer, whether you serve their area, what it costs roughly and whether you look trustworthy. An outdated or slow site loses those people quietly, often to a competitor with a cleaner one. Our guide to website trust signals explains what reassures visitors most.

What our web design Richmond VA projects include

Every project covers strategy, design, build and launch. We do not hand over a pretty template and walk away.

  • Discovery: we learn about your services, customers, competitors and goals, then plan the pages and content you need.
  • Structure: a clear sitemap and navigation so visitors and search engines can find everything easily.
  • Design: a mobile-first layout that reflects your brand, with clear calls to action on every key page.

WordPress or Shopify?

For most service businesses in Richmond, such as law firms, contractors, clinics, consultancies and restaurants, WordPress is a strong choice. It is flexible, widely supported and easy to edit. Our guide to a WordPress small business website explains why.

For businesses that sell products online, Shopify is usually the better fit. It handles payments, stock, shipping and taxes, and it scales well. Retailers with a physical shop can also use Shopify POS to keep stock in sync. If you are unsure, we help you choose based on what you sell and how your team works, not on what is easiest for us.

Design that converts

A good-looking site is not enough. It has to guide visitors to act. We focus on a few principles that matter more than visual flourishes.

First, the top of every key page should say clearly what you do, who you do it for and where. A visitor from Glen Allen should know within seconds that you serve their area. Second, there should be one obvious next step on each page, whether that is calling, booking or requesting a quote. Third, proof matters: real photos, reviews you have permission to show, accreditations you genuinely hold and clear information about your process.

We pay close attention to the hero section and the homepage because they carry so much weight. Our guides to hero section design and homepage design go deeper on both.

Mobile and speed

Most local searches happen on phones, often on the go. A site that is slow or awkward on a small screen loses customers fast. We design mobile-first, compress images, limit heavy scripts and choose reliable hosting. We test on real devices before launch and check Core Web Vitals so the site performs well for visitors and for Google.

Accessibility is part of this too. Clear contrast, readable text, sensible headings and keyboard-friendly forms help every visitor, not just those using assistive technology. Accessibility rules in the US can apply to business websites, so we follow recognised good practice, and any legal assessment rests with you and your own adviser.

SEO built in from day one

A new website is the best time to get SEO right. We plan pages around what people actually search for, such as specific services and the areas you cover. We write clear page titles and descriptions, add structured data and make sure the site is easy for Google to crawl.

If you are replacing an existing site, we map old URLs to new ones and set up redirects so you do not lose rankings you have already earned. Our guide to a website redesign without losing rankings explains how. After launch, we can continue with a monthly SEO retainer if you want to grow further.

Content and copywriting

Words do most of the selling on a website. Many businesses delay their launch for weeks because nobody has time to write the pages. We can write your core page copy based on a structured interview with you, then refine it with your feedback. The aim is plain, specific language: what you do, how it works, what it costs where you are comfortable sharing that, and why someone should choose you.

We avoid jargon and filler. Each service page answers the questions customers actually ask, which helps both conversions and search rankings. Our guide to website copywriting explains the approach in more detail. If you already have strong copy, we will use it and simply tidy the structure.

Photography matters as much as copy. Real photos of your team, premises and work build far more trust than stock images. If you do not have good photos yet, we can design the site so that stock images are easy to swap out once you do.

Care after launch

A website is not finished on launch day. Plugins need updates, content needs refreshing and small issues appear over time. We offer ongoing support so your site stays secure, fast and accurate. Our guide to website maintenance lists the tasks that matter most, whether we handle them or your own team does.

How we work remotely with Richmond clients

Richmond runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind the UK for most of the year, which makes scheduling easy. We hold calls in your morning or early afternoon. Between calls, you can message us through email or a shared board and see progress at every stage.

A typical project looks like this: a discovery call, a sitemap and content plan, design of key pages, your feedback, the full build on a staging site, testing, and launch. You own the domain, hosting account and website from day one. We write content in English, and if you need pages in another language, your team or a translator provides the text.
