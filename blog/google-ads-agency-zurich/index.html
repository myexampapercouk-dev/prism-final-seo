Google Ads Agency for Zurich Businesses: Remote PPC Management

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Zurich? How Swiss campaigns differ, language targeting, tracking, what a remote UK studio handles and how fees work.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • Management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, quoted in pounds with Swiss franc quotes on request; ad spend is paid separately to Google.

A Google Ads agency for a Zurich business should turn your ad budget into qualified leads or sales, with campaigns built around how Swiss buyers search, the languages they use and the high cost of clicks in many Swiss sectors. Good management means tight targeting, clear ads, reliable conversion tracking and honest reporting on what the spend is producing.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for businesses across Europe. We have no office in Zurich or elsewhere in Switzerland. Our paid media service runs by video call and shared reports, one hour behind Zurich time, and we write ads in English, working with your team or a translator on German, French or Italian versions.

Why Google Ads in Zurich needs careful setup

Zurich is home to finance, insurance, software, life sciences, consultancies, premium retail and hospitality. Several features of the Swiss market shape how campaigns should be built:

  • Several languages. Zurich is German-speaking, but Switzerland has four national languages, and many international professionals in the city search in English.
  • High-value clicks. In competitive sectors, cost per click can be high, so wasted spend on poor search terms hurts quickly.
  • Swiss German and standard German. People speak Swiss German but usually search in standard German spellings, with some Swiss-specific terms and spellings.
  • Cross-border reach. Some Zurich businesses want customers in Germany or Austria too, which affects targeting and currency.

Language targeting for Swiss campaigns

Google Ads language settings target the languages people use, not the language of your ads, so an English campaign can reach German speakers who use Google in English. Mixing languages inside one ad group usually leads to weak relevance and poor results. A cleaner approach is one campaign per language, each with its own keywords, ads and landing pages.

Prism writes ads in English. For German ads, and French or Italian if you serve other regions, we work with your in-house team or a native-speaking writer or translator. We manage the structure, bidding, testing and tracking, and make sure each language sends people to a matching landing page.

Conversion tracking and data rules in Switzerland

Without reliable tracking, Google Ads optimises towards the wrong things. We set up conversion actions for the results that matter, such as form submissions, calls, bookings and purchases, and connect offline lead quality where possible. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking setup explains the steps.

Switzerland is outside the EU and has its own data protection law, while the GDPR may also apply if you target people in the EU. Cookie consent and tracking choices should reflect both where relevant. We keep this general: check current official guidance and confirm your approach with your legal adviser, who has final approval.

Landing pages that match each campaign

Ads can only do so much if the page they lead to is slow, generic or in the wrong language. Each campaign should send people to a page that matches the search: a German ad to a German page, an English ad to an English page, and a specific service ad to the page for that service rather than the homepage.

  • Message match. The headline on the page should reflect the search and the ad that was clicked.
  • Clear next step. One main call to action, such as a form, call button or booking link.
  • Trust information. Accurate details about your business, how you work and what happens after someone gets in touch.
  • Speed on mobile. Many searches happen on phones, so pages should load quickly on a mobile connection.
  • Prices in francs. If you show prices, Swiss buyers expect them in Swiss francs where relevant.

We review landing pages as part of every account audit and suggest changes. Where pages need rebuilding, we can design them, with German copy written or reviewed by your team or a translator.

Common problems we look for in Swiss accounts

When we audit an existing account, the same issues come up often in multilingual markets:

  • German and English keywords mixed in one ad group, so ads rarely match the search.
  • Location settings that show ads to people interested in Zurich rather than people in or near it, when only local customers can buy.
  • Broad match keywords running without enough negative keywords, wasting budget on unrelated searches.
  • Conversion actions counting page views or duplicate form fills as leads.
  • Budgets spread too thinly across too many campaigns to give any of them enough data.

Fixing these basics is often the quickest way to improve an account before adding new campaigns or larger budgets.
