Caravan Dealer Marketing in the UK: How to Attract More Buyers to Your Forecourt

Caravan dealer marketing in the UK works best with stock pages that rank, local SEO, video walkarounds and paid campaigns that bring serious buyers to view.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Caravan buyers research for weeks before visiting, so detailed stock pages, layout information and video walkarounds win the shortlist.
  • Model and layout searches are where serious buyers look, so structure your website around makes, models, berths and layouts rather than one generic stock list.
  • Workshop, servicing and accessories keep customers coming back, so market aftersales as seriously as sales.

Caravan dealer marketing in the UK is about being found while buyers research layouts and models online, then giving them enough confidence to visit your forecourt and view. The dealers that sell steadily usually combine detailed stock pages, strong local search visibility, video content and paid campaigns that bring serious buyers rather than casual browsers.

This guide is for touring caravan dealers, used caravan specialists and dealerships that also sell motorhomes, accessories and servicing. We cover how buyers research, how to structure your website, where paid media fits and how to build long-term customer relationships through aftersales.

How caravan buyers research and decide

Buying a caravan is a significant, considered purchase. Most buyers spend weeks or months researching before they visit a dealer. They compare layouts, berths, weights, towing requirements, washroom arrangements and storage. Many already know which layouts they like and search for specific makes, models and floor plans. Others are first-time buyers trying to understand what size of caravan suits their family and their car.

By the time someone visits a forecourt, they have usually narrowed down a shortlist. The dealers that make it onto that shortlist are the ones whose websites answered their questions clearly and showed the stock in enough detail to justify the trip.

Seasonality also matters. Interest typically builds in the new year as people plan for spring and summer touring, and many dealers hold events and open weekends around those periods. Planning your marketing calendar around buying peaks helps you make the most of your budget.

Caravan dealer marketing UK: build stock pages that rank

Your stock pages are your most important marketing asset. Many dealer websites show a short list of vehicles with a few photos and a price. That rarely ranks well and rarely answers the questions buyers have. Each vehicle page should include:

  • Make, model, year and layout name in the page title and heading
  • Number of berths, layout description and a floor plan where available
  • Key weights and dimensions, explained in plain language
  • Equipment and features, organised clearly rather than as one long list
  • A full set of high-quality photos, inside and out
  • A short video walkaround
  • Clear information on what is included in the sale, such as handover and any warranty you genuinely provide
  • A simple way to book a viewing or ask a question

Video walkarounds and photography

Caravan buyers want to see inside before they travel to view. A short, well-lit video walkaround of each caravan, showing the layout, storage, washroom and bed arrangements, can do more to secure a visit than any amount of text. Keep videos simple and consistent: start outside, walk through from the door and finish with any standout features.

Photography should follow a consistent order and style across all stock, so buyers can compare easily. Our website video guide covers how to add video without slowing pages down, and image optimisation explains how to keep large photo galleries fast on mobile.

Video also works well beyond your website. Walkarounds can be shared on social channels and used in YouTube campaigns aimed at people researching caravan layouts. Our YouTube Ads guide covers how video campaigns can reach buyers earlier in their research.

Local SEO for caravan dealers

Many buyers search for caravan dealers near them, especially once they are ready to view. A complete business profile with accurate opening hours, forecourt photos, event updates and genuine reviews helps you appear in local results. Encourage customers to leave reviews after handover and mention the model and their experience.

If you have a service workshop, accessories shop or storage facility, make sure these are clearly represented in your profile and on your website. They bring in searches throughout the year, not just during the buying season. Our UK local SEO guide covers the essentials.

Paid media for caravan dealers

Paid campaigns can bring serious buyers to specific stock quickly. Search campaigns targeting make, model and layout terms tend to attract buyers who already know what they want. Remarketing can bring back visitors who viewed a caravan but did not book a viewing.

  • Focus on specific searches. Make and model terms usually convert better than generic caravan searches.
  • Promote events. Open weekends and show events can be supported with search and social ads aimed at your local area.
  • Exclude the wrong traffic. Caravan terms attract holiday park searches, static caravan holidays and rental enquiries, which are rarely relevant to sales.
  • Track viewings, not just clicks. Measure viewing requests and calls so you know which campaigns bring buyers to the forecourt.

If you offer finance, any wording about it in ads or on your website must follow the relevant rules, and approval should rest with whoever is responsible for compliance in your business. Keep statements general and check current official guidance. Our paid media service can manage search, social and video campaigns, with ad spend paid separately.

Aftersales: servicing, accessories and storage

A caravan sale can be the start of a long relationship. Servicing, repairs, accessories, awnings and storage give customers reasons to come back year after year. Each deserves its own page, and servicing in particular should be easy to book online.

An email list of past buyers is valuable here. Send service reminders, seasonal preparation tips, accessory updates and invitations to events. When a customer is ready to upgrade, they are far more likely to come back to a dealer who has stayed in touch helpfully.

Helpful content for first-time buyers

First-time buyers often feel overwhelmed by the choice of layouts, weights and equipment. Guides written for them can bring in early research traffic and make your dealership the one they trust when they are ready to view. Useful topics include how to choose the right number of berths, the pros and cons of fixed beds and end washrooms, what to look for when viewing a used caravan and what happens at handover.

Keep towing guidance general. Rules on towing weights and driving licence entitlements can change and depend on the driver and vehicle, so explain the basics in plain language and tell readers to check current official guidance. Link each guide to relevant stock and category pages, so a reader can move straight from learning to browsing.

A website that supports your forecourt

Many dealer websites are slow, hard to search on mobile and difficult to keep updated. If adding stock is a chore, pages end up thin or out of date. A well-built site with good filtering by make, berth, layout and price makes life easier for both your team and your buyers. Our website development service builds sites for businesses with large, changing inventories, and our SEO service helps stock and category pages rank. SEO and paid results typically take three to six months to build.

Where to start

  1. Improve stock pages with full photos, layouts, video and clear calls to book a viewing.
  2. Create category pages for makes, berths and layouts.
  3. Optimise your business profile and collect reviews after handovers.
  4. Launch focused search and remarketing campaigns around key models and events.
  5. Build an email list for servicing reminders and upgrade offers.

FAQs

What is the best approach to caravan dealer marketing UK?

Build detailed stock pages with photos, floor plans and video, create category pages for makes, berths and layouts, strengthen local SEO and use targeted paid campaigns to bring serious buyers to view.

How can caravan dealers get more viewings?

Give buyers enough detail online to justify the trip, including video walkarounds and clear layouts, and make booking a viewing simple. Remarketing can bring back visitors who did not book first time.

Should caravan dealers advertise on YouTube?

It can work well, because buyers research layouts visually. Short walkaround videos can reach people early in their research and build familiarity before they visit.

What should happen to stock pages when a caravan sells?

Mark it as sold briefly if helpful, then redirect the page to a closely related category so visitors and search engines are not left on dead pages.

How do caravan dealers generate business outside the buying season?

Market servicing, repairs, accessories and storage throughout the year, and keep in touch with past customers by email with useful reminders and event invitations.

Can we promote caravan finance in our ads?

Finance promotions follow specific rules. Keep wording general, have it approved by whoever handles compliance in your business and check current official guidance before publishing.
