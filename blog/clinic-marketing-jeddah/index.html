A clinic website patients trust

Health decisions feel personal, so patients look for clear, calm and accurate information. A good clinic website should:

  • Give each treatment its own page explaining what it is, who it suits, what happens on the day and what recovery involves, written or checked by your clinicians.
  • Show doctor profiles with qualifications and specialties, supplied and approved by your clinic.
  • Give each branch a page with address, map, hours, parking and directions.
  • Make booking easy with an online booking link, phone button and WhatsApp button.
  • Explain insurance and payment options in plain language, using details your team confirms.
  • Load quickly on mobile and be easy to read for older patients.

Our guide to web design in Jeddah covers our general build approach in the city, and the website development service page explains how each project runs.

SEO for Jeddah clinics

Patients search for treatments, symptoms and places: "dentist in Al Rawdah", "skin clinic Jeddah", "physiotherapy near me" or "braces price Jeddah". SEO helps your clinic appear for those searches. We focus on:

  • Fixing technical issues that slow pages or confuse search engines.
  • Planning treatment and area pages based on real search demand.
  • Adding structured data for your clinic, branches and services.
  • Keeping each branch's Google Business Profile accurate, with correct categories and hours.
  • Building a polite review process that follows platform rules and your own policies.
  • Writing clear answers that search engines and AI search tools can quote.

Read more on our SEO and GEO service page or our guide to SEO in Jeddah. SEO typically takes 3 to 6 months to show steady results.

Google and Meta ads for clinics

Paid ads can fill appointment gaps quickly, but health ads need extra care. Google and Meta both have their own healthcare advertising policies, and some treatments face restrictions. We work within those policies and your clinic's rules:

  • Google Search ads for treatments and branch searches, sending people to the matching treatment page, not the homepage.
  • Call and form tracking so you can see which campaigns bring bookings.
  • Meta ads that build awareness of your clinic and approved offers, with careful wording and no before-and-after claims unless your clinic confirms they are allowed.
  • TikTok and YouTube Ads where they suit your audience, using clips your team supplies. We do not film or edit video.

Our page on Google Ads in Jeddah explains how we set up and manage campaigns locally. Ad spend is paid separately.

Arabic and English content

Most Jeddah patients prefer Arabic, while expatriate residents may search in English. Many clinics need both. We write in English, and your clinic team or a professional medical translator writes the Arabic, which your clinicians check. We build right-to-left pages, a clear language switcher and separate page addresses for each language. See our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website for the build details.

Compliance stays with your clinic

Healthcare marketing in Saudi Arabia is regulated. Rules can cover medical advertising, claims, offers, prices, doctor information and patient privacy. We keep our guidance general and do not give legal or regulatory advice. Your clinic and its compliance team approve every page, ad and offer before it goes live, and should check official guidance from the relevant Saudi authorities and their own adviser. We never publish patient stories, results or reviews on your site unless your clinic provides them and confirms they are allowed.

Common problems we fix

Clinic websites often share the same weak points. Treatments are listed on one long page with no detail. Booking buttons are hard to find on mobile. Branch hours on Google do not match the website. Arabic pages are missing or out of date. Ads send every visitor to the homepage, so patients have to search again. Tracking is missing, so the clinic cannot tell which campaigns bring patients. We fix these issues first, then build on them.

What to measure each month

Clinic marketing should be judged by booked appointments, not only by visits. We set up tracking during the first weeks and report each month on:

  • Online bookings, form enquiries and calls from the website.
  • WhatsApp clicks and calls from each branch's Google Business Profile.
  • Search visibility for your main treatments and areas.
  • Cost per enquiry for each Google and Meta campaign.
  • New reviews and the replies sent.

Your front desk adds what only it can see, such as which enquiries became appointments and which treatments are in demand. We use that to adjust pages, keywords and budgets.

Supporting several branches

Many Jeddah clinics grow into groups with branches across the city and in other Saudi cities. Each branch needs its own page, its own Google Business Profile and its own tracking, while the brand and booking system stay the same. We set up a simple structure: one page per branch, linked from each relevant treatment page, so patients can choose the location that suits them. Campaigns can then target each area separately, and budgets can move to the branches with spare appointments.

Choosing the right first step

Not every clinic needs everything at once. If your website is slow or outdated, we usually start there, because ads and SEO both depend on it. If the site is sound, we often begin with Google Business Profile fixes and Google Search ads for your most in-demand treatments, then build SEO content month by month. We agree priorities on the first call, based on your budget and your appointment gaps.
