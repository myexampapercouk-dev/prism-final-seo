Restaurant Marketing in Riyadh: Websites, Local SEO and Social Ads

Restaurant marketing in Riyadh: bilingual websites, local SEO, TikTok and Meta ads for restaurants and cafes from a UK-based remote studio, no Riyadh office.

Restaurant marketing in Riyadh is about making sure hungry people find you, like what they see and book, order or walk in, using a menu-led website, strong map listings and short video ads. Prism Works Studio builds websites and runs local SEO and social campaigns for Riyadh restaurants and cafes, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in Riyadh or anywhere in Saudi Arabia. We work through video calls, shared previews and written reports. We write in English; Arabic menus, pages and ad copy are written with your team or a translator, and we build everything around that copy.

Restaurants and cafes we work with

Riyadh's dining scene has grown fast, with new restaurants, speciality coffee shops and entertainment districts opening across the city. Competition is strong, and diners discover places through maps, social video and word of mouth. We support:

  • Independent restaurants building a name in a busy district.
  • Speciality coffee shops and cafes that depend on regulars and social buzz.
  • Restaurant groups and franchises with several branches across the city.
  • Fine dining and hotel restaurants chasing reservations and private events.
  • Cloud kitchens and delivery-led brands that need their own channel alongside apps.
  • Bakeries, dessert and catering brands taking orders for events and occasions.

A website built around the menu

People visit a restaurant website for a few things: the menu, photos, location, opening hours and a way to book or order. If any of these are hard to find on a phone, they go elsewhere. We design restaurant sites that put these first.

  • Menus as real web pages, not only PDFs, so they load fast and can be found in search.
  • Arabic and English versions, with right-to-left layout for Arabic.
  • Branch pages with maps, hours, parking notes and phone or WhatsApp links.
  • Booking and ordering links to the reservation and delivery tools you already use.
  • Event and private dining pages with a simple enquiry form.

Our guide to restaurant website design goes deeper on layout, and our page on web design in Riyadh covers wider site projects. A new website usually takes 3 to 5 weeks.

Local SEO and map listings

Many diners in Riyadh choose where to eat from a map. Your Google Business Profile, photos, reviews, hours and menu link all affect whether you appear and whether people click. We set up and tidy profiles for each branch, choose the right categories, add fresh photos you supply and make sure hours are updated for Ramadan and holidays.

On the website, we build pages that match how people search, such as a dish or cuisine combined with a district like Olaya, Al Malqa or King Abdullah Financial District. Our Google Business Profile guide explains profile basics, and our page on SEO in Riyadh covers organic search in the city.

TikTok, Instagram and short video

Short video drives a lot of restaurant discovery in Saudi Arabia. A clip of a dish being plated or a busy evening can do more than a long caption. We plan simple video concepts with you, advise on what to film, and run TikTok and Meta campaigns that target people near your branches.

We use location targeting, interest targeting and remarketing to people who visited your site or engaged with your content. Ad copy in Arabic is written with your team or a translator. Our pages on TikTok Ads in Saudi Arabia and Meta Ads in Saudi Arabia explain how we run those platforms.

Planning around Ramadan and seasons

Ramadan changes everything for restaurants: opening hours shift, iftar and suhoor menus take over, and evenings get very busy. Eid, Founding Day, National Day and Riyadh Season events also bring peaks. We plan content, ad budgets and page updates ahead of these dates so offers are approved and live in time, and so map listings show the right hours.

Delivery apps and your own channel

Delivery apps bring orders but also take commissions and own the customer relationship. Many restaurants keep using apps while building their own direct ordering and booking routes. We help you send traffic from your site, maps and ads to the channel that works best for each goal, and we track clicks to booking and ordering links so you can see what each campaign produces.

Reviews and reputation

Reviews influence where people eat. We help you set up simple, polite ways to ask happy guests for a review, such as a card or QR code at the table, and to reply to reviews in a consistent tone. We never write fake reviews or offer incentives that break platform rules.

How a project runs

  1. Discovery call. We learn your concept, menu, branches, busy times and goals.
  2. Quick wins. We fix map listings, menu pages and tracking first.
  3. Build. We create or improve the website and set up ad accounts and audiences.
  4. Launch and test. We run ads, test creative and offers, and review results weekly.
  5. Report. Monthly reports show bookings, orders, calls and direction requests. SEO and paid results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

Rules and permits

Food businesses in Saudi Arabia work within local rules on licensing, food labelling, advertising and pricing displays. We keep marketing in line with platform policies and general good practice, but we are not legal advisers. Please check official guidance or your own adviser for local requirements.

Photos and video that sell the food

Food is visual, and diners judge a restaurant by its photos before they read a word. Good images on your website, map listings and ads usually do more than any other single change. We give you a simple shot list for each branch: signature dishes, the room at a busy time, the entrance so people recognise it, and any outdoor seating or private areas.

Phone footage often works well for TikTok and reels if it is steady, well lit and shows real moments. We advise on hooks for the first seconds, captions and length, and we reuse the best clips across your site, maps and ads so each piece of content works harder.

Measuring bookings, orders and visits

Restaurants need to know which marketing brings people through the door. We track clicks to booking tools, delivery links, calls, WhatsApp messages and direction requests from your map listings. Where your booking tool allows it, we connect bookings to the campaign that drove them. Monthly reports show which branches, dishes, offers and channels perform best, so you can shift budget and attention with confidence.

Common mistakes we fix

  • Menus only as PDFs, which load slowly on phones and are hard for search engines to read.
  • Old hours on maps, especially during Ramadan and public holidays.
  • Ads that target the whole city instead of the areas near each branch.
  • No clear booking button on the first screen of the website.
