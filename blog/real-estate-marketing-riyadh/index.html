Real Estate Marketing in Riyadh: Websites, SEO and Ads for Developers, Brokers and Agencies

Real estate marketing in Riyadh: project websites, Arabic and English SEO, Google and Meta ads and lead tracking, from a UK-based studio with no Saudi office.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Real estate marketing in Riyadh combines clear project and listing pages, Arabic and English SEO, paid ads and fast follow-up of every enquiry, often through WhatsApp.
  • We are a UK-based studio that provides websites, SEO and paid media remotely for Riyadh developers and brokers, with no office in Saudi Arabia, and Arabic content is written with your team or a translator.
  • Retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, ad spend is separate, compliance approval for property ads rests with you, and Saudi riyals are quoted on request.

Real estate marketing in Riyadh means helping developers, brokers and property companies reach buyers, tenants and investors through project websites, search engine optimisation, paid advertising and well-managed lead follow-up, in both Arabic and English. The capital's property market is busy and competitive, so the businesses that win enquiries tend to be the ones with clear information, fast websites and quick, organised responses.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with real estate businesses in Riyadh through our paid media service, alongside SEO and website development. We have no office in Saudi Arabia and work through video calls, shared documents and your own advertising and analytics accounts. This guide explains how we approach property marketing in Riyadh, what it costs and where the main risks lie.

Who this guide is for

Riyadh's real estate sector includes several kinds of business with different marketing needs.

  • Developers launching off-plan or completed residential, commercial or mixed-use projects.
  • Brokers and agencies listing homes, villas, apartments and offices for sale or rent.
  • Property managers looking for landlords and tenants.
  • Commercial specialists leasing offices, retail units, warehouses and land.

Each needs a slightly different mix of channels, but the foundations are the same: a credible website, accurate information, strong search visibility and reliable tracking of enquiries.

Websites for developers and brokers

A property website has to answer the questions buyers and investors ask before they enquire: where the project is, what types of unit are available, the stage of construction, payment plans, amenities, nearby schools and roads, and how to arrange a viewing. Vague brochure-style pages with a single contact form rarely convert well.

For developers we usually build a dedicated page or microsite for each major project, with floor plans, image galleries, location details, a short video and clear enquiry options. For brokers we focus on well-organised listings, area guides and simple search. Every page is built for phones first, because most property browsing happens on mobile. Our guide to web design in Saudi Arabia explains our approach to bilingual sites, and our landing page design guide covers the principles behind high-converting campaign pages.

SEO for Riyadh property businesses

People search for property by area, type and purpose: apartments for rent in a particular district, villas for sale in north Riyadh, offices on a major road, or off-plan projects from a named developer. SEO for real estate means building genuinely useful pages for these searches in both Arabic and English.

We focus on area guides written from information you supply, property-type pages, project pages, FAQ content and structured data that helps search engines understand listings and your business. A complete Google Business Profile matters for brokers and sales centres. Our guide to SEO in Riyadh explains our wider approach, and the same principles apply to AI search, where clear and consistent information makes a business easier to recommend.

Paid advertising for property

Paid advertising is often the fastest way to generate enquiries for a new project. Google Ads captures people already searching for property, while Meta, TikTok and YouTube build interest among people who may not yet be searching. Our guides to Google Ads in Saudi Arabia, Meta Ads in Saudi Arabia and TikTok Ads in Saudi Arabia cover each platform in more depth.

The biggest challenge in property advertising is lead quality. Instant lead forms can produce large volumes of contacts who did not mean to enquire or cannot afford the property. We reduce this by adding qualifying questions, using higher-intent forms or landing pages, sending conversations to WhatsApp where your team can respond quickly, and importing data on which leads became viewings and sales so the platforms learn what a good lead looks like.

Platforms also apply special rules to housing advertising in some countries and to property claims everywhere. We keep ads factual and avoid promises about returns or prices that cannot be supported.

Lead follow-up and tracking

In property marketing, the speed and quality of follow-up often matter more than the cost of the lead. A buyer who enquires about three projects in an evening will usually visit the one whose sales team replies first and clearly.

  1. Capture. Every enquiry, whether a form, call or WhatsApp message, is recorded with its source campaign.
  2. Respond. Your team replies quickly, in the enquirer's language, with relevant details.
  3. Qualify. Leads are marked by budget, timing and interest.
  4. Feed back. Outcomes such as viewings and reservations are shared with us so we can shift budget to the campaigns that produce them.

We set up tracking in your own accounts so the data stays with you, and we can help you design a simple process even without a full CRM.

Arabic, English and international buyers

Riyadh's property buyers include Saudi families, expatriate residents and, for some projects, investors from abroad. Most campaigns need Arabic and English versions with matching landing pages. Prism writes in English. Arabic content, ad copy and brochures are written with your team or a professional translator, and a fluent speaker on your side should approve them. Tone matters: Saudi buyers often respond to messages about family, community, quality and trust, and campaigns should be planned around Ramadan, Eid and other important periods.

If you also market property in the UAE, our guide to real estate marketing in Dubai covers that market, and our property developer marketing guide shares principles that apply to project launches anywhere.

Photography, video and content

Property is a visual purchase. Professional photography, drone footage where permitted, short walkthrough videos and accurate floor plans make a large difference to both organic and paid results. We do not produce photography or video ourselves, but we brief your team or suppliers on the formats each channel needs, such as vertical video for TikTok and Instagram and landscape footage for YouTube and project pages. Every image and claim should reflect the real property or an approved, clearly labelled visualisation.

Regulation and compliance

Real estate advertising in Saudi Arabia is regulated, and there are rules on matters such as licensing, the information that must appear in property advertisements and how off-plan projects are marketed. These rules change over time. We are not legal advisers, so check the official guidance from the relevant Saudi authorities and ask your own adviser to confirm what applies. Compliance approval for every advertisement, listing and landing page rests with your business, and we build in time for that review before anything goes live.

FAQs

What does real estate marketing in Riyadh involve?

It combines project and listing websites, Arabic and English SEO, paid advertising and lead follow-up. We provide this remotely from the UK for Riyadh property businesses.

Do you have an office in Riyadh?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK, has no office in Saudi Arabia and works remotely through calls, shared documents and your own accounts.

Which ad platform works best for property in Riyadh?

It depends on the project. Google Ads captures active searches, while Meta, TikTok and YouTube suit launches and awareness. Most developers test more than one.

Who is responsible for compliance in property ads?

Your business is. We keep ads factual, but compliance approval rests with you and your adviser, based on official Saudi guidance.
