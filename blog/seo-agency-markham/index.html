Local SEO across York Region

For many Markham businesses, the map pack is where most enquiries start. A plumber, dentist, tutor or accountant can win plenty of work from searches within a few kilometres of their location. We focus on:

  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with the right categories and services.
  • Consistent name, address and phone details across directories.
  • A fair, regular process for asking customers for reviews.
  • Location pages for nearby areas you genuinely serve, written to be useful rather than duplicated.
  • Local links from community organisations, events and business associations.

Technical SEO and site quality

Many Markham websites are held back by problems owners never see: slow mobile pages, duplicate content, pages blocked from indexing, or old site migrations that broke links. We start every project with a technical audit and fix the issues that matter most first.

If your site is outdated or hard to edit, a rebuild may be more cost-effective than patching it. Our web design Markham guide covers what a modern, search-friendly site needs, and our website development service explains how we build them.

Content for a multilingual market

Markham has many residents who search in languages other than English. Prism writes in English. If you want pages in Chinese, French or another language, the content should come from your own team or a professional translator, and we can handle the technical setup, such as language versions and correct hreflang tags.

Good content answers the questions customers really ask: prices, process, timescales, what to expect and how to choose. We avoid thin pages stuffed with keywords and focus on fewer, better pages that earn their place in search.

What to expect in the first three months

SEO is a steady process, so it helps to know what happens early on. In the first month we usually focus on the audit, fixing technical issues that block crawling or indexing, and tidying your Google Business Profile. These changes rarely produce dramatic jumps on their own, but they remove the problems that stop other work from paying off.

In the second month we turn to content. That often means rewriting your most important service pages so they answer customer questions clearly, adding pages for services that had no proper page before, and improving internal links so Google understands which pages matter most. We also start a review process if you do not already have one.

By the third month we are usually building local links, publishing helpful guides and reviewing early data from Google Search Console. You should start to see more of your pages appearing in search results and more impressions for the terms we target. Enquiries tend to follow as rankings improve, which is why we report on calls and form submissions as well as positions.

After the first quarter, the work becomes a rhythm of reviewing data, improving pages that are close to the top of results, adding new content where there are gaps and continuing to build your local reputation. We adjust priorities each month based on what the data shows rather than following a fixed checklist, and we explain every change in plain language so you always know where your budget is going.

How to choose an SEO agency for your Markham business

Whoever you work with, a few questions help separate careful agencies from those selling quick fixes:

  • Do they explain what they will actually do each month? Vague promises of "optimisation" are hard to hold anyone to.
  • Do they promise guaranteed rankings? No one controls Google, so guarantees are a warning sign.
  • Who owns the work? You should own your website, content, Google Business Profile and analytics accounts.
  • How do they build links? Bought links and private networks can do lasting harm.
  • What do reports show? Good reports connect search visibility to enquiries and sales, not just traffic.
  • Is there a long lock-in? Flexible terms keep an agency focused on earning your business each month.

We are happy to answer all of these on a first call, and we will tell you plainly if we think your budget would be better spent elsewhere for now, for example on a new website before ongoing SEO.
