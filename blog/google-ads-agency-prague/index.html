Search in the Czech Republic

Google is widely used in the Czech Republic, but it is not the only search engine. Seznam, a Czech search engine and web portal, still has a loyal audience, and it runs its own advertising platform called Sklik. Our work focuses on Google Ads. If Seznam matters for your audience, it is worth reviewing separately with a specialist.

Language is the other big factor. Czech is a highly inflected language, which means the same word can take many forms. Keyword lists need to cover these variations, and negative keywords need careful thought. We build keyword lists with your team or a translator, because Prism writes in English and Czech ad copy should come from a native speaker. We then structure campaigns so search terms map cleanly to the right ad and landing page.

Many Prague businesses also advertise in English, either to international residents and visitors or to customers in other countries. We can run separate campaigns by language and country so budgets and reporting stay clear. Our guide to international PPC covers this in more detail.

Conversion tracking and consent

Google Ads only works as well as the data it receives. Before we spend a single koruna, we check that conversions are tracked properly: form submissions, calls, purchases and other actions that matter to you.

In the European Economic Area, tracking has to respect consent. These notes are general information, not legal advice. Please check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

  • GDPR. Personal data collected through your ads and website is covered by the GDPR. That includes enquiry forms and customer lists used for audiences.
  • Cookie consent. EU ePrivacy rules generally require consent before non-essential cookies, such as advertising and analytics tags, are set. We set up Google tags with consent mode so they respect the choices your banner records.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied to many consumer-facing digital products and services in the EU since June 2025. Microenterprises that provide services are generally exempt. The usual technical reference is EN 301 549, which points to WCAG at level AA. Online shops in particular should check whether it applies to them.

Our guides to GA4 setup and cookie banner design cover the basics of tracking and consent.

Landing pages that convert

Sending paid traffic to a weak page wastes money. We review the pages your ads point to and recommend changes, such as clearer headlines, faster loading, simpler forms and stronger calls to action. If you need new pages, we can design and build them. Our guide to landing page design sets out what a good page needs.

For online shops, product pages are your landing pages. Accurate prices, clear delivery information and good product images all help Shopping and Performance Max campaigns perform.

Common mistakes in Czech Google Ads accounts

When we audit existing accounts, the same problems come up again and again. Most are easy to fix once spotted:

  • Broad keywords with no negatives. Ads show for loosely related searches and budget drains away on clicks that never convert.
  • Missing word forms. Keyword lists that ignore Czech grammatical endings miss a large share of relevant searches.
  • Mixed languages in one campaign. Czech and English ads and keywords in the same ad group make results hard to read and harder to improve.
  • Location settings left on default. Ads can reach people who are only interested in a location, rather than people actually in it.
  • Tracking that counts the wrong things. Page views or duplicate form submissions counted as conversions mislead automated bidding.
  • No brand campaign. Competitors and resellers bid on your name while you pay more to win back your own customers.

Fixing these basics often has more impact than any new campaign type. We usually start any new Prague account with a short clean-up phase, so the budget you spend afterwards works from a solid structure and reliable data. That clean-up usually takes a few weeks, and we explain every change in plain English as we go. Our guide to Google Ads for small businesses covers more of the fundamentals.

How we manage your account

  1. Audit. If you already run Google Ads, we review structure, keywords, search terms, tracking and spend.
  2. Plan. We agree goals, target cost per lead or return on ad spend, and a test budget.
  3. Build. We set up or rebuild campaigns, keywords, ads, assets and audiences.
  4. Optimise. We review search terms, bids, budgets and ads every week, and add negative keywords to cut waste.
  5. Report. Each month you get a plain-English report on spend, conversions and next steps.

Paid search can start bringing traffic within days, but campaigns usually need time to learn. Results typically settle over three to six months as data builds and the account is refined.
