Why Surrey businesses need a strong website

Surrey is one of the fastest-growing cities in British Columbia, with busy communities in Whalley and City Centre, Guildford, Fleetwood, Newton, Cloverdale and South Surrey, plus close links to Delta, Langley, White Rock and the wider Fraser Valley. Features that matter for local businesses

  • Speed: pages that load quickly on mobile data.
  • Clear service pages: one page per main service, explaining what you do, who it is for and what happens next.
  • Areas served: honest information about the neighbourhoods and nearby cities you cover.
  • Easy contact: tap-to-call buttons, short forms and booking links.
  • Trust signals: licences, insurance details and genuine reviews where you have them.
  • Accessibility: readable text, good contrast and sensible structure.
  • Privacy: clear notices about how you handle personal information.

Planning the right pages for your business

The page list is one of the most important decisions in any website project, and it is often rushed. A good page list follows how customers think about your services, not how your business is organised internally. A renovation contractor, for example, might need separate pages for kitchens, bathrooms, basements and additions, because people search for each one separately and want to see relevant information.

A typical small business site for a Surrey company might include:

  • A home page that explains what you do, who you help and where you work in a few seconds.
  • One page for each main service, with process, typical questions and a clear next step.
  • An areas page covering Surrey neighbourhoods and nearby cities you genuinely serve.
  • An about page explaining your background, licences and how you work.
  • A contact page with a short form, phone number and service hours.
  • A small number of helpful guides answering common customer questions.

We avoid creating dozens of near-identical location pages, which add little value and can harm search visibility. Fewer, better pages almost always work harder.

Common website problems we fix

When Surrey businesses come to us with an existing site, the same issues come up again and again. Phone numbers that cannot be tapped on mobile. Contact forms that silently fail, so enquiries never arrive. Huge images that make pages slow to load. Services hidden in a single long page that nobody reads to the end. No analytics, so the owner has no idea whether the site produces any work at all.

Each of these is fixable, and together they often explain why a website feels like it does nothing. During discovery we check for them and tell you honestly whether a targeted fix or a full rebuild is better value. Sometimes a few weeks of focused improvements are enough, and we will say so.

Looking after your website after launch

A website is not finished on launch day. Plugins and software need updating, content goes out of date, and new services or areas need pages of their own. We recommend checking forms regularly, keeping backups, reviewing analytics each month and refreshing key pages at least once a year so prices, processes and contact details stay accurate.

You can handle much of this yourself after training. If you would rather hand it over, ongoing SEO and marketing support starts from our Launch retainer, which also keeps your site improving in search rather than standing still. Either way, you own your domain, hosting account, content and analytics, so you are never tied to us.
