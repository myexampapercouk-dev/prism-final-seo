Ecommerce in Sofia: what shoppers expect

Sofia is Bulgaria's capital and largest city, and home to a large share of the country's online retailers, from fashion, beauty and home goods to food, supplements, electronics accessories and handmade products. Many Sofia brands also sell into Romania, Greece and the wider EU.

Bulgarian shoppers are used to buying from large marketplaces such as eMAG, and they expect a smooth experience from smaller brands too. Common expectations include:

  • Clear prices in the currency they expect, including taxes.
  • Familiar delivery options, including courier delivery to an address or to an office or locker. Couriers such as Econt and Speedy are widely used.
  • Cash on delivery as well as card payments, as many shoppers still prefer it.
  • Easy returns information and fast customer support.
  • Mobile-first browsing and checkout.

Shopify can handle all of these through its settings and app ecosystem, but the right choices depend on your products, margins and markets. Consumer, tax and data rules apply in Bulgaria and the EU, so we keep our notes general and recommend you check official guidance or your own adviser.

Shopify agency Sofia: new build or migration?

New stores. For brands starting out or moving from marketplace-only selling, we plan the catalogue, design the store, set up payments and delivery, add your products and test everything before launch.

Migrations. Many Sofia brands start on WooCommerce, OpenCart or a custom platform. A migration moves products, customers and order history where possible, and keeps your search rankings by redirecting old URLs to the matching new pages. Our Shopify vs WooCommerce comparison helps if you are still deciding whether to move.

For larger brands, we also handle Shopify Plus migrations, where checkout customisation, multiple stores or high order volumes call for it.

How we run a remote Shopify project

  1. Discovery call: products, markets, payments, delivery, integrations and content responsibilities.
  2. Plan: sitemap, collection structure, app list and timeline agreed in writing.
  3. Design: home, collection and product templates shared for comment.

Product data: the part most projects underestimate

The design of a Shopify store gets most of the attention, but product data decides how well it sells and how easily it can be found. Before the build starts, we work with you on a product data sheet that covers:

  • Product titles that describe the item clearly, including type, material, size or variant where relevant.
  • Descriptions that answer buyers' questions about fit, use, care, ingredients or compatibility.
  • Variants such as size and colour set up properly, rather than as separate products.
  • Good images on a consistent background, with alternative text for accessibility.
  • Barcodes, stock codes, weights and dimensions, which matter for shipping and for Google Shopping.
  • Collections and tags that match how shoppers browse, such as by category, occasion or material.

Clean product data makes the store easier to manage, improves filters and search inside the store, and gives SEO and Shopping campaigns a strong base. If your data currently lives in spreadsheets, a marketplace account or an old platform, we can help you plan how to bring it together for import.

Bulgarian and English store set-up

Most Sofia brands sell mainly to Bulgarian shoppers, so Bulgarian should usually be the main store language. An English version helps if you sell to expats, tourists or buyers in other EU countries. Shopify lets you run several languages in one store, each with its own URLs, so search engines can index each version separately.

We set up the language versions, navigation and templates, then load the Bulgarian text supplied by your team or translator. We check that product titles, collection names and page titles follow the keyword plan in each language, and that the language switcher works well on mobile. Cyrillic text in URLs can be handled in different ways, so we agree a consistent approach for handles before products are imported.

Owning your store

Your Shopify account, domain, apps and ad accounts should be in your name. We work as a collaborator on your accounts, so if you ever move to another agency or bring the work in-house, you keep everything we built. We also hand over notes on the theme settings, apps and integrations, so your team or a future partner can pick up where we left off.
