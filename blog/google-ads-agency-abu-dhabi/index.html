A Google Ads agency in Abu Dhabi should help your business show up when people in the capital search for what you offer, and turn those clicks into calls, messages and bookings. Prism Works Studio plans and manages search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns for Abu Dhabi businesses, working remotely from the UK.

Who we help in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi has a different feel from Dubai. Government, energy, healthcare, education and professional services play a large part, alongside a strong residential and family market. We manage Google Ads for:

  • Clinics, dental practices and wellness providers seeking local patient enquiries, with ad wording approved by the client.
  • Schools, nurseries and training centres promoting admissions and courses.
  • Real estate and property services marketing rentals, sales and management.
  • B2B suppliers and consultancies selling to larger organisations.
  • Retail and ecommerce brands selling across the UAE through Shopping campaigns.
  • Home services, such as maintenance, cleaning and cooling companies.

Regulated sectors have advertising rules in the UAE. We keep any notes general, follow Google's advertising policies and leave compliance approval with your team. Check the relevant official guidance or your own adviser before launching claims.

Targeting the capital, not the whole country

Abu Dhabi is a large emirate, with the island city, the mainland suburbs and Al Ain some distance apart. A clinic on the island may not want clicks from people a long drive away. We usually set location targeting by radius or area around your premises, then review results by location and adjust bids. For brands that sell across the UAE, we target the whole country but track how Abu Dhabi traffic performs compared with Dubai and the northern emirates.

We also set targeting to people in or regularly in your area, which avoids paying for clicks from people abroad who are only browsing, unless you deliberately want to reach visitors or relocating families.

Arabic and English search in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi has a large Emirati population and a big expatriate community, so searches happen in Arabic, English and other languages. We split campaigns or ad groups by language, with matching landing pages, so each searcher sees an ad and page in the language they used. This also makes reports clearer.

We do not write Arabic ourselves. Your team or a translator writes the Arabic keywords, ads and page copy, and we handle structure, keyword match types, bidding, testing and reporting.

Landing pages that convert Abu Dhabi traffic

Most ad clicks in the UAE happen on phones, so the landing page needs to load quickly and make the next step obvious. We look for:

  • A headline that matches the search and the ad.
  • Call and WhatsApp buttons visible without scrolling.
  • A short form with only the fields you need.
  • A correct right-to-left layout for Arabic pages.
  • Your real address, licence details where relevant and opening hours.

If your pages need work, our web design guide for Abu Dhabi covers what a good local site needs, and we can quote for landing pages separately.

Tracking calls, forms and WhatsApp

Many customers in Abu Dhabi prefer to call or message rather than fill in a form. If only forms are tracked, Google sees a fraction of your real leads and bids poorly. We set up:

  • Google Analytics 4 key events for forms, calls and purchases.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking, with enhanced conversions where suitable.
  • Call tracking from ads and from the website.
  • WhatsApp click tracking.
  • Consent handling that fits your privacy approach, which you should confirm with your own adviser.

Where possible, we also import offline outcomes, such as booked appointments or signed contracts, so bidding is guided by real business value.

AI-powered help, checked by people

We use AI-powered tools to group keywords, draft English ad variations and spot wasted spend in search term reports faster. Every change is checked by a person before it goes live. Google's automated bidding performs best when conversion data is clean, so our priority is accurate tracking and sensible exclusions rather than handing everything to automation.

Mistakes we often see

  • Targeting the whole UAE when the business only serves Abu Dhabi.
  • Ignoring calls and WhatsApp as conversions, which starves bidding of data.
  • Mixing Arabic and English keywords in one ad group.
  • Leaving search partners and display expansion on without checking quality.
  • Sending all traffic to the home page rather than a matching service page.

Seasonality and budget planning

Demand in Abu Dhabi shifts through the year. Summer heat changes how people shop and travel, school terms drive education searches, and Ramadan and Eid change browsing times and buying patterns. We plan budgets around these patterns with you, adjust ad schedules to match when your customers search, and prepare seasonal ad copy in advance so your team or translator has time to supply the Arabic versions before each peak.
