LinkedIn Ads Agency for Saudi Arabia: B2B Campaigns, Targeting and Cost

Looking for a LinkedIn ads agency in Saudi Arabia? How a remote UK studio runs B2B LinkedIn campaigns for Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam firms, and what it costs.

  • LinkedIn ads suit Saudi B2B firms that sell to specific job roles, industries or company sizes, where a single new client is worth a lot.
  • We manage campaigns remotely from the UK with no office in Saudi Arabia, and Arabic ad copy is written with your team or a professional translator.
  • Management retainers run from £350 to £1,500 per month, ad spend is paid separately to LinkedIn, and results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

A LinkedIn ads agency in Saudi Arabia plans and manages paid campaigns on LinkedIn so that B2B companies reach the decision-makers they want, by job title, seniority, industry and company size, and turn that reach into qualified leads. It is the platform to consider when your buyers are professionals and each new client is worth a meaningful amount.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based digital marketing studio that manages LinkedIn advertising remotely for businesses in Saudi Arabia and across the Gulf. We have no office in the Kingdom. Campaigns run in your own LinkedIn Campaign Manager account, with clear reports and video calls. This guide explains how our paid media service approaches LinkedIn ads in Saudi Arabia, what it costs and when another platform may be a better fit.

When LinkedIn ads make sense in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia has a large and growing B2B economy, with activity spread across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Al Khobar and many other cities. Consultancies, technology and software firms, engineering and construction suppliers, training providers, recruitment firms, logistics companies and professional services businesses often sell to a narrow group of people inside other organisations. LinkedIn is one of the few platforms that lets you target those people by their professional details rather than guessing from interests.

LinkedIn clicks usually cost more than clicks on other platforms, so it suits offers where the value of a client justifies a higher cost per lead. If your customers are consumers, or if people actively search for your service, Meta or Google may work better. LinkedIn ads agency Saudi Arabia: our approach

We follow a clear sequence on every account, adjusted to your market and budget.

  1. Offer and audience definition. We agree who the ideal buyer is, which companies they work for and what offer will make them act, such as a consultation, demo, guide or event.
  2. Tracking. We install and test the LinkedIn Insight Tag and conversion events. Our Insight Tag setup guide explains the steps.
  3. Campaign structure. We separate audiences by role, sector or language so results are easy to read and budgets easy to move.
  4. Creative and copy. We plan single image, document, video and lead form ads, in English and Arabic where needed.
  5. Launch and optimisation. We watch lead quality as well as cost, refine audiences, test offers and adjust bids.
  6. Reporting and follow-up. We report monthly and help your sales team follow up leads quickly, because speed of reply matters on LinkedIn.

Our guide to LinkedIn ads for B2B lead generation goes deeper into offers and lead forms, and the principles apply in Saudi Arabia as well as the UK.

Targeting Saudi decision-makers

LinkedIn targeting options include location, job title, job function, seniority, industry, company name, company size, skills and groups. For Saudi campaigns we usually combine location with two or three professional filters, keeping audiences large enough for LinkedIn to deliver ads efficiently but narrow enough to reach real buyers.

Account-based campaigns, where you upload a list of target companies, can work well for firms selling to a defined set of organisations such as major contractors, government-linked entities or large private groups. Matched audiences built from website visitors and contact lists help you stay in front of people who already know you.

Language is a planning decision. Many professionals in Saudi Arabia use LinkedIn in English, but Arabic creative can stand out and suits some sectors and roles better. We write in English. Arabic ad copy is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend a fluent speaker approves it before launch. Landing pages should match the language of the ad, and our guide to web design in Saudi Arabia covers bilingual sites.

LinkedIn ad formats and offers that work

LinkedIn offers several ad formats, and the right mix depends on how well your audience already knows you. Single image ads are simple to produce and good for testing messages. Document ads let people read a guide, checklist or case overview inside LinkedIn, and can ask for contact details before the full document opens. Video ads help explain a complex service or introduce a brand to a new audience. Lead gen forms fill in details from the member's profile, which makes it easier to respond on a phone.

The offer matters as much as the format. Cold audiences rarely book a sales call from a first ad, so we usually start with something useful and low-commitment, such as a practical guide, a webinar, a short assessment or an industry briefing. People who engage are then shown a stronger offer, such as a consultation or demo. This two-step approach often produces better-quality leads than asking strangers for a meeting straight away.

Whatever the format, the follow-up decides the outcome. We agree with your sales team how quickly leads will be contacted, what they will be sent and how lead quality will be fed back, so campaigns can be adjusted around the leads that turn into real conversations.

Combining LinkedIn with search and content

LinkedIn rarely works best alone. Buyers who see your ads often search for your company name or your service before they get in touch, so a strong website and organic search presence support every campaign. Rules, ownership and working remotely

LinkedIn has its own advertising policies, and some sectors such as finance, healthcare and employment face extra restrictions. Saudi rules on advertising, data and consumer protection also apply and change over time. We keep claims careful and follow platform policies, but you should check current official guidance in Saudi Arabia and confirm sector-specific requirements with your own adviser. In regulated sectors, approval of ad content rests with your compliance team.

Campaigns run in your own Campaign Manager account and LinkedIn page, with billing in your name. You keep the account, audiences and data if you stop working with us.

Working remotely is straightforward because everything happens inside the platform. Saudi Arabia is a few hours ahead of the UK, which leaves a good overlap for calls. With us, you get:

  • A single point of contact and agreed call times.
  • Monthly reports covering spend, leads, lead quality and next steps.
  • Help aligning ads with how your sales team follows up.
  • Honesty about limits: we have no office in the Kingdom, so events, meetings or on-site content need your team or a local partner.

FAQs

What does a LinkedIn ads agency in Saudi Arabia do?

It defines your target buyers, sets up tracking, builds and manages LinkedIn campaigns, creates ads and lead forms, and reports on cost and lead quality so your budget reaches real decision-makers.

How much does a LinkedIn ads agency in Saudi Arabia cost?

Our management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month depending on scope. Ad spend is paid separately to LinkedIn, and other currencies are quoted on request.

Do you have an office in Saudi Arabia?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and manages LinkedIn campaigns remotely through your own account, with regular reports and video calls.

Are LinkedIn ads worth it for small B2B firms?

They can be when each client is valuable and your buyers are clearly defined. For low-value sales or services people search for urgently, Google or Meta ads are often a better starting point.

Can you run LinkedIn ads in Arabic?

Yes. We run English and Arabic campaigns. Arabic copy is written with your team or a professional translator and approved by a fluent speaker before launch.

How long before LinkedIn ads produce leads?

Leads can arrive within weeks, but consistent results typically build over 3 to 6 months, and B2B deals may take longer to close after the first enquiry.
