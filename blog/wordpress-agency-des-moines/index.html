How we build WordPress sites

We keep builds lean. That means:

  • The block editor first. We use the native WordPress block editor and site editor where they do the job, rather than heavy page builders. Our guide to WordPress page builders explains the trade-offs.
  • Few, well-chosen plugins. Every plugin adds code and risk, so we use only what is needed and choose well-maintained options.
  • Reusable patterns. We create patterns and templates so your team can add pages that match the design without breaking it.
  • Clear user roles. Staff get the access they need, and no more.
  • Speed from the start. Optimised images, careful font loading and caching.

Fixing an existing WordPress site

Many Des Moines businesses already have a WordPress site that has slowed down, broken or become hard to edit. Common problems we fix include:

  • Slow pages caused by bloated themes, page builders and too many plugins.
  • Outdated PHP versions, plugins and themes that create security risks.
  • Contact forms that stop sending emails.
  • Mixed content warnings after a move to HTTPS.
  • Critical errors after an update.
  • Messy menus, categories and URLs that confuse visitors and search engines.

We usually start with a plugin audit and a review of hosting, then agree a list of fixes in order of impact. Our WordPress security guide covers the protective steps we put in place afterwards.

WordPress for insurance and financial firms

Des Moines is one of the best-known insurance centers in the United States, and many local firms work in insurance, financial services or related support businesses. Their websites often need detailed product pages, agent or adviser directories, resource libraries and careful handling of forms.

We can build these structures in WordPress, using custom post types for things like products, offices or team members so content stays organised. Our custom post types guide explains how this works. Hosting and maintenance

A WordPress site is only as good as the hosting under it and the care it gets after launch. We advise on managed WordPress hosting suited to your traffic and budget, and we can move your site to better hosting without downtime. Our WordPress hosting guide covers what to look for.

After launch, a care plan typically includes:

  • Regular core, theme and plugin updates, tested before they go live where possible.
  • Daily backups stored away from the main server.
  • Uptime and security monitoring.
  • Small content edits and fixes each month.
  • A monthly report on updates, speed and search performance.

SEO built into every WordPress site

We set up WordPress so search engines can read it easily: clean permalinks, sensible categories, structured data, a sitemap, fast loading and proper heading structure. For Des Moines businesses, we also make sure the site connects with your Google Business Profile and includes clear service area information for the suburbs you cover.

If you want ongoing search growth after launch, our retainers combine site care with local SEO and content work. Our website and e-commerce development service page explains what each build includes.

Our process

  1. Discovery call. We learn about your business, customers, current site and goals.
  2. Plan. We agree a sitemap, content list and fixed quote.
  3. Design. Homepage and key inner pages first, desktop and mobile.
  4. Build. Development on a private staging site you can review.
  5. Test. Speed, forms, mobile layout, accessibility basics and tracking.
  6. Launch and train. We go live, set up redirects and show your team how to edit.

Questions to ask any WordPress agency

  • Which page builder or theme will you use, and why?
  • How many plugins will the site need?
  • Who owns the hosting, domain and admin accounts?
  • How will updates be handled after launch?
  • Can my team edit pages without breaking the layout?

With us, you own the site, domain, hosting and all accounts from day one.

WooCommerce for Iowa retailers and producers

Some Des Moines businesses sell products as well as services, from specialty food producers and farm suppliers to gift shops and trade suppliers. WooCommerce adds a full online store to WordPress, which suits firms that already have a content-rich site and want to keep everything in one place. We set up products, variations, payment gateways, shipping zones and tax settings, and test the full checkout before launch. Sales tax rules vary by state, so confirm your obligations with your own accountant. If your product range is large or you expect fast growth, we will tell you honestly whether Shopify would serve you better.

Content that keeps a WordPress site useful

A WordPress site earns its keep when it is updated regularly. For professional services and insurance-related firms around Des Moines, that might mean guides that answer common client questions, updates on services, office or team news, and clear explanations of processes. We set up categories and templates so posts are easy to publish and look consistent, and we can plan a content calendar as part of an SEO retainer. Everything we write is in English, and you approve each piece before it goes live. Where content touches on regulated products, your compliance team signs it off before publication.

Accessibility and usability

Many Des Moines firms serve a broad public, including older customers and people using screen readers or keyboard navigation. We build with accessibility basics in mind: good color contrast, readable font sizes, descriptive link text, alt text on images, logical heading order and forms that work with assistive technology. These steps also tend to improve search performance and conversion rates, because they make pages clearer for everyone. We can point you to official accessibility guidance, and for formal compliance requirements you should take advice from your own legal adviser.
