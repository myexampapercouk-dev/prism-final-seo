Google Ads Agency Dubai: Choosing a PPC Partner for the UAE

Hiring a Google Ads agency in Dubai: what good PPC management covers, Arabic and English campaigns, questions to ask and our published monthly fees.

  • Ask how the agency handles location targeting, negative keywords, reporting and account ownership, and avoid anyone who promises fixed results.

Why Google Ads works differently in Dubai

Dubai is a fast-moving, highly competitive market with a large international population, heavy mobile use and strong demand in sectors such as property, hospitality, retail, healthcare, education and professional services. Several things affect how a campaign should be built:

  • Multilingual searchers. People search in English, in Arabic and sometimes in other languages. Google targets ads by the user's language settings, so separate campaigns or ad groups per language are usually clearer than mixing them.
  • Emirate-level targeting. A business serving only Dubai may waste money showing ads across the whole UAE. Equally, a delivery business may want all seven emirates. Our guide to Google Ads location targeting explains the settings that matter.
  • High competition in some sectors. Popular categories can have expensive clicks, so tight keyword selection and strong landing pages matter more than raw budget.
  • Working week and timing. The UAE working week and seasonal patterns differ from the UK. Ad schedules and budgets should follow when your customers actually search and buy.

Tracking comes first

Without reliable conversion tracking, Google's automated bidding has nothing useful to learn from, and you cannot tell which keywords make money. Before launching or taking over a campaign, we check that form submissions, calls, messaging clicks and online purchases are recorded properly. Our conversion tracking setup guide explains the steps, and Google Tag Manager usually makes this easier to manage.

Many Dubai customers prefer to message rather than fill in a form. If WhatsApp or click-to-call is your main enquiry route, it should be tracked as a conversion too, otherwise your campaigns will look weaker than they really are.

Search, Shopping and Performance Max

Google offers several campaign types, and the right mix depends on what you sell.

  • Search campaigns capture people actively looking for a service, such as a clinic, a contractor or a consultancy. They are usually the starting point for service businesses.
  • Shopping campaigns show products with images and prices, and suit online stores with a clean product feed in Google Merchant Center.
  • Performance Max spreads budget across Google's networks using automation. It can work well with good tracking and good assets, but it needs watching closely. Our Performance Max guide covers the trade-offs.

For most new accounts we start with tightly focused Search campaigns, prove what converts and then expand.

Arabic and English campaigns

Running ads in both languages can widen your reach, but only if each version is written well. We build the campaign structure, keyword lists and English ad copy, and we work with your team or a translator on the Arabic ads and landing pages. Each language gets its own ad groups and its own reporting so you can see which audience brings better results. If your site is not yet bilingual, our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website explains what to plan before sending Arabic traffic to it.

Common Google Ads mistakes in UAE accounts

When we review accounts, the same problems come up again and again. Most are easy to fix once they are spotted, and fixing them often matters more than raising the budget.

  • Location set to "presence or interest". This default can show ads to people outside the UAE who are merely interested in it. For most local businesses, targeting people who are in or regularly in your area is safer.
  • No negative keywords. Searches for jobs, free downloads or unrelated services quietly drain budget. Our negative keywords guide explains how to build a list.
  • One campaign for everything. Mixing services, languages and emirates in a single campaign makes it impossible to see what is working.
  • Automated bidding without data. Smart bidding needs conversions to learn from. Without them it guesses, often expensively.
  • Weak landing pages. Slow pages, unclear offers and long forms waste clicks you have already paid for.

How to choose a Google Ads agency in Dubai

  1. Ask for an audit or plan before you sign. A good agency can explain what it would change in your account and why. Our PPC audit checklist shows the kind of checks to expect.
  2. Check who owns the account. It should be yours, so your data stays with you if you move on.
  3. Ask how fees work. Understand whether you pay a fixed fee, a percentage of spend or both, and whether ad spend is billed separately.
  4. Ask about Arabic copy. Who writes it, and who checks it?
  5. Ask how often search terms are reviewed. Weekly or fortnightly reviews stop wasted spend building up.
  6. Be wary of guarantees. No agency can promise a fixed number of leads or a set return.

Remote management from the UK

We work with Dubai businesses through video calls, shared dashboards and written monthly reports. The UAE is a few hours ahead of the UK, which leaves a comfortable overlap in the working day. We do not offer in-person meetings in Dubai, so if face-to-face contact is essential to you, a local agency may suit you better.

A note on ad policies and regulations

Google's advertising policies vary by country and by sector, and some categories such as healthcare, financial services, property and alcohol carry extra restrictions or certification requirements. UAE rules on advertising and data protection also apply. Check current official guidance and Google's policy pages for your sector, and take local advice where needed; approval of regulated claims rests with your business.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Dubai?

Ask for a clear plan or audit, confirm you will own the account, understand the fee structure and ask who writes the Arabic ads. Avoid agencies that guarantee results or report only clicks.

Do you have an office in Dubai?

No. We are a UK-based studio and manage campaigns for UAE businesses remotely, using video calls, shared reports and email.

How much should I spend on Google Ads in Dubai?

It depends on your sector, competition and goals. We recommend starting with a budget you can sustain for several months so campaigns have enough data to learn from. Ad spend is paid separately from our management fee.

Can you run Google Ads in Arabic?

Yes. We build the campaigns and English ads, and the Arabic ad copy is written or reviewed by your team or a translator so it reads naturally.

Should I target Dubai only or the whole UAE?

Target where you can genuinely serve customers. A clinic or restaurant usually targets a tight area, while an online store delivering nationwide may target all emirates with separate reporting.

How long until Google Ads brings results?

Ads can bring clicks within days, but campaigns typically need three to six months of testing keywords, ads and landing pages to reach consistent, efficient performance.
