Why Google Ads suits Brussels businesses

Brussels is the capital of Belgium and home to the main EU institutions, along with a large community of associations, consultancies, law firms and international organisations. That creates a market with plenty of high-value searches, but also strong competition for many professional and B2B terms.

Google Ads works particularly well for Brussels businesses because it captures demand at the moment of intent. The sectors that tend to benefit most include:

  • Professional and B2B services. Consultancies, public affairs firms, translation agencies, IT providers and specialist suppliers.
  • Local services. Trades, clinics, studios and repair businesses serving particular communes or the wider Brussels region.
  • E-commerce. Online shops selling across Belgium, often alongside the Netherlands or France.
  • Education and training. Language schools, executive education and course providers.
  • Hospitality. Hotels, restaurants and event venues looking for direct bookings.

Planning for French, Dutch and English

Language is the defining feature of Google Ads in Brussels. The city is officially bilingual, and many searches happen in French, Dutch or English depending on the audience. Belgium as a whole adds Flanders, where Dutch dominates, and Wallonia, where French dominates, along with a small German-speaking community in the east.

We usually structure accounts so each language has its own campaigns or ad groups, with keywords, ads and landing pages that match. That keeps budgets and reporting clear and stops a French-language ad pointing to a Dutch page. Language targeting in Google Ads is based on signals about the user, so the keyword language and the landing page language both matter.

Location targeting also needs care. You might target the Brussels region only, a radius around your premises, or the whole of Belgium. Our guide to Google Ads location targeting explains the options, including the difference between people in a location and people interested in it.

If you also advertise in the Netherlands, campaigns are best kept separate by country, because Belgian Dutch and Netherlands Dutch differ in vocabulary and tone. Our page on choosing a Google Ads agency in Amsterdam covers that market.

Landing pages that convert

Most wasted ad spend comes from sending clicks to the wrong page. Each campaign should lead to a page in the same language as the ad, with the same offer and a clear next step. Our guide to landing page design sets out what makes a page work, and the principles apply equally to Belgian sites.

For Belgian e-commerce, familiar payment methods such as Bancontact on the checkout page can make a real difference to trust. If your shop runs on Shopify, our post on Shopify for Belgian businesses covers the platform side, and our page on web design for Brussels businesses covers new sites and redesigns.

Tracking, consent and compliance

Google Ads optimises on conversion data, so tracking has to be right before budgets increase. That means conversion actions for enquiries, calls, bookings or purchases, ideally with enhanced conversions where appropriate. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking explains the setup.

In Belgium, as across the EU, tracking must work alongside consent. The notes below are general information only. Please follow official guidance and take advice from your own legal adviser.

  • GDPR. Lead forms, call tracking and analytics involve personal data. You need a lawful basis, a clear privacy notice and sensible retention. Belgium's data protection authority publishes guidance in French and Dutch.
  • Cookie consent. Under the ePrivacy rules, as applied in Belgian law, advertising and analytics cookies generally need prior consent, and rejecting should be as easy as accepting. Google also expects advertisers in the European Economic Area to pass consent signals, typically through consent mode. Our article on cookie banner design covers the practical side.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer services, including online shops. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt. The expected standard is EN 301 549, which points to WCAG at level AA. Our overview of the European Accessibility Act for websites explains what it means for landing pages and checkouts.

Regulated sectors such as finance, health and legal services may have extra advertising rules. We provide general guidance only, and approval of ad copy rests with your own compliance team or adviser.

Questions to ask any PPC agency

  • How will you structure campaigns for French and Dutch searchers?
  • Who writes and checks ad copy in each language?
  • How will conversions be tracked with consent in place?
  • Will I own the account, and can I see it at any time?
  • How often will you review search terms and add negatives?

Clear answers to those questions usually tell you more about an agency than any sales presentation. A good partner should also be happy to explain what they would not spend money on, such as broad keywords that attract job seekers or searches for free services.

FAQs

Do you have an office in Brussels?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely. We have no office in Brussels or elsewhere in Belgium, and we work with Belgian clients by video call, email and shared documents.

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Brussels?

Look for a clear plan for French and Dutch campaigns, reliable consent-aware tracking, regular search term reviews, honest timelines and reporting tied to enquiries or sales. Make sure you own the ad account.

Should we run Google Ads in French and Dutch?

For most Brussels businesses, yes. Separate campaigns or ad groups for each language, with matching landing pages, keep budgets clear and ads relevant to each searcher.

Can you write ads in French and Dutch?

We work in English. We plan campaigns and write copy briefs, and the French and Dutch ads are written or checked by your team or a professional translator.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, quoted in pounds, with euro quotes on request. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate.

Does cookie consent affect Google Ads results?

It can reduce the conversion data Google receives. Consent mode and enhanced conversions help, and the setup should follow official guidance and your own legal advice.
