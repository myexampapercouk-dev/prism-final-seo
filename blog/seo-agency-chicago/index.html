SEO Agency Chicago: How Remote SEO Works for Chicago Businesses

Looking for an SEO agency in Chicago? Remote SEO can cover local, national and B2B search for Chicago firms. Here is how it works, the cost and what to ask.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • An SEO agency for a Chicago business does not need a Loop office; it needs US search data, a clear plan and reporting tied to enquiries.
  • Local rankings depend on your own address, Google Business Profile and reviews, not on where your SEO agency is based.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US clients, with retainers priced in pounds and other currencies quoted on request.

If you are searching for an SEO agency in Chicago, what matters most is whether the agency can grow the searches that bring you real customers, not whether it has a desk downtown. Remote SEO can cover local, national and B2B search for Chicago businesses, provided the work is built on US search data and reported against enquiries and sales.

We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US businesses. We do not have a Chicago office or any US office. This guide explains what SEO for a Chicago business should include, how a remote arrangement runs day to day, what it costs and the questions to ask any agency before you sign.

What an SEO agency in Chicago should cover

Chicago has a broad business mix: professional services, manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, hospitality, trades and a growing number of ecommerce and software firms. That variety means SEO needs differ a lot from one company to the next. A sound SEO programme usually covers:

  • Technical health: crawlability, site speed, indexing and structured data, so search engines can read your site properly.
  • Keyword and intent research: finding the US searches your buyers actually use, including longer, more specific phrases.
  • On-page work: titles, headings, service pages and internal links aligned to those searches.
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile, location pages and consistent business details where you serve customers in person.
  • Content: guides, comparisons and answers that help buyers decide.
  • Authority: earning genuine links and mentions from relevant sites.
  • Reporting: Search Console and analytics data connected to leads, calls and revenue.

Search is also changing. AI-generated answers now sit above many results, so content has to be clear and well structured enough to be quoted. Our guide to generative engine optimisation explains what that means in practice.

Local SEO for Chicago businesses

If customers visit you, or you travel to them, local search is likely to be a big part of your strategy. People in Chicago often search by neighbourhood or suburb as well as by city, so a searcher in Lincoln Park or Evanston may see different results from one in the Loop. Local rankings are driven by your own location, your profile, your reviews and how relevant your pages are to the search. They are not affected by where your agency is based.

Points worth planning for include:

  • Google Business Profile: correct categories, services, photos and regular updates.
  • Location pages: one strong page for each real location, rather than thin pages for every suburb.
  • Service areas: clear descriptions of where you work if you visit customers.
  • Reviews: a steady, rule-compliant way of asking happy customers for reviews.
  • Consistent listings: the same name, address and phone number everywhere your business appears.

Our guides to Google Business Profile and multi-location SEO explain the principles, which work the same way for US businesses.

B2B SEO for Chicago companies

A large share of Chicago firms sell to other businesses. B2B SEO works differently from consumer SEO: search volumes are smaller, buying cycles are longer and several people are often involved in a decision. Good B2B SEO focuses on specific problem and solution searches, detailed service and industry pages, and content that helps a buyer justify the decision internally.

Our guide to B2B SEO covers keyword research, page structure and measurement for longer sales cycles, and our B2B sector page explains how we approach marketing for business-to-business firms.

Choosing which searches to target first

One of the most useful things an SEO agency does early on is decide where to focus. Chasing the biggest city-wide searches straight away is rarely the best use of a budget, because those results are usually dominated by large, long-established sites. A more practical order is:

  1. Fix what is broken: technical problems that stop existing pages ranking as well as they should.
  2. Strengthen pages that already rank: pages sitting on the second or third page of results often move with better content and internal links.
  3. Target specific, high-intent searches: a service plus a neighbourhood, an industry or a clear problem tends to convert better than a broad phrase.
  4. Build towards broader terms: once the site has more authority and useful content, harder searches become realistic.

This order keeps the early months focused on searches that can bring enquiries, while still building the foundations for more competitive Chicago terms later. A good agency will explain these choices in plain language and revisit them as data comes in.

How remote SEO works day to day

Almost all SEO work happens in tools: Search Console, analytics, your content management system and shared documents. That makes it well suited to remote delivery. A typical engagement with us looks like this:

  1. Discovery: a video call to understand your services, customers, competitors and goals.
  2. Access: you grant user access to Search Console, analytics and your website, rather than sharing passwords.
  3. Audit and plan: a technical and content review, followed by a prioritised plan for the first few months.
  4. Delivery: technical fixes, page improvements, new content and local work, carried out in agreed monthly cycles.
  5. Reporting: a monthly written report and a short call to agree priorities for the next month.

Chicago is usually six hours behind the UK. Our afternoon overlaps with your morning, so regular calls are easy to arrange, and work requested at the end of your day is often under way before you are back at your desk. Our guide to SEO reporting shows what a useful monthly report should contain.

Questions to ask any Chicago SEO agency

Whether you hire locally or remotely, these questions help you separate a careful agency from a risky one:

  • Who will actually do the work each month, and what is their experience with US search?
  • What will you deliver in the first ninety days, in specific terms?
  • How do you build links, and will you show us every one?
  • Who owns the content, accounts and data if we stop working together?
  • What does the contract say about notice periods and minimum terms?

Be wary of anyone who guarantees first-place rankings or a set number of leads. Search engines do not allow anyone to guarantee positions, and honest agencies will explain likely ranges and the work involved instead.
