SEO Agency for Jordan: Ranking Your Business in Amman and Beyond

SEO for Jordan businesses: how we research Arabic and English keywords, fix technical issues and build content remotely from the UK, plus timelines and prices.

  • We are a UK-based studio that runs SEO remotely for Jordanian businesses, with no office in Jordan, and Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator.
  • SEO retainers start at £350 per month, with Growth at £750 and Scale at £1,500; results typically build over 3 to 6 months, and other currencies are quoted on request.

An SEO agency for Jordan helps a Jordanian business appear in Google when customers search for what it sells, in Arabic, in English or in a mix of both. Good SEO for the Jordanian market combines careful keyword research, a technically sound website, useful content and a steady, measurable plan that builds visibility over months rather than days.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We provide SEO through our SEO and GEO service and work remotely with businesses in Jordan and across the Middle East. We have no office in Jordan. This guide explains how we approach SEO for Jordanian businesses, what the work involves and what it costs.

Why SEO matters for Jordanian businesses

When people in Amman, Irbid, Zarqa or Aqaba need a clinic, a training course, a contractor, a software supplier or a product, most of them start with a search on their phone. Businesses that appear near the top of those results win a steady flow of enquiries without paying for every click. Businesses that do not appear rely on referrals, social media and advertising, which can be harder to scale.

Jordan also has many companies that sell beyond its borders: technology firms, outsourcing providers, exporters, tourism operators and education providers. For them, SEO is often about reaching buyers in the Gulf, Europe or North America in English while still serving local customers in Arabic. A good SEO plan reflects both audiences from the start.

SEO agency Jordan: what our work includes

Every SEO project is different, but most of our work for Jordanian businesses falls into five areas. We agree priorities at the start so the budget goes where it will make the most difference.

  1. Keyword research. We find the searches your customers use in Arabic and English, including dialect spellings, transliterations and the English terms many Jordanians search with.
  2. Technical SEO. We check how search engines crawl and index your site, fix broken pages, slow templates, duplicate content and missing language tags.
  3. On-page optimisation. We improve page titles, headings, descriptions, internal links and structured data so each page clearly matches a search intent.
  4. Content. We plan and write service pages, guides and FAQs that answer real questions, in a structure that also suits AI search tools.
  5. Reporting. We track rankings, traffic and enquiries each month and explain what changed and what we are doing next.

Our guide to keyword research explains the method we use, and our technical SEO audit checklist shows the checks we run before any content work begins.

Arabic and English search behaviour

Search behaviour in Jordan is genuinely bilingual. Some people search in Arabic script, some in English, and some type Arabic words in Latin letters. The same customer might search for a service in Arabic on a Monday and in English on a Tuesday. If your site only targets one language, you miss a large share of the people looking for you.

We map keywords for each language separately rather than translating a single list, because direct translations often miss the words people really use. Each language version of your site should have its own URLs, its own page titles and correct language tags, so Google can show Arabic pages to Arabic searchers and English pages to English searchers. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites covers the structure in more detail.

We write English content ourselves. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend that a fluent speaker reviews every Arabic page before it goes live. We then optimise the Arabic pages for titles, headings and internal links in the same way as the English ones.

Local SEO in Amman and other cities

Many Jordanian businesses serve customers in a specific city or neighbourhood: clinics, dental practices, law firms, training centres, restaurants and trades. For them, local SEO is often the fastest win. That means a complete and accurate Google Business Profile, consistent business details across directories, location pages where they genuinely help visitors, and reviews collected honestly from real customers.

If you serve several cities, each location deserves a useful page with its own details rather than a copied template with the city name swapped. Our local SEO guide is written for UK businesses, but the principles of profiles, citations and location pages apply equally in Jordan.

Content that ranks and gets cited

Search results now include AI-generated answers alongside the familiar list of links. Content that is clear, specific and well structured has the best chance of appearing in both. We write pages that answer the main question in the first two sentences, use descriptive headings, include tables where comparisons help, and finish with practical FAQs.

We also add structured data where it is appropriate, such as organisation, service, FAQ and article markup, so search engines understand what each page is about. Our schema markup guide explains which types are useful for most businesses. We do not publish thin pages written only for search engines, because they rarely rank for long and can weaken the rest of the site.

How long SEO takes in Jordan

SEO is a medium-term investment. Technical fixes can be made in the first few weeks, but rankings and enquiries typically build over 3 to 6 months, and competitive terms can take longer. Progress depends on the state of your current site, how much content you can approve, the strength of competitors and how quickly changes are made.

If you need enquiries sooner, many businesses run paid search alongside SEO while organic visibility grows. We are happy to discuss whether that makes sense for you, but we will not promise rankings or timelines that nobody can guarantee.

When your website needs work first

Sometimes the best SEO investment is fixing the website itself. If your site is slow, hard to update, missing an Arabic or English version or built on a platform that limits SEO, we may recommend improvements before or alongside the SEO retainer. Our guide to web design for Jordan explains how we plan and build bilingual sites for Jordanian businesses.

Working with a UK SEO studio remotely

We work through video calls, shared documents and a clear monthly plan. The time difference between the UK and Jordan is small, so there is plenty of overlap in the working day for calls and quick questions. You get access to your own analytics and Search Console data, and we explain every report in plain language.

If you are weighing up an overseas provider, our article on outsourcing SEO to an overseas agency lists the questions worth asking. Businesses that also target the Gulf may find our guide to SEO in Dubai useful for comparing the two markets.

Rules and compliance

Some sectors in Jordan, such as healthcare, education and financial services, have rules about what can be said in marketing. We write content that is accurate and avoids exaggerated claims, but we are not legal advisers. Check official Jordanian guidance and ask your compliance team or adviser to approve content in regulated sectors before it is published.
