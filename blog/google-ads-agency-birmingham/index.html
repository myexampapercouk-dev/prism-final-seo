Choosing a Google Ads Agency in Birmingham: Targeting, Tracking and Fair Fees

Hiring a Google Ads agency in Birmingham? How to target the West Midlands, set up tracking, judge structure and fees, and compare remote and local options.

Why Google Ads in Birmingham needs careful targeting

Birmingham is the centre of a large and varied region. A campaign set to "Birmingham plus a radius" can easily spend money on areas you do not serve, or miss towns such as Solihull, Sutton Coldfield, West Bromwich, Walsall, Dudley or Wolverhampton that you do. Search behaviour differs too: some people search with their town name, some with "near me" and some with no location at all.

Many Birmingham businesses also sell beyond the region. Manufacturers, distributors, B2B service firms and online retailers based in the West Midlands often compete for searches across the UK. For them, the challenge is not local targeting but controlling cost in a crowded national auction.

A good agency starts by working out which situation you are in, then builds the account around it.

What to check when hiring a Google Ads agency in Birmingham

Conversion tracking. Without accurate tracking of calls, forms, bookings or sales, an account cannot be optimised properly. Ask how the agency would set up and test tracking, and whether it uses Google Analytics 4 alongside Google Ads. Our GA4 setup guide covers the basics.

Account ownership. The Google Ads account should belong to your business, with the agency given access. If you part ways, you keep the history, data and settings.

Location settings. Ask how the agency would choose target areas and whether it would target people located in those areas rather than people merely interested in them. Small settings like this can make a big difference to wasted spend.

Search term reviews. Broad matching can show your ads for searches that have nothing to do with your business. Ask how often the agency reviews search terms and adds negative keywords.

Landing pages. Ads can only do so much if the page they lead to is slow, vague or hard to use on a phone. Ask whether the agency will review your landing pages, and read our landing page design guide for what makes one work.

Reporting. Ask for a sample report. It should show spend, conversions, cost per conversion and the changes made, not just clicks and impressions.

Common Google Ads mistakes in West Midlands accounts

When businesses bring an existing account for review, the same problems tend to appear. None of them is unique to Birmingham, but they waste money in any competitive regional market.

  • Targeting too wide an area. A large radius around the city centre can include towns you never travel to, while ads still show to people who are only interested in the area rather than located there.
  • One campaign for every service. Mixing high-value and low-value services in one campaign makes it hard to control spend where it matters most.
  • Tracking clicks instead of enquiries. If the account counts page visits or button clicks as conversions, automated bidding will optimise for the wrong thing.
  • No negative keywords. Searches for jobs, training, free advice or DIY tips can quietly eat budget.
  • Sending every ad to the home page. A specific landing page for each main service usually converts better.
  • Ignoring past visitors. People often compare several firms before enquiring. A modest remarketing campaign can bring them back, as our remarketing guide explains.

A good agency will spot issues like these in an initial review and explain which ones to fix first.

Questions to ask in a first call

  1. What would you check first in our current account or tracking?
  2. Which West Midlands areas would you target, and how would you decide?
  3. How often will you review search terms and adjust bids or budgets?
  4. Will we own the account, and what access will you need?
  5. How do you report cost per enquiry or sale?
  6. What is the notice period, and is there a minimum term?

Be wary of agencies that promise a fixed number of leads before seeing your account, insist on owning the account, or will not explain how their fee is calculated.

Getting the most from your first three months

The early months of a Google Ads account are about learning. Expect the agency to test ad copy, adjust bids and budgets, tighten targeting and add negative keywords as search term data comes in. Results often look uneven at first and become steadier as the account settles.

You can speed this up by sharing feedback on lead quality. If certain enquiries are poor, or some towns are not worth the travel, tell your agency quickly so it can adjust. A short call each month to review results together is usually enough.

It also helps to agree a clear target before launch, such as an acceptable cost per enquiry or a target return on ad spend. That gives everyone a shared measure and makes budget decisions easier.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Birmingham?

Look for an agency that checks your tracking first, leaves you owning the account, explains its targeting and reports on cost per enquiry or sale. Compare plans for your business, not general promises.
