Who this guide is for

Orlando and Central Florida have a business mix shaped by tourism, but far broader than theme parks. Hospitality, attractions, short-term rentals, home services, healthcare, education, simulation and technology firms, real estate and a fast-growing population of residents all create search demand. Google Ads tends to suit:

  • Hospitality and tourism businesses such as hotels, tour operators, restaurants and activity providers targeting visitors before and during their trip.
  • Home services such as HVAC, pool care, roofing, pest control and plumbing across Orlando, Kissimmee, Winter Park, Sanford and Lake Nona.
  • Clinics and professional services that need a steady flow of local enquiries.
  • Online retailers selling across Florida or nationally through Shopping campaigns.
  • B2B and technology companies targeting specific buyers searching for niche solutions.

How a Google Ads agency in Orlando should manage your account

Every account is different, but strong management always covers the same foundations.

Conversion tracking first. Before spending on ads, we make sure calls, form submissions, bookings and purchases are recorded correctly. Our conversion tracking setup guide explains what we check. Without accurate tracking, automated bidding learns from the wrong signals.

Keyword and search intent research. We separate high-intent searches, such as an emergency repair or a booking query, from research searches, and bid accordingly. We build negative keyword lists early to keep spend away from irrelevant queries; our negative keywords guide covers the approach.

Campaign structure. We group services, products or destinations into focused campaigns so budgets go where they earn the most, and so reporting is easy to read.

Ads and assets. We write responsive search ads and add sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call assets and location assets. We do not film or edit video; if you run YouTube or Performance Max video assets, the footage comes from your own team or a video producer, and we handle the targeting and campaign setup around it.

Landing pages. Ads only work if the page they lead to converts. We review your landing pages and, where needed, design new ones built for a single goal.

Ongoing optimisation. Each week we review search terms, bids, budgets, ad performance and conversion data, then make changes and record what we did and why.

Location targeting for visitors and residents

Orlando is unusual because many valuable searchers are not in Orlando yet. A family planning a holiday may search from another state or another country weeks before they arrive. A homeowner needing an urgent repair, on the other hand, searches from inside your service area and wants an answer immediately.

That means location settings matter more than usual. For local services, we target people in or regularly in your service area and exclude places you do not cover. For hospitality and attractions, we may target people searching about Orlando from elsewhere, with ads and landing pages that speak to trip planning. Our location targeting guide explains the settings in more detail.

Seasonality also matters. Visitor demand rises around school holidays and major events, while some home services peak in the hottest months or during storm season. We adjust budgets and bids through the year rather than leaving the account on a fixed setting.

Common Google Ads mistakes we see in Central Florida

When we audit accounts for service and hospitality businesses, the same problems appear often. Most are simple to fix once they are spotted.

  • Broad match with no negatives. Budgets drift onto job searches, free information queries or services the business does not offer.
  • Presence or interest targeting left on by mistake. A local plumber ends up paying for clicks from people in other states who only searched about Orlando.
  • Every service in one ad group. Ads become generic, relevance drops and costs rise.
  • Calls not tracked. For trades and clinics, the phone is often the main conversion, so missing call tracking hides the best-performing keywords.
  • Ads running when nobody answers. Call-focused ads should follow opening hours, or calls should route to someone who can respond.
  • Sending every click to the home page. Dedicated pages for each main service usually convert better than a general home page.

Privacy, accessibility and advertising policy notes

These notes are general. Please check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

Privacy. Conversion tracking and remarketing use personal data. US privacy laws vary by state, so we can set up consent tools and tag management, but your privacy policy and compliance decisions rest with you.

Accessibility. Landing pages should be usable by everyone. We design with clear headings, readable contrast and labelled forms, but questions about ADA obligations should go to a qualified adviser.

Regulated sectors. Healthcare, financial services and some other categories have extra Google Ads policies and legal rules. We work within Google's policies, and approval of ad wording rests with your compliance team.
