Web design in Toulouse should give your business a fast, clear and trustworthy website that works for French customers and for the international partners and buyers many Toulouse firms deal with. That means good structure, mobile-first design, accessibility, sensible language versions and a site that is ready for search from launch.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Toulouse or anywhere in France. We design and build websites for businesses that are comfortable working through video calls, shared design files and written updates. Why Toulouse businesses are investing in better websites

Toulouse is best known for aerospace and space, with a large network of engineering firms, suppliers and research centres. The city also has a strong university population, a growing technology and start-up scene, healthcare and life sciences, and a busy hospitality and tourism sector. Many Toulouse businesses sell to customers and partners across Europe and beyond.

For these businesses, the website is often the first serious impression. Engineering buyers check capabilities and certifications listed by the client, students and visitors look for clear information, and local customers compare service businesses quickly on their phones. A slow or dated website makes all of this harder. If you also work in other French cities, our guides to SEO in Lyon and hotel marketing in Nice may be useful.

Web design in Toulouse for B2B and engineering firms

Many Toulouse businesses sell to other businesses. For these firms, the website needs to explain capabilities clearly, show the sectors served, list quality standards the client holds and make it easy for buyers to request a quote or start a conversation. Long sales cycles mean the site must support several visits from different people in a buying team.

We design B2B sites around clear service pages, capability summaries and simple enquiry routes. Our B2B sector page explains our approach, and our technical SEO checklist shows how we make sure the site is easy for search engines to read.

Multilingual websites done properly

Most Toulouse businesses need a French site, and many also need English for international partners. Each language version should have its own URLs, proper language signals and natural, accurate copy. Automatic translation widgets rarely work well for search or for readers.

We build the structure and write in English, and your team or a translator provides French copy. If you sell online to several EU countries, our guide to Shopify Markets for Europe explains how to handle languages, currencies and shipping.

Accessibility and the European Accessibility Act

Accessibility is now a legal question as well as a design one for many businesses selling to EU consumers. Our guide to the European Accessibility Act explains the general picture. We design with accessibility in mind from the start, but legal obligations depend on your business, so we recommend checking official guidance and your own adviser.

WordPress, Shopify or a custom build

We build on WordPress for content-led sites that need flexible pages and a familiar editor, and on Shopify for online shops. For simple, fast brochure sites, a lightweight custom build can also work well. The right choice depends on what you sell, who will update the site and how much it needs to grow.

Legal points for French websites

French websites usually need legal notices, privacy information and cookie consent that meets local rules. Ecommerce sites have extra requirements on prices, delivery and returns. We keep our advice general and recommend official guidance and your own legal adviser. Compliance responsibility stays with your business.

Planning content before design starts

One of the most common reasons website projects run late is missing content. Before design begins, we agree which pages are needed, what each page should say and who will provide text, images and translations. For a typical Toulouse business, that means a homepage, service or product pages, an about page, a contact page and any sector or case pages the client wants to include.

We provide a content plan and page outlines in English, so your team knows exactly what to write or translate. Having content ready early helps keep the project within the expected timeline and makes the design more accurate, because layouts can be built around real text rather than placeholder copy.

Questions to ask a web design agency

Before you choose any web design partner, ask how they handle languages, who owns the finished website and domain, which platform they recommend and why, how updates will work after launch and what is included in the price. You should also ask how they approach accessibility and site speed, and whether SEO foundations are part of the build or an extra.

With us, you own your website, domain and content. We explain platform choices clearly, include SEO foundations in every build and make sure your team can update key pages without needing a developer for every change. If you need help after launch, we offer ongoing SEO, paid media and website support on a monthly retainer.

After launch: keeping the site working

A website is not finished on launch day. Content needs updating, plugins and apps need maintenance, and search performance should be checked regularly. We recommend reviewing key pages every few months, keeping contact details and services current, and watching Search Console for errors. Small, regular improvements keep the site useful for visitors and strong in search over time.

Good partners also explain trade-offs openly. Not every fix is worth doing at once, and budgets are rarely unlimited, so a clear plan should show what will be done first, what can wait and why. That honesty makes it easier for your team to plan time for approvals, translations and content, and keeps the project moving at a steady, realistic pace.
