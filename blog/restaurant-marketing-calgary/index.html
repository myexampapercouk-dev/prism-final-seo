A restaurant website that answers the basics fast

Most people visit a restaurant website for four things: the menu, the hours, the address and a way to book or order. If any of those are slow or hard to find, they go elsewhere. We design restaurant sites around those tasks first.

  • Menus as real web pages, not only PDFs, so they load quickly on phones and can be read by search engines.
  • Clear booking and ordering buttons that link to the booking or ordering system you already use.
  • Location and parking details, with a map and notes on transit or nearby parking.
  • Private dining and events pages with a simple enquiry form for larger bookings.
  • Dietary information such as vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, supplied and checked by your kitchen team.

A smaller restaurant website usually takes three to five weeks once photos and menu text are ready. Our guide to web design in Calgary covers build options in more detail.

Local SEO and Google Maps

For restaurants, the Google Maps listing often matters more than the website. When someone searches for ramen near me or brunch in Inglewood, the map results appear first. We help you improve that listing and the signals behind it:

  • Complete Google Business Profile details, including categories, hours, holiday hours, menu links and attributes such as patio seating.
  • Regular photo updates and posts about specials, events and seasonal menus.
  • A simple, honest approach to asking happy guests for reviews, and help drafting polite replies to reviews.
  • Consistent name, address and phone details across directories and booking platforms.
  • Location pages on your website for each venue if you run more than one.

Local SEO results typically take three to six months to show clearly. Our guides to SEO in Calgary and local SEO in Canada explain the process further.

Google Ads for hungry searchers

Google Ads can put your restaurant at the top of results when people search with clear intent, such as private dining Calgary, catering for an office lunch or a cuisine near a specific area. We keep restaurant campaigns tight: a small set of keywords, a sensible radius and ad text that mentions what makes you different.

Catering and private event campaigns often give the clearest return, because each enquiry can be worth a lot. We track calls, form fills and clicks on booking buttons so you can see what is working. Our article on Google Ads in Calgary covers budgets and structure.

Meta, Instagram and TikTok Ads

Food sells on looks. Meta and Instagram ads let you show dishes, drinks and atmosphere to people who live or work near you. We use them to promote new menus, events, patio season, holiday bookings and gift cards. Remarketing ads can remind people who visited your site but did not book.

TikTok and Instagram Reels can also work well, using short clips your team films in the kitchen or dining room. We do not film or edit video, but we can advise on what to capture and run the paid campaigns around your clips. See our guide to Meta Ads in Calgary and our overview of TikTok Ads in Canada.

Email, privacy and consent

Email and text offers can bring regulars back, but they come with rules. In general terms, CASL covers commercial electronic messages in Canada and usually requires consent, clear sender details and a working unsubscribe link. PIPEDA sets federal rules for how businesses handle personal information, and Alberta has its own private-sector privacy law that may apply to some businesses.

These notes are general information only. Please check official guidance and speak to your own adviser. We build sign-up forms, consent checkboxes and privacy page layouts using wording you approve, and final responsibility rests with you. Our guide to email marketing in Canada covers this in more depth.

Using AI-powered tools sensibly

We use AI-powered tools to speed up research and reporting, such as grouping search terms, drafting ad variations for your review and summarising review themes so you can see what guests mention most. Every piece of copy is checked by a person, and anything about allergens, prices or opening hours is confirmed by your team before it goes live.

Common mistakes we fix

Many restaurant websites lose guests for small, avoidable reasons. Hours on the website do not match hours on Google. The menu is a large PDF that takes too long to open on a phone. The booking button is buried at the bottom of the page. Holiday closures are never updated. Photos are dark or years out of date. None of these needs a big budget to fix, and fixing them often helps more than a new ad campaign.

We start most restaurant projects with a short review of your website, listing and social profiles, then give you a plain list of fixes in order of impact. Some you can handle yourselves; others we take on as part of a build or retainer.
