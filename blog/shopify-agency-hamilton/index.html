Why Hamilton brands choose Shopify

Hamilton has a strong mix of makers, independent retailers, food and drink producers and growing direct-to-consumer brands. Many sit close to the Toronto market while keeping lower costs, and many sell both in person and online. Shopify suits this well: it handles payments, inventory, shipping and point of sale in one system, and it scales from a first online shop to a larger catalogue selling across Canada and into the United States.

The platform is straightforward to start, but a store that actually sells needs more than a theme and a few products. Catalogue structure, product pages, shipping rules, tax settings, apps and speed all affect whether visitors buy. That is where a specialist build pays off.

What our Shopify service includes

Our website and ecommerce development service covers the full Shopify project:

  • Discovery and planning: we map your products, collections, customer types and the information buyers need before they purchase.
  • Theme selection and design: we start from a well-supported Online Store 2.0 theme and customise it to your brand, rather than building from scratch without reason.
  • Product and collection setup: clear titles, variants, metafields for specifications and a collection structure that helps both shoppers and search engines.
  • Canadian settings: currency, shipping zones and rates, and tax settings configured with you. Shopify can calculate GST, HST and PST, but you should confirm your registration and obligations with your accountant.
  • Apps: only the apps you need, such as reviews, subscriptions or bundles, chosen with speed in mind.
  • Speed and accessibility: compressed images, lean code and sensible layouts that work on phones.
  • Training and handover: a walkthrough so your team can add products, run discounts and manage orders.

Migrating to Shopify without losing rankings

If you already sell online, moving platforms needs care. Product URLs change when you move to Shopify, so every old URL needs a redirect to its new equivalent. Product descriptions, images, reviews and customer accounts all need to come across cleanly. We plan the migration, test it on a development copy and only switch over once it has been checked.

Our guides to moving from WooCommerce to Shopify and moving from Etsy to Shopify explain the main steps. For larger operations, our Shopify Plus migration guide covers what changes on the higher plan.

Shipping across Canada and into the United States

Hamilton is well placed for shipping. Ontario customers can often receive parcels within a day or two, and the border is close for brands that want to sell into the United States. Even so, shipping is where many Canadian stores lose sales. Customers in British Columbia, the Prairies or Atlantic Canada may face higher rates and longer delivery times, and a surprise charge at checkout is one of the most common reasons people abandon a cart.

We work with you to set shipping zones and rates that are clear and fair. That might mean flat rates by region, a free shipping threshold that protects your margin, or calculated rates from your carrier account. We show delivery information on product pages and in the cart, not only at checkout. For brands selling into the United States, Shopify Markets lets you show prices in US dollars and manage duties and import settings, though you should confirm cross-border tax and customs rules with your own adviser.

Returns matter too. A simple, visible returns policy builds trust, especially for clothing and homeware. We add clear policy pages and link them from product pages so shoppers can find answers before they buy rather than emailing you afterwards.

How we run a remote Shopify project

Every project follows the same simple steps:

  1. Discovery call: we learn about your products, customers, current setup and goals.
  2. Proposal: a fixed price, scope and timeline in writing.
  3. Planning: sitemap, collection structure and content list.
  4. Design and build: we work on your store with a staff account and share progress at agreed milestones.
  5. Content and testing: products loaded, checkout tested, shipping and tax checked with you.
  6. Launch and training: we go live, check redirects and train your team.

Because we work in writing and on video, you always have a record of what was agreed. We write in English; if you need French product content for customers in Quebec, it should come from your team or a translator, and we can set up Shopify Markets and translation apps to support it.

Common Shopify problems we fix

  • Too many apps: a slow store weighed down by apps that are no longer used.
  • Messy collections: products hard to find and duplicate content across collections.
  • Thin product pages: missing sizes, materials, care details or delivery information.
  • Shipping surprises: rates that only appear at checkout and cause abandoned carts.
  • Broken redirects: old URLs leading to error pages after a migration.
