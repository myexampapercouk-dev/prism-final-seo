TikTok Ads Agency for Saudi Arabia: Planning Campaigns for Riyadh, Jeddah and Beyond

Hiring a TikTok Ads agency for Saudi Arabia: creative that works, Arabic content, tracking, budgets, questions to ask and what our management retainers cost.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • TikTok campaigns in Saudi Arabia succeed or fail on creative: native-looking, Arabic-first video that is refreshed often, backed by working tracking.
  • A good agency sets up the pixel and events first, tests several creative angles and reports on purchases or leads rather than views.
  • Our management retainers are Launch £350, Growth £750 and Scale £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately and other currencies quoted on request.

A TikTok Ads agency for a Saudi Arabia business should plan, produce or brief, launch and optimise video campaigns that reach the right audience in Arabic and English, then prove their value through tracked sales or leads. On TikTok, creative does most of the heavy lifting, so the best partner spends as much time on hooks, scripts and testing as on targeting settings.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages paid media, including TikTok Ads, remotely for businesses in Saudi Arabia and across the Gulf. We have no office or staff in the Kingdom. This guide explains how TikTok advertising works for Saudi brands, what to look for in an agency and what our management costs.

Why TikTok suits many Saudi brands

TikTok is a discovery platform. People are not searching for you; they are scrolling, and your ad has a second or two to earn attention. That makes it a strong fit for products and services that are visual, demonstrable or tied to trends: fashion, beauty, fragrance, food, gadgets, fitness, home goods, events and many consumer services.

It can also work for less obvious categories, such as education, recruitment or local services, when the content explains something useful in a natural, human way. What tends not to work is a polished TV-style advert dropped into the feed. Viewers recognise it as an ad and keep scrolling.

Creative for Saudi audiences

Creative that works in the UK or US often needs more than translation to work in Saudi Arabia. Language, humour, references, music choices and on-screen presentation should all feel natural to your audience. Your team, local creators or a trusted partner will usually be the best judge of tone, and we treat that input as essential rather than optional.

  • Open with a strong hook. The first second should show the product, the problem or the payoff.
  • Arabic-first where your audience is. Use Arabic voiceover or captions for local consumer audiences, and test English where your customers are expatriate or international.
  • Show, do not tell. Demonstrations, before and after shots, unboxings and quick explainers tend to outperform static slides.
  • Respectful and appropriate content. Imagery, music and messaging should suit local culture and platform policies. Your team should approve every ad before it runs.
  • Refresh often. TikTok creative fatigues quickly. Plan a steady pipeline of new videos rather than one big shoot.

We can brief creators, adapt your existing footage and write scripts. Where native Arabic voiceover or on-camera talent is needed, we will be clear that it should come from fluent speakers your team trusts.

Landing pages and the path to purchase

A great TikTok ad that sends people to a slow or confusing page wastes money. Pages should load fast on mobile, match the promise in the video and make the next step obvious, whether that is buying, booking or sending a message. Arabic landing pages should use proper right-to-left layouts. Our guide to landing page design covers the principles, and tagging links properly with UTM tracking helps you see TikTok's contribution in your own analytics.

For ecommerce brands, product pages, delivery information and checkout matter just as much. Our ecommerce marketing work brings ads, product pages and tracking together so you can see what each channel really produces.

How a managed TikTok account runs month to month

Once campaigns are live, the work settles into a steady rhythm. The UK is two or three hours behind Saudi Arabia depending on the season, so review calls usually fit comfortably into the Saudi afternoon, and day-to-day changes happen without you needing to be online.

  1. Weekly checks: spend pacing, cost per result, creative fatigue and any tracking issues.
  2. Creative rounds: new hooks and video variations are briefed, approved by your team and launched against the current best performers.
  3. Budget shifts: spend moves towards the ads and audiences that produce sales or qualified leads, and away from those that only produce views.
  4. Monthly report and call: what ran, what it produced, what we learned and what we will test next.

This rhythm matters more than any single setting. Accounts that keep testing new creative and acting on clear data tend to improve steadily, while accounts left running unchanged usually drift.

Running TikTok across several Gulf markets

Many Saudi brands also sell into the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman. It is usually best to prove results in one main market first, then expand with separate campaigns or ad groups for each country so budgets, creative and delivery promises can be tailored. Our guide to running ads in multiple countries covers the structure, and the same thinking applies on TikTok.

Questions to ask before hiring a TikTok Ads agency

  1. Who writes or reviews Arabic scripts and captions?
  2. How many new creatives will you test each month, and who makes them?
  3. How will you set up and check the pixel and conversion events?
  4. Will the ad account belong to our business?
  5. What will you report on, and how often?
  6. What is the contract length and notice period?

Regulation and platform policies

TikTok has its own advertising policies, and some categories, such as health, finance, alcohol-adjacent products and certain supplements, are restricted or prohibited. Saudi Arabia also has its own rules on advertising and on influencer and creator activity, which may require licensing. We keep this guidance general: check current official guidance from the relevant Saudi authorities and TikTok's own policies, and have your legal or compliance team approve claims before campaigns go live.

How do I choose a TikTok Ads agency in Saudi Arabia?

Look for an agency that sets up tracking first, plans regular creative testing, handles Arabic content with fluent reviewers and reports on sales or leads. Make sure your business owns the ad account.
