Why London Google Ads needs a different approach

In many London sectors, a single click can cost much more than the same search elsewhere in the UK, because more advertisers are bidding. That changes how campaigns should be built.

Narrow the geography. If you serve West London, there is little point paying for clicks from Croydon or Stratford. Targeting boroughs, postcode districts or a radius around your premises keeps spend where you can actually deliver.

Focus on intent. Searches that include words such as "near me", "cost", "book" or a specific service usually convert better than broad, informational terms. Strong negative keyword lists stop you paying for searches from job seekers, students or people looking for free advice.

Use time of day carefully. If your phones are only answered during office hours, ads running late at night may waste money. Ad scheduling matches spend to when you can respond.

Compete on the offer, not just the bid. Clear pricing, fast response times, reviews and specific benefits in your ads help you win clicks without always paying the most.

Tracking and consent

Good decisions depend on good data. We set up GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking so that form submissions, calls, bookings and purchases are recorded properly. Our GA4 setup guide covers the essentials.

UK GDPR and PECR require consent for non-essential cookies, including most advertising cookies. A consent banner and Google's consent mode help campaigns respect visitors' choices while keeping as much useful measurement as the rules allow. Please check official ICO guidance or your adviser for how the rules apply to your site. Some sectors, such as financial services, healthcare and legal services, also have advertising rules and Google policies of their own, and compliance approval of ads rests with you.

Landing pages decide your costs

How to choose a Google Ads agency in London

  • Make sure the ad account is in your name and you keep access if you leave.
  • Ask how conversions are tracked and how they will report cost per lead or sale.
  • Ask how often search terms and negative keywords are reviewed.
  • Check whether fees are fixed or tied to a percentage of spend.
  • Be cautious of guaranteed results or long contracts with no exit.

Common Google Ads mistakes we see in London accounts

When we audit accounts for London businesses, the same issues tend to explain most of the wasted spend:

  • Broad match without negatives. Broad keywords can work, but without strong negative lists and good conversion data they often match irrelevant searches.
  • Targeting people interested in London, not in London. The default location setting can show ads to people outside your area who have merely searched about it. Checking this setting is a quick, valuable fix.
  • Counting the wrong conversions. Treating page views or clicks on an email address as leads makes campaigns look better than they are and teaches automated bidding the wrong lessons.
  • One campaign for every service. Mixing very different services in one campaign makes it hard to control budgets and write relevant ads.
  • No time to review search terms. Search term reports show what people actually typed. Reviewing them regularly is one of the most effective habits in account management.

Fixing these basics often makes more difference than any advanced tactic, and it gives automated bidding the clean data it needs to work well.
