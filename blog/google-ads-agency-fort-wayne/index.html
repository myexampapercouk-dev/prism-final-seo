Why Google Ads suits Fort Wayne businesses

Fort Wayne is the second largest city in Indiana and a regional hub for manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, insurance and defense suppliers. Alongside those larger employers sit thousands of local businesses, from home services and auto repair to dental practices, law firms, restaurants and retailers across downtown, the north side and nearby towns such as New Haven and Huntertown.

Google Ads suits this market because it puts you in front of people at the moment they search. A homeowner with a broken furnace in January or a manufacturer needing a machining partner is ready to act. Paid search lets you appear at the top of those results straight away, while SEO builds over months.

Costs per click in mid-sized Midwest cities are often lower than in the largest metros, but they still add up quickly if campaigns are loose. The difference between a profitable account and a wasteful one usually comes down to structure and tracking rather than budget.

What a Google Ads agency in Fort Wayne should do

We manage campaigns through our paid media service. Every account starts with the same foundations.

  • Conversion tracking first. Calls, forms, bookings and purchases are tracked properly before we spend a dollar, so bidding works from real data.
  • Tight campaign structure. Keywords are grouped by service and intent, with ads written for each group.
  • Location and schedule settings. Ads show only in the areas you serve and at the times you can answer the phone.
  • Negative keywords. We block searches for jobs, free advice, DIY and services you do not offer.
  • Matching landing pages. Each ad group sends people to a page that matches what they searched for.
  • Regular optimization. We review search terms, bids, ads and budgets every week and report monthly.

Landing pages that convert

Even the best ads fail if the landing page is slow, vague or hard to use on a phone. We review your pages before launch and suggest changes. If you need new pages, we can build them, and if the whole site is holding you back, a small website rebuild costs £1,000 to £2,000 through our website development service.

A good landing page states the offer in the headline, shows the area you serve, lists the main benefits, includes trust signals your business can genuinely show and puts the phone number and form where people can see them. Our landing page design guide goes into detail.

Signs your current account is wasting money

Many Fort Wayne businesses already run Google Ads but are unsure whether it works. These warning signs suggest it is time for a review.

  1. No one can tell you how many leads came from ads last month.
  2. The search terms report shows irrelevant searches you are paying for.
  3. All keywords sit in one or two ad groups with generic ads.
  4. Ads point to the homepage rather than a matching page.
  5. Location settings include people merely interested in your area rather than located in it.
  6. Smart bidding is running with little or no conversion data.

Our PPC audit checklist walks through each of these checks so you can review your own account.

Seasonality and budgeting in northeast Indiana

Demand in Fort Wayne shifts through the year, and budgets should follow it. Heating, plumbing and roofing searches rise sharply in winter storms. Landscaping, pools, decks and home improvement searches climb in spring and summer. Retailers see peaks around the holidays, and education, training and recruitment searches follow the academic and hiring calendars.

We set budgets month by month rather than leaving a flat daily spend running all year. When demand rises, we increase budgets on the campaigns that convert best. When it falls, we pull back and shift focus to remarketing or lower-cost keywords. We also adjust ad schedules so you are not paying for clicks at times no one can answer the phone.

For B2B suppliers and manufacturers, sales cycles are longer. Here we track earlier steps such as quote requests, spec sheet downloads and calls, and we use remarketing to stay visible while buyers compare options. Reporting shows cost per lead and, where you can share it, which leads became customers, so budget decisions rest on revenue rather than clicks.

Every change is explained in your monthly report, so you always know why spend moved and what it delivered.

Before any project starts, we send a short written proposal covering scope, deliverables, timeline, price and what we need from you. Nothing begins until you have read and approved it, and any change in scope is agreed in writing first. That keeps the work predictable for both sides, whichever time zone you are in, and gives you a clear record of what was agreed if questions come up later.

FAQs

How much does a Google Ads agency in Fort Wayne cost?

Prism Works Studio charges £350, £750 or £1,500 a month for Google Ads management, depending on scope. Ad spend is paid separately to Google, and US dollar pricing is quoted on request.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Your budget should be large enough to gather useful data each month in the areas you serve. We recommend a starting budget after reviewing keyword costs and your goals on a kickoff call.

How quickly will Google Ads bring enquiries?

Ads can bring enquiries within days of launch, but campaigns usually need several weeks of data to optimize well. Stronger, steadier results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

No, Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely with no office in Fort Wayne or the USA. We manage campaigns through your own account, with calls set around Eastern Time.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

Yes, you own the account and billing, and we work in it with manager access. If you ever stop working with us, your campaigns and data stay with you.

Can you fix an existing Google Ads account?

Yes, we can audit and restructure an existing account rather than starting over. We review tracking, keywords, search terms, ads and landing pages, then agree a plan of fixes with you.
