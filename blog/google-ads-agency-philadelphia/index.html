Google Ads Agency Philadelphia: How Remote Google Ads Management Works

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Philadelphia? Remote PPC management can cover local, B2B and ecommerce campaigns. See how it works and what it costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Google Ads agency for a Philadelphia business should focus on accurate conversion tracking, tight targeting and landing pages, not on having a local office.
  • Philadelphia campaigns often need careful location settings across the city, the suburbs and nearby New Jersey and Delaware.
  • We are a UK-based studio managing Google Ads remotely for US clients, with retainers priced in pounds, other currencies quoted on request and ad spend paid separately.

If you are looking for a Google Ads agency in Philadelphia, the right partner is one that tracks conversions properly, targets the right people in the right places and keeps improving the account each month. A remote agency can do this well for Philadelphia service firms, healthcare providers, B2B companies and online shops, because Google Ads is managed entirely online and judged against the leads and sales it produces.

We are a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for US businesses. We have no Philadelphia office and no US office. This guide explains what Google Ads management should include, how targeting works across Greater Philadelphia, how a remote arrangement runs and what it costs.

What a Google Ads agency in Philadelphia should do

Philadelphia has a large healthcare and education sector, alongside life sciences, finance, insurance, law, manufacturing, logistics, hospitality and a dense mix of trade and home service firms. Whatever you sell, good Google Ads management covers the same essentials:

  • Conversion tracking: calls, forms, bookings and purchases recorded accurately so bidding is based on real outcomes.
  • Campaign structure: campaigns and ad groups built around services, products and intent rather than one catch-all campaign.
  • Keyword and search term control: regular review of what people actually searched, with negative keywords to cut waste.
  • Ads and assets: clear, relevant ad copy, sitelinks, callouts and other assets that match each search.
  • Bidding: a bid strategy suited to your data volume and goals, reviewed as the account matures.
  • Landing pages: pages that match the ad, load quickly and make the next step obvious.
  • Reporting: cost per lead or sale and return on ad spend, not just clicks and impressions.

Tracking comes first. Our Google Ads conversion tracking guide explains the setup, and our guide to negative keywords shows how we cut wasted spend.

Location targeting across Greater Philadelphia

Philadelphia's market spreads well beyond the city limits. Customers may be in Center City, South Philly, Fishtown or Manayunk, in suburbs such as Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware and Chester counties, or across the river in South Jersey, with Wilmington close by in Delaware. Getting location settings right is one of the simplest ways to protect your budget:

  • Presence targeting: showing ads to people who are in or regularly in your area, rather than people merely interested in it, where that suits your business.
  • Radius or county targeting: matching campaigns to the areas you can genuinely serve.
  • Separate campaigns where needed: splitting areas with different costs, demand or service levels so budgets can be controlled.
  • Location exclusions: removing places you do not serve.

Rules for advertising, licensing and tax can differ between Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware. Check official guidance or your own adviser before promoting services across state lines. Our location targeting guide covers the settings in detail.

Google Ads for Philadelphia service, healthcare and B2B firms

Different businesses need different campaign set-ups:

  • Home and trade services: call-focused campaigns, tight service areas, ad schedules that match when you can answer the phone and pages built for each service.
  • Healthcare and clinics: careful wording and compliance with Google's healthcare advertising policies. Claims and copy should be approved by your own compliance team or advisers.
  • Legal and professional services: high-cost keywords, so search term control and lead quality tracking matter a great deal.
  • B2B and life sciences: longer sales cycles, so tracking should include offline outcomes such as qualified leads and meetings where possible.
  • Ecommerce: Shopping and Performance Max campaigns fed by a clean product feed, measured against revenue and margin.

For a nearby comparison, our guide to Google Ads in New York shows how the same principles apply in a larger and often costlier market.

How remote Google Ads management works

Philadelphia is five hours behind the UK, which gives a good shared window across your morning and early afternoon. Most account work happens while you are busy with other things, and calls are booked in that overlap. A typical arrangement looks like this:

  • Onboarding: access to Google Ads, Google Analytics, Tag Manager and your website, plus a call to agree goals, budgets and what counts as a good lead.
  • Audit or build: a review of any existing account, or a fresh structure if you are starting out.
  • Tracking checks: making sure every important conversion is recorded before scaling spend.
  • Ongoing management: search term reviews, bid and budget changes, ad testing and landing page advice.
  • Reporting: a monthly report in plain language with spend, leads or sales and next steps.

Your Google Ads account should stay in your name and under your ownership. Our guide to choosing a remote PPC agency for US businesses covers access, ownership and the questions worth asking.

Landing pages and Quality Score

Many Google Ads accounts are held back by the pages the ads send people to. A landing page that matches the search, loads quickly and has one clear call to action usually improves both conversion rate and Quality Score. Common Google Ads mistakes we see

When we review an existing account, the same problems come up again and again. Most of them are simple to fix once they are spotted:

  • Broken or duplicate tracking: conversions counted twice, or not at all, which leads automated bidding in the wrong direction.
  • Broad keywords with no negatives: ads showing for searches that have nothing to do with your services, such as jobs, training or free advice.
  • Location settings left on the default: ads shown to people outside your area because they showed an interest in it.
  • One campaign for everything: high-value and low-value services sharing a budget, so the cheaper clicks win.
  • Ads running when no one can answer: call campaigns running overnight or at weekends without anyone to pick up the phone.
  • Every ad pointing to the home page: visitors left to hunt for the service they searched for.
  • No review of lead quality: the account judged on cost per lead alone, even when many leads are poor fits.

Fixing these basics often matters more than any advanced feature. We start with them before suggesting new campaign types or larger budgets.

Setting a sensible Google Ads budget

There is no single right budget, because click costs vary widely by sector and area. A sensible approach is to start with a budget large enough to gather useful data in your most important services, measure the cost per lead or sale, and then grow spend where the numbers work. Budgets that are spread too thinly across many services and areas rarely produce enough data to learn from. We agree a starting budget with you during onboarding and review it monthly.

  • Growth, £750 a month: broader accounts, more testing and landing page support.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Philadelphia?

Ask how they set up conversion tracking, how often they review search terms, who owns the account and what the monthly report shows. A clear plan and honest timelines matter more than a local address.

Do you have an office in Philadelphia?

No. We are a UK-based studio and manage Google Ads remotely for US businesses. We have no Philadelphia office or any other US office.

Can you target the suburbs and South Jersey as well as the city?

Yes. Location settings can cover the city, suburban counties and areas across state lines, with separate campaigns where costs or service levels differ. Check rules for each state with official guidance or your own adviser.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month, priced in pounds with other currencies on request. Ad spend is paid separately to Google.

How quickly will Google Ads bring results?

Ads can bring traffic and enquiries soon after launch, but stronger results from testing and optimisation typically build over three to six months.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You should. We work inside an account in your name, so the data, history and billing stay with your business if you ever change agency.
