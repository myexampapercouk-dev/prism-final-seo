Who we run Google Ads for in Louisville

Louisville has a strong mix of logistics, healthcare, manufacturing, food and drink, hospitality and local trades. Google Ads suits businesses where people search with clear intent. We commonly work with:

  • Home services such as HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pest control and remodeling, where a single call can be worth a lot.
  • Professional services such as accountants, consultants and agencies that want qualified enquiries.
  • Online shops that want Shopping and Performance Max campaigns across the USA.
  • Hospitality and events businesses that want bookings around busy seasons and major local events.
  • B2B and industrial firms that need leads for specialist products and services.

For legal, health and financial advertisers, Google has its own policies and, in some cases, certification requirements. We keep ad copy general and accurate, and final compliance approval rests with you and your own adviser.

How we set up Google Ads for Louisville

Good results start with a solid setup. Our process follows the same steps for every new account.

  1. Audit or discovery. If you already run ads, we review your account structure, search terms, tracking and landing pages. If you are new, we agree goals and budget.
  2. Conversion tracking. We set up tracking for calls, forms and purchases so we can measure what matters.
  3. Keyword and location planning. We plan keywords by service and set location targeting around Louisville and the areas you serve, such as St. Matthews, Jeffersontown, Middletown and Southern Indiana if relevant.
  4. Ad copy and assets. We write clear ads with strong calls to action, sitelinks and call assets.
  5. Landing pages. We check that each ad points to a page that matches its message.
  6. Launch and weekly optimisation. We review search terms, add negative keywords, adjust bids and test new ads.

If your site is slow or hard to use on a phone, we can improve it or build a new one through our website development service. Small websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and usually take three to five weeks.

Google Ads alongside SEO and social ads

Google Ads brings fast, controllable traffic, while SEO builds lasting visibility over months. Many Louisville businesses use both: ads to fill the pipeline now, SEO to reduce reliance on paid clicks later. Our SEO and GEO service covers the organic side.

Some businesses also benefit from social ads on Meta, TikTok, YouTube or LinkedIn, especially for awareness and remarketing. Our paid media service covers these platforms. We do not film or edit video, so any video ads use footage supplied by your team.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

When we audit existing accounts, the same issues come up often:

  • Broad match keywords without enough negative keywords, leading to irrelevant clicks.
  • Location settings that show ads to people far outside your service area.
  • Missing or broken conversion tracking, so bidding has nothing reliable to learn from.
  • One ad group covering every service, with ads that do not match the search.
  • Landing pages that load slowly or bury the phone number.

Fixing these basics is often the quickest way to make an existing budget work harder. Our guides to Google Ads in Chicago and Google Ads in Atlanta show how we apply the same approach in other US cities.

Google Ads is managed entirely online, so working remotely is simple. You keep ownership of your Google Ads account and billing, and we work as a manager with access you can remove at any time. The UK is usually five hours ahead of Louisville, so we hold calls in your morning and reply within one working day. What to prepare before launching Google Ads

A little preparation on your side makes the first month of a new account much more useful. Before we launch, it helps to have the following ready:

  • A clear list of services. Tell us which services matter most, which are most profitable and which you would rather not promote. This shapes how we group keywords and split the budget.
  • Your service area. Confirm the neighborhoods, suburbs and counties you cover, including any areas across the river in Southern Indiana. Tight location settings stop budget going to people you cannot serve.
  • Opening hours and call handling. If nobody answers the phone after a certain time, we can schedule call ads around your hours so you do not pay for missed calls.
  • A realistic monthly budget. Start with a figure you are comfortable testing for at least two to three months. Very small budgets spread across many services rarely produce useful data.
  • Access to your website and analytics. We need to install or check tracking, so access to your site, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager speeds things up.
  • Offers and proof points. Anything that sets you apart, such as same-day visits, free quotes, guarantees or financing options, makes ads stronger. We only include claims you can stand behind.

Once the account is live, we review search terms closely in the first few weeks. This is when most wasted spend is found and removed, so early weeks are about learning as much as selling. From there, we shift budget towards the services, keywords and times of day that bring real enquiries, and we explain each change in our monthly report so you always know why the account looks the way it does.

If you already have an account, we can start with an audit instead. We look at structure, tracking, search terms and landing pages, then share a short list of the changes most likely to help, ranked by effort and impact.
