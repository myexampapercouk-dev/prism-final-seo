Meta ads agency Halifax: what we do

Good Meta advertising is less about clever tricks and more about solid setup, clear offers and steady testing. Our work covers:

  • Account and tracking setup: the Meta pixel and Conversions API where your platform supports it, with events for leads, bookings or purchases.
  • Campaign structure: a simple setup with clear objectives, so budget is not split too thin.
  • Audiences: location targeting around Halifax, Dartmouth, Bedford and wider Nova Scotia, plus remarketing to site visitors and customer lists you are allowed to use.
  • Creative testing: different images, short clips your team supplies, headlines and offers tested against each other.
  • Lead forms and landing pages: instant forms for quick enquiries or landing pages for more detail.
  • Reporting: monthly written reports covering spend, results and next steps.

Tracking that you can trust

Meta's own reporting can overstate or understate results, especially with browser privacy settings and ad blockers. We set up tracking carefully so the numbers are as reliable as possible:

  • Pixel events for the actions that matter, not every page view.
  • Conversions API through your website platform or a supported integration where available.
  • UTM tags so your analytics shows which campaigns sent which visitors.
  • Lead form data sent to your inbox or CRM so nothing is missed.

We also compare Meta's figures with your own records, such as bookings or sales, so decisions are based on real outcomes.

Creative that fits the feed

Creative is the biggest lever in most Meta accounts. We write ad copy, suggest image and clip ideas and plan tests. We do not film or edit video, so short clips come from your team, a phone camera or a videographer you hire. Often simple, honest footage of your work performs well alongside clean product or service images.

We usually test one variable at a time, such as the offer, the opening line or the image, so we learn what actually changes results. Winning ideas are kept and new tests are added each month.

Privacy, consent and ad rules

Meta campaigns rely on data, so privacy rules apply. In general terms, PIPEDA sets federal rules for how private-sector organisations handle personal information, including for customer lists used in advertising. CASL covers commercial emails and texts sent to leads after they enquire. Some sectors, such as health, finance, housing and employment, also face Meta's own special ad category restrictions and their own regulators.

These notes are general information only. Please check official guidance and speak to your own adviser. In regulated sectors, compliance approval of ads and forms rests with you. We set up consent wording and privacy links using text you approve.

Using AI-powered tools in campaigns

We use AI-powered tools to draft ad copy variations, group audience ideas and summarise performance trends. A person checks every ad before it runs, and you approve new offers and claims. Meta's own automated campaign types can help in some accounts, and we use them where the tracking is strong enough to guide them.

Common Meta Ads problems we fix

Many Halifax accounts we review have the same issues. Budgets are spread across too many small campaigns, so none gets enough data to learn. The pixel fires on the wrong pages or not at all. Boosted posts are used instead of proper campaigns with clear goals. Lead forms collect contact details nobody follows up on for days. Ads send people to a homepage instead of a page about the offer.

None of these needs a large budget to fix. We start with an audit, list the problems in plain English and fix the ones with the biggest effect first. Often, cleaning up structure and tracking improves results before any new creative is added.

Budgets and expectations

We do not promise fixed results, because costs depend on your offer, competition and season. What we do is set a sensible starting budget, agree what a good result looks like for your business and review it honestly each month. A new account usually needs a few weeks of steady spend and testing before patterns become clear. If Meta is not the right channel for your goals, we will say so and suggest an alternative.

Lead follow-up matters as much as the ads. We help you route form leads to the right person quickly and set up simple confirmation messages, using wording you approve, so enquiries do not go cold.

We also keep a simple log of every test we run, so you can see what was tried, what changed and why. That record stays with you, even if you later move the account to another team.
