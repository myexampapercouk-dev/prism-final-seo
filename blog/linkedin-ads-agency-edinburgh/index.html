LinkedIn Ads Agency in Edinburgh: Targeting, Tracking and Leads

Need a LinkedIn Ads agency in Edinburgh? We plan and manage LinkedIn campaigns that reach decision makers and track real leads, from £350 a month plus spend.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 28 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A LinkedIn Ads agency in Edinburgh should target by job title, seniority, company and industry, track leads into your CRM and judge success on pipeline, not clicks.
  • Prism Works Studio manages LinkedIn Ads remotely on retainers from £350 a month, with ad spend paid directly to LinkedIn.
  • LinkedIn clicks cost more than most platforms, so a clear offer, tight targeting and good follow-up matter more than budget size.

A LinkedIn Ads agency in Edinburgh plans and manages paid campaigns on LinkedIn so business-to-business companies reach the decision makers they sell to and turn that attention into qualified leads. At Prism Works Studio we build targeting around job titles, seniority, industries and named companies, set up tracking into your CRM and optimise for pipeline rather than vanity metrics.

We are a UK-based studio that works remotely, so firms in the New Town, Leith, Quartermile, Edinburgh Park or anywhere across Scotland get the same process and published prices. This guide covers what we do, how campaigns are built, what LinkedIn Ads cost and how to decide whether the channel suits you.

Why Edinburgh firms advertise on LinkedIn

Edinburgh's economy leans heavily on business services: financial services and investment, technology and software, professional firms, universities and research, energy and engineering consultancies. These businesses usually sell to specific roles inside specific organisations, and that is exactly what LinkedIn targeting is built for.

Search ads capture people already looking for a solution. LinkedIn lets you reach buyers before they search, by putting useful content and offers in front of the right job functions. For long sales cycles with several decision makers, that early visibility often matters. Our comparison of LinkedIn Ads vs Google Ads for B2B explains when each works best.

What our LinkedIn Ads agency in Edinburgh does

  • Account setup: Campaign Manager, billing and company page access, with you owning everything.
  • Tracking: the Insight Tag and conversion events, plus CRM connections for lead forms. Our LinkedIn Insight Tag guide covers the steps.
  • Audience building: job titles, functions, seniority, company size, industry, named account lists and matched audiences.
  • Offer planning: guides, webinars, demos, consultations or reports that give buyers a reason to respond.
  • Ad copy and creative briefs: single image, document and carousel ads, with copy we write and assets your team supplies or we brief.
  • Optimisation and reporting: regular bid, audience and creative changes, with monthly reports tied to lead quality.

How we build LinkedIn campaigns

  1. Discovery: we learn who buys from you, deal sizes, sales cycle length and how leads are followed up.
  2. Audience map: we define primary and secondary audiences, plus exclusions such as competitors and existing customers.
  3. Offer and funnel: we match offers to each stage, from awareness content to demo requests.
  4. Tracking: the Insight Tag, conversions and CRM connections are tested before launch.
  5. Launch: campaigns start with controlled budgets and clear tests.
  6. Optimise: we review cost per lead, lead quality and pipeline, then refine audiences, bids and creative.

Lead quality matters more than lead volume

Cheap leads are not always good leads. LinkedIn lead forms make submitting easy, which can mean more low-intent contacts. We tackle that by adding qualifying questions, matching offers to buying stage and feeding sales outcomes back into targeting decisions. A smaller number of qualified conversations is usually worth more than a long list nobody follows up.

That also means working with your sales team. We agree what a qualified lead looks like, how quickly leads are contacted and how outcomes are recorded. Our guide to LinkedIn Ads for B2B lead generation goes into more detail.

Regulated sectors in Edinburgh

Many Edinburgh firms work in financial services, investment or other regulated areas. LinkedIn has its own ad policies, and financial promotions in the UK are subject to FCA rules. We keep copy factual and avoid claims we cannot support, but compliance approval always rests with your own compliance team. Our financial advisers sector page explains how we work in that space.

Landing pages that convert senior buyers

Where ads send people to your site, the landing page needs to work hard. Senior buyers want a clear proposition, relevant proof, simple next steps and fast pages on mobile. We review existing pages and can design new ones as part of a wider project. Our landing page design guide covers the essentials, and B2B SEO helps those same pages earn organic traffic too.

Building audiences that are big enough

A common mistake on LinkedIn is narrowing targeting so far that campaigns cannot spend or learn. Stacking job title, seniority, industry, company size and location can leave an audience of only a few hundred people. LinkedIn then struggles to deliver, costs rise and results are hard to judge.

We balance precision with scale. For most Edinburgh firms we start with job functions and seniority rather than exact titles, then layer industry or company size. Named account lists work well when you have a defined set of target organisations, but they usually need to contain enough companies to give the campaign room to learn. Where the market is genuinely small, we focus budget on fewer, stronger campaigns and rely on retargeting to keep warm prospects engaged.

Geography also needs thought. Some Edinburgh firms only serve Scotland, while others sell across the UK or internationally. We set location targeting to match where you can actually do business, and we exclude regions where leads would be wasted.

Retargeting and nurturing longer sales cycles

B2B buyers rarely convert on the first click. Many visit your site, read a guide, leave and come back weeks later. LinkedIn retargeting lets you stay visible to people who have engaged with your ads, opened a lead form, watched content or visited key pages on your site.

We build simple nurturing sequences: useful content first, then case-led or proof-led messages, then a direct offer such as a consultation or demo. Each stage has its own audience and message so people are not shown the same ad repeatedly. We also exclude people who have already converted, which saves budget and avoids irritating new contacts.

Alongside paid retargeting, we encourage clients to connect LinkedIn leads to their email and CRM workflows. The ads start the conversation, but timely, relevant follow-up from your team usually decides whether it becomes a sale.

What good reporting looks like

Our monthly reports go beyond clicks and impressions. We show cost per lead, lead quality by audience and offer, how many leads became meetings or opportunities where your CRM data allows, and what we plan to change next. That keeps everyone focused on the outcomes that matter.
