WordPress Agency for Dubai: Building and Supporting WordPress Websites for UAE Businesses

WordPress for Dubai businesses: how we build, secure and support bilingual Arabic and English WordPress sites remotely from the UK, with timelines and prices.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good WordPress site for Dubai is fast on mobile, works properly in Arabic and English, is easy for your team to update and is kept secure with regular updates and backups.
  • We are a UK-based studio that builds and supports WordPress sites remotely for Dubai businesses, with no office in the UAE, and Arabic content is written with your team or a translator.
  • Small WordPress sites typically cost £1,000 to £2,000 and take 3 to 5 weeks, WooCommerce stores £1,500 to £3,500, and other currencies, including dirhams, are quoted on request.

A WordPress agency for Dubai designs, builds and maintains WordPress websites that help UAE businesses win customers, with fast pages, clear service information and proper Arabic and English versions. WordPress is a flexible, widely used platform, and when it is set up well it gives Dubai businesses a site their own team can update without calling a developer for every change.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We build WordPress sites through our website and ecommerce development service and work remotely with businesses in Dubai, across the UAE and the wider Middle East. We have no office in the UAE. This guide explains how we approach WordPress projects for Dubai businesses, what is included and what it costs.

Why Dubai businesses choose WordPress

Dubai has a crowded, competitive market for almost every service: real estate, hospitality, healthcare, consulting, education, trades and retail. Businesses need a website that looks credible, loads quickly and can grow as they add services, locations or languages. WordPress suits that well for several reasons.

  • Easy editing. Your team can update pages, add news and change details through a familiar editor.
  • Strong multilingual options. Arabic and English versions can be managed side by side with proper language tags and separate URLs.
  • Room to grow. Blogs, landing pages, booking forms, job listings and online stores can all be added later.
  • SEO friendly. Clean URLs, editable titles and descriptions and structured data are straightforward to set up.
  • No lock-in. You own the site and can move hosting or providers whenever you choose.

WordPress is not always the right choice. For a store that is mainly about products and checkout, Shopify is often simpler. We will recommend the platform that suits your business rather than the one we prefer.

WordPress agency Dubai: how we run a project

Every WordPress project follows a clear process, which matters even more when working remotely. The time difference between the UK and Dubai leaves good overlap for calls during the working day.

  1. Discovery. We agree your goals, audiences, services, languages and the actions you want visitors to take.
  2. Sitemap and content plan. We map the pages you need in each language and agree who writes and approves what.
  3. Design. We design key layouts for mobile and desktop, including right-to-left Arabic versions.
  4. Build. We build with the block editor or a lightweight, well-supported theme, and keep plugins to what is genuinely needed.
  5. SEO foundations. We set up titles, descriptions, headings, language tags, a sitemap and analytics.
  6. Testing, launch and handover. We test forms, speed and both languages, then launch and train your team.

If you are comparing build approaches, our guide to WordPress page builders explains when a builder helps and when it slows a site down.

Arabic and English WordPress sites

Most Dubai businesses serve both Arabic and English speakers, along with residents who use English as a shared language. A bilingual WordPress site needs more than a translation plugin. Menus, icons, sliders, forms and tables must mirror correctly for right-to-left reading, Arabic fonts must be clear at small sizes, and each language should have its own URLs so search engines show the right version to each searcher.

We write English content and build both versions of the design. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend that a fluent speaker reviews every Arabic page before launch. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites covers structure and navigation in more depth.

Speed, hosting and security

WordPress sites slow down and become vulnerable when they are overloaded with plugins, left without updates or placed on poor hosting. We build lean sites, compress images, limit scripts and choose hosting with good performance for visitors in the Gulf. Our guide to WordPress hosting explains what to look for in a host.

Security is an ongoing job rather than a one-off setup. We keep WordPress, themes and plugins updated, use strong access controls, run regular backups and monitor for problems. Our article on WordPress security lists the measures every business site should have in place.

WordPress sites for Dubai sectors

Different sectors in Dubai need different things from a WordPress site, so we plan the structure around how your customers decide. Real estate brokers and developers usually need clear listing or project pages, enquiry forms on every key page, floor plan and brochure downloads, and fast image galleries that still load quickly on mobile data. Hotels, restaurants and tourism operators need strong photography, menus or room details, links to booking systems and opening information that is easy to keep up to date.

Clinics and wellness providers need treatment pages that are accurate and calm in tone, simple appointment routes and careful handling of any personal information collected through forms. Consultancies, B2B firms and free zone companies often need detailed service pages, case-study style content that describes approaches without inventing results, and English copy that reads naturally to international buyers. Education and training providers need course pages that are easy to compare, with clear fees, entry requirements and application steps.

In every case we use custom post types, reusable blocks and simple templates so your team can add a new listing, service, course or location page without breaking the design. That keeps the site consistent as it grows and saves you from paying for small changes that your own staff can handle in minutes.

Ownership and handover

You should own your domain, hosting account, WordPress admin and content. We set these up in your name wherever possible, give you full administrator access and hand over the logins and guidance your team needs. Rules and compliance

Sites that collect personal data, take payments or promote regulated services such as healthcare, finance or real estate need to consider data protection, consumer protection and sector rules in the UAE, which can differ between free zones and the mainland. We build sites with clear privacy information and consent where needed, but we are not legal advisers. Check official UAE guidance and ask your adviser or compliance team to confirm what applies.
