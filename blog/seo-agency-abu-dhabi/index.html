Why SEO matters for Abu Dhabi businesses

Customers in Abu Dhabi research online before they call, visit or buy. Whether they are looking for a clinic, a property service, a training provider, a B2B supplier or a restaurant, search engines and AI assistants are often the first place they ask. If your site does not appear, or appears with thin pages that do not answer the question, a competitor gets the enquiry.

Abu Dhabi has a mix of government-linked organisations, established family businesses, international firms and fast-growing smaller companies. Many sectors are competitive in English but less developed in Arabic, which can create real opportunities for businesses willing to invest in well-written Arabic pages. Arabic and English keyword research

People in Abu Dhabi search in Arabic, in English and sometimes in a mix of both. Arabic searches can use different spellings, regional terms and transliterations written in Latin letters. The words people type rarely match a direct translation of your English keywords, so translating a keyword list is not enough.

We research each language separately, look at what is already ranking, and map searches to the pages that should answer them. Our keyword research guide explains the method. Where Arabic demand justifies it, we recommend dedicated Arabic pages on their own URLs with correct hreflang tags, rather than automatic translation. We write in English. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend a fluent speaker reviews it before publishing. Our article on bilingual Arabic and English websites explains the setup.

Common technical problems on bilingual sites

Many sites we review have content worth ranking but technical issues that stop search engines reading it properly. Bilingual sites in the UAE tend to share a few recurring problems, and fixing them is often the fastest way to see movement.

  • Language switching without separate URLs. If Arabic and English share one address and switch with a script or cookie, search engines may only ever see one version.
  • Missing or broken hreflang tags. Without them, Google can show the English page to Arabic searchers, or treat the two versions as duplicates.
  • Machine-translated pages. Automatic translations often read awkwardly, miss the terms people actually search for and can weaken trust.
  • Slow mobile pages. Large images, heavy sliders and too many scripts slow a site down, especially on mobile.
  • Thin service pages. One page listing every service gives search engines little to rank for each one.

We fix these in order of impact, explain each change in plain English and check the results in Search Console afterwards. Where the site needs development work beyond SEO, we scope it separately so you always know what you are paying for.

Local SEO across Abu Dhabi

For businesses serving customers in person, local search often brings the most valuable enquiries. People search for services near them, by district or by city, and Google shows a map pack of local businesses above the standard results.

  • Google Business Profile. A complete profile with the right categories, accurate hours, photos and regular updates.
  • Location pages. Where you serve several areas, such as Abu Dhabi city and Al Ain, useful pages for each rather than thin copies with the place name swapped.
  • Consistent details. The same business name, address and phone number across your site and directories.
  • Reviews. A simple, honest process for asking customers to leave reviews and replying to them.

If you operate in more than one emirate, the same principles apply across each location. Our guide to SEO for Dubai businesses covers the neighbouring market.

Showing up in AI search answers

More people now ask AI assistants and Google's AI Overviews for recommendations before they click any link. These tools draw on pages that answer questions clearly and directly, are well structured and come from sites with a strong reputation.

We write pages so the key answer appears early, use headings that match how people phrase questions, add FAQ sections where they help, and use schema markup so search engines understand what each page is about. Our explainer on generative engine optimisation covers this in more detail. We will not promise that any AI tool will mention your business, but clear, accurate and well-structured content gives you the best chance.

How to choose an SEO agency in Abu Dhabi

Whether you hire us or another agency, these questions help you compare options fairly:

  • How will you research Arabic searches, and who writes and checks the Arabic content?
  • What will you do in the first three months, and how will I see progress?
  • Will I have full access to Google Search Console, analytics and any accounts you create?
  • How do you build links and reputation, and what methods do you avoid?
  • Is there a minimum contract period, and what happens to the work if we stop?
  • How do you report on enquiries and sales, not just rankings?

We will not claim local experience we do not have. Where you need in-person work, such as photography or events, you may need a local partner for those parts, and we are happy to work alongside them. Rules on advertising claims, data and certain regulated sectors can change, so check current official guidance in the UAE and confirm specific requirements with your own advisers.
