Building bilingual WordPress sites for Montreal

Many Montreal businesses need English and French versions of their website. Getting the structure right early saves a great deal of rework later. When we plan a bilingual WordPress site we look at:

  • URL structure. Separate folders for each language, such as /fr/ and /en/, are usually the clearest option for visitors and search engines.
  • Language switching. Each page should link to its equivalent in the other language, not just back to the home page.
  • hreflang tags. These tell search engines which version to show to which audience, so the French page appears for French searches.
  • Forms and emails. Confirmation messages and notification emails need both language versions.
  • Editing workflow. Your team should be able to update both languages without breaking layouts.

We set up the technical side, including a multilingual plugin, templates and translated menus. The French wording itself comes from your team or a translator you choose. Quebec has specific rules about the use of French in commerce, including on websites. These rules can change, so we recommend checking the Office québécois de la langue française guidance or speaking with your own adviser about what your business needs to do.

Speed, security and hosting

WordPress has a reputation for being slow or insecure, but that usually comes from heavy themes, too many plugins and missed updates rather than the platform itself. Our builds focus on:

  • A lightweight theme or block-based design rather than a large multipurpose theme.
  • A short, well-maintained plugin list, with each plugin chosen for a clear job.
  • Compressed, correctly sized images and modern formats.
  • Caching and a content delivery network where your host supports one.
  • Automatic backups, security monitoring and a clear update routine.

We can work with your existing host or recommend options. If your audience is mostly in Canada, hosting with a Canadian or North American data centre can help page speed. For a deeper look at why speed matters, read our guide to Core Web Vitals.

SEO built into the WordPress site

A WordPress site that cannot be found does not do much for your business. We build search basics into every site from the start:

  • Clear page titles, meta descriptions and headings for each service and location.
  • Clean URLs and a logical internal linking structure.
  • Schema markup for your business, services and articles where relevant.
  • An XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console.
  • Redirects from old URLs when we replace an existing site, so you keep the search visibility you have built.

For ongoing search growth, our SEO and GEO service covers content, technical improvements and visibility in AI search tools. You can also read our guide to SEO agencies in Montreal for a fuller picture of local search in the city.

Privacy, consent and email rules in Canada

Most WordPress sites collect personal information through contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, analytics and booking tools. In Canada, that brings privacy and anti-spam rules into play. At a general level:

  • PIPEDA is the federal private-sector privacy law that applies to many businesses handling personal information in commercial activity.
  • Quebec's Law 25 has updated the province's private-sector privacy rules and adds requirements around consent, transparency and privacy governance.
  • CASL, Canada's anti-spam legislation, sets rules for commercial electronic messages such as marketing emails.

We can build cookie consent banners, privacy page templates, clear consent checkboxes on forms and opt-in flows for newsletters. What your privacy policy says and how your business meets its legal obligations is your decision. We recommend reading guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, or speaking with your own legal adviser.

How a WordPress project with us works

Working remotely does not mean working at a distance from your goals. A typical project runs like this:

  1. Discovery call. We talk through your business, audience, current site and what the new site needs to do.
  2. Sitemap and wireframes. We plan the pages, the bilingual structure and the main user journeys.
  3. Design. You review page designs on a shared link and send feedback.
  4. Build. We build on a staging site so you can click through everything before launch.
  5. Content. You supply English and French text, or we write English copy and your translator handles French.
  6. Testing and launch. We check forms, speed, mobile layouts and redirects, then go live.
  7. Handover. We show your team how to edit pages and posts, with a short written guide.

Because we are in the UK, our working day overlaps with Montreal mornings. Most clients find that a morning call followed by written updates keeps projects moving well.

Choosing a WordPress agency for your Montreal business

Whether you choose us or another studio, ask a few practical questions before you start:

  • Who owns the site, the domain and the hosting account once the project is finished?
  • How will the French and English versions be structured and maintained?
  • Which plugins will be used, and who keeps them updated?
  • What happens to existing URLs and search visibility during a redesign?
  • How will your team edit pages without breaking the design?
  • What is included in ongoing support, and what is charged separately?

Clear answers to these questions are a good sign that the agency has thought about the life of your website beyond launch day.
