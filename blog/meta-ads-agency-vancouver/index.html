Meta Ads Agency for Vancouver Businesses: Remote Facebook and Instagram Advertising

Looking for a Meta ads agency in Vancouver? How remote Facebook and Instagram advertising works for BC brands, from tracking and creative to audiences and fees.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Meta ads agency for a Vancouver business should fix tracking first, then test creative and offers against clear goals rather than chasing cheap clicks.
  • Vancouver brands often sell to the Lower Mainland, the rest of Canada and the United States, so audiences, budgets and messaging need to reflect where buyers really are.
  • Management fees are separate from ad spend, which is paid directly to Meta, and accounts usually need a few months of testing before performance settles.

A Meta ads agency for a Vancouver business should make sure your tracking is accurate, build campaigns around a clear goal such as sales, leads or bookings, and keep testing creative and offers so Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who are likely to buy. Because campaigns run inside Meta's Ads Manager and your website, the work can be done remotely. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Meta advertising for Canadian businesses online; we have no office in Vancouver or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide covers how Meta ads work for Vancouver businesses, what to get right before spending, how creative and audiences fit together, what our management costs and what to ask before choosing an agency.

What a Meta ads agency in Vancouver should do

Vancouver's economy leans heavily on consumer brands, technology, film and digital media, tourism and hospitality, real estate, health and wellness, outdoor and lifestyle products, and a large number of independent shops, studios, restaurants and service businesses across the city, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey and the North Shore. Many of these businesses sell visually appealing products or experiences, which suits Facebook and Instagram well. A good agency should handle:

  • Tracking. The Meta Pixel and Conversions API set up correctly so purchases, leads and bookings are recorded. Our Meta Pixel setup guide explains the basics.
  • Campaign structure that is simple enough for Meta's automation to learn from, with clear objectives.
  • Creative testing across images, video, Reels and carousels, with new ideas introduced regularly.
  • Audiences that balance broad targeting with remarketing to people who already know you.
  • Landing pages that match the ad and make buying or enquiring easy on a phone.
  • Reporting on cost per sale or lead and return on ad spend, not likes alone.

Our paid media service covers Meta alongside Google, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn advertising.

Creative is the main lever

Meta's automation now does much of the audience work that used to be done by hand. As a result, the ads themselves have become the biggest factor in whether a campaign works. For a Vancouver brand, that might mean short videos showing a product in use, behind-the-scenes clips from a studio or kitchen, simple customer questions answered on screen or clear before-and-after images where they are honest and permitted.

We plan creative in small batches, each built around one idea, such as a benefit, an objection or an offer. Each batch runs long enough to show a meaningful difference, and the results guide the next round. You do not need a large production budget: well-lit footage shot on a phone often performs as well as polished video, as long as the message is clear in the first few seconds. We can work from footage your team supplies and advise on what to capture.

Audiences: Lower Mainland, Canada or beyond

Where you advertise depends on what you sell. A restaurant in Gastown or a clinic in Kitsilano needs people within reach, while an outdoor apparel brand or a skincare company may sell across Canada and into the United States. Mixing very different markets in a single campaign makes results hard to read, so we usually separate them, each with its own budget and messaging.

Ecommerce and Shopify brands

Many Vancouver brands sell online through Shopify. For them, Meta advertising usually combines prospecting campaigns that introduce the brand to new people with catalogue ads that remind past visitors of products they viewed. Accurate product data, clear pricing including shipping and a fast checkout all affect results. If your store needs work before you scale spend, our guide to Shopify in Vancouver explains how we help, and our guide to web design in Vancouver covers non-ecommerce sites.

Sending traffic to a generic homepage rarely works as well as a page built for the campaign. Our guide to landing page design explains what to include.

Meta ads or Google Ads?

Meta ads reach people while they scroll, so they create demand rather than capturing it. Google Ads reach people who are already searching. Many Vancouver businesses benefit from both: Google for high-intent searches and Meta for introducing products to new audiences and remarketing to visitors. Our comparison of Google Ads vs Facebook ads explains how to decide where to start, and our guide to Meta ads costs explains the factors that affect what you pay per result.

Privacy, consent and French-language ads

Meta tracking involves personal data, so your website should explain clearly how it uses cookies and pixels. British Columbia has its own private-sector privacy law, the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), which generally applies to organisations operating within the province, while the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) can apply in other situations, such as when information crosses provincial borders. Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) applies if you follow up leads with marketing email or messages. If you advertise to customers in Quebec, that province's privacy law, often called Law 25, may add requirements. The Accessible British Columbia Act may also be relevant depending on your organisation's type. These notes are general information, not legal advice, so check official guidance from the relevant regulators.

If you want to reach French-speaking Canadians, ads and landing pages need to be written properly in French rather than machine-translated. We write in English; French content is written with your team or a professional translator.

Some sectors, including health, finance, housing and employment, face extra restrictions under Meta's advertising policies. We follow those policies, but approval of any regulated claims rests with your own compliance team or professional body.

How remote Meta ads management works

Vancouver is on Pacific Time, so the UK is usually eight hours ahead. That leaves a shorter overlap, typically early Vancouver mornings and late UK afternoons, so we agree a regular call slot and handle most communication in writing. You keep ownership of your Business Manager, ad account and pixel throughout.

  1. Audit or set-up. We check tracking, account structure and past results, or set things up from scratch.
  2. Plan. We agree goals, budgets, markets and the first creative ideas to test.
  3. Launch. Campaigns go live with clear objectives and a testing plan.
  4. Optimise. We review results, refresh creative and adjust budgets regularly.
  5. Report. Monthly updates on spend, sales or leads, cost per result and next steps.

What Meta ads management costs with Prism

Management is billed on monthly retainers in pounds sterling: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Prices in Canadian dollars or other currencies can be quoted on request. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Meta. The right level depends on the number of markets, products and creative tests involved. Full details are on our pricing page.

Accounts usually need a few months of testing before performance settles, and paid results are typically judged over three to six months. Be wary of any agency promising a guaranteed return on ad spend.

Questions to ask any Meta ads agency

  • Will the ad account and pixel stay in our name?
  • How will you check that purchases or leads are tracked correctly?
  • How often will you test new creative, and who produces it?
  • How will you split budgets between local, Canadian and cross-border audiences?
  • What will monthly reports include?
  • What notice period applies if we want to stop?

FAQs

How do I choose a Meta ads agency in Vancouver?

Look for an agency that checks tracking first, has a clear creative testing process, keeps accounts in your name and reports on sales or leads rather than reach. Ask how it would handle audiences across the Lower Mainland, Canada and the United States.

Can a UK agency run Facebook and Instagram ads for a Vancouver business?

Yes. Meta campaigns are managed online, so location is not a barrier. Prism has no Canadian office; we work remotely and agree a regular call time that suits Pacific Time.

How much does Meta ads management cost with Prism?

Retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Meta. We can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Do I need professional video for Instagram ads?

Not necessarily. Clear, well-lit phone footage often performs well, provided the message lands in the first few seconds. What matters most is testing different ideas regularly.

Should Vancouver brands advertise in the United States?

It can make sense if you can ship and support customers there. Run cross-border campaigns separately so budgets, pricing and results are easy to compare with Canadian campaigns.

How long do Meta ads take to work?

Campaigns can bring sales or leads within days, but most accounts need a few months of testing before results settle. Paid results are typically judged over three to six months.
