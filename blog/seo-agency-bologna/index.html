Why businesses look for an SEO agency in Bologna

Bologna has a strong mix of industries. The surrounding region is known for packaging machinery, automotive engineering, food production and precision manufacturing, and the city itself has one of Europe's oldest universities, a large trade fair calendar and a famous food culture that draws visitors all year. Each of these groups searches differently.

A manufacturer near Bologna may need international buyers to find detailed product pages in English and Italian. A restaurant or food shop in the historic centre needs to show up in map results when someone searches nearby. A professional firm needs to rank for service searches across Emilia-Romagna. A good SEO plan starts by working out which searches actually lead to revenue for your business, then builds pages and signals around them.

Technical SEO first

Many Italian business websites carry technical problems that hold back everything else: slow pages, duplicate URLs from filters, missing language tags, or important pages blocked from search engines by mistake. We start every engagement with a technical audit, then fix the issues in order of impact.

For sites that run in Italian and English, we check that each language has its own URLs, that language tags point to the right versions and that search engines are not seeing two copies of the same page. We also look at speed on mobile, because many Bologna customers search on their phones while out in the city. Our guide to multilingual website design explains why the structure matters so much.

Local SEO for Bologna and Emilia-Romagna

If customers visit you in person, local search is often the fastest win. We help you complete and maintain your Google Business Profile, choose the right categories, add accurate opening hours and photos, and set up a simple, fair process for asking customers for reviews. We also make sure your name, address and phone number match everywhere they appear online.

On your website, we create location pages only where they reflect real service areas, such as Bologna itself, nearby towns you serve or the wider region. Thin pages that swap one town name for another do more harm than good, so we avoid them. Our local SEO guide is written for UK businesses, but the same principles apply in Italy.

Content that ranks and gets cited

Good content answers what people are actually asking. We research the searches around your products and services, group them by intent and plan pages that answer each group well. For a machinery maker, that might mean detailed application pages and buyer guides. For a food producer, it could mean product pages, recipes and stockist information. For a law or accounting firm, it means clear service pages and answers to common questions.

We write the English versions and brief your team or translator on the Italian versions, including the keywords and headings each page should use. Translation alone is not enough: Italian searchers often phrase queries differently, so we research Italian searches separately rather than simply translating English keywords.

We also write with AI search in mind. Clear answers near the top of the page, well-organised headings, tables and FAQ sections make content easier for AI tools to understand and quote.

SEO for Bologna manufacturers and exporters

Bologna and its region have many business-to-business firms that sell to buyers around the world. These companies often have excellent products but websites that describe them in vague terms. International buyers search for specific applications, materials, capacities and standards, and they want to find that information quickly.

For these firms we focus on:

  • Product and application pages that use the language buyers search with.
  • English versions of key pages for export markets, with Italian kept for domestic buyers.
  • Technical clean-up of large catalogues so search engines index the right pages.
  • Content that supports long buying cycles, such as guides, comparisons and specification pages.

Our B2B marketing page explains how we approach long sales cycles and specialist buyers.

Food, hospitality and visitor businesses

Bologna's food reputation brings visitors from across Italy and abroad. Restaurants, food tours, cooking schools, delis and small hotels compete for searches from people planning trips, often in English. A focused local SEO plan, a fast mobile site and clear English pages can make a real difference. If paid campaigns also make sense, we can run Google Ads alongside SEO, as we describe in our guide to Google Ads in Milan.

Choosing an SEO agency for Bologna: questions to ask

Whoever you work with, ask these questions before you sign:

  • How will you research Italian searches, not just translate English keywords?
  • What will you fix in the first month, and why?
  • How do you report progress, and which numbers will you show?
  • Who writes the Italian content, and how is it checked?
  • What happens to the work if we stop the retainer?
