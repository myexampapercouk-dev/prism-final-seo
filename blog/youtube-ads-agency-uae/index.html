YouTube Ads Agency for the UAE: Video Campaign Planning, Formats and Cost

Need a YouTube Ads agency in the UAE? How a remote UK studio plans, targets and manages YouTube video campaigns for UAE businesses, and what it all costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Ads help UAE businesses reach people while they watch, from short skippable spots to Shorts, and work best when creative, targeting and tracking are planned together.
  • We plan and manage YouTube campaigns remotely from the UK with no office in the UAE, and Arabic scripts and copy are written with your team or a professional translator.
  • Management sits within our retainers (Launch £350, Growth £750, Scale £1,500 per month); ad spend is paid separately and other currencies are quoted on request.

A YouTube Ads agency in the UAE plans, launches and manages video campaigns on YouTube so that businesses in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the other emirates reach the right viewers and turn their attention into enquiries, sales or brand searches. The job covers strategy, targeting, creative guidance, tracking and ongoing optimisation inside Google Ads.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We manage YouTube advertising remotely for UAE businesses as part of our paid media service, and we have no office in the UAE. This guide covers how YouTube advertising works for UAE brands, the formats and targeting options, how we measure results and what the work costs.

Why YouTube Ads suit UAE businesses

YouTube is part of everyday viewing for many people in the UAE, on phones, laptops and connected TVs. It reaches Arabic speakers and the large international population in one place, and people watch everything from product reviews and recipes to property tours and business explainers. That makes it a strong channel for businesses that need to show, not just tell.

YouTube Ads are a good fit when:

  • Your product or service is easier to understand on video, such as property, hospitality, education, software or consumer products.
  • You want to build familiarity before people search for your category on Google.
  • You already run search or social ads and want to reach new audiences at scale.
  • You have video content, or the budget to produce it, that can be cut into ad lengths.

Because YouTube runs through Google Ads, it connects neatly with search campaigns. Our guide to Google Ads in Dubai explains the search side, and many UAE businesses run both together so that viewers who see a video later find the brand when they search.

YouTube Ads agency UAE: what we manage

As your YouTube Ads agency for the UAE, we handle the full campaign cycle. Some clients bring finished videos, while others need help planning what to make. Either way, the work looks like this:

  1. Goals and budget. We agree what the campaigns should achieve, such as leads, sales, app installs or reach, and set a realistic starting budget.
  2. Audience planning. We map who you want to reach by location, language, interests, life events, search behaviour and your own customer lists.
  3. Creative review. We review existing videos or brief new cuts so each ad opens strongly and fits the format.
  4. Tracking. We set up conversion tracking, link Google Analytics 4 and make sure results can be measured beyond views.
  5. Launch and optimisation. We launch, then adjust targeting, bids, placements and creative based on what the data shows.
  6. Reporting. You get clear, plain-English reports on spend, results and next steps.

YouTube ad formats and when to use them

YouTube offers several ad formats, and the right mix depends on your goal and the creative you have. The table below summarises the main options we use for UAE campaigns.

FormatHow it worksBest for
Skippable in-streamPlays before or during videos, viewers can skip after a few secondsLeads, sales and consideration with longer stories
Non-skippable in-streamShort ads viewers must watch to the endAwareness when the message is simple and brief
Bumper adsVery short non-skippable adsReminders and reinforcing a message across many views
In-feed videoAppears in search results and next to related videosReaching people actively looking for a topic
Shorts adsVertical ads between Shorts videosMobile-first audiences and creative also used on TikTok or Instagram
Demand Gen campaignsVideo and image ads across YouTube and other Google feedsDriving conversions from engaged audiences

For most UAE businesses starting out, we recommend skippable in-stream and Shorts placements optimised for conversions, with bumper ads added later for reminder campaigns. Our guide to YouTube Ads versus TikTok Ads explains how short vertical creative can work across both platforms.

Targeting audiences across the emirates

YouTube targeting can be very precise. For UAE campaigns we usually combine location, language and audience signals rather than relying on one setting.

  • Location. Target the whole UAE, specific emirates or areas around your locations, depending on where you serve customers.
  • Language. Run separate campaigns or ad groups for Arabic and English so creative and audience match.
  • Custom segments. Reach people based on what they have recently searched on Google, which is often the most effective option for lead generation.
  • In-market and life events. Reach people researching a category or going through changes such as moving home or starting a business.
  • Your data. Retarget website visitors and channel viewers, and upload customer lists where permitted.

We keep campaigns structured so that each audience can be judged on its own results, which makes it easier to move budget towards what works.

Creative that holds attention

On YouTube, the first few seconds decide whether someone skips. The strongest ads open with the product, the problem or a person speaking directly to camera, show the brand early and give a clear next step. They are also built to make sense without sound, because many people watch muted on their phones.

We plan scripts and edits in English. Arabic scripts, voice-overs and subtitles are written with your team or a professional translator, and we recommend a fluent speaker checks every version. We can edit supplied footage into ad lengths and formats, but we cannot film in the UAE. Our guide to using video on your website has more ideas for getting value from footage you already own.

If you also run short-form social campaigns, creative can often be shared. Our guides to TikTok Ads in the UAE and Meta Ads in the UAE explain how those platforms handle similar vertical video.

Tracking and measuring results

Views alone do not pay the bills. We set up conversion tracking for the actions that matter, such as form submissions, calls, WhatsApp clicks, purchases or bookings, and connect YouTube campaigns to Google Analytics 4. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking explains the setup in more detail.

We also look at signals that show video is working even when the final conversion happens later, such as increases in brand searches, returning visitors and view-through conversions. Video often plays a supporting role, so we judge it alongside your search and social results rather than in isolation.

Policies, rules and working remotely

Google has advertising policies for YouTube, and some categories such as financial services, healthcare and alcohol are restricted or need certification. The UAE also has its own rules on advertising, consumer protection and data, and these change over time. We keep ad claims accurate and follow platform policies, but we are not legal advisers, so check current official UAE guidance and confirm sensitive claims with your own compliance team.

Working with us remotely is straightforward. The UAE is a few hours ahead of the UK, which leaves a good overlap for calls. You keep ownership of your Google Ads account, YouTube channel and data, and we work through shared documents, agreed call times and regular written reports.

FAQs

What does a YouTube Ads agency in the UAE do?

It plans audiences, guides creative, sets up tracking and manages YouTube campaigns inside Google Ads so your video spend leads to enquiries, sales or brand growth. We do this remotely from the UK.

Do you have an office in the UAE?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK and manages UAE campaigns remotely through video calls, shared documents and direct access to your ad account.

Can you run YouTube ads in Arabic?

Yes. We run separate Arabic and English campaigns where needed. Arabic scripts, subtitles and copy are written with your team or a professional translator.

Do you make the videos?

We plan scripts and edit supplied footage into ad formats, but we cannot film in the UAE. A local production partner or your own team can capture footage for us to shape into ads.

How much should we spend on YouTube ads?

It depends on your goals and how many audiences and creatives you want to test. We agree a starting budget during planning, and ad spend is paid directly to Google.

How long do YouTube campaigns take to show results?

Early data appears within weeks, but paid results typically build over 3 to 6 months as targeting and creative are refined.
