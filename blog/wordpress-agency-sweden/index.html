WordPress Agency for Swedish Businesses: Remote Builds and Support from a UK Studio

Looking for a WordPress agency in Sweden? How Swedish and English WordPress sites are built, secured and maintained remotely, plus our website fees in pounds.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A WordPress agency for a Swedish business should build a fast, secure site with Swedish and English versions, an editor your team can use and a clear plan for updates and support.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Sweden; we work remotely, write in English and work with your team or a translator on Swedish copy.
  • Small websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and e-commerce sites £1,500 to £3,500, quoted in pounds with other currencies on request, and most websites take three to five weeks.

A WordPress agency for a Swedish business should give you a fast, secure website that your own team can update, in the languages your customers use. That means a lean theme, carefully chosen plugins, a clean multilingual structure and a plan for updates, backups and support after launch.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that builds and supports WordPress websites remotely for businesses across Europe. We have no office in Stockholm or anywhere in Sweden. Our website and e-commerce development service runs by video call, shared documents and staged previews, with Sweden one hour ahead of UK time. We write in English and work with your team or a translator on Swedish pages.

Why WordPress suits many Swedish businesses

Sweden has a highly digital economy, with technology companies, design-led consumer brands, manufacturers and exporters, professional services firms and public organisations spread across Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö and smaller cities. Many of them need websites that work in Swedish for domestic customers and in English for international buyers, partners and candidates.

WordPress is a common choice because:

  • Editors can manage content themselves. The block editor lets marketing teams publish news, case pages and job adverts without a developer.
  • Multilingual set-ups are well supported. Established plugins handle Swedish and English versions with linked pages and hreflang.
  • It is flexible. The same platform can run a brochure site, a content-led marketing site or a WooCommerce shop.
  • You own the site. You can move hosting or change agencies without rebuilding from scratch.

Swedish and English content

Most Swedish businesses need Swedish as their main language, but English matters for exporters, technology firms and companies recruiting internationally. Many Swedes read English comfortably, yet Swedish pages still tend to feel more natural for domestic buyers and are needed to rank for Swedish searches.

We set up each language with its own URLs, linked translations and hreflang so search engines show the right version. Prism writes English copy in-house. For Swedish, we supply the page structure, headings and English drafts, then a native speaker on your team or a translator writes the final copy, which we load and check before launch. Our multilingual website design guide explains the structure in more detail.

Themes, plugins and performance

Many slow WordPress sites suffer from the same problems: heavy multipurpose themes, page builders that add bloated code and dozens of plugins installed over the years. We take a different approach:

  1. A lean, custom theme. Built for your content, using the block editor and reusable patterns so editors stay within the design.
  2. Few, well-maintained plugins. Each plugin is chosen for a clear reason and checked for updates and support.
  3. Optimised images and fonts. Compressed images, modern formats and efficient font loading.
  4. Caching and good hosting. Server-level caching and a host suited to your traffic. Our guide to WordPress hosting covers what to look for.

Where hosting location matters for data protection, we can discuss hosts with servers in the EU so your legal adviser can assess the set-up.

WooCommerce for Swedish online shops

If you sell online, WooCommerce turns WordPress into a full shop. Swedish shoppers are used to a range of payment methods. Klarna, a Swedish company, is widely used for card and pay-later payments, and Swish is a popular mobile payment method in Sweden. Whether these are available depends on your payment provider and bank, so we confirm options with you before the build. For larger catalogues or brands focused purely on e-commerce, Shopify may be a better fit, and we will say so if it is.

Rebuilding or moving an existing site

Many Swedish businesses come to us with an older WordPress site that has grown slow or hard to edit, or with a site on another platform they want to move to WordPress. In both cases, the rebuild should protect what already works. We audit existing pages, decide what to keep, merge or retire, and map every old URL to its new equivalent so you keep the search rankings and links you have earned. Our guide to website redirects when relaunching explains why this step matters.

Security, updates and maintenance

WordPress is secure when it is maintained. Most problems come from outdated plugins, weak passwords and abandoned themes. Every site we build includes:

  • Regular updates. Core, theme and plugin updates tested before they go live.
  • Backups. Automatic, off-site backups with a tested restore process.
  • Access control. Strong passwords, two-factor login and sensible user roles.
  • Monitoring. Uptime and security checks with alerts.

Our guides to WordPress security and website maintenance explain what ongoing support should cover.

Consent, privacy and accessibility

Swedish websites fall under the GDPR for personal data, and the EU ePrivacy rules mean analytics and marketing cookies normally need consent before they load. We set up a clear consent banner and connect analytics to it. Contact forms, newsletter sign-ups and WooCommerce orders all need clear privacy wording.

The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer-facing products and services, including a wide range of online shops. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt, but building to WCAG AA, the level referenced by the harmonised standard EN 301 549, is good practice for any WordPress site. Legal wording and decisions on compliance should come from your legal adviser, and official guidance should be checked before relying on any summary.

Website fees and timelines

Our website prices are listed on our pricing page. A small WordPress website typically costs £1,000 to £2,000, and an e-commerce site typically costs £1,500 to £3,500, depending on the number of pages, products, languages and integrations. We quote in pounds, and quotes in other currencies are available on request. Swedish translation is arranged and paid separately if you do not have an in-house writer.

Most websites take three to five weeks, and e-commerce builds typically take five to twelve weeks. Bilingual projects can take longer if translated copy arrives late, so we agree content deadlines at the start.

FAQs

Do you have a WordPress agency office in Sweden?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Sweden. We work remotely by video call, shared documents and staged previews, one hour behind Swedish time.

Can you build a WordPress site in Swedish and English?

Yes. We set up both languages with linked pages, a language switcher and hreflang. English copy is written in-house, and Swedish copy comes from your team or a native-speaking translator.

How much does a WordPress agency in Sweden cost with your studio?

A small WordPress website typically costs £1,000 to £2,000 and an e-commerce site £1,500 to £3,500. We quote in pounds, with other currencies on request. Translation is separate.

Can WooCommerce accept Klarna and Swish?

Often, yes, but it depends on your payment provider and bank. We check which methods your chosen provider supports before the build starts.

Do you maintain WordPress sites after launch?

Yes. We can handle updates, backups, security monitoring and small changes. Ongoing support is agreed separately from the build.

Will my site meet GDPR and accessibility rules?

We build in consent, privacy pages and WCAG AA accessibility practices. Your legal adviser should approve privacy wording and confirm how the GDPR and the European Accessibility Act apply to your business.
