Shopify Agency New York: How a Remote Shopify Partner Works for NYC Brands

Looking for a Shopify agency in New York? A remote studio can design, build and grow your store. See how it works, what it costs and what to ask before hiring.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good Shopify store for a New York brand depends on a clear catalogue plan, a fast theme, clean checkout settings and solid tracking, not on the agency being based in the city.
  • New York is usually five hours behind UK time, so remote Shopify projects use a staging theme, recorded walkthroughs and calls in the NYC morning.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US clients; Shopify builds are priced in pounds and other currencies are quoted on request.

If you are looking for a Shopify agency in New York, the things that decide your result are how well the store is planned, how fast and clear the theme is, how the checkout and shipping are configured and whether tracking works from day one. A remote Shopify agency can deliver all of that for a New York brand, as long as the project runs on a written scope, a staging theme for reviews and regular check-ins.

We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US businesses. We do not have a New York office or any office in the US. This guide covers what a Shopify agency should deliver, how a remote build runs, what it costs, how long it takes and what to ask before you hire anyone.

What a Shopify agency in New York should deliver

New York is home to a huge range of direct-to-consumer brands: fashion and accessories, beauty and skincare, food and drink, homeware, art and design, and plenty of founders selling across the US from a small studio or shared space. Whatever you sell, a Shopify project should usually include:

  • Discovery: agreeing your customers, product range, margins, sales channels and growth plans.
  • Catalogue structure: collections, product types, tags, variants and metafields set up so the store is easy to browse and easy to manage.
  • Theme and design: a fast, well-supported theme customised to your brand, or custom sections where you need them.
  • Product pages: clear descriptions, good photography, size or spec guidance and reviews you genuinely collect.
  • Checkout, shipping and tax settings: configured correctly for the states and countries you sell to.
  • Apps: only the ones you need, because every extra app can slow the store and add monthly cost.
  • Tracking: analytics, Google and Meta tracking set up and tested before launch.
  • SEO foundations and redirects: clean URLs, titles and redirects from any old store.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers all of this, and our ecommerce sector page explains how we support online brands after launch.

How a remote Shopify project runs

New York is usually five hours behind UK time, with a brief change in spring and autumn when the clocks move on different dates. Calls happen in the New York morning, and the rest of the project runs in writing and on a staging theme you can preview at any time.

  1. Brief and discovery call: products, customers, channels, integrations and must-have features.
  2. Proposal: fixed scope, price and timeline, with exclusions written down.
  3. Store plan: collection structure, navigation, page list and app shortlist agreed before design.
  4. Design: homepage, collection and product templates shared for feedback.
  5. Build: work happens on an unpublished theme so your live store, if you have one, is untouched.
  6. Testing: test orders, discount codes, shipping rates, tax settings, emails and tracking checked end to end.
  7. Launch and aftercare: go-live at an agreed time, then fixes, training and optional ongoing support.

A clear brief saves time and money on any Shopify project. Our guide on how to brief a Shopify developer lists what to prepare before the first call.

Local Shopify agency or remote Shopify agency

New York has many Shopify agencies, and if you value in-person workshops or photo shoots run by the same team, a local agency may suit you better. A remote studio offers a different set of strengths. Here are the honest trade-offs.

Whichever route you take, the Shopify store should be created under your own account, with the agency invited as a collaborator. That way you keep full control of billing, data and apps.

If you would prefer a quote in another currency, we can provide one on request. Shopify's own subscription, theme licence and app fees are paid by you directly and are separate from our fee.

How long a Shopify build takes

A Shopify build usually takes five to twelve weeks from agreed brief to launch. A small catalogue on a well-supported theme sits at the shorter end; large catalogues, custom sections, subscriptions or complex integrations take longer. The biggest causes of delay are product data and photography arriving late, so we agree a content plan at the start.

It also helps to decide early which features are essential for launch and which can follow. Subscriptions, wholesale pricing, bundles or a loyalty scheme can all be added later, and launching with a smaller, well-tested store is usually better than delaying for features you are not yet sure you need.

Moving to Shopify from another platform

Many New York brands start on a marketplace or another platform and move to Shopify once they want more control over their brand and customer data. A migration needs careful handling of products, customers, order history and URLs so you do not lose search traffic or repeat buyers. Our guides to moving from Etsy to Shopify and moving from WooCommerce to Shopify explain the steps. For larger brands, we also handle Shopify Plus migrations.

Design choices that sell to New York shoppers

New York shoppers are used to polished brands and quick checkouts. A few choices make a real difference:

  • Speed: compressed images, a lean theme and a short app list keep pages fast on mobile.
  • Clear shipping and returns: state delivery times and costs plainly, and make returns easy to understand.
  • Strong product pages: real photos from several angles, sizing or dimensions and honest descriptions.
  • Simple navigation: collections that match how customers think, not how the warehouse is organised.
  • Trust signals: genuine reviews, secure payment options and a clear contact route.

Our guide to Shopify conversion rate optimisation covers more ways to turn visits into orders, and our guide to Shopify speed optimisation explains how to keep the store fast as it grows.

Growing the store after launch

Launch is the starting point. Most stores grow through a mix of search, paid ads, email and marketplace sales. Our guide to ecommerce SEO for US DTC brands explains how organic search fits in, and our guide to working with a Shopify agency as a US brand covers contracts, billing and communication across time zones.

Tax, privacy and legal points

US sales tax rules vary by state and depend on where you have nexus, and Shopify's tax settings need to reflect your position. Privacy, consumer protection and accessibility rules also apply to online stores. We configure Shopify carefully and build to good practice, but the legal and tax position depends on federal and state rules that change, so please check current official guidance and speak to your own accountant or lawyer. Decisions on tax registration and compliance rest with your business.

FAQs

Do you have an office in New York?

No. We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US clients. We do not have a New York office or any office in the US. Calls take place by video in the New York morning.

How much does a Shopify agency in New York cost with your studio?

An ecommerce website with us typically costs £1,500 to £3,500 depending on scope. Shopify subscription and app fees are separate, and we can quote our fee in other currencies on request.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Most Shopify builds take five to twelve weeks from agreed brief to launch, depending on catalogue size, custom features and how quickly product content is ready.

Will I own my Shopify store?

Yes. The store is created under your own Shopify account and billing, and we work as an invited collaborator. You keep full control of the store, domain, apps and data.

Can you migrate my existing store to Shopify?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and content, map old URLs to new ones and set up redirects so you keep search traffic and repeat customers. We also handle Shopify Plus migrations.

Can you set up US sales tax in Shopify?

We can configure Shopify's tax settings to match the position you confirm with your accountant. US sales tax depends on your nexus in each state, so the registration decisions should come from your own tax adviser.
