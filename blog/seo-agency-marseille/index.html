Why Marseille search is its own market

Marseille is France's second city by population and a port city with a very mixed economy: logistics and shipping, tourism and cruises, hospitality, healthcare, construction, professional services, retail and a growing tech and creative scene. Search behaviour reflects that mix. A logistics firm near the port is chasing business buyers across France and the Mediterranean, while a restaurant in the Vieux-Port or a dentist in the 8th arrondissement wants people within a few kilometres.

People in Marseille often search by arrondissement or neighbourhood, such as Le Panier, Castellane, La Joliette, Prado or Les Goudes, and by nearby towns such as Aix-en-Provence, Aubagne, Cassis and La Ciotat. A good plan decides early which of those areas you genuinely serve and builds pages only for those, rather than copying the same text across dozens of thin location pages.

Competition differs by sector. Tourism and hospitality terms are crowded with booking platforms and directories. Trades and local services compete mostly in the map pack. Business-to-business services compete with national firms based in Paris and Lyon, which is why a clear specialism and strong supporting content matter so much.

What an SEO agency in Marseille should cover

Whether you hire a firm down the road or a remote studio like ours, the core work is the same. We group it into four areas.

  • Technical SEO: crawling, indexing, site speed, mobile layout, structured data, redirects, duplicate pages and language settings. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows what we look at first.
  • On-page SEO: titles, headings, service pages, internal links and answers to the questions buyers ask before they enquire.
  • Local SEO: your Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details, reviews you earn yourself, and area pages for places you really serve. The principles in our local SEO guide apply in France too.
  • Content and links: guides, comparisons and resources that earn links and mentions from relevant French and Mediterranean sites.

We also look at how your pages appear in AI answers. Google's AI Overviews and chat tools pull from pages that answer questions clearly and directly, so we structure key pages for that. Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO explains the approach.

Language: French first, English where it helps

Most Marseille customers search in French, so the main version of your site should be in French. English pages can make sense for tourism, hospitality, shipping, international recruitment or business services that sell abroad.

Because Prism writes in English, we split the work clearly. We handle keyword research, page planning, technical set-up, briefs and on-page structure. Your team or a translator writes or adapts the French copy, and we then check that the headings, titles and links match the plan. If you run both French and English versions, we set up language tags and a clean URL structure so search engines show the right version to the right people.

SEO for key Marseille sectors

Every sector needs a slightly different plan. Here is how the work tends to change.

Hospitality and tourism. Hotels, guesthouses, restaurants and tour operators compete with large platforms. The aim is to win branded searches, specific local searches and direct bookings, with fast pages, clear menus or room details, and strong map listings.

Trades and home services. Plumbers, electricians, renovation firms and cleaners mostly win through the map pack and a small set of strong service and area pages. Reviews and accurate listings carry a lot of weight.

Healthcare and clinics. Dentists, physiotherapists and private clinics need accurate, careful content. Health advertising and professional rules apply in France, so we keep content factual and general, point readers to official sources, and the final approval of any claim rests with you and your professional body or adviser.

Logistics, shipping and business services. These firms sell to buyers across France and beyond, so the plan focuses on specialist service pages, sector pages and practical guides that answer procurement questions.

Ecommerce. Online shops based in Marseille need category structure, product page SEO and clean filters. If paid social is part of your mix, our guide to Meta Ads in France shows how it fits alongside search.

How Marseille compares with other French cities

If you trade across France, it helps to see how other cities differ. Paris is the most competitive market for almost every service term, which we cover in our page on an SEO agency in Paris. Lyon has a strong business services and industrial base, covered in our SEO agency Lyon guide. Marseille sits between the two: very competitive for tourism, more open for many local and specialist services.

For businesses with branches in several cities, we usually build one strong service page per service and a genuine page for each location, with local details, staff contact routes and directions, rather than duplicating the same text with the city name swapped.

Choosing an SEO agency in Marseille

A good partner should be able to explain, in plain terms, what they will do in the first ninety days, why, and how you will know whether it is working. Questions worth asking any agency:

  • Who writes the French content, and who checks it?
  • Which areas and services will you build pages for, and why those?
  • How will you handle my Google Business Profile and reviews?
  • What will the monthly report show, and how does it link to enquiries?
  • Can I leave, and do I keep all the content and accounts if I do?

Be wary of any agency that guarantees a first-place ranking, promises a fixed number of leads, or will not tell you which links it plans to build. Nobody controls Google's results, and shortcuts with links or copied content can harm a site for a long time.
