Google Shopping Agency for US Stores: How Remote Shopping Ads Management Works

Need a Google Shopping agency for your US store? A remote team can fix your feed, run Shopping and Performance Max campaigns and report on profit. Here is how.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Google Shopping performance for a US store starts with the product feed, not the campaign settings.
  • A remote agency can manage Merchant Center, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns entirely through user access on your own accounts.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US clients; management fees are in pounds, other currencies on request, and ad spend is paid separately.

If you are looking for a Google Shopping agency for your US store, the work that matters most is a clean product feed, a sensible campaign structure and reporting based on profit rather than clicks. A remote agency can handle all of this through access to your own Merchant Center and Google Ads accounts, with no need for a local office.

We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US online stores. We do not have a US office. This guide explains what a Google Shopping agency for US stores should do, how remote management works, what it costs and how to judge whether your current setup is holding you back.

What a Google Shopping agency for US stores should do

Shopping ads show a product image, title, price and store name directly in Google results. Because Google builds those ads from your product data, managing Shopping is part advertising and part data work. A good agency should cover:

  • Merchant Center setup and health: account verification, shipping and returns settings, tax settings and fixing disapprovals.
  • Feed optimisation: better product titles, descriptions, categories, identifiers and custom labels.
  • Campaign structure: deciding how to split products between Standard Shopping and Performance Max campaigns.
  • Bidding and budgets: choosing bid strategies that match your margins and stock levels.
  • Search terms and exclusions: removing irrelevant searches where the campaign type allows it.
  • Tracking: accurate conversion tracking, including purchase values, so bidding works from real revenue.
  • Reporting: results by product, category and margin band, not just one blended figure.

Our guide to Google Shopping feed and campaign management goes deeper into the day-to-day work, and our Performance Max guide explains how that campaign type behaves.

Why the product feed matters most

Shopping ads have no keywords in the usual sense. Google matches searches to products using the information in your feed, so a weak feed limits everything else. Common problems we see in store feeds include:

  • Product titles that use internal names instead of the words shoppers search for.
  • Missing or incorrect product identifiers such as GTINs and brand names.
  • Variants such as sizes and colours not linked properly.
  • Shipping costs or delivery times in Merchant Center that do not match the website.
  • Prices or availability that drift out of sync with the store, leading to disapprovals.

Fixing these issues often improves how often products show and how relevant the clicks are, before a single bid changes. If your Merchant Center account needs to be set up from scratch, our guide to Google Merchant Center setup walks through the steps, which are broadly the same for a US account.

Standard Shopping or Performance Max?

Most US stores now run some products through Performance Max, which uses Google's automation across Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and other placements. Standard Shopping still has a place where you want tighter control. The right mix depends on your catalogue, budget and data.

Using custom labels to steer budget

Custom labels are optional fields in your product feed that let you group products in ways Google does not know about by default. They are one of the simplest ways to make Shopping campaigns reflect how your business actually makes money. Useful labels include:

  • Margin band: high, medium and low margin products, so you can set different return targets for each.
  • Best sellers: products with a proven sales record that deserve protected budget.
  • Seasonal lines: products that matter at certain times of year, so they can be pushed or paused quickly.
  • Clearance: stock you want to move, even at a lower return.
  • New arrivals: products without history that need a chance to gather data.

Once labels are in the feed, campaigns can be split or prioritised around them. That means a low-margin product no longer competes for the same budget as your most profitable lines, and you can see clearly which groups are pulling their weight.

US-specific points for Shopping campaigns

Running Shopping ads for US customers involves a few details that UK guides do not always cover:

  • Sales tax: US tax rules vary by state, and Merchant Center has its own tax settings. We can configure the account to match how your store charges tax, but tax obligations themselves should be confirmed with your accountant.
  • Shipping by region: delivery times and costs can differ widely across the country, and Merchant Center lets you reflect that by region.
  • Seasonality: US shopping peaks, such as Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the holiday season, need budgets, stock and promotions planned weeks ahead.
  • Promotions: Merchant Center promotions can show offers alongside your products, but they must match what the website actually gives shoppers.
  • Policies: Google's product and advertising policies change over time, so check current official guidance for restricted categories such as supplements or health products.

How remote Google Shopping management works

Shopping management is entirely account-based work, so it suits a remote arrangement well. A typical engagement with us runs like this:

  1. Audit: a review of your Merchant Center diagnostics, feed quality, campaigns, tracking and recent results.
  2. Access: you add us as users on Merchant Center, Google Ads and analytics. You keep ownership of every account.
  3. Fix and restructure: feed improvements, tracking fixes and a campaign structure built around your margins.
  4. Ongoing optimisation: regular checks on search terms, product performance, budgets and disapprovals.
  5. Reporting: a monthly written report and a short video call to agree priorities.

The time difference helps. US mornings overlap with the UK afternoon, so calls are easy to schedule, and checks we run during our morning can catch overnight disapprovals or budget problems before your day starts. What Google Shopping management costs

Our paid media retainers are priced in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from our fees.

  • Launch: £350 a month, suited to a smaller catalogue and a single campaign structure.
  • Growth: £750 a month, for larger catalogues, more feed work and closer optimisation.
  • Scale: £1,500 a month, for bigger budgets, several campaign types or multiple channels.

Paid results typically take three to six months to settle, as campaigns gather data and the feed improves. Our pricing page explains what each level includes, and our paid media service shows how Shopping fits alongside search, social and video advertising.

Signs your Shopping campaigns need help

You may benefit from an agency, or a fresh pair of eyes, if any of these sound familiar:

  • Products are frequently disapproved or limited in Merchant Center.
  • Spend keeps rising but you cannot see which products make money.
  • All products sit in one campaign with one budget and one target.
  • Conversion values in Google Ads do not match your store's sales reports.
  • Your best-selling products rarely appear for the searches you expect.

None of these need a large budget to fix. Often the biggest gains come from cleaner data and clearer priorities rather than more spend.

FAQs

Do you work as a Google Shopping agency for US stores?

Yes. We are a UK-based studio and we manage Google Shopping remotely for US online stores. We do not have a US office, and all work happens through user access on your own Merchant Center and Google Ads accounts.

How much does a Google Shopping agency cost?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month, with other currencies quoted on request. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from our fees.

Do I need Performance Max or Standard Shopping?

It depends on your catalogue, budget and conversion data. Many stores use both: Standard Shopping for control and testing, and Performance Max to scale products that already sell well.

Can you fix Merchant Center disapprovals?

Usually, yes. Most disapprovals come from feed data, website mismatches or policy issues. We review the diagnostics, fix what can be fixed in the feed or site and explain anything that needs a decision from you.

Will I keep ownership of my accounts?

Yes. You own your Merchant Center, Google Ads and analytics accounts and add us as users. If we stop working together, you simply remove our access and keep all your data.

How long before Shopping campaigns improve?

Feed and tracking fixes can help within weeks, but paid results typically take three to six months to settle as campaigns gather data and bidding adjusts to your real sales.
