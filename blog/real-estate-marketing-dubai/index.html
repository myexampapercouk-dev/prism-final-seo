Real Estate Marketing in Dubai: Winning Enquiries for Brokers and Developers

Real estate marketing in Dubai: how brokers and developers win buyer and tenant enquiries with Google, Meta, SEO and fast landing pages, run from the UK.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Real estate marketing in Dubai works best when paid search, social ads, SEO and fast bilingual landing pages work together, with every enquiry tracked back to its source.
  • We are a UK-based studio that plans and runs property campaigns remotely for Dubai brokers and developers, with no office in the UAE; Arabic content is written with your team or a translator.
  • Retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, ad spend is paid separately, other currencies are quoted on request, and advertising approvals rest with your compliance team.

Real estate marketing in Dubai is the work of putting the right properties, projects and services in front of buyers, investors and tenants, then turning their interest into qualified enquiries for your brokers or sales team. In a crowded market, the businesses that do it well combine search advertising, social ads, SEO and fast, bilingual landing pages, and they track every lead back to the campaign that produced it.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We run property campaigns through our paid media service and work remotely with brokers, developers and property service firms in Dubai and across the UAE. We have no office in the UAE. This guide explains how we approach real estate marketing in Dubai, which channels matter and what it costs.

Who this guide is for

Dubai property businesses come in many shapes, and each needs a slightly different plan. We most often work with:

  • Brokerages marketing resale and rental listings and building a steady flow of buyer, seller and landlord enquiries.
  • Developers launching off-plan projects and needing registrations, brochure downloads and sales centre visits.
  • Property management and holiday home operators looking for landlords and owners.
  • Related services such as mortgage advisers, interior design and fit-out firms, conveyancing support and relocation services.

The audience also varies. Some campaigns target UAE residents moving within the city, while others target overseas investors in the UK, Europe, the wider Gulf, South Asia and beyond. The channels, languages and messages change accordingly.

Paid search for property

Google Search Ads are usually the first channel we set up for a Dubai property business, because they reach people at the moment they are looking. Searches for a community, a building, a project name or a property type with a budget or bedroom count often carry strong intent. We build campaigns around those themes, separate buyers from tenants, exclude searches that waste budget and send each advert to the most relevant page rather than the home page.

Location targeting needs care. A campaign aimed at residents should focus on the UAE, while an investor campaign may target selected countries in the languages those buyers use. Each audience needs its own ads, landing pages and budget so results are easy to read.

Social ads and off-plan launches

Meta, TikTok and YouTube help create demand before people search. They are especially useful for off-plan launches, where a developer needs registrations of interest in a short window. Strong creative matters: drone footage, walkthroughs, floor plan highlights, lifestyle clips of the community and clear details of the offer and payment structure, where those details are approved for advertising.

Instant lead forms on Meta can deliver many enquiries quickly, but quality varies. We usually add qualifying questions, test forms against landing pages and make sure every lead reaches your CRM or sales team within minutes. In property, the speed of follow-up often matters as much as the advert itself.

SEO and content for property businesses

Organic search builds a steady base of enquiries that does not stop when ad spend pauses. For property businesses, the most useful content tends to be area and community guides, building and project pages, guides to buying, renting and service charges, and pages for each property type you handle. Content should be accurate, regularly updated and helpful rather than thin pages created only to target keywords.

Listing portals dominate many property searches, so we focus SEO on the areas where your own site can genuinely compete: your expertise, your projects and the questions buyers ask. Our guide to SEO in Dubai explains our wider approach, and results typically build over 3 to 6 months.

Landing pages and websites that convert

Most property enquiries now happen on a phone. A landing page for a project or a listing campaign should load quickly, show the key facts at the top, include strong images and a short video where possible, and offer clear contact routes: a short form, a call button and WhatsApp. Brochure downloads and floor plans can be offered in exchange for contact details, as long as visitors are told clearly how their information will be used.

Because buyers and tenants in Dubai speak many languages, pages often need Arabic and English versions, and sometimes more. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator. Our guides to landing page design, bilingual Arabic and English websites and web design in Dubai cover the details.

Tracking leads from click to viewing

Property marketing is only as good as its tracking. We set up conversion tracking for forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks and brochure downloads, use consistent campaign tags and, where your systems allow, pass lead outcomes back from your CRM so campaigns can be judged on qualified leads and viewings rather than raw form fills. Monthly reports show spend, enquiries and cost per enquiry by channel and campaign, with plain-language next steps.

Timing matters as well. Demand in Dubai shifts through the year, with quieter and busier periods around the summer months, Ramadan, school calendars and major property events. Developers often concentrate spend around a launch, while brokerages need a steadier flow of enquiries all year. We plan budgets and creative around these patterns, keep evergreen campaigns running for core areas and property types, and add short bursts of activity for launches, price changes or new listings rather than spending the same amount every week regardless of demand.

We also ask your sales team for regular feedback on lead quality. That feedback is often the fastest way to improve targeting, questions and creative, and it stops budget being spent on enquiries that never become viewings.

Advertising rules for Dubai property

Property advertising in Dubai is regulated, and there are rules on who may advertise, what permits or registration details are needed and how prices, payment plans and returns can be described. Ad platforms also have their own policies for housing and financial claims. We avoid misleading claims and follow platform policies, but we are not legal advisers. Check official Dubai and UAE guidance, and make sure your compliance team or adviser approves every advert and landing page before it runs.

Learning from other markets

Many principles of property marketing travel well between markets. Our guides to estate agent marketing and property developer marketing are written for the UK, but the ideas on positioning, launch planning and lead handling apply to Dubai brokers and developers too. The time difference between the UK and the UAE is small, so we can hold calls during your working day and react quickly during launches.

FAQs

What is the best real estate marketing in Dubai?

Most property businesses do best with Google Search Ads for active searchers, Meta or video ads for launches and retargeting, SEO for long-term enquiries and fast bilingual landing pages.

Do you have an office in Dubai?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK and works remotely with Dubai property businesses through video calls, shared documents and your own ad accounts.

Can you market off-plan projects?

Yes. We plan launch campaigns, landing pages and lead tracking for off-plan projects. All claims and payment plan details must be approved by your compliance team.

How much does property marketing cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month. Ad spend is paid separately, and other currencies, including dirhams, are quoted on request.

Can you target overseas investors?

Yes. We can run separate campaigns for selected countries with their own ads, languages and landing pages, subject to platform policies and local advertising rules.

How soon will campaigns bring enquiries?

Paid campaigns can bring enquiries soon after launch, but results typically improve over 3 to 6 months as targeting and creative are refined. SEO takes longer.
