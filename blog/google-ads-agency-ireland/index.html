Google Ads Agency for Irish Businesses: How Remote PPC Management Works

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Ireland? What good PPC management covers for Irish campaigns, how a remote UK studio works with you and how fees are set.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Google Ads for an Irish business works best with Ireland-specific targeting, keywords, ad copy and landing pages, rather than a UK campaign stretched across the border.
  • A remote, UK-based studio can set up, manage and report on Google Ads for Irish companies, working in the same time zone with your accounts held in your name.
  • Management is priced as a monthly retainer in pounds, with other currencies such as euro quoted on request, and ad spend is always paid separately to Google.

A Google Ads agency for an Irish business should run campaigns that reach the right people in Ireland, at a cost that makes sense for your margins, and prove it with clear conversion tracking. Good management covers account structure, Irish location and language targeting, keyword and search term control, ad copy, landing pages, bidding and honest monthly reporting.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages paid media campaigns for businesses in the UK, Ireland and across Europe, working remotely. We do not have an Irish office and will not claim one. We work in the same time zone as Ireland, with accounts set up in your name and regular calls to review performance. This guide covers what to expect from any Google Ads partner, the questions to ask and how fees usually work.

Why Irish Google Ads campaigns need their own setup

The Google Ads platform is the same in every country, but the market around it is not. Treating Ireland as an add-on to a UK account is one of the most common causes of wasted spend.

  • Smaller search volumes. Ireland has fewer searches per keyword than the UK. Campaigns need enough budget per campaign for smart bidding to learn, which often means fewer, broader campaigns rather than dozens of tiny ones.
  • Precise location targeting. Targeting the island of Ireland, the Republic only, a county or a radius around a branch gives very different results. The default setting can also show ads to people merely interested in a location. Our location targeting guide explains the options.
  • Currency and pricing. Ads and landing pages should show euro prices to Irish users, and the account currency should be chosen deliberately, because it cannot be changed later.
  • Local wording. Irish searchers sometimes use different terms from UK searchers for trades, products and services. Search term reports show the real language, which then shapes keywords and ad copy.
  • Competition from UK advertisers. Many UK businesses also target Ireland, so clear local relevance in your ads and pages helps you stand out.

Conversion tracking comes first

Without reliable conversion tracking, nobody can tell which keywords and ads are earning their keep, and automated bidding has nothing useful to learn from. Before increasing spend, we check that form submissions, calls, bookings or purchases are recorded properly and counted once.

In Ireland, as across the EU, GDPR and the ePrivacy rules mean analytics and advertising cookies generally need consent. Google's consent mode lets tags adjust to each visitor's choice, but it needs to be configured correctly with your consent banner. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking setup covers the main steps. We set this up in line with common practice, but we are not lawyers, so check current official guidance for your situation.

Keeping spend focused

Most wasted budget in Google Ads comes from search terms that were never going to convert. Regular search term reviews and a well-maintained negative keyword list are some of the simplest ways to improve results. Our negative keywords guide explains how to build and maintain one.

Campaign types matter too. Search campaigns give the most control and suit most service businesses. Performance Max can work well for online stores with a good product feed and solid tracking, but it needs careful setup and monitoring. Our Performance Max guide covers when it fits and when it does not.

Landing pages are part of the picture. An ad that promises a fast quote in Dublin should land on a page that delivers exactly that, with a short form and a visible phone number. Our landing page design guide sets out what good looks like.

Running Ireland and the UK in one account

If you advertise in both Ireland and the UK, the usual approach is separate campaigns for each country, with their own budgets, location targeting, ad copy and landing pages. That keeps reporting clear and stops one market quietly using up the other's budget.

Some businesses use separate accounts for each country, particularly where billing currencies or legal entities differ. The right choice depends on how your business is set up, which we agree during onboarding.

Setting a sensible starting budget

There is no single right budget for Google Ads in Ireland. A practical way to start is to work backwards from what a lead or sale is worth to you, the likely cost per click in your sector and how many conversions bidding needs to learn. A modest test budget on a few well-chosen search campaigns usually teaches more than a large budget spread thinly across every possible keyword. Once tracking shows which campaigns produce enquiries at a cost you are happy with, budget can move towards them with confidence. We agree budgets with you and never change them without your sign-off.

How a remote Google Ads agency works with Irish clients

PPC management is almost entirely online work, so distance makes little practical difference when the process is clear.

  1. Kick-off call. We agree goals, target areas, budget, margins and how a lead or sale is valued.
  2. Access and audit. You grant access to your Google Ads, analytics and tag manager accounts. If you have an existing account, we audit it first; our PPC audit checklist shows what we review.
  3. Build or rebuild. We fix tracking, then set up or restructure campaigns, keywords, ads and assets.
  4. Ongoing optimisation. Search term reviews, bid and budget adjustments, ad testing and landing page recommendations.
  5. Monthly reporting. A clear report and call covering spend, results, what we changed and what comes next.

All accounts stay in your business name, so you keep full ownership and history whatever happens.

How much does a Google Ads agency cost in Ireland?

There are two separate costs: what you pay Google for clicks, and what you pay for management. Ad spend is always paid directly to Google and is never included in our fees. Our management retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, depending on the number of campaigns, markets and the level of support you need. Prices are in pounds by default, and other currencies such as euro can be quoted on request.

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Ireland?

Look for an agency that checks conversion tracking first, builds Ireland-specific campaigns, keeps accounts in your name and reports clearly on cost per lead or sale. Ask how often they review search terms and what notice period applies. Location matters less than transparency.

Do you have an office in Ireland?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK and manages Google Ads for Irish businesses remotely, using video calls, shared reports and direct access to your accounts. We share a time zone with Ireland for most of the year.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own account. Our retainer covers setup, management, optimisation and reporting only.

Can you run campaigns for Ireland and the UK together?

Yes. We usually set up separate campaigns per country so budgets, targeting, ad copy and landing pages suit each market, and reporting stays clear.

Should my Google Ads account be in euro?

If your business is Irish and pays in euro, a euro account usually makes reporting simpler. The account currency cannot be changed after setup, so it is worth deciding carefully before creating a new account.

How quickly will I see results from Google Ads?

Ads can bring traffic and enquiries soon after launch, but stable, efficient performance typically takes three to six months as data builds up, search terms are refined and ads and landing pages are tested.
