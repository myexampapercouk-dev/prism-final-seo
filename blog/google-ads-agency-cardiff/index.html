Google Ads Agency Cardiff: Paid Search That Brings Calls, Bookings and Sales

A Google Ads agency in Cardiff should put your business in front of people actively searching for what you offer, track every call, form and sale, and keep refining the account so your cost per lead falls over time. Prism Works Studio manages Google Ads for Cardiff businesses as a UK-based studio that works remotely, on fixed monthly retainers with your ad spend paid directly to Google.

Cardiff is the capital of Wales and the commercial centre for South Wales, with a busy mix of professional services, hospitality, retail, trades, creative firms and students. Many businesses also serve Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the Vale of Glamorgan. Google Ads lets you reach customers across that area at the exact moment they search, but only if the account is built and managed with care.

What a Google Ads agency in Cardiff does

Good paid search management is a mix of setup work and steady weekly attention. When we take on a Cardiff account, the work typically includes:

  • Audit and strategy. We review your existing account or plan a new one, agree goals and decide which services or products to prioritise.
  • Conversion tracking. We track calls from ads and your website, form submissions, bookings and online sales.
  • Campaign build. We create tightly themed campaigns and ad groups with relevant keywords and strong negative keyword lists.
  • Ads and assets. We write responsive search ads and add sitelinks, callouts, call assets and location details.
  • Bid and budget management. We choose the right bidding strategy for your data and move budget to the best performers.
  • Search term reviews. We check what people actually searched and block irrelevant queries every week.
  • Reporting. A clear monthly report covering spend, conversions, cost per conversion and next steps.

Local targeting in Cardiff and South Wales

Location settings make a big difference to wasted spend. We target the areas you actually serve, whether that is a radius around your premises or named places such as Canton, Roath, Cathays, Llandaff, Pontcanna and Cardiff Bay. We also check the location option that controls whether ads show to people outside your area who are merely interested in Cardiff, which is a common source of irrelevant clicks.

Where your business covers a wider region, we may split campaigns by area so ad copy and budgets match local demand. A firm based in the city that also serves Newport or Bridgend, for example, can mention those areas in ads aimed at searchers there.

Paid search works best alongside a strong local presence. A well-kept Google Business Profile helps your location assets and your map visibility. Our Google Business Profile guide and local SEO agency guide cover that side.

Welsh language and bilingual ads

Some Cardiff businesses want to reach Welsh speakers or present a bilingual brand. Google Ads can serve ads in different languages, but the copy needs to be right. Prism Works Studio writes in English. If you want Welsh-language ads or landing pages, the Welsh copy should come from your own team or a professional translator, and we build it into the account and site properly. We then track the Welsh and English versions separately so you can see how each performs.

Google Ads pricing for Cardiff businesses

You pay two separate costs. The first is your ad spend, paid straight to Google, which depends on how competitive your keywords are and how many clicks you want. The second is our management fee, which is a fixed monthly retainer:

  • Launch at £350 a month for a single focused campaign or a small local account.
  • Growth at £750 a month for several campaigns, Shopping or remarketing, and more regular testing.
  • Scale at £1,500 a month for larger accounts, multiple services or locations, and more frequent optimisation.

There are no hidden markups on spend. Read more on our pricing page, and see our PPC management cost guide for how agencies across the UK charge.

Tracking you can trust

Many accounts we review are optimising toward the wrong numbers. Page views might be counted as conversions, forms might be counted twice, or calls might not be tracked at all. When that happens, automated bidding chases the wrong signals and money is wasted.

We fix tracking before scaling spend. We check every conversion action, set primary and secondary goals correctly, connect call tracking and, for online shops, make sure order values pass through accurately. We also check that your cookie consent setup works properly with Google's consent requirements. For a deeper look at the checks we use, see our PPC audit checklist.

Landing pages that turn clicks into enquiries

An ad can only do so much. If the landing page is slow, unclear or hard to use on a phone, many visitors leave without calling or booking. We review the pages your ads point to and give you specific, practical recommendations, such as a clearer headline, a visible phone number, a shorter form or stronger trust signals.

If your site needs a rebuild, we can help with that too. Small business websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and usually take three to five weeks. Our web design Cardiff guide explains how we approach sites for businesses in the city.

Google Ads or SEO for a Cardiff business?

Google Ads brings traffic quickly and gives you precise control over budgets and targeting. SEO takes longer to build but can bring steady, lower-cost traffic over time. Many Cardiff businesses use both: ads to win leads now, and SEO to reduce dependence on paid clicks later. We can run either or both, and we will tell you honestly which makes more sense for your stage and budget. Our SEO vs PPC guide goes into the trade-offs.

How we work with Cardiff clients

Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely, so we do not have an office in Cardiff. We work through video calls, email and shared reports, which keeps communication clear and our costs fair. You own your Google Ads account and all its data, so you keep full visibility and control at all times.

We start with a short call, followed by a written audit or plan with a fixed retainer price. Once you are happy, we set up tracking, launch or restructure campaigns and begin weekly optimisation. In the first month, we focus on structure and waste. By months three to six, most accounts reach a steady rhythm where we can scale what works.

What happens in the first ninety days

We like clients to know exactly what the early months look like. In the first few weeks, we fix tracking, rebuild or tidy the account structure and switch off anything that is clearly wasting money. During the second month, we review search terms in detail, refine match types, test new ad copy and adjust bids by location, device and time of day. By the third month, we have enough data to judge which services, areas and keywords bring the best leads, and we start moving budget toward them with confidence.
