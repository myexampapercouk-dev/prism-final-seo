Shopify Agency in Denver: Store Design, Builds and Growth for Colorado Brands

Shopify agency for Denver brands: store design, builds, Shopify Plus migrations and ecommerce SEO, delivered remotely by a UK studio with no US office.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • A Shopify agency in Denver designs, builds and improves Shopify stores so that products are easy to find, pages load quickly and checkout converts well on mobile.
  • We are a UK-based studio that builds and grows Shopify stores remotely for American brands, with no office in Denver, Colorado or anywhere else in the United States.
  • An ecommerce site costs £1,500 to £3,500 and usually takes five to twelve weeks, US dollar pricing is quoted on request, and ongoing retainers start at £350 per month.

A Shopify agency in Denver designs and builds Shopify stores, migrates brands from other platforms and helps them grow through better product pages, faster speed, ecommerce SEO and paid advertising. The aim is a store that is easy to manage day to day and turns visitors into repeat customers.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with American brands and have no office in Denver, Colorado or anywhere else in the United States. This guide covers what a Denver Shopify project involves, how we work, what it costs and what to ask any agency.

Who this guide is for

Denver and the Front Range have a strong consumer brand scene. Outdoor gear, apparel, cycling and ski products, natural food and drink, pet products, home goods and wellness brands all sell online, often alongside a shop in Denver, Boulder, Fort Collins or Colorado Springs. We most often help:

  • Direct-to-consumer brands launching their first serious online store.
  • Retailers with a physical shop who want stock, orders and customers managed in one system.
  • Brands moving from WooCommerce, Squarespace or another platform to Shopify.
  • Growing stores considering Shopify Plus as volume and complexity increase.
  • Wholesale and B2B sellers that need trade pricing alongside retail.

What a Shopify agency in Denver should deliver

A good Shopify project is about more than choosing a theme. These are the areas we focus on.

Store structure. We plan collections, navigation and filters so customers can find products in a few clicks. Our Shopify collections guide explains how we group products for both shoppers and search engines.

Theme design and customisation. We start from a well-supported theme and customise it to your brand, rather than building everything from scratch. That keeps the store fast and easier to update. See our theme customisation guide for the approach.

Product pages that sell. Clear photography, honest descriptions, size or fit guidance, delivery and returns information and reviews you collect from customers all help people buy with confidence.

Speed. Too many apps and heavy images slow stores down. We audit apps, compress media and keep scripts lean. Our Shopify speed guide lists what we check.

Checkout and payments. We configure Shopify Payments or your chosen provider, shipping rates, local pickup and checkout settings so the buying process is smooth.

Ecommerce SEO. Titles, descriptions, headings, structured data, clean URLs and redirects are all set up before launch. We keep these settings in place after launch, so new products and collections follow the same rules.

Selling online and in store in Colorado

Many Denver brands sell at a shop, a pop-up, a farmers market or an outdoor event as well as online. Shopify POS lets you run in-person sales from the same system as your online store, so stock levels, customer records and reports stay in sync. We set up locations, staff permissions and inventory rules so your team can manage both channels without double entry. Our Shopify inventory management guide explains how to keep stock accurate across locations.

Outdoor and seasonal brands also need to plan for clear peaks, such as ski season, summer trail season and holiday gifting. We help you structure collections, landing pages and campaigns around these moments so the store is ready before demand rises.

Wholesale and trade orders on Shopify

Many Colorado brands sell to independent outdoor shops, cafes, gyms and gift stores as well as direct to consumers. Handling those trade orders by email and spreadsheet quickly becomes messy. Shopify offers ways to give approved trade customers their own pricing, minimum order quantities and payment terms, either through built-in features on some plans or through well-established apps.

When we set this up, we keep retail and trade journeys clearly separated, so consumers never see wholesale prices and trade buyers can reorder quickly. We also make sure product data, such as case sizes and barcodes, is stored consistently using metafields. Our Shopify metafields guide explains how structured product data supports filters, trade information and richer product pages.

Shopify Plus and migrations

Shopify Plus suits brands that have outgrown standard Shopify plans, need more checkout customisation, run several storefronts or sell wholesale at scale. We handle migrations to Shopify and upgrades to Plus, including products, customers, order history where possible, URL redirects and SEO checks. Our Shopify Plus migration guide walks through the process step by step.

The most important part of any migration is protecting search rankings. We map every old URL to its new equivalent, test redirects before launch and monitor Search Console afterwards so any problems are fixed quickly.

Growing your store after launch

A launch is the starting point. After the store is live, most brands need a steady plan to bring in traffic and improve conversion. Depending on your goals, that might include:

  • Ecommerce SEO for collection and product pages, plus helpful buying guides.
  • Google Shopping and Performance Max campaigns driven by a clean product feed.
  • Meta, TikTok and YouTube Ads for discovery and remarketing. We do not film or edit video; footage comes from your team or a creator, and we manage targeting, campaigns and reporting.
  • Marketplace SEO for Amazon and Walmart listings if you also sell there.
  • Conversion rate optimisation through better product pages, navigation and checkout. Our Shopify conversion guide covers the main levers.
  • AI-powered marketing such as product description workflows and search visibility in AI tools, always reviewed by a person before publishing.

Sales tax, accessibility and privacy notes

These notes are general. Please check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

Sales tax. Colorado has state and local sales taxes, and online sellers may have obligations in other states too. Shopify includes tax tools, but how they should be configured depends on your situation. Your accountant should confirm your obligations before launch.

Accessibility. We build with clear headings, alt text, readable contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation. Questions about ADA obligations should go to a qualified adviser.

Privacy. Analytics, pixels and email tools collect customer data. Several US states, including Colorado, have their own privacy laws. We can set up consent tools and tag management, but your privacy policy and compliance decisions rest with you.

FAQs

What does a Shopify agency in Denver do?

It designs, builds and improves Shopify stores, handles migrations and Shopify Plus upgrades, sets up payments, shipping and tax tools, and helps the store grow through SEO, ads and conversion work.

Do you have an office in Denver?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely. We have no office in Denver, Colorado or anywhere else in the United States.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

Our ecommerce websites cost £1,500 to £3,500. US dollar pricing is quoted on request. Shopify fees, apps and paid themes are extra.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Most Shopify stores take five to twelve weeks, depending on the number of products, integrations and whether data is being migrated from another platform.

Can you migrate our store to Shopify without losing rankings?

We map every old URL to a new one, test redirects, keep key content and monitor Search Console after launch. No migration is risk-free, but careful planning protects most of your search visibility.

Do you make product videos for our store?

No. We do not film or edit video. Your team or a creator supplies footage, and we add it to product pages and ad campaigns.
