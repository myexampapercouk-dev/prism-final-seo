SEO Agency for Paris Businesses: French and English Search

Looking for an SEO agency in Paris? How SEO works across French and English search, what a remote UK studio can handle, what to ask and how pricing works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • Paris businesses usually need French pages for the home market and English pages for international buyers, each researched and written for its own readers.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in France; we lead technical SEO, English content and reporting remotely, and work with your team or a translator on French pages.
  • SEO results typically build over three to six months, and retainers are priced in pounds, with euro and other currencies quoted on request.

An SEO agency for a Paris business should help you rank for the searches your buyers actually make, in French, in English or both, and turn that visibility into enquiries, bookings or sales. Good SEO covers technical health, language setup, keyword research, useful pages, earned mentions and reporting tied to business results rather than vanity rankings.

Our SEO and generative engine optimisation service is delivered online, one hour behind Paris time, with video calls, shared documents and clear monthly reports. We are strongest on English-language SEO and on the technical foundations that support every language version of a website.

Why Paris businesses often need SEO in two languages

Paris is a hub for fashion, luxury, food and drink, tourism, software, finance and professional services. Many of these businesses sell well beyond France, yet French customers overwhelmingly search in French, and they notice quickly when a page reads like a translation.

  • French-first businesses. Restaurants, local services, clinics and retailers selling mainly within France need strong French pages written or reviewed by native speakers.
  • English-first businesses. Software, B2B and export-led brands often lead with English to reach buyers in the UK, the rest of Europe and North America.
  • Both. Many companies need both, with a structure that tells search engines which version to show to which searcher.

Common problems include the English homepage ranking in France instead of the French one, two language versions competing for the same terms, and product pages machine-translated without review. Our international SEO guide explains the main structural choices, from subfolders to separate country domains.

France also has rules on the use of the French language in some consumer-facing material, and data protection rules under the GDPR apply to tracking and forms. We keep these points general: check current official guidance and take advice from your own legal team before relying on any approach.

Keyword research in French and English

Translating an English keyword list into French rarely works. French searchers use different word order, different everyday terms and, in some sectors, English product words that have become standard. Accents matter too: search engines usually handle them well, but the way your page titles read to a French buyer still affects whether they click.

The reliable approach is to research each language separately, with data set to the right country, then map each group of terms to one clear page. The method in our keyword research guide applies directly; you simply run it once per language and market. Prism writes in English. For French keyword checks and copy, we work alongside your in-house team or a native-speaking writer or translator, so French pages read naturally and match how people really search.

SEO for Paris fashion, hospitality and software brands

Different kinds of Paris business need different SEO priorities:

  • Fashion, beauty and luxury. Category pages, product data, image search and careful handling of brand terms matter most. Thin product pages copied from a supplier rarely rank.
  • Hospitality and tourism. Visitors plan in many languages. English pages that answer practical questions about location, booking and opening times can reach international guests, while French pages serve locals.
  • Software and B2B. Feature pages, use-case pages, comparison pages and practical guides attract buyers researching a decision, often in English.
  • Professional services. Clear service pages, proof of expertise and answers to common client questions help firms win enquiries from international clients based in Paris.

For online shops, structured data and clean product information help search engines and AI tools understand what you sell. Our schema markup guide covers the main types.

Search beyond Google: AI answers and visibility

More buyers now meet brands through AI-generated summaries and assistants, not only through the classic list of links. Pages that answer questions clearly, show who is behind the business and are referenced by trusted sources are more likely to be cited. We cover this in our guide to AI Overviews and SEO, and it applies whether your buyers search in French or English.

This does not replace traditional SEO. It builds on it: fast, crawlable pages, accurate information and helpful content remain the foundation.

Questions to ask any SEO agency in Paris

  • Who writes and reviews the French pages, and who writes the English ones?
  • How will you set up language versions so the right page shows in each market?
  • What will you do in the first three months, and how will we measure progress?
  • Who owns the content, accounts and data if we stop working together?
  • How do you handle cookie consent and tracking under the GDPR?
