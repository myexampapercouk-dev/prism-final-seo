Why so many Cardiff businesses run on WordPress

WordPress powers a large share of the web, and it is popular with Cardiff firms for good reasons. It is flexible, widely supported and you own your site rather than renting it. Your team can edit pages, publish news and add products without calling a developer every time.

The flip side is that WordPress is easy to get wrong. Many sites we review are slow because of heavy page builders, carry dozens of unused plugins, miss updates or have little thought given to structure and search. A good WordPress agency in Cardiff keeps the flexibility and removes those problems.

  • Professional services such as accountants, solicitors and consultants use WordPress for content-rich sites.
  • Hospitality and tourism businesses use it for menus, events and booking links.
  • Trades and home services use it for service pages and quote forms.
  • Retailers use WooCommerce to sell online alongside a shop.
  • Charities, education and public-facing organisations use it for news, resources and events.

What our WordPress agency service in Cardiff covers

  1. Discovery. We learn about your business, customers, competitors and goals, and review your current site and analytics if you have them.
  2. Structure and content plan. We map the pages you need, decide the navigation and plan each page around what customers search for.
  3. Design. We design a clean, accessible layout that reflects your brand and works well on phones.

  6. Launch and training. We handle redirects from old URLs, test forms and speed, then show your team how to edit the site.

How we keep WordPress fast and secure

Speed and security are the two biggest complaints we hear about WordPress. Both are mostly about decisions made during the build.

  • Lean themes. We avoid bloated multipurpose themes that load code for features you never use.
  • Few, trusted plugins. Each plugin is a potential slowdown and security risk, so we only add what has a clear job.
  • Image handling. Images are resized, compressed and served in modern formats where supported.
  • Caching. We set up caching suited to your hosting.
  • Updates and backups. We explain how core, theme and plugin updates should be handled and make sure backups are in place.
  • Logins. We recommend strong passwords, limited admin accounts and two-factor authentication for everyone who logs in.

Good hosting matters too. We can advise on choosing a host, but the hosting account and its contract stay in your name, so you always control your site.

English and Welsh content

Many Cardiff organisations want their site in both English and Welsh. Rescuing an existing WordPress site

Not everyone needs a full rebuild. If your current site is slow, keeps breaking after updates or has lost rankings, we can start with a review. We look at the theme, plugins, hosting, speed, security settings, broken links and search setup, then recommend whether a clean-up or rebuild makes more sense. Sometimes a focused fix is enough. Sometimes the old site has so many layers that rebuilding is cheaper in the long run.

Accessibility and pages that work for everyone

A good WordPress site should be usable by as many people as possible, including those using screen readers, keyboards or larger text settings. We build with accessibility in mind from the start rather than trying to bolt it on later.

  • Clear heading structure so pages make sense when read aloud.
  • Good colour contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Descriptive alt text for meaningful images, which your team can keep up as they add content.
  • Forms with proper labels and helpful error messages.
  • Buttons and links that are easy to tap on a phone.

This matters for public-facing organisations in Cardiff in particular, but it helps every business. Accessible sites are usually easier for everyone to use, and many of the same improvements also support search visibility. If your organisation has specific accessibility obligations, please check the official guidance or speak to your own adviser, as responsibility for compliance rests with you.

Forms, bookings and integrations

Most WordPress sites need to do more than display pages. We set up contact and quote forms that send enquiries to the right inbox, connect booking tools where you already use one, and link newsletter sign-ups to your email platform. We test every form before launch and add spam protection so your inbox stays usable. Where enquiries matter for reporting, we track form submissions as conversions in Google Analytics 4 so you can see which pages and channels bring leads.

How to choose a WordPress agency in Cardiff

When you compare agencies, ask a few direct questions:

  • Will I own the site, domain and hosting accounts?
  • Which theme and page builder will you use, and why?
  • How many plugins will the site need?
  • Is SEO included in the build or charged separately?
  • Who handles updates and backups after launch?
  • Are prices published, and what exactly is included?
