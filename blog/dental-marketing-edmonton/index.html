How Edmonton patients search for a dentist

Edmonton covers a lot of ground. Patients in Downtown, Old Strathcona, Whyte Avenue, Windermere, Terwillegar, the Southeast, Clareview and the West End rarely want to drive across the city for a check-up, and nearby communities such as St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove and Beaumont have their own clinics. Distance is a strong factor in Google's map results, so a clinic usually wins patients within a realistic radius rather than citywide.

That shapes the strategy. Instead of chasing one broad "dentist Edmonton" ranking, it is usually better to be clearly the best-presented clinic in your part of the city, with strong reviews, accurate hours, real photos and a website that answers patients' questions. Families, shift workers, students and newcomers to Alberta all search slightly differently, so we look at which groups you serve best and build around them.

A website that makes booking simple

Patients judge a clinic quickly. They want a clean site that loads fast on a phone, shows where you are, when you are open and how to book, and explains treatments without jargon. Extended hours and Saturday appointments matter to many working families in Edmonton, so make them obvious if you offer them.

Our dental websites include:

  • A page for each core treatment, from check-ups and hygiene to implants, aligners and children's dentistry.
  • Location pages with parking, transit links and accessibility information.
  • Click-to-call and booking buttons connected to your existing booking software.
  • Team pages written or approved by your clinic.
  • Answers to common practical questions, such as what a new-patient visit involves, approved by your staff.
  • Fast, secure hosting and an easy content management system.

A small clinic website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and takes three to five weeks. Our guides to web design in Edmonton and WordPress in Edmonton explain how we handle builds in the city.

Local SEO across Edmonton and nearby communities

Local SEO is the long-term engine for most clinics. We focus on:

  • Profile strength: a fully completed Google Business Profile with regular updates and photos.
  • Reviews: a steady, compliant process for asking patients, and replies that never disclose patient information.
  • Treatment pages: genuinely useful content on the treatments you want more of.
  • Area relevance: honest mentions of the neighbourhoods and communities you serve, without thin copy-paste location pages.
  • Technical health: speed, structured data, internal links and indexing.

Our guides to SEO in Edmonton and local SEO in Canada cover the wider picture. SEO results typically take three to six months to build.

Paid ads that bring booked appointments

Google Ads works well for urgent dental needs and for higher-value treatments where patients research before deciding. We separate campaigns by treatment, use location targeting that matches how far patients will realistically travel, exclude irrelevant searches, and track calls and booking requests so you can judge each campaign. Ad spend goes directly to Google and is separate from our fee. Our guide to Google Ads in Edmonton explains the set-up in more detail.

Meta Ads can introduce your clinic to households nearby, which is useful for new clinics and for filling hygiene or children's appointments. Platform rules on health advertising limit some targeting, so we keep audiences broad and claims factual. See our guide to Meta Ads in Edmonton.

Regulation and approvals

Dentists in Alberta are regulated professionals, and their advertising is subject to rules on matters such as testimonials, guarantees, comparisons, pricing statements and images. Privacy law also applies to patient information, including anything submitted through forms.

We give general marketing information only and are not legal or regulatory advisers. Your clinic should check its marketing against guidance from your professional regulator and, where needed, your own adviser, and approval of all content rests with the clinic. We help by writing factual copy, avoiding promises of results, keeping forms minimal and keeping a record of what was approved.

Marketing to newcomers and families in Edmonton

Edmonton keeps growing, with people moving in from other provinces and from abroad, and new residential communities appearing at the edges of the city. Newcomers have to find a dentist from scratch, often with no recommendations, which makes them one of the most valuable groups for a clinic to reach. They search, compare and decide based almost entirely on what they see online.

A few things make a clinic easier to choose for someone new to the city:

  • A clear new-patient page explaining what the first visit involves, how long it takes and what to bring.
  • Plain answers about insurance billing and payment options, written and approved by your front desk.
  • Information on languages your team speaks, if you serve patients in languages other than English. Any content in those languages should come from your team or a translator.
  • Photos of the real clinic and team, so people know what to expect when they arrive.
  • Directions that mention nearby landmarks, main roads and transit links.

None of this needs a large budget; it mostly needs clear, honest information placed where new patients will look first. Families are another priority for many clinics. Parents look for a gentle approach with children, appointment times that fit around school and work, and the chance to book several family members together. If you offer this, say so clearly on the website and your Google Business Profile, and consider a children's dentistry page. For clinics in new suburbs, Meta Ads aimed at households within a few kilometres can introduce the clinic before people have even started looking.
