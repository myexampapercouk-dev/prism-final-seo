What is search engine marketing?

Search engine marketing, often shortened to SEM, is any activity that brings customers to your business through search engines. Some people use the term only for paid search ads. Others use it for both paid and organic search. We use the wider meaning, because the two channels share the same customers, the same searches and the same landing pages.

Paid search means ads on Google and Microsoft Bing that appear for chosen keywords. You pay when someone clicks. Organic search means the unpaid results, which you earn through technical SEO, content and links. A good search engine marketing agency treats these as two halves of one plan rather than separate services run by people who never talk to each other.

How paid and organic search support each other

Running both channels together is more than doing two jobs at once. Each one makes the other better.

  • Ad data guides SEO. Paid search shows within weeks which searches lead to sales. We use that data to decide which pages to build and improve for organic search.
  • Organic rankings can lower ad costs. As your pages rank for a search, you may be able to reduce bids on it and move budget to terms where you do not yet rank.
  • Shared landing pages improve both. A fast, clear page tends to improve ad Quality Score and organic rankings at the same time.
  • Coverage on busy results pages. Appearing in both the ads and the organic results for key searches can build trust with searchers.

Search engine marketing for different US businesses

The balance between paid and organic depends on the business.

  • Local service businesses often lead with Google Ads and local SEO, targeting their service area with location settings and map pack visibility. Our location targeting guide explains the settings.
  • Online stores usually rely on Shopping campaigns alongside SEO for category and product pages.
  • B2B and professional firms tend to have long sales cycles, so organic content that answers buyer questions does a lot of the work, with paid search capturing high-intent searches.
  • Regulated sectors such as healthcare, finance and law face extra advertising rules. We keep our guidance general and approval of claims and wording rests with your compliance team.

How we start a search engine marketing project

Every project begins with a review of what already exists. If you have run Google Ads before, we audit the account structure, search terms, conversion tracking and landing pages. If you have an existing website, we run a technical and content review to see which pages already rank and which searches you are missing. Our PPC audit checklist shows the checks we run on the paid side.

From there, we agree a short list of priorities. For most businesses, the first month covers tracking fixes, a cleaned-up or rebuilt ad account and the most urgent technical SEO issues. The second and third months focus on testing ads and landing pages and improving the pages most likely to rank. After that, the work settles into a monthly rhythm of optimisation, new content and reporting.

We set budgets with you rather than for you. Ad spend is your decision, and we explain what a given budget can realistically achieve in your market before you commit. If the numbers do not make sense for paid search, we will say so and suggest leaning on organic search instead.

What to look for in a search engine marketing agency

  1. Account ownership. Your Google Ads account, Search Console and analytics should be in your name.
  2. Transparent spend. Ad spend should be paid by you directly to Google, separate from the agency fee.
  3. One plan for both channels. Ask how paid data will shape SEO and the other way round.
  4. Clear reporting. Reports should show leads, sales and cost per lead, not only clicks and impressions.
  5. No guarantees on rankings. Nobody controls Google's organic results.
  6. A realistic timeline. Paid search can start fast, but both channels need months of testing and improvement.

FAQs

What does a search engine marketing agency do?

A search engine marketing agency manages your visibility on search engines through paid ads, such as Google Ads, and organic SEO. It plans keywords, builds campaigns and pages, tracks conversions and reports on leads and sales.

Is search engine marketing the same as SEO?

Not quite. SEO focuses on unpaid organic rankings. Search engine marketing is often used for paid search, and in its wider sense covers both paid and organic search together.

How much does a search engine marketing agency cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid separately to Google. US dollar quotes are available on request.

Who pays for the ad spend?
