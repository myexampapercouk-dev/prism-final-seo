Why New York brands advertise on TikTok

New York is home to a dense mix of consumer brands: fashion and beauty labels, food and drink startups, DTC ecommerce companies, hospitality groups, fitness studios, publishers and subscription businesses. Many of them already have an audience on Instagram and want a second channel that can reach younger and broader audiences at a lower cost of attention.

TikTok works differently from search. People are not looking for you when they see your ad. They are scrolling, and your video has a second or two to earn their attention. That makes TikTok strong for discovery and impulse purchases, and weaker for slow, considered buying decisions unless you pair it with other channels. A good agency will tell you honestly where TikTok fits in your mix.

  • Ecommerce and DTC brands use TikTok to introduce products, retarget visitors and drive sales through Shopify stores.
  • Restaurants, bars and venues use it to fill tables and promote events across the five boroughs.
  • Beauty, fashion and wellness brands use creator-style content to show products in use.
  • Apps and subscription services use it to drive installs and trial sign-ups at scale.

What our TikTok Ads agency work in New York covers

Our TikTok work sits inside our wider paid media service, so it can be run alongside Google Ads, Meta and YouTube where that makes sense. A typical engagement covers these stages.

  1. Discovery. We review your offer, margins, current channels and the goal for TikTok, whether that is purchases, leads or installs.
  2. Tracking. We set up or audit the TikTok Pixel and Events API so conversions are recorded properly. Our TikTok Pixel setup guide explains what this involves.

  6. Optimisation and reporting. We adjust bids, budgets, audiences and creative, and report on the numbers that matter to your business.

Creative is the main lever on TikTok

On TikTok, creative does more of the targeting than audience settings do. The algorithm finds people who respond to your video, so the video itself decides who sees your ads. That is why we spend so much of our time on briefing and testing.

Ads that tend to perform well look native to the platform. They open with a strong hook in the first second, show the product or result early, use captions because many people watch without sound, and end with a clear next step. Polished brand films often underperform simple, phone-shot content that feels like a real person talking.

Because TikTok audiences see a lot of content, creative wears out quickly. Plan for a steady flow of new videos rather than one big shoot. We help you plan that flow with a testing calendar, so your team or creators know what to make next and why.

  • Test several hooks on the same core video before making something entirely new.
  • Use Spark Ads to promote organic posts from your account or creators, with permission.
  • Refresh winning concepts with new openings, angles and people on screen.
  • Keep landing pages fast and mobile-friendly, since almost all TikTok traffic is on phones.

Landing pages, Shopify and conversion tracking

Paid social only works if the page people land on does its job. A slow product page or a confusing checkout will waste spend no matter how good the ad is. We check page speed, mobile layout, offer clarity and checkout steps before scaling, and we can fix them through our website and ecommerce development service if needed.

Many New York DTC brands run on Shopify, and TikTok connects to Shopify through an official sales channel app that handles the pixel and product catalogue. We make sure events are firing correctly, deduplicated between browser and server, and mapped to the right conversion goals. If your store needs work first, our post on Shopify agency support in New York explains how we approach it.

We also tag links consistently so you can see TikTok traffic clearly in your analytics. Our UTM tracking links guide shows the naming approach we use.

Timelines matter too. The first few weeks are about tracking, learning and testing. Paid results typically settle in three to six months as the account gathers data and you find creative that works. Be wary of anyone who promises a fixed return before they have seen your numbers.

Working with a remote team from New York

Working with a UK-based studio from New York is straightforward in practice. The time difference means our working day overlaps with your morning, which suits weekly calls and quick approvals. Campaign changes can be made during your evening or overnight, so you often start the day with an update already waiting.

We work inside your own TikTok Business Center, Ads Manager and analytics accounts. You own the accounts, the pixel and the data. If you ever move on, everything stays with you. We write in English, and if you need ads in Spanish or another language for New York audiences, that copy should come from your team or a translator.

On rules and compliance, TikTok has its own advertising policies, and some categories such as alcohol, financial products, health claims and dating have extra restrictions. US advertising law and state rules can also apply. We keep to platform policy in our setup, but for regulated products the final compliance sign-off rests with you and your own adviser, and you should check official guidance directly.

How to choose a TikTok Ads agency in New York

Whether you pick us or someone else, a few questions will help you choose well.

  • Who owns the ad account? It should be you, always.
  • How is tracking set up? Ask about the Pixel, Events API and how conversions are deduplicated.
  • Who makes the videos? Be clear whether the agency produces content or, like us, briefs and tests content you supply.
  • What is reported? Look for cost per purchase, lead or install, not just views and clicks.
  • How are fees structured? Fees should be clear and separate from ad spend.
