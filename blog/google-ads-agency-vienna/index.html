A Google Ads agency for a Vienna business should turn your ad budget into enquiries or sales by targeting the right searches, in German for local customers and in English where your buyers expect it. That means building campaigns around real search intent, tracking conversions properly with consent in place and cutting wasted spend every week.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Google Ads remotely for businesses across Europe. We have no office in Vienna or anywhere in Austria. Our paid media service runs by video call, shared dashboards and monthly reports, with Vienna one hour ahead of UK time. We write ad copy in English and work with your team or a translator on German ads and landing pages.

What makes Google Ads in Vienna different

Vienna is Austria's capital and its largest city, with a strong mix of professional services, tourism and hospitality, healthcare, technology, retail, property and international organisations. That mix shapes how people search and what a campaign needs to do:

  • German is the main search language, and Austrian German uses some words and spellings that differ from German used in Germany, so keyword lists should reflect local terms.
  • English searches matter too for visitors, international staff, expats and business buyers working in English.
  • Location targeting needs care. A Vienna business may want the city only, the surrounding region, the whole of Austria or neighbouring German-speaking markets.
  • Consent affects measurement. Tracking depends on visitors accepting cookies, so setup and reporting must account for that.

Building German and English campaigns

We usually split campaigns by language rather than mixing German and English keywords in one campaign. That keeps budgets, ad copy and reporting clear, and means each searcher sees an ad and a landing page in the language they used. Each campaign gets its own keyword lists, negative keywords and ad assets.

Prism writes English ads in-house. For German ads we prepare a brief with keywords, selling points, character limits and calls to action, and your team or a native-speaking translator writes the copy. We then check it fits the ad format and matches the landing page. Ads that promise one thing and send people to a page about something else waste money, whatever the language.

Landing pages matter as much as the ads. If a German search leads to an English page, or the page is slow on mobile, conversion rates suffer. We review landing pages before launch and suggest changes, and our post on web design for Vienna businesses covers the website side in more detail.

Tracking, consent and measurement

Austrian websites fall under the GDPR, and the EU ePrivacy rules mean analytics and advertising cookies normally need consent before they load. For Google Ads that means a consent banner connected to Google's consent mode, so tags respect each visitor's choice. Some conversions will not be observed directly when visitors decline, so we explain how modelled data works and what your reports can and cannot show.

We set up conversion tracking for the actions that matter: form submissions, calls, bookings or purchases. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking sets out the steps. Your legal adviser should approve the consent banner wording and privacy policy, and requirements change, so check current official guidance. If your landing pages serve consumers, the European Accessibility Act, which has applied since June 2025 with an exemption for microenterprises providing services, is another reason to build pages to WCAG AA, the level referenced by EN 301 549.

Choosing campaign types

Different businesses need different campaign types:

  • Search campaigns for service businesses, clinics, professional firms and anyone who wants to appear when people search for what they offer.
  • Shopping and Performance Max for online shops with a product feed in Google Merchant Center.
  • Brand campaigns to protect your own name when competitors bid on it.
  • Remarketing to reach people who visited but did not enquire, where consent allows.

Performance Max can work well for ecommerce but gives less control over where ads appear, so we use it with clear goals and good feed data. Our Performance Max guide explains when it fits and when it does not.

Cutting wasted spend

Wasted budget is the most common problem we see in Google Ads accounts, and it usually comes from a handful of causes:

  • Broad match keywords without negatives, triggering ads for unrelated searches.
  • Location settings that include people merely interested in Vienna rather than people in or near it.
  • One campaign mixing languages, so budgets and messages are muddled.
  • Conversion tracking that counts page views or duplicate events as leads.
  • Search partner and display placements switched on without review.

We review search terms every week and keep negative keyword lists in both languages. Our negative keywords guide shows how we structure them.

Seasonality is worth planning for as well. Tourism, hospitality and events businesses in Vienna often see demand rise and fall through the year, while professional services may slow during holiday periods. We agree a budget calendar with you so spend increases when demand is strongest and is held back when searches drop, rather than running at the same level every month regardless of what customers are doing.

FAQs

Do you have a Google Ads agency office in Vienna?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Austria. We manage campaigns remotely by video call and shared dashboards, one hour behind Vienna time.

Can you run Google Ads in German and English?

Yes. We plan and build separate campaigns for each language. English ads are written in-house, and German ads come from your team or a native-speaking translator, which we then check against the ad format and landing page.

How much does a Google Ads agency in Vienna cost with your studio?

Our management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month depending on scope, quoted in pounds with other currencies on request. Ad spend is paid separately to Google.

How long before Google Ads produces results?

Campaigns can generate clicks from launch, but bids and budgets usually need a few weeks of data, and clear results typically take three to six months of refinement.

Does cookie consent affect Google Ads tracking?

Yes. Under EU rules, advertising cookies normally need consent, so some conversions are modelled rather than observed. We set up consent mode and explain what the reports show.

Can you advertise beyond Vienna into the rest of Austria or Germany?

Yes. We can target Vienna only, wider Austria or other countries, usually with separate campaigns so budgets and results stay clear for each market.
