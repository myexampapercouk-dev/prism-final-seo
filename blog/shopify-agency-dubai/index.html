Arabic and English storefronts

Dubai shoppers are a mix of Emiratis, Arab expatriates and a large international community. Many brands need both Arabic and English. Shopify supports multiple languages through its built-in tools and translation apps, but a good bilingual store needs more than translated text.

We set up separate language URLs, hreflang tags and a clear language switcher. On Arabic pages, we check the theme mirrors properly: navigation, product galleries, filters, cart drawers and checkout fields all need to read right to left. We test fonts so Arabic and Latin text sit well together. The Arabic product titles, descriptions and policies themselves are written by your team or a translator, so the tone is natural and accurate.

Payments, delivery and checkout

A smooth checkout decides whether a basket becomes an order. We set up payment options that suit your customers, using Shopify's own payment options or approved third-party providers available for your business. Many UAE shoppers use cards and digital wallets, and some brands offer buy now, pay later or cash on delivery. We configure the options you choose, but which providers you can use depends on your business setup and their own approval.

Delivery expectations in Dubai are high. We set shipping zones for Dubai, the other emirates and wider Gulf markets if you ship there, with clear rates, delivery times and free delivery thresholds. Where you use a courier app or fulfilment partner, we connect it and test the order flow end to end.

Duties, VAT and consumer rules affect how prices and policies are shown. We keep notes on these general; check official guidance or your accountant or adviser for your own obligations.

Themes, speed and mobile design

Most Dubai shoppers browse on their phones. We choose a fast, well-supported theme, keep apps to the ones you need and compress images carefully. Heavy app stacks are one of the most common reasons Shopify stores slow down, so we review every app for its effect on speed.

Design focuses on clear product photography, easy filtering, visible delivery information and trust details such as returns policies and contact options. We build collection and product templates that your team can manage without code.

Moving to Shopify from another platform

Many Dubai retailers start on WooCommerce, Magento or a custom build and later move to Shopify for simpler management. A careful migration protects your search traffic and customer data. Our process covers:

  1. Audit. Listing products, variants, collections, customers, pages and existing URLs.
  2. Data mapping. Deciding how products, options and metadata move across.
  3. Build. Setting up the new theme and templates in a preview store.
  4. Import and check. Moving data and checking it item by item for key products.
  5. Redirects. Mapping old URLs to new ones so links and rankings carry over.
  6. Launch and monitor. Going live, then watching Search Console and analytics closely.

For larger stores with complex needs, we also handle Shopify Plus migrations, scoped and quoted on request.

We write clear collection page copy in English, set titles and meta descriptions, tidy duplicate URLs, add structured data and plan internal links between collections and products. Working with us remotely

The UAE is three or four hours ahead of the UK depending on the season, leaving a good overlap for calls. We schedule meetings around the UAE working week and share everything on a preview store, so you can review on your own phone and laptop. You own the Shopify account and all apps are installed in your name.

Common Shopify mistakes we fix

  • Too many apps slowing the store and conflicting with each other.
  • Arabic pages that are not mirrored properly in menus, filters and the cart.
  • Missing redirects after migration, which loses search traffic.
  • Unclear delivery information that leads to abandoned checkouts.
  • Thin collection pages with no text for search engines to understand.

Planning for Ramadan, Eid and sale seasons

Sales peaks in Dubai often cluster around Ramadan, Eid, White Friday and the year-end shopping season. We help you prepare the store well before each peak: gift collections, landing pages, discount rules, delivery cut-off messages and stock checks. Seasonal Arabic copy needs time, so we share an English brief with your team or translator weeks in advance.

We also check that the store can handle traffic spikes cleanly, that apps used for promotions do not slow pages down, and that tracking captures every order so you can compare each campaign afterwards.

After launch: training and handover

We hand over a short written guide and a recorded walkthrough covering products, collections, discounts, translations and orders, so your team can run the store day to day with confidence. We stay available for questions in the weeks after launch.
