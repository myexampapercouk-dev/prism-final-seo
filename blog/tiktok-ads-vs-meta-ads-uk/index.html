TikTok Ads vs Meta Ads: Choosing a Social Ad Platform in the UK

TikTok Ads suit native, entertaining short video; Meta Ads offer mature targeting across Facebook and Instagram. Compare audience, creative and cost.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 8 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Meta Ads is the more established platform, with broad UK reach across Facebook and Instagram and mature targeting and measurement.
  • TikTok Ads rewards native, entertaining short video and can reach audiences in a fresh way, but it needs a steady flow of new creative.
  • Choose based on where your customers spend time, what creative you can produce and whether your tracking is ready, then test one platform properly.

TikTok Ads and Meta Ads both put paid video and image ads in front of people as they scroll, but they suit different businesses. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) is the more established option, with wide UK reach and mature targeting, while TikTok Ads rewards native, entertaining short videos and can help brands reach people in a fresher, more creator-led way.

The right choice depends on who your customers are, what kind of content you can make and how ready your tracking is. This guide compares TikTok Ads vs Meta Ads on audience, creative, targeting, cost and measurement, with a practical way to decide where to test first. Both platforms update their tools, ad policies and audience data regularly, so check their current official help centres before launching.

Audience: where your customers actually are

Start with your customers, not the platform. Meta's apps cover a very wide range of ages and interests in the UK, which is why they remain the default for many local businesses and online shops. Facebook tends to skew older and Instagram somewhat younger, so the two together reach most adult groups.

TikTok has grown well beyond its early teenage image, but it still leans younger and is especially strong for entertainment, fashion, beauty, food and hobby content. Its algorithm shows content based on what people watch rather than who they follow, which means a good ad can reach interested people without a large following behind it.

Platform audience figures change, so look at current data in each platform's ads tools and, more importantly, at where your own customers say they discovered you.

Creative: the biggest difference

On TikTok, ads that look like ads tend to struggle. The formats that usually perform are short, vertical videos that hook within the first second or two, use sound and on-screen text, and feel like something a real person would post. Creator content, demonstrations, behind-the-scenes clips and honest reviews from genuine customers often fit naturally. Because the feed moves fast, the same video can tire quickly, so you need a regular supply of new versions.

Meta is more forgiving of different styles. Clean product images, carousels and polished video can all work, alongside Reels-style content that resembles TikTok. That flexibility makes Meta easier for businesses with limited video production, although fresh creative still matters as spend grows. Targeting and audiences

Meta offers detailed options: location, age, interests, behaviours, website visitors, customer lists and lookalike audiences built from your best customers. Many advertisers now use broader targeting and let the algorithm find buyers, but the depth is there when you need it. Our guide to Meta lookalike audiences explains one of the most useful features.

TikTok also offers interest, behaviour, custom and lookalike audiences, but its delivery leans more heavily on how people react to the content itself. In practice, the creative does much of the targeting: a video about running shoes tends to find runners. That makes TikTok powerful for products that are easy to show, and harder for services that need explanation.

Costs and budgets

Both platforms run auctions, so prices change with competition, season and audience. Neither platform should be judged on cost per view or cost per click alone. Compare cost per sale, booking or lead, and allow for the fact that social ads often start a journey that finishes later through search or a direct visit.

Tracking and measurement

Each platform needs its own tracking to optimise towards sales or leads. Meta uses the Meta Pixel and its Conversions API; TikTok uses the TikTok Pixel and its own events API. Shopify and other platforms offer apps or integrations that make setup easier. Under UK GDPR and PECR, tracking tags should respect cookie consent, so check current ICO guidance and your consent banner settings.

Both platforms will tend to take credit for sales they influenced. Tag links with UTM parameters and compare performance in GA4 so you get a consistent view; our UTM tracking links guide shows how.

Which businesses suit each platform

  • Often suits TikTok first: fashion, beauty, skincare, food and drink, gadgets, hobby products, and brands with a strong personality or founder story that translates to video.
  • Often suits Meta first: local services, venues, most online shops, events, and businesses targeting older audiences or needing lead forms.
  • Often suits both: ecommerce brands with visual products and the capacity to make regular video, using Meta for steady sales and retargeting and TikTok for discovery.

Brand safety, policies and comments

Both platforms have advertising policies covering restricted products, claims and targeting, and some categories face extra rules or need approval. Read the current policies for your sector before creating ads, as a rejected campaign can delay a launch. UK advertising must also follow the CAP Code, so claims in your ads should be accurate and backed up.

Comments are part of the experience on both platforms. Ads can attract questions, praise and criticism in public, and TikTok comment sections in particular can move quickly. Plan who will monitor and reply, how fast, and what you will do with complaints or off-topic replies. A responsive comment section can build trust; an ignored one can undo the work of a good ad.

Finally, think about where your ads appear. Both platforms offer controls over placements and the type of content ads sit beside. Review these settings as part of setup rather than leaving everything on default.

How to decide where to start

  1. Check your audience. Ask recent customers where they spend time online and look at platform audience data.
  2. Be honest about creative. If you can produce several new short videos a month, TikTok is realistic. If not, start with Meta.
  3. Get tracking ready. Install the pixel, check events and confirm consent works before spending.
  4. Test one platform properly. Give it enough budget and a few weeks to learn, rather than splitting a small budget across both.
  5. Compare on the same measure. Use cost per sale or lead in GA4, then decide whether to add the second platform.

FAQs

TikTok Ads vs Meta Ads: which is better?

Neither is better for every business. Meta Ads offers broader UK reach and more mature targeting, while TikTok Ads can work very well for visual consumer products with native, entertaining video. Choose based on your audience and creative capacity.

Are TikTok ads cheaper than Facebook ads?

Costs vary with competition, season and audience on both platforms. Compare cost per sale or lead rather than cost per view or click, and include the cost of producing video for TikTok.

Do I need a TikTok account to run TikTok ads?

You set ads up through TikTok's advertising platform. Having an active business account with organic posts can help ads feel native, but check TikTok's current requirements for your ad formats.

Can I reuse my Instagram Reels as TikTok ads?

Often yes, if they are vertical, hook quickly and feel native. Remove watermarks from other apps and adapt text and sound to TikTok, as content made specifically for each platform usually performs better.

Is TikTok only for young audiences?

No, TikTok's audience has broadened, although it still leans younger than Facebook. Check current audience data in TikTok's ads tools for your target age groups in the UK.

Should a small business advertise on TikTok or Meta first?

Most small businesses find Meta easier to start with because it accepts a wider range of creative and has mature targeting. TikTok is worth testing first if your product is highly visual and you can make regular short videos.
