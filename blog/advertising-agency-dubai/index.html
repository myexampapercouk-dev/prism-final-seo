What a digital advertising agency in Dubai should handle

If your main goal is enquiries, bookings or online sales, a digital ad specialist is usually the right fit. That kind of partner should cover:

  • Strategy. Choosing the platforms, audiences and budget split that fit your goal.
  • Search ads. Google Ads campaigns for people actively searching for what you offer.
  • Social ads. Paid Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn campaigns to reach new audiences and retarget visitors.
  • Tracking. Conversion setup so every campaign can be judged on leads or sales, not clicks alone.
  • Landing pages. Fast, focused pages that turn ad clicks into enquiries.
  • Reporting. Clear monthly reports with spend, results and next steps.

These are the areas we cover. We plan creative briefs and write English ad copy, while video and photo production comes from your team or a production partner.

Google Ads in Dubai

Search advertising suits businesses whose customers look for them on Google. Think clinics, movers, legal firms, training providers, car services and property agents. Ads appear when someone searches, so the intent is already there. Our guide to Google Ads in Dubai covers keyword planning, bidding and landing pages in more depth.

Social advertising in Dubai

Paid social reaches people who are not yet searching. It works well for consumer brands, launches, restaurants, events and ecommerce stores. Each platform has its strengths:

  • Meta Ads across Facebook and Instagram offer broad reach and detailed targeting.
  • TikTok Ads suit visual products and younger audiences.
  • YouTube Ads help brands with existing video build consideration.
  • LinkedIn Ads target professionals by job title, industry and company size.

We manage paid campaigns only. We do not run organic social media accounts or post content on your behalf.

How to compare advertising agencies in Dubai

Once you know which type of agency you need, compare your shortlist on the same questions:

  • Do they publish or clearly state their management fee?
  • Is ad spend kept separate from their fee and paid directly to the platforms?
  • Will the ad accounts, pixel and analytics be in your name?
  • How will they track leads or sales, not only clicks?
  • What will a monthly report include?
  • What is the notice period?
  • Who writes Arabic copy, and how is it checked?

Clear, simple answers are a good sign. Vague answers, especially about fees and account ownership, are worth questioning before you sign anything.

Landing pages and websites matter

Ads can bring traffic from the first day, but the early weeks are for testing audiences, keywords and messages. Advertising rules in the UAE

Some sectors, such as healthcare, finance, property and education, face extra advertising rules from UAE authorities and from the ad platforms themselves. We follow platform policies and general good practice. Approval of your ads from a regulatory point of view rests with you and your own advisers, so check official guidance for your sector before launching.

Setting a realistic advertising budget

One of the first questions Dubai businesses ask is how much they should spend on ads. There is no single answer, because costs depend on your sector, your competition, your audience and the platform. Competitive sectors such as property, legal services and healthcare often have higher costs per click on Google than niche B2B services or local trades. Social platforms are priced differently again, usually by reach and engagement rather than by search demand.

A sensible approach is to start with a budget you can sustain for at least three months. That gives the platforms enough data to learn and gives you enough results to judge. Starting too small often means campaigns never leave the learning phase, while starting too large before tracking and landing pages are ready can waste money quickly.

We agree a starting range with you after reviewing your market, then adjust it each month based on cost per lead or sale. If one platform is clearly outperforming another, budget moves towards it. If a campaign is not working after a fair test, we say so and change the approach rather than keep spending.

Common mistakes when hiring an advertising agency

Businesses in Dubai often run into the same problems with agencies. Some sign long contracts before seeing any results. Some let the agency own the ad accounts, then lose years of data when they move on. Others judge success on impressions and likes rather than enquiries and sales. Many choose an agency that is strong at creative work but weak at tracking and optimisation, or the other way round.

The fix is to be clear about what you need, insist on owning your accounts, agree on the measures that matter before launch and keep notice periods reasonable. A good partner will welcome that clarity, because it makes the work easier to judge fairly.

Ecommerce advertising in Dubai

Online stores have their own advertising needs. Google Shopping and Performance Max campaigns use your product feed to show products directly in search results. Meta and TikTok catalogue ads can retarget people who viewed products but did not buy. For these to work, your product data must be accurate, your store must load quickly and your checkout must be simple on mobile. We set up feeds, tracking and campaigns together so every part supports the others.
