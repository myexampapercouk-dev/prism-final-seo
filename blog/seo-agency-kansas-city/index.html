Why Kansas City SEO needs a two-state plan

Kansas City is unusual because the metro sits across two states. A plumber, law firm or dental practice might serve Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa and Shawnee on the Kansas side, and Independence, Lee's Summit, Liberty and North Kansas City on the Missouri side. People also search with different phrasing: some type "KC", some type "Kansas City MO", others search by suburb or neighborhood such as Westport, the Crossroads or Brookside.

That shapes how we plan your site. Rather than one thin page per suburb, we build a small number of strong service area pages, each with real detail about the work you do there, and we make sure your Google Business Profile, citations and on-page wording are consistent. Our multi-location SEO guide explains the approach in more depth.

How our SEO agency Kansas City process works

We follow the same clear steps for every new client so you always know what is happening and why.

  1. Audit. We review your site, your Google Business Profile, your rankings and your competitors. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows the kind of checks we run.

SEO for AI search and Google AI Overviews

More people now get answers from Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and similar tools before they click a website. Being cited in those answers relies on the same foundations as good SEO: clear pages, direct answers, accurate facts and a site that search engines trust. We structure key pages with short answer-first paragraphs, useful tables and FAQs, and we add schema where it fits. Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO explains what helps and what does not.

We use AI-powered tools in our own research and drafting, but every page is checked and edited by people before it goes live. We do not publish unreviewed AI content on client sites.

When your website needs work first

Sometimes SEO cannot do its job because the site itself is the problem. Common signs include a site that is very slow on phones, one page trying to cover every service, a builder that limits what you can change, or a design that has not been touched in many years. In those cases we may suggest a rebuild through our website development service. Small websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and usually take three to five weeks, and we plan redirects carefully so you do not lose the rankings you already have.

Common SEO problems we see on Kansas City sites

When we audit sites from local businesses, the same issues come up again and again:

  • A single "areas we serve" page listing dozens of suburbs with no real content about any of them.
  • A Google Business Profile with the wrong primary category, old hours or no photos.
  • Different business names, addresses or phone numbers across directories.
  • Service pages that are a few lines long and say nothing a competitor could not say.
  • Pages blocked from indexing, or duplicate pages left behind after a redesign.
  • No clear call to action, so visitors who arrive from search do not get in touch.

None of these are hard to fix, but together they hold many good businesses back. Our Google Business Profile guide covers the map side in more detail.
