Leeds has a lively mix of independent retailers, fashion labels, food and drink producers and growing direct-to-consumer brands. Why Leeds brands choose a Shopify agency

Shopify is easy to start with, but a store that performs well needs more than a theme and some product photos. A Shopify agency helps you avoid the common problems: slow pages weighed down by apps, confusing navigation, thin product descriptions, poorly set up shipping rules and tracking that misses sales.

We focus on the parts that affect revenue. That means clear collection structures, product pages that answer buyer questions, a smooth path to checkout and settings that match how you actually fulfil orders. If you are still deciding between platforms, our Shopify vs WooCommerce comparison explains when each one makes sense.

What our Shopify agency service covers in Leeds

  • Store design and build. We customise a quality theme to your brand, set up collections, menus, filters and page templates.
  • Product setup. We structure products, variants and metafields so your catalogue is easy to browse and manage.
  • Payments, shipping and tax. We configure Shopify Payments, delivery zones, rates and VAT settings with you, and point you to HMRC guidance or your accountant for tax decisions.
  • Apps. We choose only the apps you need, because each one can add weight and cost.
  • Tracking. We connect analytics, Google and Meta tracking so you can see where sales come from.
  • SEO foundations. We set page titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text and clean URLs from launch.
  • Training and handover. We show your team how to add products, run discounts and manage orders.

Migrating to Shopify without losing traffic

Many Leeds businesses come to us running WooCommerce, an older custom site or another hosted platform. Moving to Shopify can make the store faster and easier to run, but a careless migration can wipe out years of search rankings.

We protect your visibility by mapping every important old URL to its new Shopify equivalent and setting up 301 redirects. We move products, customers and order history where possible, check that images and descriptions came across properly and test the store thoroughly before launch. After go-live, we monitor Google Search Console for errors and fix them quickly.

You can read more in our WooCommerce to Shopify migration guide. Larger brands with high order volumes may need Shopify Plus, which we also support, as covered in our Shopify Plus migration guide.

Shopify SEO from day one

A new store does not rank by itself. We build the foundations into every project so search can grow over time. That includes keyword-led collection pages, unique product descriptions, structured headings, compressed images, internal links between related products and collections, and a clean sitemap submitted to Google.

For Leeds brands that also sell in person, we look at local search too, such as a properly set up Google Business Profile for a shop or showroom. Our Shopify SEO guide covers the main steps, and ongoing SEO is available on our monthly retainers from £350 a month.

Choosing the right Shopify agency in Leeds

When you compare agencies, ask a few direct questions. Will you own the store and all its content? Is the price fixed? How will they protect your SEO during a migration? Which apps will they install and why? Who handles support after launch? Clear answers to these questions tell you more than a polished portfolio.

Product pages that convert

Product pages do most of the selling in a Shopify store, so we give them extra attention. Each page should show clear photos from several angles, a price and delivery promise above the fold, simple variant selection and an obvious add to basket button that stays easy to reach on a phone. Below that, we help you answer the questions buyers ask: sizing, materials, care, delivery times, returns and how the product compares with similar items in your range.

We also look at trust. Clear returns information, secure checkout messaging, real contact details and a well-written about page all help a new visitor feel confident ordering from a Leeds brand they have only just discovered. Where you collect customer reviews through an app, we place them so they support the decision rather than clutter the page.

Finally, we keep performance in check. Large images, too many apps and heavy custom scripts slow product pages down, especially on mobile. We compress images, remove unused code and test page speed before launch so the store feels quick for shoppers on the move. Our Shopify speed optimisation guide covers the main techniques.
