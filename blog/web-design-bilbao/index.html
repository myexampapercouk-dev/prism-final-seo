Web Design in Bilbao: Building a Website That Wins Customers

Web design for Bilbao businesses: fast, mobile-first sites with Spanish, Basque and English versions, built for SEO and enquiries by a remote UK studio.

Web design in Bilbao means planning and building a website that presents your business clearly, loads fast on phones and turns visitors into enquiries or sales. For many companies in the city, that also means a site that works well in Spanish, Basque and English, with each version structured so search engines can index it properly.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Bilbao, the Basque Country or anywhere in Spain. We work through video calls, shared documents and written updates. We write in English, so Spanish and Basque copy comes from your team or a professional translator. We do not take photos or film video, so your team or a photographer supplies the visuals, and we design, build and optimise the site.

Why web design matters for Bilbao businesses

Bilbao has changed a great deal in recent decades, moving from heavy industry towards culture, tourism, engineering, finance and services. Its business base is broad: industrial suppliers and engineering firms, logistics companies around the port, hotels and restaurants serving visitors, and a growing number of professional and technology firms.

That mix shapes what a good website needs to do. An engineering company selling to buyers across Europe needs detailed, credible service and capability pages in English as well as Spanish. A restaurant in Casco Viejo needs a fast mobile page with menus, opening hours and booking. A consultancy needs case-led pages that explain its approach without making claims it cannot back up. In each case, the design should serve the buyer's questions first.

Planning a multilingual site

In the Basque Country, many businesses choose to offer Basque alongside Spanish, and those selling abroad add English or French. The key decision is structure. Each language should sit in its own folder or subdomain, carry the correct hreflang tags, and have its own titles and descriptions. Automatic browser translation is not a substitute, because search engines cannot index it and the quality is uneven.

We set up the structure and the language switcher, and your translator supplies the text. We also plan which pages need every language and which can start in one or two, so the project stays manageable. Our guide to multilingual website design explains the options in more detail.

Design that builds trust

Good design is not just about looks. It is about making a visitor confident they have found the right business. For Bilbao companies, especially in B2B and industrial sectors, that usually means:

  • Clear statements of what you do and who you do it for, in the first screen of each page.
  • Real photos of your team, premises or products, supplied by you or a photographer.
  • Detailed capability pages that answer technical questions buyers ask before they call.
  • Visible contact details and simple forms that do not ask for too much.
  • Consistent branding across the site, documents and social profiles.

Our guide to landing page design covers how to structure pages for enquiries, and the principles apply equally in Spain.

Choosing a platform

Most of the business sites we build use WordPress, because it is flexible, widely supported and easy for your team to update. Online stores usually use Shopify, which handles payments, stock and multiple currencies well. We recommend the platform based on your needs, not habit.

SEO built in from the start

A new website is the best moment to get SEO right. We plan the page structure around real searches, write clear titles and headings, set up redirects from any old site and add structured data. We also check speed, image sizes and mobile layout before launch. Our guide to SEO in Barcelona covers how search works in the Spanish market, and our technical SEO checklist shows the checks we run.

After launch, many clients add an SEO or Google Ads retainer to bring in steady traffic. Our guide to Google Ads in Bilbao explains how paid search can support a new site while organic rankings grow.

Accessibility and EU rules

The European Accessibility Act brings accessibility requirements for many businesses selling products and services to consumers in the EU. We design with readable text, good colour contrast, keyboard navigation and descriptive labels as standard. Our guide to the European Accessibility Act explains what it means for websites. For your specific obligations, check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

Privacy rules also matter. Your site needs a clear privacy notice and a cookie banner that respects visitors' choices. We set these up, but final legal approval rests with your business.

Common web design mistakes

  • Launching without redirects. Old pages that disappear take their rankings with them.
  • Heavy images and sliders. They slow pages down and rarely help conversions.
  • One language only on key pages. International buyers leave if they cannot read your offer.
  • Vague service descriptions. Buyers want specifics before they contact you.
  • No tracking. Without it, you cannot tell which pages bring enquiries.

Working with a remote studio

Many Bilbao businesses already work with suppliers across Europe, and web design suits remote working well. We agree a regular call time, share designs and progress in one place and send written summaries after each stage. Spain is usually one hour ahead of the UK, so working hours overlap easily. You stay in control of content and approvals, and we handle the technical and design work.

After launch: keeping the site useful

A website keeps working best when it is kept up to date. We recommend reviewing it every few months: checking which pages bring enquiries, which ones visitors leave quickly and which searches you appear for. Adding new service pages, answering common customer questions and refreshing photos all help. Clear headings and direct answers at the start of each section also make your pages easier for AI search tools to summarise. We can support this on a monthly retainer at a fixed fee, or train your team to handle simple updates themselves.
