Why Brighton brands choose Shopify

Brighton has one of the most distinctive independent retail scenes in the UK. The Lanes and North Laine are full of small makers, vintage sellers, jewellers, homeware shops, plant stores and ethical fashion brands, and many of them now sell as much online as they do in person. The city also has a strong creative and digital community, with plenty of founders building direct-to-consumer brands from home studios and shared workspaces.

Shopify suits these businesses well. It handles payments, stock, shipping and VAT, it is easy for small teams to update, and it scales from a handful of products to thousands. For shops that sell both in store and online, Shopify POS keeps stock and customer records in one place. Our guide to Shopify POS for retail explains how that works for a physical shop.

Brighton shoppers and the wider audience these brands sell to also care about values: sustainability, ethics, local making and independent character. A Shopify store needs to tell that story clearly through design, product pages and content, not just list products.

Migrating a Brighton store to Shopify

Many independent brands start on Etsy, WooCommerce, Squarespace or Wix and outgrow them. Moving to Shopify can bring better stock control, faster checkout and room to grow, but a migration has to be handled carefully to protect search rankings and customer data.

We move products, variants, images, customers and order history where the platform allows, then set up 301 redirects from every old URL to the matching new one. That keeps the search value your old pages built up. Our guide to moving from Etsy to Shopify covers the steps in more depth, and the same careful approach applies to WooCommerce, Squarespace and Wix stores.

For larger brands with high order volumes or complex needs, we also handle Shopify Plus migrations, including checkout customisation and multi-store setups.

Designing for independent brands

Brighton brands often have strong personalities, and their stores should show it. We design product pages that tell the story behind each item, with clear photos, honest descriptions, sizing or material details, delivery and returns information, and reviews where you have them.

We also think about how people browse. Many shoppers arrive on a single product from Instagram or a search result, so every product page needs to work as a landing page. Others want to explore, so collections, filters and search need to make finding things easy. Good navigation matters as much as good looks.

Sustainability and ethics claims need care. If you describe products as eco-friendly, recycled or ethically made, make sure you can back that up. Keep claims specific and check official UK guidance on green claims, or ask your own adviser if you are unsure. That approval rests with you as the business owner.

Speed and mobile shopping

Most Shopify traffic now comes from phones, often from people browsing on social media or on the move around the city. Every extra second of load time costs sales. We choose a lightweight theme, compress images, remove unused apps and keep scripts to a minimum, so pages load quickly even on a patchy mobile signal. We also test checkout, filters and menus on real devices before launch, so the experience feels smooth for every shopper.

SEO for Brighton Shopify stores

Shopify gives you a solid base for SEO, but it needs setting up properly. We write clear page titles and meta descriptions, add useful text to collection pages, fix duplicate content issues, set up structured data for products and make sure images have descriptive alt text.

For a store with a physical shop, local search matters too. A well-managed Google Business Profile helps people find you when they search for gifts, plants or vintage clothing in Brighton. For online sales across the UK, collection and product page content does most of the work. Our full guide to Shopify SEO covers the details, and if you want a local SEO partner, our post on choosing an SEO agency in Brighton explains what to look for.

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Brighton?

Look for an agency that plans your store around your products and customers, sets up SEO from day one, keeps apps lean for speed, hands over full ownership and trains your team. Ask to see their process and what support is available after launch.

Do you have an office in Brighton?

No. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We run Brighton projects through video calls, shared boards and a staging store, which keeps costs down without changing the quality of the work.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

Our Shopify builds cost £1,500 to £3,500 depending on the number of products, custom features and integrations. Shopify's own subscription, apps and payment fees are paid separately to Shopify and app providers.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Most Shopify projects take 5 to 12 weeks from discovery to launch. Simpler stores with a small catalogue sit at the shorter end, while migrations and custom features take longer.

Can you move my store from Etsy or WooCommerce?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and order history where possible, and set up redirects from old URLs so you keep the search value your old store built up.

Can you help my store grow after launch?

Yes. Our retainers from £350 a month cover SEO, Google Shopping, Meta and TikTok Ads, marketplace SEO and conversion improvements, so you can keep growing once the store is live.
