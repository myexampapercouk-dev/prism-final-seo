A YouTube Ads agency in Manchester helps businesses use video advertising to reach the right people, then turn those views into enquiries, sales or sign-ups that can be measured. At Prism Works Studio we choose the campaign types, build the audiences, set up conversion tracking in Google Ads and test your videos until the cost of each result makes commercial sense.

One thing to be clear about from the start: we do not film or edit video. You supply the footage, or work with your own production team, and we focus on strategy, targeting, briefing and performance.

Why Manchester businesses advertise on YouTube

Manchester has a large and varied economy, from retail, hospitality and events to digital, media, professional services, education and a strong ecommerce scene. YouTube reaches a huge share of UK internet users, on phones, laptops and increasingly on TV screens at home. That makes it useful for both building awareness and driving action.

YouTube advertising runs through Google Ads, which means you can use the same audience data and conversion tracking as your search campaigns. For many businesses, that link between search and video is the real advantage: you can reach people who have searched for your type of product or service, then follow up with video while they decide.

  • Ecommerce brands use YouTube to show products in action and drive sales.
  • Service businesses use it to explain what they do and build trust before an enquiry.
  • Education and training providers use it to reach people researching courses.
  • Hospitality and events use it to promote venues, seasons and ticketed nights.
  • Software firms use it to demonstrate products and drive trials or demos.

What a YouTube Ads agency in Manchester should do

  1. Set the goal. Sales, leads, sign-ups or awareness each need a different setup and different measures of success.
  2. Fix tracking first. We set up or audit conversion tracking in Google Ads and Google Analytics 4, including enhanced conversions where suitable.
  3. Choose campaign types. We select the formats and bidding that match the goal.
  4. Build audiences. We combine search-based audiences, interests, custom segments, your customer lists and remarketing.
  5. Brief the creative. We write briefs covering the hook, message, length and call to action, and review the videos you supply.
  6. Test and optimise. We test videos, audiences and bids, exclude poor placements and move budget to what works.
  7. Report clearly. Monthly reports show spend, views, conversions and cost per result, with notes on what we will change.

Creative: briefing videos that work as ads

The video does a lot of the work on YouTube. Viewers can skip after a few seconds, so the opening matters most. Because we do not produce video, we help you get the most from the footage you have or plan to film. Our briefs usually cover:

  • A strong opening. Show the product, the problem or the brand within the first few seconds.
  • One clear message. A single benefit or reason to act, not a full company history.
  • Several lengths. A short version for Shorts and bumpers, and a longer version for in-stream.
  • Vertical and horizontal versions so ads fit both Shorts and standard placements.
  • A clear call to action that matches the landing page.
  • Captions, since many people watch with the sound off.

All ads must follow the CAP Code and Google's advertising policies. If you work in a regulated sector, we keep messaging general and final approval of claims rests with your compliance team.

Targeting options for Manchester audiences

You can target YouTube ads to Manchester, Greater Manchester or the whole UK, depending on where your customers are. Within that, we build audiences from:

  • Custom segments based on what people have searched for on Google, which is often the strongest signal.
  • In-market and life event audiences from Google's own data.
  • Your customer lists, uploaded in line with UK GDPR and Google's policies.
  • Remarketing to past site visitors and people who watched your videos.
  • Placement and topic exclusions to keep ads away from content that does not suit your brand.

Measuring YouTube Ads properly

YouTube is often judged unfairly because people only look at direct clicks. Many viewers watch an ad, do not click, and then search for the brand or visit the site later. Others click days afterwards from a different device. If you only count immediate clicks, video can look weaker than it really is. If you count every view as a win, it can look far better than it is. We aim for the honest middle.

  • Conversions in Google Ads, including view-through conversions, reported separately so you can see the difference.
  • Google Analytics 4 to see how video visitors behave on your site compared with other traffic.
  • Branded search trends in Google Ads and Search Console, which often rise when video is working.
  • Remarketing performance, because video viewers who later see a search or display ad are a key part of the value.
  • Consent and privacy, with a cookie banner that supports UK GDPR and PECR. We set this up technically, but your privacy policy and legal position remain your responsibility.

Before launch, we agree which measures matter most for your business, so monthly reports answer the questions you actually care about rather than filling a page with numbers.

Timelines and realistic expectations

YouTube campaigns can start delivering views and some conversions within weeks, but stable results take time. Paid results typically take three to six months to settle as Google's bidding learns, videos are tested and remarketing lists grow. We do not promise fixed view counts, leads or returns, because these depend on your offer, prices, creative and competition. We do commit to clear tracking and honest monthly reporting.

Is YouTube right for your Manchester business?

YouTube suits businesses that already have, or can produce, decent video, and that sell something worth explaining or showing. It is harder to make work if you have no video at all or a very small budget. In that case, search or Meta Ads may be a better first step, and we will tell you so.
