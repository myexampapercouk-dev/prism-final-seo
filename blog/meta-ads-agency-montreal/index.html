Meta Ads for Montreal Businesses: How Remote Facebook and Instagram Advertising Works

Looking for a Meta ads agency in Montreal? How remote Facebook and Instagram advertising works for Quebec businesses, from French ads and tracking to costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

  • Meta ads for a Montreal business should reach the right people on Facebook and Instagram with clear, well-made creative, then turn their interest into visits, enquiries and sales.
  • Montreal campaigns usually need French as the main language, so creative and landing pages should be planned with a fluent French writer from the start, alongside Quebec's privacy and language rules.
  • Our management retainers start at £350 a month with ad spend paid separately, results typically build over three to six months, and Canadian dollar quotes are available on request.

A Meta ads agency working with a Montreal business should reach the right people on Facebook and Instagram with clear, well-made ads, in the right language, and turn their interest into website visits, enquiries, bookings and sales at a cost that makes sense. Because account setup, creative planning, management and reporting all happen online, Meta advertising can be managed remotely. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages paid social for Canadian businesses online; we have no office in Montreal or anywhere else in Canada.

This guide explains how Meta ads work for a Montreal business, how to plan French and English campaigns, what Quebec's privacy and language rules mean in general terms, what our management costs and what to ask before choosing a provider.

What a Meta ads agency in Montreal should do for you

Montreal's economy includes aerospace, technology and artificial intelligence, video games and creative industries, life sciences, fashion, food and hospitality, tourism, education and a large community of independent shops, restaurants, studios and professional firms. Meta ads suit many of them because Facebook and Instagram let you show visual, engaging ads to people based on their location, interests and behaviour, even before they start searching.

That reach is useful, but it needs careful management. A good agency should:

  • Agree clear goals, such as online sales, bookings, enquiries or event sign-ups, rather than likes alone.
  • Set up tracking with the Meta pixel and server-side events, so the platform can optimise for real results.
  • Plan the audience, including location, language and the customers most likely to buy.
  • Develop creative in formats that suit each placement, from Reels and Stories to the feed.
  • Test and refine messages, images and videos, keeping what works and replacing what does not.
  • Report honestly on spend, results and next steps.

Our paid media service covers Meta alongside Google, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn advertising, so we can recommend the right mix for your business. Our guide to Meta ads in Canada covers points that apply nationwide.

Planning French and English campaigns

French is the main language of daily life and business in Montreal, and many residents also use English. For most Montreal businesses, that means French should lead, with English versions where they genuinely serve your customers. Ads that read naturally in Quebec French tend to feel more relevant than ads translated word for word from English.

We plan campaigns and creative in English; French ad copy, captions and landing pages are written with your team or a professional translator, and we review the structure so each language version is consistent. Meta lets you run separate ads by language or provide multiple language versions within one ad, and the right approach depends on your audience and budget.

Quebec's Charter of the French Language sets rules for commercial content, including advertising, websites and product information, and these rules were updated in recent years. What applies to your business depends on its circumstances, so check the official guidance from the Office québécois de la langue française and take advice where needed. Responsibility for compliance rests with your business.

Audiences and targeting around Montreal

Location targeting decides who can see your ads. A café or clinic in the Plateau, Rosemont or Griffintown may only want people nearby, while a retailer shipping across Canada may target the whole country. Many Montreal businesses also serve Laval, Longueuil, the South Shore and the West Island, and separate ad sets for core and outer areas can help control budgets.

Meta's targeting has become broader over time, with the platform increasingly finding the right people based on your goals and tracking data rather than detailed interest lists. That makes accurate tracking and strong creative more important than ever. Retargeting people who have visited your website or engaged with your content often brings good results, as long as your privacy notices and consent setup support it.

Creative that works on Facebook and Instagram

Creative is the biggest lever in most Meta accounts. Short vertical videos, clear product images, real photos of your space or work and simple, direct messages usually perform better than polished but generic visuals. The first few seconds of a video and the first line of copy matter most, because people scroll quickly.

We plan creative around a small number of clear messages, produce variations for different placements and test them against each other. We work with photos and videos you supply or commission, and we never invent reviews, results or endorsements. Our guide to Meta ads in Toronto covers creative and targeting in another large Canadian market, with many of the same principles.

Tracking, landing pages and Law 25

Without accurate tracking, Meta cannot optimise for the results you want. We set up the Meta pixel and server-side events through the Conversions API where appropriate, and configure the events that matter, such as purchases, bookings and form submissions. Our Meta pixel setup guide explains the process.

Quebec's privacy law, often called Law 25, updated the province's private-sector privacy rules and includes requirements on consent, transparency and technologies that can identify, locate or profile people. This can affect how tracking and cookie consent are set up on your website. Elsewhere in Canada, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) generally governs private-sector handling of personal information, and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) applies if you follow up leads by marketing email. These notes are general information rather than legal advice, so refer to official guidance from the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec and other relevant regulators.

Where people land after clicking matters as much as the ad. A fast, mobile-friendly page in the same language as the ad, with a clear next step, converts better than a generic homepage. Accessible pages also help every visitor; Quebec has its own legislation protecting the rights of people with disabilities, so check official guidance on what applies to your organisation. How Meta ads fit with Google Ads and SEO

Meta ads create demand by reaching people who may not yet be searching, while Google Ads and SEO capture people who already are. Many Montreal businesses use Meta to build awareness and retarget visitors, Google Ads to catch high-intent searches, and SEO for long-term visibility. Our guides to Google Ads in Montreal and SEO in Montreal cover those channels.

How remote Meta ads management works

The UK is usually five hours ahead of Montreal, so we agree a regular call slot in the UK afternoon, which falls in the Montreal morning. You keep ownership of your Meta Business account, ad account, pages and billing.

  1. Discovery. We learn about your products or services, customers, languages and goals.
  2. Tracking and setup. We check or set up the pixel, server-side events and consent configuration with your team.
  3. Creative plan. We agree messages and formats, and plan French versions with your writer or translator.
  4. Launch and testing. We launch focused campaigns and test creative variations.
  5. Optimisation and reporting. We refine audiences, budgets and creative, and report monthly in plain language.

Questions to ask any Meta ads provider

  • Will we own the ad account, pages and all data?
  • How will you set up tracking and consent for Quebec?
  • Who will write our French ads and landing pages?
  • How often will you test new creative?
  • How will you report on sales and enquiries, not just reach?
  • How is your fee separate from our ad spend?

FAQs

How do I choose a Meta ads agency in Montreal?

Look for a provider who sets up tracking and consent properly, plans French creative with a fluent writer, tests creative regularly, reports on real results and leaves you owning every account.

Can a UK studio manage Meta ads for a Montreal business?

Yes. Meta accounts are managed online, with targeting set to your Canadian locations. Prism has no Canadian office; we work remotely and schedule calls in the Montreal morning.

Do my Montreal Facebook ads need to be in French?

In most cases French should lead, and Quebec's language rules cover commercial advertising. Check official guidance on what applies to your business.

How much does Meta ads management cost with Prism?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month depending on scope, with ad spend paid separately. We can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Does Law 25 affect Meta pixel tracking?

It can, because it includes rules on consent and tracking technologies. We set up tracking with your team and recommend checking official guidance or taking legal advice.

How long do Meta ads take to work?

Ads can reach people as soon as they are approved, but results typically improve over three to six months as tracking data builds and creative is refined.
