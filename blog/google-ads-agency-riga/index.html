Google Ads Agency for Riga Businesses

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Riga? How we plan, build and manage Google Ads remotely for Riga firms selling in Latvia, the Baltics and wider Europe.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 26 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Google Ads for a Riga business should match the language your customers search in, target the right Baltic and EU locations, and track real leads or sales.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Riga or Latvia; management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, with ad spend paid separately and euro pricing quoted on request.
  • Most accounts need 3 to 6 months of testing and refinement before performance settles, so a clear plan and honest reporting matter more than quick promises.

A Google Ads agency in Riga should help you reach people who are already searching for what you sell, in the language they search in, at a cost that makes sense for your margins. For most Riga businesses that means careful keyword choices across Latvian, English and often Russian searches, tight location targeting and conversion tracking that records real enquiries or orders.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Riga or anywhere in Latvia. We manage Google Ads for businesses that are comfortable working with a remote team through video calls, shared dashboards and written monthly reports, and we say so clearly before any work begins.

Why Google Ads suits Riga businesses

Riga is the largest city in the Baltic states and a hub for logistics, technology, business services, tourism, retail and manufacturing. Many Riga companies sell to customers in Latvia and also to buyers in Estonia, Lithuania, the Nordics, Germany and the UK. Google Ads helps because it lets you:

  • Appear for high-intent searches the same day a campaign goes live.
  • Target Riga, the rest of Latvia or other countries separately, with different budgets.
  • Run different ads and landing pages for each language.
  • Measure cost per lead or cost per sale and adjust quickly.
  • Promote products through Shopping campaigns if you run an online store.

Search volumes in Latvia are smaller than in larger EU markets, so wasted spend shows up quickly. That makes structure and tracking especially important.

Language and keyword strategy

Language is the first decision for most Riga accounts. Latvian is the official language and the default for most local searches, but many people in Riga also search in Russian or English, and business buyers often search in English when looking for suppliers.

  • Separate campaigns by language. Each language should have its own ad groups, ads and landing pages, rather than mixed keywords in one campaign.
  • Match the landing page. An English ad that sends people to a Latvian-only page wastes clicks.
  • Translation. Prism writes in English. Latvian and Russian ads and pages come from your team or a translator, and we build the structure around them.
  • Negative keywords. We add negatives from day one to filter jobs, free, DIY and irrelevant searches. Our negative keywords guide shows how this works.

Location targeting across Latvia and the Baltics

Many Riga businesses serve the city first, then the rest of Latvia, then neighbouring countries. Google Ads lets you set each of these up separately so budgets stay under control.

  • Use presence targeting so ads show to people physically in your area, not just people interested in it, unless you sell to visitors.
  • Split Riga and the rest of Latvia when delivery costs or service areas differ.
  • Run Estonia and Lithuania as separate campaigns with their own budgets and languages.
  • Exclude areas you do not serve.

Our location targeting guide explains each setting in plain terms.

Conversion tracking and measurement

Without reliable tracking, Google's bidding cannot learn and you cannot judge whether ads are worth the spend. Before we raise any budget we make sure tracking is in place.

  1. Install Google tag or Google Tag Manager on your site.
  2. Track key actions: form submissions, calls, bookings, purchases.
  3. Set up enhanced conversions where appropriate.
  4. Connect Google Analytics and check data matches.
  5. Respect EU consent rules with a proper consent banner and consent mode.

Our guides to conversion tracking setup and enhanced conversions go into detail. Privacy wording and consent decisions rest with you and your adviser; check official Latvian and EU guidance.

Landing pages that turn clicks into enquiries

Ads only work as well as the pages they send people to. We review your landing pages before launch and suggest changes, or build new ones where needed.

  • Match the headline to the search term and the ad.
  • Show the main offer, price guide or next step clearly near the top.
  • Keep forms short and test them on mobile.
  • Load quickly; slow pages raise cost per click and lose visitors.

Our first month on a Riga account

The first month sets the direction for everything that follows. We begin with an audit or a fresh plan, agree goals and budgets with you, and set up tracking before launching or restructuring campaigns. In weeks three and four we watch search terms closely, add negatives, adjust bids and check that leads or sales are recorded correctly. By the end of the month you receive a written report explaining what we launched, what we learned and the tests planned for month two. That steady rhythm keeps spend under control while the account builds the data it needs.
