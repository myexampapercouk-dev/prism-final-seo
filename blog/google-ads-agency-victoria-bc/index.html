How we set up Google Ads for Victoria businesses

Good campaigns start with structure. We build accounts around the searches that lead to enquiries, not around vanity traffic. Our standard setup includes:

  • Keyword research focused on high-intent phrases, such as emergency plumber Victoria or whale watching tours Victoria.
  • Tight ad groups so each ad matches the search closely.
  • Negative keywords to block irrelevant searches from day one.
  • Ad assets such as call, location and sitelink assets to make ads more useful.
  • Clear landing pages that match the ad and make the next step obvious.

Our negative keywords guide shows how much wasted spend a good list can prevent.

Location targeting on Vancouver Island

Victoria is a compact market with clear boundaries, which makes location targeting especially important. A landscaper in Saanich does not want clicks from Nanaimo, while a tour operator may want to reach visitors planning trips from Vancouver, Seattle or further afield. We set targeting based on:

  • Where your customers actually are, by postcode, municipality or radius.
  • Whether you want people located in an area or people interested in it, which matters for tourism.
  • Ferry and travel patterns that affect when visitors search.
  • Seasonal peaks, especially for tourism and outdoor services.

Our location targeting guide explains the settings in detail. If you also serve the mainland, our Vancouver Google Ads guide covers that larger market.

Conversion tracking that you can trust

Without accurate tracking, Google cannot optimise and you cannot judge results. We set up tracking for the actions that matter to you, such as form enquiries, phone calls, bookings and purchases, and we check it works before spending money. We also connect Google Ads to analytics so you can see what happens after the click. Our conversion tracking guide explains the main options.

Landing pages built for paid traffic

Many campaigns fail because they send traffic to a home page that does not answer the search. We recommend dedicated landing pages for important campaigns, with a clear headline, proof of what you offer, simple forms and fast loading. If your site needs work, we can build pages as part of the project. Our landing page design guide and Victoria web design guide explain more.

Using AI-powered features carefully

Google Ads now includes many AI-powered features, such as automated bidding, broad match and Performance Max. These can work well, but they need good conversion data and firm guardrails. We use AI-powered tools to speed up research and reporting, and we turn on automated features when the account is ready for them, with negative keywords, sensible budgets and regular checks of where ads are showing.

Pairing Google Ads with SEO

Paid search brings enquiries quickly, while SEO builds visibility that does not stop when budgets pause. Many Victoria businesses use both: ads for immediate leads and competitive terms, and SEO for long-term growth. Search term data from ads also shows which phrases are worth targeting with content. Our Victoria SEO guide explains how we approach local search, and our paid media service covers Google, Meta and other platforms.

Privacy and advertising basics

At a general level, Canadian businesses should consider PIPEDA, and in British Columbia the provincial PIPA may apply to private-sector organisations instead. CASL covers commercial emails and texts sent after an enquiry. Regulated sectors such as health and finance may face extra advertising rules. We build clear consent options and privacy notices, but compliance decisions rest with you; please check official guidance or your own adviser.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

When we audit accounts for Victoria businesses, the same problems come up again and again. Most are simple to fix once spotted:

  • Broad targeting by default. Campaigns set to show ads across all of British Columbia, or to people merely interested in the area, when the business only serves Greater Victoria.
  • No negative keywords. Budget spent on searches for jobs, free advice, DIY guides or competitors' names.
  • Display network switched on. Search campaigns quietly showing banner ads on unrelated websites.
  • Broken or missing tracking. Forms and calls that are not recorded, so Google optimises for the wrong thing.
  • One ad group for everything. Dozens of unrelated keywords sharing the same ad and landing page.
  • Ads running at all hours. Spend continuing overnight when nobody can answer the phone.

Fixing these basics often improves performance more than any advanced tactic. We always start an account takeover by tackling the biggest sources of waste first.

Seasonality in the Victoria market

Victoria's mild climate means some businesses stay busy all year, but many still see clear seasonal patterns. Tourism, whale watching, patios and outdoor activities peak in summer. Home services such as roofing and gutter cleaning pick up ahead of the wetter months, while landscaping and exterior work rise in spring. Education and student-focused businesses follow the academic calendar of the city's university and colleges. We plan budgets around these patterns, raising spend before peak demand and easing off in quieter months, so your money works hardest when people are ready to buy.
