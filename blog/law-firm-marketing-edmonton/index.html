How people look for a lawyer in Edmonton

Edmonton is Alberta's capital, so its legal market has a distinctive mix. Government, the courts, universities, health services, energy, construction, logistics and agriculture all generate legal work. Public sector employment brings labour and employment questions, a large student and newcomer population drives immigration and tenancy enquiries, and a growing metro area keeps family law, wills and estates, real estate and personal injury in steady demand. Clients come from across the city and from communities such as St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Fort Saskatchewan.

Private clients usually begin with a search: a practice area plus Edmonton, or their problem described in everyday words. Business clients search too, but they also ask for referrals, check LinkedIn and read a firm's website closely before making contact. Either way, the website is where most decisions are made. A credible law firm website

Your website should explain in plain language who you help, with which problems, and how to take the first step. Visitors want practice-area pages they can understand, a sense of how you work, information on how fees and first consultations are handled, and an easy way to get in touch. Business clients want to see depth on the issues their sector faces.

We build law firm websites with:

  • Separate, well-structured pages for each practice area and important sub-topic.
  • Lawyer profile pages written or approved by your firm.
  • Clear contact routes on every page, with forms that ask people not to send confidential details before a retainer is agreed.
  • Fast, accessible, mobile-friendly layouts on secure hosting.
  • A content management system your team can update without a developer.
  • Clean tracking so you can see which pages and channels bring enquiries.

A small firm website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and usually takes three to five weeks. Many firms choose WordPress for its flexibility; our guide to WordPress in Edmonton and our article on web design in Edmonton explain the options. Prices in Canadian dollars are quoted on request.

SEO for Edmonton law firms

Search engine optimisation is often a law firm's most valuable long-term channel, because people with legal problems search for answers before they search for a lawyer. A well-written page that explains a process in general terms can earn trust long before someone is ready to call. We focus on:

  • Practice-area pages that target the terms clients actually use, with Edmonton and the nearby communities you genuinely serve.
  • Helpful guides that answer common questions in general terms, reviewed and approved by your lawyers.
  • A complete Google Business Profile with accurate categories, hours, address and contact details.
  • Technical health: page speed, crawlability, structured data and sensible internal links.
  • Clear, well-structured content that AI search tools can understand and summarise accurately.

Results typically take three to six months to build, and longer in crowded practice areas such as personal injury. Our guides to SEO in Edmonton and local SEO in Canada go further, and our SEO and GEO service sets out what a monthly programme includes.

Google Ads and paid social for lawyers

Google Ads can bring enquiries quickly for urgent practice areas, but legal keywords are among the most expensive in search. Careful structure matters more than budget size. We build campaigns around tightly grouped practice-area keywords, add negative keywords to filter out job seekers, students and people looking for free help, and send each ad to the most relevant page rather than the home page. Call tracking and form tracking show which searches lead to real enquiries.

Meta Ads can work for practice areas where people research over time, such as immigration, wills and estates or family law, especially with helpful content rather than hard selling. LinkedIn Ads suit corporate, commercial and employment firms targeting business owners, HR leaders and in-house counsel. Our guides to Google Ads in Edmonton and LinkedIn Ads in Canada explain how we set these up. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms and is separate from our fees.

Professional rules and compliance

Lawyer marketing in Alberta is subject to professional conduct rules on advertising, including what you can say about expertise, fees and outcomes. We are not lawyers or regulatory advisers, so we keep our guidance general. Please check the Law Society of Alberta's current guidance or your own adviser before publishing anything. In practice, we work like this:

  • We avoid claims about results, rankings or being the best, and we do not invent reviews or testimonials.
  • Your firm reviews and approves every page, ad and post before it goes live.
  • We keep a simple record of approved wording so changes can be tracked.
  • Privacy notices and consent banners are agreed with your firm, reflecting Canadian privacy law as your advisers interpret it.

Approval of all content rests with your firm and its compliance processes.
