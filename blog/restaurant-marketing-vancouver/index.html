Restaurant Marketing in Vancouver: Local Search, Social Media and Ads for Restaurants

Restaurant marketing for Vancouver: local SEO, Google Business Profile, Instagram and Meta ads, menus and bookings, run remotely by a UK studio with no office.

Restaurant marketing in Vancouver is about making sure hungry people find you, like what they see and book a table or place an order without friction. For most venues that means strong local search visibility, a fast mobile website with a clear menu, appealing social content and targeted ads that fill quieter nights.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with Canadian businesses and have no office in Vancouver or anywhere else in Canada. This guide explains the channels that matter for Vancouver restaurants, how to prioritise them and what our support costs.

Who this guide is for

Vancouver has a varied food scene, from neighbourhood cafes and family restaurants to fine dining, food trucks, breweries and multi-site groups. Visitors and locals alike search for places to eat in areas such as Gastown, Yaletown, Kitsilano, Main Street, Commercial Drive and the North Shore. This guide suits:

  • Independent restaurants and cafes that want more bookings and walk-ins.
  • Multi-location groups that need consistent local listings and pages for each site.
  • Bars, breweries and wine bars promoting events and seasonal menus.
  • Takeaway and delivery-led kitchens that want more direct orders.
  • Caterers and event venues looking for private dining and group enquiries.

Start with Google Business Profile and local search

When someone in Vancouver searches for "sushi near me" or "brunch in Kitsilano", the map results usually appear first. Your Google Business Profile is the main thing that decides whether you show up there. We make sure your category, hours, holiday hours, address, phone number, menu link and booking link are accurate, and we help you add fresh photos and posts regularly.

Reviews matter a great deal for restaurants. We help you build a simple process for asking happy guests to leave a review and for replying to every review, positive or negative, in a calm and helpful way. We never write fake reviews or offer incentives that break platform rules.

Beyond the map, a well-structured website helps you rank for cuisine and neighbourhood searches. Our guide to SEO in Vancouver and our local SEO in Canada guide explain this in more detail.

Your website and menu

Most people checking a restaurant are on their phone, often standing nearby or deciding where to go that evening. Your website should load quickly, show your menu as readable text rather than a slow PDF, and make it obvious how to book, order or call.

We usually recommend:

  • A menu page in HTML, with prices and dietary notes you supply and keep up to date.
  • Clear booking and ordering buttons near the top of every page.
  • A page for each location if you have more than one.
  • Pages for private dining, events and group bookings.
  • Restaurant schema markup so search engines understand your opening hours, cuisine and location.

If your site needs a rebuild, see our guide to web design in Vancouver or, if you prefer WordPress, our WordPress agency Canada guide.

Social media and visual content

Food is visual, so Instagram and Facebook remain important for restaurants. Short videos of dishes being prepared, new menu items, the room on a busy night and behind-the-scenes clips from the kitchen all help people imagine visiting. TikTok can also work well for venues with a strong personality or a signature dish.

We plan content themes and posting rhythms, write English captions and help you turn your team's phone footage into usable posts and ads. We do not invent reviews, awards or results, and we keep claims about your food and service accurate.

Paid ads for restaurants

Ads are most useful for restaurants when they have a specific job, such as promoting a new opening, a seasonal menu, a festival weekend, a private dining offer or quiet weekday evenings. Meta ads can target people within a short radius of your venue, while Google Ads can capture people searching for your cuisine or for catering and event spaces.

Our guides to Meta Ads in Vancouver and Google Ads in Vancouver explain how we set up and manage these campaigns. Ad spend is always paid separately, directly to the platform.

Delivery apps and direct ordering

Delivery apps bring visibility, but they also take commission on every order. Many restaurants use them for reach while encouraging regulars to order directly through their own website. We help you make your direct ordering option easy to find, both on your site and on your Google Business Profile, and we track how many orders come through each route so you can make informed decisions.

Email, SMS and Canadian rules

A list of guests who have agreed to hear from you is one of the most valuable assets a restaurant can build. Simple monthly emails about new menus, events and offers often bring regulars back more cheaply than ads.

In Canada, CASL covers commercial electronic messages, so you generally need consent before sending marketing emails or texts, and every message should identify you and include an easy way to unsubscribe. PIPEDA and British Columbia's private sector privacy law cover how personal information is collected and used, and Quebec's Law 25 applies if you hold data on people in Quebec. This is general information only, so check official guidance or ask your own adviser. Our email marketing in Canada guide covers the practical side.

Bringing it together

For most Vancouver restaurants, the order of priority looks like this:

  1. Fix your Google Business Profile and start a review routine.
  2. Make sure your website is fast, mobile-friendly and makes booking easy.
  3. Post consistent, appealing social content.
  4. Build a consent-based email list.
  5. Use ads for specific goals, such as launches and quiet nights.

Our hospitality marketing page explains how we work with restaurants, cafes and venues more broadly. If you also operate in Ontario, our guide to restaurant marketing in Toronto covers that market.

Measuring what works

Restaurants often find it hard to tell which marketing brings guests through the door. We set up simple tracking for booking clicks, calls, direction requests and online orders, and we use unique links or codes for specific campaigns where your booking system allows. Monthly reports then show which channels drove actions, so you can put more effort into what works and less into what does not.

FAQs

What does restaurant marketing in Vancouver include?

Restaurant marketing in Vancouver usually covers Google Business Profile, local SEO, a fast website with a clear menu, social media content, targeted ads and email. We provide this remotely from the UK.

How do restaurants get more Google reviews?

Ask happy guests at the right moment, make it easy with a short link or QR code, and reply to every review. Never buy reviews or offer rewards that break platform rules.

Should my menu be a PDF?

We recommend an HTML menu page. It loads faster on phones, is easier to read and can be understood by search engines.

Are Instagram ads worth it for restaurants?

They can be, especially for openings, events and quieter nights, when targeted to people near your venue. Results depend on your creative, offer and budget.

Do you have an office in Vancouver?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely with Canadian businesses, with calls scheduled to suit Pacific Time.

Do I need consent to email my guests in Canada?

Generally, yes. CASL covers commercial electronic messages, so get consent and include an unsubscribe option. Check official guidance or your adviser for your situation.
