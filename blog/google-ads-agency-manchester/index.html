Targeting Greater Manchester properly

Greater Manchester includes the cities of Manchester and Salford along with Stockport, Trafford, Tameside, Oldham, Rochdale, Bury, Bolton and Wigan. A plumber in Stockport, a clinic in the city centre and a retailer in Bolton will each need very different targeting.

We usually start by agreeing the areas you can genuinely serve, then target them by town, postcode district or radius. Location reports show where clicks and conversions come from, so we can increase bids where results are strong and reduce them where they are not. For businesses that also serve Cheshire, Lancashire or West Yorkshire, separate campaigns can keep budgets and messages clear.

Ad scheduling matters too. If calls are only answered during working hours, running ads late at night may waste budget, while hospitality businesses may want the opposite.

Tracking, consent and sector rules

We set up GA4 and Google Ads conversions so that forms, calls, bookings and purchases are measured accurately. Our GA4 setup guide explains the basics.

UK GDPR and PECR require consent for non-essential cookies, including most advertising cookies, so a proper consent banner and Google's consent mode are part of a sound setup. Please check official ICO guidance or your own adviser on how the rules apply to you. Regulated sectors such as health, finance and legal services also face their own advertising rules and Google policies, and compliance approval of ads rests with you.

How to choose a Google Ads agency in Manchester

  • Make sure the ad account belongs to you and you keep access if you leave.
  • Ask how conversions are tracked and how cost per lead or sale is reported.
  • Ask how often search terms and negative keywords are reviewed.
  • Check whether the fee is fixed or a percentage of spend.
  • Avoid guaranteed results and long contracts without a clear exit.

An agency that explains its reasoning clearly, shares access openly and reports honestly on what is and is not working is usually a safer choice than one promising quick wins.

Common Google Ads mistakes we see in Manchester accounts

When we audit accounts for businesses across Greater Manchester, a small number of problems explain most of the wasted budget:

  • Location settings left on the default. Ads can appear for people who have only shown interest in Manchester rather than people who are actually there. Changing this setting is a simple, valuable fix.
  • Broad match without negative keywords. Broad keywords can work well with good data, but without negatives they often match job searches, training courses or unrelated services.
  • Tracking the wrong actions. Counting page views or button clicks as conversions misleads both you and Google's automated bidding.
  • Every service in one campaign. Grouping very different services together makes budgets and ad messages hard to control.
  • Sending all traffic to the homepage. A general homepage rarely converts as well as a page built for the specific search.
  • Infrequent search term reviews. The search terms report shows what people really typed, and reviewing it regularly is one of the most effective habits in paid search.

Fixing these basics usually makes more difference than advanced tactics. Once they are in place, automated bidding has cleaner data to learn from, and budget increases are far more likely to bring proportionate results.

What does a Google Ads agency in Manchester do?

A Google Ads agency in Manchester sets up tracking, researches keywords, builds and targets campaigns across the right parts of Greater Manchester, writes ads and manages budgets. It then reviews search terms, tests ads and landing pages and reports on results each month.

Do you have an office in Manchester?

No. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. Manchester clients work with us through shared dashboards, video calls and written monthly reports.

Should I target all of Greater Manchester?

Only if you genuinely serve all of it. Most businesses get better value by targeting the towns, postcodes or radius they can reach easily, then adjusting based on location reports.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Is Google Ads or SEO better for a Manchester business?
