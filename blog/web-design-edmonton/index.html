Web Design for Edmonton Businesses: A Remote Studio Guide

Need web design in Edmonton? How a remote studio builds websites for Edmonton businesses, what is included, timelines, costs and how to choose a designer.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Edmonton businesses can get a well-built website from a remote studio when the brief, content, approvals and ownership are agreed before design starts.
  • A typical small business website takes three to five weeks and an online shop five to twelve weeks, depending mostly on content and integrations.
  • Own your domain, hosting and website accounts from day one so you are never locked in to any designer or agency.

Web design for an Edmonton business should produce a fast, clear website that turns visitors into enquiries or sales, and that you fully own once it is finished. That can be done remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that designs and builds websites for Canadian businesses online, with no office in Edmonton, working through calls, shared documents and staged previews.

This guide explains what web design for an Edmonton business usually involves, how a remote project runs across time zones, what timelines and costs look like with us and how to choose any web designer with confidence.

What web design in Edmonton should deliver

Edmonton's economy is wide-ranging. Energy services, construction, engineering, trades, health clinics, professional firms, hospitality and independent retailers all rely on their websites to win work, and many serve customers across Alberta rather than just the city. Whatever the sector, a good website should do a handful of things well:

  • Make it obvious what you do within a few seconds of landing on the page.
  • Work properly on phones, since many visitors will find you on a mobile. Our guide to mobile website design covers the essentials.
  • Give each main service its own page, so both visitors and search engines understand your offer. Our service page design guide explains how to structure them.
  • Load quickly, with optimised images and lean code.
  • Make contact easy, with clear calls to action, forms that work and your details in obvious places.
  • Be accessible to people using screen readers, keyboards or zoom.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers design, build, content structure and launch in one project.

Designing for customers across Edmonton and Alberta

Edmonton covers a large area, and many businesses also serve nearby communities such as St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Fort Saskatchewan. A website for a business like that should make its service area clear without filling pages with thin lists of place names.

In practice, that means a clear service area statement, genuinely useful location content where you have real work to talk about, and contact details that match your Google Business Profile exactly. If search visibility is a priority alongside the new site, our guide to SEO for Edmonton businesses explains how the two fit together. Building the site with search in mind from the start is far easier than fixing it afterwards.

Online shops for Edmonton retailers

If you sell products, the platform matters. Shopify suits most retailers who want a reliable checkout without managing servers, while WooCommerce suits businesses that want everything inside WordPress. Our guide to Shopify for Canadian businesses covers Canadian-specific set-up points such as shipping zones, currency display and sales tax settings.

Tax rules for online sales can be complex, especially when selling into other provinces. We set up the platform settings you give us, but the tax position itself is for you and your accountant to confirm, so check current official guidance before launch.

Content, photos and proof of your work

The design is only as strong as what goes into it. Visitors want to see real evidence that you can do the job: photos of finished work, clear descriptions of what you offer, honest answers to common questions and straightforward information about how to get started. Stock photos have their place, but your own images of your premises, products or completed projects usually build far more trust.

We help by planning what each page needs before design starts, so you know exactly which text and images to gather. Where you have little written content, we can draft English copy from a short interview or a questionnaire about your services, then refine it with your feedback. Gathering this early is the single best way to keep a project on schedule, because waiting for content is the most common reason website launches slip.

Accessibility and legal pages

An accessible website is easier for everyone to use, and accessibility expectations for websites are growing across Canada. We design with sensible contrast, readable text sizes, keyboard-friendly navigation, descriptive link text and proper alt text. Our website accessibility guide explains the main principles, which apply wherever your customers are.

Privacy policies, cookie notices and terms should reflect how your business actually handles data. We can lay out the pages, but the wording should be checked against current official guidance and, where needed, reviewed by your legal adviser.

How a remote web design project works

Edmonton runs on Mountain Time, so the UK is usually seven hours ahead. UK late afternoons overlap with Edmonton mornings, which is enough for calls at key stages. Most of the work happens asynchronously through shared documents and a private preview site that you can review whenever suits you.

  1. Discovery. We agree goals, pages, features and who approves what.
  2. Content and structure. We plan the sitemap and page layouts, and gather your text and images.
  3. Design. You see the design on a preview link and give written feedback.
  4. Build. We build the site on a staging copy, test forms, speed and mobile layouts.
  5. Launch. We connect your domain, set up redirects if replacing an old site, and check everything works.
  6. Handover. You receive logins, guidance and the option of ongoing support.

Prices in Canadian dollars or other currencies can be quoted on request. Ongoing SEO or marketing support is available on monthly retainers, and full details are on our pricing page.

How to choose a web designer in Edmonton

Whether you work with a local designer or a remote studio, these questions help you compare fairly:

  • Will you own the domain, hosting, website files and all accounts?
  • What exactly is included in the price, and what counts as extra?
  • How many rounds of design changes are included?
  • Who writes the content, and who uploads it?
  • How will the old site's search rankings be protected if you are replacing one?
  • What support is available after launch, and what does it cost?

Ownership deserves special attention. Our guide to website ownership and handover lists what you should receive at the end of any project.

FAQs

How much does web design in Edmonton cost with Prism?

A small business website typically costs £1,000 to £2,000 and an ecommerce website £1,500 to £3,500, depending on scope. We can quote in Canadian dollars or other currencies on request.

Can a UK studio design a website for an Edmonton business?

Yes. Prism is a UK-based studio with no Edmonton office. We work with Canadian businesses remotely using calls, shared documents and preview links, with calls scheduled in the overlap between UK afternoons and Edmonton mornings.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical small business website takes three to five weeks and an ecommerce site five to twelve weeks. Content readiness and the speed of approvals usually make the biggest difference.

Will I own my website when it is finished?

Yes. Your domain, hosting, website files and accounts should all be in your name. We hand over logins and guidance at launch, and you can move to another provider at any time.

Do you write the content for my website?

We can plan page structure and write or edit English content for your site, working from your knowledge of your services. Any French content is written with your team or a professional translator.

Can you redesign my existing website without losing search rankings?

Yes, with care. We map old pages to new ones, set up redirects, keep useful content and check search settings at launch, which helps protect the visibility you have already earned.
