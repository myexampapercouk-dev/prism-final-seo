Hotel Marketing in Abu Dhabi: Direct Booking Websites, SEO and Paid Ads

Hotel marketing in Abu Dhabi: booking websites, SEO and Google, Meta and TikTok ads for hotels and resorts, from a UK-based remote studio with no local office.

Who we work with in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi draws guests for culture, beaches, motorsport, theme parks, conferences and family breaks, and each group searches and books in its own way. We support:

  • Independent hotels that want a stronger direct booking channel.
  • Beach and island resorts selling packages, spa breaks and day passes.
  • Serviced apartments and hotel apartments chasing longer corporate and family stays.
  • Boutique and desert properties with a clear story to tell.
  • Hotel restaurants, spas and beach clubs that need local residents as well as guests.

A hotel website built for direct bookings

Most guests compare your website with the same room on a booking site. If your site is slow, confusing or short on detail, they go back and book there instead. A direct booking website should make the decision easy:

  • A clear "Book now" button on every page that opens your booking engine with dates already chosen where possible.
  • Room pages with honest sizes, bed types, views, inclusions and real photos.
  • Offer pages for staycations, long stays, event weekends and family packages.
  • A location page that explains travel times to the airport, the Corniche, the islands and major venues in plain language.
  • Restaurant, spa and pool pages that can also rank and earn local bookings.
  • Fast mobile pages, because most guests search on their phones.

We explain the thinking behind this in more depth in our guide to winning more direct hotel bookings. The principles apply in Abu Dhabi just as well as in the UK.

Arabic and English content

Abu Dhabi hotels serve Emirati and Gulf guests, expatriate residents and international travellers, so most need both Arabic and English pages. We write in English. Your team or a professional translator writes the Arabic, and we build right-to-left layouts, language switchers and separate page addresses so search engines understand both versions. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website covers the build details, and the page on multilingual website design covers adding other languages for source markets.

SEO for Abu Dhabi hotels

Guests search for more than your hotel name. They look for "beach hotel Abu Dhabi", "hotel near the Grand Prix circuit", "family resort Saadiyat" or "hotel apartments Abu Dhabi monthly". SEO helps your own pages appear for those searches next to the booking sites. Our work usually includes:

  • Fixing technical problems such as slow pages, broken links and duplicate room pages.
  • Writing area and occasion pages that answer real guest questions.
  • Adding hotel structured data so search engines understand your rooms, location and facilities.
  • Keeping your Google Business Profile complete, with accurate photos, hours for outlets and answers to common questions.
  • Making content clear enough to be quoted in AI search answers.

Read more on our SEO and GEO service page, or see how we handle hotel search in our hotel SEO guide. SEO usually takes 3 to 6 months to show steady results, so we often pair it with ads in the first months.

Paid ads that bring bookings, not just clicks

Paid ads give hotels control over timing. You can push a summer staycation, a long-stay rate or an event weekend exactly when it matters. We set up:

  • Google Search campaigns for brand terms, so booking sites do not take guests searching for you by name, plus area and occasion terms.
  • Meta campaigns on Facebook and Instagram for offers, dining and spa promotions aimed at UAE residents.
  • TikTok and YouTube Ads using clips your team supplies. We do not film or edit video, but we plan the campaigns, write the copy and handle targeting and testing.
  • Conversion tracking on your booking engine where it allows, so you can see which campaigns produce bookings.

Our paid media service page explains how we run and report on campaigns. Ad spend is paid separately from our fees, straight to the platforms.

Seasons and events to plan around

Abu Dhabi's hotel demand moves with the cooler months, school holidays, public holidays, Ramadan and Eid, and the major sports, music and business events in the city. A good plan sets content and campaigns live weeks before each peak, not on the day. In the hotter months, many hotels shift budget towards residents with staycation, dining and pool offers. During Ramadan, messaging and timing should be respectful, and your team is best placed to judge tone and offers.

Common problems we fix for hotels

When we first look at a hotel's marketing, the same issues tend to come up. The booking button is hidden or opens a slow, unbranded page. Room photos are outdated or too small to judge. Arabic pages are missing, machine-translated or hard to find. Brand searches show booking sites above the hotel's own website, because nobody is bidding on the hotel name. Tracking stops at the booking engine, so nobody knows which campaigns bring revenue. Fixing these basics often matters more than any new channel, so we deal with them first and then build on top.

FAQs

What does hotel marketing in Abu Dhabi include?

It usually includes a direct booking website, hotel SEO, a Google Business Profile, Google Ads and social ads on Meta, TikTok or YouTube. The aim is to win more bookings on your own site and depend less on commission-based booking sites.

Do you have an office in Abu Dhabi?

No. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Abu Dhabi or the UAE and work through video calls, shared previews and written reports.

Can you write our Arabic content?

We write in English. Arabic content is written by your team or a professional translator, and we build the Arabic pages, right-to-left layout and language switcher around it.

How much does a hotel website cost?

Smaller websites cost £1,000 to £2,000, and larger sites with shops or voucher sales cost £1,500 to £3,500. Dirham pricing is quoted on request. Ad spend is paid separately.

Do you film hotel videos for TikTok and YouTube ads?

No. We do not film or edit video. We run TikTok and YouTube campaigns using clips your team or a video supplier provides, and we handle planning, copy, targeting and testing.

How long until hotel SEO brings more bookings?

SEO and paid campaigns typically take 3 to 6 months to show steady results. Ads can bring traffic sooner, so many hotels run both while SEO builds.
