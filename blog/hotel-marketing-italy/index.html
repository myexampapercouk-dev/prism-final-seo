Hotel Marketing in Italy: Win More Direct Bookings with SEO, Ads and a Better Website

Hotel marketing for Italy: SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads and booking-focused websites that grow direct bookings, from a UK-based studio that works remotely.

Who we help in Italian hospitality

Italy has one of the most varied hotel markets in Europe, from city hotels in Rome, Milan and Florence to coastal properties, lake resorts, rural agriturismi and mountain lodges. We work with:

  • Independent and boutique hotels that want to rely less on booking platforms.
  • Small hotel groups managing several properties and websites.
  • Guesthouses and bed and breakfasts that need a simple, effective website.
  • Agriturismi and countryside properties marketing experiences as well as rooms.
  • Resorts and seasonal properties that need to fill shoulder seasons.

A website built for direct bookings

Your website is where a direct booking happens or does not. A good hotel website for the Italian market usually has:

  • Fast, mobile-first pages. Most travellers browse on a phone, often on slow connections while travelling.
  • Clear booking buttons on every page, linked to your booking engine.
  • Room pages with honest descriptions, good photos, sizes and amenities.
  • Location content covering how to get there, what is nearby and why your area is worth visiting.
  • Language versions. Italian for domestic guests, English for international visitors and often German or French. Prism writes in English. Italian and other language content comes from your team or a translator.
  • Reasons to book direct, such as flexible terms or small extras, shown clearly.

SEO for Italian hotels

Hotel SEO is mostly about relevance and location. Guests search for things like a hotel type in a particular town, a property near a landmark, or a break with a certain feature. We help you rank for these searches with:

  • Keyword research by language and market, covering both domestic and international guests.
  • Well-structured room, location and experience pages.
  • Hotel structured data so search engines understand your property.
  • Correct hreflang tags for each language version.
  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with regular photos and replies to reviews.

Our hotel SEO guide and our guide to multilingual website design explain the approach. SEO results typically take three to six months to build.

Paid ads for hotels

Paid ads can bring bookings much faster than SEO. For hotels we usually focus on:

  • Brand search campaigns so guests searching your hotel name book with you, not through a reseller.
  • Destination campaigns targeting people searching for hotels in your area.
  • Meta Ads using your best photos and video you already have, to inspire travellers in key source markets.
  • Remarketing to people who viewed rooms but did not book.

Prism does not film or edit video. We work with photos and footage your team or photographer provides. Ad spend is paid separately to the ad platforms.

Planning around the Italian travel calendar

Italian hotels often have sharp seasonal patterns. City properties may stay busy most of the year, while coastal, lake and mountain hotels depend on a few strong months. Good marketing plans around this rather than running the same activity all year.

  • Early booking windows. Many international travellers plan summer trips months in advance, so SEO content and ads for those markets need to be live well before the season.
  • Shoulder seasons. Spring and autumn are often the best time to win extra direct bookings with packages, longer stays and themed breaks.
  • Domestic travel. Italian guests often book closer to their stay, especially for weekends and public holidays, so short-term offers can work well.
  • Events and local highlights. Festivals, food seasons, trade fairs and sporting events give you timely reasons to advertise and create content.

We build a simple marketing calendar with you at the start, so budgets rise when demand from each market is growing and fall when the property is already full. That keeps ad spend efficient and avoids paying for bookings you would have won anyway.

Measuring direct bookings properly

It is hard to grow what you cannot measure. Many hotel booking engines run on a separate domain, which can break analytics and make it look as if bookings come from nowhere. We work with your booking engine provider to set up cross-domain tracking where it is supported, so you can see which channels and campaigns lead to real bookings. We also check that booking confirmations are counted once, so reports and ad platforms receive accurate figures they can learn from over the season.

Privacy, cookies and accessibility

Hotels collect personal data at every step, from enquiry forms to bookings and email lists. These notes are general information, not legal advice. Please check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

  • GDPR. Personal data from guests is covered by the GDPR. Forms, booking flows and email lists should collect only what you need, with a clear privacy notice.
  • Cookie consent. EU ePrivacy rules generally require consent before non-essential cookies, such as analytics and advertising tags, are set. We set up tracking so it respects the choices your banner records. See our guide to cookie banner design.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied to many consumer-facing digital services since June 2025, and online booking is one of the areas it can affect. Microenterprises that provide services are generally exempt. The usual technical reference is EN 301 549, which points to WCAG at level AA. Our guide to the European Accessibility Act for websites covers the basics.
