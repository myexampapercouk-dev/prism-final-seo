Why Cambridge businesses use Google Ads

Cambridge has an unusual mix of buyers. Local residents search for trades, clinics, restaurants and shops. Students and visiting academics look for accommodation, tutoring and services. Science, technology and life sciences companies sell to other businesses across the UK and abroad. Each group searches differently, and Google Ads lets you reach exactly the searches that matter to you without waiting months for organic rankings.

The catch is cost. Busy markets can push up the price of each click, and a poorly built account can spend its budget on irrelevant searches. Good management is mostly about focus: the right keywords, the right locations, honest tracking and landing pages that convert.

What our Google Ads management includes

Our paid media service covers the full setup and ongoing management of your account:

  • Account audit: we review structure, keywords, search terms, bidding and tracking in any existing account.
  • Conversion tracking: GA4 and Google Ads conversions for calls, forms, bookings and sales. Our GA4 setup guide explains the basics.
  • Search campaigns: tightly themed ad groups, relevant ad copy and careful match types.
  • Shopping and Performance Max: product feeds and asset groups for online shops.
  • Negative keywords: regular search term reviews to block wasted spend.
  • Location and schedule targeting: focusing budget on the areas and hours that bring enquiries.
  • Landing page advice: clear pages that match each ad's promise.
  • Monthly reporting: spend, conversions and cost per lead explained with next steps.

Google Ads for local Cambridge services

For trades, clinics, salons, solicitors and other local firms, we usually start with search campaigns targeted at Cambridge and the areas you serve, such as Chesterton, Cherry Hinton, Trumpington, Histon, Milton, Ely, St Ives or Royston. We set radius or postcode targeting carefully so you do not pay for clicks from people you cannot serve.

Call tracking matters for local firms because many customers phone rather than fill in a form. We set up call conversions so you can see which keywords lead to real conversations, then move budget toward them.

Google Ads for Cambridge technology and B2B firms

Technology, software and science companies often sell to specialist buyers with long decision cycles. Search volume for niche terms can be small, so every click needs to count. We build campaigns around problem-based and product searches, add remarketing for return visits and track early actions such as demo requests or downloads alongside final enquiries.

If you sell software, our SaaS marketing page explains how we combine paid search with SEO and content. Some B2B firms also do well on LinkedIn, which we can run alongside Google Ads.

How we manage your budget

Your ad spend is separate from our fee and goes straight to Google. We agree a starting budget with you based on your goals and the likely cost of the searches you want. In the first weeks we watch search terms closely, add negative keywords and pause anything that is not pulling its weight.

We prefer to start narrow and expand once we know what works. A smaller, well-focused account usually beats a large one spread thinly across many loosely related keywords.

Pricing for Google Ads management

Our monthly retainers are listed on our pricing page:

  • Launch, £350 a month: one focused campaign for a local or single-service business.
  • Growth, £750 a month: several campaigns, Shopping or remarketing, and regular landing page testing.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: larger accounts, multiple channels or busy online shops.

Ad spend is paid separately to Google. If you need a landing page or new website first, a small website costs £1,000 to £2,000. Our guide to PPC management costs in the UK compares common fee models.

How long until Google Ads works

Ads can show within days of launch, so you can see clicks and early enquiries quickly. Getting the account to a steady, efficient cost per lead takes longer, and paid results typically settle over 3 to 6 months as we gather data and refine targeting, bids and ads.

We will not promise a fixed number of leads, because results depend on your market, offer, prices and website. We will explain what the data shows each month and what we are changing as a result.

Landing pages make or break your ads

Even the best campaign struggles if visitors land on a slow or confusing page. Every ad should lead to a page that repeats the promise, shows prices or next steps clearly and makes calling or booking simple on a phone. Our landing page design guide covers the essentials, and our web design in Cambridge post explains how we build full sites.

Signs your current account needs help

  • You cannot see which keywords bring in calls or sales.
  • The search terms report shows lots of irrelevant searches.
  • All your keywords sit in one or two large ad groups.
  • Performance Max is running without proper conversion tracking.
  • Your budget runs out before lunchtime most days.
  • Nobody has reviewed the account in months.

If several of these sound familiar, an audit is a good first step. Our UK Google Ads agency guide also explains what to expect from any management partner.

What happens in the first month

The first month sets the foundations for everything that follows. We begin with a discovery call to understand your services, margins, target areas and the enquiries that matter most. If you already have an account, we audit it and record a baseline so later changes can be judged fairly.

Next we check or rebuild conversion tracking, because bidding decisions are only as good as the data behind them. We then build or restructure campaigns, write new ads, add a starting list of negative keywords and set sensible location and schedule targeting. Before anything goes live, you review the plan and the ad copy.

Once ads are running, we look at the search terms report often in the early weeks, since this is where most wasted spend shows up. By the end of the month you receive a short report covering spend, clicks, conversions, what we changed and what we plan to test next.

Regulated and sensitive sectors

Some Cambridge businesses, such as clinics, financial firms and education providers, face extra rules on what their ads can say. Google also has its own policies for areas like healthcare and financial services. We keep ad copy factual and point you to official guidance, but approval of claims and wording rests with you and your own advisers or compliance team.

Working with firms across Cambridgeshire

We work with businesses in Cambridge and nearby towns such as Ely, Huntingdon, St Neots, Newmarket, Saffron Walden and Royston, as well as Cambridge companies that sell across the UK. Because we work remotely, location does not limit what we can manage for you.
