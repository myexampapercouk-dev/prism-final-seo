Real Estate Marketing in Calgary: Websites, Local SEO and Ads for Agents, Brokerages and Developers

Real estate marketing in Calgary: websites, local SEO, Google and Meta ads and lead follow-up for agents and brokerages, from a UK studio with no local office.

Real estate marketing in Calgary means getting your listings, your name and your brokerage in front of buyers and sellers at the moment they start searching, then following up fast enough to turn that interest into appointments. The agents and teams who do this well combine a quick, mobile-friendly website, neighbourhood-focused content, a well-kept Google Business Profile, targeted search and social ads, and a simple system for tracking every enquiry.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with Canadian businesses and have no office in Calgary or anywhere else in Canada. This guide explains how we approach marketing for Calgary real estate professionals, what each channel does, what it costs and where the common mistakes sit.

We wrote this guide for people selling or leasing property in Calgary and the surrounding area, including:

  • Independent agents who want a personal brand that is not tied only to a brokerage page.

Whether you focus on inner-city condos, family homes in the suburbs or acreages outside city limits, the same foundations apply. If you sell in other Canadian cities too, our guides to real estate marketing in Toronto and real estate marketing in Vancouver cover those markets.

Start with a website you own

Portals and brokerage pages are useful, but they belong to someone else. Your own website is where you control the message and collect enquiries directly. For a Calgary agent or team, a good real estate website usually includes:

  • A clear home page that says who you help, which parts of Calgary you cover and how to get in touch.
  • Listing pages or an approved listings feed, set up within the rules of your board and brokerage.
  • Neighbourhood and community pages written from your own knowledge of the area.
  • A home valuation page for sellers, with a short form and a clear promise of what happens next.
  • Buyer and seller guides that answer common questions in plain language.
  • Fast load times and a layout that works well on phones, where most property browsing happens.

We cover the wider build process in our guide to web design in Calgary. For real estate, the priority is speed, trust and an obvious next step on every page.

Local SEO for Calgary agents and brokerages

Search engines reward pages that answer real local questions well. For real estate, that usually means building content around communities, property types and the decisions buyers and sellers face. Useful page ideas include:

  • Community guides covering housing types, schools, transport links, amenities and the feel of each area.
  • Pages for property types such as condos, townhouses, detached homes and acreages.
  • Guides for first-time buyers, relocating families and people moving to Calgary from other provinces.
  • Seller guides on preparing a home, timing and what a listing process involves.
  • Plain explanations of closing costs and the buying process, with a note to confirm details with a lawyer or mortgage professional.

Write from experience and avoid repeating the same text across community pages. Our guide to choosing an SEO agency in Calgary explains how we plan this kind of content and how long it takes to build momentum, which is typically three to six months.

Google Business Profile and reviews

When someone searches for a real estate agent near them, the map results often appear before the regular listings. A complete, accurate Google Business Profile is one of the cheapest ways to win those enquiries. Choose categories carefully, set a realistic service area, add good photos and post regular updates. Our guide to Google Business Profile categories explains how to pick the right primary and secondary options.

Reviews matter a great deal in real estate because clients are trusting you with one of the biggest decisions of their lives. Ask every happy client for an honest review soon after closing, make the request easy and reply to every review politely. Never offer incentives for reviews or write them yourself.

Google Ads for Calgary real estate

Search ads put you in front of people actively looking for an agent, a valuation or a specific type of property. The strongest campaigns we see in real estate usually focus on:

  • Seller intent, such as searches for home valuations or selling a house in a particular part of the city.
  • Agent searches, such as people looking for a realtor in a named community.
  • New development searches for builders marketing a specific project.

Each campaign should send traffic to a focused landing page rather than the home page, with conversion tracking for forms and calls. Our guide to landing page design covers the structure we use. We also explain budgets and account set-up in our guide to choosing a Google Ads agency in Calgary.

Meta Ads for listings and developments

Facebook and Instagram are well suited to showing homes visually through video walk-throughs, photo carousels and open house announcements. They also work well for building an audience of local homeowners who may sell in the next year or two.

Ads about housing may need to run under Meta's special ad category, which restricts some targeting options such as age and detailed demographic targeting. Rules change, so we check the current requirements before launching any campaign. We explain the wider approach in our guide to choosing a Meta Ads agency in Calgary.

Advertising rules, privacy and consent

Real estate is a regulated profession in Alberta. Advertising by licensed professionals is subject to rules set by the provincial regulator and to the policies of your brokerage and local board. These rules can cover how you identify your brokerage in ads, how you describe listings and what claims you make. We are not lawyers or compliance advisers, so we keep our notes general. Final approval of advertising content rests with you and your brokerage, and you should check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

Privacy and consent matter too. Canada's federal privacy law, PIPEDA, and Alberta's own private sector privacy law set out how businesses collect and use personal information. Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, known as CASL, governs commercial emails and texts, so build your lists with clear consent and include an easy unsubscribe option. We set up forms, consent wording placeholders and email tools in a way that supports this, but you are responsible for confirming your obligations.

Lead follow-up and tracking

Real estate leads lose value quickly. A buyer who enquires on a Saturday morning may have booked viewings with another agent by the afternoon. A good set-up includes:

  • Instant email or text notifications for every new enquiry.
  • A CRM or shared inbox so nothing is missed when a team is busy.
  • Automated reminders for valuation follow-ups and long-term buyer nurture.
  • Conversion tracking so you can see which channels, campaigns and pages produce real conversations.

Where it helps, we use AI-powered tools to draft listing descriptions or follow-up messages, and a person on your team always reviews them first.

FAQs

What is the best real estate marketing in Calgary for a new agent?

Start with a fast personal website, a complete Google Business Profile and a habit of asking every client for a review. Add a few strong community pages, then test Google Ads for seller valuation searches once the basics are in place.

Do you have an office in Calgary?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely. We hold calls at times that suit Calgary and handle all strategy, design and campaign work online.

How much should a Calgary agent spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your goals, areas and competition. We usually suggest a modest test budget focused on high-intent searches, then increasing spend only once tracking shows which campaigns produce real conversations. Ad spend is paid directly to Google.

Can you add listings to my website?

Yes, where your board and brokerage allow it. We work with approved listing feeds or manual listing pages, and you remain responsible for meeting the data and display rules that apply to you.

Are there rules for real estate advertising in Alberta?

Yes. Licensed professionals must follow the provincial regulator's advertising rules and their brokerage policies. We keep our notes general, so please check official guidance or ask your own adviser, as final approval rests with you and your brokerage.

How long does real estate SEO take in Calgary?

SEO results typically take three to six months to build, and competitive terms can take longer. Community pages, reviews and a technically sound site all help progress over time.
