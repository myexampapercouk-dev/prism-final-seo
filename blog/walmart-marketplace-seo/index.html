Walmart Marketplace SEO: How to Optimise Listings That Get Found

Walmart Marketplace SEO means accurate attributes, clear titles, strong images and competitive offers. Here is how to optimise listings for Walmart.com.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Walmart Marketplace SEO depends heavily on complete, accurate product attributes, alongside clear titles, key features, descriptions and images.
  • Visibility on Walmart.com is tied to the offer as well as the content, so price, stock, shipping speed and reviews all affect how listings perform.
  • Walmart's style guides and listing rules change, so check current Seller Center guidance for your category; an outside team can research and write while you control the account.

Walmart Marketplace SEO is the work of helping your product listings appear when shoppers search on Walmart.com, and then giving them enough confidence to buy. It relies on complete product attributes, clear titles and key features, good images and a competitive offer, all written within Walmart's current content rules for your category.

We are a UK-based studio offering marketplace listing SEO, and we work remotely with US sellers. We have no US office. This guide explains how Walmart search tends to behave, how to approach each part of a listing, and how outside help fits alongside your own Seller Center account.

How Walmart search differs from Amazon and Google

Like other marketplaces, Walmart.com is built to sell products rather than to answer questions. Search results are shaped by relevance (whether your listing matches what was searched) and by the strength of your offer (price, availability, delivery speed and customer feedback). Walmart does not publish a full ranking formula, so anyone promising a guaranteed position should be treated with caution.

Two points stand out compared with Amazon:

  • Attributes carry real weight. Walmart relies heavily on structured product data such as brand, size, colour, material and category-specific fields. Missing or wrong attributes can keep a product out of filtered results.
  • Content quality is scored. Seller Center has shown sellers a listing quality view that highlights gaps in content, offer and ratings. Use whatever quality tools Walmart currently provides as a checklist for each listing.

If you already sell on Amazon, our guide to Amazon SEO for US sellers covers the overlap. Much of the research can be shared, but the content should be adapted to Walmart's own rules rather than copied across.

Walmart Marketplace SEO starts with research

Good listings start with the words US shoppers really use. Research for Walmart usually draws on:

  • Search suggestions on Walmart.com as you type your main product terms.
  • The structure and wording of strong competitor listings in your category.
  • Search data from any Walmart advertising you run, which can show the queries that led to clicks.
  • Customer reviews and questions, which reveal the problems and features buyers care about.
  • Your own store search and Google data, which often uncover terms marketplaces share.

Group terms by meaning rather than chasing every variation. A main term goes in the title, closely related terms go into key features and the description, and specifics such as size or material go into the right attribute fields. Our keyword research guide explains how to group terms by intent.

Titles

Walmart titles tend to follow a predictable pattern of brand, product type and defining details such as size, count or colour. Keep them readable. Repeating keywords or adding promotional phrases usually breaks the style guide and makes the listing look less trustworthy. Check the current title guidance for your category before writing.

Key features and description

Key features are the scannable bullets shoppers see near the top of the page. Lead each one with the benefit, then the detail. The description gives you room to explain who the product suits, how to use it and what is included. Our guide to writing ecommerce product descriptions covers the structure in more depth, and it applies just as well to marketplace copy.

Attributes

Attributes are where many listings fall short. Fill in every field that applies, use the exact values Walmart expects where a list is provided, and keep variant data (sizes and colours) consistent. Accurate attributes help your products appear when shoppers use filters, which many do on large marketplaces.

Images

Walmart has specific rules for main images, usually a clean product shot on a white background, plus requirements for size and quality. Additional images should answer questions copy cannot: scale, texture, what is in the box and the product in use. Our ecommerce product photography guide explains how to brief a shoot that works across your own store and marketplaces.

The offer matters as much as the copy

A perfectly written listing will still struggle if the price is uncompetitive, stock runs out or delivery is slow. Walmart also considers how your offer compares with others selling the same item. Areas to review regularly include:

  • Price compared with other sellers and with the same product elsewhere online.
  • Stock levels, particularly before seasonal peaks.
  • Shipping speed and accuracy, whether you fulfil yourself or use Walmart's fulfilment service.
  • Ratings and reviews, gathered only through methods Walmart allows.
  • Order defect, cancellation and late shipment rates, which affect account health.

Pricing, fulfilment and account health decisions stay with you. We focus on research, content and recommendations, and we flag offer issues when we see them affecting visibility.

Walmart alongside Amazon, eBay and your own store

Many US sellers treat Walmart as a second or third marketplace. That can widen reach, but each channel has its own rules and audience. Our comparison of selling on Amazon vs your own website sets out the trade-offs between marketplaces and direct sales, and our guide to eBay SEO shows how another marketplace handles listings differently.

Your own website still matters. It is the only channel where you fully own the customer relationship. Strong product page SEO on your store and strong marketplace listings tend to support each other, because the same research and product content can feed both.

How remote listing optimisation works

  1. Audit: we review a set of listings against Walmart's current guidance and competitor examples.
  2. Research: we build keyword groups for each product or product family.

A note on rules and compliance

Walmart's content guidelines, prohibited product rules and seller policies change. Product claims, safety information and labelling may also be covered by federal or state rules depending on what you sell. Check Walmart's current Seller Center guidance and take advice from your own lawyer or compliance adviser where claims or regulated products are involved.

FAQs

What is Walmart Marketplace SEO?

It is the process of improving your Walmart.com listings so they appear for relevant searches and convert shoppers. It covers keyword research, titles, key features, descriptions, attributes and images, alongside a competitive price and reliable delivery.

Is Walmart SEO different from Amazon SEO?

The principles overlap, but Walmart places heavy weight on structured attributes and has its own content rules and quality tools. Research can be shared between channels, but listings should be adapted for each marketplace rather than copied word for word.

How long does Walmart listing optimisation take to work?

Changes can be indexed fairly quickly, but judging the effect takes time because sales history, reviews and seasonality all play a part. As with other SEO work, meaningful results are typically assessed over three to six months.

Can a UK agency optimise Walmart listings for a US seller?

Yes. Listing optimisation is research and content work that can be done remotely. We write in American English for US shoppers, and you keep control of your Seller Center account and pricing.

Do attributes really affect Walmart search?

Accurate attributes help Walmart understand and categorise your product, and they decide whether it appears when shoppers use filters such as size, colour or brand. Missing fields are one of the most common gaps in Walmart listings.

How much does Walmart Marketplace SEO cost?

It depends on the number of products and how much work each needs. Our monthly retainers start at £350, with Growth at £750 and Scale at £1,500. Any advertising spend on Walmart is separate and paid directly by you.
