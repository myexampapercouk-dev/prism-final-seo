Real estate marketing in Amman: property websites, local SEO, Meta and Google lead campaigns and enquiry tracking for agencies and developers, from a UK studio.

Why real estate marketing in Amman needs a local focus

Amman's property market is shaped by its neighbourhoods. Buyers looking in Abdoun or Dabouq often have different budgets and expectations from those looking in Khalda, Tla' Al Ali, Jubeiha or the newer areas towards the airport road. Renters, first-time buyers, families upgrading and Jordanians living abroad who want to invest back home each have their own questions.

That last group matters. Many Jordanians working in the Gulf, Europe and North America search for property in Amman from overseas. They cannot easily visit, so they rely on detailed listings, clear photos, floor plans and a trustworthy website. Good marketing reaches them where they are and gives them the confidence to enquire.

Property portals play a big role, and most agencies will keep using them. Your own website and campaigns are where you build your brand, capture enquiries you own and show the depth of your knowledge about each area.

A property website that generates enquiries

A property website should make it easy to search, compare and enquire. We design sites with simple filters for area, property type, price range and bedrooms, and listing pages that show photos, floor plans, key features and location clearly. Every listing has a visible enquiry button, a call option and WhatsApp where you use it.

For developers, each project gets its own page with the location, unit types, payment plan information you choose to publish, progress updates and a focused enquiry form. Our website and ecommerce development service handles design and build, and our guide to website speed optimisation explains why fast loading matters for image-heavy property sites.

For a bilingual site, we handle Arabic and English versions with proper right-to-left layouts, as described in our article on multilingual website design. Our web design guide for Jordan covers the wider local context.

SEO for Amman real estate

People search for property by area, type and purpose: apartments for sale in a particular neighbourhood, villas for rent, offices in a business district, land plots on the edge of the city. Each of these searches is a chance for a well-built page to rank.

Through our SEO and GEO service we research those searches and build area guides, property type pages and project pages that answer real questions: what the area is like, what types of property are common, how it connects to the rest of the city and what buyers should consider. We also fix technical issues such as duplicate listings, expired listing pages and slow image loading, which often hold property sites back. Our SEO agency guide for Jordan covers the search landscape in more depth.

For agencies with an office, a complete Google Business Profile helps you appear in map results. Our Google Business Profile guide explains the essentials.

Lead campaigns on Meta and Google

Paid campaigns can bring enquiries quickly, especially for new projects and launches. Meta lets you reach people in Amman and Jordanians living abroad with targeted ads and built-in lead forms. Google Ads captures people actively searching for property in specific areas.

We plan and run campaigns through our paid media service. We focus on lead quality, not just volume, using qualifying questions in lead forms, sending leads instantly to your sales team and tracking which campaigns produce real viewings. Our article on Meta Ads in Amman explains targeting and creative in more detail.

Housing is a special ad category on Meta in many regions, which can limit some targeting options, and policies change over time. We follow current platform rules and adjust campaigns when they change. Prism does not film or edit video. Property tour footage comes from your team or a creator, and we manage the advertising.

Following up on leads

Property leads go cold quickly. A buyer who enquires on Friday evening and hears nothing until Monday has often moved on. We help you set up simple systems so leads reach the right person immediately, by email, WhatsApp notification or your CRM. Where it helps, we can add automated acknowledgements so the enquirer knows their message arrived.

We also set up tracking so you can see which listings, areas and campaigns generate enquiries, and which of those become viewings and sales when your team records outcomes.

Compliance and accuracy

Property advertising must be accurate. Prices, sizes, payment terms and availability should always be confirmed by your team before they appear in ads or on your website. Rules on property marketing and data protection apply in Jordan, so check official guidance and confirm details with your own adviser. Final approval of listings and advertising rests with your agency or development company.

Common problems on property websites

We often see the same issues on property websites. Listings stay live long after a property is sold or let, which frustrates visitors and wastes their time. Photos are uploaded at huge file sizes and make pages slow. Listing descriptions are copied from portals, so the site adds nothing new for search engines. Enquiry forms ask for too much information, and enquiries arrive in an inbox nobody checks at weekends.

Each problem costs leads. A buyer abroad who enquires about a property that was sold weeks ago may not trust the agency again. A slow page on a mobile connection loses visitors before the first photo appears. Duplicate descriptions make it hard for your site to rank above the portals that carry the same text.

We fix these in a practical order. First we set up a clear process for marking properties as sold or let and handling those pages properly for search. Then we optimise images, rewrite key listing and project descriptions with your team, simplify enquiry forms and make sure every enquiry reaches someone who can respond quickly.

Marketing new developments in Amman

Off-plan and new-build projects need a slightly different approach. Buyers want to understand the developer's track record as you choose to present it, the location and its future, the unit mix, the payment plan and the expected handover timeline. A dedicated project page, a short focused landing page for campaigns and a clear brochure request or enquiry form usually work well together. We plan launch campaigns in phases, building awareness first and then focusing budget on audiences who have shown interest.
