Dental marketing for Dublin practices: local SEO, Google Ads, a clear website and careful patient messaging, run remotely by a UK studio from £350 a month.

Dental marketing in Dublin is about being found by local patients at the moment they search, then making it easy for them to book. For most practices, the core mix is a well-kept Google Business Profile, clear treatment pages, local SEO for each area you serve, targeted Google Ads and a fast, mobile-friendly website.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Dublin or anywhere in Ireland, and we work through video calls, shared documents and written reports. We provide general marketing support, not clinical or legal advice. All claims about treatments must be checked and approved by your practice, and you should follow guidance from the Dental Council of Ireland and your own advisers.

How Dublin patients choose a dentist

Most people start with a search such as "dentist near me", "emergency dentist Dublin" or a treatment name with an area, such as "teeth whitening Rathmines". They look at the map results, read reviews, check opening hours and then visit a website or call. Some compare prices and finance options, especially for higher-value treatments such as implants, Invisalign-style aligners or cosmetic work.

Dublin is spread out, and patients usually prefer a practice close to home or work. That makes local search especially important. A practice in Dublin 2 competes for different searches than one in Swords, Tallaght or Dún Laoghaire. Our local SEO guide explains the basics that apply here too.

Getting your Google Business Profile right

For many practices, the Google Business Profile drives more calls than the website. Make sure your name, address and phone number match everywhere, choose the right primary category, list your services, add real photos of your practice and keep opening hours up to date, including holidays.

Reviews matter a lot. Ask happy patients to leave a review, and reply politely to every review. Never offer incentives for reviews and never share patient details in a reply. Replies should be general and professional, especially when responding to a complaint.

Treatment pages that rank and convert

Each main treatment should have its own page. A single "services" page listing everything rarely ranks well. Good treatment pages explain what the treatment is, who it may suit, what happens at the appointment, how long it takes and how to book. They should be written in plain language, without promises of results.

We write treatment page drafts in English based on information your clinicians provide, and your team checks and approves every word before it goes live. We avoid before-and-after claims and anything that could be read as a guarantee. The dental marketing guide on our blog covers page structure in more detail.

Area pages can help too, but only where they offer real value, such as directions, parking, nearby transport and which treatments are available at that location. Thin pages that only swap the area name will not help.

Google Ads for dental practices

Google Ads work well for searches with clear intent, such as emergency dental care or specific treatments. A good campaign uses tight keyword groups, location targeting around your practice, call extensions and ads that match each treatment page.

Healthcare ads come with platform rules and professional guidelines, so wording needs care. Our paid media service handles setup and management, while your practice approves all ad copy. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from our fee. For a wider view of paid search in the city, see our article on the Google Ads approach in Dublin.

Your website and booking path

Most patients will view your site on a phone. It needs to load quickly, show your phone number and booking button clearly, list your fees or fee ranges if you choose to share them, and explain how to find you. Online booking, if your practice software supports it, removes friction.

Trust signals help: real photos of your team and rooms (with consent), clear information about your clinicians' registration, accessibility details and payment options. Our guide to website accessibility explains how to make your site easier for all patients to use.

If your site needs a rebuild, our website development service can help. Small websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and typically take three to five weeks.

Privacy, consent and professional rules

Dental practices handle sensitive health information, so privacy matters. Contact and booking forms should collect only what you need, link to a clear privacy policy and be stored securely. Tracking cookies should only run after consent, in line with GDPR and Irish data protection guidance.

Advertising by dentists in Ireland is also covered by professional guidance. In general, that means avoiding misleading claims, unfair comparisons and anything that pressures patients. We are not legal or clinical advisers, so please check the Dental Council of Ireland's guidance and take advice where needed. Final approval of all content rests with your practice. Meta Ads and patient awareness

Not every patient is searching right now. Some have been thinking about straightening their teeth or replacing a missing tooth for months, but have not taken action. Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram can reach local adults in the areas around your practice and remind them that help is close by.

These campaigns work best with a simple, honest message and a clear next step, such as booking a consultation. Avoid emotional pressure, unrealistic images or claims that a treatment will change someone's life. Meta also has its own rules for health-related ads, and targeting options for sensitive categories are limited. We keep audiences broad by location and age, rather than by any health interest.

Photos and video should come from your own practice, with written consent from anyone shown. We do not film or edit video, so your team or a local photographer supplies these. Real images of your rooms and staff usually build more trust than stock photos.

Measuring what matters

Good dental marketing is measured in booked appointments, not clicks. We set up tracking for calls from your website and profile, form submissions and online bookings, so you can see which channels bring patients in. Where possible, we compare these numbers with what your front desk records, as some patients call after seeing an ad without clicking it.

Your monthly report shows:

  • Calls, forms and online bookings by channel.
  • Rankings for your main treatments and areas.
  • Google Business Profile views, calls and direction requests.
  • Ad spend and cost per enquiry for each campaign.
  • What we changed this month and what we plan next.

This makes it easy to decide where to spend more, and where to cut back, based on your own capacity and the treatments you most want to grow.
