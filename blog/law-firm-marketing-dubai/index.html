Dubai's legal market serves local businesses, international companies, investors, families and individuals, across onshore courts and the free zone legal systems. We support:

  • Corporate and commercial firms advising on company setup, contracts and disputes.
  • Real estate and construction practices serving developers, investors and owners.
  • Family and personal law practices helping residents with sensitive matters.
  • Employment and immigration advisers working with employers and staff.
  • Legal consultancies and international firms with a Dubai presence.

A website that earns trust

A legal website must look professional, but it also needs to answer practical questions fast. The best law firm sites we build include:

  • One page for each practice area, explaining who you help, common situations and how an engagement usually starts.
  • Lawyer profiles with qualifications, languages and areas of focus, supplied and approved by your firm.
  • Clear contact options: a short form, phone, email and WhatsApp where your firm uses it.
  • Insight articles that explain legal topics in plain language, written or reviewed by your lawyers.
  • A privacy notice and cookie settings that your firm approves.
  • Fast pages that work well on mobile and in both Arabic and English.

See our website development service page for how projects run, or our guide to web design in Dubai for the local view. Turning visitors into enquiries is covered in our guide to conversion rate optimisation for law firms.

SEO for Dubai law firms

People search for legal help in specific ways: "employment lawyer Dubai", "property dispute lawyer", "company setup lawyer DIFC" or "will registration Dubai". SEO helps your practice area pages rank for those searches. Our SEO work includes:

  • Fixing technical problems that slow your site or confuse search engines.
  • Planning practice area and topic pages around real search demand.
  • Adding structured data for your firm, offices you actually have and services.
  • Keeping your Google Business Profile accurate and complete.
  • Writing clear answers that search engines and AI search tools can quote.

Read more on our SEO and GEO service page or our guide to SEO in Dubai. SEO typically takes 3 to 6 months to show steady results, which suits legal services where clients often research for a while.

Paid ads for legal services

Paid campaigns can bring enquiries quickly, but legal ads need care. Platforms have their own policies, and the professional rules your firm follows may limit what you can say. We keep ads factual, avoid promises about outcomes and send each ad to the matching practice area page.

  • Google Search ads for practice areas where people search with urgent needs, with call and form tracking.
  • LinkedIn Ads for corporate and commercial work, aimed at job titles, industries and company sizes. Our page on LinkedIn ads in the UAE covers this in detail.
  • Meta ads for personal law services where your firm is comfortable with social advertising.

For search campaigns in the city, see our page on Google Ads in Dubai. Ad spend is paid separately, straight to the platforms.

Arabic and English content

Dubai firms serve Arabic-speaking clients and a large English-speaking business community, so most need both languages. We write in English. Your lawyers or a professional legal translator write the Arabic, and your firm checks it. We build right-to-left pages, a clear language switcher and separate page addresses so both versions can rank. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website explains how.

Professional rules stay with your firm

Legal services in Dubai are regulated, and rules on advertising, client contact, titles and claims can differ between onshore practice and the free zones. We keep our guidance general and do not give legal or regulatory advice. Your firm approves every page, profile and ad, and should check official guidance from the relevant regulator and its own adviser. We do not publish client names, case results or testimonials unless your firm provides them and confirms they are allowed.

Common problems we fix

Law firm websites often list practice areas as short bullet points with no detail, so they rarely rank. Contact forms ask too many questions. Lawyer profiles are out of date. Arabic pages are missing or hard to find. Ads send every visitor to the homepage. Nobody tracks calls, so the firm cannot see which channels bring enquiries. We fix these first because they affect every other channel.

What to measure each month

Legal marketing should be judged by good enquiries, not only by traffic. We set up tracking in the first weeks and report each month on:

  • Form enquiries, calls and email clicks from each practice area page.
  • Calls and website clicks from your Google Business Profile.
  • Search visibility for your main practice areas.
  • Cost per enquiry for Google and LinkedIn campaigns.
  • Which insight articles bring readers who go on to contact you.

Your team adds what only it can judge, such as which enquiries were a good fit and which became instructions. We use that to adjust pages, keywords and budgets.

Insight content that builds authority

Many clients read several articles before they contact a lawyer. Clear, accurate insight pages show expertise and give search engines more reasons to rank your site. We plan topics around real questions, such as how a process works, what documents are needed or what changed in a recent rule, then your lawyers write or review each piece. We handle structure, headings, internal links and publishing, so each article supports a practice area page. Content must stay general and not read as advice for a specific case, and your firm decides what is published.

Choosing the right first step

Not every firm needs every channel. If your website is outdated or hard to use, we usually start there, because every ad and search result leads back to it. If the site is sound, we often begin with practice area pages for your most valuable work, a cleaned-up Google Business Profile and a small Google Search campaign. LinkedIn Ads usually follow once the corporate pages are strong. We agree priorities on the first call, based on your budget, practice areas and capacity to take on new matters.
