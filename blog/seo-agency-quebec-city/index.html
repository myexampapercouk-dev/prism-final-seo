French-first keyword research

The biggest mistake we see on bilingual sites is translating English keywords word for word into French. Quebec searchers use their own vocabulary, and the terms with real demand are often not the literal translation. Good keyword research looks at actual French search data and the phrasing competitors already rank for.

Our process is collaborative. We run research in both languages using search tools and live results, then review the French term lists with your team or translator, who confirm which wording sounds natural to local customers. That combination of data and native judgement gives a much stronger base than translation alone.

Technical SEO for bilingual websites

A bilingual website has to tell search engines clearly which version of each page is for which language. Without that, Google may show English pages to French searchers or treat the two versions as duplicates. Our technical work includes:

  • A clean URL structure for each language, such as separate folders.
  • Hreflang tags that link each French page to its English equivalent.
  • Translated title tags, meta descriptions and image alt text, supplied by your translator.
  • Structured data for your business, services and FAQs. See our schema markup guide for how it works.
  • Fast page speed, mobile usability and clean crawling and indexing.

Our guide to multilingual website design explains how we structure sites with more than one language. If your website needs rebuilding first, our guide to web design in Quebec City covers that process.

Content that ranks and gets cited

Search engines and AI assistants favour content that answers questions clearly and specifically. We plan content around customer questions, structure it with clear headings and short answers, and add FAQs where they genuinely help. For bilingual sites, we typically write the English version and a content brief, and your translator or team adapts it into natural Quebec French rather than translating line by line.

We also use AI-powered tools to speed up research, outlines and first drafts in English. Every piece is edited by a person, and French versions always come from your team or a professional translator.

Language rules, privacy and consent

Quebec has its own rules on the use of French in business, including commercial websites, under the Charter of the French Language and its more recent amendments. These rules can affect which content must be available in French and how prominently. We keep our notes general and are not legal advisers, so please check official guidance or ask your own adviser about your obligations.

Privacy also matters. Quebec's Law 25 has introduced privacy requirements for businesses in the province, alongside PIPEDA, the federal private sector privacy law that applies in many situations. Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, known as CASL, covers commercial emails and texts. We set up forms, analytics and consent tools to support good practice, but responsibility for compliance rests with you.

FAQs

How do I choose an SEO agency in Quebec City?

Look for an agency that understands French-first search behaviour, local SEO and bilingual technical set-up, and that is clear about who writes your French content. Ask how they report on results by language and page.

Do you write French content?

No. We write in English. French content is created by your team or a professional translator, and we provide the strategy, briefs and technical set-up.

Do you have an office in Quebec City?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely, with calls scheduled around Eastern Time. We have no office in Canada.

How long does SEO take in Quebec City?

SEO results typically take three to six months to build, depending on competition, your website's current state and how quickly content is published in both languages.

Does my website need to be in French?

Quebec has rules on the use of French in commerce that can apply to websites. Please check official guidance or ask your own adviser. For SEO, a strong French version is usually essential to reach local customers.

What are hreflang tags?

Hreflang tags tell search engines which language each page is written in and link equivalent pages together, so French searchers see the French page and English searchers see the English one.
