TikTok Ads Agency in Vancouver: Campaigns That Turn Views Into Sales

A TikTok Ads agency for Vancouver brands: audience targeting, Spark Ads, the TikTok Pixel and budgets, managed remotely by a UK-based studio with clear reports.

Key takeaways

  • TikTok Ads suit Vancouver brands selling to younger and mid-age consumers, especially in food, fitness, fashion, beauty, outdoor gear and local experiences.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no office in Vancouver or Canada; retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 per month, ad spend is paid separately and CAD pricing is quoted on request.
  • We plan, launch and optimise campaigns, but we do not film or edit video; footage comes from your team or creators you work with.

A TikTok Ads agency in Vancouver helps local brands use TikTok's paid placements to reach people in the Lower Mainland and across Canada, then turn that attention into visits, sign-ups and sales. The work is mostly about targeting, testing and measurement: choosing the right audience, running several versions of each ad, and tracking what happens after someone taps through.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We have no office in Vancouver or anywhere in Canada. We manage campaigns through TikTok Ads Manager, share live dashboards and send written monthly reports, with calls scheduled to suit Pacific Time.

One thing to be clear about from the start: we do not film or edit video. The footage for your ads comes from your own team, your customers or creators you work with. Our job is the campaign around it: the strategy, briefs, setup, targeting, tracking and optimisation.

Why TikTok works for Vancouver brands

Vancouver has a strong consumer brand scene. Outdoor and activewear labels, specialty coffee roasters, bakeries, craft breweries, skincare and wellness brands, restaurants, fitness studios and tour operators all compete for attention in a city where people spend a lot of time on their phones and care about lifestyle. TikTok's feed rewards content that feels native and personal, which suits brands with a story to tell.

TikTok is also useful because it is less crowded with advertisers than Meta in many categories. A smaller brand with honest, well-briefed content can still reach a large local audience without the budget of a national chain.

What a TikTok Ads agency in Vancouver does

Our TikTok work is part of our paid media service. For Vancouver clients it usually includes:

  • Account and tracking setup: TikTok Business Center, Ads Manager, the TikTok Pixel and, where possible, the Events API so conversions are measured reliably.
  • Creative briefs: written briefs for your team or creators covering the hook, the message, the call to action and the format, so the footage you supply is ready for testing.
  • Campaign structure: choosing objectives, audiences, placements and budgets.
  • Spark Ads: boosting organic posts from your account or from creators who give permission, so ads look and feel like normal TikTok content.
  • Testing and optimisation: rotating creative, adjusting bids and budgets, and pausing what does not work.
  • Reporting: clear monthly reports on spend, results and next steps.

Targeting options for the Lower Mainland

TikTok targeting is broader than LinkedIn or Google, and the algorithm does much of the work once it knows who converts. Still, the setup matters. Here is how we usually think about it for a Vancouver brand:

Targeting choiceTypical Vancouver useNotes
LocationMetro Vancouver, British Columbia or all of CanadaLocal services stay tight; online shops can go wider
Age and genderMatched to your real customer baseAvoid narrowing too much at the start
Interests and behavioursOutdoor activities, food and drink, beauty, fitnessUseful early on, less needed once the Pixel has data
Custom audiencesWebsite visitors, video viewers, customer listsStrong for retargeting and exclusions
Lookalike audiencesPeople similar to past buyersHelps scale once you have enough conversions
Broad targetingFew restrictions, letting the algorithm find buyersOften works well with strong creative and good tracking

For restaurants, studios and venues, a tight radius around the business usually makes sense. For an online shop shipping across Canada, we often start broader and let results guide the next step.

Creative that works on TikTok

Ads that look like adverts tend to get skipped. The ones that perform usually feel like ordinary TikTok posts: a person talking to camera, a product being used, a quick before-and-after, a behind-the-scenes clip or a reply to a common question. The first two seconds matter most.

Because we do not film, we focus on making your footage work harder. We write briefs, suggest hooks, plan which variations to test and tell you which clips are worth making more of. Many Vancouver brands already have a team member who is comfortable on camera, or work with local creators. If you work with creators, make sure your agreements cover paid use of their content.

We write in English. If you want ads in French, Punjabi, Cantonese, Mandarin or another language spoken in Metro Vancouver, that copy comes from your team or a translator, and we run those versions alongside your English ads.

Where TikTok traffic should land

Most TikTok users are on a phone, often with limited patience. If your ad sends them to a slow page or a confusing checkout, the click is wasted. Before scaling spend, we check that the landing page loads quickly on mobile, matches the ad and has one clear next step.

If your website needs work first, our web design in Vancouver article explains what a good mobile landing page needs. Ecommerce brands can also read our ecommerce marketing page for how we plan product pages and checkouts.

Budgets and costs

TikTok has minimum budgets at campaign and ad group level, and campaigns need enough conversions to learn. A very small daily budget split across many ad groups usually stalls. We would rather run fewer ad groups with a meaningful budget and clear tests.

Our management fees follow our standard pricing:

  • Launch, £350 a month: one TikTok campaign with core tracking and monthly reporting.
  • Growth, £750 a month: ongoing creative testing, retargeting and often another channel alongside TikTok.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: multi-channel paid media with deeper testing and reporting.

Ad spend is paid directly to TikTok and is separate from our fee. Our prices are in pounds; Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request. Results from paid social typically take three to six months to settle as creative and audiences are refined.

Privacy, consent and advertising rules

Tracking pixels, customer lists and lead forms all involve personal information. In Canada, federal privacy law applies to many businesses, and British Columbia has its own private sector privacy law. Canada's anti-spam legislation also applies if you follow up leads by email. In general terms, be transparent about tracking, get proper consent for marketing messages and make opting out easy.

Some product categories, such as alcohol, cannabis, health products and financial services, have extra advertising restrictions both in law and in TikTok's own policies. We keep notes general and you remain responsible for compliance, so please check official guidance or speak to your adviser before launching in a regulated category.

Common TikTok Ads mistakes

  • Reusing polished TV-style or website videos that look out of place in the feed.
  • Running the same few ads for months until the audience stops responding.
  • Launching without the Pixel firing correctly, so the algorithm cannot learn who buys.
  • Splitting a small budget across too many ad groups.
  • Judging results after a few days instead of giving campaigns time to learn.
  • Ignoring comments on ads, where questions and objections often appear first.

Most of these are easy to avoid with a clear plan and a steady supply of fresh footage. We give you a simple monthly list of what to film next, based on what the data shows, so your team knows exactly where to spend its time.
