Why Zagreb businesses use a Google Ads agency

Zagreb is Croatia's capital and its main business centre. Its economy covers IT and software development, pharmaceuticals, food production, logistics, finance, professional services and retail, while tourism has grown steadily alongside the much larger coastal market. Many Zagreb companies sell nationally, and a growing number sell across the EU.

Google Ads can do very different jobs for these businesses. A software firm might use it to reach a small number of high-value buyers. An online shop might use Shopping campaigns to drive daily orders across Croatia and neighbouring countries. A clinic or service business might want a steady stream of calls from people nearby. In each case, results depend on clear goals, reliable tracking and steady improvement.

Tracking comes before spend

The most common problem we find in existing accounts is weak or broken conversion tracking. When Google cannot see which clicks led to sales or enquiries, automated bidding has nothing reliable to learn from and the budget drifts towards cheap clicks that never convert.

Before we scale any campaign, we check tracking for the actions that matter: purchases with correct values, form submissions, phone calls and important steps such as booking requests. We also make sure consent mode is set up properly, because Croatia follows European privacy rules and visitors must be given a real choice about cookies. For how the rules apply to your business, check official guidance or ask your own adviser.

Building campaigns for Croatian searches

Croatian is a highly inflected language, so the same product or service can appear in many different word forms. Searchers also mix Croatian and English terms, particularly in technology, software and some product categories. We research both, study the real search terms in your account and build keyword lists that reflect how people actually search.

We usually separate campaigns by language and by intent. Brand searches, high-intent product or service searches and broader research searches each get their own budget and messages. A thorough list of negative keywords stops spend leaking to job seekers, free downloads and unrelated searches.

For businesses targeting neighbouring markets such as Slovenia, Austria or Serbia, we set up separate campaigns for each country with their own language, budget and landing pages. Our guides to Google Ads in Vienna and Google Ads in Prague show how we approach other central European markets.

Google Ads for Zagreb online shops

For e-commerce brands, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns usually drive most of the results, and their performance depends on the product feed. We improve titles, descriptions, categories and attributes so Google can match your products to the right searches. We then group products by margin and performance, so the best sellers get the budget they deserve and weak products stop draining spend.

The shop itself matters just as much. Slow product pages, unclear delivery costs and long checkouts waste paid traffic. If you sell across borders, our guide to Shopify Markets for Europe explains how to handle languages and local payment options, and our e-commerce marketing page shows how we combine ads with store improvements.

Google Ads for tourism and hospitality

Zagreb attracts city-break visitors, business travellers and events traffic, especially around its winter festivities, while many Croatian hotels, apartments and tour operators also target summer visitors to the coast. Travellers search in many languages, often months ahead, and booking platforms compete for the same clicks.

For hotels, apartments and tour operators, we focus on brand protection, specific long-tail searches and campaigns timed around booking windows in each source market. We send clicks to pages with clear availability and a simple booking path. Our hospitality marketing page and our guide to hotel marketing in Greece explain how we approach seasonal tourism markets.

Landing pages that convert paid clicks

Sending ads to a general homepage rarely works well. Each campaign should lead to a page that matches the search, explains the offer clearly and makes the next step obvious. A visitor who searched for a specific service should land on a page about that service, with prices or a clear way to get a quote, answers to common questions and a short form or a tap-to-call button on mobile.

We design and build landing pages as part of our web design work, and we can also improve pages on your existing site. Small changes often make a large difference to the cost of each lead: a clearer headline, fewer form fields, visible delivery or booking information, and pages that load quickly on a phone. Where your site has both Croatian and English versions, each campaign points to the page in the matching language.

We also test. Once a campaign has enough traffic, we compare headlines, offers and page layouts, keep what works and drop what does not. Over time this steady testing lowers costs and makes each euro of ad spend go further.

Common mistakes we fix in Zagreb accounts

  • Broad keywords with no negatives. Budget goes on searches for jobs, free tools and unrelated products.
  • All conversions valued equally. A newsletter sign-up should not count the same as a booking or a large quote request.
  • One campaign for every language. Mixing Croatian and English searches hides what is working.
  • Auto-applied recommendations left on. Some raise spend without improving results, so we review each one.
  • Ads pointing at the homepage. Each campaign should land on a page that matches the search.
