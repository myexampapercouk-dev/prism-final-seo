What Shopify experts in Canada actually do

The phrase "Shopify expert" covers a wide range of work. Some people use it for theme designers, some for app developers and some for marketers who only run ads to Shopify stores. For most Canadian merchants, the useful definition is simpler: a team that understands the whole store, from the theme and checkout settings to search visibility and the campaigns that bring buyers in.

In practice, Shopify experts usually help with:

  • New store builds, from choosing a theme to designing key templates and loading products.
  • Redesigns for stores that look dated, convert poorly or no longer match the brand.
  • Migrations from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or older platforms.
  • Theme customisation, such as new sections, product page layouts and collection filters.
  • Speed and app clean-ups, removing scripts that slow the store down.
  • SEO, so collections and products can be found in Google.
  • Paid media, such as Google Shopping, Meta, TikTok and YouTube Ads.

Shopify experts Canada: what to look for

When you search for Shopify experts in Canada, you will find freelancers, local agencies and remote studios. Each can work well. The right choice depends on what you need done and how you like to work. These are the questions worth asking before you sign anything.

  • Do they understand Canadian commerce? GST, HST and provincial sales taxes, bilingual needs, and shipping across a very large country all affect how a store should be set up.
  • Can they show their process? Ask how they plan, design, build, test and hand over a store.
  • Do they think beyond launch? A store that is beautiful but invisible in search, or never tested with real traffic, rarely pays back.
  • Are prices and scope written down? A clear scope protects both sides.
  • Who owns the store? Your Shopify account, domain, theme and data should always belong to you.

How Canadian stores differ from UK and US stores

A Canadian Shopify store is not just a US store with a maple leaf on the logo. A few practical differences shape the build.

Sales tax settings

Canada uses a mix of the federal GST, harmonised HST in some provinces and separate provincial sales taxes in others. Shopify has tax settings for Canadian regions, but you still need to confirm your registrations and obligations with an accountant or the Canada Revenue Agency. We set up the store according to the decisions you and your adviser make; we do not give tax advice.

Shipping across long distances

Shipping from Ontario to British Columbia or the territories can cost far more than shipping within one city. Clear shipping zones, honest delivery estimates and free shipping thresholds that make sense for your margins all matter. Carriers such as Canada Post, Purolator and UPS are common choices, and Shopify supports rate calculation for several of them.

English and French

Many brands selling across Canada want French pages for Quebec shoppers. Shopify can run more than one language on the same store. We build and configure the multilingual setup, while the French copy comes from your team or a translator. For Quebec-specific language rules, check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

Privacy and email consent

Canadian privacy law and Canada's anti-spam legislation affect how you collect emails and run marketing. We build consent-friendly sign-up forms and cookie banners, but compliance decisions rest with you and your advisers.

Common mistakes we see on Canadian Shopify stores

  • Prices shown in US dollars to Canadian visitors, or currency switching that confuses shoppers.
  • Shipping rates that look fine in the city but are unrealistic for remote provinces.
  • French pages created with machine translation and never reviewed.
  • Too many apps installed during launch and never removed.
  • No tracking set up for purchases, so ad platforms cannot learn.
  • Collection pages with no text and duplicate tag pages competing in Google.

Most of these can be fixed without a full rebuild. A focused review is often the best first step. Our guide to Shopify conversion rate optimisation lists the checks we use.
