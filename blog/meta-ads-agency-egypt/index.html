Why Egyptian businesses use a Meta Ads agency

Egypt is the most populous country in the Arab world, with a young, mobile-first audience that spends a lot of time on Facebook and Instagram. For many Egyptian shoppers, these platforms are where they discover brands, compare products and send a message before buying. That makes Meta Ads one of the most important paid channels for businesses in Cairo, Giza, Alexandria and beyond.

Businesses using Meta Ads in Egypt include fashion and beauty brands, online shops, restaurants and cafes, real estate developers, clinics, training providers, education businesses and service companies. Some sell only within Egypt. Others, such as exporters or brands serving Egyptians abroad, target audiences across the Gulf and further afield.

The catch is that it is easy to spend on Meta without getting much back. Boosting posts, broad targeting and untracked results can burn through budget quickly. A good agency brings structure: clear goals, organised campaigns, steady creative testing and honest reporting.

Getting targeting right in Egypt

Targeting choices shape both cost and quality. Egypt is a large market, and running ads nationwide without thought can bring clicks from people you cannot serve.

Location and delivery area

If you deliver only in Greater Cairo, there is little point paying to reach people in Upper Egypt. If you have a branch in Alexandria, you may want separate campaigns with local offers. We set locations to match where you can actually sell and deliver, and review results by region.

Language and creative

Egyptian audiences respond to content that feels local. Many brands use Egyptian Arabic in ads, others use Modern Standard Arabic or English for certain products or audiences. We plan the messaging and structure in English, and your team or translator writes the Arabic copy. We then test versions to see which language and tone perform best.

Audiences and lookalikes

Once tracking is in place, your own data becomes the most valuable targeting asset. We build custom audiences from website visitors, engaged followers and, where you have permission, customer lists, then create lookalikes to find similar people. Use of customer data must follow your privacy obligations and Meta's terms, and those decisions rest with your business and your own adviser.

Landing pages, shops and chat

Where you send people matters as much as the ad itself. For ecommerce, a fast product page with clear prices, delivery details and cash on delivery information, if you offer it, usually works best. For lead generation, a short focused page or Meta lead form can suit. For chat-based selling, the team answering WhatsApp needs to respond quickly, or the ad spend is wasted.

Common Meta Ads mistakes we fix

When we audit Egyptian ad accounts, the same problems come up again and again. Fixing them is often the quickest way to get more from the same budget.

  • Relying on boosted posts: boosting is simple, but it offers far less control over goals, placements and audiences than a proper campaign.
  • No tracking: without a working pixel and conversion events, Meta cannot optimise for sales or leads, and you cannot see what works.
  • Too many small ad sets: splitting a modest budget into many tiny audiences slows learning and raises costs.
  • Stale creative: running the same ad for months leads to fatigue, so results slowly decline.
  • Judging only on cheap clicks or messages: low-cost results mean little if they never turn into orders, so we look at real sales and qualified leads.

In our experience, most of these can be fixed within the first few weeks, which gives a much cleaner, more reliable base for testing and scaling.
