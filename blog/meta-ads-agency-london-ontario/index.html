Who uses Meta Ads in London, Ontario

London has a broad local economy. There are healthcare and education employers, a large student population, growing residential neighbourhoods, independent retailers, restaurants, fitness studios, home services firms and many professional practices. Most of these businesses serve people within the city and the surrounding area, which makes local targeting on Facebook and Instagram a natural fit.

Meta Ads work particularly well for businesses where people are not actively searching yet. A new restaurant, a fitness class, a home renovation offer, a spring landscaping service or an ecommerce brand can all reach people before they think to search. Search ads then capture people once they are ready to act.

Start with tracking

Meta's ad system learns from the actions people take after clicking. If tracking is missing or wrong, it learns from the wrong signals. We set up the Meta pixel and Conversions API so that leads, bookings and purchases are recorded accurately, even with browser privacy limits.

We also connect results to what actually matters to your business. A form submission is not the same as a booked job or a paying customer. Where possible, we feed back better quality signals so campaigns can focus on people more likely to become customers.

Creative that stops the scroll

On Facebook and Instagram, creative does most of the work. A clear image or short clip, a strong first line and an obvious next step usually beat polished but vague brand messaging. We plan and write ad copy and work with your images and footage to build ad variations for testing.

We do not film or edit video, so any video content needs to come from your own team or a provider you choose. Simple phone footage often works well on Instagram and Facebook, as long as it is clear and genuine.

We use AI-powered tools to speed up research, generate copy ideas and spot patterns in results, with every output checked by a person before it goes live. Our AI marketing agency Canada guide explains how that works in practice.

Targeting for a local market

For local businesses, targeting usually starts with a radius around your location or service area. London's neighbourhoods and nearby communities, such as St. Thomas or Strathroy, may also be worth including depending on how far customers travel.

Modern Meta campaigns often perform best with broader targeting and strong creative, letting the system find the right people. Retargeting people who visited your website or engaged with your page is usually one of the most efficient parts of a campaign. Some categories, such as housing, employment and credit, have special rules that limit targeting, so campaigns need to follow those from the start.

Landing pages and your website

Sending ad traffic to a slow or cluttered home page wastes budget. A focused landing page that matches the ad, loads quickly on phones and makes the next step obvious will almost always convert better. It should repeat the offer, show trust signals and keep forms short.

Meta Ads, Google Ads and SEO together

Meta Ads create demand, while search captures it. Many people see a Facebook or Instagram ad, then search for the business later. If you do not appear in search when they look, you may lose them to a competitor.

That is why we often recommend combining channels. Our Google Ads agency London Ontario guide covers paid search in the city, and our SEO agency London Ontario guide covers organic search.

Budgets and what to expect

There is no single right budget. It depends on your goals, your margins, how competitive your sector is and how many locations you serve. A small local business can learn a lot from a modest monthly spend, while ecommerce brands or multi-location firms usually need more to test properly. What matters is giving each test enough budget and time to produce a clear answer.

In the first month, we focus on setting up tracking, launching a small number of campaigns and gathering data. Over the following months, we cut what does not work, scale what does and keep introducing new creative, because ad fatigue sets in quickly when the same people see the same ads. Seasonal moments, such as the back-to-school period around the universities and colleges or the holiday shopping season, can also shape when to spend more.

Privacy and advertising rules

Canadian privacy law applies to personal information you collect through ads and forms, and Canada's anti-spam legislation sets rules on sending marketing emails to leads. Meta also has its own advertising policies, which can restrict some products, services and claims. We build campaigns with these in mind, but please check official guidance or your own adviser about what applies to your business. In regulated sectors, compliance approval rests with you.

How to choose a Meta Ads agency

  • Ask how they set up tracking and what they measure.
  • Ask how often they test new creative and how they decide what wins.
  • Make sure you own your ad account, pixel and data.
  • Check that reports focus on leads and sales, not just clicks and reach.
  • Avoid long lock-in contracts without clear goals.

FAQs

How do I choose a Meta Ads agency in London, Ontario?

Look for an agency that sets up accurate tracking, tests creative regularly, gives you ownership of your ad account and reports on leads and sales. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely, with no office in London, Ontario.

How quickly do Meta Ads work?

Campaigns can bring enquiries within weeks, but steady results usually take three to six months of testing creative, audiences and offers.

Is boosting posts the same as running Meta Ads?
