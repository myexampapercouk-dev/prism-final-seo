Why Google Ads suits Coventry businesses

Coventry has changed a great deal in recent years. Two large universities bring a big student population, the city has a growing reputation in automotive engineering, battery technology and logistics, and new residential development continues across the city and towards Warwickshire. Local businesses also serve nearby towns such as Kenilworth, Nuneaton, Bedworth and Rugby.

Google Ads helps in a market like this because it lets you choose exactly which searches, places and times you pay for. A letting agent can target student accommodation searches before term begins. A garage can target drivers searching for an MOT in specific postcodes. An engineering firm can reach buyers across the UK searching for specialist services. And unlike SEO, campaigns can bring traffic within days of launch.

Getting tracking right first

Google's automated bidding learns from the conversions you tell it about. If tracking counts the wrong actions, such as page views or clicks on a phone number that never connect, the system will optimise toward the wrong outcome. Before spending on growth, we make sure your conversions are real and measured accurately. Where suitable, we also set up enhanced conversions, which our enhanced conversions guide explains.

We connect Google Ads with GA4 so you can see what happens after the click, and we agree with you which conversions matter most to your business.

Cutting wasted spend

Most accounts we review are wasting part of their budget. The common causes are:

  • Broad keywords matching to irrelevant searches.
  • Few or no negative keywords.
  • Location settings that include people outside your service area.
  • Ads running overnight when no one can answer the phone.
  • Search Partners or Display placements switched on without review.
  • Landing pages that do not match the ad.

We tackle these early. Our negative keywords guide explains one of the simplest and most effective fixes.

Quality Score and landing pages

Google rewards relevant ads and useful landing pages with better ad positions at lower cost. We improve Quality Score by keeping ad groups tightly themed, writing ads that reflect the search and making sure landing pages answer the visitor's question clearly. Our Quality Score guide covers this in more depth.

If your website is slow or your pages are unclear, we will tell you. We can build new landing pages or a full website when needed, and our web design Coventry article explains that process. A small website typically costs £1,000 to £2,000 and takes 3 to 5 weeks.

What a good first month looks like

The first month of any new account sets the foundations for everything that follows, so we keep it structured. In week one we review your goals, your current account if you have one, your website and your competitors' ads. We confirm which actions count as conversions and check that they fire correctly. By the end of the first fortnight we aim to have tracking verified, the campaign structure agreed and the first campaigns live with conservative budgets.

Weeks three and four are about learning. We read the search terms report closely, add negative keywords, pause poor performers and adjust bids by location and time of day. We also check that calls and forms are reaching the right person in your team, because a lead that is not followed up quickly is often lost. At the end of the month you receive a short report covering spend, conversions, cost per conversion, what we changed and what we plan next.

Seasonality in the Coventry market

Demand in Coventry shifts across the year, and campaigns should shift with it. Student-related searches rise sharply in the months before each academic year and again before the summer as students look for next year's housing. Home improvement searches usually pick up in spring. Retail and gifting searches build through the autumn towards Christmas. Heating and boiler services see more urgent searches when the weather turns cold.

We plan budgets around these patterns so that money is available when demand is high and not wasted when it is low. For businesses with strong seasonal peaks, we often prepare ads and landing pages in advance so campaigns can be switched on quickly at the right moment.

Signs your current account needs help

If you already run Google Ads, a few warning signs suggest it is time for a review. Your cost per lead may be rising without explanation. You may be getting clicks but few enquiries. The search terms report may be full of irrelevant phrases, or you may not know what it contains at all. Reports may show impressive conversion numbers that do not match the enquiries your team actually receives. Any of these is a good reason to ask for an audit before you increase your budget.
