Hiring a TikTok Ads Agency in Canada: What Good Campaign Management Involves

Looking for a TikTok Ads agency in Canada? How remote management works, what a sound setup covers, how fees and ad spend split and what to ask before you hire.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good TikTok Ads agency starts with tracking, creative and audience planning, and only then scales budget, because the platform rewards native-looking video over polished adverts.
  • Management fees and ad spend are separate: the agency fee pays for strategy, creative direction and optimisation, while the media budget is paid to TikTok.
  • Paid social results typically take three to six months to settle, so agree tests, reporting and a review point before committing to a long engagement.

A TikTok Ads agency for a Canadian business should set up accurate tracking, plan short native-style video creative, test audiences and offers in a structured way, and report on sales or leads rather than views. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages TikTok Ads remotely for Canadian clients; we have no office or staff in Canada, and we work through video calls, shared documents and your own ad accounts.

This guide covers what TikTok Ads can and cannot do for a Canadian brand, how a remote agency relationship works, how fees and ad spend are structured, and the questions to ask before you hand over an ad account.

Is TikTok advertising right for a Canadian business?

TikTok works best when your product or service can be shown, demonstrated or explained in a short, lively video. It tends to suit:

  • Ecommerce brands in beauty, fashion, food and drink, homeware, pets, fitness and gifts.
  • Apps and subscription products that can show value in seconds.
  • Local and experience businesses such as restaurants, attractions and studios looking to reach younger audiences in cities like Toronto, Montreal or Vancouver.
  • Employers who want to reach younger candidates with a behind-the-scenes view of the business.

It is usually a weaker fit for slow, considered B2B purchases or highly regulated products, where search and LinkedIn often do more. Our comparison of TikTok Ads and Meta Ads sets out the trade-offs, and if you already advertise on Facebook and Instagram, our guide for a Meta Ads agency in Canada explains how the two channels can work together.

The platform's position in Canada has also attracted government attention, and the rules around how TikTok operates in the country can change. Check the current position and the platform's own advertiser terms before committing a large budget, and keep other channels in your plan so you are not dependent on one.

Creative: the part that decides most results

On TikTok, the creative is the targeting. The algorithm finds people based on how they respond to each video, so the quality and variety of your clips matters more than clever audience settings. In practice that means:

  • Opening with a clear hook in the first second or two.
  • Filming vertically, with real people, natural speech and captions.
  • Showing the product in use, not just on a white background.
  • Making several variations of each idea, so the platform has options to test.
  • Refreshing ads before performance fades, rather than after.

We write creative briefs and scripts and advise on editing, and many clients film the footage themselves or with creators they already know. We do not invent customer reviews or results in ads; any claims need to be true and supportable, and anything regulated should be approved by your own compliance or legal adviser.

Where the clicks go matters too

A strong TikTok ad sent to a slow or confusing page wastes money. Visitors arrive on a phone, often with low attention, so the landing page needs to load fast, repeat the promise of the ad and make the next step obvious. Our guide to landing page design covers the essentials. For Shopify stores, checkout speed and clear delivery information for Canadian customers make a noticeable difference; our guide to Shopify for Canadian brands covers the store side.

How remote TikTok management works from the UK

The UK is between four and eight hours ahead of most of Canada, depending on the province. Campaign management suits that gap well: optimisation, creative planning and reporting happen during the UK day, and calls are booked for your morning.

  • Your accounts, your data. The TikTok Ads account, pixel and business centre stay in your business's name. We are added as partners or users.
  • Shared plans. Test plans, creative briefs and reports live in shared documents you can open any time.
  • Regular check-ins. A short call each month, or more often during launches and seasonal peaks.

Fees and ad spend

Two costs are involved. The ad spend goes to TikTok and is paid separately. The management fee pays for strategy, setup, creative direction, optimisation and reporting. Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, depending on the number of channels, campaigns and creative volume. We quote in other currencies on request, and the details are on our pricing page.

When comparing agencies, check whether the fee is flat or a percentage of spend, whether creative production is included, and how long the minimum term is. Results from paid social typically take three to six months to settle, because creative and audience testing takes time.

Common TikTok Ads mistakes

Most underperforming TikTok accounts we review share a few patterns:

  • Judging too early. Turning campaigns off after a few days, before the algorithm has had enough data to find the right people.
  • Too few creatives. Running one or two videos and expecting them to carry a whole campaign for months.
  • Ignoring the comments. Questions and objections under ads are useful research, and unanswered ones put buyers off.
  • Wrong optimisation event. Optimising for clicks or video views when the goal is purchases or leads.
  • Mixed geographies. Combining Canada with other countries in one campaign, which makes results hard to read and budgets hard to control.

What to prepare before you start

To get moving quickly, have your TikTok business centre and ads account created in your company's name, access to your website or store so tracking can be installed, a clear view of your margins and target cost per sale or lead, and any existing video footage, even rough clips from a phone. A short list of your best-selling products or most profitable services also helps us decide where to test first.

TikTok alongside other channels

TikTok is often strongest at the top of the funnel: introducing a brand to people who did not know it. Many of those people will later search for you or see a retargeting ad elsewhere before they buy, so judge TikTok on blended results as well as last-click numbers. If video is central to your plans, our comparison of YouTube Ads and TikTok Ads helps decide where to start. Our paid media service covers TikTok, Meta, Google, YouTube and LinkedIn, so channels can be planned together rather than in silos.

FAQs

How do I choose a TikTok Ads agency in Canada?

Look for an agency that sets up tracking before spending, has a clear approach to creative testing, keeps the ad account in your name and reports on sales or leads. Ask to see how they would structure your first month.

Does Prism Works Studio have an office in Canada?

No. We are a UK-based studio working with Canadian clients remotely, using video calls, shared documents and access to your own ad accounts.

How much should I spend on TikTok Ads?

It depends on your margins, product price and goals. The budget needs to be large enough for each test to gather meaningful data. We agree a starting budget with you during planning, and ad spend is always paid separately from our fee.

Do you make the TikTok videos?

We write creative briefs and scripts and advise on editing. Many clients film footage themselves or with creators they work with, which often suits the platform's native style.

Can you run TikTok Ads in French for Quebec?

We write in English. French ad copy and scripts are written with your team or a professional translator, and we can set up separate campaigns so English and French audiences receive the right versions.

How long do TikTok Ads take to work?

Early signals can appear within weeks, but results typically take three to six months to settle as creative, audiences and offers are tested and refined.
