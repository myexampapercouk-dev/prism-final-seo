Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

When we audit accounts for local businesses, the same problems come up again and again. Most of them waste budget quietly, month after month:

  • Broad match keywords with no negatives. Ads show for loosely related searches, including job seekers, students and people looking for free advice or DIY instructions.
  • Location settings left on the default. Google's default can include people who have merely shown interest in an area, not just people located there. For a local service business, that often means clicks from far outside Brampton.
  • No conversion tracking, or the wrong conversions. Counting page views or button clicks as conversions makes campaigns look better than they are and trains automated bidding on the wrong signals.
  • Every ad pointing to the home page. A searcher looking for a specific service should land on a page about that service with a clear way to call or enquire.
  • Automated bidding switched on too early. Smart bidding needs reliable conversion data. Without it, the system has little to learn from.
  • Ads running when nobody can answer the phone. For call-led businesses, ad scheduling should match the hours someone is available to take enquiries.
  • Search terms never reviewed. The search terms report shows what people actually typed. Checking it weekly is one of the simplest ways to cut waste.

Fixing these basics often improves results more than raising the budget. If you are currently running ads, we can review your account first and show you what we would change before you commit to a retainer.
