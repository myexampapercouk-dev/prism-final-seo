How we build Shopify stores

We aim for stores that are simple to run and quick to load. Our build approach:

  • A well-supported theme, customised to your brand rather than rebuilt from scratch, so updates stay manageable.
  • Clean product data, with consistent titles, variants, descriptions and specification fields.
  • Collections that match how people shop, by category, use or need.
  • Few apps, each chosen for a clear job, because every app can slow the store.
  • Shipping and pickup settings that reflect your real delivery zones and local pickup options.
  • Tax settings confirmed with your accountant, since sales tax varies by province.

You review everything on a preview before launch, and we give your team training on adding products, editing pages and managing orders. For a wider view of Shopify in Canada, read our guide to Shopify agencies in Canada, and for the nearby city, see Shopify in Toronto.

Migrating to Shopify

Many Mississauga businesses move to Shopify from WooCommerce, an older custom platform or a basic website builder. A careful migration protects search rankings and customer trust. We:

  1. List every important URL on the old site.
  2. Move products, collections and customer records where the platforms allow.
  3. Map redirects from old URLs to new ones.
  4. Check titles, descriptions and images after the move.
  5. Monitor search console and fix issues after launch.

Larger stores may consider Shopify Plus for features such as advanced checkout customisation and automation. We can scope a Plus migration if your volumes justify it. If you are comparing platforms, our guide to WordPress in Toronto explains when WooCommerce may still fit.

Shopify SEO

Shopify handles many technical basics, but stores still need work to rank. Common tasks include fixing duplicate product URLs, writing unique collection descriptions, improving product titles, adding structured data and building helpful guides that answer buyer questions. SEO results typically take three to six months to build.

Our guide to e-commerce SEO in Canada goes deeper, and our article on SEO in Mississauga covers local search for businesses with a physical location.

Ads for Shopify stores

Paid campaigns help new stores get early traffic and help established stores scale. We set up Google Shopping and search campaigns using a clean product feed, plus Meta and Instagram campaigns for discovery and remarketing. TikTok and YouTube Ads can work for some products, using clips your team supplies; we do not film or edit video.

Good tracking comes first. We connect Shopify to your ad platforms using supported integrations and check that purchases are recorded correctly. See our guides to Google Ads in Mississauga and Meta Ads in Canada.

Marketplaces alongside Shopify

Many Mississauga brands also sell on Amazon. We offer marketplace listing SEO to improve titles, bullet points, images guidance and backend search terms. Running Shopify and Amazon side by side lets you reach marketplace shoppers while building your own customer list. Our guide to Amazon SEO in Canada explains this approach.

Privacy, consent and email

Online stores collect personal information, so privacy matters. In general terms, PIPEDA sets federal rules for how private-sector organisations handle personal information. CASL covers marketing emails and texts and generally requires consent and an easy unsubscribe. If you sell to customers in Quebec, Law 25 may also apply, and other provinces have their own rules in some areas.

These notes are general information only. Please check official guidance and speak to your own adviser. We set up consent checkboxes, cookie banners and privacy page layouts using wording you approve, and final responsibility rests with you.

Using AI-powered tools

We use AI-powered tools to help clean product data, draft product description variations for your review, group search terms and summarise store performance. A person checks every output, and you approve product claims, especially for food, health and beauty items.

Shipping, pickup and conversion basics

Small details at checkout often decide whether a visitor buys. Shoppers want to know delivery costs and times before they reach the last step. Many Mississauga customers also like local pickup or same-day options when a warehouse is close by. We make delivery information clear on product pages and in the cart, set up pickup where you offer it and keep checkout as short as Shopify allows.

Other conversion basics we check on every store:

  • Product images that show size, detail and use, supplied or approved by your team.
  • Clear returns and exchange information linked from product pages.
  • Trust signals such as secure payment logos and contact details that are easy to find.
  • Fast mobile loading, since most store visits come from phones.
  • Search and filters that work well for larger catalogues.

Wholesale and trade orders

Many Mississauga businesses sell to trade customers as well as the public. Shopify can support wholesale pricing, customer-specific catalogues and order minimums, either through built-in features on some plans or through well-supported apps. We scope this carefully, because trade features can add complexity. Where your volumes justify it, Shopify Plus offers more native options. We will explain the trade-offs before you commit.

Email and repeat customers

Repeat buyers are often the most valuable customers a store has. We set up simple email flows in Shopify or a supported email tool, such as welcome messages, abandoned cart reminders and post-purchase follow-ups, using wording you approve. Our guide to email marketing in Canada covers consent and planning in more detail. Each flow is checked on mobile, tested before it goes live and reviewed monthly so it stays useful rather than noisy.
