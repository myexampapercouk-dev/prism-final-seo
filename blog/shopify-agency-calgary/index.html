Shopify for Calgary Businesses: How Remote Store Builds and Migrations Work

Looking for a Shopify agency in Calgary? How remote Shopify builds and migrations work for Alberta brands, from tax and shipping to speed, timings and costs.

What a Shopify agency in Calgary should do for you

Calgary's economy includes energy, technology, agriculture and food, transport and logistics, construction, tourism and a growing number of independent retailers and direct-to-consumer brands. Products sold online range from outdoor and western wear to food and drink, home goods, beauty, equipment and parts. Some businesses run a shop in the city and sell online as well; others sell only online to customers across Canada.

Whatever you sell, a good Shopify agency should:

  • Plan the store structure so collections, filters and navigation match how customers shop.
  • Design for mobile first, with clear product pages, simple navigation and a smooth checkout.
  • Set up tax, shipping and payments correctly for where you sell.
  • Keep the store fast by choosing a well-built theme and only the apps you need.
  • Build in search foundations such as page titles, product content, structured data and clean URLs.
  • Train your team so you can add products, run promotions and manage orders yourselves.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers new Shopify stores, redesigns and migrations, and our guide to Shopify in Canada covers considerations that apply nationwide.

Planning a new Shopify store

A new store starts with decisions rather than design. How many products do you sell, and how do customers choose between them? Do you need variants such as size and colour, product bundles or subscriptions? Will you sell to businesses as well as consumers? Do you need to sync stock with a physical shop? The answers decide the theme, the apps and the structure.

We then plan collections and navigation, design key page templates such as the homepage, collection page and product page, and set up the store. Product content matters a great deal: clear titles, honest descriptions, good photos, sizing or specification details and delivery information all help customers buy with confidence. Our Shopify store setup guide walks through the main settings, much of which applies in Canada too.

Canadian tax, shipping and local pickup

Alberta has no provincial sales tax, so sales to customers in Alberta generally carry only the federal Goods and Services Tax. Customers in other provinces may be charged the Harmonized Sales Tax or GST plus a provincial sales tax, depending on where they are. Shopify can calculate these taxes, but the settings need to reflect your registrations and obligations. We set up tax in line with what your accountant confirms, and approval of your tax position rests with you and your accountant.

Shipping across Canada can involve long distances and very different costs between urban and remote addresses. Shopify supports flat rates, free shipping thresholds, weight-based rates and calculated carrier rates, and the right choice depends on your products and margins. Many Calgary retailers also offer local pickup or local delivery within the city, which can be a strong reason for local customers to buy online.

Migrating to Shopify from another platform

Many Calgary businesses come to Shopify from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or older platforms. A migration should move products, customers and order history where needed, keep or improve the design, and protect existing search rankings. The most important step for search is mapping every old URL to its new equivalent and setting up redirects, so links and rankings carry across.

Our guide to WooCommerce to Shopify migration explains the process in detail. For larger brands with high order volumes, wholesale channels or complex requirements, we also handle Shopify Plus migrations. We plan migrations around your quieter trading periods so there is time to test before busy seasons.

Speed, apps and search

Shopify stores often slow down as apps are added over time, each loading its own code. A lean store with a well-built theme and only essential apps usually loads faster and converts better. Our Shopify speed optimisation guide explains what to check.

Search is the other half of a store's long-term success. Collections and product pages should target the phrases customers use, with unique descriptions rather than copied manufacturer text. If you also want customers in Calgary to find your physical shop, local search matters too. Privacy, accessibility and French content

An online store collects personal information with every order, so your privacy policy and data handling need care. In Alberta, many private-sector organisations are covered by the province's own Personal Information Protection Act, while the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) applies in other situations, such as some activity across provincial or national borders. Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) applies to marketing email, so newsletter sign-ups need proper consent. If you sell to customers in Quebec, that province's privacy law, often called Law 25, may add requirements. These notes are general information rather than legal advice, so refer to official guidance from the relevant regulators.

Accessibility law differs by province. Ontario, Manitoba, Nova Scotia and British Columbia, among others, have their own accessibility acts, and requirements can apply depending on where your customers and organisation are. Alberta's position may develop over time, so check current official guidance. An accessible store, with clear labels, good contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation, is good practice for every customer.

If you sell to French-speaking customers, Shopify supports multiple languages. We work in English; French product content and pages are written with your team or a professional translator, and we set up the store so each language version is indexed correctly. Selling into Quebec can bring additional French-language requirements, so check official guidance.

How a remote Shopify project works

The UK is usually seven hours ahead of Calgary, so we agree a regular call slot in the UK afternoon, which falls in the Calgary morning, and share designs and a staging store online so you can review them whenever suits you.

  1. Discovery. We agree products, channels, features, apps and the customers you want to reach.
  2. Structure and content plan. We plan collections, navigation and who provides product content and photos.
  3. Design. We design key templates for your feedback.
  4. Build and setup. We build the store, configure tax, shipping and payments, and import products.
  5. Testing and launch. We test orders, tracking and redirects on phones and desktops, then launch and train your team.

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Calgary?

Choose an agency that asks about your products and customers, explains its theme and app choices, sets up Canadian tax and shipping carefully, protects search rankings during migrations and leaves you owning everything.

Can a UK studio build a Shopify store for a Calgary business?

Yes. Shopify projects are planned, designed and built online. Prism has no Canadian office; we work remotely and schedule calls in the Calgary morning.

How much does a Shopify store cost with Prism?

Ecommerce websites typically cost £1,500 to £3,500. Shopify Plus migrations are quoted individually, and we can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Typically five to twelve weeks, depending on the number of products, templates, integrations and whether data is being migrated from another platform.

Does Shopify handle sales tax for Alberta and other provinces?

Yes. Shopify POS shares inventory between your physical shop and online store, and you can offer local pickup or delivery to Calgary customers.
