Meta Ads Agency for German Businesses: Remote Facebook and Instagram Ads

Looking for a Meta ads agency in Germany? German and English creative, tracking with consent, what a remote UK studio manages and our fees in pounds.

  • Meta ads in Germany work best with German-language creative, careful consent and tracking setup, and campaigns organised by country and language.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Germany; we manage Meta ads remotely, write English copy and work with your team or a translator on German ads.
  • Management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month with ad spend paid separately to Meta, quoted in pounds with other currencies on request.

A Meta ads agency for a German business should turn your Facebook and Instagram spend into measurable sales or leads, with creative that speaks to German buyers and tracking that respects consent rules. That means a clear campaign structure, ads in the right language, accurate conversion data and honest reporting on what is working.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages Meta ads remotely for businesses across Europe. We have no office in Germany. Our paid media service runs by video call and shared reports, one hour behind German time. We write English ad copy in-house and work with your team or a translator on German copy.

What makes Meta ads in Germany different

Germany is the largest consumer market in the EU, with strong ecommerce, fashion, home and garden, health and wellbeing, consumer technology and B2B sectors. Several features shape how Meta campaigns should be run:

  • German-language creative. Most German buyers respond better to ads written naturally in German than to English or machine-translated copy.
  • Careful buyers. Many German shoppers research before buying and look for clear information on price, delivery, returns and the company behind the ad.
  • Privacy and consent. Consent for tracking is taken seriously, which affects how much conversion data reaches Meta.
  • The wider German-speaking region. Campaigns often extend to Austria and Switzerland, which have their own prices, delivery terms and, in Switzerland's case, rules outside the EU.

If you advertise in several countries, our guide to running Meta ads in multiple countries explains how to organise campaigns by market and language.

German-language creative

Creative is the biggest lever in most Meta accounts. Ads need to stop the scroll, explain the product quickly and give a reason to click. In Germany, that usually means specific, factual messages: what the product does, what it costs, how quickly it arrives and how returns work. Vague claims tend to perform poorly and can raise questions about advertising rules.

Prism writes English ad copy in-house and plans creative concepts, formats and testing. For German copy, we brief your team or a native-speaking writer or translator with the concept, key messages and character limits, then build and check the ads in the account. German text often runs longer than English, so we design images and video captions with space for it.

Tracking, the Conversions API and consent

Meta's delivery system learns from conversion data, so accurate tracking matters. We set up the Meta pixel and the Conversions API so that purchases, leads and other key actions are recorded, and we check that events are not counted twice. Our Meta pixel setup guide covers the basics.

In Germany and the rest of the EU, tracking for advertising normally needs consent, and the way your consent banner works affects how much data Meta receives. We set up tracking to respect the choices visitors make, and we compare results with your shop or CRM data so reporting is realistic. We keep legal advice general: check current official guidance and confirm your setup with your legal adviser, who has final approval on consent and privacy wording.

  • Catalogue ads that show products from your feed, including dynamic remarketing to recent visitors.
  • Lead campaigns using instant forms or your own landing pages, with lead quality checked against your sales team's feedback.
  • Awareness and video campaigns for launches and new markets, measured carefully alongside sales.

Our guide to lookalike audiences explains when they still help, and our guide to remarketing covers bringing back visitors who did not buy first time.

Meta ads for ecommerce brands selling into Germany

Many brands from the UK and elsewhere in Europe advertise into Germany as a new market. Before spending heavily, it is worth checking that the basics are in place:

  • A German-language store or landing pages, not just German ads pointing to English pages.
  • Prices shown in euros, with delivery costs and times for Germany stated clearly.
  • Payment methods German customers commonly use available at checkout.
  • Returns information that matches EU consumer rules, confirmed with your adviser.
  • Customer service that can respond to German-speaking customers.

If your store is on Shopify, our guide to Shopify Markets for Europe explains how to add German language and euro pricing to one store.

Common problems in inherited accounts

When we review an existing account, we often find too many small ad sets splitting the budget, the same creative running for months without testing, tracking events firing twice or not at all, and English ads shown to German audiences. Fixing tracking, simplifying the structure and building a steady creative testing routine usually makes every later change more effective.

FAQs

Do you have a Meta ads agency office in Germany?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Germany. We manage accounts remotely by video call and shared reports, one hour behind German time.

Can you run Facebook and Instagram ads in German?

Yes. We plan, build and manage German campaigns. German ad copy is written or reviewed by your team or a native-speaking translator, as Prism writes in English.

How much does a Meta ads agency in Germany cost?

Our management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, quoted in pounds with other currencies on request. Ad spend is paid separately to Meta.

Does consent reduce our Meta ads tracking?

It can, because visitors who decline tracking are not measured in the same way. We set up the pixel and Conversions API to respect consent and compare results with your own sales data.

Can you also run ads in Austria and Switzerland?

Yes. We can set up separate campaigns for each country. Switzerland is outside the EU, so check current official guidance on its rules for delivery, pricing and data.

How long before Meta ads show results?

Early data comes within weeks, but stable results usually take three to six months of creative testing, tracking fixes and refinement.
