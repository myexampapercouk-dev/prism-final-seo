Why Riyadh brands choose Shopify

Riyadh is the capital and the largest business market in the Kingdom, home to established retailers, fast-growing direct-to-consumer brands and many new founders. Online shopping is part of daily life, and buyers expect stores that load quickly on a phone, show prices clearly and deliver on time.

Shopify suits many of these brands because it is hosted, secure and relatively simple to run day to day. Your team can add products, run discounts and manage orders without a developer for every change. The app ecosystem covers reviews, subscriptions, loyalty, bundles and more, and themes can be adapted for right-to-left layouts.

Shopify is not the answer for every business. Stores with very complex product logic or heavy content needs sometimes suit WordPress with WooCommerce instead. We build on both and will tell you honestly which fits.

Bilingual Shopify stores for Saudi shoppers

Most Riyadh brands need both Arabic and English. Doing this well on Shopify takes more than switching on a language. The layout must mirror correctly for right-to-left reading, menus and filters must make sense in both languages, and each language version needs proper page setup so search engines show the right one.

We set up the structure, theme behaviour and language settings, and we work with your translator so product names, collections and key pages read naturally. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website covers the details, from fonts to form fields.

Payments, delivery and trust

Saudi shoppers want to know how they can pay, when their order will arrive and what happens if they need to return something. A store that answers those questions clearly converts better.

  • Payments: we set up the payment options your business has chosen and that are supported for your Shopify account, including card payments and any local methods available to you.
  • Cash on delivery: if you offer it, we make the rules and any fees clear before checkout.
  • Delivery: shipping zones, rates and estimated times shown on product and cart pages.
  • Returns: a plain-language returns page linked from every product.
  • Trust signals: genuine reviews, clear contact details and secure checkout messaging.

Consumer protection, data and ecommerce rules apply to online sellers in Saudi Arabia. Our notes are general; please check official guidance or ask your own adviser, as compliance rests with you as the seller.

Shopify SEO for Riyadh stores

Shopify gives a solid base for search, but it still needs work. Common fixes include tidying duplicate product URLs, writing unique collection intros, improving image sizes, adding structured data and building helpful buying guides.

We also prepare content so it can be cited by AI search tools, which increasingly answer shopping questions directly. Our SEO and GEO service explains this approach, and our Shopify SEO guide walks through the platform-specific fixes. For a local view, see our notes on SEO in Riyadh.

Questions to ask any Shopify agency

Before you hire anyone, ask who will own the store and its accounts (it should be you), which apps they plan to install and what they cost each month, how they will handle Arabic layout, how they protect SEO during a redesign, and what support looks like after launch. Clear answers to those questions usually tell you more than a portfolio.

Common Shopify mistakes we fix

Many Riyadh stores we review were launched quickly and then left alone. The same issues come up again and again, and most are simple to fix once spotted.

  • Too many apps: every app adds monthly cost and often slows the store. We remove what is not earning its place.
  • Half-mirrored Arabic layouts: icons, sliders and arrows that still point the wrong way after switching language.
  • Thin collection pages: no introduction, no filters and no reason for search engines to rank them.
  • Weak product photos: small or inconsistent images that make good products look cheap.
  • Hidden delivery costs: fees that only appear at checkout, which causes people to abandon their carts.
  • Broken tracking: ad platforms that cannot see purchases, so automated bidding works blind.

Fixing these basics often does more than any new campaign. We usually start a growth retainer with a short clean-up phase so that ad spend lands on a store that is ready to convert.

Shopify themes and custom design

Not every store needs a fully custom theme. A well-chosen premium theme, adapted carefully for your brand, products and right-to-left layout, is often the fastest and most cost-effective route. Custom sections make sense when you need something a theme cannot do well, such as a product builder, detailed comparison tables or a lookbook layout.

We keep the design focused on what Saudi shoppers need to see: clear prices, delivery promises, trust signals and easy navigation in both languages. Brand colours, typography and photography then do the work of making the store feel like yours. Our web design guide for Riyadh covers the wider design side for local brands.

After launch, we hand over a short guide so your team can add products, update banners and run discounts without waiting for us. Training is done over video call, and we stay available on retainer for anything more involved.
