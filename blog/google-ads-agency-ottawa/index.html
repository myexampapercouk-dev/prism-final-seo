Google Ads Agency for Ottawa Businesses: Remote PPC Explained

Choosing a Google Ads agency in Ottawa? How remote PPC works for Ottawa firms, bilingual campaigns, tracking, management fees and what to ask before you sign.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A remote Google Ads agency can manage Ottawa campaigns well when you own the account, tracking is set up properly and reporting is agreed in advance.
  • Ottawa campaigns often need careful location targeting across both sides of the river and a clear plan for English and French ads.
  • Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from management fees, so ask any agency to show both clearly before you start.

A Google Ads agency for an Ottawa business should put your ads in front of people actively searching for what you sell, track which clicks turn into enquiries or sales, and cut spend that does not pay its way. That work happens inside your Google Ads account, so it can be done remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages paid search for Canadian businesses online, with no office in Ottawa.

This guide explains how Google Ads usually works for Ottawa organisations, how to handle location and language, what a remote arrangement looks like, what management costs with us and how to judge any agency before you hand over your budget.

What a Google Ads agency in Ottawa should do

Ottawa's market is unusual. Alongside trades, clinics, restaurants and retailers, the city has a large community of technology firms, consultancies, associations and government suppliers, many of them in Kanata and the west end. Each needs something slightly different from paid search, but the core responsibilities of a good agency stay the same:

  • Accurate conversion tracking, so you can see which campaigns produce calls, forms and sales. Our conversion tracking guide explains the set-up.
  • Tight keyword and search term control, including a maintained list of negative keywords. Our negative keywords guide shows why this matters so much.
  • Sensible bidding that matches your goals and the amount of conversion data you have. Our bidding strategies guide compares the options.
  • Landing pages that convert, because even well-targeted clicks are wasted on a weak page.
  • Clear reporting on spend, results and what was changed each month.

Our paid media service covers Google Ads alongside Microsoft Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube, so campaigns can be planned together rather than in isolation.

Location targeting across Ottawa and Gatineau

The National Capital Region spans two provinces. Ottawa sits in Ontario and Gatineau sits across the river in Quebec, and many businesses serve customers on both sides. Location settings in Google Ads need care, because the default options can show ads to people who are merely interested in a place rather than located in it.

For most local businesses, we target people in or regularly in your chosen areas, then check the location reports to see where clicks and conversions actually come from. Some services, such as home trades, may want a tight radius around communities like Barrhaven, Orléans, Kanata, Nepean and Stittsville. Others, such as B2B suppliers, may target the whole country. Our location targeting guide explains the settings in detail.

English and French campaigns

Many Ottawa organisations serve customers in both official languages. Google Ads targets by the language settings people use, so a campaign written only in English may miss French-speaking searchers, and French searches need their own keywords rather than straight translations.

Google Ads for B2B and technology firms in Ottawa

Technology companies and B2B suppliers often have long sales cycles, small search volumes and high-value leads. That changes how campaigns should be run. Broad keywords can burn budget on job seekers, students and researchers, so search term reviews and negative keywords become even more important. Conversion tracking should focus on qualified actions such as demo requests or quote forms rather than any page view.

For some B2B offers, LinkedIn can reach decision-makers by job title and company in a way search cannot. Our comparison of LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads for B2B explains when each channel makes sense and when to use both.

Government suppliers should note that public sector buying often follows formal procurement routes, so search ads usually support awareness and enquiries rather than replace those processes.

Which campaign types suit Ottawa businesses

Google Ads offers several campaign types, and not every business needs all of them. Search campaigns are usually the starting point, because they reach people actively looking for a service. Shopping and Performance Max campaigns suit retailers with a product feed. Remarketing can remind past visitors about you, while video campaigns on YouTube can build awareness before people search.

We usually start with the campaign type closest to buying intent, prove that tracking and landing pages work, and only then expand into other formats. That keeps early budgets focused and makes it easier to see what each pound or dollar of spend is achieving.

Landing pages and the click after the ad

Paid search sends people to a specific page, and that page does much of the selling. A good landing page repeats the promise in the ad, explains the offer clearly, shows real evidence of your work and makes the next step obvious. It should load quickly on a phone and avoid distractions that pull visitors away.

Our guide to landing page design covers the elements that usually matter most. We review landing pages as part of campaign set-up and can design or improve them where needed.

How remote Google Ads management works

Ottawa runs on Eastern Time, so the UK is usually five hours ahead. UK afternoons overlap comfortably with Ottawa mornings, which suits regular calls and quick replies. Day-to-day optimisation happens inside your account, and we share changes and reports in writing so your team can review them at a convenient time.

  1. Audit or set-up. We review an existing account or build a new one in your name.
  2. Tracking. We confirm conversions are recorded accurately before scaling spend.
  3. Launch. Campaigns go live with agreed budgets, locations and languages.
  4. Optimisation. Regular search term reviews, bid and budget adjustments, and ad testing.
  5. Reporting. A monthly summary of spend, results and changes, with recommendations.

If you already run campaigns, our PPC audit checklist shows what we look at first.

What Google Ads management costs with Prism

Management is billed on monthly retainers priced in pounds sterling: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Prices in Canadian dollars or other currencies can be quoted on request. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own account and is always separate from our fees. Full details are on our pricing page.

Paid search can bring traffic from day one, but campaigns usually need time to gather data and improve. Results typically settle over three to six months as targeting, bidding and landing pages are refined.

Questions to ask any Google Ads agency

  • Will the Google Ads account be in my name, with full admin access for me?
  • How will conversions be tracked, and can I see that tracking working?
  • How are management fees separated from ad spend?
  • How often will search terms be reviewed and negative keywords added?
  • What will the monthly report include, and who will explain it?
  • How do you handle English and French campaigns for the Ottawa area?

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Ottawa?

Choose an agency that keeps the account in your name, sets up conversion tracking before scaling spend, separates fees from ad spend and reports clearly. Ask how they handle location targeting across Ottawa and Gatineau and whether they plan for French searches.

Can a UK agency manage Google Ads for an Ottawa business?

Yes. Google Ads is managed online, so location is not a barrier. Prism has no Ottawa office; we work remotely with Canadian businesses and schedule calls in the overlap between UK afternoons and Ottawa mornings.

How much does Google Ads management cost with Prism?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Ad spend is paid to Google separately. We can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Should I run Google Ads in French as well as English?

If a meaningful share of your customers search in French, separate French campaigns with matching French landing pages usually work better than one mixed campaign. The French text is written with your team or a translator.

How quickly will Google Ads bring enquiries?

Ads can bring clicks from the day they go live, but campaigns usually improve over three to six months as data builds up and targeting, bids and landing pages are refined.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. We set up or work within an account in your name, with your payment details, and you can remove our access at any time without losing data or history.
