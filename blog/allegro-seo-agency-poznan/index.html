What an Allegro SEO agency in Poznan actually does

Allegro SEO for Poznan sellers: a practical process

Our process is built for sellers with anything from a few dozen listings to several thousand. We start with the products that matter most to revenue and work outwards.

  1. Audit. We review a sample of your listings against search terms, competitor listings and Allegro's listing guidance, and identify the biggest gaps.
  2. Keyword map. We group search phrases by product type and buyer intent, so each listing has a clear primary phrase and supporting terms.
  3. Title and parameter templates. We create rules your team can use to build consistent titles and fill parameters across the catalogue.
  4. Description structure. We design a clear layout for descriptions, with sections for key benefits, specifications, what is in the box and care or usage notes.
  5. Priority rewrites. We rework the top listings first, with Polish copy written or checked by your team or translator.
  6. Monthly review. We track visibility for key phrases and adjust templates as Allegro and your range change.

If you also sell on other marketplaces, the same thinking applies. Our guides to Amazon SEO in Germany and bol marketplace SEO in the Netherlands show how the approach adapts to different platforms.

Common Allegro listing problems we see

Most underperforming listings share a handful of problems. They are usually easy to fix once they have been spotted.

  • Titles written for the warehouse, not the buyer. Internal codes and abbreviations rarely match search terms.
  • Incomplete parameters. Sellers often fill only the required fields, which removes listings from filtered results.
  • One description copied across many products. This gives buyers little reason to choose a specific item.
  • Weak main images. Busy backgrounds or small product shots reduce clicks from the results page.
  • Unclear delivery and returns. Buyers compare offers quickly and choose the one that feels safest.
  • Duplicate or overlapping offers. Several similar listings can compete with each other instead of supporting one strong offer.

How AI-powered tools help, and where they do not

We use AI-powered tools to speed up parts of the work, such as grouping large keyword lists, spotting missing parameters across a catalogue or drafting description structures. This helps sellers with large ranges get through their listings faster.

AI does not replace judgement. Every recommendation is checked by a person, and Polish copy is written or reviewed by a fluent speaker on your side. Automated translations that read awkwardly can put buyers off, so we always recommend a native review before listings go live.
