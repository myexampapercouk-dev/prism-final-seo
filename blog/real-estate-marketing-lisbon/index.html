A property website that wins enquiries

Property portals are useful, but your own website is where you control the story and keep the lead. A good Lisbon property site has:

  • Fast listing pages. Large galleries that still load quickly on mobile.
  • Smart search and filters. By area, price, bedrooms and property type.
  • Area guides. Pages for neighbourhoods such as Alfama, Príncipe Real, Parque das Nações or Cascais, explaining what life there is like.
  • Development microsites. Dedicated pages for each project, with floor plans, specifications and a clear enquiry form.
  • Several languages. Portuguese, English and any other languages your buyers use, with proper hreflang tags.
  • Clear calls to action. Book a viewing, request a brochure or arrange a video call.

We often build property sites on WordPress, which connects well with property feeds and handles multilingual content. Our website development service page covers the build process, and our page on web design in Lisbon shows how we approach local projects.

SEO for Lisbon property

Property search is competitive, with large portals near the top for many generic terms. Agencies and developers win by focusing on specific, useful content.

  • Area and property-type pages. For example, apartments in a particular district or new builds near the river.
  • Buyer guides. Plain-English guides to the buying process in Portugal, written in general terms and pointing to official sources and qualified advisers.
  • Google Business Profile. For agencies with a real office, accurate details and steady reviews help in map results.
  • Technical care. Expired listings handled with sensible redirects, no thin duplicate pages and fast load times.

Our page on SEO in Lisbon covers the wider local search picture.

Paid ads for property

Ads can bring in enquiries quickly, especially for launches. We run:

  • Google Ads. Search campaigns for development names, areas and property types. Our page on Google Ads in Lisbon explains how we set these up.
  • Meta Ads. Image and video campaigns aimed at buyers in specific countries, with lead forms or landing pages.

Reaching overseas buyers

A large share of interest in Lisbon property can come from outside Portugal. Marketing to overseas buyers needs a slightly different approach from marketing to local families.

  • Country-by-country campaigns. We split ad campaigns by the countries your buyers come from, so budgets, messages and timings suit each market.
  • Language versions that match. If you advertise in a country, the landing page should be in the language buyers there expect, supplied by your team or a translator.
  • Video viewings. Many overseas buyers shortlist from abroad, so video tours and an easy way to book a video call can shorten the process.
  • Clear process information. Plain-English pages that explain the general steps of buying in Portugal, while pointing buyers to official sources and qualified local advisers for anything legal or tax-related.
  • Prompt follow-up. Enquiries from other time zones still expect quick replies, so we help set up auto-responses and lead routing.
  • Currency clarity. Prices shown in euros, with any conversions labelled as estimates.

We also help you separate overseas investor enquiries from local buyer enquiries in your reporting, so you can see which markets bring the most serious leads and adjust budgets accordingly.

Regulatory and compliance notes

Property marketing in Portugal sits alongside licensing, advertising and data rules. Keep these notes general, and check official guidance or your own legal adviser. Compliance approval for your listings and claims rests with you.

  • Licensing and listing information. Portuguese estate agencies operate under a licensing regime, and listings may need to show certain details, such as energy certificate information. Your adviser or professional body can confirm what applies.
  • GDPR. Enquiry forms and email lists hold personal data, so you need a lawful basis, a privacy notice and secure handling.
  • Cookie consent. ePrivacy rules generally require consent before non-essential cookies and ad pixels are set. We configure tracking to respect the choice.
  • European Accessibility Act. From June 2025, the EAA applies to many consumer-facing digital services. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt. The harmonised standard EN 301 549 refers to WCAG AA, which we build to as standard.

Our accessibility guide covers the practical checks.

Measuring what matters

Property leads vary hugely in quality, so we track more than clicks. We set up tracking for enquiry forms, phone calls, brochure downloads and viewing requests, and where possible we connect this to your CRM so you can see which campaigns lead to viewings and sales. Monthly reports focus on cost per qualified enquiry rather than vanity numbers, and we use your sales team's feedback to refine targeting.
