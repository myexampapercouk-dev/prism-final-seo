Google Ads Agency for Saudi Arabia: How Remote Campaign Management Works

Looking for a Google Ads agency in Saudi Arabia? How a remote UK studio plans, tracks and manages Arabic and English campaigns, with fees in pounds.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good Google Ads agency for Saudi Arabia builds campaigns around how people in the kingdom actually search, in Arabic, English or a mix of both, and tracks real enquiries and sales.
  • A remote agency can manage Saudi campaigns well when the account sits in your name, tracking is set up before launch and reporting is clear and regular.
  • Our management retainers start at £350 per month, ad spend is paid separately to Google, and other currencies are quoted on request.

A Google Ads agency for a Saudi Arabian business should plan campaigns around how your customers search in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and beyond, write ads in the languages they use, and measure the calls, forms, WhatsApp messages and sales that follow. The agency does not need an office in the kingdom, but it does need a clear process, honest reporting and an account that belongs to you.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio. We have no office in Saudi Arabia and manage campaigns for Gulf businesses remotely, through shared dashboards, written reports and planned video calls. This guide explains how we approach Google Ads for Saudi businesses as part of our paid media service, what it costs and what to ask any agency before you hand over your budget.

What a Google Ads agency in Saudi Arabia should do

Google Ads covers several campaign types, and the right mix depends on what you sell and how people find it. For most Saudi businesses, the work falls into a few areas.

  • Search campaigns that show text ads when people type a query such as a service, product or brand name.
  • Shopping and Performance Max campaigns for online shops with a product feed in Google Merchant Center.
  • YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns for building awareness and reaching people before they search.
  • Remarketing to bring back visitors who looked but did not enquire or buy.
  • Tracking and reporting so spend can be linked to a real outcome where the data allows.

An agency should explain which of these suit your goals and budget, rather than switching on everything at once. A small budget spread across too many campaigns rarely gives Google enough data to learn.

Arabic and English search in the kingdom

Language is the biggest planning decision for Saudi campaigns. Many people search in Arabic, many search in English, and plenty mix the two, typing a brand or product name in English with the rest of the query in Arabic. Some also type Arabic words in Latin letters. A sound account plans for all of these patterns instead of translating one keyword list.

Search patternExample of the issueHow we handle it

We write in English. Arabic ad copy and landing pages are written with your team or a professional translator, then reviewed before anything goes live. Ads in Arabic should lead to Arabic pages; sending Arabic searchers to an English page wastes clicks. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites covers right-to-left layouts and page structure.

Targeting the right cities and regions

Saudi Arabia is large, and a business in Riyadh may not want clicks from Jeddah or the Eastern Province. Google Ads lets you target the whole country, specific regions or a radius around a location. It also lets you choose between people physically in an area and people showing interest in it, which matters for businesses that serve visitors or buyers from elsewhere in the Gulf.

We set location targeting at the start, then review performance by area once data comes in. Our guide to Google Ads location targeting explains the settings in more detail. Ad scheduling also needs thought: the Saudi working week and the timing of prayer, Ramadan and public holidays all affect when people search and when your team can answer calls.

Tracking before spending

An agency that launches campaigns without conversion tracking is guessing. Before any budget goes live, we check that the account can see the actions that matter to you.

  • Form submissions and booking requests
  • Calls from ads and from the website
  • Clicks on WhatsApp buttons, which are often the main contact route for Saudi customers
  • Online purchases with order values for e-commerce sites

We usually set this up through Google Tag Manager and GA4, with enhanced conversions where suitable. Our Google Ads conversion tracking guide walks through the steps. Any consent requirements depend on your business and the rules that apply to it, so we ask clients to check current official guidance on data protection and confirm the approach with their adviser.

Landing pages that match the ad

Many Saudi campaigns underperform because the ad promises one thing and the page delivers another. A visitor who searches in Arabic for a specific service should land on an Arabic page about that service, with clear prices or next steps, a visible contact button and fast loading on mobile. Our notes on landing page design apply just as well in the Gulf. If your current site cannot support this, we can build focused landing pages as part of the work, quoted separately.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

There are two separate costs: your ad spend, which you pay directly to Google, and the management fee you pay the agency. We price management in pounds sterling and quote other currencies on request.

Ad spend is always paid separately and stays under your control. Our pricing page sets out what each retainer includes. Paid search can bring traffic quickly, but campaigns usually need several weeks of data to settle, and steady improvement typically builds over three to six months.

How a remote engagement runs

  1. Discovery call. We discuss your services, margins, target areas, languages and current results.
  2. Account review. If you already run ads, we audit structure, keywords, search terms and tracking.
  3. Plan. You receive a written plan covering campaigns, budgets, languages and the outcomes we will measure.
  4. Build and launch. Campaigns are built in your own Google Ads account with tracking checked first.
  5. Weekly management. Bids, search terms, ads and budgets are reviewed and adjusted.
  6. Monthly report. A plain-English summary of spend, results and what we will change next.

Saudi Arabia is a few hours ahead of the UK, so we agree regular call slots that suit both sides and keep most communication in writing, which gives you a clear record of every change.

Negative keywords and wasted spend

In both Arabic and English, broad queries can trigger ads for searches that will never convert, such as jobs, free downloads or unrelated meanings of a word. We review search term reports often and build negative keyword lists in both languages. Our negative keywords guide explains the method.

Google Ads alongside other channels

Google Ads works best as part of a wider plan. Many Saudi brands also use Snapchat, TikTok and Meta to reach people before they search. Our guide to choosing a Meta Ads agency in Saudi Arabia covers social campaigns, and if you want longer-term organic traffic, our notes on hiring an SEO agency in Riyadh explain how search engine optimisation fits in. Regulated sectors such as finance, health and property have extra advertising restrictions, and approval of any ad wording rests with your compliance team.

Questions to ask any Google Ads agency

  • Will the Google Ads account be in my name, and will I have full access?
  • Who writes and checks the Arabic ads and keywords?
  • Which conversions will you track, and how will you test them before launch?
  • How often will you review search terms and adjust bids?
  • What does the report show, and how often will I receive it?
  • What is the notice period if I want to stop?

FAQs

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Saudi Arabia?

Look for an agency that keeps the account in your name, plans Arabic and English campaigns separately, sets up conversion tracking before launch and reports clearly every month. Office location matters less than process and transparency.

Do you have an office in Saudi Arabia?

No. Prism Works Studio is based in the UK and manages campaigns for Saudi businesses remotely through shared dashboards, written reports and scheduled video calls.

Can you run Google Ads in Arabic?

Yes. We plan and build Arabic campaigns, while the Arabic ad copy and landing pages are written with your team or a professional translator and reviewed before launch.

How much should I spend on Google Ads in Saudi Arabia?

It depends on competition in your sector, your target areas and the value of a customer. We agree a starting budget during discovery and adjust it once real cost and conversion data is available.

How quickly will Google Ads bring results?

Ads can bring clicks within days of launch, but campaigns usually need several weeks of data to settle. Steady improvement in cost per enquiry or sale typically builds over three to six months.

Are there advertising rules I need to follow in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Google has its own policies, and local rules apply to some products, services and claims. Rules change, so please check current official guidance and confirm any sensitive wording with your adviser or compliance team.
