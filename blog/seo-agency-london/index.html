Choosing an SEO Agency in London: A Practical Guide for London Businesses

Choosing an SEO agency in London? Judge them on strategy, reporting and fit with your market, not postcode. What to check, what it costs and how remote works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 30 October 2026 · 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • London search results are some of the most competitive in the UK, so pick an agency that can explain exactly where you can realistically win, borough by borough and topic by topic.
  • Being based in London is not a skill. Judge an agency on its process, reporting, honesty about timelines and how well it understands your buyers.
  • Remote SEO works well for London businesses when there is a clear monthly rhythm, shared reporting and direct access to the people doing the work.

The right SEO agency for a London business is one that understands how crowded London search results are, picks battles you can actually win, and reports on enquiries and revenue rather than vanity rankings. Where the agency sits matters far less than how it works, so this guide focuses on what to check before you sign anything.

Prism Works Studio is a UK agency that works remotely with businesses across the country, including London. We do not have a London office and we are not going to pretend otherwise. What we can offer is a clear view of what good SEO looks like for a London business, and the questions that separate a strong agency from an expensive one.

Why SEO in London is different

London is not one market. It is dozens of overlapping ones. A dentist in Islington competes with clinics in Camden and King's Cross, not with one in Croydon. A B2B software firm in Shoreditch competes nationally, and often internationally, regardless of its address. A restaurant in Soho is fighting for searches made by people who may be standing on the same street.

That mix creates three things you will not find to the same degree elsewhere in the UK:

  • Very dense local competition. For many service searches, the map results in central London are packed with businesses within a few hundred metres of each other. Small differences in your Google Business Profile, reviews and location pages matter a great deal.
  • Well-funded national competitors. Many national brands and head offices sit in London, which means they also target London searches with large content and link budgets.
  • Neighbourhood-level intent. People search for "accountant Clapham" or "physio near Canary Wharf", not just "London". Borough and neighbourhood phrasing can be more valuable than the city head term.

A good agency will not promise you page one for "London" plus your service in a few weeks. It will show you where the realistic openings are.

What to check when choosing an SEO agency in London

There is no shortage of agencies that will happily take a London retainer. These are the checks that matter.

1. Do they start with an audit and a plan?

A proper agency looks at your site before quoting. That means technical health, content gaps, the competitors actually ranking for your searches, and your local listings. If someone quotes a fixed package without looking at your site, they are selling a product, not a strategy. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows what a reasonable first review covers.

2. Can they explain where you can win?

In London, the honest answer is often "not the city head term, at least not yet". A good agency will point you towards neighbourhood searches, specific services and question-style searches where you have a real chance, then build from there.

3. What do they report on?

Rankings alone are not a result. Ask to see a sample report. It should cover organic enquiries, calls, form fills or sales, alongside visibility. If they cannot connect SEO to leads, you will struggle to judge whether the fee is worth it.

4. Who actually does the work?

Ask who writes the content, who handles technical fixes and who you speak to each month. You want direct access to the people doing the work, not a chain of account managers.

5. Do they cover AI search?

More London buyers now research through AI answers as well as Google. Ask whether the agency thinks about generative engine optimisation, meaning content that is structured and clear enough to be cited by AI tools.

6. What are the contract terms?

Watch for long lock-ins, unclear deliverables and ownership of content or accounts. Our guide to marketing agency contracts lists the clauses worth reading twice.

Red flags to watch for

  • Guaranteed rankings or guaranteed first-page results.
  • Large numbers of cheap links with no explanation of where they come from.
  • Reports that only show rankings for terms nobody searches.
  • Refusing to tell you what has changed on your site each month.
  • Pushing you into a long contract before any work has been done.

Remote versus local: do you need an agency in London?

Many London businesses assume they need an agency with a London address. In practice, almost all SEO work happens on screens: auditing, writing, fixing pages, building links and reading data. Being in the same city does not make any of that better.

Face-to-face meetingsEasier to arrangeUsually video calls

What makes remote work well is structure: an agreed monthly plan, a shared report, a fixed call and quick replies by email. Local knowledge matters for neighbourhood targeting, but a good agency will get that from research, search data and from you. You know your customers and your patch better than any agency will.

That said, if in-person meetings genuinely matter to your team, it is fair to make that a requirement. Just be clear that you are paying for convenience, not for better rankings.

How we approach SEO for London businesses

Our SEO and GEO service follows the same principles for every client, adjusted for the market:

  1. Audit: technical health, content, competitors and local listings.
  2. Focus: choose the services, areas and topics where you can realistically compete.
  3. Fix: technical issues and weak pages first, because they often hold everything else back.
  4. Build: service and area pages, helpful guides and content structured for AI answers.
  5. Earn authority: relevant mentions and links rather than bulk link buying.
  6. Report: monthly, in plain English, tied to enquiries and sales.

We work remotely, using video calls, shared documents and a regular reporting rhythm. That suits busy London teams who would rather not travel to a meeting to hear about a report they could read in five minutes.

Preparing before you speak to agencies

You will get better proposals if you have a few things ready:

  • Your main services and the areas of London you most want to win.
  • Your best customers and how they usually find you today.
  • Access to Google Search Console and analytics, or a note that they are not set up.
  • A rough monthly budget and how long you can commit before judging results.
  • Any past SEO work, especially links bought or content outsourced.

FAQs

How do I choose an SEO agency in London?

Look for an agency that audits your site before quoting, explains where you can realistically rank, reports on enquiries and sales, and gives you direct access to the people doing the work. Location matters far less than process and honesty.

Do I need an SEO agency based in London?

Not necessarily. SEO work is done online, so a remote UK agency can do the same work. If in-person meetings are important to you, factor that in, but it does not change the quality of the SEO itself.

Does Prism Works Studio have a London office?

No. Prism Works Studio is a UK agency that works remotely with businesses anywhere in the UK, including London, using video calls, shared reports and a regular monthly rhythm.

How long does SEO take for a London business?

SEO results typically take three to six months to build. Highly competitive London searches can take longer, which is why it often makes sense to target neighbourhood and specific service searches first.

Should I target "London" or specific areas?

Usually both, but specific areas and services often give quicker, more relevant wins. People frequently search by borough, neighbourhood or landmark, and those searches face fewer competitors than the city-wide term.

How much does an SEO agency in London cost?

Fees vary widely. Prism Works Studio's retainers start at £350 a month for Launch, with Growth at £750 and Scale at £1,500 a month. Compare what each agency includes rather than the headline price.
