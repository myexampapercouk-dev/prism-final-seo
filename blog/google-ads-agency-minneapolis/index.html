Who we help in Minneapolis and the Twin Cities

The Twin Cities metro takes in Minneapolis and St. Paul along with Bloomington, Edina, Plymouth, Eden Prairie, Maple Grove and many more suburbs. Each has its own search patterns, so we plan targeting around where your customers really are. We manage campaigns for:

  • Home services companies such as HVAC, roofing, snow removal, plumbing and remodeling, where demand shifts sharply with the weather.
  • Healthcare, dental and wellness providers looking for patients in particular suburbs.
  • Professional services, including law, accounting and consulting firms.
  • B2B, manufacturing and technology firms that need qualified leads rather than volume.
  • Retail and ecommerce brands selling locally or nationally through Google Shopping.

Planning around Minnesota seasons

Few markets have seasons as strong as Minnesota. Furnace repairs, snow removal and ice dam searches rise in winter, while landscaping, decks, lake services and outdoor events peak in the warmer months. A flat monthly budget rarely matches that pattern.

We plan budgets and campaigns around your real demand. That can mean:

  • Raising budgets ahead of the first cold snap or the spring thaw.
  • Switching ad copy and extensions to match seasonal services.
  • Pausing or reducing campaigns for services that are out of season.
  • Using ad schedules to focus spend on hours when you can answer the phone.

We review search terms every week during busy periods so that budget is not wasted on searches that will never turn into work.

Location targeting across the metro

Targeting the whole state is rarely right for a local business. We set campaigns to the suburbs, ZIP codes or radius you actually serve, and we can split campaigns by area if costs or demand vary. We also check location settings so ads reach people in or regularly in your area, not people who simply show interest in it.

For businesses serving both cities and the suburbs, we often build separate ad groups so ad copy can mention the area directly. That tends to improve relevance and click-through rates.

Landing pages and conversion tracking

Ads can only perform as well as the page they lead to. A good landing page matches the ad, explains the offer clearly and makes it easy to call or fill in a short form. Our landing page design guide covers the structure we use, and if your site needs work, our website development service can build dedicated pages.

Accurate tracking is just as important. We set up conversions for calls, forms, bookings and purchases, and link Google Ads to Google Analytics. Our GA4 setup guide shows the events we track as standard. Without reliable data, automated bidding cannot work well.

Common problems we find in Twin Cities accounts

When we audit an existing Google Ads account, a few issues appear regularly. None of them are unusual, and fixing them is often the quickest way to improve results without raising budgets:

  • Broad match keywords without enough negatives, so ads show for job seekers, DIY searches or services you do not offer.
  • Location settings left on the default, which can show ads to people outside your service area.
  • All traffic sent to the homepage instead of pages that match each service.
  • Conversion tracking that counts the wrong things, such as page views or duplicate form submissions.
  • One campaign for everything, which makes it hard to control budgets for different services or areas.
  • Ads running overnight when nobody is available to answer calls.
  • Automated recommendations accepted without review, which can widen targeting or raise budgets unexpectedly.

We document every issue we find in a written audit, explain why it matters and agree priorities with you before we change anything.

Choosing keywords for the Minneapolis market

Keyword choice is where most budget is won or lost. We group keywords by service and intent, separating searches that signal a clear need, such as emergency repairs or quote requests, from research searches that rarely lead to a call. We also look at how people in the Twin Cities phrase their searches, including the names of suburbs and neighborhoods, so ad copy and landing pages can reflect them.

Competitor keywords can be useful in some sectors, but we use them carefully and never use another company's trademark in ad text. Brand campaigns protecting your own business name are often worth running, because they are usually inexpensive and stop competitors taking clicks from people already looking for you.

For ecommerce brands, product feed quality matters as much as keywords. Clear titles, accurate prices, good images and correct availability help Shopping campaigns reach the right buyers. If you also sell through marketplaces, our team can support listing optimisation as part of a wider plan.
