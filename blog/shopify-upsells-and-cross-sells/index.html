Shopify Upsells and Cross-Sells: How to Raise Order Value Without Annoying Shoppers

How to set up Shopify upsells and cross-sells: product recommendations, complementary products, basket offers, bundles and post-purchase offers for UK shops.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 8 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start with what Shopify and your theme already offer, such as related and complementary product recommendations, before adding apps.
  • Good upsells and cross-sells are relevant, few in number and placed where the shopper is already deciding, not scattered across every page.
  • Measure average order value and conversion rate together, because an offer that raises one while lowering the other may not be helping.

Shopify upsells and cross-sells encourage shoppers to buy a better version of what they are looking at, or add something that goes with it. On Shopify you can do this with your theme's product recommendations, complementary products set up through Shopify's search and discovery tools, basket suggestions, bundles and, if you use them, apps that add offers after checkout. The best offers feel like helpful advice, not a sales pitch.

This guide is for UK Shopify store owners who want to lift average order value without making the shopping experience worse. It explains the difference between upsells and cross-sells, where each works best, what Shopify offers natively, when an app is worth it and how to measure whether it is working. Features and app options change, so check Shopify's current help documentation for the latest details.

Upsells versus cross-sells

UpsellCross-sell
What it suggestsA higher-spec, larger or premium version of the same itemA different item that complements the one being bought
ExampleThe 500ml bottle instead of the 250mlA cleaning kit with a pair of leather boots
Best placedOn the product page while the shopper is choosingOn the product page, in the basket or after purchase
Main riskFeeling pushy or confusing the choiceShowing irrelevant items that distract

Bundles sit between the two: a set of products sold together, often at a small saving. Our guide to product variants and bundles explains how to structure them.

How to set up Shopify upsells and cross-sells natively

Before installing anything, look at what your store already has. Most current Shopify themes include a product recommendations section, and Shopify's free search and discovery tools let you choose which products appear.

  1. Check your theme. In the theme editor, open a product template and look for a related products or product recommendations section. Our guide to Shopify theme sections and blocks explains how to add and arrange them.
  2. Set related products. Shopify can generate related product suggestions automatically from data such as purchase history and product descriptions. Check that what it shows is actually sensible for your catalogue.
  3. Set complementary products. Using the search and discovery tools, you can pick specific complementary products for a given item, such as a case for a phone accessory. Your theme needs a block that displays them.
  4. Use variants for size and spec upsells. If a bigger or premium version is simply a different option, a variant with a clear price difference is often the simplest upsell of all.
  5. Review on mobile. Make sure recommendations do not push the add to basket button out of sight.

For more control, metafields can store hand-picked pairings or "pairs well with" notes that your theme then displays. Our Shopify metafields guide covers how they work.

Where to place offers

Product pages: This is where shoppers are deciding, so it suits upsells and closely related cross-sells. Keep it to a few options, show prices clearly and make it easy to add an item without leaving the page. A "complete the look" row on fashion products, or a refill alongside a starter kit, works because it answers a real question.

Basket and cart drawer: Low-cost add-ons fit well here: batteries, care products, gift wrap or a small accessory. Shoppers have already made their main decision, so offers should be quick to add and not require choosing options. Some stores use a free delivery threshold message here too, which works best when the suggested items genuinely help reach it.

After checkout: Post-purchase offers appear after payment, often letting customers add an item with one click. These generally need an app and can work well because the purchase is already secure. They need careful testing to make sure they do not confuse customers or cause fulfilment problems.

Emails: Order confirmation and follow-up emails can suggest complementary products, as long as the main job of the email, confirming the order, comes first. Our guide to ecommerce transactional emails covers what they must include.

What makes a good offer

  • Relevance. The suggested product should make sense with the item, based on how customers actually use it.
  • Proportion. Cross-sells are usually cheaper than the main item. A £90 suggestion on a £15 product rarely lands.
  • Restraint. Two or three suggestions are usually enough. More choice can stall a decision.
  • Clarity. Show price, key detail and an image, and make adding it easy.
  • Honesty. If you show a saving on a bundle, it must be genuine. Check current official guidance on pricing and promotional claims, such as the CMA's and the CAP Code's.

Discounts can help, but they are not required. Our guide to ecommerce discount code strategy explains how to use discounts without training customers to wait for them.

Native features or an app?

Apps can add one-click post-purchase offers, bundle builders, frequently bought together widgets and more detailed rules. They also add monthly costs, extra scripts and another thing to maintain.

Basket add-onsOften, depending on themeYour theme's cart has no recommendation option

Every app you add can affect page speed. Check the impact before and after installing, and remove apps you do not use. Our guides to auditing Shopify apps and Shopify speed optimisation explain how.

Measuring whether it works

The obvious measure is average order value, but it should never be looked at alone. If average order value rises while conversion rate falls, you may be making fewer, bigger sales and losing money overall. Track:

  • Average order value.
  • Conversion rate.
  • Revenue per visitor, which combines the two.
  • How often recommended items are added, if your theme or app reports it.
  • Return rates on cross-sold items, which can reveal poor matches.

Make one change at a time and give it enough traffic and time to judge fairly. Seasonal swings can easily look like the effect of a new offer. Our guide to Shopify conversion rate optimisation explains how to test changes properly.

Choosing pairings for your catalogue

The strongest pairings usually come from your own order history. Export recent orders and look for products that are often bought together, then check them against what your customer service team hears: the questions people ask, the accessories they come back for and the items they return because something was missing. Write the best pairings down in a simple spreadsheet, review it each season and update it when ranges change, so recommendations stay as current as the catalogue itself.

Common mistakes

  • Showing the same generic bestsellers on every product, regardless of relevance.
  • Recommending out of stock items.
  • Pop-ups that interrupt adding to basket.
  • Too many apps doing similar jobs and slowing the store.
  • Offers that do not work properly on mobile.
  • Never reviewing pairings after the range changes.

FAQs

How do I set up Shopify upsells and cross-sells?

Start with your theme's product recommendations section and Shopify's search and discovery tools, which let you set related and complementary products. Use variants for size or spec upgrades, add basket suggestions if your theme supports them, and consider an app only for features such as post-purchase offers.

What is the difference between an upsell and a cross-sell?

An upsell suggests a better or bigger version of the item a shopper is looking at. A cross-sell suggests a different item that goes with it. Both aim to make the order more useful to the customer and more valuable to you.

Do I need an app for Shopify upsells?

Not always. Many themes and Shopify's own tools handle related and complementary products. Apps are most useful for post-purchase offers, complex bundles or detailed rules, and should be weighed against their cost and speed impact.

Where should I put cross-sells on a Shopify store?

Product pages, the basket or cart drawer and post-purchase pages are the main places. Keep product page suggestions closely related, use the basket for low-cost add-ons and keep offers few and relevant.

Can upsells hurt conversion rates?

Yes, if they distract, slow the page or feel pushy. Track conversion rate and revenue per visitor alongside average order value, so you can see whether an offer is helping overall.

How many product recommendations should I show?

Usually a small number, often two to four, is enough. Too many choices can slow decisions and push key buttons down the page, especially on mobile.
