Why WordPress suits businesses in Jordan

WordPress powers a large share of websites worldwide, and there are good reasons it suits Jordanian businesses:

  • Easy editing: your team can update pages, add news and change images without a developer.
  • Bilingual support: with the right setup and a multilingual plugin, English and Arabic versions can sit side by side.
  • SEO foundations: clean URLs, editable titles and descriptions, schema and sitemaps are straightforward to manage.
  • Flexibility: booking forms, course listings, property listings and online shops through WooCommerce.
  • Wide support: many developers in Jordan and abroad know WordPress, so you are never locked in.

For a tech-focused hub like Amman, with a strong start-up scene and many firms selling across the region, a well-built WordPress site is often the right balance of cost, control and growth potential.

Building a bilingual WordPress site

Many Jordanian businesses need both English and Arabic. A good bilingual WordPress setup includes:

  • A multilingual plugin that keeps each language on its own URLs, such as /ar/ for Arabic.
  • Hreflang tags so search engines show the right language to the right visitor.
  • Right-to-left layout support for Arabic pages, including menus, forms and tables.
  • Fonts that display Arabic clearly and load quickly.
  • Translated titles, descriptions and image alt text, not just body text.

We build the structure in English, and your team or a translator supplies the Arabic content. We then check that Arabic pages display and index correctly.

Speed, security and hosting

A slow or hacked WordPress site costs leads and damages trust. We keep sites lean by choosing a lightweight theme, avoiding plugin bloat, compressing images and using caching. For security, we use strong admin accounts, limited user roles, automatic backups and regular updates.

Hosting matters too. Pick a reliable host with good performance for your main audience, whether that is Jordan, the Gulf or Europe, and a content delivery network if your visitors are spread across regions. For more detail, read our guides to WordPress security, WordPress hosting and running a WordPress plugin audit. The advice applies wherever your business is based.

Choosing a WordPress agency in Jordan

When comparing agencies, ask:

  • Will you own the domain, hosting account and admin logins?
  • Which theme and plugins will be used, and why?
  • How will Arabic and English versions be handled?
  • What training and support come after launch?
  • Is the agency local or remote, and how will communication work?

We are open about being a remote, UK-based studio. Many Jordanian businesses work happily with remote teams, especially when the process is clear and communication is regular.

How a WordPress project runs with a remote studio

Working with a remote studio is simple when the process is clear. A typical WordPress project with us runs like this:

  1. Kick-off call: we talk through goals, audience, competitors, pages and languages.
  2. Sitemap and wireframes: we share the page structure and rough layouts for your feedback.
  3. Design: we design the key pages, usually the home page, a service page and a contact page, then agree changes.
  4. Content: you supply text, images and any Arabic translations, or we write English copy where agreed.
  5. Development: we build the site on a staging link so you can review it before launch.
  6. Testing: we check mobile layouts, forms, speed, right-to-left pages and basic SEO.
  7. Launch and handover: we go live, submit the sitemap and train your team.

Most communication happens over video calls, email and shared documents. Jordan's working day overlaps well with UK hours, so arranging calls is usually easy.

Common WordPress problems we fix

Many Jordanian businesses already have a WordPress site that is not performing. Common issues include:

  • Slow loading caused by heavy page builders, large images or too many plugins.
  • Outdated themes and plugins that create security risks.
  • Arabic pages that are not indexed or that show the wrong language in search.
  • Broken contact forms that quietly stop sending enquiries.
  • Missing titles, descriptions and headings, which hold back SEO.

Sometimes a careful clean-up is enough. Other times a fresh, lighter build is better value in the long run. We will tell you honestly which makes more sense for your site and budget.
