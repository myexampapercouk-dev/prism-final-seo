Shopify Agency Los Angeles: Remote Shopify Builds for LA Brands

Need a Shopify agency in Los Angeles? A remote studio can build, migrate and improve your store. See how it works, what it costs and what to ask before hiring.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify agency for a Los Angeles brand should be judged on store structure, speed, product data and conversion, not on having a studio in LA.
  • Most Shopify work, from theme builds to migrations and app audits, runs well remotely with a clear brief and a private preview store.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US brands; prices are in pounds and other currencies are quoted on request.

If you are looking for a Shopify agency in Los Angeles, the most important question is whether the agency can build a fast, well-organised store that turns visitors into customers, not whether it has a studio in Silver Lake or Santa Monica. Shopify is a hosted platform, so design, development, migration and optimisation work can all be done remotely without any loss of quality.

We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US brands. We do not have a Los Angeles office or any office in the US. This guide covers what a Shopify agency should do for an LA brand, how a remote project runs, what it costs and what to check before you hire anyone.

What a Shopify agency in Los Angeles should do

Los Angeles is home to a huge number of direct-to-consumer brands, especially in fashion, beauty, wellness, food and drink, homeware and creative products. Many sell nationally from day one, so the store has to work as hard for a buyer in Ohio as for one in Echo Park. A capable Shopify partner should be able to help with:

  • Store set-up and theme builds: configuring a theme properly or building custom sections that fit your brand.
  • Collection and navigation structure: organising products so shoppers and search engines can find them.
  • Product pages: clear photography, descriptions, variants, size guides and reviews.
  • Checkout, shipping and tax settings: set up correctly for US customers and any other markets you sell to.
  • Apps: choosing only the apps you need and removing those that slow the store down.
  • Migrations: moving from WooCommerce, Etsy, BigCommerce or another platform without losing data or rankings.
  • Speed and conversion: finding and fixing what stops people from buying.
  • Ongoing support: updates, new features and seasonal campaign pages.

Our website and ecommerce development service covers all of these, and our ecommerce sector page explains how we approach online retail more broadly.

How remote Shopify work runs from the UK

Los Angeles is usually eight hours behind UK time, and the gap shifts briefly in spring and autumn when the clocks change on different dates. In practice that means we hold calls early in the LA morning, which is late afternoon for us, and do most of the build work while you are offline. You often wake up to finished work ready for review.

  1. Discovery: we review your current store or plans, products, customers, apps and goals.
  2. Scope and quote: a fixed scope, price and timeline in writing.
  3. Structure: collections, navigation, filters and key templates are agreed.
  4. Design and build: we work on an unpublished theme or development store, so your live shop keeps trading.
  5. Review: you test the preview, leave comments and approve each stage.
  6. Launch: we publish at a quiet time, check orders, payments and tracking, and watch for issues.

Shopify staff accounts let us work without you sharing your own password, and you can remove access at any time. Our guide to Shopify staff accounts and permissions explains how to set that up safely.

Shopify costs for Los Angeles brands

Our prices are published in pounds sterling. An ecommerce website typically costs £1,500 to £3,500, depending on the number of products, custom sections, integrations and whether data is being migrated. Larger or more complex builds are quoted individually. Other currencies can be quoted on request. If you want ongoing marketing alongside the store, our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, with any ad spend paid separately.

You will also have Shopify's own subscription and any paid app or theme fees, which are paid directly to Shopify and app developers. Full details of our packages are on the pricing page.

How long a Shopify project takes

An ecommerce build usually takes five to twelve weeks from agreed brief to launch. A simple store with a configured theme and a modest catalogue sits at the shorter end. A migration with thousands of products, customer accounts, redirects and custom features sits at the longer end. Clean product data and early decisions on collections are the two things that keep projects on schedule.

It helps to plan launches around your trading calendar. Many LA brands lean heavily on autumn and holiday sales, so we usually advise launching a new store or theme well before your busiest weeks rather than during them. That leaves time to fix small issues, test discount codes and check that order emails, shipping rates and stock levels all behave as expected under real traffic.

Migrating to Shopify without losing sales

Many LA brands start on another platform and move to Shopify as they grow. A careful migration covers products, variants, customers, order history where needed, blog content and, most importantly, URL redirects so that search rankings and bookmarked links still work. We map every important old URL to its new home before launch. Our guide to moving from WooCommerce to Shopify walks through the steps, and the principles apply to other platforms too.

If you are already on Shopify and outgrowing it, Shopify Plus may be worth considering. We handle Shopify Plus migrations as well, and our guide to Shopify Plus migration explains when the upgrade makes sense.

Speed, apps and conversion

A common pattern in growing stores is a pile of apps added over time, each slowing the site a little. An app audit often finds tools that are no longer used, overlapping features and leftover code from apps that were uninstalled. Our Shopify apps audit guide shows what to look for. After that, the biggest conversion wins usually come from clearer product pages, better filtering, honest shipping information and a smoother path to checkout. Our Shopify conversion rate optimisation article goes into detail.

Tax, privacy and compliance

US sales tax rules vary by state and depend on where you have a tax obligation, so set-up should follow advice from your accountant or tax adviser. California also has its own privacy law, and accessibility expectations apply to US stores. We configure Shopify's settings and build to accessibility good practice, but we do not give legal or tax advice. Check current official guidance and speak to your own professional advisers about what applies to your business.

Questions to ask any Shopify agency

  • Will you work on a preview theme so our live store keeps trading?
  • How will you handle redirects and SEO if we are migrating?
  • Which apps do you plan to add, and what will they cost each month?
  • Who owns the theme code and any custom sections you build?
  • How many rounds of revisions are included?
  • What support do you offer after launch?

FAQs

Do you have a Shopify agency office in Los Angeles?

No. We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US brands. We do not have a Los Angeles office or any US office. Calls run by video, usually in the LA morning.

How much does a Shopify store cost with your studio?

An ecommerce website typically costs £1,500 to £3,500, depending on scope. Prices are in pounds, and other currencies can be quoted on request. Shopify and app fees are paid separately.

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Los Angeles?

Look for a clear written scope, work on preview themes, a sensible approach to apps, a redirect plan for migrations, ownership of the code in your name and support after launch. Location matters far less than process.

Can you migrate our store to Shopify without losing rankings?

We map old URLs to new ones and set up redirects before launch, which is the main way to protect existing search visibility. Some short-term movement is normal after any migration.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Usually five to twelve weeks from agreed brief to launch, depending on the size of the catalogue, any migration and the number of custom features.

Do you handle US sales tax set-up?

We configure Shopify's tax settings to match the instructions you give us, but sales tax obligations depend on your circumstances, so please confirm the set-up with your accountant or tax adviser.
