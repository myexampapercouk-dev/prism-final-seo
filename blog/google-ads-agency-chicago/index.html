Google Ads Agency Chicago: How Remote PPC Management Works

Searching for a Google Ads agency in Chicago? Remote PPC management can target Chicago buyers precisely. Here is how it works, the cost and what to check first.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Google Ads agency for a Chicago business needs accurate tracking, tight location targeting and disciplined search terms, not a downtown office.
  • Location settings, negative keywords and conversion tracking do more for results than any amount of extra budget.
  • We are a UK-based studio managing campaigns remotely for US clients; management fees are in pounds and ad spend is paid directly to Google.

If you are looking for a Google Ads agency in Chicago, judge it on how it tracks results, targets the right people and controls wasted spend, because those decide whether your budget turns into customers. A remote agency can run Google Ads for Chicago businesses just as effectively as a local one, since every part of campaign management happens inside the Google Ads platform and your analytics.

We are a UK-based studio that manages paid media remotely for US clients. We do not have a Chicago office or any office in the US. This guide explains what good Google Ads management looks like for a Chicago business, how a remote arrangement works, what it costs and the questions to ask before you hand over your account.

What a Google Ads agency in Chicago should do

Chicago businesses advertise for very different reasons. A law firm in the Loop wants enquiries from a tight area, a manufacturer in the suburbs wants national B2B leads, and an online retailer wants sales from anywhere in the country. Good management adapts to the goal, but the core work is consistent:

  • Conversion tracking: recording calls, forms, bookings and sales accurately before spending meaningful budget.
  • Account structure: campaigns and ad groups organised around services, products and locations.
  • Keyword strategy: choosing search terms with real buying intent and sensible match types.
  • Negative keywords: blocking searches that waste money, reviewed regularly.
  • Location targeting: reaching people in the areas you actually serve.
  • Ads and assets: clear, relevant ads with sitelinks, callouts and call assets.
  • Landing pages: pages that match the ad and make the next step easy.
  • Bidding: choosing a strategy that suits your data and goals.
  • Reporting: plain-English reports tied to leads and revenue, not just clicks.

Our paid media service covers Google Ads alongside Microsoft Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube, so channels can be planned together rather than in isolation.

Location targeting for Chicago campaigns

Location settings are one of the most common sources of waste in Google Ads. By default, campaigns can show ads to people who are interested in a location as well as people who are actually in it, which may not suit a business that only serves local customers. For a Chicago service business, it is usually better to target people present in or regularly in your chosen area.

Chicago also covers a wide metro area, and many firms serve some suburbs but not others. You can target by city, ZIP code or a radius, and adjust bids for areas that perform better. Our Google Ads location targeting guide explains the settings step by step.

Tracking comes first

Without reliable conversion tracking, smart bidding has nothing useful to learn from and reports cannot tell you what is working. Before we scale any account, we check that form submissions, calls and purchases are recorded properly, ideally with Google Tag Manager and GA4 in place. Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking set-up covers the essentials, and enhanced conversions can improve measurement further where privacy rules and your consent set-up allow.

How remote Google Ads management works

Chicago is usually six hours behind UK time, with a short period in spring and autumn when the gap changes because the clocks move on different dates. We hold calls in your morning, and most optimisation work happens on our side of the day. Google Ads itself does not care where the manager sits.

  1. Audit or set-up: we review your existing account or build a new one from scratch.
  2. Tracking check: conversions are verified before budgets increase.
  3. Launch: campaigns go live with agreed budgets, locations and schedules.
  4. Weekly optimisation: search terms, bids, ads and budgets are reviewed and adjusted.
  5. Monthly reporting: results against your goals, with clear next steps.

You keep ownership of the Google Ads account and billing. We are given manager access, which you can remove at any time.

Google Ads costs for Chicago businesses

There are two separate costs: what you pay Google for clicks, and what you pay an agency to manage the account. Click costs depend on your industry, competition and how well your account is run, so we will not guess at a figure here. Competitive sectors such as legal and home services tend to be more expensive than niche B2B terms, and a tightly managed account usually wastes less of whatever budget you set.

Our management retainers are priced in pounds: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Ad spend is always paid separately and directly to Google. Other currencies can be quoted on request, and the details are on our pricing page. Paid search often shows early signals within weeks, but results typically settle over three to six months as data builds and the account is refined.

Ad schedules, time zones and landing pages

Because your campaigns run in Chicago time, schedules should be set in the account's own time zone and matched to when your team can answer calls. There is little point paying for call clicks at nine in the evening if nobody picks up until morning. Ad scheduling lets you show call assets only during staffed hours while still running text ads that drive form enquiries around the clock.

The page a visitor lands on matters just as much as the ad. A searcher looking for a specific service in a specific area should arrive on a page about that service, with clear contact options and reasons to trust you, rather than on a general homepage. Small changes to headlines, forms and calls to action often lift conversion rates without any increase in spend. Where landing pages need rebuilding, we can design and build them alongside the campaign work so the ad and the page tell the same story.

Stopping wasted spend

Most underperforming accounts we review share the same few problems: broad keywords with no negative list, location settings left on defaults, Performance Max or Display running without clear goals, and conversions that count page views or duplicate actions. A regular search terms review is the single most useful habit. Our negative keywords guide shows how to build and maintain a list, and our PPC audit checklist lists the wider checks worth running on any account.

B2B Google Ads in Chicago

Chicago has a strong base of manufacturing, logistics, professional services and technology firms. B2B campaigns need a different approach from consumer ones: lower search volumes, longer sales cycles and enquiries that may take months to become revenue. Importing offline conversions from your CRM, where possible, helps Google learn which leads become customers. If you are weighing up channels, our comparison of LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads for B2B may help.

If you also need organic visibility in the city, our guide to SEO for Chicago businesses explains how search engine optimisation and paid search can support each other.

Compliance and advertising rules

Google has its own advertising policies, and some sectors such as legal, financial, health and some home services may face additional verification or restrictions. US federal and state rules on advertising claims and privacy also apply. We follow Google's policies when building campaigns, but approval of claims and wording in regulated sectors rests with your own compliance team or lawyer, and you should check current official guidance.

FAQs

Do you have a Google Ads agency office in Chicago?

No. We are a UK-based studio and we manage campaigns remotely for US clients. We do not have a Chicago office or any office in the US.

How do I choose a Google Ads agency in Chicago?

Ask how they set up conversion tracking, how often they review search terms, how they handle location targeting, who owns the account and what their reports include. A clear answer to each matters more than the agency's address.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month depending on scope, priced in pounds. Ad spend is paid separately and directly to Google. Other currencies can be quoted on request.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

Yes. The account and billing stay in your name. We work through manager access, which you can remove whenever you choose.

How quickly will Google Ads bring leads?

Ads can start getting clicks soon after launch, but results typically take three to six months to settle as the account gathers data and is optimised.

Can you target only certain Chicago suburbs?

Yes. Campaigns can target specific cities, ZIP codes or a radius, and exclude areas you do not serve, with bids adjusted for the areas that perform best.
