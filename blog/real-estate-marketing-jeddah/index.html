Real Estate Marketing in Jeddah: Property Websites, SEO and Lead Generation

Real estate marketing in Jeddah: property websites, SEO and Google and Meta ads for developers and brokers, from a UK-based remote studio, no local office.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 13 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Real estate marketing in Jeddah works when a fast listings website, project landing pages, SEO and targeted ads all lead buyers and tenants to a quick enquiry.
  • Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that markets Jeddah developers and brokers remotely and has no office in Jeddah or anywhere in Saudi Arabia.
  • Smaller websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and retainers start at £350 a month; riyal pricing is quoted on request and Arabic copy comes from your team or a translator.

Real estate marketing in Jeddah means putting your projects and listings in front of serious buyers, tenants and investors, then turning their interest into enquiries your sales team can follow up. Prism Works Studio builds property websites and runs SEO and paid campaigns for developers, brokers and property managers in Jeddah, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in Jeddah or anywhere in Saudi Arabia, and we work through video calls, shared previews and written reports. We write in English; Arabic pages, brochures and ad copy are written with your team or a translator, and we build everything around that copy.

Who we work with in Jeddah

Jeddah's property market spans waterfront towers, family villas, compounds, new master-planned communities and commercial space, with demand from Saudi families, expatriate residents and investors. We support:

  • Developers launching residential, mixed-use or waterfront projects.
  • Brokers and agencies with sales and rental listings across the city.
  • Property managers and compound operators filling units and handling enquiries.
  • Commercial property firms letting offices, retail and warehouse space.
  • Furnished apartment operators seeking longer stays.

If you also sell in the capital, our guide to real estate marketing in Riyadh covers that market.

A property website that captures leads

Buyers compare projects quickly and leave if they cannot find the basics. We build property websites with:

  • Project landing pages with location, unit types, floor plans, payment plan details your team confirms and a clear enquiry form.
  • Listing pages with good photos, key facts and a WhatsApp button on mobile.
  • District guides covering areas your buyers ask about, written in plain language.
  • Short forms that ask only what your sales team needs, so more people finish them.
  • Lead routing so enquiries reach the right person fast, through email or your CRM.
  • Fast pages, because most property searches happen on phones.

Our guide to landing page design explains how to build pages that convert, and the website development service page shows how a build runs. For the wider local view, see our page on web design in Jeddah. If you run on WordPress, our page on WordPress in Saudi Arabia covers platform choices.

SEO for Jeddah property businesses

Property searches are specific: "apartments for sale North Jeddah", "villa for rent Al Shati", "off-plan projects Jeddah" or a project's own name. SEO helps your pages show up for those searches instead of only the big portals. Our work covers:

  • Fixing technical issues such as slow listing pages, duplicate pages and expired listings that still appear in search.
  • Building project and district pages around real search demand.
  • Adding structured data so search engines understand your listings and business.
  • Keeping your Google Business Profile accurate for each office you actually have.
  • Writing clear answers to common buyer questions that AI search tools can quote.

See our guide to SEO in Jeddah for more. SEO typically takes 3 to 6 months to show steady results, so many developers run ads alongside it for launches.

Paid ads for launches and listings

Paid ads let you control timing, which matters for project launches and sales events. We set up:

  • Google Search campaigns for project names, districts and property types, sending people to the matching landing page.
  • Meta campaigns on Instagram and Facebook with lead forms or website enquiries, aimed at the right areas and audiences.
  • TikTok and YouTube Ads using renders and clips your team or a video supplier provides. We do not film or edit video, but we plan, write, target and test the campaigns.
  • Lead tracking so you can see which campaigns bring real enquiries, not just clicks.

Our paid media service page explains how we run and report on campaigns, and our page on TikTok ads in Saudi Arabia covers that platform. Ad spend is paid separately.

Arabic and English content

Most Jeddah buyers prefer Arabic, while expatriate tenants and some investors search in English. We write in English, and your team or a professional translator writes the Arabic. We build right-to-left pages, a language switcher and separate page addresses for each language. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website explains the details.

Rules and responsibilities

Property advertising in Saudi Arabia is regulated, and rules can cover licensing details in ads, how projects and prices are described and how personal data is handled. We keep our advice general. Your team should check official guidance from the relevant authorities and speak to its own adviser, and your company approves every ad and page before it goes live. We include licence numbers and other required details exactly as you supply them.

Common problems we fix

Property websites often lose leads in the same places. Project pages are slow because of huge images. Enquiry forms ask for too much. WhatsApp buttons are missing on mobile. Expired listings stay live and frustrate buyers. Ads send everyone to the homepage. Leads land in a shared inbox and wait too long for a reply. We fix these first, then build campaigns on a solid base.

What to measure each month

Property marketing should be judged by qualified leads and viewings, not only by clicks. We set up tracking in the first weeks and report each month on:

  • Enquiries, calls and WhatsApp clicks from each project and listing page.
  • Lead form submissions from Meta and TikTok campaigns.
  • Search visibility for project names, districts and property types.
  • Cost per lead for each campaign and each project.
  • Response times, where your CRM or inbox makes them visible.

Your sales team adds what only it can judge: which leads were serious, which booked viewings and which bought or signed. We use that feedback to move budget towards the campaigns and audiences that bring better buyers.

Planning a project launch

A new project launch needs careful timing. We usually start with a holding page and a register-interest form a few weeks before launch, so early buyers can join a list. Search and social campaigns then build that list while your team prepares prices, plans and approvals. On launch day, the full project page goes live with floor plans, unit types and an enquiry form, and campaigns switch from awareness to enquiries. After launch, we keep the page updated as units sell, so buyers always see accurate information.

Choosing the right first step

Not every property business needs every channel at once. If your website is slow or your project pages are thin, we usually start there, because every ad and search result leads back to them. If the site is sound, we often begin with Google Search campaigns for your active projects, fix your Google Business Profile and then build SEO district pages month by month. Meta and TikTok campaigns are usually added for launches and sales events, when fresh creative is ready. We agree priorities on the first call, based on your budget, your sales targets and your team's capacity to follow up leads quickly. Fast follow-up matters, because property buyers often contact several developers in the same week.
