Shopify Agency for San Francisco Brands: Store Builds, Migrations and Growth

Shopify agency for San Francisco brands: store design, Shopify Plus migrations, SEO and ads for Bay Area sellers, from a UK-based studio that works remotely.

  • Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that builds and grows Shopify stores remotely for Bay Area brands and has no office in San Francisco or anywhere in the United States.

A Shopify agency in San Francisco should build a store that looks right for your brand, loads fast, ranks for the products you sell and makes checkout simple. Prism Works Studio designs, builds and grows Shopify stores for San Francisco and Bay Area brands, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in San Francisco, California or anywhere in the United States. We run projects through video calls, shared design files and written updates, and we schedule meetings to suit Pacific time.

Who we work with in the Bay Area

San Francisco and the wider Bay Area, from Oakland and Berkeley to San Jose and Palo Alto, are home to many direct-to-consumer brands and product companies. We work with:

  • DTC brands in apparel, beauty, wellness, home goods and food and drink.
  • Founder-led startups launching their first product online.
  • Established retailers moving from another platform to Shopify.
  • Growing brands that are outgrowing their theme or considering Shopify Plus.
  • Technology companies selling hardware or accessories alongside software.

Store design that fits your brand

Bay Area shoppers see a lot of well-designed brands, so your store needs to feel considered. We start with your brand, products and customers, then plan the layout around how people actually shop: browsing collections, comparing products, checking reviews and sizing, and moving to checkout.

Most stores start from a well-built theme that we customise rather than a fully custom build. That keeps costs sensible and makes future updates easier. Our guide to Shopify theme customisation explains how far a theme can be taken.

We also plan collection structure carefully, because it affects both navigation and SEO. Our guide to Shopify collections sets out the approach.

Migrations and Shopify Plus

Moving platform is one of the riskiest moments for an online store. Product data, customer accounts, order history and years of search rankings all need to move safely. We map every URL to its new home and set up redirects so you keep as much search visibility as possible.

For larger brands, Shopify Plus adds more control over checkout, automation and multiple storefronts. Our Shopify Plus migration guide covers when it makes sense and how we plan the move. If you are coming from WooCommerce on WordPress, we follow the same redirect-first approach, checking product URLs, categories, customer records and blog posts before anything goes live.

Shopify SEO for Bay Area brands

Organic search can become one of the most cost-effective channels for an online store, but Shopify needs some care to rank well. We focus on:

  • Collection pages built around the terms shoppers actually search.
  • Unique product descriptions rather than copied manufacturer text.
  • Product structured data so prices, availability and reviews can show in results.
  • Fixing duplicate URLs and thin tag pages.
  • Fast-loading templates and images.
  • Helpful guides and blog content that link to key collections.

Our ecommerce SEO guide for US DTC brands goes deeper on strategy, and our Shopify speed optimisation guide covers performance.

Paid growth: Google, Meta, TikTok and YouTube

Once your store converts well, paid ads can scale traffic. We manage Google Shopping and Performance Max for product searches, and Meta, TikTok and YouTube campaigns for discovery and remarketing. We do not film or edit video, so video ads use footage and creative your team or a content partner supplies. We focus on targeting, structure, budgets, feeds and measurement.

Our guide to Meta Ads for US ecommerce explains how we plan social campaigns for online stores.

Apps, integrations and operations

A Shopify store sits at the center of many other tools. Bay Area brands often connect their store to email and SMS platforms, review apps, subscription tools, inventory and fulfilment systems, accounting software and customer support desks. Each app adds features, but each one can also add page weight, cost and complexity.

During planning we review every app you use or are considering and ask three questions: does it do something the store genuinely needs, does Shopify now handle it natively, and does it slow the storefront down? Our Shopify apps audit guide explains the process in more detail. Removing unused apps is one of the simplest ways to improve speed and reduce monthly costs.

We also make sure integrations are set up so data flows cleanly. Orders should reach your fulfilment partner, stock levels should stay accurate, and customer data should sync to your email platform with the right consent settings. Problems in these areas are often invisible until a busy sales period, so we test them before launch.

Common Shopify problems we fix

Many brands come to us with a store that works but is not performing as well as it could. The issues we see most often are:

  • Slow product pages caused by large images, heavy themes or too many apps.
  • Confusing navigation that makes it hard to find the right collection.
  • Thin product descriptions that do not answer questions about sizing, materials, care or delivery.
  • Duplicate content from tag pages, variant URLs or copied supplier text.
  • Weak mobile checkout journeys, such as hidden shipping costs or extra steps.
  • Missing or broken tracking, which makes it hard to judge ad performance.

We start with an audit, explain each issue in plain English and agree which fixes will have the most impact before we begin.

Our process and timelines

  1. Discovery: we learn about your brand, products, customers and goals.
  2. Planning: we agree the theme, structure, apps and data that need to move.
  3. Design: we design key templates for your review.
  4. Build: we set up the store, products, collections, shipping and tax settings, analytics and SEO.
  5. Testing: we test checkout, mobile layouts, speed and redirects.
  6. Launch and support: we go live and help with the first weeks of trading.

Ecommerce builds typically take 5 to 12 weeks depending on catalog size, integrations and migration work. We write store copy in English. If you need storefronts in Spanish, Chinese or another language, that content comes from your team or a translator, and we handle the technical setup.

Legal and practical notes

US online stores need to think about sales tax, privacy and accessibility. Shopify includes tax tools, but the right settings for your business should be confirmed by your accountant. California has its own privacy rules, and accessibility expectations apply to many US websites. We follow good practice in design and build, but we do not give legal or tax advice, so please check official guidance and speak to your own adviser.
