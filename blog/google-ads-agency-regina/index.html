How we build Google Ads campaigns for Regina

Good campaigns start with structure. Instead of one broad campaign covering everything, we build focused campaigns around the services that matter most to your business:

  • Keyword research based on how people in Regina actually search, including local phrases and neighbourhood names.
  • Tight ad groups so each search sees an ad that closely matches what they typed.
  • Negative keywords to stop budget going on irrelevant searches, such as jobs or DIY questions.
  • Location targeting around Regina and any nearby communities you serve, such as White City or Moose Jaw.
  • Ad scheduling so ads run when you can answer the phone or respond quickly.
  • Ad assets such as call buttons, location details and links to key pages.

Tracking that shows what is working

Without proper tracking, it is impossible to know which keywords and ads bring real enquiries. We set up:

  • Conversion tracking for form submissions, bookings and purchases.
  • Call tracking for calls from ads and from your website.
  • Google Analytics 4 linked to your Google Ads account.
  • Enhanced conversions where your setup allows it.

Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking walks through the setup in more detail. With good data, we can cut spend on what does not work and move it to what does.

Landing pages that turn clicks into enquiries

Many Google Ads accounts lose money because clicks go to a slow, general home page. A focused landing page usually performs better. We build or improve pages that:

  • Match the search and the ad closely.
  • Load quickly on mobile.
  • Show your phone number, service area and main benefits clearly.
  • Have a short form or a clear call button.
  • Include trust signals you genuinely have, such as licences, guarantees or real reviews.

  • Performance Max can work for ecommerce and some lead generation, once conversion tracking is reliable.
  • Shopping campaigns suit retailers with a product feed.
  • YouTube ads can build awareness using clips you supply. Prism does not film or edit video.

We recommend these only when they fit your goals and budget, not by default.

Privacy, consent and follow-up messages

Google Ads campaigns often collect personal information through forms and calls, and many businesses follow up by email or text. In Canada, a few rules apply at a general level:

  • PIPEDA is the federal privacy law that applies to many private-sector businesses handling personal information in commercial activity.
  • CASL sets rules for commercial electronic messages, including consent and unsubscribe requirements.
  • Some sectors, such as health and finance, have extra provincial or professional rules on advertising.

We set up consent banners and clear form wording, but compliance decisions rest with your business. In regulated sectors, your own compliance team or adviser should approve ad copy and landing pages. We recommend official guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the CRTC.

Common Google Ads mistakes we fix

When we review accounts for Regina businesses, the same issues come up again and again. Each one quietly wastes budget:

  • Broad match keywords with no negatives. Ads show for loosely related searches that never turn into customers.
  • Location settings that are too wide. Ads reach people interested in Regina from far away, rather than people actually in your service area.
  • No conversion tracking, or tracking that counts the wrong actions. Page views or clicks on a menu are counted as leads, so automated bidding chases the wrong thing.
  • One campaign for every service. Budget flows to the cheapest clicks rather than the most valuable services.
  • Ads running overnight when nobody can answer calls or reply to enquiries.
  • Accepting every automated recommendation in the Google Ads interface without checking whether it suits your goals.

Fixing these basics often improves cost per enquiry before any increase in spend. We explain each change in our reports, so you understand why it was made.

Seasonal demand in Saskatchewan

Demand in Regina changes noticeably through the year. Furnace and heating searches rise as temperatures drop, roofing and landscaping searches grow in spring, and many retailers see peaks around the holidays. We adjust budgets, ad copy and bids ahead of these shifts, rather than reacting after the busy period has started. For businesses that serve rural customers across the province, we can also plan wider location targeting during the seasons when those customers are most active, while keeping the core Regina campaigns focused.
