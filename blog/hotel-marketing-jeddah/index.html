Why hotel marketing in Jeddah is different

Jeddah serves several very different kinds of traveller at once. Pilgrims pass through on their way to Makkah and Madinah for Umrah and Hajj. Business travellers visit for trade, logistics and the port. Families come for the Corniche, the Red Sea coast and the historic Al Balad district. Event visitors arrive for concerts, motorsport and festivals. Each group searches differently, books on a different timeline and cares about different details.

A pilgrim group organiser cares about transfer options, prayer facilities and room configurations. A business guest wants fast Wi-Fi, meeting rooms and easy airport access. A family wants pools, beach access and connecting rooms. Your website and campaigns should speak clearly to the guests you most want, rather than trying to say everything to everyone.

Seasonality matters too. Ramadan, the Hajj season, school holidays and major events can all change demand sharply. Good hotel marketing plans for these peaks months in advance and uses quieter periods to build search visibility for the next one.

The direct booking challenge

Online travel agencies bring reach, but they charge commission on every booking and keep much of the guest relationship. Most hotels will always use them to some degree. The aim is not to abandon them, but to win a healthier share of bookings through your own website, where you control the guest experience and keep more of the room rate.

Guests often find a hotel on a travel agency or on Google, then visit the hotel's own website to check details before deciding where to book. If your site is slow, confusing or lacks the information they want, they go back and book elsewhere. If it is fast, clear and offers a simple reason to book direct, many will stay.

A hotel website built to convert

Your website is the one place where you control every detail of the guest's first impression. We design and build hotel websites that load quickly on phones, show rooms and facilities clearly, and keep the booking button visible at all times. Each room type gets its own page with real descriptions, not just a photo gallery. Facilities such as restaurants, pools, spas and meeting rooms get their own pages too, so they can appear in search.

We work with your existing booking engine rather than replacing it, and we make the handover from website to booking engine as smooth as possible. Our website and ecommerce development service covers the design and build, and our guide to landing page design explains the principles we use for campaign pages.

For a bilingual Arabic and English site, we handle right-to-left layouts and language versions carefully so that search engines show the right version to the right guest. Our article on multilingual website design explains the approach.

SEO for Jeddah hotels

Most hotels rank for their own name and little else. That leaves a lot of searches on the table: people looking for hotels near the Corniche, near Al Balad, near King Abdulaziz International Airport, with sea views, for families, or suitable as a stop before Umrah. Each of those searches is a chance for a well-built page to appear.

Through our SEO and GEO service we research the searches that matter to your property, build helpful pages around them and fix technical issues that hold your site back. We also make sure your content is clear and structured enough to be cited by AI search tools, which travellers increasingly use to plan trips. Our SEO agency guide for Jeddah covers the local search landscape in more depth.

Your Google Business Profile matters as much as your website. Accurate categories, current photos, amenities, and polite, prompt replies to reviews all help you appear in map results. Our Google Business Profile guide explains the essentials, and the platform works the same way in Saudi Arabia.

Paid campaigns for hotels

Paid search lets you appear when travellers search for hotels in Jeddah, including searches for your own hotel name where travel agencies may be bidding against you. We set up and manage Google Ads campaigns that target the right dates, locations and traveller types, and we send clicks to pages built to convert rather than to a generic homepage.

Meta campaigns on Instagram and Facebook work well for building awareness and promoting packages, such as family summer stays, Ramadan offers, staycations or event weekends. We can target travellers in Riyadh, the Eastern Province and wider GCC markets, as well as international audiences where you have demand. Through our paid media service we plan budgets, structure campaigns and report on bookings rather than just clicks. Our article on Meta Ads in Saudi Arabia covers targeting in the Kingdom.

Prism does not film or edit video. Where campaigns use video, your team or a creator supplies the footage and we handle the advertising side.

Packages, offers and seasonal planning

Direct booking offers give guests a reason to book on your website. Common examples include a free breakfast, flexible cancellation, a room upgrade when available, late checkout or a welcome amenity. We help you present these clearly across the site and in campaigns, without cluttering the booking path.

We plan content and campaigns around the year: Ramadan and Eid, the Hajj season, school holidays, national occasions and major events in the city. Seasonal landing pages are built ahead of demand so they have time to be indexed, then refreshed each year rather than replaced. Marketing to pilgrims and religious travellers should be handled respectfully and in line with official Saudi guidance, so check current rules with the relevant authorities and your own adviser. Compliance with local regulations rests with the hotel.

Measuring what matters

We set up analytics so you can see where website visitors come from, which pages they view and how many reach your booking engine. Where your booking engine supports it, we track completed bookings and revenue back to each channel. Each month you receive a clear report covering direct booking trends, search visibility, campaign performance and next steps.
