School Marketing in Dubai: Websites, SEO and Ads for Admissions

School marketing in Dubai: admissions websites, SEO, Google and Meta ads for private schools and nurseries, by a UK-based remote studio with no UAE office.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 13 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • School marketing in Dubai works when a clear admissions website, search visibility and well-timed ads help parents compare schools and book a visit or open day.
  • Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that markets Dubai private schools and nurseries remotely and has no office in Dubai or anywhere in the UAE.
  • Smaller websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and retainers start at £350 a month; dirham pricing is quoted on request and Arabic copy comes from your team or a translator.

School marketing in Dubai is about helping parents find your school, understand what makes it different and take the next step, usually a tour, an open day or an admissions enquiry. Prism Works Studio builds admissions-focused websites and runs SEO and paid campaigns for private schools, nurseries and education providers in Dubai, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We have no office in Dubai or anywhere in the UAE. We work through video calls, shared previews and written reports, timed around the school calendar. We write in English; Arabic and other language pages are written with your team or a translator, and we build everything around that copy.

Schools and education providers we support

Dubai has one of the most competitive private school markets anywhere, with many curricula and a large, mobile expatriate population. Parents often compare several schools before applying, sometimes while still living abroad. We work with:

  • Private schools offering British, American, IB, Indian and other curricula.
  • Nurseries and early years settings building waiting lists in their area.
  • School groups that need a shared approach across several campuses.
  • Tutoring centres and after-school providers filling classes each term.
  • Training and language institutes recruiting adult learners.

How Dubai parents choose a school

Choosing a school is a big decision, and parents research carefully. Many begin months before a move or a new academic year. They search for schools by curriculum, area, fees and age range, read inspection outcomes published by the regulator, ask other parents and then visit the shortlist. Your website and marketing need to support every stage, from first search to the visit booking.

Parents want facts: curriculum, year groups, fee ranges, transport, facilities, admissions steps and key dates. Schools that make this information easy to find tend to receive better quality enquiries. Our article on school marketing covers these principles in more depth.

Admissions-ready school websites

A school website serves many audiences, including current parents, staff and the wider community. For marketing, the most important visitors are prospective parents, and they should not have to dig. We put admissions front and centre with a clear path: learn about the school, check fit and fees, then book a tour or enquire.

We build pages for each phase or year group, each curriculum strand where relevant and each key admissions step. We link tour and enquiry forms to your admissions system or inbox, and we make sure the site works well on phones, since many parents research during the working day. Our guide to school website design explains our approach to layout and content, and our post on web design in Dubai covers the wider service in the city.

Many Dubai families speak languages other than English at home. If you need Arabic pages, our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites explains how we handle right-to-left layouts and separate language URLs. For other languages, our article on multilingual website design covers the options.

SEO for Dubai schools

Parents search in very specific ways, combining curriculum, area and age. A school that has a clear page for each of these combinations, where it genuinely fits, stands a better chance of appearing. We also care for your Google Business Profile so parents searching nearby see accurate details, photos and directions.

Our SEO work typically includes technical fixes, page structure, curriculum and phase pages, admissions content and local search. We keep inspection references factual and point parents to the regulator's own published reports rather than paraphrasing them. Our article on SEO in Dubai explains our wider approach. SEO usually takes three to six months to show clear results, so we start early in the admissions cycle.

Paid ads around the admissions calendar

Admissions in Dubai follow a rhythm. There are busy enquiry periods before each academic year, mid-year moves and relocation peaks. We plan paid campaigns around your calendar rather than running the same ads all year.

  • Google Ads for parents searching by curriculum, area or school type, linked to the matching page.
  • Meta Ads to promote open days and tours to parents in nearby communities.
  • LinkedIn Ads where it makes sense to reach professionals relocating with families.
  • Retargeting to remind parents who visited the admissions pages but did not enquire.

Advertising to parents needs care. We avoid targeting children and follow each platform's policies, and your school approves all wording and imagery. Our article on Google Ads in Dubai covers campaign set-up in detail. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms and is separate from our fees.

Working with a remote studio

We work remotely from the UK and do not visit schools. That suits most projects because the work is online, and we can align our calls with the Dubai working week. Your school remains responsible for anything that needs local knowledge or approval, including fee details, inspection references, Arabic content, photography of pupils and any permissions required by the education regulator. Keep regulatory notes general and check them against official guidance or your own adviser.

We use only the photos your school supplies, and your school confirms it has the right consents for any image that shows pupils. Prism does not film or edit video, so campaigns use creative you already have.

A typical first 90 days

Most schools come to us with an existing website and some advertising already running. A common first quarter looks like this:

  • Weeks 1 to 2: we review the website, admissions journey, search visibility and any ad accounts, and agree priorities with your admissions and marketing leads.
  • Weeks 2 to 6: we fix the most damaging technical issues, set up tracking for tour bookings and enquiries, and simplify the admissions pages.
  • Weeks 6 to 12: we add curriculum and phase pages, refresh your Google Business Profile and launch campaigns around your next open day or enquiry peak.

A new or rebuilt school website normally takes three to five weeks once content and photos are ready. Schools with large websites and many stakeholders often need longer for review and approvals, so we plan that time in from the start.

Common school marketing mistakes

When we review school marketing in Dubai, the same issues appear often. Fee information is hard to find or missing, which frustrates parents comparing several schools. Admissions forms are long and do not work well on phones. Open day pages are left up after the event has passed. Ads send parents to the home page rather than the page that matches their search. And pages for different curricula or phases are thin, so they rarely appear in search. We list these problems in a short written review and fix the most important ones first.

Measuring admissions enquiries

We track the actions that matter to your admissions team: tour bookings, open day registrations, enquiry forms and calls. Each month you receive a short report showing enquiries by channel, so you can see which campaigns bring families who go on to visit. We do not collect information about children through tracking, and we keep analytics set up in line with your school's own data policies.

FAQs

What does school marketing in Dubai include?

It usually includes an admissions-focused website, SEO and Google Business Profile care, and paid campaigns on Google, Meta and sometimes LinkedIn, all timed around your admissions calendar.

Do you have an office in Dubai?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely, with no office in Dubai or anywhere in the UAE.

Can you write Arabic content for our school website?

We write in English. Arabic and other language content comes from your team or a translator, and we build the pages around that copy.

Do you film school videos?

No. Prism does not film or edit video. We use photos and videos your school already has and has the consent to use.

Ideally several months before the main enquiry period. SEO takes three to six months to build, while paid ads can support open days at shorter notice.
