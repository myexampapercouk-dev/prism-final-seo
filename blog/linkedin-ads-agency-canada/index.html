LinkedIn Ads Agency for Canadian Businesses: How Remote B2B Campaigns Work

Looking for a LinkedIn Ads agency in Canada? How remote B2B campaigns work, targeting, lead forms, tracking, fees in pounds and what to ask before you sign.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • LinkedIn Ads suit Canadian B2B firms selling higher-value services, because targeting by job title, seniority and company can reach buyers search cannot.
  • Campaigns work best with a clear offer, reliable tracking through the Insight Tag and a plan for following up leads quickly.
  • Ad spend is paid to LinkedIn from your own account and is separate from management fees, which should be shown clearly before you begin.

A LinkedIn Ads agency for a Canadian business should help you reach the people who make or influence buying decisions, by job title, seniority, industry and company, and turn that attention into qualified leads at a cost that makes sense. Campaigns are built and managed inside your LinkedIn Campaign Manager account, so the work can be done remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that runs paid social for Canadian businesses online, and we have no office in Canada.

This guide explains when LinkedIn Ads are worth it for Canadian companies, how targeting and campaign formats work, how to handle English and French audiences, what a remote arrangement looks like, what management costs with us and what to ask any agency before you commit a budget.

When LinkedIn Ads make sense for Canadian businesses

LinkedIn clicks usually cost more than clicks on most other platforms, so the channel suits businesses where one new client is worth a meaningful amount. Typical examples include software and SaaS companies, IT and cyber security providers, consultancies, engineering and industrial suppliers, recruitment firms, financial and professional services, and education providers selling to working adults.

It is less suited to low-cost consumer products or local services where people search when they need help. In those cases, search and other social platforms usually give better value. Our comparison of LinkedIn Ads vs Google Ads for B2B sets out when each channel fits and when to combine them.

What a LinkedIn Ads agency in Canada should do

  • Define the audience precisely, using job functions, titles, seniority, company size and industry, then check the estimated audience size is workable.
  • Set up tracking with the LinkedIn Insight Tag and conversion events. Our Insight Tag setup guide explains the steps.
  • Build offers worth responding to, such as a consultation, demo, guide or event, rather than a generic advert.
  • Test creative and formats to find which messages and assets get qualified responses.
  • Report on lead quality, not just clicks and impressions, ideally with feedback from your sales team.

Our paid media service covers LinkedIn alongside Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta, TikTok and YouTube, so LinkedIn can sit within a wider plan.

Targeting Canadian audiences on LinkedIn

Canada is a large country with business clusters in very different places: technology and finance in Toronto, Waterloo region and Vancouver, energy and engineering in Calgary and Edmonton, government suppliers in Ottawa and a bilingual market in Montreal. Location targeting can be set to the whole country, specific provinces or metropolitan areas, depending on where you can sell and deliver.

Narrow targeting can make audiences too small to deliver efficiently, while very broad targeting wastes budget on people with no buying role. We usually start with a focused core audience, then test sensible expansions once we know which segments respond.

We also exclude groups that rarely buy, such as students, job seekers or your own employees, and review demographic reports regularly to see which job functions and seniority levels actually engage. Those reports often reveal surprises that shape the next round of targeting and creative.

Campaign formats and lead forms

LinkedIn offers several ad formats. Single image and video ads in the feed are the most common. Document ads let people preview a guide or report. Message ads appear in the inbox and suit event invitations and personal offers when used sparingly. Lead Gen Forms let people submit details without leaving LinkedIn, pre-filled from their profile.

Lead Gen Forms usually produce more leads than sending people to a website, but the quality can vary, so we often add a qualifying question and connect the form to your CRM or email platform for fast follow-up. Where a website visit matters more, we send traffic to a focused landing page instead. Our guide to landing page design covers what that page needs.

English and French campaigns

LinkedIn lets you target by profile language, which matters in Quebec and in bilingual markets such as Ottawa and parts of New Brunswick. Separate English and French campaigns, each with matching landing pages or forms, usually perform better than one mixed campaign. Prism writes in English; French ad copy and pages are written with your team or a professional translator.

Language, privacy and anti-spam rules can affect how you collect and follow up leads in Canada. Requirements change and vary by situation, so check current official guidance and take your own advice on consent wording and email follow-up.

Lead follow-up and sales alignment

LinkedIn campaigns are only as good as what happens after a lead arrives. B2B buyers often compare several suppliers, so a quick, relevant reply makes a real difference. We agree with you in advance who receives leads, how quickly they are contacted and which outcomes count as qualified, so reporting reflects real pipeline rather than form fills alone.

Nurturing matters too. Many people are not ready to talk on day one, so a short, useful email sequence can keep you in mind until they are. Our B2B sector page explains how we support longer sales cycles across channels.

How remote LinkedIn Ads management works

Canada spans six time zones, from Newfoundland to British Columbia. The UK is usually between three and a half and eight hours ahead, so calls are booked in the overlap between UK afternoons and Canadian mornings. Day-to-day work happens inside Campaign Manager, and we share changes and reports in writing.

  1. Discovery. We agree your ideal customer, offer and what counts as a qualified lead.
  2. Tracking. We install or check the Insight Tag and set up conversion events.
  3. Build. We create audiences, ads and forms in your account.
  4. Optimise. We test creative, refine audiences and adjust bids and budgets.
  5. Report. A monthly summary of spend, leads, lead quality and next steps.

What LinkedIn Ads management costs with Prism

Management is billed on monthly retainers priced in pounds sterling: Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Prices in Canadian dollars or other currencies can be quoted on request. Ad spend is paid directly to LinkedIn from your own account and is always separate from our fees. Full details are on our pricing page, and our guide to LinkedIn Ads for B2B lead generation explains budgeting principles in more depth.

LinkedIn campaigns usually need several months of testing to find the right audiences and messages. Results typically become clearer over three to six months as data builds and lead quality feedback comes in.

Questions to ask any LinkedIn Ads agency

  • Will the Campaign Manager account and Company Page access stay in my name?
  • How will you define and measure a qualified lead?
  • How are management fees separated from ad spend?
  • How will leads reach my team, and how quickly?
  • How do you plan English and French campaigns where needed?
  • What will the monthly report show, and who will explain it?

FAQs

How do I choose a LinkedIn Ads agency in Canada?

Choose an agency that asks about your ideal customer and sales process first, keeps the account in your name, sets up tracking properly and reports on lead quality rather than clicks alone. Ask how it would handle Canadian regions and French-speaking audiences.

Can a UK agency run LinkedIn Ads for a Canadian business?

Yes. LinkedIn campaigns are managed online through Campaign Manager. Prism has no Canadian office; we work remotely and schedule calls in the overlap between UK afternoons and Canadian mornings.

Are LinkedIn Ads worth it for small B2B companies?

They can be when a new client is worth a meaningful amount and the audience is well defined. For low-value or very local services, search or other social platforms often give better value.

How much does LinkedIn Ads management cost with Prism?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Ad spend is paid to LinkedIn separately, and we can quote in Canadian dollars on request.

Should I use Lead Gen Forms or a landing page?

Lead Gen Forms usually bring more leads with less friction, while landing pages can give more context and sometimes better quality. Many campaigns test both and keep whichever brings more qualified enquiries.

How long before LinkedIn Ads bring results?

Campaigns can generate leads soon after launch, but finding the best audiences and messages usually takes three to six months of testing and refinement.
