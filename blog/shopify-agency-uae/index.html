Shopify Agency for UAE Brands: What to Look For and What It Costs

Choosing a Shopify agency for your UAE brand? What a good partner handles: Arabic and English stores, payments, delivery, speed, SEO, timelines and cost.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify store for UAE customers needs more than a theme: plan languages, payment options, delivery and returns, and mobile speed before design starts.
  • Check which payment providers, buy-now-pay-later services and apps are available for your store's country, and confirm tax and consumer rules with official guidance.
  • Our ecommerce builds start from £1,500 to £3,500 and typically take five to twelve weeks, with other currencies quoted on request.

A Shopify agency for UAE brands should build a store that feels local to shoppers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the other emirates: fast on mobile, clear about delivery and returns, ready for the payment methods customers expect and, where your audience needs it, available in Arabic as well as English. It should also leave you with a store your own team can run day to day.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that builds and improves Shopify stores remotely for brands in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. We do not have a UAE office or local staff, and we work through video calls, shared boards and staged approvals. This guide covers what a Shopify project for the UAE involves, how to judge an agency and what our website and ecommerce development work costs.

Why UAE brands choose Shopify

Shopify suits many UAE brands because it is hosted, secure and well supported, so you are not managing servers or plugin updates. It handles multiple currencies and languages through its markets features, has a large app ecosystem and makes it easy for a small team to add products, run promotions and fulfil orders.

It is not right for every business. Very complex catalogues, unusual pricing rules or heavy custom functionality can push you towards other platforms or a higher Shopify plan. Our comparison of Shopify and WooCommerce explains the trade-offs, and they apply in the Gulf just as they do in the UK.

Payments and checkout

UAE shoppers often expect a choice of card payments, digital wallets and instalment options, and cash on delivery remains common for some categories. Availability of Shopify's own payment service, third-party gateways and buy-now-pay-later providers depends on where your business is registered and how it is set up, and it changes over time. A good agency will check what is available to you rather than assuming. Once providers are chosen, the checkout itself needs careful setup, which our guide to Shopify checkout settings walks through.

Delivery and fulfilment

Many UAE customers are used to fast delivery within the emirates. Your store should state delivery zones, costs and cut-off times plainly, and your promises should match what your courier or fulfilment partner can actually achieve. If you sell across the Gulf, shipping profiles and duties need extra thought. Our article on selling internationally from an online store covers the cross-border basics.

Arabic and English storefronts

If your customers include Arabic speakers, an Arabic version of your store can make a real difference to trust and conversion. Doing it well involves more than switching on translation:

  • Right-to-left layout. Navigation, product galleries, icons and forms should mirror correctly. Some themes handle this better than others, so test before you commit.
  • Human-quality copy. Product descriptions, policies and checkout text should be written or reviewed by a fluent Arabic writer. Machine translation alone often reads awkwardly.
  • Fonts. Choose an Arabic typeface that pairs well with your English font and stays readable at small sizes on phones.
  • Separate SEO metadata. Each language needs its own titles, descriptions and URLs so it can be found in search.

Our guide to Shopify SEO covers collection and product page optimisation, which applies to each language version of your store.

Speed, apps and day-to-day management

Most UAE shoppers browse on their phones, so speed is not optional. Heavy apps, oversized images and unused theme features slow a store down. We keep the app list short, compress media and test on real mobile connections. Our Shopify speed optimisation guide explains the process.

After launch, your team should be able to add products, update collections and run offers without calling a developer. We build with that in mind and hand over clear notes. For larger brands with high order volumes or several regional stores, a move to Shopify Plus may be worth considering, and we cover that in our guide to Shopify Plus migration.

Rules and compliance to check

Ecommerce in the UAE is subject to consumer protection, e-commerce, tax and data protection rules, and some product categories need extra approvals or have advertising restrictions. VAT applies in the UAE, and registration and pricing obligations depend on your circumstances. These rules change, so check current official guidance and take advice from a local accountant or lawyer. We can build clear policy pages, consent-aware tracking and transparent pricing displays, but responsibility for compliance stays with your business.

How our Shopify projects run

Every project follows the same staged process, which works well across time zones because each stage ends with something clear for you to approve.

  1. Discovery. We agree goals, markets, languages, payments, delivery and the apps you need.
  2. Structure. We plan navigation, collections and key templates.
  3. Design. We design the homepage, collection, product and cart pages in both languages where needed.
  4. Build. We set up the theme, products, policies, payments, shipping and tracking.
  5. Testing. We test checkout, mobile layouts, right-to-left views and forms before launch.
  6. Launch and handover. We go live, monitor early orders and train your team.

The more prepared you are, the faster it goes. Our guide on how to brief a Shopify developer lists what to have ready, from product data to brand assets.

After launch: growing the store

Launch is the start rather than the finish. In the first weeks, watch how shoppers move through the store: which collections they browse, where they drop out of the checkout and which payment and delivery options they choose. Those patterns usually point to quick improvements, such as clearer delivery messages on product pages or a simpler cart.

Seasonal peaks deserve planning too. Ramadan, Eid, national days and regional sales events can change demand and delivery expectations, so prepare stock, offers, banners and courier capacity well ahead. Bilingual stores need both language versions updated at the same time, which is easier with a simple content calendar.

Many UAE brands then add paid social, search ads or SEO to bring in more traffic. We can support that through our marketing retainers, or hand over to your in-house team with clear notes on how the store is set up.

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency for UAE brands?

Look for experience with multi-language stores, a clear plan for payments and delivery, a focus on mobile speed, transparent pricing and a handover that leaves your team in control.

Can Shopify stores display Arabic properly?

Yes, with a theme that supports right-to-left layouts and a careful setup. Always test navigation, product pages, cart and checkout in Arabic before launch.

Can a UK agency build a Shopify store for a UAE business?

Yes. Shopify projects are planned, built and tested online. We work remotely with staged approvals and agreed meeting times that suit both time zones.

Does Shopify support cash on delivery in the UAE?

Shopify supports manual payment methods such as cash on delivery. Whether it suits you depends on your products, courier and returns rates, so plan it alongside card and wallet payments.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Our ecommerce builds typically take five to twelve weeks, depending on catalogue size, languages, integrations and how quickly content and approvals arrive.

Do you price Shopify projects in dirhams?

Our standard prices are in pounds sterling. We can provide quotes in dirhams or other currencies on request.
