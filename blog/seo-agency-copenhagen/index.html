SEO Agency for Copenhagen Businesses: Danish and English Search

Looking for an SEO agency in Copenhagen? How SEO works across Danish and English search, what a remote UK studio can handle, what to ask and how pricing works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Copenhagen companies often need SEO for two audiences: Danish-speaking customers at home and English-speaking buyers across Europe and beyond.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based with no office in Denmark; we lead technical SEO, English content and reporting remotely, working with your team or a translator on Danish pages.
  • SEO results typically build over three to six months, and retainers are priced in pounds, with Danish krone, euro and other currencies quoted on request.

An SEO agency for a Copenhagen business should help you rank for the searches your buyers actually make, whether in Danish, in English or both, and turn that visibility into enquiries, sign-ups or sales. Good SEO covers technical health, language setup, keyword research, strong pages, earned mentions and reporting tied to business outcomes.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio working remotely with companies in Denmark and across Europe. We have no office in Copenhagen. Our SEO and generative engine optimisation service is delivered online, one hour behind Copenhagen time, with regular video calls, shared documents and clear monthly reporting. We are strongest on English-language SEO and on the technical foundations that support every language version of a site.

Why Copenhagen businesses often need SEO in two languages

Copenhagen is home to design, food, life sciences, software, shipping and consumer brands, and many of them look beyond Denmark early. English is widely spoken in Danish business, yet customers often search in Danish, particularly for local services, retail, healthcare and anything that feels personal or high-trust.

  • Danish-first businesses. Local services, restaurants and retailers selling mainly within Denmark need strong Danish pages written or reviewed by native speakers.
  • English-first businesses. Software, B2B and export-led companies often lead with English to reach buyers in the UK, the rest of Europe and North America.
  • Both. Many companies need both, with a structure that helps search engines show the Danish page to Danish searchers and the English page to everyone else.

Problems here are common: English pages ranking in Denmark when the Danish ones should, machine translations that read awkwardly, or two language versions competing for the same terms. Our international SEO guide explains the main structural choices, from subfolders to separate domains.

Keyword research in Danish and English

Translating an English keyword list into Danish rarely works. Danish searchers often join words into compounds, use different phrasing, or search with English product terms that have become standard in their sector. The reliable approach is to research each language separately, with data set to the right country, then map each group of terms to one clear page.

The method in our keyword research guide applies directly; you simply run it once per language and market. Prism writes in English. For Danish research checks and copy, we work alongside your in-house team or a native-speaking writer or translator, so Danish pages read naturally and match how people actually search.

SEO for Copenhagen software, design and ecommerce brands

Different kinds of Copenhagen business need different SEO priorities. A software company selling internationally relies on feature pages, use-case pages, comparison pages and practical guides. A design or homeware brand selling online leans on category pages, product pages and clean product data. A professional services firm needs clear service pages and proof of expertise.

For online shops, collection and product pages usually carry most commercial traffic, so their structure, copy and internal linking matter more than a busy blog. Our ecommerce SEO checklist covers the essentials, and many of the same checks apply whether you sell from Denmark into the UK or elsewhere in Europe.

Whatever the model, we focus first on searches that show buying intent, such as comparisons, pricing questions and problem-led queries, rather than chasing broad terms with little commercial value.

SEO and AI search for Danish companies

Buyers increasingly ask AI assistants and see AI-generated summaries at the top of search results. The pages most likely to be cited tend to answer questions clearly, state facts plainly and show who is behind the business. That applies in Danish and in English.

Our work includes generative engine optimisation alongside classic SEO: clear answers near the top of key pages, structured data, consistent business details and content that earns mentions from credible sources. Our guide to generative engine optimisation explains the approach in more detail.

Hiring a remote SEO agency from Copenhagen

Many Danish companies are comfortable working with suppliers abroad, and SEO suits remote delivery because the work happens in shared tools, documents and analytics. What matters is clear communication, sensible overlap in working hours and honesty about what the agency can and cannot do.

Here is how we typically work with clients in Denmark:

  1. Discovery call. We learn about your markets, languages, goals and current site, and agree whether SEO is the right priority.
  2. Audit and plan. We review technical health, content and competitors, then set out a prioritised plan for the first few months.
  3. Delivery. We carry out technical fixes, English content and on-page work, and coordinate Danish content with your team or translator.
  4. Reporting. Monthly reports show what was done, what moved and what comes next, with separate views per language where relevant.

Because we work one hour behind Copenhagen, there is plenty of shared working time for calls. Data protection matters too: we work within your existing analytics and consent setup, and you should check current official guidance on GDPR and cookie rules in Denmark with your own advisers.

Danish krone, euro and other currency quotes are available on request. Translation by a third party, if needed, is agreed separately. The full breakdown is on our pricing page.

For a broader view of how fees are set, see our guide to how much SEO costs.

SEO results typically build over three to six months. Technical fixes can show effects sooner, while content and authority take longer. Be wary of any agency, local or remote, that guarantees rankings.

Questions to ask any SEO agency in Copenhagen

  • How do you handle Danish and English versions of the site, and who writes each one?
  • What technical checks will you run first, and how do you prioritise them?
  • How will you report progress per language and per market?
  • Who owns the content, accounts and data if we stop working together?
  • What do you need from our team each month?
  • How do you approach AI search alongside traditional rankings?

Clear answers to these questions tell you more than a polished pitch. Our guide on how to choose a digital marketing agency covers further points worth checking before you sign.
