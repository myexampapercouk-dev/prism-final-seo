LinkedIn Ads Agency for US B2B Companies: How Remote LinkedIn Advertising Works

Looking for a LinkedIn Ads agency for a US B2B company? Remote management can cover targeting, ads, lead forms and reporting nationwide. Here is how it works.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A LinkedIn Ads agency for a US B2B company should start with your ideal buyers and sales process, then build targeting, offers and tracking around them.
  • LinkedIn suits high-value B2B sales where job title, seniority and company matter, but clicks cost more, so offers and lead quality need close attention.
  • We are a UK-based studio managing LinkedIn Ads remotely for US companies, with retainers in pounds, ad spend paid separately and other currencies quoted on request.

If you are looking for a LinkedIn Ads agency for a US B2B company, the right partner is one that can put your offer in front of the people who actually buy, then prove which campaigns lead to qualified pipeline. Remote LinkedIn Ads management works well for US software firms, professional services, manufacturers and B2B service providers, because the platform, the targeting and the reporting are the same wherever the agency sits.

We are a UK-based studio that manages LinkedIn Ads remotely for US businesses. We have no US office. This guide explains what a LinkedIn Ads agency should do for a US B2B company, how targeting and ad formats work, how to judge results, how remote management runs across US time zones and what it costs.

What a LinkedIn Ads agency for US B2B companies should do

LinkedIn is different from search and consumer social platforms. People are not usually searching for a solution when they see your ad, but they are in a work mindset, and you can reach them by role, seniority, industry and company. A good agency makes the most of that by covering:

  • Audience planning: defining the job functions, seniority levels, industries, company sizes and US regions that match your best customers.
  • Tracking: the LinkedIn Insight Tag, conversion events and CRM links, so leads can be followed beyond the form. Our Insight Tag setup guide explains the basics.
  • Offers: guides, demos, webinars, assessments or consultations that are worth a busy buyer's time.
  • Creative: ad copy, images, documents and video written for the buyer's problems, not just your features.
  • Lead handling: lead gen forms or landing pages, connected to your CRM so sales can follow up quickly.
  • Reporting: cost per lead, lead quality and pipeline, not just clicks and impressions.

Targeting US B2B buyers on LinkedIn

LinkedIn's targeting is its main strength. For US B2B campaigns, we usually combine a few layers rather than relying on one:

  • Location: the whole US, specific states or major metro areas, depending on where you sell and any licensing or service limits.
  • Job function and seniority: for example finance directors, IT managers or operations leaders, rather than long lists of exact job titles.
  • Company attributes: industry, company size and growth, to focus on the firms most likely to buy.
  • Company lists: uploaded lists of target accounts for account-based marketing, useful when you already know who you want to reach.
  • Retargeting: website visitors, video viewers and people who opened a lead form, who are often warmer than cold audiences.

Audiences that are too narrow struggle to deliver, while audiences that are too broad waste money. We test sizes and layers carefully and adjust as data comes in. Our guide to LinkedIn Ads for B2B lead generation goes deeper into audience and offer planning.

Is LinkedIn right for your US B2B company?

LinkedIn clicks usually cost more than clicks on most other platforms, so it suits businesses where a single customer is worth a lot: software subscriptions, consulting, professional services, industrial equipment, recruitment and other high-value B2B sales. It is less suited to low-cost products or offers that rely on impulse decisions.

It also works best alongside other channels. Google Ads captures people who are already searching, while LinkedIn reaches buyers who are not searching yet. Measuring LinkedIn Ads beyond the lead form

A low cost per lead means little if the leads never become customers. For US B2B companies with longer sales cycles, we focus on:

  • Lead quality: whether leads match your ideal customer profile by role, company and need.
  • Pipeline: how many leads become meetings, opportunities and deals, using data from your CRM.
  • Cost per opportunity: a better guide than cost per lead when deal values are high.
  • Landing page performance: where landing pages are used, how well they turn visits into enquiries. Our landing page design guide covers what helps.

Collecting and using personal data from forms brings privacy responsibilities. Rules differ between US states, so check your privacy notices, consent and data handling against official guidance or with your own adviser. Regulated sectors, such as financial or healthcare services, should have ad copy approved by their own compliance team before it runs.

How remote LinkedIn Ads management works for US companies

The continental US spans four main time zones, usually five to eight hours behind the UK. That means a shared window in the US morning for calls, with most management work done while your team is offline. A typical arrangement looks like this:

  1. Onboarding: access to Campaign Manager, your company page, analytics and CRM, plus a call to agree buyer profiles and what counts as a qualified lead.
  2. Strategy and build: audiences, offers, creative and tracking, set out in a written plan before anything goes live.
  3. Ongoing management: regular reviews of audiences, creative, bids and budgets, with tests running throughout.
  4. Monthly report: leads, lead quality, pipeline where available, and clear next steps.

Our guide to remote PPC agencies for US businesses covers access, ownership and communication in more detail. Whoever you work with, make sure the ad account and company page stay in your name.

Close contact with your sales team makes a big difference on LinkedIn. Sales staff hear the objections, questions and phrases buyers use every day, and that feedback shapes better ads and offers. We ask for short, regular notes on lead quality, such as which leads were a good fit and which were not, and use them to refine audiences and messages. Over time, that loop between campaigns and sales conversations is often what separates a costly experiment from a channel that steadily feeds the pipeline.

How much a LinkedIn Ads agency costs

LinkedIn Ads management is billed separately from your ad spend, which you pay directly to LinkedIn. Our management retainers are in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request:

  • Launch, £350 a month: a focused campaign for one audience and offer.
  • Growth, £750 a month: several audiences, regular creative testing and CRM-linked reporting.
  • Scale, £1,500 a month: larger accounts, account-based campaigns and multi-channel coordination.

FAQs

How do I choose a LinkedIn Ads agency for a US B2B company?

Look for an agency that asks about your buyers and sales process first, sets up tracking properly, reports on lead quality and pipeline and keeps the ad account in your name.

Do you have an office in the US?

No. We are a UK-based studio and manage LinkedIn Ads remotely for US companies. We work through video calls, shared reports and written updates.

Can you target specific US states or cities on LinkedIn?

Yes. LinkedIn lets you target the whole US, individual states or major metro areas, and combine location with job function, seniority, industry and company size.

Should we use LinkedIn lead gen forms or landing pages?

Both can work. Lead gen forms usually bring more leads at a lower cost, while landing pages often bring fewer but better-qualified enquiries. We often test both.

How much does LinkedIn Ads management cost?

Our retainers are Launch £350 a month, Growth £750 a month and Scale £1,500 a month, with ad spend paid directly to LinkedIn. Prices are in pounds, and other currencies are quoted on request.

How long do LinkedIn Ads take to work?

Early leads can arrive within weeks, but most accounts need three to six months of testing and data before results are steady, particularly where sales cycles are long.
