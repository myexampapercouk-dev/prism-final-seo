How we build Meta campaigns

Meta's ad system has changed a lot. Detailed interest targeting matters less than it used to, and creative, offers and conversion data matter more. Our approach is:

  • Start with the offer. A clear reason to act, such as a free quote, a first-visit offer or a seasonal promotion, beats clever wording.
  • Get tracking right. We set up the Meta pixel and Conversions API where possible, so Meta can learn from real results.
  • Simple structure. Fewer, larger campaigns usually give Meta's AI-powered delivery enough data to optimise.
  • Location focus. We target the parts of Hamilton and the surrounding area you actually serve, such as Burlington or Grimsby where relevant.
  • Retargeting. We reach people who visited your site or engaged with your pages but did not act.

Creative that works on Facebook and Instagram

Creative is now the biggest lever in Meta advertising. We write ad copy, plan formats and design static and carousel ads from your photos and brand assets. For each campaign we test a few clear angles, such as price, convenience, quality or local trust, and keep what works.

Short video often performs well on Instagram Reels and Facebook. Prism does not film or edit video. If your team records short clips on a phone, we can use them in campaigns and advise on what to capture.

Landing pages and lead forms

Meta gives you two main ways to collect enquiries: send people to your website, or use an instant form inside Facebook or Instagram. Instant forms are quick but can bring lower-quality leads; website landing pages take more effort but often bring better enquiries. We help you choose, and we build pages that match the ad and load fast on phones. Our landing page design guide explains what makes a page convert, and the Hamilton web design guide covers full site builds.

Meta ads or Google Ads?

Meta and Google do different jobs. Google Ads reach people already searching for a product or service. Meta ads reach people who are not searching yet but fit your audience, which makes them strong for offers, events, visual products and building demand. Many Hamilton businesses use both. Our Google Ads vs Facebook Ads guide compares them in detail, and the Hamilton Google Ads guide covers search campaigns in the city.

Meta ads for small businesses

You do not need a large budget to use Meta well. A small business can start with one campaign, one clear offer and a modest daily budget, then grow as results come in. The key is patience in the learning phase and not changing everything every few days. Our guide to Facebook ads for small businesses covers the basics, and our Meta ads agency Canada guide explains how we work with businesses across the country.

Special ad categories and privacy

Some ads on Meta fall under special ad categories, such as housing, employment, credit and social issues, which limit targeting options. If your ads fall into these areas, we plan campaigns around the current rules.

At a general level, Canadian businesses should also consider PIPEDA for personal information and CASL for follow-up emails and texts sent to leads. Regulated sectors such as health and finance may face extra advertising rules. We build clear consent options, but compliance approval rests with you; please check official guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your own adviser.

Common Meta ads mistakes we fix

When we review Meta accounts for Hamilton businesses, a few problems appear often:

  • Boosting posts instead of running campaigns. The boost button is easy, but it offers little control over goals, placements and tracking.
  • Optimising for the wrong goal. Campaigns set to clicks or reach when the business actually wants leads or sales.
  • Missing or broken pixel events. Meta cannot learn who converts, so delivery drifts toward cheap, low-value clicks.
  • Too many tiny ad sets. Budget split so thinly that no ad set leaves the learning phase.
  • Tired creative. The same image running for months until people stop noticing it.
  • Slow follow-up. Leads collected but not contacted for days, by which time they have chosen someone else.

We tackle the biggest issues first, then build a steady routine of creative testing and budget review.
