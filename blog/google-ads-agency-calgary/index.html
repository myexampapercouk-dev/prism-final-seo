Google Ads Agency for Calgary Businesses: How Remote PPC Management Works

Hiring a Google Ads agency for a Calgary business? How remote PPC management works, local targeting across Alberta, tracking, costs and how to judge any agency.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Google Ads for a Calgary business works best with tight location targeting, accurate conversion tracking and landing pages built for the searches you pay for.
  • Agency management fees and ad spend are separate, and the ad budget is paid directly to Google from your own account, so you keep control and visibility.
  • Judge any Google Ads agency on its tracking, account ownership terms and honest reporting, not on promises of instant results or guaranteed returns.

A Google Ads agency for a Calgary business should put your ads in front of people searching for what you sell in the areas you actually serve, measure which clicks turn into calls, forms and sales, and keep cutting wasted spend. That work is done inside your Google Ads account, so it can be managed remotely: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that manages paid campaigns for Canadian businesses online, with no office or staff in Calgary or elsewhere in Alberta.

This guide explains what Google Ads management involves for a Calgary business, how local targeting and tracking should be set up, how a remote relationship runs on Mountain Time, and what to check before hiring any agency.

What a Google Ads agency does for a Calgary business

Good PPC management is less about launching ads and more about the steady work that follows. A typical engagement includes:

  • Account audit or setup. Reviewing an existing account for waste and gaps, or building a new one with a clear campaign structure. Our PPC audit checklist shows what we look at.
  • Keyword research. Finding the searches that signal real buying intent, and the ones to exclude.
  • Ad copy and assets. Writing responsive search ads with clear offers and adding call, location and sitelink assets.
  • Conversion tracking. Measuring calls, forms, bookings and purchases so budget follows results.
  • Bidding and budget management. Choosing a bidding strategy that suits your data and adjusting it as results come in.
  • Ongoing optimisation. Search term reviews, negative keywords, ad testing and landing page recommendations.
  • Reporting. Plain-language reports that connect spend to enquiries and revenue.

Targeting Calgary and the surrounding area

Location targeting is one of the most common sources of wasted spend. By default, Google Ads can show ads to people who have shown interest in a location, not only people physically there. For most local businesses, targeting people in or regularly in your area is the safer starting point. Our guide to Google Ads location targeting explains these settings in detail.

For a Calgary business, targeting usually depends on how far you will travel or how far customers will come:

Search terms need the same attention. Without negative keywords, ads can appear for job seekers, DIY searches or services you do not offer. Our negative keywords guide shows how to build and maintain exclusion lists.

Tracking that tells you what is working

Without accurate conversion tracking, nobody can say which keywords are worth paying for. Before spending seriously on ads, make sure the account records:

  • Form submissions, measured on a thank-you page or confirmed submission event.
  • Phone calls from ads and from the website, where call tracking suits your business.
  • Bookings or purchases, ideally with values attached.
  • Offline outcomes, such as quotes won, where your sales process allows them to be imported.

Our guide to Google Ads conversion tracking walks through the setup. Tracking on a Canadian website should also reflect privacy law. Private sector organisations in Alberta are generally covered by the province's own privacy legislation, with federal law (PIPEDA) applying in other contexts. Check current official guidance and make sure your cookie consent and privacy policy match what your tracking actually does.

Landing pages, ads and bidding

Ads bring the click, but the landing page wins or loses the enquiry. A page built for paid traffic should match the search, show your offer clearly, load fast on a phone and make contacting you easy. Our article on landing page design sets out what that looks like.

Ad copy should speak to Calgary searchers directly: the area you serve, what makes you different and what happens next. Our guide to responsive search ads explains how to supply headlines and descriptions that combine well.

Bidding strategy should follow your data. New accounts with little conversion history often start with more controlled approaches, then move to automated strategies once tracking is reliable and enough conversions are recorded. Our bidding strategies guide compares the options.

Working with a remote Google Ads agency from Calgary

The UK is typically seven hours ahead of Calgary. In practice, that means we review search terms, adjust bids and prepare changes during the UK day, and calls happen in your morning. Because everything happens inside your Google Ads account, you can see every change in the account history whenever you like.

Ownership matters. Your Google Ads account, Google Analytics property and tag manager container should be in your name, with the agency given access as a manager. If you ever part ways, you keep the account, its history and its data. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own billing method, so it never passes through the agency.

It also helps to agree how quickly urgent changes are handled. If you need to pause ads because your diary is full, or switch on a promotion at short notice, you should know who to contact and how fast it will happen. A shared channel for quick requests alongside a monthly call usually covers both routine work and the occasional urgent change.

What does Google Ads management cost?

There are two separate costs. Ad spend is set by you and paid directly to Google, usually in Canadian dollars. Management is our fee for running the account. Our published management prices are in pounds sterling, with monthly retainers of £350, £750 and £1,500 depending on the scope and number of channels. Quotes in Canadian dollars or other currencies are available on request, and our pricing page shows what each level includes.

Results from paid campaigns typically take three to six months to settle, as the account gathers data, tracking is refined and waste is removed. Early weeks often show useful signals, but stable performance takes time to build.

Google Ads or Meta Ads for a Calgary business?

Google Ads reaches people already searching for what you offer, which suits urgent and high-intent needs. Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram reach people based on interests and behaviour, which suits building demand, promoting events or selling visual products. Many businesses use both, with search capturing demand and social creating it. Our comparison of Google Ads and Facebook Ads explains how to decide where to start.

If you collect email addresses from ads or landing pages, Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) sets rules on consent for marketing emails, so check current guidance before adding contacts to campaigns.

How to choose a Google Ads agency in Calgary

  • Will the ad account be in your name, with spend billed directly to you?
  • How will conversions be tracked, and will they show you the setup?
  • What happens each month, in specific tasks rather than general promises?
  • Do they report on enquiries, sales and cost per lead, not only clicks?
  • Is there a minimum contract term, and what notice is needed to leave?

Be cautious of guaranteed results. Costs per click and competition change, and no agency controls the auction.

FAQs

Can a UK-based Google Ads agency manage campaigns for a Calgary business?

Yes. All management happens inside your Google Ads account, so location matters far less than process. The UK is typically seven hours ahead of Calgary, so calls are held in your morning.

How much does a Google Ads agency Calgary businesses use cost with you?

Our management retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month in pounds sterling, with Canadian dollar quotes on request. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Google.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account, billing and data stay in your name, and we are given manager access. If you stop working with us, you keep everything.

How long until Google Ads shows results?

Ads can bring clicks within days of launch, but results typically take three to six months to settle as tracking is refined and wasted spend is removed.

Should I target all of Alberta or just Calgary?

Target where you can genuinely serve customers. Local service businesses usually do best with a tight area around Calgary, while B2B and ecommerce brands may target wider Alberta or all of Canada.

Do you also manage Meta Ads for Calgary businesses?

Yes. We manage Google Ads, Meta Ads, YouTube Ads and other paid channels, and many businesses use search and social together.
