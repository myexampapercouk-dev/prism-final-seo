LinkedIn Ads Agency in Edmonton: Reaching Alberta Decision Makers

A LinkedIn Ads agency in Edmonton targets decision makers by job title, industry and company, with tracking and lead forms that win Alberta clients, remotely.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 28 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • LinkedIn Ads suit Edmonton firms selling to energy, construction, public sector, health and professional buyers, where job title and company targeting matter more than cheap clicks.
  • Prism Works Studio is UK-based, works remotely and has no Edmonton office; we plan, build and manage campaigns, while any video footage comes from your team.
  • Retainers run from £350 to £1,500 a month, ad spend is paid directly to LinkedIn, and Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request.

A LinkedIn Ads agency in Edmonton plans and manages paid campaigns on LinkedIn that reach decision makers by job title, seniority, industry and company, then turns that attention into qualified leads. The work covers account setup, Insight Tag tracking, audience building, offers, ad copy, lead gen forms, budget control and reporting tied to pipeline.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based digital marketing studio that works remotely. We have no office in Edmonton or anywhere in Canada, and we work through video calls, shared documents and written reports. We write in English, so any French copy comes from your team or a translator. We do not film or edit video, so any video ads use footage your team supplies.

Why LinkedIn Ads fit the Edmonton market

Edmonton is Alberta's capital and a major hub for energy, petrochemicals, construction, engineering, logistics, health, education and government. Many local firms sell to other businesses or public bodies, often with long sales cycles and several people involved in each decision. LinkedIn is one of the few platforms where you can target those people by the job they do and the company they work for.

Typical Edmonton advertisers include engineering and environmental consultancies, industrial suppliers and equipment firms, construction and project management companies, technology and software firms, accounting and legal practices, recruiters and training providers, and organisations hiring skilled staff. Our guide on LinkedIn Ads for lead generation covers the core approach, which carries over to Alberta.

What a LinkedIn Ads agency in Edmonton handles

LinkedIn clicks are expensive, so waste hurts. Our work on an Edmonton account follows a clear process.

  1. Goals and lead value: we agree what a qualified lead is worth and what cost per lead your sales process can support.
  2. Tracking: we install or audit the LinkedIn Insight Tag and conversion events, and connect lead form data to your CRM where possible.
  3. Audiences: we build target lists by job function, seniority, industry, company size and location, plus matched audiences from site visitors and company lists.
  4. Offers: we help shape an offer worth a click, such as a guide, checklist, consultation, assessment or webinar.
  5. Ads and forms: we write ad copy and lead gen forms, and brief any images or videos your team creates.
  6. Optimisation: we review bids, audiences and ads weekly and pause what does not earn its keep.
  7. Reporting: monthly reports show spend, leads, cost per lead and, where your CRM allows, pipeline.

Our LinkedIn Insight Tag setup guide explains the tracking step in detail.

Targeting Edmonton and Alberta decision makers

LinkedIn lets you target by location, so campaigns can focus on the Edmonton region, the wider province, Western Canada or the whole country. Within that, the main levers are job function, title, seniority, skills, industry, company name and company size.

The trick is balance. Audiences that are too narrow struggle to deliver and get expensive quickly. Audiences that are too broad waste money on people who will never buy. For most Edmonton B2B accounts we start with a mid-sized audience built from job function and seniority within relevant industries, then test tighter account lists for high-value deals.

Account-based campaigns work well for firms with a defined list of target companies, such as suppliers selling to large energy operators or consultancies targeting public bodies. We upload the company list, match it on LinkedIn and serve ads to the right roles inside those companies.

Budgets and what LinkedIn Ads cost

LinkedIn typically costs more per click than other platforms, but the audience quality can justify that when each deal is worth a lot. We do not promise a set cost per lead. We start with a test budget large enough to learn, then move money toward the audiences and offers that bring in qualified leads.

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to LinkedIn. Fees are shown in pounds on our pricing page, and Canadian dollar pricing is quoted on request. B2B paid results typically build over three to six months, partly because sales cycles are long.

LinkedIn alongside Google Ads and SEO

LinkedIn finds people by who they are. Google finds people by what they are searching for. Most Edmonton B2B firms benefit from both. Our comparison of LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads explains how to split budgets, and our Edmonton Google Ads guide covers the search side.

Organic visibility matters too. Buyers who see your LinkedIn ad often check your website and search results before getting in touch. Our Edmonton SEO guide and Edmonton web design guide cover how to make that research step work in your favour.

Lead quality and follow-up

LinkedIn lead gen forms are easy to complete, which is good for volume but can bring in weaker leads if the offer is too broad. We use qualifying questions, clear offer wording and audience refinement to keep quality up. We also help you set up quick alerts and a simple follow-up process, because a lead contacted the same day is far more likely to become a conversation.

Using LinkedIn Ads for hiring in Edmonton

Many Edmonton employers struggle to find skilled engineers, tradespeople, technicians and healthcare staff. LinkedIn Ads can support hiring as well as sales. Job and employer brand campaigns can reach people with the right skills and experience in the region, or in other provinces if you are willing to help with relocation.

Hiring campaigns work best with clear role details, honest information about the work and location, and a simple way to apply. We set up targeting, write the ad copy and track applications, while your team supplies any photos or video of the workplace. We keep hiring and sales campaigns separate so each budget is judged on its own goal, and we make sure the wording is reviewed by your HR team before launch, since employment advertising has its own rules.

Privacy and Canadian rules

Canada has federal privacy law and anti-spam rules for commercial messages, and Alberta has its own private-sector privacy law. Lead forms need clear privacy wording, and follow-up emails must respect consent. We set things up in line with common good practice, but we are not legal advisers. Check official guidance or your own adviser, as compliance rests with your business. Regulated sectors such as finance and health need extra care, and your compliance team should approve ads before launch.

Working with us remotely

Edmonton runs on Mountain Time, seven hours behind the UK for most of the year, so we schedule calls for your morning and our afternoon. Most updates happen in shared documents with a clear change log. You own your LinkedIn Campaign Manager account, company page, Insight Tag and lead data, and we work through partner access.
