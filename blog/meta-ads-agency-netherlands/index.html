Why Dutch businesses advertise on Facebook and Instagram

The Netherlands is a highly connected, mobile-first market where online shopping is part of everyday life. Facebook and Instagram remain major platforms for discovery, especially for consumer brands, and Meta's targeting and ad formats make it possible to reach specific audiences across the country or in particular regions and cities.

The businesses that tend to get most from Meta Ads in the Netherlands include:

  • E-commerce brands. Fashion, home, beauty, food and lifestyle products that sell well visually.
  • Local service businesses. Studios, clinics, salons and trades looking for leads in a specific city or region.
  • Hospitality and events. Restaurants, venues and experiences promoting bookings and seasonal offers.
  • Education and training. Course providers and academies running enrolment campaigns.
  • B2B firms with visual products. Suppliers who want to build awareness before buyers search.

Creative that works in the Netherlands

Dutch audiences tend to respond to clear, direct messages and can be quick to scroll past anything that feels overblown. Good creative for the Dutch market usually means:

  • Natural Dutch copy. Written or checked by a native speaker, not machine translation alone. Tone matters, including the choice between the informal and formal forms of address.
  • Vertical video and images for Reels and Stories. Made for phones from the start rather than cropped from landscape assets.
  • A clear offer. Price, delivery, returns or the next step, stated plainly.
  • Local cues where relevant. Familiar payment options such as iDEAL on the landing page, delivery times within the Netherlands and Dutch customer service details.
  • Several variations. Different hooks, formats and messages so the algorithm and the data can show what works.

If you also sell into Flanders, Dutch copy may need adjusting for Belgian audiences, and campaigns are often better split by country. Our guide to running Meta Ads in multiple countries covers the structure in more detail.

Tracking with consent in place

Meta campaigns learn from conversion data, so tracking is not optional. The Meta Pixel and the Conversions API together send purchase, lead and other events back to Meta. Our Meta Pixel setup guide explains the basics.

In the Netherlands, as across the EU, tracking has to work alongside cookie consent. The notes below are general information only. Please follow official guidance and take advice from your own legal adviser.

  • GDPR. Advertising data and lead forms involve personal data. You need a lawful basis, a clear privacy notice and sensible retention. The Dutch data protection authority publishes guidance in Dutch and English.
  • Cookie consent. Under the ePrivacy rules, as applied in Dutch law, tracking and advertising cookies generally need prior consent. Your banner should make accepting and rejecting equally easy, and tags should only fire after consent. See our article on cookie banner design.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer services, including online shops. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt, and the expected standard is EN 301 549, which points to WCAG at level AA. Our overview of the European Accessibility Act for websites explains what it means for landing pages and checkouts.

Consent means some conversions will not be reported. We set expectations on that from the start and read the data accordingly, rather than chasing every missing number.

Meta Ads for Dutch e-commerce

For online shops, Meta works best when connected to a well-maintained product catalogue. That allows dynamic ads showing the products people viewed, and Advantage+ shopping campaigns that test audiences and placements automatically. The catalogue needs accurate titles, prices, stock and images in Dutch.

If your store runs on Shopify, the catalogue connection is straightforward, and our post on Shopify for Dutch businesses covers the platform side. Many Dutch sellers also list on bol, and Meta campaigns can build awareness that leads people to search for your brand there. How we manage campaigns remotely

A typical engagement follows a clear sequence:

  1. Discovery call. We discuss your products or services, customers, margins and goals, and agree the numbers that matter.
  2. Account and tracking review. We check the ad account, Pixel, Conversions API, catalogue and consent setup.
  3. Plan. Campaign structure, audiences, budget split and a creative brief. Dutch copy is written or checked by your team or translator.
  4. Launch and testing. Campaigns go live with several creative variations so we can learn quickly.
  5. Optimisation. We adjust budgets, audiences and creative based on results, and refresh ads before they tire.
  6. Monthly reporting. Spend, results and cost per result in English, with clear actions for the next month.

Questions to ask any Meta Ads agency

  • How will you track conversions with consent in place?
  • Who writes and checks the Dutch ad copy?
  • How often will creative be refreshed?
  • Will I own the ad account and all data?
  • What will monthly reports show, and in what language?

We always work inside an ad account you own, so you keep the history and data if you ever move on. We also ask for access to your analytics and shop platform, so we can compare what Meta reports with what actually happened in your sales data. Where the two differ, we explain why, whether that is consent, attribution windows or customers who saw an ad on a phone and bought later on a laptop. That honest view of performance is what lets budgets grow with confidence rather than guesswork.

FAQs

Do you have an office in the Netherlands?

No. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely. We have no office in Amsterdam or anywhere else in the Netherlands, and we work with Dutch clients by video call, email and shared documents.

What should a Meta Ads agency in the Netherlands do?

Set up reliable, consent-aware tracking, structure campaigns around clear objectives, test Dutch-language creative, manage budgets and report on sales or leads rather than vanity metrics.

Can you write Facebook and Instagram ads in Dutch?

We work in English. We plan the messages and write creative briefs, and the Dutch copy is written or checked by your team or a professional translator.

How much should we spend on Meta Ads?

It depends on your goals, margins and market. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and is separate from our retainer of £350, £750 or £1,500 a month. We recommend a budget once we understand your targets.

Does cookie consent reduce Meta Ads performance?

It can reduce the data Meta receives, which affects reporting and optimisation. A well-built consent setup with the Conversions API helps, and the approach should follow official guidance and your own legal advice.

Can you run campaigns for both the Netherlands and Belgium?

Yes. We usually split campaigns by country so budgets, copy and reporting stay clear, with Dutch copy adjusted for Flemish audiences where needed.
