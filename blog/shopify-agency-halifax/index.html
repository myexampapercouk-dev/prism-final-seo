Who this guide is for

Halifax and the wider Nova Scotia economy include seafood and food production, craft beverages, tourism, ocean technology, education, healthcare, defence and a lively community of makers and independent retailers. Shopify tends to suit:

  • Food and beverage brands selling preserves, coffee, seafood products or craft drinks where rules allow.
  • Makers and lifestyle brands selling clothing, home goods, jewellery or gifts.
  • Retailers with physical shops who want an online store linked to in-person sales.
  • Tourism-linked businesses selling merchandise, gift cards or experiences.
  • Growing brands outgrowing another platform and considering Shopify or Shopify Plus.

Shipping, taxes and selling from Atlantic Canada

Shipping is often the biggest challenge for Halifax brands. Customers in Ontario, the Prairies or British Columbia may face longer delivery times and higher costs, so shipping rates and messaging need to be set up carefully. Shopify lets you create shipping zones, set flat or calculated rates, offer free shipping thresholds and show delivery estimates. We help you choose a structure that protects your margins while staying clear for customers.

Nova Scotia uses the Harmonized Sales Tax, and other provinces apply GST, HST or provincial sales tax in different ways. Shopify can calculate taxes automatically once your registrations are configured, but the decisions about where you are registered and how you charge tax rest with you and your accountant. We set up the store according to their guidance. If you sell into the United States or overseas, Shopify Markets can manage currencies and duties, and we can help configure it.

What a Shopify project looks like

E-commerce builds usually take 5 to 12 weeks, depending on product numbers, integrations and content.

  1. Discovery: we agree your goals, products, customers and must-have features.
  2. Structure: we plan collections, navigation, filters and the keywords each page should target.
  3. Design: we design product, collection and home pages for mobile first.
  4. Build: we configure the theme, apps, payments, shipping and taxes.
  5. Products and content: we import products and write or edit English descriptions where agreed.
  6. Testing and launch: we test checkout, speed, redirects and tracking before going live.
  7. Training: we show your team how to manage orders, products and content.

Migrating to Shopify

Many brands move to Shopify from WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix or older custom platforms. A careful migration moves products, variants, images, customers and order history, then sets up redirects from every old URL to its new equivalent so search rankings and bookmarks keep working.

We also check metadata, structured data and internal links before launch, and monitor Search Console afterwards. Larger brands with wholesale channels, high order volumes or complex checkout needs may benefit from Shopify Plus, and we can handle those migrations too. Our Shopify agency Canada guide covers national considerations in more depth.

Product SEO and visibility

Launching a store is only the start. To sell, people need to find it. Product SEO means writing clear, useful product titles and descriptions, structuring collections around how customers search, adding product schema and building helpful guides that link to your products.

Our e-commerce SEO in Canada guide explains this in detail, and our SEO in Halifax guide covers local search for brands that also have a shop or showroom. If you sell on Amazon as well as Shopify, our Amazon SEO in Canada guide explains how marketplace listings fit alongside your own store.

Ads and email for Shopify stores

Google Shopping and Performance Max campaigns can show your products with images and prices to people searching across Canada. Meta and Instagram ads work well for visual products and for reaching people who have not yet searched. Our guides to Google Ads in Halifax and Meta Ads in Canada explain how we run these campaigns.

Email is often the most cost-effective channel for repeat sales. Welcome series, abandoned cart reminders and post-purchase follow-ups can all be automated in Shopify or a connected email platform. In Canada, CASL generally requires consent before sending commercial electronic messages, and PIPEDA covers how personal information is handled more broadly. Some provinces have their own privacy laws, and Quebec's Law 25 applies if you hold data on people in Quebec. These notes are general, so please check official guidance or ask your own adviser.

French content and bilingual stores

If you sell into Quebec or New Brunswick, a French version of your store can help. Shopify supports multiple languages, and we can set up the structure, language switcher and translated URLs. We write in English, so French product descriptions and pages should be written by your team or a professional translator.

Apps, integrations and point of sale

The Shopify app store covers reviews, subscriptions, bundles, loyalty programmes, wholesale pricing and much more, but every app adds code and often a monthly fee. We recommend a short list of well-supported apps and remove anything that slows the store or duplicates built-in features. For brands with a shop, market stall or pop-up in Halifax, Shopify POS can connect in-person and online sales so stock levels, customers and orders stay in one place.

We also connect the store to the tools you already use, such as accounting software, email platforms, shipping apps and analytics. Where a connection needs custom work, we scope it clearly before starting so there are no surprises.

Designing product pages that sell

Product pages carry most of the weight in a Shopify store. Clear photos from several angles, honest descriptions, sizing or ingredient details, shipping information and visible returns policies all help shoppers decide. For food and drink brands, accurate labelling details you supply matter too. We design pages so the key information sits near the add-to-cart button on mobile, and we keep pages light so they load quickly on slower connections.
