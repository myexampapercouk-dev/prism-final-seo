What are organic SEO services?

When someone searches Google, the results page usually has two kinds of listing. Paid listings, marked as sponsored, appear because an advertiser pays for clicks. Organic listings appear because Google judges those pages to be the most relevant and useful answers. Organic SEO services focus entirely on the second kind.

The main benefit is that organic traffic does not stop when a budget runs out. A page that ranks well can bring visitors for months or years, as long as it stays accurate and useful. The trade-off is time: organic results typically take three to six months to build, while paid ads can bring traffic on day one.

What our organic SEO services include

Organic SEO is a set of connected tasks. We group them into four areas, and each retainer covers all four at a level that fits the size of your site.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO makes sure search engines can crawl, render and index your pages. We check site speed, mobile layout, broken links, redirects, duplicate pages, canonical tags, sitemaps and structured data. On many sites, fixing a handful of technical issues unlocks rankings that content alone could not. Our technical SEO audit checklist lists the checks we run, and our guide to Core Web Vitals covers the speed measures Google uses.

On-page SEO and content

On-page work improves the pages you already have. We rewrite title tags, meta descriptions and headings, tighten copy so it answers real questions, add internal links and mark up content with schema where it helps. Then we plan new content around searches your customers make but your site does not yet answer. Our keyword research guide explains how we choose those searches.

Links and digital PR

Links from relevant, trusted websites remain one of the clearest signals of authority. We earn links through useful content, digital PR, resource pages and genuine industry relationships. We never buy links in bulk or use private networks, because these break Google's guidelines and can harm your site. Our guide to digital PR and link building shows the approach.

Local organic SEO

For businesses that serve a set area, local organic SEO covers your Google Business Profile, local citations, reviews and location pages. This helps you appear in the map pack as well as the regular results. Our local versus national SEO guide explains which mix suits your business.

Signs a provider is doing organic SEO properly

Because SEO happens mostly out of sight, it can be hard to tell whether a provider is doing good work. These signs help.

  • They start with an audit and a written plan, not a list of keywords and a promise.
  • They explain changes in plain language, so you know what was done to which pages and why.
  • They track leads and sales, not just rankings for terms nobody searches.
  • They avoid guarantees, because nobody controls how Google ranks pages.
  • They work on your accounts, so Search Console, analytics and your profile stay in your name.
  • They talk about quality over volume, in both content and links.

Warning signs include guaranteed number one rankings, hundreds of links a month, content written only for search engines and reports with no link to business results.

Organic SEO for online stores

E-commerce sites have their own organic SEO challenges. Large catalogues create thousands of URLs, filters can produce endless duplicate pages, and product descriptions copied from manufacturers rarely rank. For online stores, our organic SEO work focuses on a clear category structure, unique and helpful product copy, sensible handling of filters and out-of-stock items, and structured data so products can show rich details in search.

Category pages usually matter most. They match the broad searches shoppers make, such as a product type plus a feature, and they link down to individual products. Improving a few dozen key category pages often brings more organic revenue than rewriting hundreds of product pages. We also look at how the store handles seasonal ranges, so pages that rank each year are kept live rather than deleted and rebuilt.

If your store runs on Shopify, WordPress with WooCommerce or another platform, the principles are the same, though the technical fixes differ. Where a store also sells on Amazon or Walmart, we can align marketplace SEO with the main site so listings support each other rather than competing. Our e-commerce marketing page explains how we approach online retail.

Organic SEO for AI search

AI Overviews and AI assistants now answer many questions before anyone clicks. They tend to cite pages that are clear, accurate and well structured. The same organic SEO work, such as direct answers, good headings, schema and strong topical coverage, improves your chances of being cited. We use an AI-powered approach for research and drafting, with every page checked by a person before it is published. Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO covers this in more depth.

FAQs

What are organic SEO services?

Organic SEO services improve your website's rankings in the unpaid search results. They cover technical fixes, on-page improvements, new content, link building and local SEO, with reporting on the traffic and leads that result.

How much do organic SEO services cost?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month, depending on the size of the site and the competition. US dollar quotes are available on request.

How long does organic SEO take to work?

Organic results typically take three to six months to build. Highly competitive national terms can take longer, while smaller local terms often move sooner.

Is organic SEO better than Google Ads?

Neither is better in every case. Google Ads brings fast, controllable traffic for a cost per click. Organic SEO builds traffic that lasts longer. Many businesses use both, with ads covering the gap while SEO grows.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No honest provider can guarantee rankings, because Google decides them. We commit to a clear plan, quality work and transparent reporting on progress.
