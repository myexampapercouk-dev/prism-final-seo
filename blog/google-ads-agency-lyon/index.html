Why Google Ads matters for Lyon businesses

Lyon is France's third-largest city and a major centre for life sciences, manufacturing, software, finance, logistics and, of course, food and hospitality. The wider Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region adds strong industrial and tourism economies. That means competition in paid search varies widely. Some local service terms are affordable, while sectors such as legal, health, finance and software can be expensive per click.

Because Google is the starting point for so many purchases in France, being visible for the right searches at the right moment can make a big difference. The aim is not to bid on everything, but to focus budget on the searches most likely to turn into customers.

Start with tracking

Before spending more, you need to know which clicks become customers. We set up conversion tracking in Google Ads and Google Analytics 4 for form submissions, calls, bookings and purchases. Where possible, we add enhanced conversions and import offline sales, so Google's bidding learns from real outcomes rather than simple page visits.

In France and across the EU, consent rules affect tracking. Your site needs a cookie banner that records choices properly, and Google's consent mode should be set up so data is handled correctly. We help configure this, but final legal approval rests with your business and its advisers. Our guide to cookie banner design covers the basics.

Keywords and match types

Good keyword work separates profitable searches from wasted spend. We group keywords by service or product, use match types carefully and build negative keyword lists to block irrelevant searches such as jobs, free advice or competitor brand names you do not want. For local businesses, we target Lyon and nearby areas such as Villeurbanne, Vénissieux and Caluire-et-Cuire, adjusting radius and bids based on where customers actually come from.

French keyword research is done with your team or translator, because search terms often differ from direct translations. We bring the structure and the data, and you bring the language and local knowledge.

Ads and landing pages

Ads must match the search and lead to a page that delivers on the promise. Sending every click to a homepage usually wastes budget. We recommend focused landing pages for each main service or product group, with a clear offer, proof points you can support, and a simple form or call button.

Our guide to landing page design explains what makes a page convert. If your site needs work first, our website and ecommerce development service covers rebuilds.

Google Shopping for Lyon ecommerce brands

For online stores, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns often bring the best return. The product feed is central: titles, descriptions, prices, stock and images must be accurate and match the store. Mismatches cause disapprovals. We optimise titles with the terms shoppers use and split products by margin and performance so budget goes where it earns most.

If you sell into several EU countries, our guide to Shopify Markets in Europe covers multi-currency setup, and our guide to Shopify in France explains platform choices.

Paid search works best as part of a wider plan. SEO builds lasting traffic so you rely less on paid clicks over time. Our guide to SEO in Lyon covers organic search in the city. Regulated sectors

Health, legal, finance and some other sectors face extra advertising rules in France and under Google's own policies. We keep ad claims general and accurate and follow platform rules. Compliance approval of ads and landing pages rests with your business, and you should check official guidance or your adviser for your specific obligations.

Common Google Ads mistakes

  • Broad keywords without negatives. Budget leaks into irrelevant searches.
  • Tracking page views instead of results. Bidding then optimises for the wrong thing.
  • One landing page for everything. Relevance and conversion rates suffer.
  • Changing too much too fast. Campaigns need time and data to learn.
  • Ignoring the search terms report. It shows exactly what you are paying for.

Budgeting and bidding strategy

A sensible Google Ads budget starts from what a customer is worth to you. If you know your average order value or the typical value of a new client, you can work out how much you can afford to pay for each lead or sale. We use that figure to set targets and decide which campaigns deserve more budget.

New accounts usually begin with manual or conservative bidding while conversion data builds up. Once campaigns have recorded enough conversions, smart bidding strategies such as target cost per acquisition or target return on ad spend can work well. Switching too early, before Google has reliable data, often leads to unstable results and wasted spend.

We also look at timing. Many Lyon service businesses get most enquiries during working hours, while online stores may see evening and weekend peaks. Ad schedules and bid adjustments help focus spend on the hours and days that bring results. Seasonal moments matter too, such as the January and summer sales, back-to-school in September and the run-up to Christmas, as well as local events that bring extra visitors to the city.

Reviewing an existing account

If you already run Google Ads, we start with an audit. We check tracking accuracy, account structure, search terms, negative keywords, ad relevance, landing pages and budget distribution. Most accounts have some quick fixes, such as blocking wasteful search terms or pausing ads that never convert. We share findings in plain language and agree priorities with you before making changes, so you always understand why something is being adjusted.

You keep ownership of your Google Ads account at all times. We work inside it with access you grant, and if you ever move on, your campaigns and data stay with you.
