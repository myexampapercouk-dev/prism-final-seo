WordPress Agency for Qatar: Building, Securing and Running Bilingual WordPress Sites

Looking for a WordPress agency in Qatar? How a remote UK studio builds fast, secure Arabic and English WordPress sites for Doha businesses, and what they cost.

This guide explains when WordPress is the right choice, how we build bilingual WordPress sites, how we keep them secure and what the work costs.

Is WordPress the right platform for a Qatar business?

WordPress powers a very large share of the web, and for good reason. It is flexible, it has a huge range of plugins and themes, and almost any developer can work on it. For many Qatar businesses it is a sensible default, but it is not the only option.

WordPress tends to suit:

  • Professional services firms, consultancies and contractors that need many service pages and regular updates.
  • Organisations that publish news, insights or case studies and want an easy editing experience.
  • Businesses that need a bilingual site with flexible content structures.
  • Smaller online stores using WooCommerce that want full control over their site and data.

If your main goal is selling a large product range online, a hosted ecommerce platform may be simpler. If you need a small brochure site that rarely changes, a lighter build could work. We talk through the options before recommending WordPress, and our guide to working with a WordPress agency covers the questions to ask in more detail.

WordPress agency Qatar: how we build your site

Our WordPress projects for Qatar businesses follow a clear sequence. You see progress at every stage on a private staging site, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

  1. Discovery. We learn about your business, customers and goals, and agree the pages and features the site needs.
  2. Sitemap and content plan. We map pages in both languages to what people search for and decide what each page must say.
  3. Design. We design key pages for mobile and desktop, including right-to-left Arabic layouts.
  4. Build. We build with a lightweight theme and the block editor, keeping plugins to the essentials so the site stays fast and easy to maintain.
  5. Testing. We test on real phones and browsers, check forms, analytics and on-page SEO, and review speed.
  6. Launch and training. We connect your domain, go live, and show your team how to edit pages, add posts and manage media.

Every site is handed over with full admin access, and you own the domain, hosting account and content. Our article on website ownership and handover lists what you should always receive.

Building bilingual WordPress sites in Arabic and English

Many Qatar businesses serve Arabic speakers and an international audience, so a bilingual site is often essential. WordPress supports right-to-left languages, but getting a bilingual site right takes more than switching a setting.

We set up each language as its own set of pages with separate URLs, connected by a clear language switcher and hreflang tags. The theme has to mirror properly in Arabic, including menus, icons, forms and tables, and the Arabic font must stay readable on small screens. We write English content. Arabic content is written with your team or a professional translator, and a fluent speaker should review it before launch. Our guide to bilingual Arabic and English websites goes into the technical details.

Speed, hosting and security

WordPress sites can be fast or painfully slow. The difference usually comes down to hosting, theme choice and plugin discipline. We keep pages light, compress images, load fonts carefully and avoid page builders that add heavy code. Our guide to WordPress hosting explains what to look for in a host, including server location and support.

Security is the other big concern. Because WordPress is so widely used, it is a common target for automated attacks. We reduce that risk on every build by:

  • Keeping WordPress core, themes and plugins up to date.
  • Using only well-maintained plugins and removing anything unused.
  • Setting strong user roles, so each person has only the access they need.
  • Adding login protection and two-factor authentication where possible.
  • Setting up regular off-site backups that can be restored quickly.

Our article on WordPress security covers these steps in more depth and explains what to do if a site is compromised.

Search and marketing built into WordPress

A new WordPress site should be ready to rank from launch. We set up page titles, meta descriptions, headings, structured data, an XML sitemap and Google Search Console for both languages. Each core service gets its own page, rather than one page trying to cover everything.

Improving an existing WordPress site

Many Qatar businesses already have a WordPress site that has grown untidy over the years. Common signs include slow loading, a long list of plugins nobody remembers installing, a theme that no longer receives updates, Arabic pages that break on mobile and an admin area that the team avoids using.

In these cases we start with a review rather than a rebuild. We check the theme, every active plugin, hosting, backups, user accounts, speed and on-page SEO in both languages. You then get a plain-English list of issues ranked by risk and effort. Sometimes a careful clean-up is enough. Other times a rebuild on a lean theme is cheaper over the long run than patching an old setup. We explain the trade-offs and let you decide, and if we do rebuild, we plan redirects so existing pages keep their search value.

Compliance and working remotely

Rules on privacy, cookies, consumer information and online selling vary by country and change over time. We build sensible privacy and cookie features into every WordPress site, but you should check current official guidance in Qatar and any market you sell into, and confirm requirements with your own legal adviser.

Working with a remote studio is common and works well when the process is clear. Qatar is a few hours ahead of the UK, which leaves a comfortable overlap for calls. We cannot attend meetings in Doha or arrange on-site photography, but we work through video calls, shared project plans and a staging site you can open on your own phone at any time.
