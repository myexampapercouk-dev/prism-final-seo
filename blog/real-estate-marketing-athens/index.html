Why real estate marketing in Athens needs an international view

The Athens property market attracts a wide mix of buyers. Local families look for homes in neighbourhoods such as Kifisia, Glyfada, Marousi, Kolonaki and Pangrati. International buyers look for apartments in the centre, homes along the Athens Riviera and properties they can rent out. Investors and people relocating for work or retirement compare Athens with other Mediterranean cities before they ever contact an agent.

Many of those international buyers begin their research months before they visit. They search in English for information on buying property in Greece, the best areas to live in Athens, the buying process and running costs. Agencies and developers that answer those questions clearly, in English, earn trust long before a viewing, and are well placed when the buyer is ready to act.

A property website built for overseas buyers

International buyers cannot simply drive past a property, so your website has to do much of the work a first viewing would do. They want high-quality photos, floor plans, clear details on size, condition and energy rating, accurate locations and a sense of the neighbourhood. They also want to know who they will deal with and how the process works when buying from abroad.

We build property sites with detailed listing pages, filters that make sense to buyers, area guides and clear calls to action for viewings, video tours or calls. Where your listings come from a property management system or feed, we work with that data rather than asking your team to enter everything twice. Our estate agent website design guide explains the main features we include.

Speed matters, because property sites are image-heavy and many buyers browse on their phones. We compress images, load galleries efficiently and monitor page speed, following the approach in our Core Web Vitals guide.

Area and buying-process SEO

The large property portals dominate broad listing searches, so most agencies will not outrank them for every apartment query. Agencies can win searches that need local knowledge and honest guidance. Guides to living in particular Athens neighbourhoods, comparisons of the centre and the coast, explainers on the buying process for foreign buyers and pages on property types such as renovated apartments or new-build homes are all realistic targets.

We plan that content with your team, write it in clear English and keep it factual. For topics that touch on law, tax or residency, such as transfer taxes, notary fees or residence permits linked to property purchases, we keep the content general, avoid stating specific figures that may change and point readers to official sources and their own advisers. Your agency approves every page before it goes live.

We also add structured data for your agency, offices and listings where appropriate. Our schema markup guide explains how that helps search engines and AI-powered answers understand your site.

Paid campaigns for agencies and developers

Paid campaigns bring faster results than SEO and suit new developments, seller campaigns and specific buyer groups. A developer launching a new building can use search ads to catch people looking for new apartments in a particular area, and Meta Ads to show the project to people likely to be interested. An agency can run seller campaigns aimed at local homeowners considering a sale.

Reviews and local trust

Buyers and sellers want to know that an agency is reliable before they hand over keys or deposits. A well-kept Google Business Profile with genuine reviews, accurate office details and recent photos helps both local and overseas clients feel confident. We set up or tidy your profiles and help your team build a simple process for asking clients for reviews after a sale or letting. Our Google Business Profile guide covers the essentials. A short, polite reply to every review, positive or negative, also shows future clients how your team handles people.

English and Greek content

We write all of our content in English. For many Athens agencies that is exactly what international buyers need. Your Greek content for local buyers and sellers should come from your own team or a translator you appoint. We can build your site to handle both languages properly, with clear switching and correct language tags, and add further languages later if your team provides the content.
