Why Milan businesses run Google Ads differently

Milan is Italy's main centre for fashion, design, finance, publishing, trade fairs and business services, and many companies there sell both at home and internationally. That usually means two kinds of campaign running side by side.

  • Italian campaigns. Aimed at customers in Milan, Lombardy or the rest of Italy, written in Italian and sending visitors to Italian landing pages.
  • International campaigns. Aimed at buyers in the UK, the rest of Europe or further afield, often in English, with local currencies and delivery details where relevant.

Mixing the two in one campaign makes budgets hard to control and results hard to read. We separate campaigns by language and country so you can see what each market costs and returns. Our guide to international PPC explains this structure in more detail.

Google Ads agency Milan: campaigns for fashion, design and ecommerce brands

Many Milan brands sell products online, from clothing and accessories to furniture, lighting and homeware. For them, Google Shopping and Performance Max often drive most paid revenue, and results depend heavily on the product feed.

That means clear product titles with the words shoppers actually search, accurate prices and availability, good images and sensible grouping by margin or best sellers. Where you sell in several countries, each market needs its own feed details, currency and delivery information. Our guide to Google Shopping management explains what a well-run Shopping account looks like, and our Performance Max guide covers when automated campaigns help and when they need tighter control.

Google Ads for Milan service and B2B companies

Consultancies, agencies, software firms, trade suppliers and professional services in Milan usually want enquiries rather than online sales. Their campaigns focus on high-intent search terms, strong landing pages and careful filtering of irrelevant traffic.

Two habits make the biggest difference here. First, regular review of the search terms report, adding negative keywords so budget is not spent on job seekers, students or unrelated searches; our negative keywords guide explains how. Second, precise location settings, so ads show to people in the areas you actually serve. Our location targeting guide covers the settings that most often cause wasted spend.

Landing pages that turn clicks into enquiries

Even the best campaign struggles if visitors land on a slow or confusing page. Sending Italian searchers to an English page, or international buyers to a page full of Italian-only details, wastes budget quickly. Each campaign should point to a page in the right language that matches the promise made in the ad.

The landing pages that tend to perform best share a few traits:

  • A clear match with the ad. The headline repeats what the searcher was looking for, so they know they are in the right place.
  • Fast loading on mobile. Many clicks come from phones, and slow pages lose visitors before they read anything.
  • One main action. A single, obvious next step, whether that is a form, a call, a booking or adding to basket.
  • Local details where relevant. Prices in the right currency, delivery information for the visitor's country and contact details that make sense to them.
  • Trust signals. Clear business information, straightforward policies and honest descriptions of what you offer.

We review landing pages as part of every account audit and can design or rebuild them where needed. Small changes, such as a clearer headline or a shorter form, often improve results more than another round of bid changes.

Working with a remote Google Ads agency

PPC management suits remote delivery well, because the work happens inside the ad account, analytics and shared reports. You keep ownership of your Google Ads account and grant us access, so your data and history stay with you whatever happens.

  1. Audit. If you already run ads, we review structure, tracking, keywords, ads and landing pages and set out what to fix first.
  2. Plan. We agree goals, markets, languages and budgets, and decide which campaigns to run.
  3. Build or rebuild. We set up campaigns and tracking, write English ads and coordinate Italian copy with your team or translator.
  4. Manage. We adjust bids, budgets, keywords and ads, test new ideas and remove waste.
  5. Report. Monthly reports show spend, conversions and cost per result for each market, with next steps.

Questions to ask any Google Ads agency in Milan

  • Will we own the Google Ads account and all its data?
  • How will you separate Italian and international campaigns?
  • Who writes the Italian ads, and who checks them?
  • Which conversions will you track, and how do you check they are accurate?
  • How is your fee separate from our ad spend?
  • What will monthly reports show, and how often will we speak?

Clear, specific answers are a good sign. Be cautious of guaranteed results, fees hidden inside ad spend or reluctance to give you account access.
