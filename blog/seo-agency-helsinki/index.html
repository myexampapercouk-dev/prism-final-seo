What an SEO agency in Helsinki should do

Helsinki has a strong technology and software sector, a design and consumer brand scene, maritime and industrial firms, professional services and a wide range of local businesses. Many sell to other businesses, many sell abroad, and many serve local customers in person. Good SEO covers:

  • Technical health. Fast, crawlable, well-structured pages without duplicate or broken content.
  • Finnish keyword research. Finnish is highly inflected and uses long compound words, so search phrases vary a lot. Research must reflect how people actually type.
  • Language structure. Finnish and Swedish are both official languages, and many firms also need English. Each version needs its own properly translated pages.
  • Local visibility. A complete Google Business Profile and accurate listings for businesses serving Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa.
  • Content and authority. Useful pages and guides that earn links and citations over time.

Finnish search behaviour and keyword planning

Finnish words change their endings depending on case, so "Helsinki" becomes "Helsingissä" (in Helsinki) or "Helsingin" (of Helsinki). Compound words such as "tilitoimisto" (accounting firm) or "kattoremontti" (roof renovation) are common search terms. Google handles some of this variation, but pages still rank best when they use the forms people search for naturally.

We research keywords using Finnish location and language settings, then review them with your team. If you target several languages or countries, we plan a clear structure with hreflang so each audience reaches the right page. Our international SEO guide and multilingual website design article explain the setup.

SEO for Helsinki technology and B2B firms

Helsinki's software and technology companies often sell internationally from day one, so English-language SEO matters as much as Finnish. Buyers research carefully, compare options and look for clear explanations of use cases, integrations, security and pricing models.

Effective content for these firms includes product and feature pages, use-case pages by industry, comparison pages and practical guides. Our SaaS SEO guide covers this approach in depth, and our SaaS sector page explains how we support software companies.

Local SEO in the Helsinki area

For businesses serving customers in person, local SEO often brings the fastest gains. Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa form one connected metropolitan area, and people often search by city or neighbourhood, such as Kallio, Töölö or Tapiola.

  • Keep your Google Business Profile complete, accurate and active.
  • Use consistent business details across your website and directories.
  • Create location pages only for areas you genuinely serve, with useful local information.
  • Ask happy customers for reviews and respond to every review professionally.

Compliance notes for Finnish websites

This is general information, not legal advice. Check official guidance or speak to your own adviser.

  • GDPR. Finnish businesses are subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation. Your website needs a clear privacy notice and a lawful basis for collecting personal data through forms, newsletters and analytics.
  • Cookie consent. Under EU ePrivacy rules, non-essential cookies such as analytics and advertising tags generally need consent before they are set, and rejecting should be as easy as accepting. Our cookie banner design article covers practical options.
  • Accessibility. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many consumer-facing products and services, including e-commerce. Microenterprises providing services are generally exempt. EN 301 549, which refers to WCAG at level AA, is the usual technical reference.

In regulated sectors such as healthcare and finance, approval of website content rests with your own compliance team.

Choosing an SEO partner in Helsinki

Whether you choose a local agency or a remote studio, ask:

  • Will they research Finnish search phrases properly, including word forms?
  • How will Finnish, Swedish and English versions be structured?
  • Who writes and checks local-language content?
  • What will reporting show, and how often?
  • Do they avoid guaranteeing rankings? No one can promise a position on Google.

A local agency may suit you if you prefer working in Finnish. A remote UK studio can suit businesses comfortable working in English, especially those also targeting the UK or international buyers.

Common SEO mistakes on Finnish websites

When we review websites for Finnish businesses, several issues come up repeatedly. The first is relying on machine translation for Finnish or Swedish pages without review. Search engines can index these pages, but they often target the wrong word forms and read awkwardly, which hurts both rankings and trust.

The second is putting all languages on one URL and switching them with a script or cookie. Search engines then see only one version, and the others never rank. Each language should have its own URL, properly linked with hreflang.

The third is thin service pages. A single page listing every service in a few lines rarely ranks for any of them. Separate pages for each main service, each with real detail and answers to common questions, perform far better.

The fourth is neglecting the Google Business Profile, especially for firms serving Espoo and Vantaa as well as Helsinki. Out-of-date opening hours, missing categories and unanswered reviews all weaken local visibility.

Finally, many sites measure the wrong things. Rankings for a few vanity terms matter far less than enquiries, calls and sales, so we set up reporting around the actions that bring in revenue, with consent handled properly.

Fixing these basics first usually does more for a Finnish website than publishing new content on top of weak foundations. Once the structure is sound, each new page has a much better chance of ranking and bringing in the right visitors.
