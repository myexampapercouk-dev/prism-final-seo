Web Design in London, Ontario: Fast, Accessible Websites for Small Businesses

Web design in London, Ontario: fast, accessible small business websites with local SEO built in, from a UK studio that works remotely with no office in Canada.

  • Web design in London, Ontario should focus on speed, clear local service pages, accessibility and a simple path to call, book or enquire.
  • We are a UK-based studio that designs and builds websites remotely for businesses in London and Southwestern Ontario, with no office in Canada.

Web design in London, Ontario is about building a website that loads fast, explains clearly what you do and who you serve, and turns local visitors into calls, bookings or sales. For most small and growing businesses in the city, that means a mobile-first design, focused service pages, local SEO built in from day one and accessibility treated as a standard rather than an extra.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio. We work remotely with Canadian businesses and have no office in London, Ontario or anywhere else in Canada. Since there is also a London in England, we should be clear: this guide is about London in Southwestern Ontario, and how we approach website projects for businesses there.

  • Trades and home services covering the city and nearby towns.
  • Professional firms such as accountants, consultants and agencies that need a credible, modern presence.
  • Clinics and wellness businesses that need clear service information and online booking.
  • Independent retailers selling in store and online.
  • Manufacturers and B2B suppliers that need to explain technical products to buyers across Canada.
  • Restaurants, cafes and venues that rely on local discovery.

Signs your current website needs replacing

Not every business needs a full rebuild. A refresh or a few targeted fixes can be enough. A new website is usually worth considering when:

  • The site is slow or awkward to use on a phone.
  • You cannot easily update text, photos or prices yourself.
  • Your services have changed but the website still describes the old business.
  • Enquiries arrive by phone or word of mouth, but almost never through the site.
  • The design looks dated next to competitors in London and the surrounding area.

We review your current site first and tell you honestly whether a rebuild or a set of improvements makes more sense.

Speed and Core Web Vitals

Most local searches happen on phones, often on mobile data. A site that takes several seconds to load will lose many of those visitors before they see your message. We design for speed from the start: limited scripts, properly sized images, efficient fonts and clean templates. Our guide to Core Web Vitals explains the measurements Google uses and how we improve them.

Accessibility in Ontario

Ontario has its own accessibility law, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, often shortened to AODA, which includes standards for information and communications that can apply to some organisations' websites. Whether and how it applies depends on the size and type of your organisation. We keep our notes general and are not legal advisers, so please check official provincial guidance or speak to your own adviser about your obligations.

Whatever the legal position, accessible design is good design. We build to widely used accessibility practices: sensible heading structure, readable colour contrast, descriptive link text, alt text for meaningful images, forms with proper labels and layouts that work with keyboards and screen readers.

Local SEO built into the website

A new website is the best moment to get search foundations right. For London businesses we typically:

  • Plan pages around the services and areas people actually search for.
  • Write clear title tags and meta descriptions for every page.
  • Add structured data so search engines understand your business details.
  • Make sure your name, address and phone number match your Google Business Profile.
  • Set up redirects from old URLs so you do not lose existing rankings.

Our guide to choosing a local SEO agency in Canada explains what ongoing local SEO looks like after launch. SEO results typically take three to six months to build.

Which platform is right for you?

We build on WordPress and Shopify, and choose based on what your business needs rather than habit.

  • WordPress suits service businesses, professional firms and content-led sites that need flexible pages and a blog. Our guide to a WordPress small business website explains why, and our guide to choosing a WordPress agency in Canada covers Canadian considerations.
  • Shopify suits retailers and brands selling products online, with payments, shipping and taxes handled within the platform.

Either way, you own the site, the domain and the content, and you get training so your team can make everyday updates.

Landing pages for ads

If you run Google Ads or Meta Ads, sending all traffic to your home page usually wastes budget. A focused landing page with one offer, one message and one form tends to convert better. Our guide to landing page design explains the structure we use for campaign pages.

Privacy, consent and French content

Most websites collect some personal information through forms, analytics or email sign-ups. PIPEDA, Canada's federal private sector privacy law, sets out how businesses should handle that information, and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, known as CASL, covers commercial emails and texts. If you serve customers in Quebec, the province's Law 25 adds its own privacy requirements. We set up forms, cookie notices and consent options to support good practice, but we keep our notes general. Please check official guidance or ask your own adviser, as responsibility for compliance rests with you.

We write in English. If you want pages in French for customers elsewhere in Canada, the content should come from your team or a professional translator, and we build the site to support both languages properly.

  2. Sitemap and content plan covering every page and its purpose.
  3. Design of key page layouts for your review and feedback.
  4. Build on WordPress or Shopify, with speed, accessibility and SEO built in.
