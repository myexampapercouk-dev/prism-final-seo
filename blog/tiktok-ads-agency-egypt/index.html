Why TikTok Ads matter in Egypt

TikTok is widely used in Egypt, especially among younger audiences in Cairo, Giza, Alexandria and other cities. People use it for entertainment, but also to discover products, restaurants, fashion, beauty and services. For brands, that means a chance to reach people before they start searching.

TikTok is a discovery channel, not a search channel. People are not looking for your product when they see your ad, so the creative must earn attention in the first seconds. That changes how campaigns are planned compared with Google Ads, where people already know what they want.

What a TikTok Ads agency in Egypt does

A good agency handles everything around the creative so that each video has the best chance to perform. Our work typically includes:

  • Setting up or auditing your TikTok Ads Manager account and business centre.
  • Installing the TikTok pixel and events API, and checking that purchases or leads are tracked correctly.
  • Planning campaign structure around objectives such as sales, leads or traffic.
  • Building audiences, from broad targeting to custom and lookalike audiences.
  • Writing creative briefs for your team or creators, based on what the data shows.
  • Testing hooks, formats and offers, then moving budget to the winners.
  • Reporting on cost per result, return on ad spend and what to test next.

Creative that works on TikTok

Creative is the biggest factor in TikTok performance. Ads that look like native TikTok content usually outperform polished adverts. Good creative tends to:

  • Hook the viewer in the first two seconds with a question, problem or striking visual.
  • Show the product in use, not just on a plain background.
  • Use real people speaking directly to camera, in the language and dialect of the audience.
  • Include captions, since many people watch without sound.
  • End with a clear, simple call to action.

We do not film or edit video. We write briefs, review the videos your team or creators supply, and use the results of each test to shape the next brief. Fresh creative is important, because TikTok audiences tire of the same ad quickly.

Tracking and landing pages

TikTok campaigns only optimise well if conversions are tracked accurately. We set up the pixel and server-side events, test them, and make sure the right events are sent. Landing pages need to load fast on mobile data, because most TikTok traffic arrives on phones. If your site is slow or hard to use, we may suggest fixes first. Our guide to web design in Cairo covers what a fast, mobile-first site needs.

For ecommerce, cash on delivery is common in Egypt, which can affect how you measure success. We help you track confirmed orders where possible, not only checkouts, so the numbers reflect real revenue.

Rules and responsible advertising

TikTok has its own advertising policies, with restrictions on some product categories and on how certain claims can be made. Egyptian law also applies to advertising and consumer protection. We keep guidance general: check TikTok's current policies and take advice from your own adviser where needed. Approval of ad claims rests with your business. We never target ads at children.

Campaign structure that scales

A clear structure makes TikTok campaigns easier to test and scale. We usually separate prospecting campaigns, which find new customers, from retargeting campaigns, which reach people who have already visited your site or watched your videos. Within prospecting, we start fairly broad, because TikTok's algorithm is good at finding buyers when it has room to learn, then add interest or lookalike audiences to compare against it.

Each ad group gets several creatives so the platform can find the strongest. We avoid changing budgets or settings too often during the learning phase, because constant edits reset the algorithm. When an ad group performs steadily, we raise its budget in measured steps rather than all at once.

Common TikTok Ads mistakes

The mistakes we see most often in Egyptian accounts are simple to avoid:

  • Launching without a working pixel, so TikTok cannot learn who buys.
  • Reusing polished television-style adverts that viewers scroll past.
  • Running one creative for months until performance fades.
  • Judging results on clicks or views instead of confirmed orders or leads.
  • Sending traffic to a slow page with a long checkout.
  • Splitting a small budget across too many ad groups for any to learn.

An audit of an existing account usually starts with these points, because fixing them often improves results before any new spend is added.

It also helps to agree from the start what success looks like. For an online store that may be a target cost per confirmed order; for a service business it may be cost per qualified lead. A shared target keeps every test focused and makes monthly reports easy to act on.
