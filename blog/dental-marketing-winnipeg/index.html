How Winnipeg patients choose a dentist

Most people choose a dentist based on three things: location, availability and trust. They search on a phone for a dentist near them, a specific treatment, or an emergency appointment. They scan the map results, read reviews, look at a couple of websites and then call or book online. In a city spread across neighbourhoods such as St. Vital, Transcona, St. James, River Heights, Fort Garry and the Exchange District, being visible in the right part of the city matters as much as being visible at all.

Winnipeg also has a large newcomer population, a big university community and many families. That means steady demand for general check-ups and hygiene, children's dentistry and emergency care, along with patients researching orthodontics, implants and cosmetic treatment over weeks rather than days. A dental website that turns visits into bookings

Your website should answer the questions patients ask before they call. Are you taking new patients? Where are you and is there parking? What are your hours? Do you see children? How do I book? We build dental websites with:

  • A clear booking button or phone link on every page, especially on mobile.
  • Separate pages for each main treatment, written in plain language and approved by your dentists.
  • Information on new patients, insurance and payment, written in general terms you confirm.
  • Team and clinic pages your practice provides, so patients know what to expect.
  • Fast loading, accessible design and secure hosting.
  • Tracking for calls, forms and online booking clicks.

A small clinic website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and usually takes three to five weeks. Many clinics prefer WordPress; see our guide to WordPress in Winnipeg and our article on web design in Winnipeg. Prices in Canadian dollars are quoted on request.

Local SEO for Winnipeg dental clinics

Local SEO is usually the most important channel for a dental practice. It covers the map results and the organic results below them. We focus on:

  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with the right categories, hours, photos and services.
  • Consistent clinic name, address and phone number across directories.
  • Treatment pages that target the terms patients use, with Winnipeg and the neighbourhoods you genuinely serve.
  • A simple, compliant process for asking happy patients to leave honest reviews, without incentives.
  • Technical health: speed, mobile usability, structured data and internal links.
  • Clear content that AI search tools can read and summarise accurately.

SEO results typically take three to six months to build. Our guides to SEO in Winnipeg and local SEO in Canada go into more detail, and our SEO and GEO service explains the monthly work.

Google Ads and Meta Ads for dentists

Google Ads put your clinic at the top of results for searches such as emergency dentist or dental implants, which is useful while SEO is still building. We structure campaigns around treatments, use location targeting around your clinic, add negative keywords to cut wasted clicks, and send each ad to the matching treatment page. Call and booking tracking shows what actually brings patients.

Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram can work well for family dentistry, cosmetic treatment and new clinic openings, especially with helpful, friendly content. Our guides to Google Ads in Winnipeg and Meta Ads in Winnipeg explain how we set these up. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta and is separate from our fees.

Advertising rules and patient privacy

Dental advertising in Manitoba is subject to professional rules about claims, before-and-after images, testimonials and pricing. We are not regulatory advisers, so our guidance is general. Please check the current guidance from the Manitoba Dental Association or your own adviser before publishing. In practice:

  • We avoid claims of being the best, guaranteed outcomes or misleading offers.
  • We never invent reviews, testimonials or patient stories.
  • Your clinic reviews and approves every page, ad and post before it goes live.
  • Forms ask patients not to share detailed health information, and privacy notices are agreed with your clinic.

Approval of all content rests with your clinic and its compliance processes.

Winnipeg has a long-standing francophone community in St. Boniface and many newcomer communities. If your team speaks other languages, mentioning them clearly can help patients choose you. We write in English; French or other language content comes from your team or a professional translator, and we set it up correctly for search.

Common dental marketing mistakes to avoid

Many clinics spend money on marketing without fixing the basics first. These are the problems we see most often when we audit a practice:

  • An incomplete Google Business Profile. Missing hours, the wrong primary category or no photos make it harder to appear in the map results where most patients choose.
  • One page for every treatment. A single services page cannot rank for implants, orthodontics, emergency care and children's dentistry at the same time. Each main treatment needs its own page.
  • Ads sent to the home page. Someone searching for an emergency dentist wants to see emergency information and a phone number, not a general welcome message.
  • No tracking. Without call and booking tracking, it is impossible to tell which channel brings patients, so budgets are set by guesswork.
  • Slow mobile pages. Most dental searches happen on phones. Heavy images and cluttered layouts lose patients before the page even loads.
  • Inconsistent details. Different phone numbers or old addresses across directories confuse both patients and search engines.

Fixing these usually costs less than a month of advertising and makes every other channel work harder. We start every engagement by checking them.
