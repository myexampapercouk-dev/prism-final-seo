What an SEO company for small business should actually do

Small businesses have limited time and limited budgets, so SEO has to be focused. A large brand can afford to chase thousands of keywords. A plumber in Ohio, a dental practice in Arizona or an online store in Oregon needs to win the few dozen searches that bring real customers.

In practice, a good provider does five things well:

  1. Fixes the foundations: makes sure Google can crawl and index the site, pages load quickly on phones and there are no broken links or duplicate pages holding it back.
  2. Chooses the right searches: identifies terms with clear buying intent that the business can realistically win.
  3. Improves key pages: rewrites service and product pages so they answer customer questions and match those searches.
  4. Builds local visibility: optimises the Google Business Profile, citations and reviews for businesses that serve a local area.
  5. Reports on leads: tracks calls, forms and sales, not just rankings.

If any of these is missing, the work tends to stall. Our small business SEO guide covers each step in more detail. It is written for UK firms, but the method is the same in the US.

Local SEO versus national SEO for small businesses

The first decision is whether you are competing locally or nationally. A roofer in Tampa competes with other roofers within driving distance. An online shop selling specialist tea competes with every store in the country. The work overlaps, but the priorities differ.

Local service businessMap pack and city searchesGoogle Business Profile, location pages, reviews, citations
Multi-location small businessEach branch's local areaOne profile and page per real location, consistent details

Professional or B2B serviceSpecific problem and service searchesService pages, guides that answer buyer questions

How to judge an SEO company before you sign

The small business SEO market is crowded, and quality varies widely. Some providers sell cheap packages that publish low-value content or buy poor links. Others charge high fees for reports that nobody reads. A few simple checks will protect you.

  • Ask what they will do in the first ninety days. A clear answer names specific pages, fixes and searches.
  • Ask how they build links. Avoid anyone who mentions bulk links, private networks or guaranteed numbers of links per month.
  • Ask who owns the accounts. Your website, Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics should be in your name.
  • Ask what the report shows. It should connect rankings and traffic to enquiries or sales.
  • Be wary of guarantees. No honest provider can guarantee a number one ranking, because nobody controls Google.
  • Check the contract. Long fixed terms with heavy exit fees are a warning sign for a small business.

How long SEO takes for a small business

SEO results typically take three to six months. The timeline depends on how competitive your market is, how healthy your site is when we start and how much content needs creating. A new website in a busy city will take longer than an established site in a smaller town.

A typical first six months looks like this. Month one covers the audit, tracking setup and the most urgent technical fixes. Months two and three improve the main service or category pages and strengthen local listings. Months four to six add new content, earn links and refine pages based on what the data shows. Small business SEO mistakes we see often

Many small business sites have the same handful of problems, and fixing them is often the fastest win.

  • One page trying to rank for every service, rather than a page per service.
  • Location pages that copy the same text with a different city name.
  • No clear call to action on mobile, so visitors leave without calling.
  • Missing or inconsistent business details across directories.
  • Blog posts written for search engines rather than for customers.
  • No conversion tracking, so nobody knows which pages bring leads.

What you need to provide as a small business owner

SEO works best as a partnership. Even with a strong provider, a small business owner holds knowledge nobody else has. We usually ask for a short list of things at the start.

  • Access to your website, Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics.
  • A clear list of your services, the areas you cover and the jobs you most want more of.
  • The questions customers ask most often on the phone or by email.
  • Photos of real work, premises or products, if you have them.
  • About thirty minutes a month to review the plan and approve new content.

That last point matters. You know your customers, your prices and what you can actually deliver, so we check content with you before it goes live. In regulated fields such as healthcare, finance or law, final approval of any claims always rests with you or your compliance adviser.

The fundamentals of small business SEO, such as well-structured service pages, accurate business details and honest FAQs, also improve the chance of being mentioned in AI answers. FAQs

What does an SEO company for small business do?

It improves your website and online presence so that customers find you in Google. That includes technical fixes, page improvements, local listings, content, link building and reporting on the calls and sales SEO brings in.

How much should a small business pay for SEO?

Our retainers are £350, £750 or £1,500 a month depending on scope. US dollar quotes are available on request. Be cautious of very cheap packages that rely on low-quality content or links.

Does Prism Works Studio have a US office?

No. We are UK-based, work remotely and have no office in the United States. We work with US clients through video calls, dashboards and email.

How long before small business SEO shows results?

SEO results typically take three to six months. Less competitive local searches often improve first, while national or busy city markets take longer.

Is local SEO or national SEO better for my small business?

It depends on who your customers are. If they come from a set area, local SEO comes first. If you sell online across the country, national SEO for product and category searches matters more.

Who owns the accounts if I stop working with an SEO company?

You should. We set up your website access, Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics in your name, so you keep your website, profile and data if you decide to stop. Agree contract terms in writing before you start.
