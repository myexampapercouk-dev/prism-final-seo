Shopify Agency Chicago: How a Remote Shopify Partner Works for Chicago Brands

Looking for a Shopify agency in Chicago? A remote team can design, build, migrate and grow your Shopify store. Here is what the work covers and what it costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify agency for a Chicago brand needs to understand US shoppers, Shopify itself and your operations, not to sit in an office in the Loop.
  • Most Chicago Shopify projects fall into four types: a new build, a redesign, a migration from another platform or ongoing growth work.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US brands, with ecommerce builds from £1,500 to £3,500 and other currencies quoted on request.

If you are looking for a Shopify agency in Chicago, what matters most is whether the team can build a fast, clear store that sells to US shoppers and that your own staff can run day to day. A remote Shopify partner can do that well for Chicago food brands, apparel labels, homeware makers and B2B suppliers, as long as the project is planned around your products, your fulfilment and your customers.

We are a UK-based studio that works remotely with US brands. We have no Chicago office and no US office. This guide explains what a Shopify agency should handle for a Chicago business, the main types of project, how remote working runs across the time difference and what a Shopify build costs.

What a Shopify agency in Chicago should handle

Chicago has a broad mix of online sellers. Food and drink producers, fashion and accessories labels, furniture and homeware makers, beauty brands and wholesale suppliers all sell through Shopify, often alongside a physical shop, a market stall or trade accounts. Whatever you sell, a good Shopify partner should cover:

  • Store planning: collections, navigation, product data and the journeys shoppers take from search or social to checkout.
  • Theme design and build: a theme that suits your brand, loads quickly on phones and is easy for your team to edit.
  • Apps and integrations: only the apps you need, connected properly to your inventory, shipping, email and accounting tools.
  • Checkout and settings: payments, shipping rates, tax settings and policies set up correctly for the US.
  • SEO foundations: clean URLs, useful product and collection copy, structured data and redirects if you are moving platforms.
  • Training and handover: clear notes so your team can add products, run sales and update pages without calling us.

Our guide to setting up a Shopify store walks through the core settings in order. The screens are the same for US stores, though shipping, tax and payment options differ.

Shopify for Chicago food, apparel and B2B sellers

Chicago's online sellers have different needs depending on what they sell and how they get it to customers.

  • Food and drink brands: local delivery zones, local pickup, shipping rules for perishable items and clear allergen and ingredient information. Labelling and food rules sit with you and your adviser, but the store needs space to show that information clearly.
  • Apparel and accessories: size guides, product variants, good photography and easy returns pages, because fit questions are a common reason shoppers hesitate.
  • Homeware and furniture: large images, delivery lead times, freight shipping rules for bulky items and options such as swatches or made-to-order products.
  • B2B and wholesale suppliers: trade accounts, price lists, minimum order quantities and quick reorder tools, either through Shopify features or carefully chosen apps.

Many Chicago brands also sell in person, at a shop, a market or events. Shopify can keep online and in-person stock in one place, which saves a lot of manual work if it is set up properly from the start.

Speed, conversion and SEO on Shopify

A store that looks good but loads slowly or confuses shoppers will lose sales, wherever it is built. We plan every Shopify project around three things:

  • Speed: a lean theme, compressed images and as few apps as possible. Our Shopify speed optimisation guide covers the usual causes of slow stores.
  • Conversion: clear product pages, honest delivery information, trust signals and a short path to checkout. Our Shopify conversion guide explains what to test first.
  • Search: collection pages built around real US search terms, unique product descriptions and structured data. Our Shopify SEO guide covers the platform's strengths and limits.

If organic search is a big part of your plan, our guide to SEO in Chicago explains how search work fits around a Chicago business, and our Chicago web design guide covers sites that are not built on Shopify.

Sales tax, accessibility and privacy

US stores carry a few responsibilities that are worth raising early. Shopify includes tools for collecting sales tax, but which states you need to collect in, and at what rates, depends on your own situation. Illinois and Chicago have their own rules, and selling into other states can create obligations there too. We set up the settings you ask for, but the decisions should come from your accountant or tax adviser, using official state guidance.

Accessibility matters for both shoppers and legal risk. We build to recognised accessibility guidelines, with good contrast, keyboard access, clear labels and alt text, but we do not give legal advice on the Americans with Disabilities Act. Privacy is similar: if you use tracking, email marketing or customer accounts, check your notices and consent approach against official guidance or with your own adviser.

How a remote Shopify agency works with a Chicago brand

Chicago is usually six hours behind the UK, so your morning overlaps with our afternoon. That shared window is enough for calls, reviews and quick decisions, and the rest of the work moves forward while you are busy running the business. A typical project runs like this:

  1. Discovery call: your products, customers, operations and goals, plus access to your current store or platform.
  2. Plan and quote: a written scope covering pages, features, apps, migration needs and timeline.
  3. Design: layouts for the key templates, shared for comments in writing or on a call.
  4. Build and content: theme work, product setup, integrations and redirects, on a development store you can review.
  5. Testing: checkout, shipping, tax settings, emails and mobile layouts checked before launch.
  6. Launch and handover: go-live, training and a short period of support for fixes.

Everything is written down, from decisions to logins to next steps, so nothing depends on a single meeting. If you want a second view on working with a remote team, our guide to Shopify agencies for US brands covers the same questions from a national angle.

How much a Shopify agency costs for a Chicago brand

Shopify costs depend on the number of products, the amount of custom design, migration work and the apps and integrations involved. Our prices are in pounds, and we can quote other currencies, including US dollars, on request:

  • Ecommerce build: typically £1,500 to £3,500, depending on scope.
  • Timeline: usually five to twelve weeks from agreed plan to launch.
  • Ongoing support and growth: monthly retainers at Launch £350, Growth £750 or Scale £1,500.

Shopify's own subscription, paid apps and any ad spend are paid separately. Our pricing page sets out what each option includes, and our website and ecommerce development service explains how we plan and build stores.

Questions to ask any Shopify agency

  • Will you build on an existing theme or a custom one, and why?
  • Which apps will we need, and what will they cost each month?
  • How will you protect our search traffic if we are migrating?
  • Who owns the store, theme and accounts when the project ends?
  • What support do you offer after launch, and how do we request changes?

Straight answers to these questions tell you more than a portfolio page. Be cautious of quotes that do not mention redirects, testing or training.

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Chicago?

Look for a clear written scope, honest timelines and a plan for speed, SEO and training. Ask how they handle apps, migrations and support after launch, and who will own the store and accounts.

Do you have an office in Chicago?

No. We are a UK-based studio and work remotely with US brands. We have no Chicago office or any other US office, and we run projects through video calls, shared documents and a development store you can review.

Can you migrate our store to Shopify from another platform?

Yes. We move products, customers and content where the platforms allow it, set up redirects from old URLs and check key pages before and after launch so search traffic is protected as far as possible.

Can you set up sales tax for our Shopify store?

We can configure Shopify's tax settings based on your instructions. Which states you collect in and how is a decision for you and your accountant, using official state guidance.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

Our ecommerce builds typically cost £1,500 to £3,500, with ongoing retainers from £350 a month. Prices are in pounds, and we can quote US dollars or other currencies on request.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Most builds take five to twelve weeks from an agreed plan to launch. Large catalogues, complex integrations or migrations sit at the longer end of that range.
