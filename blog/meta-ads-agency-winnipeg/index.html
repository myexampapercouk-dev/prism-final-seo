Who this guide is for

Winnipeg has a diverse economy that includes transport and logistics, agriculture and food processing, manufacturing, finance and insurance, healthcare, education, retail and hospitality. It is also the main market for a large surrounding region, so many businesses advertise to the city and to communities across Manitoba. Meta advertising tends to suit businesses that sell to consumers or small firms and can show their offer visually. We usually hear from:

  • Home services and trades such as renovation, roofing, HVAC, landscaping and snow clearing firms.
  • Retailers and online shops selling across Manitoba or nationally.
  • Restaurants, venues and leisure businesses promoting events, menus and bookings.
  • Clinics and wellness businesses that need careful, policy-aware ads.
  • Education and training providers recruiting for courses and programmes.

Targeting Winnipeg and Manitoba audiences

Location targeting on Meta is flexible. You can target the whole city, specific postal areas, a radius around a location, or the province as a whole. For many Winnipeg businesses, the right choice depends on how far customers will travel or how far you will go to serve them.

Meta's delivery system has become better at finding buyers with broad targeting, so we often start with wide audiences inside the right location and let creative do the filtering. Custom audiences built from website visitors, video viewers and customer lists, where you have the right consent, then help with retargeting and with finding similar people.

Seasonality matters in Manitoba. Winter and summer change what people buy, when they buy it and how they spend their time. Trades may push heating, snow clearing and indoor work in colder months and outdoor projects when the weather turns. Retailers and venues see their own patterns around holidays and events. We plan budgets and creative around these cycles rather than running the same ads all year.

Creative that works on Facebook and Instagram

Most of the improvement in a Meta account now comes from creative. We plan concepts around the questions, worries and wants of your customers, then produce variations that test different hooks, formats and offers.

  • Short video for Reels and Stories, often filmed simply on a phone.
  • Static images with a clear message and strong visual.
  • Carousels for showing products, steps or before-and-after work.
  • Lead forms for quick enquiries without leaving the app.

We use AI-powered tools to speed up research, draft copy variations and organise testing, but every ad is reviewed by a person before it runs. Our guide to an AI marketing agency in Canada explains how this fits into wider campaigns.

French and multilingual ads

Most Winnipeg campaigns run in English, but some businesses also reach French-speaking customers in Manitoba or across Canada. Prism writes in English. French ad copy and landing pages should be written or checked by your own team or a professional translator. We then set up the campaigns, audiences and tracking so each language version is measured properly.

Privacy, consent and advertising rules

Meta advertising involves tracking pixels, customer lists and lead forms, so privacy matters. In general terms, PIPEDA is the federal privacy law that covers how many private-sector businesses handle personal information. CASL sets rules for commercial electronic messages, which affects how you follow up with leads by email or text. Some provinces have their own privacy laws, and Quebec's Law 25 adds specific requirements for businesses dealing with people in Quebec. Meta also has its own advertising policies, with stricter rules for areas such as housing, employment, credit, health and politics.

These notes are general information only, not legal advice. Please check official guidance and speak to your own adviser. In regulated sectors, approval of ad copy, targeting and lead handling rests with you and your compliance team.

How remote management works

We manage Meta accounts from the UK through your own Business Manager, so you keep ownership of your ad account, pixel and data. A typical process is:

  1. Discovery call about your goals, offers, customers and past results.
  2. Account and tracking audit to fix any measurement gaps first.
  3. Campaign plan setting out audiences, creative concepts and budget.
  4. Launch and testing with weekly checks and changes.
  5. Monthly reporting by email and video call.

Winnipeg is usually six hours behind the UK, so calls work well in your morning. We have no local office, so all meetings are online.

What to expect and when

Meta campaigns can start bringing leads or sales within days, but it usually takes a few weeks of data for the delivery system to settle and for testing to show clear winners. Paid results typically build over three to six months as creative, audiences and landing pages improve together. We do not promise specific results or costs per lead.

Common mistakes we fix in Meta accounts

When we review existing accounts, the same issues come up often. Tracking is missing or double counting, so the delivery system optimises towards the wrong signal. Budgets are spread across too many small ad sets, so none gathers enough data to learn. Creative has not changed in months, so the same people see the same ads until results fade. Landing pages load slowly on mobile or ask for too much information. Fixing these basics usually comes before any new campaign ideas, because they affect every dollar you spend.
