Advertising in Turku

Turku is the oldest city in Finland and a major centre for shipbuilding and maritime industries, biotechnology and pharmaceuticals, universities, logistics and tourism linked to the archipelago and the ferry routes to Sweden and Åland. The city is officially bilingual, with a significant Swedish-speaking population, so many businesses advertise in both Finnish and Swedish.

That shapes how Google Ads accounts should be built:

  • Separate campaigns or ad groups by language, with ads and landing pages to match.
  • Tight location targeting for local services, covering Turku and nearby municipalities.
  • Specialist B2B terms for maritime, industrial and life sciences suppliers.
  • Seasonal demand for tourism, events and summer archipelago trips.

Search campaigns first

For most Turku businesses, search campaigns are the best place to start. They show ads to people actively searching for a product or service, which usually brings the highest-intent traffic. We build tightly themed ad groups, use negative keywords to block irrelevant searches and review the search terms report regularly.

Performance Max and display campaigns can work well once tracking is reliable and there is enough conversion data, especially for ecommerce. We add them when they make sense rather than by default.

Tracking that measures real results

Without reliable tracking, Google's bidding works blind. We set up conversion tracking for form submissions, phone calls, purchases and, where possible, leads that turn into customers later. This lets the account optimise for business results rather than clicks. Cookie consent matters here: Finland follows the EU GDPR, so tracking should respect visitors' choices, and consent mode should be configured properly. Your own adviser should check your privacy notice, and official guidance is available from the Finnish data protection authority.

Landing pages that convert

Ads only pay off if the page they lead to does its job. A good landing page matches the search, explains the offer quickly, shows trust signals you genuinely have and makes the next step obvious. For bilingual campaigns, Finnish ads should lead to Finnish pages and Swedish ads to Swedish pages. If your site needs work, our multilingual website design guide and our Core Web Vitals guide explain the foundations.

How long until Google Ads delivers?

Google Ads can bring traffic from the first day, but a new account usually needs several weeks to gather data and settle. Meaningful, stable improvements in cost per lead or return on ad spend typically take 3 to 6 months of steady testing and optimisation.

Working with a remote UK studio

Turku is normally two hours ahead of the UK, so working days overlap comfortably. You keep ownership of your Google Ads account and billing, and we work in it with manager access. Each month we share a short report covering spend, enquiries or sales, cost per result and the actions planned next.

Common Google Ads mistakes

Many accounts we review waste a large share of their budget on avoidable problems. The most common include:

  • Broad match keywords with no negatives, which show ads for loosely related searches.
  • One campaign for everything, so budget drifts to the cheapest clicks rather than the best ones.
  • No conversion tracking, or tracking that counts page views as conversions.
  • Sending every ad to the home page instead of a matching landing page.
  • Mixing languages in a single ad group, which confuses targeting and reporting.
  • Set and forget, where nobody reviews search terms or tests new ads.

Fixing these basics usually improves results before any new spend is needed. When we take over an account, we start with an audit and a short list of priority fixes.

Your first ninety days

A typical start follows a simple pattern. In the first weeks we audit any existing account, agree goals and set up tracking. Next we build or restructure campaigns, write ads and check landing pages. Over the following weeks we review search terms, adjust bids and budgets and test new ads, then report on what is working and what to change. By the end of the third month you should have a clear picture of cost per enquiry or sale and where extra budget would be best spent. Good results also depend on fast follow-up: enquiries that are answered quickly are far more likely to turn into customers.
