Shopify Agency Miami: How Remote Shopify Design and Builds Work

Looking for a Shopify agency in Miami? A remote team can design, build and grow your Shopify store. Here is what the work covers, how it runs and what it costs.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify agency for a Miami brand should cover design, build, speed, SEO foundations and a clean handover, not just a theme install.
  • Miami brands often sell in English and Spanish and ship beyond Florida, so languages, markets and shipping rules need planning early.
  • We are a UK-based studio building Shopify stores remotely for US clients; ecommerce builds are priced in pounds with other currencies on request.

If you are searching for a Shopify agency in Miami, you need a team that can design a store your customers trust, build it properly on Shopify and set it up to grow. A remote agency can do all of that for a Miami brand, because Shopify projects run through shared designs, a staging store and video calls rather than a local office.

We are a UK-based studio that designs and builds Shopify stores remotely for US businesses. We have no Miami office and no US office. This guide explains what a Shopify agency should deliver for a Miami brand, the decisions to make about languages and markets, how a remote project runs, how long it takes and what it costs.

What a Shopify agency in Miami should deliver

Miami's online brands cover fashion and swimwear, beauty and wellness, food and drink, homeware, art and lifestyle goods, often with a strong visual identity and a customer base that stretches across the US and Latin America. A good Shopify project usually includes:

  • Discovery: agreeing goals, products, customers, integrations and what success looks like before any design work.
  • Design: a theme customised or extended to fit your brand, with clear product pages, collections and navigation on mobile first.
  • Build: products, variants, collections, metafields, shipping, taxes and payments set up correctly.
  • Apps: only the apps you genuinely need, chosen with speed and running costs in mind.
  • SEO foundations: clean URLs, titles, structured data, redirects from any old site and a sensible collection structure.
  • Testing: checkout, emails, discounts and devices tested before launch.
  • Handover: training, documentation and full ownership of the store and its content.

Our guides to Shopify theme customisation and auditing Shopify apps explain how we keep stores fast and easy to manage.

Selling in English and Spanish from Miami

Many Miami brands serve customers who prefer Spanish, either in Florida or across Latin America. Shopify supports multiple languages and markets, but doing this well takes planning:

  • Translated content: product descriptions, policies and key pages written properly in each language, not left to raw automatic translation.
  • Language URLs: separate language versions that search engines can index, with the right hreflang tags.
  • Markets: deciding which countries you will sell to, in which currencies, and how duties and shipping are shown.
  • Customer service: making sure order emails, returns and support work in the languages you offer.

Our multilingual website design guide covers the planning, and our guide to selling internationally from an online store explains the wider decisions on shipping, currency and returns.

Shipping, taxes and payments for a Florida store

Shopify handles much of the set-up, but each store still needs decisions on shipping zones, rates and delivery promises, especially if you ship across the US or internationally. Sales tax obligations in the US depend on where you have nexus and where your customers are. We can configure Shopify's tax settings to match the approach you choose, but the decisions on registration and collection rest with you and your accountant, who should check current official guidance.

Payment options matter too. Shopify Payments, digital wallets and buy-now-pay-later options can all be set up, and your choice affects checkout speed and fees. Our guide to Shopify checkout settings covers the options worth reviewing.

How a remote Shopify project works

Miami is usually five hours behind the UK, which gives a comfortable overlap across your morning and our afternoon. A typical remote project runs like this:

  • Discovery call: goals, products, customers, integrations and timeline agreed, then written up as a brief.
  • Design: key templates designed and shared for comments, usually homepage, collection, product and cart.
  • Build on a development store: you can review progress at any time on a private preview.
  • Content and products: imported or entered, with your team trained to manage them.
  • Testing and launch: test orders, devices and redirects checked, then the domain connected.
  • After launch: fixes and support, with optional ongoing help.

Your store, domain and apps stay in your name throughout. If you are comparing remote options, our guide to the Shopify agency choices for US brands sets out what to check.

Designing a Shopify store that converts

A store can look beautiful and still lose sales. For Miami brands with a strong visual style, the challenge is keeping that personality while making the buying journey simple. The areas we pay most attention to are:

  • Product pages: large, fast-loading images, clear prices, variant pickers that are easy to use on a phone, and answers to common questions about sizing, materials, delivery and returns close to the add-to-cart button.
  • Collections: logical groupings and filters so shoppers can narrow a large range quickly, rather than scrolling through everything.
  • Navigation: a menu that reflects how customers think about your products, not how your warehouse is organised.
  • Trust: clear contact details, delivery and returns information, secure payment badges where appropriate and genuine reviews if you collect them.
  • Cart and checkout: few distractions, visible delivery costs and the payment methods your customers expect.
  • Speed: compressed images, restrained use of apps and scripts, and a theme that performs well on mobile connections.

Most Shopify traffic arrives on mobile, so we design and test the mobile experience first and then scale up to larger screens. We also make sure the store is accessible, with readable text, sensible colour contrast and controls that work with a keyboard and screen readers.

Growing the store after launch

Launch is the start rather than the finish. Once the store is live, growth usually comes from a mix of search, paid campaigns and email. Product and collection pages need ongoing SEO work as your range changes, Shopping and social campaigns depend on an accurate product feed, and abandoned cart and post-purchase emails help turn more visitors into repeat customers. Planning for these during the build, for example by setting up clean product data and tracking from day one, saves rework later.

What to ask before hiring a Shopify agency

  • Will we own the store, theme changes, content and domain?
  • Which apps do you plan to use, and what will they cost each month?
  • How will you protect our existing rankings if we are moving platform?
  • How will you handle Spanish content and international markets, if we need them?
  • What training and documentation do we get at handover?
  • What support is available after launch?

FAQs

How do I choose a Shopify agency in Miami?

Look for a clear process, examples of how they approach design and performance, honest timelines and a commitment that you own everything. Ask how they handle apps, SEO, testing and support after launch.

Do you have an office in Miami?

No. We are a UK-based studio and work remotely with US businesses. We have no Miami office or other US office, and we run projects through video calls, shared designs and a private preview store.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

Our ecommerce websites are typically £1,500-£3,500, with larger or custom projects quoted after discovery. Prices are in pounds, and other currencies are quoted on request. Shopify plan and app costs are separate.

Can you build a Shopify store in English and Spanish?

Yes. Shopify supports multiple languages and markets. We plan the language structure, set up translated content and make sure each version can be indexed properly by search engines.

How long does a Shopify build take?

Ecommerce builds typically take five to twelve weeks, depending on the number of products, templates, integrations and languages, and how quickly content and feedback are ready.

Can you move our store to Shopify without losing rankings?

We plan migrations to protect rankings, with URL mapping, redirects and content checks. Some short-term movement is normal, but careful redirects and testing reduce the risk considerably.
