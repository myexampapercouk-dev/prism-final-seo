Choosing an SEO Agency for a Vancouver Business: What Good Remote SEO Looks Like

Hiring an SEO agency in Vancouver? How remote SEO works on Pacific Time, what a sound plan covers, what it costs and the questions to ask before you sign.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Vancouver business does not need a local office to get good SEO, but it does need an agency that understands local search, Canadian searchers and how Google treats British Columbia locations.
  • A sound SEO plan covers technical health, content mapped to real queries, local signals and links earned on merit, and it reports on enquiries rather than rankings alone.
  • SEO results typically take three to six months to show, so agree reporting, ownership of accounts and a review point before any retainer starts.

The right SEO agency for a Vancouver business is one that can fix your site's technical foundations, write content that matches what British Columbia customers actually search for, and show you how that work turns into enquiries or sales. That agency does not have to sit in Gastown: Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that handles SEO remotely for Canadian clients, and we have no office or staff in Vancouver or elsewhere in Canada.

This guide explains how remote SEO works on Pacific Time, what a good plan should include, how pricing is usually structured, and the questions worth asking any agency before you commit.

What an SEO agency in Vancouver should actually do

SEO is often sold as a mystery. It should not be. Whether you run a dental clinic in Kitsilano, a construction firm in Surrey or an online store shipping from Richmond, the work falls into a handful of clear areas:

  • Technical health. Making sure Google can crawl, render and index your pages, that redirects are clean and that the site loads quickly on mobile. Our technical SEO audit checklist shows what that review covers.
  • Keyword and intent mapping. Matching each page to the phrases people in Vancouver and across BC really use, including suburb and neighbourhood variants where they matter.
  • Content. Service pages, location pages and guides that answer questions properly rather than repeating a keyword.
  • Local signals. A complete Google Business Profile, consistent business details and genuine reviews.
  • Authority. Links and mentions earned through useful content and digital PR, not bought in bulk.
  • Measurement. Tracking enquiries, calls and sales, so you can see whether SEO is paying its way.

Increasingly, it also means making content easy for AI search tools to quote. Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO explains why clear, well-structured answers now matter in two places at once.

Local search in Vancouver: what makes it different

Metro Vancouver is a cluster of cities rather than one market. Someone in North Vancouver searching for a physiotherapist is unlikely to travel to Langley, and Google knows it. That shapes local SEO in a few ways:

  • Proximity matters. Map results lean heavily on where the searcher is. A single Google Business Profile in Burnaby will not rank everywhere across the Lower Mainland.
  • Service areas need honest pages. If you serve several municipalities, each location page should say something genuinely useful about that area, not swap the town name into the same template.
  • Multilingual audiences. Vancouver has large communities who search in languages other than English. If that is part of your market, the content needs to be written or reviewed by fluent speakers, and the site structure needs to support it. Our guide to multilingual website design covers the options.
  • Canadian spelling and terms. Searchers in BC use Canadian conventions. Content should match the language of your customers, which is something we check with you during planning.

For a broader view across provinces, our local SEO guide for Canadian businesses goes into map rankings, citations and reviews in more depth.

What SEO costs for a Vancouver business

We price SEO as a monthly retainer. Our published tiers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, and the right one depends on how competitive your market is and how much content and technical work is needed. We can quote in other currencies on request. Full details sit on our pricing page.

Whoever you hire, a few principles help you compare quotes fairly:

  • Ask what is delivered each month. A retainer should map to specific work: fixes, pages, outreach, reporting.
  • Be wary of guarantees. No honest agency can guarantee a position on Google, because nobody controls the algorithm.
  • Look at the minimum term. SEO takes time, but a long lock-in with no review point is a risk for you.
  • Check who owns what. Your Google Business Profile, analytics and Search Console should stay in your name.

Our article on how much SEO costs was written for UK buyers, but the logic of what drives price applies just as well in Canada.

Is a remote SEO agency a sensible choice?

Working with an agency overseas is common in SEO, because the work happens on websites and in shared tools. It suits some businesses better than others.

Privacy, accessibility and other checks

SEO touches tracking, forms and content, so a few regulatory points come up. Private-sector organisations in BC have obligations under provincial and federal privacy law, and commercial email in Canada is covered by anti-spam legislation. Accessibility expectations are also rising. We build with good accessibility practice and transparent tracking in mind, but we are not lawyers: rules change, so check current official guidance or take legal advice for your own situation.

What to prepare before an SEO project starts

A little preparation on your side makes the first months far more productive. Before the discovery call, it helps to have:

  • Admin or shared access to your website, Google Analytics, Search Console and Google Business Profile.
  • A list of the services or products that matter most to revenue, in order of priority.
  • The areas you genuinely serve, and any you want to grow into.
  • A note of past SEO work, including any links bought or content removed, so nothing surprises us in the audit.

If your site is due a rebuild anyway, it is usually better to plan SEO alongside it than to bolt it on afterwards.

Questions to ask before hiring

  1. What will you do in the first three months, and how will I know it is working?
  2. Will the accounts, content and data stay in my business's name?
  3. How do you build links, and can I see examples of the type of content you would pitch?
  4. How do you handle local SEO for several locations or service areas?
  5. What does the monthly report include, and does it show enquiries or sales?
  6. What happens if I want to stop or change the scope?

Clear answers to these tell you more than any sales deck. Our SEO and GEO service page explains how we approach each of these areas, and you can get in touch to talk through your site.

FAQs

How do I choose an SEO agency in Vancouver?

Look for an agency that explains its plan in plain terms, keeps accounts in your name, reports on enquiries as well as rankings and avoids guarantees. Location matters less than clarity, method and how well they understand your market.

Does Prism Works Studio have an office in Vancouver?

No. We are a UK-based studio and work with Canadian clients remotely, using video calls, shared documents and dashboards. We schedule calls for Vancouver mornings.

How long does SEO take to work for a BC business?

Results typically take three to six months to show, depending on how competitive your market is, the state of the site and how much new content is needed. Technical fixes can sometimes show earlier effects.

How much does an SEO agency in Vancouver cost?

Our retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month, with other currencies quoted on request. Other agencies price differently, so compare what is delivered each month rather than the headline figure.

Can you help with local SEO across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. We work on Google Business Profiles, location and service-area pages, reviews and consistent business details, while keeping each location page genuinely useful rather than templated.

Can you write SEO content in languages other than English?

We write in English. Where a site needs other languages, those pages are written with your team or a professional translator, and we handle the structure and technical setup so each version can be indexed properly.
