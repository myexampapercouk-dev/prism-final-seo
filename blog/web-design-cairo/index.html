Web Design in Cairo: Fast, Bilingual Websites for Egyptian Businesses

Web design in Cairo from a UK-based remote studio: fast, mobile-first Arabic and English websites, from £1,000, with no office in Egypt and clear timelines.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 13 November 2026 · 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • Web design in Cairo should start with mobile speed, clear Arabic and English pages and easy contact options such as WhatsApp and click-to-call.
  • Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that designs and builds websites for Cairo businesses remotely, with no office in Egypt.
  • Smaller websites cost £1,000 to £2,000 and take 3 to 5 weeks; e-commerce sites cost £1,500 to £3,500, and Egyptian pound pricing is quoted on request.

Web design in Cairo works best when the site loads quickly on a phone, explains what you do in both Arabic and English, and makes it easy for visitors to call, message or buy. Prism Works Studio designs and builds websites for Cairo businesses, from small service sites to online shops, working remotely from the UK.

We are a UK-based web design and digital marketing studio with no office in Cairo or anywhere in Egypt. We work through video calls, shared design previews and a clear project plan. We write in English; Arabic copy comes from your team or a translator, and we build both language versions properly.

Who we build websites for in Cairo

Cairo is a huge, busy market with businesses of every size. We build sites for:

  • Service businesses such as clinics, law and accounting firms, training centres and consultancies.
  • Online shops selling clothing, cosmetics, home goods, food products or accessories.
  • Real estate developers and brokers showing compounds and units in New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed and the New Administrative Capital.
  • Startups and software companies that need a sharp product site.
  • Exporters that want a professional English site for buyers abroad.

Most visitors in Cairo arrive on a phone, often on mobile data and sometimes on a slower connection. They want to know quickly what you offer, whether you can be trusted and how to get in touch. A good site covers:

Designing for Arabic and English

A bilingual site is more than a translated copy. Arabic pages read right to left, so menus, icons, sliders and forms must mirror properly. Arabic fonts need to be clear on small screens. Numbers, dates and prices should display correctly in both languages. Each language version needs its own URL and the right language tags, so Google shows Arabic visitors the Arabic page and English visitors the English one.

We plan both versions from the start rather than adding Arabic later. We write the English copy, and your team or a translator provides the Arabic, which we place and check for layout issues. Our guide to a bilingual Arabic and English website explains the details.

Choosing a platform

We build mainly on WordPress and Shopify. The right choice depends on what your site needs to do:

  • WordPress suits service businesses, content-heavy sites and companies that want to edit pages and publish articles easily. Our guide to a WordPress small business website covers the basics.
  • Shopify suits online shops that want a reliable checkout, simple product management and app integrations. You will need to check which payment options are available for your Egyptian business, and our team can help connect the ones you choose.
  • WooCommerce on WordPress can suit shops that need more control or a local payment gateway plugin.

Whatever we build on, you own the site, the domain and the hosting account. Our WordPress hosting guide explains what to look for in a host.

Online shops for Egyptian customers

E-commerce in Egypt has its own habits. Cash on delivery is still common, many shoppers ask questions on WhatsApp before ordering, and delivery fees and times vary by governorate. A good Cairo shop should:

  • Show delivery areas, fees and timings clearly before checkout.
  • Offer the payment methods your customers expect, including cash on delivery if you support it.
  • Make returns and exchanges easy to understand.
  • Keep product pages light, with clear photos you supply and honest descriptions.
  • Include a WhatsApp button for quick product questions.

Our ecommerce marketing page covers how we support online shops after launch.

Our design and build process

Every project follows a clear set of steps, so you always know what is happening next:

  1. Discovery call. We discuss your goals, customers, pages and languages.
  2. Sitemap and content plan. We agree the pages and who writes what.
  3. Design. We share a design preview for the home page and a key inner page.
  4. Build. We build the site on a private preview link you can review.
  5. Content and Arabic. We place your English and Arabic copy and check layouts.
  6. Testing. We test speed, forms, mobile layouts and both languages.
  7. Launch. We go live, set up analytics and submit the sitemap to Google.

SEO built in from day one

A new website should be ready to rank when it launches. We set clean URLs, page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, structured data and an XML sitemap for both languages. If you are replacing an old site, we map old URLs to new ones so you do not lose existing rankings. After launch, SEO and Google Ads can bring steady traffic, and we can support both on a monthly retainer.

Common problems we fix

Many Cairo businesses come to us with sites that look fine on a laptop but break on phones. Others have Arabic pages that were added later and display badly, menus that do not mirror, or a single page that mixes both languages. Some sites have no analytics at all, so the owner cannot tell where enquiries come from. Contact forms that send to an unused inbox are another common issue. We fix these during a rebuild or as a smaller set of improvements.

Working with us from Cairo

Egypt is usually one or two hours ahead of the UK, depending on the season, so it is easy to find time for calls. You get a shared folder, a clear timeline and a preview link at each stage. If you also operate in the Gulf, our guides to web design in Dubai and web design in Saudi Arabia cover those markets, and our web design in Jordan guide covers Amman.

Writing content that sells

Design gets attention, but words do most of the selling. Many Cairo websites say very little about what the business actually does, who it serves or why a customer should choose it. Before the build starts, we help you plan the message for each page. Your home page should say in one or two lines what you offer and who it is for. Service pages should explain what is included, how the process works, how long it takes and what the next step is. About pages should explain your experience and your way of working, using facts your team supplies.

We write the English copy from a short questionnaire and a call with your team. If you prefer to write it yourselves, we give you a simple template for each page type. The Arabic version is then written by your team or a translator, so it reads naturally to Egyptian customers rather than sounding like a literal translation. Clear, honest copy also helps search engines and AI search tools understand and quote your pages.

Photos, branding and trust

Real photos of your team, premises and products build far more trust than generic stock images. Where you have them, we use them, compressed so they load quickly. Where you do not, we suggest a short list of shots worth taking. We also check that your logo, colours and fonts work well in both Arabic and English, and on small screens. If your brand needs a refresh, our design work can tidy it up so the site looks consistent from the first page to the last.

Trust signals matter in a crowded market. Clear contact details, a physical address if you have one, business registration details if you choose to show them, and simple policies for delivery, returns and privacy all help visitors feel safe getting in touch or placing an order.

After launch

A website is never quite finished. Once your site is live, we can look after updates, backups and small changes, and run SEO or Google Ads on a monthly retainer. If you prefer to manage the site yourselves, we hand over full access and a short written guide. Either way, we check analytics after the first month to see which pages are working and which need attention.
