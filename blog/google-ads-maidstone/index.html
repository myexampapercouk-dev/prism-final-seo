Google Ads in Maidstone: How Kent Businesses Can Get More Enquiries From Paid Search

Google Ads in Maidstone explained: how to set up local campaigns, avoid wasted spend across Kent, what management costs in £ and how quickly results arrive.

  • Management retainers start at £350 a month, and ad spend is always paid separately to Google.

Google Ads in Maidstone can put your business at the top of search results as soon as a campaign goes live, which makes it one of the fastest ways to win enquiries from people actively looking for what you sell. The difference between a profitable account and a costly one usually comes down to location settings, keyword choice, ad copy, landing pages and conversion tracking.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that works remotely. We do not have an office in Maidstone, but we manage campaigns for local businesses through shared reports, video calls and direct access to your own Google Ads account.

Why Maidstone businesses use Google Ads

Maidstone is the county town of Kent, with a large retail centre, a busy professional services sector, health and care providers, and many trades serving the surrounding villages. Nearby towns such as Tonbridge, Sittingbourne, Ashford and the Medway towns are close enough that customers often shop around between them.

That creates two challenges. First, many local searches are competitive, with businesses from across Kent and London bidding on the same terms. Second, broad location settings can easily spend your budget on people who are too far away to become customers. A well-run Google Ads account deals with both by targeting carefully and measuring what actually brings in work.

Paid search works particularly well for urgent and high-value services, such as emergency trades, legal advice, dental treatment, home improvements and business services, where one new customer can justify many clicks.

Google Ads Maidstone: setting up a local campaign

For most local businesses, a strong search campaign includes these elements:

  • Location targeting. Set to people in or regularly in your service area, not people who have only shown interest in it. We often build a radius or list of postcodes around Maidstone and the towns you genuinely serve.
  • Focused ad groups. Separate groups for each main service, so the ad and landing page match the search closely.
  • Keyword choice. Phrase and exact match keywords with clear buying intent, plus a growing list of negative keywords to block irrelevant searches.
  • Relevant ads. Responsive search ads that mention the service, the area and a clear reason to choose you.
  • Assets. Call, location, sitelink and callout assets that make the ad larger and more useful.
  • Conversion tracking. Tracking for calls, forms and bookings, so you know which searches bring in enquiries.

Landing pages that turn clicks into enquiries

Even a perfect campaign struggles if the landing page is slow or unclear. A good landing page for a Maidstone service business should repeat the search intent in its headline, explain the service briefly, show the areas you cover, offer clear proof such as accreditations you genuinely hold, and make calling or booking easy on a phone. Our landing page design guide explains the layout we recommend.

If your website makes this hard, a rebuild may pay for itself through better conversion rates. A small business website costs £1,000 to £2,000 and usually takes three to five weeks.

We usually suggest starting with a budget that gives enough clicks each month to learn which searches convert, then adjusting once the data is clear. Google Ads alongside SEO

Paid search delivers quick visibility, while SEO builds lasting rankings over time. Many Maidstone firms use both: ads bring enquiries now, and the search term data shows which topics deserve SEO pages. Campaign types worth considering

Search campaigns are the starting point for most Maidstone businesses, but they are not the only option. Depending on what you sell, other campaign types can add value once search is working well:

  • Performance Max. Useful for online shops and some lead generation businesses with good conversion data, as it shows ads across search, shopping, YouTube and other Google placements.
  • Shopping campaigns. For retailers selling online, product ads with images and prices often perform strongly. They rely on an accurate product feed.
  • Remarketing. Ads shown to people who visited your site but did not enquire, which can help with higher-value decisions that take time.
  • Local service focus. For trades and home services, call-focused ads and location assets help people contact you straight from the results page.

We usually recommend proving that search campaigns are profitable before adding more campaign types. Spreading a small budget across too many campaigns makes it harder for each one to gather enough data to improve.

What to expect in the first three months

In the first month, the focus is on tracking, structure and early data. Expect some adjustment as we learn which search terms bring enquiries and which need blocking. By the second month, the account usually becomes more efficient as negative keywords build up and weaker ads are replaced. By the third month, there is normally enough data to make confident decisions about budget, bidding and which services deserve more spend. Performance typically keeps improving over three to six months as the account matures.

Is Google Ads right for your business?

Google Ads tends to suit businesses that have clear search demand, a service people look for when they are ready to buy, and enough margin to pay for clicks. It is less suitable if few people search for what you offer, or if your website cannot yet handle enquiries well. In those cases, building the website or starting with SEO may be the better first step.

FAQs

How much does Google Ads in Maidstone cost?

Google Ads costs two things: your ad spend, paid directly to Google, and management fees. Our management retainers start at £350 a month, with Growth at £750 and Scale at £1,500, and ad spend is always separate.

How quickly can Google Ads bring enquiries?

Google Ads can bring enquiries within days of a campaign going live. It usually takes a few weeks of data to refine targeting and bids, and steady performance typically builds over three to six months.

Can I target only Maidstone and nearby villages?

Yes, you can target a radius, specific postcodes or named towns. We set location targeting to people in or regularly in your area, which helps avoid spending on searchers who are too far away.

Do you have an office in Maidstone?

No, we do not have an office in Maidstone. Prism Works Studio is UK-based and works remotely, using video calls, shared reports and direct access to your own account.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

Yes, you own your Google Ads account and all its data. We work inside it with access you grant, so you keep full control if you ever change provider.

Should I run Google Ads or SEO first?

Start with Google Ads if you need enquiries quickly and have a good landing page, and start with SEO if you want lasting visibility and can wait three to six months. Many businesses run both together.
