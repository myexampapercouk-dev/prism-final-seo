Shopify Agency for Canadian Brands: Building, Migrating and Growing a Store Remotely

Looking for a Shopify agency in Canada? What a store build involves, Canadian tax, shipping and language set-up, timelines, costs and how to judge any agency.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 9 November 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A Shopify agency for a Canadian brand should handle more than design: Canadian dollar pricing, provincial sales tax settings, shipping zones and, for many brands, French content.
  • Most new Shopify stores take five to twelve weeks to build, and product data, photography and policies are usually the deciding factors in the timeline.
  • Tax registration, rates and legal wording are matters for your accountant and advisers, while the agency's job is to configure the store so it follows their advice.

A Shopify agency for a Canadian brand should design a store that sells well and set it up properly for Canada, from Canadian dollar pricing and provincial sales taxes to shipping zones, French content and email consent. Prism Works Studio is a UK-based studio that builds and improves Shopify stores for Canadian brands remotely. We have no Canadian office or staff, and the whole project runs online through calls, shared drafts and a private preview of your store.

This guide covers what a Shopify project includes, the Canada-specific settings to get right, how long it takes, what it costs and how to judge any agency you are considering.

What a Shopify agency does for Canadian brands

Shopify makes it easy to open a store, but a store that converts, ranks and runs smoothly takes planning. A typical agency engagement covers:

  • Store structure. Collections, navigation, filters and product templates planned around how customers shop. Our guide to Shopify collections structure explains why this matters for both sales and search.
  • Theme design and customisation. A theme adapted to your brand, with fast-loading product and collection pages.
  • Product setup. Variants, images, descriptions, metafields and bulk imports.
  • Checkout, payments and taxes. Configured for Canadian currency and your accountant's tax advice.
  • Shipping. Rates and zones for Canada and, if needed, the United States and further afield.
  • Apps and integrations. Only the apps you need, connected properly to email, reviews, accounting or fulfilment tools.
  • Launch and SEO foundations. Redirects, analytics, Search Console and clean page titles.

If you are starting from scratch, our Shopify store setup guide walks through each stage in order.

Canada-specific Shopify settings to plan for

Several settings work differently for a Canadian store than for one selling mainly in the UK or Europe. These notes are general, not tax or legal advice, so confirm details with your accountant and current official guidance from the Canada Revenue Agency and provincial authorities.

Shipping deserves particular attention in Canada because distances are large and costs vary widely between urban and remote addresses. Shopify's shipping profiles let you set different rates for different products and regions, and our guide to Shopify shipping profiles explains how they work.

Selling in French and English

If you sell into Quebec, French content is usually expected, and Quebec's French-language rules may apply to how you present products and information. Check current guidance from the Office québécois de la langue française and take advice on your own situation. On the build side, Shopify supports multiple languages, so product descriptions, menus, policies and checkout can all be offered in French. We set up the structure and your translator supplies and checks the French text, as we do not translate content ourselves.

Plan the second language at the start rather than adding it later. It affects theme choice, image text, email notifications and the time needed to load content.

Moving an existing store to Shopify

Many Canadian brands come to Shopify from WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix or an older custom platform. A migration involves moving products, customers and order history where possible, rebuilding the design and, most importantly, redirecting old URLs so search traffic is not lost. Our guide to migrating from WooCommerce to Shopify covers the process step by step.

Larger brands may be weighing Shopify Plus for its extra checkout control, automation and multi-store options. We handle Shopify Plus migrations too, and our article on Shopify Plus migration explains when the upgrade makes sense and what it involves.

Product content and photography

The quality of your product content shapes how well the store sells and ranks, and it is usually the part that takes longest to prepare. Each product needs a clear title, a description that answers real buyer questions, accurate variant options, consistent photography and, where relevant, sizing, materials and care details. For a bilingual store, every one of those elements needs a French version as well.

We recommend gathering product data in a single spreadsheet before the build starts, with one row per product and a column for each field. That makes bulk importing faster, shows gaps early and gives your translator a clean list of text to work through. If photography is still to be shot, agree a consistent style, background and set of angles so that collection pages look tidy once everything is loaded. Small inconsistencies multiply quickly across a large catalogue.

How long does a Shopify build take?

An ecommerce site typically takes five to twelve weeks. The range depends on the number of products, the amount of custom design, the integrations needed and, above all, how quickly product data, photos and policies are ready. A small catalogue with good photography and existing descriptions sits at the shorter end. A large catalogue moving from another platform, or a bilingual store, usually sits at the longer end.

What does a Shopify agency cost for a Canadian brand?

Our published prices are in pounds sterling. An ecommerce site is £1,500 to £3,500 depending on scope, and ongoing growth work runs on monthly retainers of £350, £750 or £1,500. Quotes in Canadian dollars or other currencies are available on request. Our pricing page shows what each option includes.

Your Shopify subscription, paid apps, theme licence and payment processing fees are paid directly to the providers and are separate from agency fees. A good agency will help you keep app costs down. Our Shopify apps audit guide shows how to check what you are paying for and whether you still need it.

After launch: growing the store

Launch is the start, not the finish. Once the store is live, the focus moves to bringing in the right visitors and converting more of them. That usually means search optimisation for collections and products, paid campaigns on Google Shopping and Meta, email flows for abandoned carts and repeat purchases, and steady testing of product pages. Our guide to Shopify SEO covers the search side, and SEO and paid results typically take three to six months to show clearly.

How to choose a Shopify agency in Canada

Whether you hire a Toronto agency or a remote studio, ask:

  • Will the store, theme, domain and all app accounts be in your name?
  • How will Canadian taxes, shipping and languages be configured, and who signs them off?
  • Which apps do they plan to install, and what will each cost per month?
  • How will they protect existing search traffic during a migration?
  • What training and support do you get after launch?

FAQs

Can a UK-based Shopify agency build a store for a Canadian brand?

Yes. Shopify projects are planned, built and tested online. What matters is that the agency configures Canadian currency, taxes, shipping and language correctly, following your accountant's and advisers' guidance.

How much does a Shopify agency Canada brands hire typically charge with you?

Our published prices are in pounds sterling: £1,500 to £3,500 for an ecommerce site and retainers from £350 a month. Canadian dollar quotes are available on request. Shopify fees and apps are paid separately.

Can Shopify handle Canadian sales taxes?

Shopify has tax settings for Canadian sales taxes, including GST, HST, PST and QST. Registration and which taxes you collect are matters for your accountant, and we configure the store to match their advice.

Can my Shopify store be in French and English?

Yes. Shopify supports multiple languages across products, menus, policies and checkout. Your translator supplies the French text and we build it into the store.

How long does a Shopify store take to build?

Typically five to twelve weeks, depending on catalogue size, design work, integrations and how quickly product content is ready.

Can you migrate my existing store to Shopify?

Yes. We migrate from platforms such as WooCommerce, Squarespace and Wix, and handle Shopify Plus migrations, with redirects set up to protect existing search traffic.
