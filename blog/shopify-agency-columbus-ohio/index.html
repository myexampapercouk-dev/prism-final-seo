How we build a Shopify store

Because we work remotely, every stage ends with something you can review in a shared preview.

  1. Discovery. We learn about your products, customers, shipping rules and the apps you rely on.
  2. Structure. We plan collections, filters, navigation and key page templates.
  3. Design. We customise a well-supported theme to match your brand rather than building everything from scratch, which keeps the store fast and easier to maintain.
  4. Build and data. We set up products, variants, collections, shipping and taxes, and connect payment options.
  5. Testing. We test orders, emails, mobile layouts and page speed.
  6. Launch and training. We launch, submit the sitemap and show your team how to manage the store.

US sales tax rules vary by state, and Shopify offers tools to help. We set up the settings you specify, but tax decisions and registrations rest with you and your accountant.

Migrating to Shopify without losing search traffic

Many Columbus brands come to us from WooCommerce, Etsy or older platforms. A migration can unlock a better store, but it must be handled carefully to protect search rankings. We map every old URL to its new home, set up redirects, move product data cleanly and check that page titles and descriptions carry over.

Our guides to moving from WooCommerce to Shopify and moving from Etsy to Shopify explain the main steps and risks. If you are deciding between platforms, our Shopify vs WooCommerce comparison sets out the trade-offs.

Shopify SEO for Columbus brands

A Shopify store needs ongoing SEO work to win traffic from search. The main tasks are:

  • Collection pages with useful intro text, clean filters and strong internal links.
  • Product pages with unique descriptions, clear images and structured data.
  • Technical clean-up to manage duplicate URLs, tags and app-generated pages.
  • Content such as buying guides that answer the questions shoppers ask before they buy.
  • AI search readiness, so AI tools can understand and recommend your products.

Our Shopify SEO guide covers these in more detail. SEO results typically take three to six months to build, so it pays to start early.

Choosing the right Shopify theme and apps

Two decisions shape most Shopify stores: the theme and the apps. Getting them right early saves time and money later.

  • Start with a well-supported theme. Shopify themes from established developers receive regular updates. A good theme gives you speed, accessibility and modern features without heavy custom code.
  • Customise with care. We change layouts, sections and styles to match your brand, but we avoid edits that make future theme updates difficult.
  • Keep apps to what you need. Every app can add scripts that slow the store. We review each one and look for built-in Shopify features that do the same job.
  • Plan for reviews, subscriptions and bundles. If you need these features, we choose reliable apps at the start rather than adding several later that overlap.
  • Think about operations. Inventory, fulfilment, shipping labels and accounting links should fit the way your team already works, not force a new process on day one.

For brands with a physical shop in Columbus, such as in the Short North, German Village or Clintonville, Shopify POS can connect in-store and online sales in one system. That keeps stock levels accurate across both channels and gives you a single view of customers and orders. We can set up the online side and help connect it to your POS hardware, which you buy directly from Shopify.

Before launch, we also look at the pages shoppers check before they buy, such as shipping, returns and contact details. Clear, easy-to-find policies reduce questions and help build trust with first-time buyers. The legal wording of those policies rests with you and your own adviser, but we make sure they are well presented and linked from every product page. We also set up basic analytics, so from the first day you can see where visitors come from, which products they view and where they leave the store.
