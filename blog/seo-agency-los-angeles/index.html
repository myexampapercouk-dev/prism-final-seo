SEO Agency Los Angeles: How Remote SEO Works for LA Businesses

Looking for an SEO agency in Los Angeles? A remote studio can run technical, local and content SEO well. Here is how it works, what it costs and what to ask.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 10 November 2026 · 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • Good SEO for a Los Angeles business comes from sound technical foundations, content that answers real searches and steady authority building, not from the agency having an LA address.
  • Los Angeles is eight hours behind UK time for most of the year, so a remote SEO project runs on written plans, monthly reporting and calls in the LA morning.
  • We are a UK-based studio working remotely with US clients; retainers are priced in pounds and other currencies are quoted on request.

If you are looking for an SEO agency in Los Angeles, what matters most is whether the agency can fix your technical problems, publish content that matches what your buyers search for and build genuine authority over time. A remote SEO agency can do all of that for an LA business, as long as the work is planned in writing, reported clearly and reviewed with you on a regular schedule.

We are a UK-based studio and we work remotely with US businesses. We do not have a Los Angeles office or any office in the US. This guide explains what an SEO engagement should include, how remote work runs across the time difference, what it costs and the questions worth asking any agency before you sign.

What an SEO agency in Los Angeles should deliver

Los Angeles is a huge and varied market: entertainment and media, fashion and beauty brands, hospitality, health and wellness, property, professional services and a large ecommerce scene. The search landscape is crowded, and many businesses compete both locally across a sprawling metro area and nationally online. Whatever the sector, a solid SEO engagement usually covers these areas:

  • Technical SEO: crawlability, indexing, site speed, mobile layout, structured data and fixing errors that hold pages back.
  • Keyword and intent research: mapping the searches your buyers actually use to the pages that should answer them.
  • On-page optimisation: titles, headings, internal links and copy that make each page clearly relevant.
  • Content: service pages, guides and comparison content that answer real questions rather than padding.
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile, consistent business details and location pages where you genuinely serve an area.
  • Authority: earning links and mentions through useful content and digital PR rather than buying them.
  • AI search visibility: structuring content so it can be understood and cited by AI-generated answers as well as classic results.
  • Reporting: plain-English monthly reports tied to enquiries and sales, not just rankings.

Our SEO and GEO service follows this structure, and the same approach applies whether you run a single studio in Silver Lake or an online brand shipping across the country.

How remote SEO works across the time difference

Los Angeles is usually eight hours behind UK time, with a short spell in spring and autumn when the gap shifts because the clocks change on different dates. Our afternoon overlaps with the LA morning, which is when we schedule calls. Most SEO work does not need live meetings anyway: audits, research, content and technical fixes are planned and recorded in shared documents.

  1. Discovery call: we agree goals, priority services or products, target areas and how leads or sales are tracked.
  2. Audit: a technical, content and authority review of your site and your main competitors in search.
  3. Roadmap: a prioritised plan for the first three to six months, with the reasoning written down.
  4. Delivery: fixes, new and improved pages, local work and outreach, carried out in agreed monthly blocks.
  5. Reporting: a monthly report and a short call to review progress and agree the next priorities.

Our technical SEO audit checklist shows the kind of issues the audit stage looks for, and our guide to SEO reporting explains what a useful monthly report should contain.

Local SEO in a city the size of Los Angeles

Los Angeles is not one search market. Someone searching in Santa Monica, Pasadena or Long Beach may see different local results for the same query, and many searches include a neighbourhood rather than the city name. That changes how local SEO should be planned:

  • Be honest about where you serve: location pages should exist only where you genuinely work or have premises, with content that is specific to that area.
  • Keep business details consistent: your name, address and phone number should match across your site, your Google Business Profile and major directories.
  • Use the right categories: your Google Business Profile category has a large effect on which searches you appear for.
  • Earn reviews properly: ask real customers, respond to every review and never buy or gate them.

If you sell nationally rather than locally, the balance shifts towards content and authority. Our comparison of local SEO and national SEO explains which approach suits which kind of business, and our guide to multi-location SEO covers businesses with several sites.

Links and mentions from relevant websites help pages rank and get cited. Our digital PR service earns them through stories worth covering.

Our published prices are in pounds sterling. Our monthly retainers are Launch at £350 a month, Growth at £750 a month and Scale at £1,500 a month. The right level depends on how competitive your searches are, how much content and technical work is needed and how many locations or product lines are involved. The full breakdown is on our pricing page. When you compare SEO quotes, look at what is actually delivered each month: the number of pages written or improved, the technical work included, whether link building or digital PR is part of the fee and how results are reported. A low monthly fee that buys only a ranking report rarely moves anything.

How long SEO takes to work

SEO results typically take three to six months to show clearly, and competitive Los Angeles markets can take longer. Technical fixes can help quickly when something was blocking pages, but content and authority build over time. Anyone promising first-page rankings within weeks is either targeting searches nobody uses or taking risks with your site. A good agency will show early signs of progress, such as more pages indexed, more search impressions and better positions for long-tail searches, before the bigger gains arrive.

Keyword research for LA audiences

Good research starts with how your buyers describe their problem, not with the words you use internally. In Los Angeles that often means neighbourhood names, terms specific to the industry, and plenty of comparison searches from people weighing options. We combine your knowledge of your customers with US search data to find the searches that bring enquiries, then map each one to a single page so that your own pages do not compete with each other. Our keyword research guide explains the method step by step.

SEO and AI search

More searches now show AI-generated answers above the classic results. The fundamentals still apply: clear pages that answer specific questions, accurate business information and genuine authority. We structure content with direct answers, comparison tables and FAQs so it is easier for both search engines and AI tools to understand. Our guide to AI Overviews and SEO covers what has changed and what has not.

SEO and your website

SEO works best when the website itself is fast, well structured and easy to use. If your site is slow, hard to navigate on a phone or built on a platform that makes changes difficult, some of the SEO budget will go on fixing those foundations first. If a rebuild is on the cards, it is worth planning search from the start. Our guide to web design for Los Angeles businesses explains how we approach builds for LA clients.

Questions to ask an SEO agency before you sign

  • What exactly will you deliver each month, and how will I see it?
  • Will my Search Console, analytics and Google Business Profile stay in my name?
  • How do you build links, and will you avoid paid or low-quality links?
  • How will you measure success in enquiries or sales, not just rankings?
  • What is the minimum contract length, and what is the notice period?
  • Who will actually do the work, and how often will we speak?

Be wary of guaranteed rankings, secret methods or reports that show only vanity numbers. A good agency explains what it is doing and why, and is open about what it cannot control.

Legal and advertising points

Some sectors that are common in Los Angeles, such as health, wellness, finance and property, have rules about what can be claimed in marketing. Privacy rules in California also affect how websites collect and use visitor data. We write content to good practice and avoid unsupported claims, but the legal position depends on state and federal law and changes over time, so you should check current official guidance and speak to your own lawyer about your obligations. Final sign-off on regulated claims rests with your business.
