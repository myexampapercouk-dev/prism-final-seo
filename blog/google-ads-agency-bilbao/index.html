Common Google Ads mistakes

  • Broad keywords with no negatives. Budgets drain on irrelevant searches.
  • No conversion tracking. Decisions are based on clicks rather than enquiries or sales.
  • Sending all traffic to the homepage. Visitors cannot find what they searched for.
  • Wrong location settings. Ads show to people who cannot buy from you.
  • Set and forget. Accounts that are not reviewed regularly waste money over time.

Our PPC audit checklist walks through these checks in more detail if you want to review an existing account.

Advertising rules

Google has its own advertising policies, and Spanish and EU law also covers consumer advertising, privacy and cookies. Some sectors have extra rules. We keep our advice general and recommend official guidance and your own adviser. In regulated sectors, compliance approval rests with your business.

How we plan a first Google Ads campaign

For a business new to Google Ads, we usually start small and focused. We pick one service, product group or location where demand is clear and margins can support paid traffic. We research the search terms people use, build a tight campaign around them and add a list of negative keywords from day one, so that budget is not wasted on searches that will never convert.

Next, we make sure conversion tracking is in place. That might mean tracking contact form submissions, phone calls from ads, table reservations or online purchases. Once tracking is reliable, we can see which keywords and ads bring real enquiries, and shift budget towards them.

In the first weeks, we review search term reports closely, adjust bids and pause anything that is spending without results. As data builds up, we test new ad copy, refine audiences and consider extra campaign types such as remarketing or Shopping. This gradual approach keeps risk low and gives you a clear view of what paid search can do for your business before you commit larger budgets.

Landing pages that support your ads

Even the best ads will struggle if the landing page is slow, confusing or in the wrong language. Each campaign should send visitors to a page that matches what they searched, explains the offer quickly and makes the next step obvious. For many Bilbao businesses, that means dedicated pages for key services or products rather than sending everyone to the homepage.

We can advise on landing page structure and, if needed, design and build new pages as part of a website project. Fast loading on mobile is especially important, as many searches for restaurants, hotels and local services happen on phones.

Good partners also explain trade-offs openly. Not every fix is worth doing at once, and budgets are rarely unlimited, so a clear plan should show what will be done first, what can wait and why. That honesty makes it easier for your team to plan time for approvals, translations and content, and keeps the project moving at a steady, realistic pace.
