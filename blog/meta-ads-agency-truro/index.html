A Meta Ads agency in Truro plans and manages Facebook and Instagram advertising so Cornish businesses reach the right people, at the right time, with ads that lead to bookings, enquiries or sales. Meta Ads work especially well for visual products, local services and seasonal offers, provided tracking and targeting are set up properly.

Prism Works Studio is a UK-based digital marketing studio that works remotely. We run Meta Ads for businesses in Truro and across Cornwall through video calls, shared dashboards and plain monthly reports.

Why Meta Ads suit Truro and Cornwall

Truro is Cornwall's only city and a key centre for retail, hospitality, healthcare, education, public services and professional firms. Around it, towns such as Falmouth, Redruth, Camborne, St Austell, Newquay and the Roseland peninsula support a large tourism economy, along with food and drink producers, makers, surf and outdoor brands, and many trades.

Much of this activity is visual and seasonal. Holiday lets, restaurants, attractions, wedding venues, gift shops and craft makers can all show what they offer through images and short clips supplied by their own team. Meta Ads let you put those visuals in front of people planning a Cornish holiday, locals looking for something to do at the weekend, or past customers ready to return. Unlike search ads, Meta can create demand before someone starts actively searching.

Tracking comes first

Meta's targeting relies heavily on data. Without accurate tracking, the platform cannot learn which people are likely to book or buy. We set up the Meta Pixel and, where possible, the Conversions API, then define the events that matter: bookings, purchases, enquiry forms, calls or newsletter sign-ups. We also add UTM tags so results show clearly in Google Analytics.

If you want to understand how UTM tags work, see our UTM tracking links guide. Clean tracking also helps you compare Meta against other channels fairly.

Planning around Cornish seasons

Seasonality shapes almost every Cornish business. Holiday accommodation might push early bookings in winter and spring, last-minute availability in summer and short breaks in autumn. A Truro restaurant might promote festive bookings from October. A garden centre might focus on spring. Running the same ads all year usually wastes budget.

We build a simple calendar of key periods, then adjust budgets and messages to match. During quiet months, we may shift spend towards locals and repeat customers. During peak periods, we focus on capacity, because there is little point paying for demand you cannot serve.

Audiences that work locally

For local services in Truro, a radius around the city or chosen towns is often enough, combined with broad targeting so Meta can find likely customers. For tourism and ecommerce brands, we may target people across the UK with relevant interests, then retarget website visitors and social engagers. Lookalike audiences built from past customers can work well once you have enough data.

Retargeting is often where Meta Ads earn their keep. Someone who looked at a holiday cottage or a product page is far more likely to book or buy when reminded. We set sensible frequency so people are not shown the same ad too often.

Creative that performs

On Facebook and Instagram, the ad itself does most of the work. Strong images, short clips, clear offers and genuine detail about your business usually beat polished but generic material. We write ad copy, suggest formats such as carousels and collections, and test different hooks and offers. Over time, the data shows which messages people respond to.

Your landing page matters too. Ads should lead to a page that matches the promise, loads quickly on mobile and makes booking or buying simple. Our landing page design guide explains the essentials.

Common Meta Ads mistakes we see

Many Cornish businesses try Meta Ads by boosting posts from their Facebook page. Boosting can raise awareness, but it offers limited control over objectives, audiences and tracking, so it rarely produces measurable bookings or sales. Running campaigns through Ads Manager with clear objectives usually works far better.

Other common issues include optimising for clicks rather than conversions, which attracts people who browse but never act; targeting audiences that are too narrow, which stops Meta finding new customers; running one ad for months until people tire of it; and sending traffic to slow pages that do not match the offer. We also see accounts where the Pixel fires twice or not at all, which confuses the algorithm and hides real results.

Fixing these basics often improves results without increasing spend. That is why we always start with an audit of your account, tracking and landing pages before suggesting new budgets.

Offers that suit Cornish businesses

A clear reason to act helps any ad. For accommodation, that might be a short-break offer for quieter weeks or added value for longer stays. For restaurants, it could be a set menu, a midweek offer or festive booking reminders. For makers and shops, it might be free delivery thresholds, gift bundles or new collection launches. For trades, it could be seasonal checks or a simple way to request a quote.

We help you choose offers that protect your margins and fit your capacity. Discounts are not always needed; sometimes clear availability, strong images and honest reviews are enough. Testing different messages shows what your audience actually responds to.
