GEO Agency UK: What an AI Search Optimisation Agency Does and How to Hire One

A GEO agency in the UK helps your brand get cited in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Here is what it does and how to choose one.

By the Prism Works Studio team · 30 October 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • A GEO agency works to get your brand cited and recommended in AI answers, building on solid SEO rather than replacing it.
  • Core GEO work covers answer-ready content, structured data, entity consistency, third-party mentions and tracking AI citations.
  • Be wary of agencies promising guaranteed AI rankings; ask how they measure citations and how GEO connects to your existing SEO.

A GEO agency in the UK helps your business get mentioned, cited and recommended in AI-generated answers from tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. It does this by building on strong SEO foundations, then shaping your content, structured data and third-party reputation so AI systems can confidently use you as a source.

If you want the definition and theory first, start with our explainer on what generative engine optimisation is. This guide takes the practical, hiring angle: what a GEO agency actually delivers, how it differs from a standard SEO agency, what to ask before you sign, and how to judge whether the work is paying off.

What a GEO agency does

Generative engine optimisation sits on top of search engine optimisation. AI assistants still rely heavily on content they can crawl, understand and trust, so the work starts with the same basics: a technically healthy site and pages that answer real questions well. A GEO agency then adds a layer focused on how AI tools select, summarise and cite sources.

Typical GEO services include:

  • AI visibility audit: checking which prompts in your market mention you, which mention competitors and which cite no one useful.
  • Answer-first content: restructuring key pages so the direct answer appears early, supported by clear headings, tables and FAQs.
  • Entity and brand consistency: making sure your business name, services, locations and descriptions match across your site, Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles.
  • Structured data: schema markup that clarifies your organisation, services, articles and FAQs.
  • Third-party mentions: digital PR, reviews and expert commentary that give AI tools independent evidence about you.
  • Crawler access: reviewing robots.txt and site settings so legitimate AI crawlers can reach the content you want used.
  • Tracking and reporting: monitoring citations and mentions across AI tools alongside traditional rankings and traffic.

How GEO differs from SEO

The two disciplines overlap heavily, which is why many agencies offer them together. The differences are mainly in the goal and how success is measured.

AreaTraditional SEOGEO (AI search optimisation)
Main goalRank pages in search results and win clicksBe cited, mentioned or recommended in AI answers
Content focusTopical coverage, keyword targeting, internal linksDirect answers, clear facts, quotable passages, comparisons
Authority signalsBacklinks and on-site expertiseBrand mentions, reviews, consistent entity data and backlinks
Technical workCrawlability, speed, indexation, schemaThe same, plus AI crawler access and structured facts
MeasurementRankings, organic traffic, conversionsCitation share, brand mentions in AI answers, referral traffic from AI tools
Speed of changeUsually three to six monthsVaries; AI answers can shift quickly and inconsistently

In practice, a GEO agency that ignores SEO is building on sand. Many AI tools retrieve live web results before answering, so pages that do not rank or cannot be crawled are less likely to be cited. That is why our SEO and GEO service treats them as one programme.

Do you need a GEO agency or an SEO agency?

For most UK businesses, the honest answer is that you need an SEO agency that understands GEO, rather than a separate specialist. Splitting the work between two suppliers often creates duplicated audits and conflicting content changes.

A dedicated GEO focus makes more sense when:

  • Your SEO foundations are already solid and you rank well for core terms.
  • Your buyers research heavily through AI assistants, which is common in B2B, SaaS and considered consumer purchases.
  • Competitors are being recommended in AI answers for prompts where you are absent.
  • You operate in a sector where trust and reputation strongly influence recommendations.

If your site still has basic issues, fix those first. Our technical SEO audit checklist is a good place to start, and our guide on SEO for small businesses in the UK covers the essentials.

What GEO work looks like in the first few months

A sensible GEO programme usually starts with a baseline. The agency agrees a list of prompts your customers are likely to use, such as "best accountant for small businesses in Leeds" or "which ecommerce platform suits a UK fashion brand", and records which brands and sources AI tools mention today. That gives you a starting point that is specific to your market rather than a generic score.

The next phase is usually on your own site. Priority pages are rewritten so the answer comes first, facts such as prices, locations and service areas are stated clearly, and FAQs reflect the questions people genuinely ask. Structured data is added or corrected, and inconsistencies in your business details across directories and profiles are tidied up.

Alongside this, authority work begins: expert commentary, digital PR and review generation that give AI tools independent sources to draw on. Over the following months, the agency re-runs the prompt set, compares results and adjusts priorities. Expect progress to be uneven; some prompts move quickly while others take longer or depend on sources outside your control.

How to choose a GEO agency in the UK

Because GEO is new, there is a lot of confident marketing and not much shared standard. Use these questions to separate substance from buzzwords.

Question to askGood answerWarning sign
How do you measure AI visibility?A defined set of prompts tracked over time across several AI toolsVague talk of "AI rankings" with no method
How does GEO connect to our SEO?One plan covering technical, content and authority workGEO sold as a replacement for SEO
What will change on our site?Specific page rewrites, schema, FAQs and entity fixesOnly "AI-optimised" content at volume
How do you build authority?Digital PR, reviews, expert commentaryBulk directory or link packages
Can you guarantee citations?No; AI answers vary and are not controlled by any agencyYes, with fixed targets

GEO agency checklist

Before you hire, make sure the proposal covers each of these:

  1. A baseline audit of AI mentions and citations for prompts that matter to your customers.
  2. A technical review covering crawlability, indexation and AI crawler access.
  3. Rewrites of priority pages with answer-first introductions, tables and FAQs.
  4. Structured data for your organisation, services and key content.
  5. A plan to make your business details consistent across the web.
  6. Authority building through digital PR, reviews and expert commentary.
  7. Regular reporting that combines AI citations, rankings, traffic and enquiries.
  8. Clear scope, deliverables and notice period in writing.
  9. For regulated sectors, a process where final approval rests with your compliance team.

How results are measured

GEO reporting is less mature than SEO reporting, so expect a mix of evidence rather than a single number. Useful measures include the share of tracked prompts where you are mentioned or cited, the sources AI tools cite instead of you, referral traffic from AI platforms in GA4, branded search trends and, ultimately, enquiries.

Be cautious with week-to-week comparisons. The same prompt can produce different answers on different days, so trends over several months tell you more than snapshots. Authority work, such as digital PR and content, tends to support AI visibility over time because it creates independent evidence about your business.

Different sectors also need different approaches. A SaaS company will care about comparison and "best tool for" prompts, as covered in our SaaS SEO agency guide, while a local service business will focus on recommendations for its towns and services.

FAQs

What does a GEO agency in the UK do?

It helps your business appear in AI-generated answers by improving content structure, structured data, brand consistency and third-party mentions, on top of strong SEO foundations. It also tracks how often AI tools cite or recommend you.

Is GEO different from SEO?

GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it. SEO aims to rank pages in search results; GEO aims to have your brand cited in AI answers. Much of the underlying work, such as technical health and quality content, is shared.

Can a GEO agency guarantee my business appears in ChatGPT?

No. AI tools decide what to cite, and their answers vary between sessions. A good agency improves your chances and tracks progress but will not guarantee placement.

Do I need a separate GEO agency if I already have an SEO agency?

Usually not. Ask your current agency how it approaches AI search. Splitting the work often leads to duplicated effort and conflicting changes.

How long does GEO take to show results?

Some changes, such as clearer answers on key pages, can be picked up relatively quickly, but building the authority AI tools rely on typically takes several months, similar to SEO.

Which businesses benefit most from GEO?

Businesses whose customers research with AI assistants before buying, such as B2B services, SaaS, professional services and considered consumer purchases, tend to see the most value.
