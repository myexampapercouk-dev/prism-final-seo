What a B2B digital marketing agency in the UK should do

A B2B digital marketing agency in the UK should help you reach the people who influence and sign off a purchase, and turn their research into qualified sales conversations. That usually means search visibility for the problems you solve, targeted LinkedIn activity, credible content and lead capture that feeds straight into your CRM.

This guide adds depth to the overview above. It covers how UK business buyers really buy, which channels suit each stage, what to measure, the rules to respect and how to choose a partner. For a wider comparison of options, see our B2B marketing agency guide.

How UK business buyers choose a supplier

B2B purchases involve more people, more risk and longer timelines than most consumer decisions. A typical journey includes:

  1. Trigger. A new regulation, growth, a failing supplier or a new leader with a mandate.
  2. Independent research. Searches, industry content, peer recommendations and AI assistants, usually before contacting anyone.
  3. Shortlist. Three or four suppliers chosen on credibility, relevance and evidence.
  4. Evaluation. Several stakeholders, from users to finance and procurement, each with their own questions.
  5. Decision and onboarding. Contracts, pricing and reassurance that the switch will be smooth.

Much of the decision is shaped before a buyer fills in a form. Your website and content need to answer the questions each stakeholder will ask, and your campaigns need to keep you visible across a long cycle.

Symptoms and fixes

  • Leads that sales ignores. Offers attract the wrong people. Tighten targeting and add qualifying questions to forms.
  • A website that says what you do but not who it is for. Add industry and problem-led pages.
  • No organic visibility. Service pages are thin or compete with each other. A technical SEO audit usually shows why.
  • Content nobody uses. Topics chosen without sales input. Build content around the questions buyers ask your team.
  • Enquiries lost between systems. Forms not connected to the CRM, or no source data captured.

Rules to respect

This is general information rather than legal advice; your solicitor or data protection lead should confirm details.

  • Email marketing to businesses. PECR rules for corporate subscribers differ from those for individuals and sole traders, and UK GDPR still applies to personal data such as named contacts.
  • Data from third parties. Bought or scraped lists carry legal and reputational risk.
  • Claims and case studies. Advertising claims should be substantiated, and case studies should only be published with your client’s permission.
  • Regulated industries. If you sell into finance, health or similar sectors, extra rules may apply to your messaging.

Content that supports every stakeholder

B2B decisions are rarely made by one person, so content needs to work for several roles. Users want to know the service or product will solve their day-to-day problem. Managers want evidence it will deliver results and fit existing processes. Finance wants clear pricing logic and a sense of return. IT or operations may want detail on security, integration and support. Procurement wants to know you can meet their requirements and paperwork.

A practical content plan maps a small number of pages to each of these questions: service and industry pages for relevance, case studies for evidence, a clear process page for reassurance, FAQs that answer objections and a few in-depth guides that show expertise. Sales teams can then send the right page at the right moment, and search engines and AI assistants get clear, specific content to rank and cite.

We gather this material through short interviews with your sales team and subject experts, which keeps the content accurate and grounded in the questions buyers really ask. Over time, the same material can be repurposed into LinkedIn posts, email nurture sequences and sales presentations, so one piece of research supports several channels.

Measuring a long sales cycle

Because B2B deals can take months, monthly reports should look at leading indicators as well as closed revenue: qualified enquiries, meetings booked, opportunities created and the pages and campaigns that touched them. Reviewing these alongside sales every quarter shows which channels deserve more budget long before final revenue figures are in, and stops promising campaigns being cut too early.

Where possible, we also record how each lead first found you, such as a search, a LinkedIn ad or a referral, so that later analysis does not rely on memory. Even a simple source field in the CRM, filled in consistently, makes quarterly budget decisions far easier and gives sales useful context before the first call.

Questions to ask a B2B digital marketing agency

  • How will you define a qualified lead with our sales team?
  • Can you connect our forms and campaigns to our CRM?
  • How do you approach long sales cycles in reporting?
  • How will you learn our technical subject well enough to write credibly?
  • Who owns the content, accounts and data?
  • What would you prioritise in the first three months, and why?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LinkedIn worth it for B2B?

For many B2B offers, yes, because of its job-title and company targeting. Costs per click are higher, so offers and landing pages must be strong.

How long is a B2B SEO campaign?

SEO typically begins showing meaningful results within 3–6 months, and B2B sales cycles add time before revenue shows.

Can you connect forms to our CRM?

Yes, in most cases, so leads are recorded and attributed to their source.

What is account-based marketing?

A focused approach that targets a defined list of companies with tailored campaigns, rather than a broad audience.

Do you write B2B content?

Yes: service pages, guides, case studies (with your client’s permission) and thought leadership.

What does a B2B digital marketing agency in the UK do?

It helps B2B companies generate qualified leads through SEO, LinkedIn and Google Ads, content and CRM-connected lead capture, measured against pipeline rather than clicks.

How do marketing and sales agree on a qualified lead?

By writing down simple criteria, such as company size, role and need, and reviewing leads together regularly. We help set this up at the start.

Are case studies worth the effort?

Usually yes. B2B buyers look for evidence from similar companies, so permission-based case studies are often among the most used sales assets.

Can we email business contacts without consent?

Rules differ for corporate subscribers and individuals under PECR, and UK GDPR still applies. Your data protection lead should confirm what fits your situation.

Do industry pages help B2B SEO?

Often. Pages that explain how you help a specific industry match how buyers search and give sales a useful page to share.

How long until B2B marketing produces revenue?

Leads may appear within weeks for paid campaigns, but revenue depends on your sales cycle, which can be several months.
