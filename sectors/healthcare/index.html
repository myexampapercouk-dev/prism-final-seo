Health & healthcare

What healthcare marketing needs to get right

Patients research carefully before they book. They want to know who will treat them, what a treatment involves, what it costs and whether the clinic is properly registered. Healthcare marketing also sits under rules that most sectors do not face, including the CAP Code and ASA guidance on health claims, and, depending on the service, professional body and regulator guidance.

We plan content and campaigns with those constraints in mind from the start: clear, factual service pages, careful claims and calls to action that respect patient choice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you market private clinics within advertising rules?

Yes. We write and review healthcare content with the CAP Code and platform healthcare policies in mind, and every claim is signed off by you before publishing.

Do you build clinic websites?

Yes, on WordPress or another suitable platform, with treatment pages, practitioner profiles and enquiry or booking routes.

How do patients find local clinics online?

Mostly through Google searches and Maps. Local SEO and a complete Google Business Profile are usually the first priorities.

Can you run Google Ads for healthcare?

Yes, where the treatment is permitted to advertise. Some healthcare categories need certification or have restrictions, which we check first.

Who checks medical accuracy?

Your clinicians. We write clear, plain-English content, but medical facts are always reviewed and approved by your team.
