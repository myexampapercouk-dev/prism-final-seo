Financial advisers

Marketing within the rules

Websites, ads, social posts and emails from regulated firms are financial promotions, so they must be fair, clear and not misleading, with appropriate risk warnings and balance. Consumer Duty raises the bar further on how clearly you communicate with customers.

We write and build with this in mind, and we always route content through your compliance sign-off process before anything is published. We are a marketing agency, not a compliance adviser, so final approval stays with your firm or network.

For more detail, read our guide to FCA-compliant marketing for financial advisers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is our website a financial promotion?

Generally, marketing content from regulated firms is treated as a financial promotion. Your compliance team or network should approve it; we prepare content to make that process straightforward.

Can you guarantee FCA compliance?

No marketing agency can. We write carefully and follow your compliance process, but sign-off is always your firm’s responsibility.

Which channels work for IFAs?

Search (for people actively looking for advice), LinkedIn (for professional audiences) and referrals supported by a credible website usually work best.

Do you work with mortgage brokers?

Yes. Mortgage brokers, IFAs and wealth managers are all part of this sector.

Can you write blog content for us?

Yes. Educational content works well for advice firms, as long as it informs rather than gives personal recommendations.
