Our story

Why We Started Prism Works Studio

We founded Prism Works Studio because we saw too many businesses — talented, ambitious, well-run businesses — being let down by agencies that overpromised and underdelivered. Agencies that treated clients as accounts, not partners. That focused on vanity metrics rather than commercial outcomes. That charged premium fees for generic strategies that could have belonged to any business in any sector.

We built Prism Works Studio to be different. Our entire model is built around one principle: everything we do must have a measurable, positive impact on your business. Every strategy we create, every website we build, every campaign we run is accountable to that standard. If it is not growing your business, we change it.

From our first client to our most recent, that commitment to genuine, transparent, results-focused partnership has been the foundation of everything we do.

Client workflow

From first conversation to renewal

Every client follows the same clear path, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Lead

    You get in touch.

  2. Qualify

    We check there is a real need we can help with.

  3. Call

    A discovery call to understand your goals.

  4. Proposal

    A tailored, clearly scoped proposal.

  5. Agreement

    Terms agreed so both sides know what is included.

  6. Payment

    Deposit for projects; retainers billed in advance.

  7. Onboard

  8. Deliver

    We deliver to the agreed scope and timeline.

  9. Report

    Clear reporting and a regular review.

  10. Renew

    We look at what to continue, improve or add.
